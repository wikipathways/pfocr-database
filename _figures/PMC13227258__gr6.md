caption: CircRNAs regulate Hippo signaling in cardiovascular diseases (By Biorender).
  CircRNAs orchestrate the Hippo-YAP/TAZ pathway through multifaceted mechanisms,
  contributing to cardiovascular disease progression. MST1/2-SAV1 phosphorylation
  activates LATS1/2, which phosphorylates YAP/TAZ. Inactivated YAP/TAZ remains cytoplasmic,
  while nuclear YAP/TAZ binds TEAD1-4 to drive transcription of genes linked to proliferation,
  apoptosis, and fibrosis in cardiac fibrosis, cardiomyopathy, and myocardial infarction
