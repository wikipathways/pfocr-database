caption: 'Representative lncRNA–miRNA–mRNA axes driving TM fibrosis and senescence.
  The representative lncRNA–miRNA–mRNA (ceRNA) axes involved in the remodeling of
  the TM in glaucoma are depicted as follows: In the upper panel, oxidative stress
  leads to the upregulation of lncRNA GAS5, which acts as a sponge for miR-29b-3p
  (resulting in decreased levels of miR-29b-3p). This interaction releases STAT3 from
  miRNA-mediated repression, thereby enhancing STAT3 activity and promoting apoptosis
  in TM cells as well as ECM deposition. In the middle panel, lncRNA RP11-820 sequesters
  miR-3178 (resulting in decreased levels of miR-3178), which in turn upregulates
  MYOD1 and STAT3, leading to the induction of ECM-related genes such as COL1A1 and
  fibronectin. In the lower panel, lncRNA ANRIL sponges miR-7 (resulting in decreased
  levels of miR-7), thereby enhancing mTOR and MEK–ERK signaling pathways, which contribute
  to the senescence of TMCs, particularly in cases of steroid-induced glaucoma. Collectively,
  these axes demonstrate how lncRNA-driven ceRNA networks integrate fibrotic and senescent
  signals within the glaucomatous TM'
