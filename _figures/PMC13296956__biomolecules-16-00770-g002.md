caption: NMDAR-mediated pathological signaling pathways following ischemic stroke.
  The schematic illustrates the excitotoxic cascade triggered by cerebral ischemia.
  Cerebral ischemia triggers excessive glutamate release, leading to NMDAR over-activation
  and intracellular Ca2+ overload. (Left) The Ca2+-dependent CaMKII/ERK/CREB axis
  modulates BDNF expression; under pathological conditions, aberrant LTP induction
  contributes to post-stroke hyperexcitability. (Right) Alternatively, PSD-95/nNOS
  coupling promotes NO production, which inhibits the ERK pathway and stimulates mitochondrial
  ROS generation. These synergistic processes lead to impaired neuroplasticity and
  accelerate neuronal death
