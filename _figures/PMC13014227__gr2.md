caption: Therapeutic mechanisms of EVs in chronic wound healing. Schematic illustration
  of key signaling pathways involved in EV-mediated skin repair. EVs suppress inflammation
  via miR-20a-mediated inhibition of the JAK/STAT pathway and miR-146a-induced suppression
  of the NF-κB pathway. VEGF and EGF carried by EVs activate the PI3K/AKT/mTOR and
  Ras/MEK/ERK pathways, respectively. miR-21 and miR-145 further enhance angiogenesis
  by targeting both pathways. Wnt proteins in EVs activate the β-catenin pathway to
  promote cell proliferation. Anti-fibrotic effects are achieved through 14-3-3ζ,
  which binds to phosphorylated LATS and subsequently to phosphorylated YAP, resulting
  in cytoplasmic retention of YAP and preventing its translocation into the nucleus,
  thereby reducing abnormal cell proliferation and scar formation. miR-145 and miR-125b
  suppress the TGF-β/Smad2/3 pathway, reducing fibrosis
