caption: 'Canonical pathway of miRNA biogenesis. (A) Nuclear Processing: The process
  initiates with the transcription of primary miRNA (pri-miRNA) by RNA Polymerase
  II/III. The Microprocessor complex, comprising the RNase III enzyme Drosha and the
  cofactor DGCR8, cleaves the pri-miRNA into a hairpin-structured precursor miRNA
  (pre-miRNA) of approximately 70 nucleotides. (B) Nuclear Export: The pre-miRNA is
  recognized by Exportin-5 (XPO5) in a Ran-GTP-dependent manner and translocated from
  the nucleus to the cytoplasm. (C) Cytoplasmic Processing: In the cytoplasm, the
  RNase III enzyme Dicer, associated with TRBP, cleaves the terminal loop of the pre-miRNA,
  generating a mature miRNA duplex (~ 22 nucleotides). (D) RISC Loading: The functional
  strand (guide strand, typically 5p or 3p depending on thermodynamic stability) is
  loaded into the Argonaute (AGO) protein to form the RNA-induced silencing complex
  (RISC), while the passenger strand is degraded. The RISC then targets complementary
  mRNA sequences to induce translational repression or mRNA degradation'
