caption: Left of the dotted line represents signaling in quiescent myocytes, and the
  right represents synthetic myocytes. TRPV1 and TRPM8 channels, which are activated
  by heat and cold respectively, are predominantly expressed in quiescent myocytes
  and downregulated in synthetic myocytes. Activation of these channels was shown
  to inhibit the activation of fibroproliferative genes through various mechanisms.
  TRPV1 activation by capsaicin activates AMPK (to induce autophagy) and activates
  PPAR⍺ while inhibiting AKT (to reduce remodeling). TRPM8 inhibits RhoA, ROCK, and
  JAK2 to cause myocyte relaxation. TRPC1,3,4, and 6, TRPM7, TRPP1/2 and Piezo-1 are
  functional in quiescent myocytes but show increased expression in synthetic arterial
  myocytes. TRPC1,3,4, and 6 and TRPM7 are activated by PLC-coupled receptors. Piezo-1
  is a bona fide mechanosensitive nonselective channel while TRPP1/2 were also proposed
  as mechanosensitive channels. Activation of these channels induces the induction
  of fibroproliferative pathways through various mechanisms. Studies proposed that
  TRPC3 activates EGFR, TRPC4 activates CREB, TRPM7 activates Pyk2, ERK, and MEK,
  and Piezo-1 couples to the YAP pathway. Created using BioRender
