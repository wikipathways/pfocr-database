caption: 'Pre-IL-36 require processing by neutrophil-derived proteases, resulting
  in bioactive IL-36 agonists or antagonists. IL-36α/β/γ form complexes with IL-36R
  to recruit IL-1 receptor helper protein (IL-1RAcP) co-receptors. Then, binds to
  MyD88, activates NF-κB and mitogen-activated protein kinase signaling pathways,
  regulates downstream transcription of target genes, and generates inflammatory responses.
  This pathway can be antagonized by IL-36Ra. IL-36 cytokines also induce the expression
  of multiple cytokines (IL-1β, IL-17A, IL-23, and TNF-α), further spreading this
  pro-inflammatory cycle. CD4+: CD4+ T cells; CXCL: Chemokines (CXCL1, CXCL2, CXCL8)
  ; DC: Dendritic cells; Mon: Monocytes; MPO: Myeloperoxidase; NETs: Neutrophil extracellular
  traps; NF-κB: Nuclear factor-kappa B; Pre-IL-36: Precursor form of IL-36 cytokines;
  pDC: Plasmacytoid dendritic cells; SERPINA1/3: Serine protease inhibitors 1/3; TNF:
  Tumor necrosis factor'
