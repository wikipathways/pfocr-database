caption: 'Collagen remodeling in breast cancer: structural and signaling changes.
  a Lysyl oxidase (LOX)-mediated cross-linking leads to collagen network stiffening
  and increased anisotropy, which in turn promotes epithelial-mesenchymal phenotypic
  transition, stemness maintenance, migration and invasion, and therapeutic resistance
  through the integrin β1-FAK/ILK-YAP/TAZ mechanotransduction axis. b Discoidin domain
  receptors 2 (DDR2), activatedby COL1, stabilizes the epithelial-mesenchymal transition
  (EMT) regulator Snail1 via Src-dependent ERK2 phosphorylation, and facilitating
  invasion through collagen-dense stroma. c The α3 chain of collagen V interacts with
  glypican-1 (GPC1), a cell surface proteoglycan, to enhance GPC1’s function as a
  co-receptor for FGF2. This interaction amplifies FGF2-mediated Ras/Raf/ERK signaling,
  promoting tumor cell proliferation. d COL10α1 binds integrin β1 to activate the
  PI3K/AKT pathway; its knockdown abrogates chronic stress-stimulated TNBC progression.
  e Collagen XVIII forms a complex with ErbB receptors and α6-integrin, activating
  MAPK/AKT cascades to maintain cancer stem cell (CSC) properties. Targeting collage
  XVIII improves the efficacy of ErbB inhibitors, overcoming intrinsic drug resistance.
  f Platelet glycoprotein VI (GPVI) interacts with cancer cell-derived galectin-3
  to promote metastatic dissemination. g H3K27 acetylation-activated COL6A1 facilitates
  osteosarcoma lung metastasis via STAT1 repression and pulmonary fibroblast activation'
