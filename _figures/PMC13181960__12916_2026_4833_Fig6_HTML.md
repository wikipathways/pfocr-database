caption: 'Immunosenescence in RTIs. Age-related innate immunity dysfunction: Neutrophils.
  Exhibit impaired phagocytosis and opsonization. DCs. Show reduced infection response
  capacity and impaired priming of CD4⁺/CD8⁺ T cells. NK cells. Aging leads to decreased
  NK cell numbers and function, with a shift toward more immature and fewer fully
  mature NK cells. Aging also reshapes NK cell subsets, reducing early high‑function
  subsets while increasing late low‑cytotoxic subsets. Aging upregulates inflammation‑related
  genes and downregulates antiviral‑related genes in NK cells. Age‑related changes
  also impaired ability to clear pathogens of NK cells. Monocytes/macrophages. Exhibit
  impaired antigen presentation, dysfunctional phagocytosis, and excessive inflammatory
  responses. Senescent macrophages show abnormal activation of the cGAS/STING pathway
  and NLRP3 inflammasome, contributing to increased susceptibility to influenza A
  and SARS-CoV-2 infections with severe clinical outcomes. Age-related adaptive immunity
  dysfunction: T cells. Display reduced TCR diversity and T cell subset imbalance.
  Expanded Treg populations suppress effector T cells, fostering an immunosuppressive
  microenvironment. B cells. Exhibit age-related defects in CSR and SHM, impaired
  differentiation of memory B cells to plasmablasts/ASCs, expansion of pro-inflammatory
  double negative B cells, contracted B cell repertoires, defects in IgM B-cell proliferation,
  diminished antibody production and protective capacity, and epigenetic modifications.
  DCs dendritic cells, CD cluster of differentiation, NK natural killer, cGAS cyclic
  GMP-AMP synthase, STING stimulator of interferon genes, NLRP3 NLR family pyrin domain
  containing 3, SARS-CoV-2 severe acute respiratory syndrome coronavirus 2, TCR T
  cell receptor, Treg regulatory T cell, CSR class switch recombination, SHM somatic
  hypermutation, ASCs antibody-secreting cells, IgM immunoglobulin M'
