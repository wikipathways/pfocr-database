caption: 'Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) illustrating the relationships between the
  Nova food classification (measured in grams per day) and colorectal cancer incidence,
  with potential confounders and mediators. Green nodes represent the covariates included
  in Model 1 (marital status, educational level, smoking status, physical activity,
  and height), constituting the minimally sufficient adjustment set for estimating
  the total association between ultra-processed food intake and colorectal cancer.
  Orange nodes represent additional variables included in Model 2 (BMI and macronutrients:
  protein, carbohydrates, fat, and alcohol intake). Blue nodes represent further dietary
  variables included in Model 3 (fibre, vitamin D, vitamin C, and calcium intake).
  The latter variables may lie on the causal pathway and were included to estimate
  composition-adjusted associations. Arrows depict the hypothesised causal relationships
  between exposure, mediators, and outcome'
