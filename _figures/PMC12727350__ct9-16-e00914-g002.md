caption: Mechanism diagram of LPS-regulating GLS expression through the TLR4/NF-κB
  signaling axis to promote HCC progression. This figure illustrates that LPS activates
  the NF-κB signaling pathway through TLR4, thereby upregulating the expression of
  hepatic GLS. GLS promotes glutamine metabolism to generate glutamate and α-KG; the
  latter enters the TCA cycle to provide energy and biosynthetic precursors, supporting
  the proliferation and migration of HCC cells. In addition, enhanced GLS activity
  also promotes tumor invasion through EMT. The figure also indicates that the GLS
  inhibitor BPTES can effectively inhibit the growth and migration of HCC cells. α-KG,
  α-ketoglutarate; EMT, epithelial-mesenchymal transition; GLS, glutaminase; HCC,
  hepatocellular carcinoma; LPS, lipopolysaccharide; NF-κB, nuclear factor κB; TCA,
  tricarboxylic acid; TLR4, toll-like receptor 4
