caption: Pathogenesis of ADPKD. In ADPKD, polycystin deficiency leads to downregulation
  of intracellular calcium signaling, which releases the inhibition of the mTOR and
  NF-κB pathways, driving cystic epithelial cell proliferation and hypersecretion,
  and triggering basement membrane degradation and abnormal ECM deposition (including
  laminin 5 disorder). These changes recruit and polarize M2 macrophages, which further
  deteriorate the interstitial environment through pro-fibrotic effects, ultimately
  constituting a self-sustaining malignant cycle that drives the occurrence, expansion,
  and progression of renal cysts
