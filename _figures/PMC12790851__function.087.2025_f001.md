caption: 'Schematic representation of DAMPs and PAMPs with their PRRs. This schematic
  illustrates how the innate immune system detects sterile and infectious stimuli
  via PRRs. A: DAMPs derived from the extracellular matrix, cytosol, and mitochondria—including
  HMGB1, histones, ATP, DNA, tenascin C, and others—are recognized by TLRs and purinergic
  receptors. B: PAMPs from viruses, fungi, and gram-positive or gram-negative bacteria—such
  as LPS, peptidoglycan, flagellin, and microbial nucleic acids—engage corresponding
  PRRs. Together, these interactions activate downstream inflammatory signaling pathways
  central to immune defense and tissue homeostasis. ATP, adenosine triphosphate; CLR,
