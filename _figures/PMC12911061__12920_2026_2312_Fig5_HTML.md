caption: FGFR2 pathway regulates limb development. Mg2+ or other fibroblast growth
  factors (FGFs) can interact with the FGFR2 receptor, leading to structural changes
  in FGFR2, which in turn induces dimerization and activation of FGFR2. Activated
  FGFR2 then phosphorylates FRS2, causing FRS2 to associate with Grb2. Grb2 then binds
  to SOS, initiating downstream signaling pathways such as RAS/RAF/MAPK and PI3K/AKT.
  In addition, activated FGFR2 can also trigger the JAK/STAT pathway. PLCγ binds to
  FGFR and catalyzes the hydrolysis of PIP2 to produce DAG and IP3. IP3 activates
  PKC and Ca2+ signaling, leading to activation of downstream gene expression and
  limb development. FGFR substrate 2 (FRS2), growth factor receptor-bound 2 (Grb2),
  son of sevenless (SOS), Phospholipase C gamma (PLCγ), diacylglycerol (DAG), inositol
  trisphosphate (IP3), protein kinase C (PKC)
