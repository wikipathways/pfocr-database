caption: 'Schematic Overview of Vitamin C–Mediated Anti-tumor Mechanisms: Pro-oxidative
  Stress, Metabolic Inhibition, Epigenetic Regulation, and TME Modulation. VitC enters
  tumor cells via SVCT2, while DHA competitively enters through GLUT1. Extracellular
  VitC undergoes Fe3⁺/Fe2⁺-dependent oxidation to generate H2O2, which passes through
  aquaporins and reacts with the intracellular labile iron pool to produce ROS. DHA
  further increases ROS by disrupting redox balance through GSH/GSSG cycling, subsequently
  inhibiting glycolysis (red X) via GAPDH blockade and redirecting glucose flux to
  the pentose phosphate pathway (PPP), (blue arrows). Accumulated ROS impair mitochondria
  (↓OCR, ↓ATP) and induce DNA damage with PARP activation. VitC also enhances TET-dependent
  DNA demethylation and stimulates antitumor immunity by activating STAT1 signaling,
  cGAS–STING pathways, and T-cell responses'
