caption: Vitamin D can reduce oxidative stress and inflammatory responsesIn obesity,
  an excess supply of electron transport chain (ETC) molecules leads to an increase
  in the NADH/NAD+ ratio, resulting in mitochondrial dysfunction, impaired SIRT-3
  activity, and increased generation of reactive oxygen species (ROS). Vitamin D can
  negatively regulate the ETC, improve mitochondrial function, and increase SIRT3
  activity. Ferroptosis can also result in the production of many types of ROS, and
  vitamin D can inhibit ferroptosis through multiple signaling pathways. Furthermore,
  vitamin D can inhibit the activity of the NLRP3 inflammasome, thereby decreasing
  the production of inflammatory factors such as IL-1β and IL-18. Therefore, vitamin
  D can ameliorate insulin resistance by reducing oxidative stress and inflammatory
  responses
