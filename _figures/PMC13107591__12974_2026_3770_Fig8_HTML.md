caption: 'Mechanism of enhanced endogenous neuroprotection via the Nrf2-ARE antioxidant
  pathway. The schematic illustrates the molecular cascade by which phytochemical
  agents counteract pollution-induced oxidative stress. (Left Panel) Chronic exposure
  to particulate matter (PM) and diesel exhaust generates excessive reactive oxygen
  species (ROS), exhausting basal cellular defenses and compromising neurovascular
  integrity. (Middle Panel) Under homeostatic conditions, the transcription factor
  Nrf2 is sequestered in the cytoplasm by the inhibitor protein Keap1. Pharmacological
  inducers, such as sulforaphane (green) and curcumin (yellow), disrupt this interaction,
  facilitating the nuclear translocation of Nrf2. (Right Panel) Within the nucleus,
  Nrf2 binds to the Antioxidant Response Element (ARE) in the promoter regions of
  target genes. This binding drives the robust transcriptional upregulation of Phase
  II detoxification enzymes, particularly Heme Oxygenase-1 (HO-1). The resulting restoration
  of redox homeostasis leads to the stabilization of BBB tight junctions and a significant
  reduction in peripheral leukocyte infiltration. Abbreviations: ARE, Antioxidant
  Response Element; HO-1, Heme Oxygenase-1; Keap1, Kelch-like ECH-associated protein
  1; Nrf2, nuclear factor erythroid 2-related factor 2; ROS, reactive oxygen species'
