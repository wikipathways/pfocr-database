caption: Three pathways of tryptophan metabolism. tryptophan undergoes metabolism
  via three principal pathways (kynurenine pathway, 5-HT pathway, and indole pathway)
  to generate bioactive metabolites. Neuroprotective metabolites in colored green
  words, neurotoxic metabolites in colored red words, and key enzymes in metabolism
  in colored blue words. The thickness of the arrows with tryptophan as the source
  represents the competition of each pathway for the tryptophan pool. NFK, N-formyl-L-kynurenine;
  KYN, kynurenine; KYNA, kynurenic acid; 3-HK, 3-hydroxykynurenine; XA, xanthurenic
  acid; AA, anturanilic acid; 3-HAA, 3-hydroxyanthranilic acid; ACMS, 2-amino-3-carboxymuconic
  6-semialdehyde; QA, quinolinic acid; AMS, 2-aminomuconic-6-semialdehyde; PA, picolinic
  acid; 5-HTP, 5-hydroxytryptophan; 5-HT, Serotonin; 5-HIAA, 5-hydroxyindole acetic
  acid; IAAld, indole-3-acetaldehyde; IAM, indole-3-acetamide; IAA, indole acetic
  acid; IAld, indole-3-aldehyde; IPYA, indole-3-pyruvate; ILA, indole-3-lactic acid;
  IA, indole acrylic acid; IPA, indole-3-propionic acid; IDO, Indoleamine 2,3-Dioxygenase;
  TDO, tryptophan 2,3-Dioxygenase; KAT, kynurenine aminotransferase; KMO, kynurenine
  3-Monooxygenase; KYNU, Kynureninase;AMO, anthranilate 3-monooxygenase; 3-HAAO, 3-hydroxyanthranilic
  acid 3,4-dioxygenase; QRPT, quinolinate phosphoribosyl transferase; ACMSD, 2-amino-3-carboxymuconate-semialdehyde
  decarboxylase; AMSD, 2-aminomuconic acid semialdehyde dehydrogenase; TPH1, tryptophan
  hydroxylase 1; AADC, aromatic L-amino acid decarboxylase; MAO, Monoamine oxidase;
