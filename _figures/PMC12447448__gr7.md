caption: Proposed model for the function of RISBZ1 in grain filling and ER stress
  signaling.Under normal conditions, RISBZ1 primarily activates genes involved in
  starch and storage protein synthesis. Under ER stress, bZIP50 and bZIP60 translocate
  from the ER to the nucleus and activate the UPR signaling pathway. In the nucleus,
  RISBZ1 interacts with bZIP50 and bZIP60 to inhibit their DNA-binding activity, thereby
  suppressing the expression of UPR-related genes. This interaction also represses
  RISBZ1-mediated transcription of starch and storage protein synthesis genes. Thus,
  RISBZ1 alleviates ER stress at the cost of reduced dry-matter accumulation, maintaining
  a balance between grain filling and adaptation to environmental stress
