caption: External physical stimulation strategies and their role in bone regeneration.
  Mechanical loading, magnetic fields, and ultrasound activate mechanotransduction
  pathways in bone cells, triggering signaling cascades such as Wnt/β-catenin, Smad,
  and MAPK. These pathways enhance endogenous secretion of growth factors (e.g., BMPs,
  VEGF, IGF-1) and cytokines, driving osteoblast differentiation, osteocyte activity,
  and mineralization while modulating the immune microenvironment to balance osteoclast
  and osteoblast functions. Collectively, these stimuli promote coordinated bone remodeling
  and regeneration. Created with BioRender.com
