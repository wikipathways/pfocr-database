caption: The Regulatory Landscape of miRNAs in Glioblastoma (GBM) Pathogenesis. This
  schematic illustrates how the dysregulation of microRNAs drives the six canonical
  hallmarks of GBM malignancy. The central tumor is depicted as the battleground between
  OncomiRs (represented in red/promoting motifs), which drive cancer progression,
  and Tumor Suppressor miRNAs (TS-miRNAs) (represented in blue/protective shields),
  which inhibit it. Regarding Proliferative Signaling, OncomiRs such as miR-21 and
  miR-10b bypass growth suppressors to sustain division, whereas TS-miRNAs like miR-7
  block these pathways. Resistance to Cell Death is shown as an active evasion of
  apoptosis and autophagy mediated by OncomiRs, while TS-miRNAs attempt to trigger
  cell death. The figure highlights Replicative Immortality via the maintenance of
  Glioblastoma Stem Cells (GSCs) by OncomiRs, contrasted with the differentiation
  promoted by TS-miRNAs. Angiogenesis and Invasion are depicted as key structural
  changes driven by AngiomiRs (e.g., miR-296) and invasion-promoting miRNAs that activate
  EMT. Finally, the Drug Resistance panel identifies OncomiRs as active contributors
  to Temozolomide (TMZ) failure, while TS-miRNAs offer a mechanism for chemosensitization.
  The illustration concludes by positioning Exercise as a potential therapeutic modulator
  capable of restoring the balance of these miRNAs to favor tumor suppression
