caption: 'Schematic illustration of MDPA-mediated mechano-immune reprogramming in
  colorectal cancer. MDPAs leverage platelet-mimetic targeting to accumulate in tumors
  and initiate a dual-pathway remodeling process. Path 1 (Mechanical): Released Defactinib
  inhibits the Integrin-FAK-YAP axis, reducing ECM stiffness and cytoskeletal tension
  to dismantle physical barriers. Path 2 (Immune): This mechanical softening facilitates
  CD8+ T cell infiltration, which synergizes with anti-PD-L1 checkpoint blockade to
  drive tumor cells toward an antigen-presenting phenotype and activate anti-tumor
  immunity. Collectively, this strategy reshapes the immune-epithelial-stromal crosstalk
  to promote tumor regression'
