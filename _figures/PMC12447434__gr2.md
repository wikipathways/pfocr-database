caption: 'WRKY-mediated regulation of flavonoid metabolism, illustrated by the anthocyanin
  biosynthesis pathway.Anthocyanins are synthesized through the phenylpropanoid pathway
  (PPP), which involves early biosynthesis genes (EBGs) and late biosynthesis genes
  (LBGs) that convert phenylalanine into various flavonoid precursors. These metabolites
  are transported to vacuoles for storage or further modification. The MBW complex
  regulates the expression of genes involved in these biosynthesis and modification
  processes. WRKY TFs play a crucial role in anthocyanin biosynthesis by directly
  activating or repressing target biosynthesis genes and by interacting with the MBW
  complex to modulate gene expression. In the schematic, gray boxes indicate WRKY
  TFs; rectangles represent direct transcriptional regulation by WRKYs, while ovals
  depict regulation through the MBW complex. Abbreviations: 4CL, 4-coumaroyl-CoA ligase;
  ANR, anthocyanidin reductase; ANS, anthocyanin synthase; C3H, coumarate 3-hydroxylase;
  C4H, cinnamate 4-hydroxylase; CHI, chalcone isomerase; CHS, chalcone synthase; DFR,
  dihydroflavonol 4-reductase; F3H, flavanone 3-hydroxylase; PAL, phenylalanine ammonia
  lyase; OMT, O-methyltransferase. Species-specific gene prefixes used in all figures:
  At, Arabidopsis thaliana; Gh, Gossypium hirsutum; Ii, Isatis indigotica; Pp, Prunus
  persica; Sl, Solanum lycopersicum; Md, Malus domestica; Cs, Camellia sinensis; Vv,
  Vitis vinifera; Os, Oryza sativa; Bn, Brassica napus; Cr, Citrus reticulata; Ma,
  Musa acuminata; Of, Osmanthus fragrans; Sm, Solanum melongena; Py, Pyrus pyrifolia'
