caption: 'Pathways involved in circRNA degradation. Several cellular mechanisms tightly
  regulate CircRNA stability. (i) RNAi pathway: AGO2, in association with miRNAs,
  can guide the degradation of circRNAs, often mediated through GW182, linking circRNAs
  to post-transcriptional silencing complexes. (ii) m6A modification–dependent degradation:
  m6A marks on circRNAs recruit the m6A reader YTHDF2, which interacts with HSRP12
  and facilitates the recruitment of RNase P/MRP endoribonucleases, thereby promoting
  selective circRNA decay. (iii) RBP complex–mediated degradation: Several RBP complexes
  participate in circRNA turnover. For instance, RNase H1 can degrade circRNAs that
  form DNA–RNA hybrids; UPF1, together with G3BP1, recognises specific circRNA structures
  to promote their clearance; and RNase L is activated during viral infection, leading
  to circRNA cleavage and the subsequent release of PKR, which enhances antiviral
  responses. Created in BioRender. Ling, K. (2025) https://BioRender.com/q5kq0pa'
