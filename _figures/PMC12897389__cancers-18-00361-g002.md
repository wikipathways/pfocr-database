caption: The phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase (PI3K)/protein kinase B (AKT)/mechanistic
  target of rapamycin (mTOR) signaling pathway and therapeutic points of intervention.
  Growth factor (GF)–mediated activation of receptor tyrosine kinases (RTKs) leads
  to PI3K-dependent conversion of phosphatidylinositol-4,5-bisphosphate (PIP2) to
  phosphatidylinositol-3,4,5-trisphosphate (PIP3), which activates AKT via phosphoinositide-dependent
  kinase-1 (PDK1) and mTOR complex 2 (mTORC2). AKT regulates downstream targets including
  tuberous sclerosis complex 1/2 (TSC1/2), Ras homolog enriched in brain (RHEB), and
  proline-rich AKT substrate 40 kDa (PRAS40), leading to activation of mTOR complex
  1 (mTORC1). PTEN denotes phosphatase and tensin homolog. Red symbols indicate pharmacological
  points of inhibition
