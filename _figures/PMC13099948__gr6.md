caption: 'Roles of other types of regulated cell death (RCD) in atherosclerosis (AS).
  Some other RCD have been confirmed to be related to AS. First, cuproptosis is initiated
  by copper dyshomeostasis, characterized by the aggregation of lipoylated proteins
  and the loss of Fe-S cluster proteins, which ultimately results in mitochondrial
  dysfunction, and cell death. Second, lysosome-dependent cell death (LDCD), triggered
  by oxidized low-density lipoprotein (ox-LDL), is marked by lysosomal membranes permeabilization
  (LMP) and the release of cathepsins. Third, NETotic cell death is characterized
  by nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide phosphate hydrides (NADPH) oxidase-mediated
  reactive oxygen species (ROS) production, histones citrullination, and excessive
  neutrophil extracellular traps (NETs) release in response to infection or injury.
  Fourth, paraptosis is a nonapoptotic form of cell death mediated through insulin-like
  growth factor I receptor (IGFIR)-mitogen-activated protein kinase (MAPK) signaling
  pathways. It is characterized by the accumulation of large vacuoles derived from
  both dilated endoplasmic reticulum (ER) and mitochondria under conditions of impaired
  proteostasis and imbalanced ion homeostasis. In addition, there are some other types
  of RCD that are suggested to be involved in the progression of AS, but direct evidence
  is still lacking. Fifth, alkaliptosis is triggered by alkaline agents, which inhibit
  carbonic anhydrase 9 (CA9) via nuclear factor-κB (NF-κB) and facilitates the formation
  of ATPase H+ transporting V0 subunit D1 (ATP6V0D1)-signal transducer and activator
  of transcription 3 (STAT3) complex, leading to lysosomal acidification and cytosolic
  alkalinization. Sixth, oxeiptosis is a ROS-induced cell death type, characterized
  by dissociation of phosphoglycerate mutase 5 (PGAM5) from Kelch-like ECH-associated
  protein 1 (KEAP1), subsequently leading to PGAM5 translocation into the nucleus,
  where it interacts with and dephosphorylates apoptosis-inducing factor mitochondria-associated
  1 (AIFM1). Seventh, entotic cell death is primarily driven by matrix detachment
  via Rho family GTPase 3 (Rnd3)-RhoA-Rho-associated protein kinase (ROCK)-Actomyosin
  pathway to promote cell internalization, and then the engulfed cells may undergo
  division, release, or degradation. Eighth, PANoptosis is characterized by a multifaceted
  mechanism, which simultaneously activates pyroptosis, apoptosis, and necroptosis.
  ox-LDL-treated macrophages, human umbilical vein endothelial cell (HUVEC) and vascular
  smooth muscle cell (VSMC) highly expressed PANoptosis-based genes, specifically
  absent in melanoma 2 (AIM2) and Z-DNA-binding protein 1 (ZBP1). GSDMD: gasdermin
  D; MLKL: mixed lineage kinase domain-like protein'
