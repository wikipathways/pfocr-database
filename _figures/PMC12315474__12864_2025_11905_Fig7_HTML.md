caption: 'Bioactive components related proteins. A Triterpenoids biosynthesis pathway.
  Black characters represent metabolites. HMG-CoA: 3-hydroxy-3-methylglutaryl-CoA;
  IPP: isopentenyl diphosphate; DMAPP: dimethylallyl diphosphate; GPP: geranyl diphosphate;
  FPP: farnesyl diphosphate; HLDOA: 3-hydroxy-lanosta-8,24-dien-26-oic acid; DHLDOA:
  3,28-dihydroxy-lanosta-8,24-dien-26-oic acid. Red characters represent enzymes identified
  in this study, and grey characters represent enzymes not identified in this study.
  AACT: acetyl-CoA acetyltransferase; HMGS: 3-hydroxy-3-methylglutaryl CoA synthase;
  HMGR:3-hydroxy-3-methylglutaryl CoA reductase; MK: mevalonate kinase; PMK: phosphomevalonate
  kinase; MVD: mevalonate pyrophosphate decarboxylase; IDI: isopentenyl diphosphate
  isomerase; FDPS: farnesyl-diphosphate synthase; SQS: squalene synthase; SE: squalene
  epoxidase; LSS: oxidosqualene-lanosterol cyclase. The heatmaps under enzymes represent
  expression profiles of enzymes in the 3 G. lucidum strains, and the 3 squares of
  the heatmap from left to right represent expressions in CGMCC5.0026, G.201702252-13,
  and G.2017030625-3, respectively. B Expression levels of immunomodulatory proteins.
  Letters (a and b) at the top of each plot show significantly different expressions
  (p < 0.05) in Tukey’s multiple comparison test'
