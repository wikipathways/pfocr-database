caption: NACT induces significant upregulation of lactylation on key DDR proteins,
  including PARP1. A, differential expression of lactylation sites enriched in the
  DDR pathway within CLANs, shown as tumor/normal fold change (FC) across the three
  groups. p Values were adjusted using the BH method. ∗Adjusted p < 0.05. B, impact
  of Kla on DDR pathways. The left bar plot shows the GSEA normalized enrichment scores
  (NESs) for four DDR pathways across the three groups. Bar color indicates -log10
  (p value). The DDR pathways comprise base excision repair (BER), nucleotide excision
  repair (NER), nonhomologous end joining (NHEJ), and homologous recombination (HR).
  The right heatmap displays Spearman's correlation coefficients between CLAN Kla
  site expression (from Fig. 5A) and the ssGSEA enrichment scores of the four DDR
  pathways. ∗Adjusted p < 0.05 (BH method). Gene sets were obtained from the GO-BP
  database. C, NACT-induced dynamic profile of proteins and Kla within the DDR pathway.
  Red oval indicates proteins with upregulated DELs, followed by their specific Kla
  sites and corresponding fold changes. Protein expression fold changes (tumor/normal)
  are shown below protein identifiers. D, MS/MS spectrum of the PARP1 K654la peptide.
  The b-ions represent N-terminal fragments, whereas the y-ions indicate C-terminal
  fragments. E, lactate-induced PARP1 lactylation. FLAG-tagged PARP1 was ectopically
  expressed in KYSE-150, KYSE-450, and KYSE-30 cell lines, followed by treatment with
  or without 10 mM sodium lactate (NaLa) for 24 h. PARP1 lactylation was analyzed
  by immunoprecipitation and Western blot (IP–WB) analysis. F, suppression of lactate-induced
  PARP1 lactylation by K654R mutation. FLAG-tagged PARP1 WT and K654R mutant were
  expressed in KYSE-150, KYSE-450, and KYSE-30 cell lines, followed by 10 mM NaLa
  treatment for 24 h. PARP1 lactylation status was analyzed by IP–WB analysis. BH,
  Benjamini–Hochberg; CLAN, crucial lactylation regulator associated with NACT; DDR,
  DNA damage response; DEL, differentially expressed Kla site; GO–BP, Gene Ontology
  Biological Process; GSEA, gene set enrichment analysis; Kla, lysine lactylation;
  MS, mass spectrometry; NACT, neoadjuvant chemotherapy; PARP1, poly(ADP-ribose) polymerase
  1; ssGSEA, single-sample gene set enrichment analysis
