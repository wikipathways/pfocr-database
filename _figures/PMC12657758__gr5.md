caption: Dietary plant protein modulates metabolic mechanisms in Pacific white shrimp
  via nutrient-sensing pathways. This figure contrasts the effects of adequate fishmeal
  diets versus high plant protein diets in this regulatory process. Adequate fishmeal
  activates the mTOR signaling pathway and suppresses GCN2/AMPK pathways, supporting
  protein synthesis and balanced lipid metabolism. High plant protein diets inhibit
  mTOR signaling, while activating GCN2 and AMPK signaling pathways. This signaling
  shift induces lipid deposition in the hepatopancreas. Green arrows represent pathway
  activation; red arrows (with blocked ends) represent pathway inhibition. mTOR =
  mechanistic target of rapamycin; GCN2 = general control non-derepressible 2; AMPK
  = AMP-activated protein kinase; p70S6K = p70 ribosomal protein S6 kinase; 4E-BP1
  = eukaryotic initiation factor 4E-binding protein 1; eIF2α = eukaryotic initiation
  factor-2α. The circled “P” (upper right) indicates protein phosphorylation
