caption: The apoptosis pathway model proposed for Haemonchus contortus. Hc-EGL-1,
  -CED-9, -CED-4, and -CED-3 form the canonical pathway. EGL-1 antagonises CED-9 to
  release CED-4, which then activates CED-3 to execute apoptosis. Additional regulators,
  including Hc-DRE-1 and -PUF-8, are also present; Hc-DRE-1 can substitute for Hc-EGL-1
  by binding to Hc-CED-9 to activate apoptosis, and Hc-PUF-8 modulates apoptosis by
  suppressing the translation of Hc-CED-9 or -CED-3. Created in BioRender. Taki, A.
