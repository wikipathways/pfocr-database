caption: The scheme of the underlying mechanisms about WBVT-induced improvements on
  muscle mass and strength in elderly people. WBVT might improve muscle mass and strength
  in elderly people through intra- (promoting myogenesis by increasing the levels
  of MRFs, stathmin, type I collagen and decorin, and activating PI3K/Akt pathway,
  increasing protein synthesis (via activating PI3K/Akt/mTOR pathway) and decreasing
  protein degradation (by FoxO1-mediated MURF1 and MAFbx expression)) and extra-muscular
  pathways (improving muscle capillary via downregulating anti-angiogenic factor CD36
  and upregulating pro-angiogenic factor (VEGF-A), and enhancing neuromuscular transmission
  via improving NMJ structure)
