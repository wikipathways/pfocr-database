caption: Roles of Piezo2 activation in diverse cells across physiological systems.
  (a) In pulmonary neuroepithelial cells, Piezo2 activation drives foetal lung growth
  and this effect is mediated by upregulated ghrelin, bombesin, ɑ‐SMA and MLC2 expression
  [117]. (b) In enterochromaffin cells, Piezo2 activation may have dual effects, with
  one pathway promoting secretory cell differentiation through NOTCH signalling, and
  the other pathway balancing self‐renewal/proliferation through WNT signalling [20].
  (c) In umbrella cells, Piezo2 activation regulates both ionotropic P2X receptors
  and Piezo2 channels on afferent nerve terminals through secretion of signalling
  molecules, ultimately resulting in appropriate micturition reflexes [22, 92, 93].
  (d) In cochlear progenitor cells, activation of Piezo2 channels has been observed
  to affect the intracellular Ca2+/ERK1/2/KLF2 signalling cascades, ultimately leading
  to hair cell differentiation [98]. Created in BioRender
