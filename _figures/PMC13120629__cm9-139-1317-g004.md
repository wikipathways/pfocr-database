caption: 'Effect of reprogramming macrophage glucose metabolism on ferroptosis signaling.
  The schematic integrates how M1 and M2 macrophage-specific metabolic programs differentially
  configure the ferroptosis machinery. M1 macrophages, despite having a high labile
  iron pool, are resistant to ferroptosis due to a metabolic configuration that supports
  robust antioxidant defense: high glycolytic and PPP flux ensures ample NADPH production.
  Conversely, M2 macrophages, reliant on OXPHOS and with lower glycolytic/PPP activity,
  lack this metabolic capacity for rapid antioxidant generation, making them more
  sensitive to ferroptosis inducers. α-KG: α-Ketoglutarate; Acetyl-CoA: Acetyl–coenzyme
  A; CoQ: Coenzyme; CoQH2: Ubiquinol; CPT1: Carnitine palmitoyltransferase 1; F-6-P:
  Fructose 6-phosphate; FAO: Fatty acid oxidation; G-6-P: Glucose 6-phosphate; GLS:
  Glutaminase; GPX4: GSH peroxidase 4; HIF-1α: Hypoxia inducible factor-1α; FSP1:
  Ferroptosis suppressor protein 1; IDH: Isocitrate dehydrogenase; NADPH: Nicotinamide
  adenine dinucleotide phosphate; Nrf2: Nuclear factor E2-related factor 2; OXPHOS:
  Oxidative phosphorylation; PDH: Pyruvate dehydrogenase; PKM2: Pyruvate kinase M2;
  PPP: Pentose phosphate pathway; PUFA: Polyunsaturated fatty acid; ROS: Reactive
  oxygen species; SDH: Succinate dehydrogenase; SLC7A11: Solute carrier family 7 member
  11; TCA: Tricarboxylic acid. Drawn by Figdraw'
