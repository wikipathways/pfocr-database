caption: Function of Hippo-YAP pathway in cardiomyocytes. Activation of the core kinases
  MST1 and LATS2 in cardiomyocytes is mediated by scaffold proteins NF2 and RASSF1A,
  and the cofactor SAV1. MST1 promotes apoptosis and inhibits autophagy through inhibitory
  phosphorylation of Bcl-xL and Beclin-1, respectively. LATS2 inhibits YAP activation
  and promotes p53-mediated apoptosis. Hippo activation elicits cardiomyocyte death,
  adverse remodeling, and heart failure. Conversely, Hippo inactivation and YAP stimulation
  promotes cardiomyocyte proliferation and survival, and cardiac regeneration. The
