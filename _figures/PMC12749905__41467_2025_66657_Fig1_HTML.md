caption: PtlrFeMoZn HEA nanozymes were synthesized via a one-step hydrothermal method,
  and the AC-HEA nanozymes were prepared under AC stimulation pre-treatment. AC-HEA
  nanozymes can generate ROS in tumor cells through the multi-enzymatic reaction (POD,
  NOX, MPO and GPx), and induce GPX4 protein downregulation and interfere with the
  glycolytic pathway, which promote apoptosis and ferroptosis. Additionally, two immune
  pathways (lCD and the cGAS-STING) are activated simultaneously to hinder distant
  tumor metastasis
