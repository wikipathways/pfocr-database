caption: The figure illustrates the CXCR4 signaling pathway and its role in cell survival
  and proliferation, highlighting the impact of inhibitors such as Plerixafor and
  Dasatinib. CXCL12 binds to CXCR4, activating downstream signaling through Gαi proteins,
  which subsequently stimulate SRC-ERK and PI3K-AKT pathways, promoting cell survival
  and proliferation. CXCR7 also contributes to CXCR4 activation. Plerixafor inhibits
  CXCR4 by blocking CXCL12 binding, while Dasatinib and PTX disrupt SRC and PI3K signaling.
  Additionally, PDGFB activates PDGFRB and receptor tyrosine kinases (RTKs), which
  interact with CXCR4 signaling to enhance survival pathways
