caption: Mechanisms of M6A Regulators and miRNA in Gastric Cancer. Overexpression
  of METTL3 promotes m6A modification and maturation of the miR-17–92 cluster, activating
  the AKT/mTOR signaling pathway and suppressing PTEN, thereby enhancing cell proliferation
  and migration in gastric cancer. miR-4429 targets METTL3, inhibiting its function
  and consequently reducing the m6A-induced stabilization of Sect. 62, which helps
  halt GC progression. METTL3 regulates the miR-1184/TRPM2 axis through m6A modification,
  influencing calcium signaling pathways and contributing to GC progression. miR-338-5p
  targets METTL3, affecting its m6A methyltransferase activity and enhancing the translation
  of CDCP1, a protein essential for promoting GC cell proliferation and invasion
