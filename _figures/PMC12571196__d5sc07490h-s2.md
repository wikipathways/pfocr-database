caption: Isomerisation of methyl 3-butenoate 19 to methyl crotonate 1 by [(dmpe)2FeH2]
  3. Methyl crotonate 1 can then be dimerised by the base-mediated pathway to give
  the dimer intermediate dimethyl 2-ethenyl-3-methylpentanedioate 7 which itself can
  be isomerised by [(dmpe)2FeH2] 3 to give (E),(Z)-2-ethylidene-3-methylpentanedioate
  2. 31P NMR spectroscopic analysis of the reaction shows that the major iron species
  is trans-[(dmpe)2FeH(CH2CHCHCO2Me)] 21, with minor amounts of [(dmpe)2FeHY] 23.
  Minor amounts of [(dmpe)2Fe(η2-1)] 22 by thermal activation of [(dmpe)2FeH2] 3 were
  also observed
