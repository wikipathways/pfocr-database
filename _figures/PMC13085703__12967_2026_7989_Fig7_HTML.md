caption: 'This panel delineates distinct sphingolipid-related pathogenic mechanisms
  across four cancers: A) BC:​​ Four molecular subtypes are characterized: (1) ER⁺
  BC:​​ Altered sphingolipid metabolism promotes hormone-dependent proliferation;
  (2) TNBC:​​ ASAH1↑ drives Cer hydrolysis to sphingosine, optimizing the immunosuppressive
  tumor microenvironment; (3) Metastatic BC:​​ SGMS2↑ elevates SM, activating TGF-β1/Smad
  to induce EMT. (4) Post-chemotherapy BC:​​ UGCG↑ enhances Cer glycosylation, reducing
  pro-apoptotic Cer and conferring drug resistance. B) HCC:​​ Hypoxia upregulates
  nSMase, reducing Cer and promoting exosome release, angiogenesis, and metastasis
  via VEGF/MMP signaling. C) Lung Cancer:​​ Imbalanced CerS1/CerS6 expression alters
  Cer subspecies (e.g., C18‑Cer/C16‑Cer), regulating EMT, metastasis, and apoptosis
  resistance via Bcl-2/caspase pathways. D) PC:​​ FASN overexpression lowers Cer levels
  and activates SPHK1/S1P signaling, driving chemotherapy resistance and tumor proliferation
  through AKT/mTOR pathways'
