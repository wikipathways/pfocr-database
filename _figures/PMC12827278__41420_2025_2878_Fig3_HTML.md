caption: This figure depicts the reciprocal regulation between ER stress and lipid
  metabolism in tumors. UPR signaling promotes lipid biosynthesis and remodeling via
  PERK–ATF4–SREBP1, IRE1–XBP1s, and ATF6-mediated pathways. These include upregulation
  of fatty acid and cholesterol synthesis, lipid droplet formation, and adaptation
  to metabolic stress. In parallel, chronic ER stress activates the ER-anchored transcription
  factor NFE2L1, which restores proteasome function and senses cholesterol overload
  within the ER membrane to maintain proteostasis and lipid homeostasis. Conversely,
  lipid imbalance—such as saturated fatty acid overload or cholesterol accumulation—disrupts
  ER membrane properties and calcium homeostasis, triggering or exacerbating ER stress.
  Lipid droplets buffer lipotoxicity and mitigate oxidative stress, serving as protective
  compartments. This bidirectional loop supports tumor survival, metabolic plasticity,
  and therapeutic resistance
