caption: Regulation of tumor translation/organogenesis by FAT4. Purple arrows indicate
  intercellular signaling pathways. Green ovals represent specific pathway names.
  Green arrows denote intracellular protein or gene interactions. Orange ovals represent
  molecules, proteins, or genes that act on FAT4. Pink-purple shapes represent molecules,
  proteins, or genes that are targeted by FAT4. Black perpendicular lines at the end
  of arrows indicate inhibitory effects, while arrows without such lines represent
  positive/activating effects. FAT4 is regulated by various upstream factors including
  Six1, Src, miRNAs, mRNAs, GATA2, USP51, lncRNAs, and HDACs. Through PCP and Hippo
  signaling pathways, FAT4 modulates multiple downstream targets. The PCP pathway
  involves Dchs, Vangl2, and Celsr1. The Hippo pathway affects YAP/TAZ, leading to
  the expression of CTGF, CYR61, and C4orf48, while inhibiting Notch. Additionally,
  FAT4 influences Wnt/β-catenin/PAK6 signaling (regulating PD-L1, EMT, Bmp4, Fgf18,
  SWI/SNF, APC, and CRMP2), PI3K/AKT/mTOR/GSK-3β/Ca²⁺ pathways, ROS/NF-κB/IκB-α, JAK-STAT,
  and RET-mediated GFRA1/GDNF signaling, ultimately regulating tumor metastasis and
  organogenesis
