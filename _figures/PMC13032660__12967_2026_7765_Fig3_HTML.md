caption: 'Neutrophil uses NET to phagocytize and kill bacteria, which is a fibrous
  network consist of several antimicrobial substances, such as histones, PR3, granule
  protein, cathepsin, MPO, NE and so on. NET can secret a few cytokines like VEGF
  and IL-8 to promote the progression of NPC while it has the ability to recruit immune
  cells like innate-like B cell, Tregs and MDSCs to make an immunosuppressive microenvironment.
  NET is also connected with CD8⁺T cell. For example, NET DNA inhibits NF-κB pathway
  via TMCO6, then the secretion of TGFβ1 is enhanced, which promotes NET generation
  and makes a positive feedback loop. NET can influence NPC cell proliferation via
  TLR9 pathway immediately as well.Abbreviations: NET, neutrophil extracellular traps;
  MDSCs, myeloid suppressor cells; Tregs, regulatory T cells; NE, neutrophil elastase,
  MPO, myeloperoxidase, PR3, protease 3'
