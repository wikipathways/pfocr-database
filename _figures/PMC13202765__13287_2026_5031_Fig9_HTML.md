caption: TGF-β/SMAD–mediated induction of NPSC quiescence enhances oxidative stress
  resilience and supports disc repair. Schematic representation of the TGF-β/SMAD
  signaling cascade regulating quiescence in NPSCs. Upon ligand binding, TGF-β receptor
  type I/II activation induces phosphorylation of Smad2/3, which complexes with Smad4
  and translocates into the nucleus to regulate downstream transcription. This pathway
  establishes a reversible quiescent state, metabolic down-shifting, and suppression
  of mitochondrial apoptotic signaling. Functionally, quiescent NPSCs exhibit stress
  adaptation, preservation of reparative competence, and improved long-term survival
  under degenerative disc conditions. This mechanism provides a controllable, non-genetic
  strategy to enhance stem cell resilience and supports the translational potential
  of quiescence-based interventions for intervertebral disc degeneration
