caption: 'Complement factor h (CFH) – apolipoprotein E (APOE) interaction and isoform-specific
  effects in retinal immunoregulation. APOE, secreted primarily by Müller glia, retinal
  ganglion cells, and the RPE, interacts with CFH to regulate complement activity.
  Isoform-specific binding determines complement control: APOE ε2 and APOE ε3 bind
  CFH strongly, providing effective inhibition of complement activation and maintaining
  immune homeostasis. In contrast, APOE e4 exhibits a weaker affinity, leading to
  complement activation, increased neuroinflammation, and microglial over-pruning
  via C1q/C3 tags. This dysregulated pathway contributes to synaptic loss and neurodegeneration
  in retinal aging and age-related macular degeneration. CFH: Complement factor h,
  APOE: Apolipoprotein E'
