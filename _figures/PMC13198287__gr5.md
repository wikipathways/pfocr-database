caption: Electrophysiology observations to distinguish 2-for-1 tachycardia from other
  mimics. A, Schematic shows the mechanism of 2-for-1 tachycardia in which a single
  atrial impulse conducts simultaneously via both fast and slow AV nodal pathways,
  producing 2 ventricular activations. B, His extrasystole occurring in a bigeminal
  pattern should be considered in the differential diagnosis. This schematic diagram
  shows that His extrasystole will manifest with a shorter HV interval and a change
  in His activation compared with the conducted QRS complex. In contrast, the HV interval
  and His activation will remain constant for each QRS complex in the presence of
  2-for-1 conduction (not shown). C, Programmed PACs delivered during tachycardia
  can help distinguish 2-for-1 tachycardia from junctional ectopy. This schematic
  shows that PAC delivered during 2-for-1 tachycardia may block in the fast and/or
  slow pathway, terminating the tachycardia. In contrast, junctional automaticity
  will not be reset by PACs (not shown). AV = atrioventricular; PAC = premature atrial
  contraction; SA = sinoatrial
