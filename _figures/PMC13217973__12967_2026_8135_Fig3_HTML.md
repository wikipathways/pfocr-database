caption: STAT3 regulates the stemness of liver cancer stem cells. (A) Liver cancer
  cells first produce large amounts of exosomes. Exosomes can mediate the joint suppression
  of p-STAT3 by p120-catenin and lnc-DILC, which activates the IL-6/JAK pathway. p-STAT3
  enters the nucleus to regulate gene transcription, ultimately promoting LCSC proliferation.
  (B) p-STAT3 induces TFCP2L1 to upregulate stemness markers such as CD133 and NANOG,
  thereby promoting LCSC proliferation. The small molecule Ti3 can inhibit this process.
