caption: 'Map of drought‐responsive soybean metabolites. Lime green lines indicate
  confirmed metabolic pathways in soybean. Red points indicate all drought‐responsive
  compounds identified by GC–MS. Insets show representative 2D image of abundance
  per genotype (S‐81, S; Virginia, V), treatment (phosphate sufficient, +; phosphate
  deficient, −), and time period (predrought, PD; drought, D), for select soybean
  metabolites. Colorbars on insets represent abundance from absent (black), low (blue)
  to high (yellow). Inset compounds are described in Table S5; full list of identified
  compounds is listed in Table S5. Connecting line colors indicate metabolite pathway
  function: Red—nucleotide metabolism; orange—amino acid metabolism; light pink—cofactor
  and vitamin biosynthesis; dark pink—other secondary metabolites; teal—lipid metabolism;
  blue—carbohydrate metabolism. Background map was generated using KEGG Mapper (Kanehisa
  et al. 2022)'
