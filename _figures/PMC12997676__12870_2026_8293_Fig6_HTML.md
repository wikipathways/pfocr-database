caption: 'Effects of silicon nanoparticles (SiNPs) on the flavonoid biosynthesis pathway
  in P70 pepper fruits during postharvest storage. This pathway was reconstructed
  based on the KEGG pathway database (ko00940, ko00941, and ko00944) and the detected
  metabolites. Yellow boxes indicate metabolites with significant changes. The heatmap
  displays the log₂ fold change (log₂FC) values of differential flavonoid metabolites
  in each comparison group (LT-NP vs. LT, LT-NP vs. RT, and LT-NP vs. RT-NP). Red
  indicates upregulated metabolites, while blue indicates downregulated metabolites.
  The treatments were as follows: room temperature (RT), RT + SiNPs (RT-NP), low temperature
  (LT), and LT + SiNPs (LT-NP). Each treatment included three independent biological
  replicates, and the same applies to the following figures'
