caption: 'MicroRNA mechanism of action and therapeutic implications in cancer. The
  upper panel illustrates the canonical biogenesis of miRNAs, from nuclear transcription
  and processing to cytoplasmic maturation and loading into the RNA-induced silencing
  complex (RISC), which guides post-transcriptional gene silencing by binding to target
  mRNA 3′-UTRs to induce degradation or translational inhibition. The lower panel
  highlights the therapeutic trade-off: while the pleiotropic ability of a single
  miRNA therapeutic to simultaneously inhibit multiple oncogenic pathways offers comprehensive
  anti-cancer efficacy and may overcome drug resistance, this broad targeting capacity
  also presents significant risks of off-target effects on healthy cellular pathways,
  necessitating rigorous validation to balance clinical benefit against potential
  adverse events'
