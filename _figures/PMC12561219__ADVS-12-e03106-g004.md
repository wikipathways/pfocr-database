caption: Schematic presentation of the role of USP45 in melanoma cells. MRGPRF is
  ubiquitinated by an uncharacterized E3 ligase, promoting its degradation via the
  proteasome. USP45 deubiquitinates and stabilizes MRGPRF, which in turn interferes
  with the formation of p110γ/p101 complex, thereby repressing the oncogenic PI3K/AKT
  pathway. This pathway inhibition leads to increased apoptosis and reduced proliferation,
  cell cycle progression, migration, and invasion of melanoma cells
