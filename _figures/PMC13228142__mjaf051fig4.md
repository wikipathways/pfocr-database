caption: The roles of PTMs in regulating tumor angiogenesis through the Notch signaling
  pathway. (A) The hypoxic tumor microenvironment alters glycosylation status. Glycosylated
  galectin-3 secretion downregulates DLL4 expression. Concurrently, this enhances
  the JAG1–Notch interaction, promoting angiogenesis. (B) In melanoma, FBXW7 mutation
  impedes Notch1 degradation via the phosphorylation–ubiquitination pathway. This
  results in sustained Notch signaling activation, driving tumor angiogenesis. (C)
  Conditioned medium from SPARC-overexpressing neuroblastoma (pSPARC-CM) inhibits
  Notch signaling molecule expression. It also suppresses transcription of Stat3 target
  genes phosphorylated at Tyr-705/Ser-727, inhibiting HMEC proliferation and inducing
  apoptosis. (D) TRIM28/UBC9 mediates SUMOylation of HEY1, promoting its dimerization
  and binding to E-box elements. This inhibits upstream target gene expression in
  the Notch and VEGFR/FGFR signaling pathways, ultimately suppressing tumor angiogenesis
