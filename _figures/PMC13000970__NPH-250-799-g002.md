caption: Model for membrane trafficking in the immune response to powdery mildew fungi.
  The papilla is made up of two layers, where the second extends into the encasement
  around the haustorium. The first and second layers are deposited by endosomal sorting
  complexes required for transport (ESCRT)‐independent and ESCRT‐dependent multivesicular
  body (MVB) pathways, respectively. Both layers contain extracellular vesicles (EVs)
  labelled by the syntaxin PEN1 in Arabidopsis and ROR2 in barley. In Arabidopsis,
  the second layer also contains SYP122‐labelled EVs. The ESCRT‐dependent pathway
  is in addition required for nucleotide‐binding leucine‐rich repeat (NLR)‐mediated
  hypersensitive reaction (HR) and targeted by Blumeria hordei effectors, CSEP0162
  and CSEP0214
