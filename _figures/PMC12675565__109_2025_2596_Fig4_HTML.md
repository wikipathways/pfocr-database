caption: 'LRG1 signaling pathways in fibrosis. Schematic overview of signaling pathways
  modulated by LRG1 in regulating fibrosis in a cell-specific manner. LRG1 promotes
  fibrosis through the TGF-β canonical pathway (TGF-β/Smad2/3) and non-canonical pathways,
  such as MAPK/p38, HIF-1α, and PI3K/Akt signaling. The ROCK1/MRTF-A/SRF pathway may
  also contribute to LRG1/TGF-β-mediated fibrosis. In addition to the TGF-β pathways,
  LRG1 has been shown to enhance fibrosis via the JAK/STAT3 pathway driven by IL-6.
  Additionally, Wnt/β-catenin signaling may also participate in LRG1-mediated fibrotic
  processes. Other potential receptors for LRG1''s fibrotic effects remain to be identified.
  Interestingly, LRG1 can also inhibit fibrosis through the TGF-β/Smad1/5/8 pathway,
  a mechanism it uses to induce pathogenic angiogenesis. TF: Transcription factor.
  LRP: Low-density lipoprotein receptor-related protein. FZD: Frizzled receptor. Created
