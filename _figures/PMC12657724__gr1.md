caption: Glycerol-3-phosphate pathway with structures of intermediates. Lipin is a
  magnesium dependent phosphatidic acid phosphatase (PAP) that catalyzes the penultimate
  step of the glycerol-3-phosphate pathway. This reaction is essential for generation
  of the primary energy storage molecule in eukaryotes, triacylglycerol, and major
  membrane phospholipids phosphatidylcholine, phosphatidylethanolamine and phosphatidylserine.
  Lipin preferentially hydrolyzes PA over other intermediates of this pathway, like
  lysophosphatidic acid (LPA). Glycerol-3-phosphate is acylated to generate LPA by
  glycerol phosphate acyltransferase (GPAT). LPA is further acylated to generate phosphatidic
  acid (PA) by acylglycerol phosphate acyltransferase (AGPAT). Lipin dephosphorylates
  PA to generate diacylglycerol (DAG). In the final step, diacylglycerol acyltransferase
  (DGAT), acylates DAG to generate TAG. DAG, diacylglycerol; PA, phosphatidic acid;
  PAP, phosphatidic acid phosphatase; LPA, lysophosphatidic acid
