caption: 'Circadian regulation of bone metabolism by gut microbiota-derived short-chain
  fatty acids (SCFAs) via GPR43 signaling. The gut microbiota ferments dietary cellulose
  to produce SCFAs (acetate, propionate, butyrate), which exhibit diurnal fluctuations
  in concentration. SCFAs activate the rhythmically expressed GPR43 (G protein-coupled
  receptor 43) in bone cells, peaking at midnight. This signaling cascade promotes
  osteogenesis by enhancing the Wnt/β-catenin pathway and suppressing NF-κB-mediated
  inflammation. Concurrently, SCFAs drive metabolic reprogramming in bone cells, balancing
  glycolysis and oxidative phosphorylation to favor bone mineralization over resorption.
  Ultimately, the gut microbiota–SCFA–GPR43 axis orchestrates bone homeostasis through
  integrated circadian, metabolic, and immune mechanisms. ⊥: Inhibits/decreases'
