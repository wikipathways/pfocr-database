caption: Pathogenesis of AIDP pathway. (1) Post-infectious foreign antigens with molecular
  mimicry are sensed by antigen presenting cells (e.g., macrophage). (2) Macrophage
  initiates Innate immune response by activating naive T-cell activation. (3) Pro-inflammatory
  markers are released. (4) Th1, Th17 cells along with autoantigens released by the
  B-cells released into the body infiltrate the nerve cells and disrupt the blood–nerve
  barrier and attract macrophages. (5) Activated Immune cells attack the myelin sheath
  proteins such as P0, PM22, P2 and strip Schwann-cell myelin via Fc and complement
  pathways leading to loss of myelin causing slowed nerve conduction and muscle weakness
