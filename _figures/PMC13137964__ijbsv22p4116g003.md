  in GDM exert multiple effects on the offspring metabolism. These exosomes enter
  the fetus through the placenta and then disrupt placental function via trophoblast
  cells, regulate fetal growth through umbilical vein endothelial cells, and modulate
  glucose and lipid metabolism by impacting the pancreas, adipose tissue, and liver.
