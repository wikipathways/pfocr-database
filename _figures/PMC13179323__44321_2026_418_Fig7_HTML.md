caption: CpfC is a key enzyme in the process by which S. aureus synthesizes heme using
  the coproporphyrin-dependent (CPD) pathway, and it is responsible for catalyzing
  the insertion of iron ions into coproporphyrin III (CPIII). SC-43 can inhibit the
  activity of CpfC and affect the heme synthesis of S. aureus. Since heme is a cofactor
  of various complexes in the bacterial electron transport chain, SC-43 may potentially
  inhibit ATP synthesis by affecting the electron transport chain. The Y12, H181,
  and E263 of CpfC are the key sites that determine its interaction with SC-43
