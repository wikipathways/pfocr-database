caption: Metabolic basis of neutrophil dysfunction and neutropenia in G6PC3 deficiency.
  Loss of G6PC3 function impairs dephosphorylation of G6P and 1,5-AG6P within the
  ER, despite normal activity of the G6PT1. Accumulation of 1,5-AG6P in the cytosol
  inhibits glycolysis and the oxPPP, thereby reducing NADPH levels and impairing NOX2-dependent
  ROS production. Impaired glycolytic flux also limits UDP-sugar generation, compromising
  protein glycosylation in the ER and promoting ER stress. This metabolic disruption
  limits energy supply and impairs neutrophil survival and functions, such as chemotaxis
  and NET formation, ultimately leading to neutropenia. Figure created with BioRender
  (BioRender.com). NOX2, NADPH oxidase complex 2; oxPPP, oxidative pentose phosphate
  pathway
