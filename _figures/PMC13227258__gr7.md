caption: CircRNAs regulate NF-κB signaling in cardiovascular diseases (By Biorender).
  CircRNA modulates the NF-κB signaling pathway via multiple regulatory mechanisms,
  influencing cardiovascular disease progression. TNF binds to its receptor, triggering
  TRAF2 ubiquitination and subsequent release of TAK1 and RIP. These activate the
  NEMO complex, leading to IκB phosphorylation and ubiquitination. Free NF-κB (p50/p65)
  translocates to the nucleus, regulating genes involved in apoptosis, angiogenesis,
  and proliferation in cardiovascular pathologies
