caption: The metabolic products of gut microbiota, SCFAs, promote tumour proliferation
  via the vagus nerve. Compared to the absence of gut microbiota influence, SCFAs
  secreted by the microbiota activate the FFAR2/3 receptors on intestinal endocrine
  cells, promote 5‐HT release, stimulate the vagus nerve through 5‐HT3R, regulate
  Treg and NK cells, and simultaneously enhance ACh release. ACh further activates
  nicotinic cholinergic receptors (nAChR) and muscarinic cholinergic receptors (mAChRs)
  on tumour cells, with mAChRs activating the PI3K‐AKT and Wnt signalling pathways,
  while nAChR regulates Treg cells and promotes macrophage polarisation to the M2
  phenotype, thereby enhancing tumour proliferation. Created with BioGDP.com (Jiang,
