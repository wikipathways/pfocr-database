caption: 'IQGAP3-mediated signalings governing cell proliferation, EMT, and DNA repair.
  Illustration of the diverse molecular interactions through which IQGAP3 modulates
  key signaling pathways involved in cancer progression, genomic stability, EMT, and
  DNA Repair. a RAS/RAF/MEK/ERK pathway: IQGAP3 binds to RAS-GTP and facilitates RAF
  and MEK phosphorylation, leading to ERK activation. Activated ERK promotes G1/S
  phase transition and cell proliferation. b DNA damage response: IQGAP3 interacts
  with Rad17 and activates the ATM/ATR-Chk2/Chk1 signaling, promoting efficient DNA
  repair via homologous recombination and non-homologous end joining, thus contributing
  to radio-resistance. c PI3K-AKT signaling axis: In the presence of IQGAP3, the interaction
  between PKCα and PKCδ is inhibited, resulting in phosphorylation of PKCα and activation
  of PI3K, leading to PIP3 generation and AKT activation, while inhibiting PTEN. IQGAP3
  depletion disrupts this coordination, reducing AKT phosphorylation and suppressing
  proliferation. d TGF-β/SMAD signaling: IQGAP3 facilitates Smad2/3 phosphorylation
  and nuclear translocation even in the absence of TGF-β ligand, promoting Smad-dependent
  gene transcription. (Created in https://BioRender.com)'
