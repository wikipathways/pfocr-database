caption: 'The impact and mechanisms of lactylation in various cancers. Lactylation
  modification regulates gene expression and protein function through various signaling
  pathways, thereby promoting tumor progression in multiple cancer types. This figure
  illustrates the role of lactylation in different tumors, including (A) ocular melanoma,
  (B) hepatocellular carcinoma, (C) gastric cancer, (D) colorectal cancer, (E) clear
  cell renal cell carcinoma, (F) bladder carcinoma, (G) anaplastic thyroid cancer,
  (H) prostate cancer, (I) lung carcinoma, (J) breast cancer, and (K) endometrial
  carcinoma/cervical carcinoma. In each of these cancers, lactylation contributes
  to malignancy through distinct molecular mechanisms. Arrows indicate activation;
  bar‐headed lines indicate inhibition; up/down arrows indicate molecular upregulation
  or downregulation. Abbreviations: ABC, ATP‐binding cassette; ABCF1, ATP binding
  cassette subfamily F member 1; AKT, protein kinase B; ATF5, activating transcription
