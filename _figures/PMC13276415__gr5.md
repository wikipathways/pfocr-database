caption: Effects of HMGB1-mediated RAGE and TLR4 pathways on osteoblasts and osteoclasts.
  Extracellular HMGB1 binds to RAGE and has pro-inflammatory cytokine properties,
  activating the downstream signaling pathway NF-κB and promoting the production of
  inflammatory factors IL-6, IL-1β, and TNF-α. Inflammatory factors further amplify
  HMGB1 expression, forming a loop that promotes osteoclast differentiation and maturation.
  Extracellular HMGB1 binds to TLR4, activating the downstream signaling pathway NF-κB
  to act on osteoblasts. Through RANK/RANKL, NF-κB expression is further promoted.
  (RAGE, receptor for AGEs; HMGB-1, high-mobility group protein B1; TLR-4, Toll-like
  receptor 4; OPG, osteoprotegerin)
