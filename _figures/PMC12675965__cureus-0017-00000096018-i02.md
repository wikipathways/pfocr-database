caption: 'Beyond protein synthesis, mTORC1 regulates lipid and nucleotide biosynthesis
  to support cell growth and proliferation. By activating SREBP1 and SREBP2, mTORC1
  promotes fatty acid synthesis in the liver and cholesterol synthesis through LDL
  receptor upregulation. PPARγ further enhances lipid uptake and adipogenesis. In
  parallel, SREBP activation stimulates the pentose phosphate pathway, generating
  NADPH for lipid and nucleotide production. mTORC1 also facilitates nucleotide synthesis
  by activating ATF4 and MTHFD2 for purine biosynthesis and by upregulating CAD, a
  key enzyme complex for pyrimidine biosynthesis.Image credits: Figure created by
