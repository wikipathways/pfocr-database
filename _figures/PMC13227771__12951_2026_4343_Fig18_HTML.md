caption: 'Schematic illustration of CoF2 NN preparation and their mechanism of action
  in catalytic metalloimmunotherapy. The CoF2 NNs were prepared via high-temperature
  thermal decomposition and functionalized with DSPE-PEG5000. The therapeutic mechanism
  operates through a dual action: firstly, the NNs act as an intrinsic pyroptosis
  inducer by activating the caspase-1/GSDMD pathway and causing mitochondrial damage,
  the latter of which releases mtDNA to initiate the cGAS-STING pathway. Secondly,
  the CoF2 NNs-generated ROS under H2O2 and ultrasound irradiation further amplify
  both pyroptosis and cGAS-STING activation. This coordinated cascade stimulates a
  potent immune response that, when combined with αPD-1, suppresses distal tumor growth
  and validates the strategy of catalytic metalloimmunotherapy'
