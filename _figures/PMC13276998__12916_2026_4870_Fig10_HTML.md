caption: Schematically summarizes the neuro-modulatory mechanisms influencing breast
  cancer progression. The upper section illustrates how norepinephrine (NE) from sympathetic
  nerves activates the β2-adrenergic receptor (β2-AR) on cancer cells, promoting proliferation,
  EMT, and angiogenesis while also suppressing CD8⁺ T cells function and recruiting
  immunosuppressive cells; this pathway can be inhibited by β-blockers like propranolol.
  The lower section depicts how neurotrophic factors (NGF, BDNF) from Schwann cells
  activate Trk receptors on cancer cells, triggering PI3K/AKT and MAPK/ERK signaling
  to drive tumor cell proliferation, thereby highlighting the role of the nerve-tumor
  interaction axis in breast cancer development
