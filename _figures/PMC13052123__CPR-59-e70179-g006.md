caption: The central role of the hypoxia‐inducible factor (HIF) pathway in oxygen
  homeostasis. Under hypoxic conditions, the HIF‐α subunit is stabilised and dimerizes
  with HIF‐1β to form the active transcription factor. The primary HIF isoforms, HIF‐1
  and HIF‐2, regulate distinct sets of target genes to coordinate cellular adaptation,
  including metabolic reprogramming and erythropoiesis. This response is critical
  for survival in low‐oxygen environments but can also contribute to pathological
  processes of HAPH
