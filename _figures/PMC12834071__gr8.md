caption: Graphical illustration of oxycodone pretreatment-mediated suppression of
  mitophagy in LPS-induced ALI or cell damage by regulating the HO-1 pathway. Oxycodone
  activated the HO-1 pathway when LPS was administered, leading to the response to
  induce target gene of HO-1 expression. Additionally, pretreatment with oxycodone
  downregulated the expressions of mitophagy markers PINK1, Parkin, and LC3Ⅱ/Ⅰ. In
  conclusion, oxycodone pre-treatment alleviates mitophagy in endotoxin-related ALI
  and cell damage, thus alleviating the inflammation and oxidative stress of lung
  tissue and cells, ultimately reducing cell death
