caption: Mechanistic synergy between TLS‐inducing factors and immune checkpoint therapy.
  Strategies that promote the formation of HEVs in tumours can drive the development
  of mTLS, thereby improving immune cell infiltration and antitumour responses. Multiple
  pathways – including IL‐33–ILC2–LTβR, LIGHT cytokine delivery, STING activation,
  and antiangiogenic therapy combined with immune checkpoint inhibitors – have been
  shown to induce HEV formation. These HEVs recruit naïve lymphocytes into the tumour
  microenvironment and organise local immune structures, facilitating antigen presentation
  and sustained cytotoxic T cell activity. Therapeutic induction of HEV and mTLS thus
  reprograms the tumour microenvironment into an immune‐reactive niche, enhancing
  the efficacy of PD‐1/PD‐L1 blockade and other immunotherapies. HEVs, high endothelial
  venules; ILC2, group 2 innate lymphoid cells; STING, stimulator of interferon genes
