caption: 'The antitumor mechanisms of iNKT cells. a. Direct cytotoxicity: TCRs recognize
  lipid antigens presented by CD1d to activate iNKT cells, which then exert direct
  cytotoxic effects through exocytosis of cytotoxic granules or via FasL/FasLR pathways.
  b. Indirect cytotoxicity: DCs activate iNKT cells via CD1d-TCR interaction. Activated
  iNKT cells rapidly upregulate CD40L expression, binding to CD40 on DC surfaces while
  massively secreting IFN-γ. This feedback signal from iNKT cells promotes DC maturation.
  Mature DCs secrete large amounts of IL-12, which strongly stimulates iNKT cell proliferation
  and increased IFN-γ production. This elevated IFN-γ further activates DCs, creating
  a positive feedback loop. The IFN-γ and IL-12 produced by iNKT cells and DCs powerfully
  activate multiple cell types–including NK cells, CD8+ T cells, and M1 macrophages–to
  drive antitumor responses. c. Regulatory function: iNKT cells can kill or suppress
  the function of immunosuppressive cells such as TAMs, MDSCs, and Treg T cells, thereby
  remodeling the TME, and following interaction with B cells, iNKT cells enable B
  cells to exert immunoregulatory functions. Image created in BioRender'
