caption: 'The source and regulators of short‐chain acyl‐PTMs, including “Writers”,
  “Erasers” and “Readers”. Short‐chain acyl‐PTMs originate from various metabolic
  pathways (text in purple font color), including glycolysis, TCA cycle, amino acid
  catabolism, fatty acid metabolism, ketogenesis pathway, etc. Lactylation primarily
  originates from the glycolysis; butyrylation mainly derives from the fatty acid
  metabolism pathway; crotonylation arises from amino acid catabolism and fatty acid
  metabolism pathways; glutarylation is predominantly sourced from the amino acid
  catabolism pathway; β‐hydroxybutyrylation stems from amino acid catabolism and ketogenesis
  pathways; 2‐hydroxyisobutyrylation is primarily derived from amino acid catabolism
  and fatty acid metabolism pathways; malonylation mainly comes from the fatty acid
  metabolism pathway; succinylation is primarily generated via the TCA cycle (ref.
  [38]). In addition, short‐chain acyl‐PTMs are added by “Writers” to specific amino
  acid sites; “Erasers” removes these acylation modifications; and “Readers” recognizes
  PTMs on proteins. Abbreviations: AARS1/2, alanyl‐tRNA synthetase 1/2; ACAT2, acetyl‐CoA
  acetyltransferase 2; Bhb, β‐hydroxybutyryl group; BRD9, bromodomain‐containing protein
  9; Brg1, Brahma‐related gene 1; Bu, butyryl group; CBP, CREB‐binding protein; CDYL1,
  chromodomain Y‐like 1; CECR2, chromosome region candidate 2; CPT1A, carnitine palmitoyltransferase
  1A; Cr, crotonyl group; DPF, double PHD zinc finger; DLAT, dihydrolipoamide S‐acetyltransferase;
  ENL, eleven‐nineteen‐leukemia; GAS41, glioma‐amplified sequence 41; GCN5, general
  control non‐depressible 5; Glu, glutaryl group; HAT1, histone acetyltransferase
  1; HBO1, histone acetyltransferase binding to ORC1; HDAC, histone deacetylase; Hib,
  2‐hydroxyisobutyryl group; KAT8, lysine acetyltransferase 8; La, lactyl group; Ma,
  malonyl group; MOF, males absent on the first; MYST, Moz, Ybf2/Sas3, Sas2, Tip60;
  OXCT1, 3‐oxoacid CoA‐transferase; p300, E1A binding protein P300; PCAF, p300/CBP‐associated
  factor; PMAT1, phenolic glucoside malonyl‐transferase 1; SIRT, sirtuin; Suc, succinyl
  group; TAF1, TATA‐box binding protein associated factor 1; TCA, tricarboxylic acid
  cycle; TIP60, Tat‐interacting protein of 60 kDa; YEATS, Yaf9, ENL, AF9, Taf14, Sas5'
