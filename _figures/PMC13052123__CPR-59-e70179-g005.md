caption: Multilayer epigenetic mechanisms driving vascular remodelling in HAPH. Epigenetic
  dysregulation, through histone modifications and non‐coding RNA networks, is a key
  driver of HAPH. These alterations transcriptionally suppress key genes like BMPR2
  and post‐transcriptionally modulate pathways critical for vascular cell proliferation
  and inflammation. Hypoxia stabilises this maladaptive state by reinforcing a pro‐remodelling
  epigenetic landscape, often centred on enhanced HIF‐1α signalling. The dynamic nature
  of these changes highlights their potential as treatment targets
