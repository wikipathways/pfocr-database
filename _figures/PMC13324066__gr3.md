caption: Ginsenosides ameliorate hepatic fibrosis through induction of HSC death.
  (1) Ginsenosides phosphorylate LKB1 and thus activate the LKB1-AMPK signaling pathway,
  inducing HSC apoptosis. (2) Ginsenosides bind with HK2 to prevent its degradation
  by serine proteases (SP), promoting ACSL4 expression. Elevated ACSL4 increases LPCAT3
  levels, which then upregulate ALOX5, ultimately triggering HSC ferroptosis. Ginsenosides
  also upregulate miR-6945-3p which suppresses DNMT3B expression, consequently inhibiting
  ACSL4 methylation that facilitates ACSL4 ubiquitination and degradation, and thus
  inducing HSC ferroptosis via upregulating LPCAT3 and ALOX5. Ginsenosides increase
  BECN1 expression which inhibits SLC7A11 activity through direct interaction, therefore
  leading to HSC ferroptosis. Furthermore, ginsenosides elevate IRF1 recruitment to
  the SLC7A11 promoter, suppressing SLC7A11 transcription and thus initiating HSC
  ferroptosis
