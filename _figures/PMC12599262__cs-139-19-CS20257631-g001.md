caption: IPs are synthesized via a lipid-dependent pathway from membrane-localized
  PI(4,5)P₂, which is hydrolysed by phospholipase C (PLC) to Ins(1,4,5)P₃. It can
  be sequentially dephosphorylated to myo-inositol or phosphorylated to generate higher-order
  IPs, including InsP₆ (i.e., IP6 or phytic acid) and inositol pyrophosphates. In
  parallel, myo-inositol can also be synthesized de novo from glucose-6-phosphate
  (G6P) via Ins(3)P. 1,5-InsP₈, 1,5-bisdiphosphoinositol tetrakisphosphate; 1-InsP₇,
