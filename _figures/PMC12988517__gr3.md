caption: 'Tissue- and Stage-Specific Coupling of cGAS-STING, Pyroptosis, and mtDNA
  in OA (This schematic summarizes the proposed “multilevel coupling” hypothesis in
  OA: Joint tissues (cartilage, synovium, subchondral bone) exhibit distinct but interconnected
  patterns of mtDNA leakage, cGAS-STING pathway activation, and pyroptosis during
  OA progression. Mechanical overload, aging, and oxidative stress initially induce
  mitochondrial damage and mtDNA release in cartilage, triggering cGAS-STING signaling,
  chondrocyte pyroptosis, and production of inflammatory cytokines and matrix-degrading
  enzymes. These mediators diffuse to the synovium, where hypoxic and inflammatory
  conditions further amplify cGAS-STING and inflammasome activation, leading to synovial
  fibroblast activation, macrophage pyroptosis, persistent synovitis, and immune cell
  infiltration. In later stages, subchondral bone is affected by chronic cartilage
  injury and synovitis, resulting in metabolic stress, activation of cGAS-STING and
  pyroptosis in osteoblasts/osteoclasts, and abnormal bone remodeling. Crosstalk among
  compartments is facilitated by extracellular vesicles, DNA fragments, and inflammatory
  mediators, forming a joint-wide, self-amplifying inflammatory network that drives
  disease propagation and chronicity). Agreement number：GZ28TUMD5G；WN28TUMAO0'
