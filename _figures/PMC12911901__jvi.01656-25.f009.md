caption: Proposed model for SVV 3C proteinase (3Cpro) targeting Nrf2/HO-1 to facilitate
  viral replication. SVV infection elicited cellular oxidative stress through the
  induction of reactive oxygen species (ROS) production, glutathione depletion, and
  the suppression of the nuclear factor erythroid 2-related factor 2 (Nrf2)/heme oxygenase-1
  (HO-1) pathway. Mechanistically, the viral 3Cpro targets the Nrf2/HO-1 axis for
  degradation via caspase pathway, thereby promoting viral replication. The overexpression
  of Nrf2/HO-1 exerted antiviral activity against SVV, whereas inhibition of this
  pathway significantly enhanced viral proliferation. HO-1 metabolic products carbon
  monoxide (CO) and biliverdin (BV) exhibited inhibition of SVV replication. Additionally,
  HO-1 promotes IFN-I response and ISG expressions, which contribute to its antiviral
  mechanism
