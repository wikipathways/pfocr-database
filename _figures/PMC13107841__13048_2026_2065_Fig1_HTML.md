caption: 'Drug-specific resistance mechanisms in OC. A Mechanisms of platinum resistance
  include: transport protein(①SLC31A1 (SLC family) reduces intracellular DDP levels,
  ② ABCB1, ABCC1, and ABCG2 promote DDP efflux). ③GSTP1 promotes the metabolism of
  DDP and CBP. ④ERK activation leads to excessive activation of autophagy. ⑤Dysregulation
  of glucose metabolism, lipid metabolism (OCCC), and glutamine metabolism (endometrioid)
  affects drug resistance.⑥Resistance to cisplatin in OC is associated with up-regulation
  of miRNA21, miRNA-93, lnc RNA EBIC, and lnc RNA PVT1. ⑦Upregulation of PI3K/mTOR
  axis and inactivation of p53 allow cancer cells to evade apoptosis. B Mechanisms
  of taxane include: ①Increased expression levels of ABCB1 and ABCC1 and increased
  P-gp activity accelerate the efflux of PTX from cancer cells. ②Overexpression of
  CYP1B1 (cytochromeP450 enzyme, involved in drug metabolism) is associated with DTX
  resistance. ③Activation of the PI3K/Akt pathway promotes tumor growth, invasion
  and migration. ④Highly expressed CLU prevents the interaction of PTX with microtubules
  by binding to PTX. ⑤Cancer cells can activate autophagy pathway to promote resistance.
  C The mechanisms of resistance to anti-angiogenics mainly include: ①pharmacodynamic
  tolerance (drug tolerance was caused by the increased expression of VEGF and VEGF
  receptors), ②induction of hypoxia, ③tachyphylaxis (an acute decrease in the response
  to Bevacizuma after its administration), ④alteration of the neovascular architecture
  (remodeled neovascular architecture resulted in resistance to available antiangiogenic
  agents), ⑤redundant angiogenic factors (in addition to VEGF, many pro-angiogenic
  factors can also promote angiogenesis, such as FGF, PDGF), and ⑥increased perivascular
  cell coverage (perivascular cell coverage increases after bevacizumab treatment,
  affecting the intratumoral distribution of the drug). D The mechanisms of resistance
  to anti-angiogenics mainly include: Upregulation of ABCC1 promotes P-gp activity
  and promotes PARPi efflux. ②CSC enhanced ③HRR efficiency. ④Enhanced replication
  fork stability. ⑤Reduced PARP1 trapping limits the cytotoxicity of PARPi.⑥Deletion
  of PARG inhibits the capture of PARP1 on DNA by partially restoring PARylation.
  ⑦Activation of the ATR/CHK1 signaling pathway leads to global inhibition of cell
  cycle progression and local restoration of stalled replication forks. E ①Low PD-L1
  expression level, ②low MSI-H, ③abnormal cGAS/STING signaling pathway, and ④heavy
  tumor burden lead to the resistance of OC to ICIs'
