caption: SIRT5 desuccinylates HADHA, enhances its enzymatic activity, and promotes
  fatty acid oxidation (FAO). Inhibition of SIRT5 disrupts mitochondrial structure,
  increases mitochondrial reactive oxygen species (ROS), and decreases intracellular
  glutathione (GSH) and ATP levels, impairing FAO. Venetoclax induces ROS accumulation
  and reduces GSH and ATP levels in AML cells, leading to cell death, while residual
  cells exhibit increased reliance on the FAO pathway. When combined, the two agents
  act together to impair mitochondrial integrity, suppresses ATP production, accelerates
  GSH depletion, exacerbates oxidative stress, promotes MCL-1 degradation, and enhances
  cell death
