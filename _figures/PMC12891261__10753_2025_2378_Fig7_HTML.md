caption: Mechanism diagram of Cav2 regulating MHS cells polarization, apoptosis and
  inflammation. In the context of sepsis-induced ALI, there is an observed upregulation
  of Cav2 expression, which subsequently activates the Hippo signaling pathway. This
  activation results in an increased presence of p-YAP/TAZ proteins, leading to their
  retention within the cytoplasm and a consequent decrease in their translocation
  to the nucleus. As a result, this mechanism facilitates the M1 polarization of alveolar
  macrophages, thereby exacerbating cellular apoptosis, oxidative stress, and inflammatory
  responses. In contrast, the knockdown of Cav2 expression inhibits the Hippo pathway,
  reduces levels of p-YAP/TAZ, and enhances the nuclear translocation of these proteins.
  This alteration promotes the M2 polarization of alveolar macrophages, thereby attenuating
  cellular apoptosis, oxidative stress, and inflammatory responses (The mechanism
  diagram was created using the Pathway Builder Tool)
