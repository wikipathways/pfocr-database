caption: Dual calcium–calci-sensitization pathways in asthmatic airway smooth-muscle
  hyperresponsiveness. Inflammatory mediators trigger the GPCR → RhoA-ROCK axis; ROCK
  phosphorylates MYPT1, inhibits MLCP and increases Ca2⁺ sensitivity. Concurrently,
  PLC-IP₃-mediated intracellular Ca2⁺ release and mechanically induced extracellular
  Ca2⁺ influx (detailed in Fig. 1B) elevate [Ca2⁺]i; Ca2⁺/CaM activates MLCK, promoting
  myosin phosphorylation. The two arms synergistically enhance sustained Actin–Myosin
  cross-bridge cycling, leading to prolonged ASM contraction and airway hyperresponsiveness
