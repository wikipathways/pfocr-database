caption: 'TMAO has been implicated in multiple chronic inflammatory conditions, including
  cardiovascular disease (CVD); metabolic disorders such as diabetes and obesity;
  neurological diseases such as Alzheimer’s disease (AD) and Parkinson’s disease (PD);
  chronic kidney disease (CKD); and cancers such as pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma
  (PDAC), triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC), and colorectal cancer (CRC). TMAO
  increases the risk of CVD, CKD, AD/PD, diabetes, obesity, and CRC, while exhibiting
  disease-limiting effects in PDAC and TNBC. Mechanistically, TMAO influences vascular
  inflammation and foam cell formation in atherosclerosis and CVD. It modulates glucose
  tolerance and insulin sensitivity in diabetes and suppresses reverse cholesterol
  transport and bile acid synthesis in obesity. In CKD, TMAO activates the NLRP3 inflammasome,
  leading to renal inflammation. In AD/PD, it promotes neuroinflammation, amyloid-β
  aggregation, and plaque formation. In CRC, TMAO supports proliferation, angiogenesis,
  and disease progression. In PDAC, it polarizes tumor-associated macrophages (TAMs)
  to a proinflammatory phenotype via activation of type I interferon signaling, rendering
  PDAC responsive to anti-PD1 immunotherapy. In TNBC, TMAO exhibits antitumor effects
  by inducing tumor cell pyroptosis through the PERK pathway and enhancing tumor responsiveness
  to immunotherapy. PERK: protein kinase RNA-like endoplasmic reticulum kinase; NLRP3:
  NOD like receptor family pyrin domain containing 3'
