caption: 'the role network of GRK2 in TME [7, 53, 57]: On one hand, GRK2 interacts
  with PD-L1 and IL-10/TGF-β to directly inhibit the T-cell receptor (TCR) signaling
  cascade, and simultaneously interferes with the TCR-mediated activation of downstream
  CARMA1/BCL10/MALT1 molecules and NF-κB activation, affecting cytokine production;
  on the other hand, after the chemokine CXCL12 binds to its receptor CXCR4, it triggers
  the phosphorylation of GRK2 at tyrosine residues with the promotion of Src and Zap-70
  molecules, and the phosphorylated GRK2 is further phosphorylated at the Ser-339
  site to promote TCR-CXCR4 transactivation; in addition, GRK2 is involved in the
  PDGF-B signaling pathway, acting on the PDGF-B receptor (R). After PDGF-B binds
  to the receptor, it not only regulates the function of cancer-associated fibroblasts
  (CAFs) but also activates PREX1, which indirectly participates in the regulation
  of cytokine expression in the TME by promoting the stabilization of IL-2/IL-10 mRNA'
