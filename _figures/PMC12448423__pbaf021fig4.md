caption: Major signaling pathways implicated in CCA pathogenesis. The Hippo pathway
  (WWC2–MST1/2–LATS1/2–YAP/TAZ) modulates proliferation and chemoresistance via TEAD-
  and FGFR-mediated transcription. TGF-β signaling activates SMAD2/3–SMAD4 and non-canonical
  p38/JNK branches to regulate transcriptional programs. ERBB2, MET, and EGFR drive
  RAS–RAF–MEK–ERK and PI3K–AKT–mTOR pathways, promoting growth and survival. Wnt signaling
  inhibits GSK3β, leading to β-catenin activation of TCF/LEF target genes. Notch activation
  produces Notch intracellular domain (Notch-IC), which regulates nuclear transcription.
  Interleukin-6/JAK1–STAT3 signaling links inflammation to oncogenic transcription,
  while Hedgehog/SMO–GLI promotes GLI-dependent gene expression. Crosstalk among these
  cascades supports tumor progression and therapy resistance
