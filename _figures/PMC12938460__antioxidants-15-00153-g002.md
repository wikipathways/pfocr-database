  and downstream signaling under normoxic and hypoxic conditions. Under normoxia,
  HIF-1α undergoes hydroxylation and proteasomal degradation via the pVHL pathway,
  whereas hypoxia stabilizes HIF-1α, enabling its nuclear translocation and transcriptional
  activation of target genes involved in angiogenesis, metabolism, cell survival,
  and mtROS production. Calcium overload and BNIP3 induce mitochondrial dysfunction,
  leading to apoptosis and cell death
