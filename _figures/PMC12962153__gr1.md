caption: A comparison of prokaryotic protoporphyrin-dependent (PPD) and coproporphyrin-dependent
  (CPD) heme biosynthesis pathways. Plants and most bacteria initiate heme synthesis
  using the C5 pathway, which makes aminolevulinic acid (ALA) reductively from glutamyl-tRNA.
  ALA is subsequently condensed, dehydrated, and decarboxylated to form coproporphyrinogen
  III (Copro’gen). The PPD pathway is used by Gram-negative bacteria and eukaryotes
  to make heme from coproporphyrinogen. The CPD pathway is only found in Bacillota
  and Actinomycetota, including mycobacteria. The figure is adapted from Ref. (4)
