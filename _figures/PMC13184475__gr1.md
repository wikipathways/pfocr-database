caption: 'The PINK1/Parkin and non-PINK1/Parkin pathways in mitophagy. Under ROS stimulation,
  mitochondria are damaged, leading to mitophagy activation. In the PINK1/Parkin pathway,
  PINK1 accumulates on the outer mitochondrial membrane, recruiting and activating
  Parkin. Parkin ubiquitinates damaged mitochondrial proteins, facilitating the recruitment
  of autophagy receptors such as SQSTM1 and OPTN, and promoting autophagosome formation
  through LC3. In the non-PINK1/Parkin pathways: ①Autophagy receptors on the outer
  mitochondrial membrane, such as NIX, BNIP3, and FUN14 domain-containing protein
  1, directly bind to LC3, mediating mitophagy; ② PINK1 directly recruits OPTN and
  NDP52 to mitochondria, triggering autophagy and autophagosome membrane expansion
  through ULK1, DFCP1, and WIPI; ③ Other E3 protein ligases generate ubiquitin chains
  on the outer mitochondrial membrane, thereby recruiting autophagy receptors and
  triggering mitochondrial autophagy. Finally, autophagosomes fuse with lysosomes,
  where the mitochondria are degraded.ROS: reactive oxygen species; PINK1: phosphatase
  and tensin homolog-induced kinase 1; Parkin: E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase parkin;
  NIX: NIP3-like protein X; BNIP3: BCL2/adenovirus E1B 19-kDa protein-interacting
  protein 3; FUNDC1: FUN14 domain-containing protein 1; OPTN: optineurin; NDP52: nuclear
  dot protein 52 kDa; p62: sequestosome 1 (SQSTM1); Ub: Ubiquitin; Gp78: glycoprotein
  78; SIAH1: seven in absentia homolog 1; SMURF1: SMAD ubiquitination regulatory factor
  1; ARIH1: Ariadne RING finger protein 1; ULK1: unc-51-like kinase 1; WIPI: WD repeat
  domain phosphoinositide-interacting protein; DFCP1: double FYVE domain-containing
  protein 1; LC3: microtubule-associated protein 1A/1B-light chain 3'
