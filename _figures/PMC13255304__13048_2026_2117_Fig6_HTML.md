caption: 'This diagram summarizes three mainstream therapeutic approaches for PCOS:
  traditional Chinese medicine (TCM) treatment, pharmacological treatment, and stem
  cell and exosome therapy. The inner ring lists representative agents for each category,
  while the outer ring delineates the core regulatory pathways, upstream pathological
  targets, and downstream therapeutic effects of each strategy in PCOS. Traditional
  Chinese medicine interventions target multiple core pathological mechanisms to reduce
  testosterone synthesis, improve islet function, and suppress inflammatory activation
  and aberrant angiogenesis. Pharmacological treatments alleviate hyperandrogenism
  and insulin resistance, while modulating gut microbiota homeostasis to exert anti-inflammatory
  and anti-fibrotic effects. Stem cell and exosome-based therapies correct abnormal
  ovarian folliculogenesis by regulating the ovarian microenvironment, and simultaneously
  ameliorate chronic inflammation and ovarian fibrosis. All three strategies reverse
  the core pathological damage of PCOS via multi-target regulatory networks. Abbreviations:
  PCOS Polycystic Ovary Syndrome, TCM Traditional Chinese Medicine, COCPs Combined
  Oral Contraceptive Pills, QUR Quercetin, CFDT Cangfu Daotan Decoction, BBR Berberine,
  MSC Mesenchymal Stem Cell, BM-MSC Bone Marrow-derived Mesenchymal Stem Cell, UC-MSC
  Umbilical Cord-derived Mesenchymal Stem Cell, MUC-MSC Mucosal Umbilical Cord-derived
  Mesenchymal Stem Cell, exos Exosomes, apoVs Apoptotic Vesicles.)'
