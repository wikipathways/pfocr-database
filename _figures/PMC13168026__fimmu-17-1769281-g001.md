caption: Adenosinergic immune network in the BM niche. Extracellular nucleotides (ATP,
  NAD+, cGAMP) are metabolized by ectonucleotidases including CD38, CD39, CD203a,
  and CD73 (41). The CD38/CD203a/CD73 axis forms an adenosinergic pathway sequentially
  converting NAD+ to ADPR, AMP, and ADO, respectively, bypassing ATP- and cGAMP-dependent
  routes (27, 40). The NAD+-dependent pathway is highly active in the hypoxic and
  acidic BM niche, favoring ADO accumulation. ADO activates A2A/A2B receptors, triggering
  cAMP-mediated immunosuppression. INO, derived from ADO or IMP via ADA, has context-dependent
  immune effects (40, 42)
