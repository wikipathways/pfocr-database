caption: Mechanisms of anoikis in breast cancer. This schematic integrates membrane,
  cytoplasmic, and mitochondrial cues that shape apoptotic fate. Integrin–ECM engagement
  (e.g., COLXIII, CEACAM6, SEMA7A) activates FAK/Src/ILK → PI3K–PDK1–AKT and AMPK
  pathways, promoting survival; modulators (PTP1B, PFKFB4, PARVB, DOC-2) tune these
  signals. Cadherin/α-/β-catenin complexes link adhesion to intracellular control.
  RTKs (EGFR/ERBB2) and upstream nodes (RTK/BRAF/PRKCQ/PEA15) drive RAS–RAF–MEK–ERK,
  which post-transcriptionally regulates BH3-only proteins (BIM, BMF, PUMA) with additional
  input from Cav-1/Bit1, TDAG51, TrkB/NTF3, and chemokine receptors (CCR7/CXCR4).
  EGFR/integrin cues also engage TRAF2–IKK–NF-κB, elevating c-FLIP and extrinsic apoptosis
  resistance. p53 and hypoxia/HIF-1α reshape this landscape by inducing pro-apoptotic
  genes (PUMA, NOXA, BMF). Convergence occurs at the mitochondria, where the balance
  between anti-apoptotic Bcl-2/Bcl-xL and pro-apoptotic Bax/BH3-only proteins dictates
  MOMP and apoptosis resistance
