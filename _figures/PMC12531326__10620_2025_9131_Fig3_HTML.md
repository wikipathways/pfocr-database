caption: 'MicroRNAs involved in the regulation of TLR4 signaling pathway in intestinal
  epithelium. Blue arrows indicate stimulation, red arrows indicate inhibition, and
  dashed red arrows represent the removal of inhibition LPS: Lipopolysaccharide, TLR4:
  Toll-like receptor-4, TRAP: TNF Receptor-associated protein, MyD88: Myeloid differentiation
  primary response gene 88, IRAK: Interleukin:1 receptor-associated kinase, TRAF:
  TNF Receptor-associated factor, TAK1: Transforming growth factor beta-activated
  kinase-1, TAB: TAK1 binding protein, MAPK: Mitogen-activated protein kinase, NEMO:
  NF:κB essential modulator, IKKα: IκB kinase alpha, JNK: c:Jun N-terminal kinase,
  P38: p38 mitogen-activated protein kinase, AP-1: Activator protein-1, IRF3: Interferon
  regulatory factor-3, IKKα: IκB kinase alpha, TBK1: TANK-binding kinase-1, TRIF:
  TIR-domain-containing adapter-inducing Interferon-β, TRAM: TRIF-related adapter
  molecule, NF-κB: Nuclear factor kappa-light-chain enhancer of activated B cells,
  MD2: Myeloid differentiation factor -2. TNFAIP3: Tumor necrosis factor alpha-induced
  protein 3. Various miRNAs regulate the TLR4/NF-κB signaling pathway in intestinal
  epithelial cells, impacting inflammatory responses and epithelial barrier function.
  hsa-miR-375 inhibits TLR4 expression and NF-κB activation. MiR-155 targets SOCS1
  and SHIP1, promoting TLR4/myD88 pathway activation and enhancing inflammation. miR-23a
  targets TNFAIP3, a negative regulator of the TLR4/NF-κB pathway, leading to increased
  NF-κB activation and epithelial barrier disruption, contributing to IBD development.
  MiR-16 inhibits TLR4/NF-κB signaling, enhancing epithelial cell viability, preventing
  apoptosis, and maintaining tight junction integrity. MiR-146a and miR-323b-5p inhibit
  IRAK1/4 in the MyD88 pathway, while miR-223 suppresses IKKα, preventing TLR4/MyD88/NF-κB
  activation. MiR-146a-5p also reduces inflammation by downregulating the IRAK1/TRAF6
  pathway. Finally, miR-125b and miR-128-3p regulate the NF-κB pathway by inhibiting
  TRAF6 in the TRIF-dependent pathway, aiding in barrier integrity maintenance'
