caption: Schematic diagram of the mechanism by which PFME promotes lung carcinogenesis.
  Chronic inflammation, as a core pathological feature of PF, induces genomic instability
  through multidimensional mechanisms, providing a critical driving force for the
  malignant transformation of lung epithelial cells. ECM remodeling increases matrix
  stiffness, activates mechanical signaling, and reprograms glucose metabolic pathways
  in tumor cells, thereby promoting malignant progression, drug resistance, and metastasis
  of cancer. The formation of an immunosuppressive microenvironment exerts a significant
  impact on the malignant progression of cancer. The aberrant expression of Tregs,
  M2-type macrophages, and the immune checkpoint PD-1/PD-L1 in PFME contributes to
  the formation of an immunosuppressive microenvironment, influencing cancer progression.
  ATII lung stem cells exhibit active Wnt signaling and form a Wnt signaling niche.
  The Wnt niche drives the proliferative potential and disease progression of lung
  adenocarcinoma. Copyrighted from the BioRender platform
