caption: 'The mechanism for the alleviation of PCOS phenotypes by RS2 and AX via the
  gut microbiota-SCFA-liver-AMPK pathway. Schematic illustration of the hypothesized
  pathway: In PCOS-like mouse models, interventions with Resistant Starch (RS2) and
  Arabinoxylan (AX) promote the colonization of beneficial gut microbiota, particularly
  genera such as Ligilactobacillus. These bacteria ferment the RS2 and AX to produce
  key SCFAs, including acetic acid. The SCFAs then activate their cognate receptors
  (GPR41, GPR43, GPR109a) in liver and subsequently the downstream AMPK signaling
  pathway. This cascade ultimately contributes to the amelioration of core PCOS phenotypes,
  including the alleviation of hyperinsulinemia, hyperglycemia, polycystic ovarian
  morphology, and restore normal estrous cycles'
