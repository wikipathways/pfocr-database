caption: Tryptophan is metabolized via multiple pathways, including the Kynurenine,
  Indole, and Serotonin pathways. Tryptophan metabolites from these pathways play
  roles in various homeostatic processes and disease states. Ultra-high performance
  liquid chromatography-mass spectrometry analysis of plasma samples from the pre-renal
  azotemia (PRA) cohort detected 12 tryptophan metabolites (bolded metabolites in
  the figure). 4 of the 12 metabolites were significantly increased in PRA (red arrows)
  as outlined in Table 1. Kynurenine was significantly increased in PRA as compared
  to controls based on a separate analysis (see Figure 9C). 2-oxoadipate and 3-methyleneoxindole
  were detected but are not depicted in this figure. Chemical structures from the
  MolView library. Created in BioRender. Budnick, I. (2026) https://BioRender.com/7ch618m.
  AAAD = aromatic L-amino acid decarboxylase, AFMID = arylformidase, CYP2E1 = cytochrome
