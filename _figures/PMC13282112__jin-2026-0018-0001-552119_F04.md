caption: PPP dysfunction and impaired neutrophil effector responses in G6PD deficiency.
  In G6PD-deficient neutrophils, glucose uptake and phosphorylation generate G6P,
  but impaired G6PD activity limits flux through the oxPPP. Reduced NADPH production
  compromises the regeneration of reduced GSH and limits NADPH availability for the
  NOX2 complex, leading to diminished ROS production. Attenuated PPP activity and
  redox imbalance impair neutrophil effector functions, including NET formation. Figure
  created with BioRender (BioRender.com). NOX2, NADPH oxidase complex 2; oxPPP, oxidative
  pentose phosphate pathway
