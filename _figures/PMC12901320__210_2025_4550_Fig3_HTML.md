caption: The assembly and activation of the TLR4 pathway. This figure depicts the
  innate immune response to DAMPs released from injured cells due to cyclophosphamide.
  DAMPs are recognized by TLRs on macrophages, dendritic cells, and mast cells. TLR
  activation triggers downstream signaling cascades involving adaptor proteins (MyD88,
  TIRAP, TRAM, TRIF) and kinases (IRAK), leading to activation of TRAF-6 and IRF3.
  These transcription factors, along with NF-κB, AP-1, and interferon regulatory factors,
  translocate to the nucleus to induce expression of inflammatory cytokines
