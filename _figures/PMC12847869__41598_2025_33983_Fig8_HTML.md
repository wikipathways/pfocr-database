caption: Overview of hub genes within co-expression modules, including their functional
  annotations and putative interactions. (a) Brown module network. Green and red dashed
  lines represent gene induction and inhibition relationships, respectively, while
  solid black lines denote potential functional interactions. Network model of brown
  module hub gene interactions in Brassica napus under drought stress, illustrating
  the coordinated co-expression that enhances stress adaptation. ABA Signaling serves
  as the central regulatory hub, influencing multiple downstream genes involved in
  drought tolerance. RAB18, a key ABA-responsive gene, modulates stomatal function
  and LD dynamics, facilitating water retention. LTP4 complements RAB18 by regulating
  lipid transfer and also enhancing ROS detoxification via antioxidant enzymes. AITR4
  functions as a feedback regulator within the ABA signaling pathway, preventing excessive
  stomatal closure and optimizing energy allocation. LEA30 contributes to stomatal
  regulation by modulating density and closure, further reducing transpiration. Structural
  stability is maintained by AT4G01985 and EXLB1, which reinforce the cell wall through
  lignin deposition and extensibility modulation, respectively. DAA1 ensures cellular
  homeostasis by supporting protein folding and membrane integrity under stress. Collectively,
  these genes form an integrated network that enhances drought resilience by balancing
  water conservation, oxidative stress reduction, structural reinforcement, and energy
  management. (b) Black and cyan module networks. Functional annotations reflect biological
  roles when genes are downregulated under stress conditions. Green and red dashed
  lines indicate induction and inhibition effects associated with target gene downregulation,
  respectively, with solid black lines representing putative functional interactions.
  Under drought stress, rapeseed downregulates several hub genes to reallocate resources
  toward more critical drought-responsive pathways. Suppression of MIK2 alleviates
  its inhibition on calcium signaling, thereby facilitating enhanced ROS scavenging
  and osmoprotectant accumulation. Similarly, reduced expression of PCS1 and ABCC3
  minimizes the energy-intensive process of heavy metal detoxification, conserving
  glutathione for oxidative defense. Downregulation of CNGC3 limits excessive calcium
  influx, preserving ABA-mediated stomatal regulation and ROS balance, while decreased
  PEARLI 4 expression reflects a shift away from pathogen defense. Moreover, lower
  CRK24 levels enhance ABA-induced stomatal closure, and reduced GSTU24 expression
  suggests that redundant GST isoenzymes may compensate for ROS detoxification. Together,
  these changes represent a strategic metabolic reconfiguration that prioritizes water
  conservation and stress adaptation under drought conditions
