caption: Schematic diagram illustrating the proposed mechanism by which erianin alleviates
  allergic rhinitis. Erianin inhibits the activity of KAT2A, reducing H3K79 succinylation
  (H3K79succ) at the promoter of the p65 (RELA) gene. This leads to decreased transcription
  and expression of p65, a key subunit of the NF-κB pathway. The downregulation of
  the KAT2A/H3K79succ/p65 axis ultimately attenuates the production of inflammatory
  cytokines (eotaxin-1, MUC5AC, GM-CSF), chemokines, and cellular apoptosis, thereby
  ameliorating IL-13-induced inflammation in nasal epithelial cells and OVA-induced
  allergic rhinitis in mice
