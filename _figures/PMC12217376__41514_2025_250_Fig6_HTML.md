  NF-κB pathway by affecting IκB kinase. ROS also promotes transcription and translation
  HIF-1α positive feedback via promoting the phosphorylation of NF-κB and affects
  the expression of NOX to promote the generation and aggregation of reactive oxygen
  species ROS. ROS affects the expression of NLRP3 inflammasome through NF-κB, induces
  the production of IL-18 and IL-1β, and affects the immune activity of T cells and
  dendritic cells. ROS promotes the production of IFN-γ and IL-6 and induces the expression
  of PD-L1 through the JAK-STAT pathway to achieve immune escape217–231
