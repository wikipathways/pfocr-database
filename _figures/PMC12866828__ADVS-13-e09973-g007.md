caption: Mechanism diagram. TMEM131 in AECIIs was upregulated during radiation, which
  promoted the binding between TRAIL and COPII molecule SEC23A. This interaction subsequently
  facilitated the transport of TRAIL from endoplasmic reticulum to Golgi, thereby
  boosting TRAIL secretion. sTRAIL combined with DR5 receptor on AECIIs to activate
  mTOR pathway, ultimately inhibiting mitophagy flux. Accumulation of impaired mitochondria
  lead to AECII senescence in the radiated area, and ultimately exacerbated post‐IR
  lung dysfunction (depicted on the right in irradiated AECIIs)
