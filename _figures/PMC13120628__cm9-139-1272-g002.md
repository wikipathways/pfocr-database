caption: 'Mechanisms summary of acquired resistance to immunotherapy. Left, pink boxes:
  The tumor-cell-intrinsic mechanisms of immunoresistance include loss of the IFN-γ
  signaling pathway, downregulation of antigen presentation machinery, antigen loss,
  epigenetic modifications, activation of oncogenic pathways and the formation of
  a physical barrier via tumor cell-derived collagens. Right, blue boxes: The extrinsic
  mechanisms of immunoresistance involve upregulation of additional inhibitory checkpoints,
  the immunosuppressive functions of CAFs, the role of VEGF and the activity of various
  immunosuppressive immune cells. APC: Antigen-presenting cell; CXCL12: C-X-C motif
  chemokine ligand 12; CAFs: Cancer-associated fibroblasts; COL3A1: Collagen type
  III alpha 1 chain; COL6A1: Collagen type VI alpha 1 chain; COL11A1: Collagen type
  XI alpha 1 chain; DNMT1: DNA methyltransferase 1; EZH2: Enhancer of zeste homolog
  2; HDACs: Histone deacetylases; H3K27me3: Histone H3 lysine 27 trimethylation; IFN-γ:
  Interferon gamma; IFNGR: Interferon gamma receptor; LAG-3: Lymphocyte-activation
  gene 3; MDSC: Myeloid-derived suppressor cells; PD-1: Programmed cell death protein
  1; PD-L1: Programmed death-ligand 1; STAT1: Signal transducer and activator of transcription
  1; TGFB1: Transforming growth factor beta 1; TGF-β: Transforming growth factor beta;
  TIM-3: T cell immunoglobulin and mucin domain 3; Treg: Regulatory T cells; VISTA:
  V-domain immunoglobulin suppressor of T cell activation; VEGF: Vascular endothelial
  growth factor; Wnt: Wingless/integrated'
