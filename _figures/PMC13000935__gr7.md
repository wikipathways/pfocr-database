caption: 'Biological pathways affected by IGF-1c.258A > G. (A) Summary of pathway
  analysis with MetaboAnalyst. Key: 1, Linoleic acid metabolism; 2, Arachidonic acid
  metabolism; 3, Steroid hormone biosynthesis; 4, Glycerophospholipid metabolism;
  5, Glycerolipid metabolism. (B) Integrative view of the major metabolic pathways
  affected by IGF-1 c.258 A > G. Acyl chains, circles; genes, rectangles; DNA, triangle.
  The colors indicate increased (red) or decreased (blue) expression of the gene (encoding
  proteins that catalyze the indicated conversions); increased (yellow), decreased
  (green), and no significant difference (gray) levels of total lipid content in mice
  with IGF-1 c.258 A > G. (C) The interactions were analyzed with Cytoscape. The darker
  the circle color of the protein indicates more interacting partners (except for
  IGF-1). The sticks linked different circles depicted the interactions, and the thickness
  of the sticks was correlated with the strength of interactions. (For interpretation
  of the references to color in this figure legend, the reader is referred to the
