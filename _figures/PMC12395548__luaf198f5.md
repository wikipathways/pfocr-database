caption: The insulin signaling pathway. Insulin binds to the insulin receptor, which
  has tyrosine-kinase activity and mediates the metabolic functions of insulin. Binding
  of insulin stimulates the association of the receptor with downstream mediators,
  including insulin receptor substrate-1 and PI3K. PI3K activates AKT, which works
  on downstream effector molecules to allow for the downstream effects of insulin.
  These effects include inhibition of glycogen synthase kinase-3 GSK-3, which removes
  inhibition from GS, allowing for glycogen synthesis, and translocation of glucose
  transporter type 4 to the plasma membrane, allowing for glucose transport into cells.
  An AKT inhibitor, such as capivasertib, blocks the actions of AKT after activation
  by insulin through the insulin receptor, so despite high levels of insulin and insulin
  receptor activation, downstream effects are unable to be performed
