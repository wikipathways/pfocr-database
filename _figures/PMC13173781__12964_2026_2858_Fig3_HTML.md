caption: mtDNA retrograde signalling. mtDNA is located in the mitochondrial matrix,
  but under triggers such as metabolic disorders, defective mitophagy, ROS, and genetic
  replication errors, it can be released into the cytoplasm, where it can initiate
  inflammatory pathways, particularly the STING pathway, inducing pro-inflammatory
  cytokine expression. Co-leakage with TFAM leads to LC3-mediated autophagy to clear
  cytoplasmic mtDNA. Autophagy itself can inhibit NRPL3, thereby activating TRL9 and
  amplifying proinflammatory cytokines. Released mtDNA can enter the nucleus and integrate
  into the genome, generating an unstable nuclear genome
