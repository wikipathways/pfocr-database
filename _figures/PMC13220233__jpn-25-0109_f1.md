caption: 'Frequency-specific framework of inhibitory control and its proposed link
  to impulsivity vulnerability spectrum. (A) The hypothesized two-step process of
  inhibitory control illustrates frequency-specific neural pathways. Step 1 involves
  early fast-response inhibition mediated by low gamma (30–50 Hz) activity within
  rIFG and preSMA. Step 2 reflects later, sustained behavioral control supported by
  beta-band (13–30 Hz) synchrony between preSMA and the lM1. (B) M1 and M2 jointly
  characterize a gradient of inhibitory vulnerability across diagnostic groups. As
  abnormalities in both the neurophysiological model (M1) and behavioral model (M2)
  progressively increase, inhibitory control declines and impulsivity rises—from healthy
  controls (HCs), to first-degree relatives (FDR), to first-episode schizophrenia
  (FES) patients. Abbreviations: rIFG, right inferior frontal gyrus; preSMA, pre-supplementary
  motor area; lM1, left primary motor cortex; M1, MEG-based neurophysiological model
  (Model 1); M2, non-imaging behavioral model (Model 2)'
