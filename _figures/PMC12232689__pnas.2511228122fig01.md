caption: Cotargeting Nav1.8 along with other ion channels might yield more robust
  therapeutic effect. (A) Shown is a simplified diagram of the acute pain pathway.
  DRG neurons (pink), spinal neurons sending pain signals rostrally (dark blue), and
  thalamic neurons (maroon) send signals to the cortex. (B) Pain signals originating
  at distal axons of nociceptive DRG neurons (Left, pink) are transmitted in the form
  of APs (Top panel, black trace). Blocking Nav1.8 channels (Bottom panel) results
  in partial inhibition of AP firing (Top panel, blue trace) and reduction in pain
  intensity. Cotargeting other channels along with Nav1.8 might enhance the attenuation
  of pain signaling (Top panel, green trace) yielding more robust therapeutic outcome
