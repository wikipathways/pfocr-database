caption: Model illustrating how IFN-γ induces the formation of FMs through CD40-DGAT1
  signaling to control M. abscessus pulmonary infection. IFN-γ activates JAK1, which
  shifts CD40-induced metabolic reprogramming profiles, leading to the accumulation
  of LDs. The increased formation of FMs reprogrammed by IFN-γ significantly inhibits
  the intracellular survival of M. abscessus. Moreover, FMs induced by free fatty
  acids (oleic acid and palmitic acid) also enhance the clearance of M. abscessus.
  In summary, the addition of IFN-γ represents a potential pathway to rescue M. abscessus
  infection
