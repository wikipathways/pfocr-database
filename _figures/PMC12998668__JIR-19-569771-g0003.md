caption: Pyroptosis pathway. DAMPs and PAMPs activate inflammasomes, such as AIM2
  and NLRP3, which initiate the classical pyroptotic pathway through the activation
  of caspase-8 and caspase-1. In the non-classical pathway, cytosolic lipopolysaccharide
  forms an inflammasome complex that activates caspase-4, caspase-5, and caspase-11.
  These enzymes induce pyroptosis by cleaving GSDMD and generating its activated form,
  which subsequently translocates to the plasma membrane, associates with phospholipids,
  and initiates pore formation. This series of events compromises membrane integrity,
  induces cytoplasmic swelling, and promotes the release of intracellular contents,
  ultimately resulting in cell death. By BioRender (https://BioRender.com/h37i302)
