caption: Dietary fiber regulates bone homeostasis by preventing oxidative stress.
  It inhibits and alleviates oxidative stress through through SCFAs, which reduce
  ROS production and affect various signaling pathways, such as AKT/mTOR. These pathways
  contribute to protective autophagy and thereby ehance bone formation. Dietary fiber
  protects the bone against excessive autophagy via SCFAs, which reduce the expression
  LC3 and BCL1, which are involved in autophagy, and enzymes that involved in bone
  remodeling, thereby inhibiting excessive autophagy. Overall, dietary fiber alleviates
  oxidative stress at both cellular and molecular levels, thus leading to homeostasis.
  SCFAs = short-chain fatty acids; ROS = reactive oxygen species; FOXO3 = forkhead
  box O3; AMPK = adenosine monophosphate-activated protein kinase; mTOR = mammalian
  target of rapamycine; DKKI = dickkopf-1; RANKL = receptor activator of nuclear factor
  kappa-B; OPG = osteoprotegerin; Akt/PI3k = protein kinase B/Phosphoinositide-3-kinase;
  TFEB = transcritipn factor EB; SIRT1 = silent information regulator sirtuin 1; LC3
  = microtubule associated protein1 light chain 3; BCL1 = B-cell-lymphoma-1
