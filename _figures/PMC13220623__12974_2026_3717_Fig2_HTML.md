caption: Enzymatic steps in the kynurenine pathway. Tryptophan can be processed into
  kynurenine by idoleamine 2,3-dioxygenase and tryptophan 2,3-dioxygenase, both of
  which are activated by cytokines and stress hormones. In astrocytes, kynurenine
  is metabolized into kynurenic acid, forming what is commonly referred to as the
  neuroprotective branch of the pathway. In microglia, kynurenine is converted into
  3-hydroxykynurenine, which undergoes further enzymatic processing to produce picolinic
  acid and quinolinic acid, key metabolites in the neurotoxic branch of the pathway
