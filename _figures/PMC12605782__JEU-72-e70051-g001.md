caption: Schematic of the hypothetical metabolism of foraminifera. The metabolic pathway
  enzymes indicated by full arrows were found either in the genome or transcriptome
  of previously studied foraminifera (Glöckner et al. 2014; Gomaa et al. 2021; Orsi
  et al. 2020; Powers et al. 2022). The dotted arrows indicate potential metabolic
  pathways whose enzymes have not yet been found in previously studied foraminifera.
  Metabolites detected by GC–MS in Haynesina germanica, Ammonia confertitesta, and
  Elphidium oceanense are indicated in bold font. Metabolites detected by GC–MS only
  in Ammonia confertitesta and Elphidium oceanense are indicated in bold and framed.
  Metabolites detected by GC–MS common to Ammonia confertitesta and Haynesina germanica
  are indicated in bold italic font. Metabolites detected only in Ammonia confertitesta
  are framed. TCA cycle, tricarboxylic acid cycle
