caption: BAP1 protein signaling pathway. ① The BAP1 protein, together with one of
  the ASXL1/2/3, constitutes a Pr-DUB [84]. This complex can deubiquitinate ubiquitinated
  H2AK119ub1 and regulate gene expression. Loss of BAP1 results in hyperubiquitination
  of H2AK119, leading to gene expression errors in uveal melanoma cells and contributing
  to carcinogenesis. The Pr-DUB can also be recruited to chromatin by the forkhead
  transcription factor Foxk2. This recruitment promotes localized deubiquitination
  of histone proteins, alters the local chromatin transcriptional state, and modifies
  gene expression [85]. ② HCF-1 is a chromatin-associated protein that regulates the
  G1/S transition of the cell cycle by binding to the transcriptional repressor E2F4
  or the transcriptional activator E2F1, forming the HCF-1–E2F1 and HCF-1–E2F4 complexes,
  respectively. HCF-1 then selectively recruits the histone H3 lysine 4 methyltransferase
  or Sin3 histone deacetylase [87]. The BAP1 protein binds to HCF-1 and deubiquitinates
  it, thereby regulating the cell cycle. ③ YY1 interacts directly with BAP1 in vivo
  and requires the participation of HCF-1 to form a ternary complex. This complex
  can bind to chromatin and coregulate the expression of genes, such as mitochondrial
  COX7C [74]. ASXL1/2/3, additional sex combs-like proteins 1, 2, and 3; HCF-1, host
  cell factor-1; PR-DUB, polycomb repressive deubiquitinase
