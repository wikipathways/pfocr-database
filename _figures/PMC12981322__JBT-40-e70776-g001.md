caption: Demonstration of the potential antioxidant and protective effects of GA in
  LPS‐induced ARDS/ALI. LPS triggers inflammatory cascades leading to disruption of
  epithelial–alveolar membrane integrity, pro‐inflammatory cytokine release (IL‐6,
  IL‐1β, IL‐17A), fluid passage, and edema, ultimately resulting in ARDS and ALI.
  These processes are associated with downregulation of tight junction and water channel
  proteins (Claudin‐5, ZO‐1, AQP2). Gallic acid enhances IL‐10 signaling, which activates
  AKT1 and inactivates GSK3β, thereby promoting NRF2‐mediated antioxidant defense.
  Through these pathways, gallic acid reduces inflammation, preserves barrier integrity,
  and mitigates lung tissue damage. ALI, acute lung injury; AKT1, serine/threonine
  kinase 1; AQP2, aquaporin 2; ARDS, Acute respiratory distress syndrome; Clau‐5,

  factor erythroid 2–related factor 2; ZO‐1, Zonula Occludens 1
