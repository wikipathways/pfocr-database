caption: 'Antitumor mechanisms of tumor-targeting bacteria. Bacterial species employ
  distinct and shared intrinsic mechanisms to combat cancer. Salmonella directly induces
  cancer cell death through apoptosis, autophagy, and cell rupture. It upregulates
  Cx43, facilitating bacterial-induced gap junctions between tumor cells and DCs,
  thereby enhancing the cross-presentation of tumor antigens and reducing the expression
  of the immunosuppressive enzyme IDO. LPS from Salmonella stimulate IL-1β secretion
  by macrophages and DCs in tumor sites, driving antitumor responses. In addition,
  Salmonella induces TNF-α secretion via the CD14/TLR4/MyD88 pathway, disrupting tumor
  vasculature. Its flagellin activates antitumor responses in CD8+ T-cells and NK
  cells, reduces Tregs, and directly inhibits tumor proliferation through TLR5 signaling.
  Clostridium spp. secrete exotoxins that disrupt cancer cell membranes and essential
  cellular processes, leading to cell death. Clostridium infections recruit CD8+ T-cells,
  granulocytes, and macrophages to tumor sites and induce neutrophil-mediated secretion
  of TNF-TRAIL. Listeria spp. target tumor cells directly or via immunosuppressive
  MDSCs. Tumor reduction by Listeria involves ROS-mediated cytotoxicity and enhanced
  T-cell responses to bacterial antigens. Listeria also converts a subset of MDSCs
  into an immune-stimulating phenotype that produces IL-12, which amplifies T-cell
  and NK cell responses. Probiotic bacteria generate short-chain fatty acids that
  promote cancer cell apoptosis and exhibit immunomodulatory effects by interacting
  with diverse immune cells. CD8+: Cluster of differentiation 8-positive; CD14: Cluster
  of differentiation 14; Clostridium spp.: Clostridium species; Cx43: Connexin 43;
  DC: Dendritic cell; IDO: Indoleamine 2,3-dioxygenase; IFN-γ: Interferon-gamma; IL-12:
  Interleukin-12; IL-1β: Interleukin-1 beta; Listeria spp.: Listeria species; LPS:
  Lipopolysaccharide; MDSC: Myeloid-derived suppressor cell; MyD88: Myeloid differentiation
  primary response 88; NK Cell: Natural killer cell; PMN: Polymorphonuclear cell;
  ROS: Reactive oxygen species; SCFA: Short-chain fatty acid; TLR5: Toll-like receptor
  5; TNF-α: Tumor necrosis factor-alpha; TRAIL: TNF-related apoptosis-inducing ligand;
  Treg Cell: Regulatory T cell'
