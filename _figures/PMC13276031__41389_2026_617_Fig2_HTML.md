caption: Oncogenic programs are induced by genetic alterations (highlighted in blue),
  that are characteristic of specific pediatric cancer types and give rise to fusion
  proteins or dysregulate master transcription factors and signaling pathways. The
  resulting transcriptional programs (highlighted in red) coordinately upregulate
  enzymes of various lipid pathways (highlighted in yellow). This cascade provides
  essential lipids for cancer cell growth and survival. BMAL1, basic helix-loop-helix
  ARNT like protein 1; C/EBPα, CCAAT-enhancer-binding protein α; CLOCK, circadian
