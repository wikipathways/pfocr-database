caption: 'The ITM reprograms through sugar metabolism and undergoes epigenetic regulation
  to influence tumor progression. The figure illustrates how the ITM reprograms tumor
  cells via sugar metabolism and undergoes epigenetic regulation to influence tumor
  progression. It systematically summarizes the key mechanisms by which the ITM regulates
  tumor glucose metabolism and the immune microenvironment. Metabolic pathway intervention:
  The ITM directly regulates host glycolysis, the TCA cycle, and oxidative phosphorylation
  through metabolites like lactic acid and butyrate [169]. Akkermansia muciniphila
  inhibits key glycolytic enzymes such as HK1, while Clostridium butyricum inhibits
  aerobic glycolysis via butyrate and induces ferroptosis [170, 171]. Epigenetic modification:
  Fn inhibits the expression of HK1 through histone methylation, and the DPY30-PI3K/AKT
  axis regulates the methylation of glucose metabolism gene promoters [172]. Moreover,
  there is an immune - metabolic synergy: The microbiota metabolite I3A activates
  the AhR of CD8+ T cells, enhancing glycolytic ability and improving the efficacy
  of ICI. Created with BioGDP.com [173]'
