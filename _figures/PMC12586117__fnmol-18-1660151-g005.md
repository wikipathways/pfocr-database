caption: 'Multimodal pathological seed propagation mechanisms. This figure illustrates
  three primary mechanisms of pathological seed propagation: (A) exosome pathway:
  the ESCRT complex mediates the maturation of MVBs, with nSMase2 playing a key role
  in exosome release, and Rab27a regulates exosome transport; (B) tunneling nanotubes
  (TNTs): cargo, including α-synuclein aggregates and mitochondria, is transported
  through TNTs, driven by F-actin filaments; (C) synaptic transmission: amyloid-β
  fibrils propagate in the synaptic cleft, interacting with SV2A, and influencing
  the postsynaptic membrane. These pathways highlight the prion-like mechanisms involved
  in Alzheimer’s disease, providing insight into the spread of pathological seeds
  within neuronal networks. Created in BioRender. Wang, W. (2025) https://BioRender.com/p7ji4d7'
