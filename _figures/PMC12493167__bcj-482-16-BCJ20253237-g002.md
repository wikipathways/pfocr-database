caption: Mitochondria employ dedicated solute carriers to import deoxynucleosides
  and (deoxy)nucleotides across the inner mitochondrial membrane. Imported dNTPs and
  NTPs can be used directly for mtDNA and mtRNA synthesis, respectively. Imported
  (d)NMPs and (d)NDPs must first be phosphorylated by nucleotide kinases in the mitochondrial
  nucleotide salvage pathway. DGUOK and TK2 phosphorylate deoxynucleosides specifically.
  AK3/4, adenylate kinase 3/4; CMPK2, cytidine monophosphate kinase 2; DGUOK, deoxyguanosine
  kinase; (d)NDP, (deoxy)nucleoside diphosphate; (d)NMP, (deoxy)nucleoside monophosphate;
  (d)NTP (deoxy)nucleoside triphosphate; dNs, deoxynucleosides; DTYMK, deoxythymidylate
  kinase; ENT1/3, equilibrative nucleoside transporter 1/3; GMPK, putative guanylate
  kinase; mtDNA, mitochondrial DNA; mtRNA, mitochondrial RNA; NME4/6, nucleoside diphosphate
