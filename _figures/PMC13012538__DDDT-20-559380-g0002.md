caption: Agarwood’s Multi-Target Anti-inflammatory Action (Overview). Agarwood’s Multi-Target
  Anti-inflammatory Action (Overview). Inflammatory stimuli initiate pro-inflammatory
  responses by activating key signaling pathways such as NF-κB and MAPK. Agarwood
  and its bioactive components exert anti-inflammatory effects by inhibiting these
  central pathways, thereby reducing the production of various pro-inflammatory cytokines
  (eg, TNF-α, IL-1β, IL-6, IL-8, IL-12, IL-18) and inflammatory enzymes (eg, iNOS,
  COX, LOX). Furthermore, agarwood demonstrates cytoprotective properties by modulating
  anti-apoptotic pathways (eg, upregulating Bcl-2) and exerts synergistic antioxidant
  effects through Nrf2 activation. This multi-level intervention highlights agarwood’s
  comprehensive anti-inflammatory and tissue-protective potential
