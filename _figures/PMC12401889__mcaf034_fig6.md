caption: Overlaps between IDA and innate immunity signalling pathways. (Left) When
  mIDA binds, a complex between HAE/HSL2 and its co-receptor BAK1 (and other SERKs)
  is formed. BSKs are phosphorylated to initiate signal transduction, including ROS
  production, a Ca2+ burst and the induction of an MAPK cascade. These signals lead
  to abscission and the induction of pathogen defence genes associated with innate
  immunity. (Right) When flg22 binds, a complex between FLS2 and its co-receptor,
  BAK1, is formed. BSKs are phosphorylated, initiating the same signalling events
  and resulting in an innate immunity response
