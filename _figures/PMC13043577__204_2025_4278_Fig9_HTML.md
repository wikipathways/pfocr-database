caption: Schematic representation of potential molecular mechanism of bisphenol Z
  action on mitochondrial metabolism in human ovarian GCs. Upon exposure to bisphenol
  Z, the compound has the capacity to directly affect mitochondrial metabolism by
  causing initial decrease in ATP Production, accompanied by a change in metabolic
  substrate utilization (switch from a glucose and glutamine-dominant metabolism to
  fatty acids-dominant metabolism). This action can be modulated by activation of
  PPAR pathway, as increase in mRNA expression of PPARα and PPARδ is observed. PPARα
  function is to promote fatty acid oxidation, while PPARδ acts as a ligand-activated
  transcription factor that promotes fatty acid uptake, transport, and oxidation.
  The observed change in substrate utilization profile subsequently leads to an increase
  in ATP production, further disrupting overall metabolic homeostasis
