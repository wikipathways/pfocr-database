caption: circRNA-miRNA-PI3K/AKT regulatory networks in human gastric cancer. Schematic
  overview illustrating the contribution of circRNAs and miRNAs to the dysregulation
  of the PI3K/AKT signaling pathway in gastric cancer. The circGRAMD1B/miR-130a-3p
  axis suppresses the tumor suppressors PTEN and p21, thereby promoting the proliferation,
  migration, and invasion of gastric cancer cells. Notably, p21 functions as a cyclin/CDK
  inhibitor that is closely linked to PI3K/AKT pathway activity. In parallel, the
  tumor-suppressive miR-375 negatively regulates PDK1, leading to the attenuation
  of PI3K/AKT signaling. Several oncogenic circRNAs, including circ0008035, circSERPINE2,
  and circUBA1, act as miR-375 sponges, relieving the repression of the miR-375/PDK1/PI3K/AKT
  axis and promoting gastric cancer cell growth, survival, and motility. Additionally,
  circNRIP1 functions as a miRNA sponge that targets miR-149-5p. Functional studies
  demonstrated that circNRIP1 knockdown reduced AKT1 expression, a validated miR-149-5p
  target, an effect that was reversed by miR-149-5p inhibition. Collectively, these
  interactions highlight the role of circRNAs in promoting gastric carcinogenesis
  through the coordinated modulation of the PI3K/AKT signaling pathway
