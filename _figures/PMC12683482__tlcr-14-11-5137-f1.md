caption: Mechanisms associated with EMT in PSC (figure was created in Figdraw; https://www.figdraw.com).
  Abnormal activation of ILK further activates SLUG, leading to the upregulation of
  fibronectin and vimentin expression. The absence of OVOL2 and GRHL2 triggers the
  upregulation of TWIST, ZEB, and membrane kinase DDR2 expression. The direct inhibition
  effect of ZEB1 and the high methylation of CDH1 promoter synergistically inhibit
  the expression of E-cadherin and promote the expression of vimentin. In LUSC-like
  PCC, NCAM1 is highly expressed and forms a signaling complex with FGFR to activate
  the Ras/MAPK pathway. In LUAD-like PCC, PD-L1 is highly expressed. The decreased
  expression of USP9X reduces the inhibition of MMP9, thereby promoting angiogenesis,
  reducing immune infiltration of CD4+/CD8+ T cells, and leading to a decrease in
  E-cadherin expression and an increase in vimentin expression. CDH1, cadherin 1;
  DDR2, discoidin domain receptor tyrosine kinase 2; E-cadherin, epithelial cadherin;
  EMT, epithelial-mesenchymal transition; FGFR, fibroblast growth factor receptor;
