caption: 'Diabetes drives maladaptive endothelial plasticity through interconnected
  pathways. In physiological conditions (upper panel), ECs dynamically transition
  between tip, stalk, and phalanx states to maintain organ-specific vascular functions
  in the retina, kidney, heart, adipose tissue, and during wound hea§ling. This adaptive
  plasticity is reversible, tightly regulated, and supports controlled vascular remodeling.
  In contrast, chronic diabetic stress (lower panel) drives maladaptive endothelial
  cell plasticity through four interconnected pathways: dysregulated angiogenesis,
  inflammatory reprogramming, metabolic dysfunction, and endothelial-to-mesenchymal
  transition (EndoMT) associated with fibrosis. Bidirectional arrows indicate mutual
  reinforcement among these pathways, forming self-amplifying networks that progressively
  constrain ECs into maladaptive states. Abbreviations are listed in Supplementary
  Table S1. Generated using BioRender'
