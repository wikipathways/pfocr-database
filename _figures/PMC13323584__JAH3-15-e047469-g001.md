caption: Following MI, cardiomyocytes experience an imbalance between lipid and glucose
  metabolism. ACSL1 catalyzes the conversion of LCFAs into LCFA‐CoAs, a critical step
  for ATP production and maintaining metabolic homeostasis. ACSL1 expression is upregulated
  post‐MI, leading to triglyceride accumulation, mitochondrial dysfunction, and increased
  ROS, which collectively impair fatty acid oxidation. IRI‐induced ACSL1 upregulation
  can be mitigated either by ACSL1 knockdown or metformin treatment, restoring the
  metabolic balance and promoting cardiomyocyte proliferation through modulation of
  the PKB/FOXO1 signaling pathway. Created with BioRender.com. ACSL1 indicates acyl‐CoA
