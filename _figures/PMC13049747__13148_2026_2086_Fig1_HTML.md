caption: 'The dynamic regulation of DNA methylation via the coordinated activity of
  writer and eraser enzymes in mammalian cells. A WRITERS: DNMT3a and DNMT3b catalyze
  de novo methylation by transferring a methyl group from S-adenosylmethionine (SAM)
  to cytosine (C), generating 5-methylcytosine (5mC) and producing S-adenosylhomocysteine
  (SAH) as a byproduct. DNMT1 maintains existing methylation patterns during DNA replication
  by targeting hemimethylated CpG sites. B ERASERS: DNA demethylation occurs through
  both active and passive pathways. One active mechanism is the deamination pathway.
  Deaminases such as activation-induced cytidine deaminase (AID) and apolipoprotein
  B mRNA editing catalytic polypeptide-like (APOBEC) convert 5mC to thymine (T), creating
  G:T mismatches that are corrected by thymine DNA glycosylase (TDG)-mediated base-excision
  repair (BER). Passive demethylation occurs when DNMT1 activity is absent or inhibited
  (indicated by the dashed border), resulting in a failure to maintain methylation
  during successive rounds of cell division'
