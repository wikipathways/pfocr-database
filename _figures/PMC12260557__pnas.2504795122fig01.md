  active hemoproteins. Abbreviations: α-KG, α-ketoglutarate; L-Glu, L-glutamate; L-Glu-tRNA,

  ALA, 5-aminolevulinic acid; hemB encodes porphobilinogen synthase; PBG, porphobilinogen;
  hemC encodes HMB synthase; HMB, hydroxymethylbilane; UPG I, uroporphyrinogen I,
