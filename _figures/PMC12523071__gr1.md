caption: ERVs are beneficial to chickens. (A) The expression of the ERV env gene interferes
  with the expression of normal cell surface receptors through glycoproteins, thereby
  enabling cells to resist the re-entry of exogenous retroviruses. (B) ERVs (ERVK18P,
  ERVK25P) mediate the up-regulation of the expression of immune or inflammation-related
  genes (DDX41, IFIH1, IFR7, IFNG, STAT3) in the livers of chickens during fasting.
  (C) The insertion of ev21 on the K allele of slow-feathering chickens induces the
  expression of ISGs(PKP,ISG12,CH25H)in DF-1 cells infected with ALV-J. (D) lnc-ALVE1-AS1
  transcribed from ALVE1 mediates the production of Interferon-beta (IFN-β) and interferon-stimulated
  genes (ISGs) by activating the TLR3 signaling pathway. lnc - LTR5B derived from
  LTR5B of the ERV - L family inhibits the invasion of ALV-J by competitively binding
  to Binding immunoglobulin protein (BiP)
