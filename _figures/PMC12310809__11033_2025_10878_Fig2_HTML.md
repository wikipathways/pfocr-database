caption: NRF2/KEAP1 signaling pathway regulation. Under basal condition, NRF2 is bound
  to the KEAP1/CUL3/RBX1 E3-ubiquitin ligase complex that targets NRF2 for proteasomal
  degradation. However, under oxidative stress, ROS oxidate the cysteine residues
  of KEAP1 causing a conformational change of KEAP1 that inhibits NRF2 ubiquitination,
  then leading to NRF2 nuclear translocation and its binding to the ARE regions of
  antioxidant genes (NQO1, HO-1, etc.)
