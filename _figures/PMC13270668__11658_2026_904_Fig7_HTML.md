caption: Protein palmitoylation in TCR signaling and T-cell activation. When the TCR
  engages with MHC-peptide complexes, it triggers a cascade of events. Several proteins
  are involved in this pathway, such as CD4, CD8, LCK, Fyn, ZAP-70, and PLCγ-1, undergo
  palmitoylation. This PTM facilitates their proper localization and interaction on
  the cell membrane. Ultimately, this process leads to the activation of downstream
  signaling molecules like ERK1/2 and the transcription factor AP-1, which drive T-cell
  activation
