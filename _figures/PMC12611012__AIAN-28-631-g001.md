caption: 'Biosynthesis pathway of sialic acid leading to low sialylation in GNE myopathy.
  The GNE gene encodes the bifunctional UDPGlcNAc 2epimerase/Nacetylmannosamine kinase
  enzyme (GNE/MNK), which drives the first two committed and ratelimiting steps of
  Neu5Ac biosynthesis in the cytoplasm: converting UDPGlcNAc to ManNAc and then phosphorylating
  it to ManNAc6P. Pathogenic GNE variants impair these activities, resulting in hyposialylation.
  Oral supplementation with Neu5Ac, ManNAc, or 6′sialyllactose restores sialylation
  and reduces muscle weakness, with these compounds classified into two groups based
  on their targeted sialic acid biosynthesis pathways: the salvage pathway (NeuAc,
  and sialyllactose) and the de novo pathway (ManNAc). NeuAc: N-acetylneuraminic acid,
  ManNAc: N-acetylmannosamine, UDP-GlcNAc: Uridine 5′-diphospho-N acetylglucosamine,
  CMP-NeuAc: Cytidine monophosphate-N-acetylneuraminic acid'
