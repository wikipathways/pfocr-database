caption: Transcending the JA-mediated GDT by targeted protein degradation.Targeted
  protein degradation selectively triggers specific JA-mediated defense processes.(A)
  Schematic of targeted protein degradation via E3 ligase. JA-Ile agonists enable
  recognition of specific substrates by SCF-type E3 ligase complexes (comprising F-box
  protein, SKP1, Cullin, and Rbx1). U-box-type E3 ligases directly recruit substrates.
  Both types of E3 ligases recruit specific substrate proteins for polyubiquitination
  by an E2 conjugating enzyme, targeting them for degradation by the 26S proteasome.(B)
  NOPh, an artificially designed JA-Ile agonist, specifically induces degradation
  of JAZ9/10 without affecting other JAZ isoforms in A. thaliana. This process activates
  the JAZ9–EIN3/EIL1–ORA59–PDF1.2 defense branch while suppressing the MYC-regulated
  herbivore resistance pathway. Ultimately, NOPh enhances resistance to Alternaria
  brassicicola without a growth penalty.(C) In-Ile, a synthetic JA-Ile mimic that
  targets a specific COI1 subtype, activates the OsCOI1a/2–OsJAZ2/3/6/7/12–OsMYB55
  defense module. In-Ile does not affect the growth-suppressive OsCOI1b–OsJAZ module,
  thus maintaining normal growth.(D) SDE5 from CLas enhances the PUB21–MYC2 interaction,
  leading to MYC2 degradation and HLB susceptibility. AI-designed APP3 protects MYC2
  from SDE5-enhanced and PUB21-mediated degradation by inhibiting PUB21 activity in
  the presence of SDE5, forming a pathogen-triggered stabilization circuit for MYC2.EIN3,
  ETHYLENE-INSENSITIVE 3; EIL1, ETHYLENE-INSENSITIVE 3-LIKE 1; ORA59, OCTADECANOID-RESPONSIVE
  ARABIDOPSIS AP2/ERF 59; VSP1, VEGETATIVE STORAGE PROTEIN 1; PDF1.2, PLANT DEFENSIN
  1.2; B-CHI, BASIC CHITINASE; HEL, HEVEIN-LIKE; CLas, Candidatus Liberibacter asiaticus;
  SDE5, SEC-DELIVERED EFFECTOR 5; PUB21, PLANT U-BOX PROTEIN 21; HLB, Huanglongbing;
  APP3, AI-designed anti-proteolytic peptide 3. Solid lines indicate direct relationships;
  gray dashed lines in (B) and (C) indicate signaling pathways that are not activated
  by NOPh or In-Ile; blunt-ended arrows indicate inhibition; black dashed curved lines
  indicate plants infected with SDE5, and red dashed curved lines indicate the exogenous
  application of APP3 in (D)
