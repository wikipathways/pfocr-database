caption: Schematic of cGAS-STING signaling in dendritic cells (DCs) regulating anti-tumor
  immunity and immune escape. a Tumor double-stranded DNA (dsDNA) activates cGAS to
  produce cGAMP, triggering STING and type I interferon (IFN-I) secretion. Plasmacytoid
  DCs (pDCs) exert anti-tumor effects via CXCL10/IFN-α or mediate immune escape when
  hijacked by prostaglandin E₂ (PGE₂). b “ Dual STING activation” promotes PolySTING
  formation to drive conventional DC₁ (cDC₁) maturation; mature cDC₁ prime Th1 and
  CD8⁺ T cell responses via MHC-I costimulation, chemokines (CCL4/5), and pathways
  like PI3K-AKT. c Conventional DC₂ (cDC₂), activated via the cGAS-STING pathway,
  secretes IFN-I, CXCL9/10 to enhance immune responses. STING-targeting (CAL-1), small
  interfering RNA (siRNA)-mediated mRNA degradation, and combination therapy (ADU-S100 + anti-Tim-3)
  also regulate anti-tumor immunity
