caption: Attenuated responses to HER2‐targeted therapy and candidate intervention
  points within the RUNX–SOS1–RAS signaling axis. (A) Under HER2‐targeted therapy,
  receptor‐level inhibition may not fully suppress downstream oncogenic signaling
  when RUNX‐dependent transcription sustains SOS1 expression, leading to continued
  RAS–MAPK pathway activity. (B) Functional redundancy among RUNX proteins and transcriptional
  plasticity may contribute to maintenance of signaling activity under therapeutic
  pressure. Candidate intervention points within the RUNX–SOS1–RAS signaling axis
  are indicated, including modulation of RUNX‐dependent transcription, targeting of
  SOS1, inhibition of downstream MAPK components, and rational combination strategies
  with HER2‐directed therapies. These approaches aim to attenuate transcriptional
  reinforcement and potentially improve the durability of response to HER2‐targeted
  therapy
