caption: FGF secreted from PAECs enhanced PAEC survival and hyperproliferation in
  an autocrine manner. FGF also bound to the fibroblast growth factor receptor on
  PASMCs and imposed similar effect. NGF bound to the TrkA and activated the connexin‐43
  between PAECs and PASMCs, resulting in hypercontractility. BDNF was produced and
  secreted by PAECs via HIFs pathway. BDNF bound to the TrkB on PASMCs and promoted
  survival and hyperproliferation. TrkB on PAECs activated HIF signaling and subsequent
  BDNF expression upon ligand binding. The signaling triggered by EPH and EFN was
  defined as forward signals and reverse signals respectively. Forward signaling induced
  by EPHB4 induced hyperproliferation and contractility in PASMCs. Hypoxia triggered
  the expression of PDGF‐B through HIFs in PAECs. PDGF‐B then activated PDGF receptor
  β on PASMCs, inducing survival signaling and hyperproliferation. BDNF indicates
  brain‐derived neurotrophic factor; EFN, ephrin; FGF, fibroblast growth factor; HIF,
  hypoxia‐induced factor; NGF, nerve growth factor; PAEC, pulmonary artery endothelial
  cell; PAH, pulmonary arterial hypertension; PH, pulmonary hypertension; PDGF, platelet‐derived
  growth factor‐B; and Trk, tropomyosin receptor kinase
