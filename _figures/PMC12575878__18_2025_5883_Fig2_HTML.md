caption: 'The complex network of lysosomal quality control (QC) pathways, divided
  into early response (primarily repair), and late response (primarily clearance).
  Ca2+ release is a hallmark early response of lysosomal membrane permeabilization
  (LMP). Following this, membrane repair can be ESCRT mediated, or via phosphoinositide-initiated
  membrane tethering and lipid transport (PITT). The latter requires the formation
  of transient ER-lysosome contacts and lysosome-directed lipid transport for efficient
  repair, which is facilitated by PI(4)P (synthesized locally by PI4K2A), ORP proteins
  ORP9/10/11 [70] and VPS13C (for ER-lysosome tethering) [51], and ATG2 (for lysosome-directed
  lipid transport) [70]. During mild damage, the autophagy protein and lipid scramblase
  ATG9 is recruited to redistribute phosphatidylethanolamine (PE) and alter membrane
  curvature to assist repair [69]. Another LMP hallmark is conjugation of Atg8-PE/LC3-II
  to the outer lysosomal membrane [165], which is directed by the v-ATPase-Atg16L1
  complex, or TECPR1 coupled to exposed sphingomyelin [166]. LC3-PE and other signals
  recruit ubiquitin ligases and other factors for lysosomal repair or removal by autophagy
  (microautophagy and lysophagy) [163, 165]. LRRK2, a Parkinsons’ Disease protein,
  is recruited to damaged lysosomes by LC3 to restore cathepsin activity and lysosomal
  size [164]. This LC3 can also recruit the Rab7 GAP TBC1D15 which takes part in lysosomal
  tubulation [167]. A third LMP hallmark, primarily facilitating lysophagy upon sustained
  damage, is binding of galectins such as Gal-3, Gal-8 to the now-exposed luminal
  β-galactosides on the inner leaflet, which then acts as a signal for ESCRT recruitment,
  mTORC1 inhibition and lysophagy [41, 54, 168]. Finally, in some migratory cells,
  autolysosomes fuse with the basal plasma membrane and are extracellularly dispersed
  as migrasome-like bodies [169], while stress granules protect free mRNA during LMP
  by phase-separated sequestration [170]. Abbreviations: ALR: autophagic lysosomal
  reformation, Atg16L1: Autophagy Related 16 Like 1, ATG2/9: autophagy-related 2/9,
  GAP: GTPase activating protein, IST1: Increased Sodium Tolerance 1 Homolog, ITCH:
  Itchy E3 Ubiquitin Protein Ligase, LRRK2: Leucine Rich Repeat Kinase 2, NUFIP2:
  Nuclear FMR1 Interacting Protein 2, ORP: Oxysterol-binding protein-related protein,
  PI4K2A: phosphatidylinositol 4-kinase type 2 alpha, SPG20: Spastic Paraplegia 20,
  STK38: Serine/Threonine Kinase 38, TBC1D15: TBC1 Domain family member 15, TECPR1:
  tectonin beta-propeller repeat containing 1, VPS13C: Vacuolar protein sorting 13 C'
