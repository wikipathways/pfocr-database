caption: 'Aerobic exercise - induced miRNA signaling pathways in liver metabolic regulation.
  There are two parts (a, b) regarding the impacts of aerobic exercise. Part (a) shows
  that aerobic exercise stimulates fat tissue to secrete exosomes containing elevated
  miR-324, which get transported via the blood to the liver. Part (b) presents three
  key regulatory axes: aerobic exercise upregulates ADAR2, which mediates A - to -
  I RNA editing of pre-miR-34a, reducing mature miR-34a levels and thus decreasing
  transcription of Fasn/Srebp1c mRNA to alleviate liver lipid accumulation. Exercise
  also downregulates miR-212 to relieve its inhibitory effect via RISC on FGF21 mRNA,
  allowing increased FGF21 production. Additionally, the miR-324 delivered to the
  liver targets ROCK1 mRNA via RISC, causing its degradation or translational repression
  and thereby enhancing insulin signaling by increasing pAKT/AKT, promoting glucose
  entry, and collectively improving liver metabolic function'
