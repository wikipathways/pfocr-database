caption: Exercise stimulates the release of DAMPs from the mitochondria such as ATP,
  ROS and mtDNA, which can activate mitochondrial biogenesis and modulate immune function.
  Schematized is the recent finding of exercise-induced downregulation of CHCHD4,
  leading to decreases in TRIAP1 and cardiolipin (CL) levels with resultant oligomerization
  of VDAC, pore formation and mtDNA release for activating the cGAS-STING pathway.
  This innate immune signaling via NF-κB promotes oxidative muscle fiber formation
  as part of the adaptation to exercise training. Created in BioRender. Ma, J. (2025)
