  systemic circulation, where they directly or indirectly influence the expression
  of ncRNAs, thereby modulating transcription, translation, and signal pathway activation.

  for the dysregulation of ncRNA expression profiles. Through novel epigenetic mechanisms
  involving snoRNAs and tRFs/tiRNAs, this dysregulation affects ribosomal preference
  and mRNA expression within various cell types of the myocardial tissue. Created
