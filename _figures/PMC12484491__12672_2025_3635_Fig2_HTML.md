caption: Schematic representation of apoptosis and growth factor pathways and the
  role of tamoxifen in apoptosis induction. This diagram illustrates both extrinsic
  (A) and intrinsic (B) apoptotic pathways, highlighting key regulatory factors involved
  in apoptosis. The extrinsic pathway is initiated by death receptors on the cell
  surface, triggering caspase activation (C). Conversely, the intrinsic pathway is
  activated by cellular stress signals, leading to mitochondrial membrane permeabilization
  and the release of cytochrome c (D). Tamoxifen, is depicted influencing these pathways
  by promoting apoptosis in breast cancer cells through modulation of growth factor
  signaling (E) and induction of pro-apoptotic factors. Tamoxifen induces apoptosis
  by decreasing BCL-2 levels (F), promotes mitochondrial-mediated apoptosis (G), and
  suppresses cell growth by inhibiting CIP2A (H), AKT (I) and p53 (J)
