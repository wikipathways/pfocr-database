caption: 'Mechanisms of lactate metabolism and lactylation generation. Glucose is
  transported into cells via GLUT and undergoes glycolysis, generating pyruvate. Pyruvate
  can either enter the TCA cycle for further energy production or be converted to
  lactate by LDHA, while lactate can also be converted back to pyruvate via LDHB.
  Lactate is transported into cells via MCT‐1 and exported via MCT‐4. Once inside
  the cell, lactate can be converted into lactyl‐CoA by ACSS2 and GTPSCS, which serves
  as a donor for enzymatic lysine lactylation catalyzed by “writers” (e.g., p300/CBP).
  Alternatively, lactate can be directly incorporated into proteins via AARS1/2 without
  conversion to lactyl‐CoA. Lactylation occurs on both histone and non‐histone proteins.
  Lactylation can be reversed by “erasers,” such as HDACs and SIRTs, or be recognized
  by “readers,” such as DPF2 and Brg1. Additionally, lactate can activate GPR81 signaling,
  which promotes PI3K/AKT pathway activation and suppresses apoptosis in cancer cells.
  Moreover, MGO, a by‐product of glycolysis, is converted to LGSH by GLO1, and further
  metabolized to D‐lactate by GLO2. LGSH can mediate non‐enzymatic lactylation by
  directly transferring its lactyl group to lysine residues, resulting in Kla formation
  without enzyme involvement. Abbreviations: AARS1/2, alanyl‐tRNA synthetase 1/2;
  ACSS2, acetyl‐CoA synthetase 2; AKT, protein kinase B; Brg1, brahma‐related gene
  1; CBP, CREB‐binding protein; CD147, basigin; DPF2, double PHD fingers 2; GLO1,
  glyoxalase 1; GLO2, glyoxalase 2; GLUT, glucose transporter; GTPSCS, guanosine triphosphate‐specific
  succinyl‐CoA synthetase; HDACs, histone deacetylases; KAT2A (GCN5), lysine acetyltransferase
  2A (also known as GCN5, general control non‐derepressible 5); KAT5 (TIP60), lysine
  acetyltransferase 5 (also known as TIP60, tat‐interacting protein 60 kDa); KAT7
  (HBO1), lysine acetyltransferase 7 (also known as HBO1, histone acetyltransferase
  binding to ORC1); KAT8 (MOF), lysine acetyltransferase 8 (also known as MOF, males
  absent on the first) Kla, lysine lactylation; LDHA, lactate dehydrogenase A; LDHB,
  Lactate Dehydrogenase B; MCT‐1, monocarboxylate transporter 1; MCT‐4, monocarboxylate
  transporter 4; MGO, methylglyoxal; p300, E1A binding protein p300; PI3K, phosphoinositide
  3‐kinase; SIRTs, sirtuins; TCA, tricarboxylic acid cycle; TME, tumor microenvironment'
