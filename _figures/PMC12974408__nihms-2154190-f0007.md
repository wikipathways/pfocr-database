caption: Loss of MondoA leads to alterations in expression of direct MondoA-bound
  targets, as well as secondary effects on the genomic engagement of MYC network members,
  even at nonbound promoters. MYC and MNT binding at UPR/TNFα pathway targets is decreased,
  whereas the MondoA-paralog ChREBP is enhanced. This co-occurs with loss of ATF4
  protein, which may contribute to lack of MYC and MNT engagement at critical shared
  targets. This ultimately unbalances the tumorigenic and apoptosis-promoting effects
  of amplified MYC, resulting in nonremediated stress and cell death
