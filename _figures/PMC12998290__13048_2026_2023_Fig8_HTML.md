caption: Ybx3 negatively regulates meiotic resumption via an AR-dependent pathway.
  Elevated androgen levels activate the androgen receptor (AR), leading to increased
  Ybx3 expression. Ybx3 suppresses the translation of meiotic-associated genes Pde3a
  and Ccnb1, reducing their protein levels. The decrease in Pde3a impairs cAMP degradation,
  while the reduction in Ccnb1 prevents the activation of maturation-promoting factor
  (MPF). Consequently, oocytes remain arrested at the germinal vesicle (GV) stage,
  inhibiting germinal vesicle breakdown (GVBD) and meiotic progression
