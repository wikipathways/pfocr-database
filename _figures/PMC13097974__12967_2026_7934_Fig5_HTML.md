caption: The interactions between the TME and the HER3 of tumor cells. CAFs secrete
  NRG1 ligands that act on HER3 receptors of tumor cells. Through heterodimerization,
  signaling pathways such as PI3K/AKT and MAPK/ERK are activated, which promotes cell
  survival and proliferation. This process can be blocked by both NRG1 antibodies
  and HER3 antibodies. In the liver microenvironment, LRG1 secreted by liver endothelium
  binds to HER3, which activates a protein synthesis pathway via the PI3K-PDK1-RSK-eIF4B
  axis to promote cell survival and proliferation. Preliminary pilot in silico analysis
  indicated that LRG1 may induce HER3 homodimerization by binding to HER3 domain I,
  which awaits validation. Only LRG1 antibodies can effectively block this alternative
  signaling mechanism
