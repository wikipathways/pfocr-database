caption: The physiological roles of IKKα. Schematic diagram of the different mechanisms
  through which IKKα regulates cell behaviour under normal physiological conditions.
  IKKα regulates gene expression through phosphorylation of p65, the transcriptional
  co-factors SMRT and CBP, and histone H3. IKKα also plays a role in the termination
  of canonical NF-κB signalling by phosphorylating TAXBP1 and p65, and the termination
  of non-canonical NF-κB signalling by phosphorylating NIK. During differentiation,
  IKKα regulates the expression of several developmental genes, including those of
  Notch and Hedgehog pathways. In response to TGF-β, IKKα binds to the TF Smad3 and
  promotes the expression of Mad1, a Myc antagonist. IKKα also regulates cell cycle
  progression and apoptosis, by phosphorylating cyclin D1 and promoting its degradation,
  by phosphorylating Aurora A in the centrosomes, and by promoting the expression
  of the factor 14-3-3σ. Upon DNA damage, IKKα phosphorylates p53 and p73, regulating
  their transcriptional activity
