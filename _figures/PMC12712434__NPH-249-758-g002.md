caption: Precise regulation of Toll/interleukin‐1 receptor (TIR)/TIR‐type NLR (TNL)
  immune signaling across various levels. (1) The chromatin remodeling protein BAF60/SWP73A
  interacts with H3K9me2 to suppress TIR/TNL protein expression, while high expression
  of osmotically responsive genes 15 (HOS15) and histone deacetylase 9 (HDA9) reduce
  H3K9ac at TNL loci, impacting transcriptional control. (2) At the posttranscriptional
  level, alternative splicing and small RNAs can introduce stop codons or lead to
  mRNA degradation through RNA‐Induced Silencing Complex (RISC). (3) The translational
  repressor essential for poteXvirus accumulation 1 (EXA1) negatively impacts TIR/TNL
  peptide levels. (4) E3 ubiquitin ligases SNIPER1 and SNIPER2 facilitate protein
  degradation via the 26S proteasome pathway. (5) NRG1C competes with NRG1A for binding
  to the enhanced disease susceptibility 1 (EDS1)–senescence‐associated gene 101 (SAG101)
  complex. Solid arrows indicate stimulation, or single‐step conversions. Blunt‐ended
  arrow represents inhibition. This figure was created in BioRender (BioRender.com/ff4p1rq).
