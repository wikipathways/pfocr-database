caption: 'Bidirectional interplay between RT and ICI: proposed mechanisms of mutual
  sensitization. Conceptual illustration summarizing how RT and ICI reciprocally modulate
  the TME and influence therapeutic responsiveness. RT induces immunogenic cell death,
  releasing DAMPs and TAAs that activate dendritic cells and prime CD8⁺ T cells. RT
  additionally enhances type I IFN signaling, upregulates PD-L1 expression, and normalizes
  tumor vasculature, thereby facilitating immune-cell recruitment and proliferation—mechanisms
  proposed to contribute to ICI sensitization. The RT-induced accumulation of cytosolic
  dsDNA activates the cGAS–STING pathway, leading to cGAMP production, IRF3/NF-κB
  activation, and downstream induction of type I IFN and inflammatory cytokines, further
  amplifying innate and adaptive antitumor immunity. Conversely, ICI enhances radiosensitivity
  by reinvigorating exhausted CD8⁺ T cells, augmenting IFN-γ and TNF-α signaling,
  and sustaining post-RT cytotoxic activity within both irradiated and distant lesions
  (abscopal effects). These bidirectional, partially overlapping mechanisms remain
  incompletely understood, underscoring the need for biomarker-guided RT–ICI combination
  strategies and optimized sequencing to achieve durable therapeutic synergy'
