caption: Mechanism of ergothioneine-mediated H2S production via CSE and 3-MST pathways.
  Ergothioneine serves as an alternative substrate for cystathionine γ-lyase (CSE),
  enhancing hydrogen sulfide (H2S) generation and promoting widespread protein persulfidation,
  including activation of cytosolic glycerol-3-phosphate dehydrogenase (cGPDH), which
  elevates NAD+ levels. Additionally, ergothioneine directly activates mitochondrial
  3-mercaptopyruvate sulfurtransferase (3-MST), augmenting mitochondrial respiration
  and improving exercise performance
