caption: 'Applications of genetically engineered microbes for melatonin productionThis
  figure presents the metabolic engineering strategies used to construct melatonin-producing
  microorganisms, focusing on the integration of key biosynthetic pathways including
  the serotonin biosynthesis route, the tetrahydrobiopterin (BH4) biosynthesis and
  regeneration cycles, and the S-adenosylmethionine (SAM) cycle. Also, genetic modifications
  involve the overexpression of genes such as TPH, DDC, AANAT, and ASMT, as well as
  optimization of cofactor pathways to enhance melatonin yields. The resulting engineered
  strains have broad potential applications across multiple sectors: in agriculture
  and plant science (stress tolerance, seed priming, and microbiome engineering),
  medical biotechnology (psychobiotic development and pharmaceutical melatonin production),
  neuroscience and mental health (sleep regulation), food technology (functional food
  and beverage fortification), dermatology (skin microbiome modulation), animal husbandry
  (probiotic feed additives and reproductive health), industrial biotechnology (biomanufacturing
  and synthetic biology toolkits), environmental biotechnology (soil health restoration
  and bioremediation), and climate resilience research (climate-smart bioinoculants).
  Collectively, these developments highlight genetically engineered microbes as versatile
  platforms for sustainable melatonin production and market expansion.Created in BioRender.
