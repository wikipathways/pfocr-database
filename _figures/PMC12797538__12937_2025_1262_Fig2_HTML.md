caption: 'Schematic illustration of the neuroendocrine and hormonal regulation linking
  obesity and sleep through primary brain pathways. Leptin activates receptors in
  the ARC, inhibiting NPY/AgRP neurons and depolarizing POMC/CART neurons. Insulin
  stimulates both NPY/AgRP and POMC/CART neurons. The intestinal hormones CCK and
  PYY act on the NTS to stimulate POMC/CART neurons. The activation of POMC neurons
  leads to the release of α-MSH, which activates MC3/4R receptors, reducing wakefulness
  and energy expenditure. POMC/CART neuron activity inhibits orexin release from the
  LHA, triggering a downregulation of orexin receptor (OX1R and OX2R) mRNA that ultimately
  mediates reductions in wakefulness and energy expenditure. This reduction in wakefulness
  and energy expenditure exacerbates obesity over time. Abbreviations: CCK: cholecystokinin;
  PYY: peptide YY; NTS: nucleus tractus solitarius; ARC: arcuate nucleus; NPY: neuropeptide
  Y; AGRP: agouti-related peptide; POMC: pro-opiomelanocortin; CART: cocaine- and
  amphetamine-regulated transcript; LHA: lateral hypothalamus; PVN: paraventricular
  nucleus; MCH: melanin concentrating hormone; MC3/4R: melanocorfin 3 and 4 receptors;
  NTS: nucleus of the solitary tract. (Adapted from Ref [53])'
