caption: 'The role of GRK2 in thyroid cancer [118, 142, 143]: Regulation mechanism
  of GRK2 overexpression and GRK5 deficiency on thyrotropin receptor (TSHR) signaling
  pathway and cell fate. When GRK2 is overexpressed, on one hand, it cooperates with
  β-arrestin to induce receptor desensitization; on the other hand, it blocks the
  interaction between KRM-CT and TSHR, affects the Ser/Thr phosphorylation sites of
  TS5, inhibits the apoptotic process, and simultaneously promotes the accumulation
  of P53 protein, which in turn induces events such as cytochrome c release, ultimately
  facilitating the formation of apoptotic bodies. In addition, TSHR activation can
  mediate cAMP accumulation to regulate cell proliferation; however, in the case of
  GRK5 deficiency, the lack of feedback regulation leads to more significant accumulation
  of cAMP'
