caption: 'Clinical implementation pathway based on predicted hidden blood loss (HBL)
  risk stratification. This pathway outlines a personalized management strategy for
  elderly patients with femoral shaft fractures (FSF). Following admission and preoperative
  assessment, the patient’s HBL risk is calculated using the nomogram. Based on the
  predicted HBL volume, patients are stratified into three distinct risk groups, each
  triggering a tailored management plan: Low Risk (HBL < 900 mL):​ Standard perioperative
  care is applied, including routine hemodynamic monitoring and a postoperative hemoglobin
  check at 24 hours. Intermediate Risk (HBL 900–1300 mL):​ Enhanced monitoring is
  initiated, involving scheduled postoperative hemoglobin assessments at 6 and 24
  hours, coupled with consideration for iron supplementation. High Risk (HBL > 1300
  mL):​ Aggressive, multimodal management is mandated. This includes preoperative
  administration of tranexamic acid (TXA), ensuring blood product availability, and
  close postoperative monitoring in a high-dependency setting. All patients receive
  foundational postoperative care, including daily hemoglobin monitoring and early
  mobilization. This risk-adapted framework aims to optimize outcomes by aligning
  intervention intensity with individual patient risk, potentially reducing transfusion
  needs and complications related to significant blood loss'
