caption: The complement system and its functional compartmentalization. Circulating
  liver‐produced complement can be activated through three pathways that result in
  the formation of C3 and C5 convertases, in which cleavage activates C3 into C3a
  and C3b and C5 into C5a and C5b, respectively. This process leads to the formation
  of the membrane attack complex (MAC) and the induction of classical complement functions.
  Activation of cell‐autonomous, intracellular complement in immune and nonimmune
  cells can occur at different subcellular locations. C3aR, C3a receptor; C5aR, C5a
  receptor; CTSL, cathepsin L; MAC, membrane attack complex; MASP1/2, MBL serine proteases
  1 and 2; MBL, mannose‐binding lectin
