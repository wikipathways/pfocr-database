caption: Schematic diagram of common osteogenic pathways. (A) Simplified diagram of
  the Wnt/β-catenin pathway, Wnt binds to its receptor to inhibit GSK-3β activity,
  stabilizes β-catenin and promotes its nuclear translocation, thereby activating
  Runx2 and Osterix expression. (B) Simplified diagram of the BMP/Smad pathway, After
  BMP binds to its receptor, it phosphorylates Smad1/5/8, forming a complex that enters
  the cell nucleus and upregulates the expression of osteogenic-related genes such
  as Runx2, Osterix, ALP, and COL1. (C) Simplified diagram of the RANKL/OPG pathway,
  OPG acts as a “pseudo-receptor” to antagonize RANKL, and maintains the balance between
  bone resorption and formation by regulating the RANKL/OPG ratio during osteogenesis.
  Schematic diagram by Biorender
