caption: 'Integrated schematic of EMT-, invasion-, and metastasis-associated signaling
  pathways and their regulation by miRNAs in NSCLC. The diagram illustrates five major
  developmental signaling pathways: Notch, Wnt, TGF-β/SMAD, Hedgehog, and Hippo. Each
  pathway is represented as an independent module for visual clarity. Ligand–receptor
  interactions at the plasma membrane initiate downstream cascades that culminate
  in nuclear transcriptional responses that promote the EMT. miRNAs modulate the EMT
  by acting at multiple levels, including receptor engagement, cytoplasmic signal
  transduction, and transcription factor activity, thereby shaping the balance between
  epithelial maintenance and mesenchymal transition. Arrowheads indicate activation;
  blunt ends indicate inhibition. The scissors indicate γ-secretase. NOTCH, Notch
  receptor; NICD, Notch intracellular domain; RBP1, Retinol binding protein 1; Wnt,
  Wingless-related integration site ligand; FZD, Frizzled receptor; DVL, Dishevelled;
  CK1, Casein kinase 1; GSK3β, Glycogen synthase kinase 3 beta; AXIN, Axis inhibitor
  protein; APC, Adenomatous polyposis coli; TGF-β, Transforming growth factor beta;
  TGFBR1, TGF-β receptor type I; TGFBR2, TGF-β receptor type II; SMAD2/3, Small Mothers
  against decapentaplegic homolog 2/3; SMAD4, Mothers against decapentaplegic homolog
  4; HH, Hedgehog ligand; PTCH1, Patched 1; SMO, Smoothened; SUFU, Suppressor of fused;
  GLI1/2, GLI family zinc finger 1/2; MST1/2, Mammalian STE20-like kinase 1/2; LATS1/2,
  Large tumor suppressor kinase 1/2; SAV1, Salvador homolog 1; KIBRA, Kidney-brain
  protein (WWC1); OTUB1/2, OTU domain–containing ubiquitin aldehyde-binding protein
  1/2; YAP, Yes-associated protein; TAZ, Transcriptional coactivator with PDZ-binding
  motif'
