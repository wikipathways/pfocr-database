caption: Shared mitochondrial dysfunction pathways in neonatal Hypoxic‑Ischemic, preterm,
  and infectious brain injury. This figure summarizes the shared and condition‑specific
  mitochondrial mechanisms underlying hypoxic‑ischemic, preterm, and infectious neonatal
  brain injury. Diverse primary insults in these three entities converge on common
  mitochondrial pathways, including disturbed energy metabolism, Ca2 + overload, excessive
  production of reactive oxygen and nitrogen species, pathological opening of the
  mitochondrial permeability transition pore, dysregulated mitochondrial dynamics
  and mitophagy, and mitochondria‑mediated cell death and inflammatory amplification.
  These processes form a self‑reinforcing cycle in which persistent ROS generation
  and the accumulation of damaged mitochondria progressively impair neuronal and glial
  function (created in https://biorender.com.)
