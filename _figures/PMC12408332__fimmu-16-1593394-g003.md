caption: Illustration of the proteasomal and lysosomal DAPK1 degradation pathways.
  The ubiquitin E3 ligases Mib1, CHIP, and Cullin3 are known to regulate DAPK1 protein
  levels. Mib1 interacts with the ankyrin repeat region of DAPK1 via its RING finger
  domain. CHIP engages with the kinase domain of DAPK1 indirectly through its U-box
  domain’s interaction with Hsp90. Cullin3 forms a complex with KLHL20, which binds
  to the DD of DAPK1. TSC2 is depicted as facilitating lysosomal degradation of DAPK1
  by binding to its DD through the C-terminus of TSC2. Additionally, the splice variant
  s-DAPK1 is shown to mediate lysosomal degradation of DAPK1, and the lysosomal protease
  cathepsin B is known to cleave DAPK1
