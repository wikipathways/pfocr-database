caption: The canonical Wnt/β-Catenin signaling pathway. In the absence of Wnt ligands,
  β-catenin is recruited to a multiprotein destruction complex comprising AXIN, APC,
  CK1, and GSK-3β, where it undergoes phosphorylation-mediated proteasomal degradation.
  In Wnt-ON states, the engagement of Wnt ligands with Frizzled receptors activates
  Dvl protein, which antagonizes GSK-3β activity and destabilizes the destruction
  complex. Therefore, accumulated cytosolic β-catenin translocases to the nucleus,
  forming a transcriptional complex with TCF/LEF, and initiating the expression of
  Wnt-responsive genes
