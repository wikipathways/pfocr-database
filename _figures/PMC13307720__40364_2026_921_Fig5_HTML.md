caption: Mechanisms of ZDHHC5 in cancer. A. ZDHHC5 promotes FAK activation by mediating
  its S-palmitoylation, which facilitates FAK’s membrane localization. This triggers
  downstream signaling cascades that induce epithelial-mesenchymal transition, enhance
  GBM cell proliferation and invasion, and drive tumor growth. B. ZDHHC5 palmitoylates
  EZH2, stabilizing it and enhancing its activity by inhibiting phosphorylation-dependent
  degradation. This leads to increased H3K27me3 modification, which epigenetically
  silences differentiation genes while promoting stemness genes like SOX2, thereby
  sustaining the self-renewal and tumorigenicity of glioma stem cells. C. It is speculated
  thatZDHHC5 mediates S-palmitoylation of INCENP, which stabilizes INCENP and promotes
  its integration into the Chromosomal Passenger Complex (CPC). This enhances self-renewal
  and tumorigenic capacity of LUAD stem cells. D. The palmitoyltransferase activity
  of ZDHHC5 is critically required for the survival and proliferation of diverse NSCLC
  cells, but not for normal bronchial epithelial cells, indicating a selective therapeutic
  window. ZDHHC5 promotes tumorigenesis by palmitoylating key substrate(s) that remain
  to be validated. E. In TNBC, ZDHHC5 is the primary enzyme that palmitoylates and
  stabilizes the oncoprotein Flotillin-1. This modification protects Flotillin-1 from
  ubiquitin-proteasome-mediated degradation. By maintaining Flotillin-1 stability,
  ZDHHC5 promotes TNBC cell migration, invasion, and metastasis, highlighting its
  role in driving tumor aggressiveness. F. In colon cancer, ZDHHC5 palmitoylates the
  epigenetic regulator EZH2. This enhances EZH2-mediated H3K27me3 deposition, which
  transcriptionally represses the expression of anti-tumor type I interferons (IFN-α/β).
  This mechanism promotes cancer cell proliferation and migration while suppressing
  anti-tumor immunity. G. In PDAC, high ZDHHC5 expression correlates with poor patient
  survival. Mechanistically, disruption of the ZDHHC5 gene leads to the loss of Ras
  activity and membrane localization, thereby reducing the MAPK pathway in pancreatic
  cancer cell lines. H. In pancreatic cancer, ZDHHC5 upregulates the palmitoylation
  of SDHB, which stabilizes the SDHB protein and inhibits its lysosomal degradation.
  This process enhances succinate dehydrogenase activity, leading to increased production
  of fumarate.fumarate.The excess fumarate generated by the SDHB palmitoylation cycle
  elevates acetyl-CoA levels in T cells and promotes T cell exhaustion by upregulating
  the H3K27ac-PD1 axis
