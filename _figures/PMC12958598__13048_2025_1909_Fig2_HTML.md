caption: 'Key lncRNA-mediated regulatory mechanisms of VEGF in Epithelial Ovarian
  Cancer. This diagram illustrates two primary pathways by which lncRNAs regulate
  VEGF expression. A Hypoxia-Driven Regulation: Under hypoxic conditions, HIF-1α is
  stabilized. The lncRNA HOTTIP further promotes HIF-1α stability, leading to its
  translocation to the nucleus. There, HIF-1α acts as a transcription factor, binding
  to the promoter region of the VEGF gene and upregulating its expression, which in
  turn promotes angiogenesis. B ceRNA (miRNA Sponging) Mechanism: The lncRNA (e.g.,
  circRhoC) acts as a molecular sponge for a specific microRNA (e.g., miR-302e). By
  sequestering the miRNA, the lncRNA prevents it from binding to its target site on
  the VEGF mRNA. This relieves the miRNA-mediated repression, leading to increased
  VEGF protein translation and enhanced angiogenesis'
