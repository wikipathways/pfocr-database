caption: Matrix stiffness triggers the YAP-OPA1-TET1/3 loop to drive chemoresistance
  via nuclear-mitochondrial communicationHigh matrix stiffness activates YAP, leading
  to dysregulation of the Hippo signaling pathway, which subsequently upregulates
  the expression of OPA1 and induces mitochondrial fusion. Furthermore, mitochondrial
  fusion is followed by a reprogramming of glutamine metabolic pathways. The metabolite
  α-KG generated from glutamine metabolism activates the DNA demethylases TET1 and
  TET3, resulting in epigenetic modifications of YAP downstream targets and further
  exacerbating Hippo pathway dysregulation. Thus, the YAP-OPA1-TET1/3 loop triggered
  by high matrix stiffness mediates a positive feedback loop in nuclear-mitochondrial
  communication, which, in turn, drives chemoresistance
