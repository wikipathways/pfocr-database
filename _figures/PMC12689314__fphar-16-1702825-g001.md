caption: 'Pathways of the unfolded protein response (UPR) activated during endoplasmic
  reticulum (ER) stress. Schematic representation of unfolded protein response (UPR)
  activation in intestinal epithelial cells under ER stress. Accumulation of misfolded
  proteins due to hypoxia, oxidative stress, or nutrient deprivation activates three
  key sensors: PERK, IRE1, and ATF6. PERK phosphorylates eIF2α, inducing ATF4/CHOP-mediated
  apoptosis and autophagy; IRE1 splices XBP1 mRNA, generating XBP1s that regulate
  protein folding, ER-associated degradation (ERAD), and inflammation; and ATF6 translocates
  to the Golgi, where cleavage produces ATF6-N, which upregulates chaperones and lipid
  biosynthesis to restore ER homeostasis'
