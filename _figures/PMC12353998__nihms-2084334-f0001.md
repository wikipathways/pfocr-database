caption: Exercise activates complex cell signaling pathways, such as AMPK and NF-κB,
  in skeletal muscle and promotes the release of a diverse array of myokines. These
  myokines play important roles in mediating the beneficial effects of physical activity,
  not only within the muscle but also on other tissues and organs such as adipose
  tissue, adrenal glands and pancreas. Myokines can regulate various physiologic process
  such as metabolism, immune function and blood circulation. Through these systemic
  effects, exercise contributes to improving health by reducing the risk of various
  conditions including obesity, diabetes, cardiovascular diseases and cancer. However,
  it is speculated (red dashed line) that immune signaling triggered by exercise stimulation
  can also contribute to the worsening of disabling symptoms in specific patient populations
  such as those with chronic fatigue syndrome (ME/CFS). Created in BioRender. Wang,
