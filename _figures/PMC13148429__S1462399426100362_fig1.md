caption: 'Lactate metabolism and lactylation in cells. Within the cytoplasm, lactate
  enters cells via MCTs and is generated through both glycolysis and glutaminolysis.
  Intracellular lactate metabolism proceeds through two distinct pathways. In one
  pathway, lactate is oxidised to pyruvate, which subsequently enters the mitochondria
  and is metabolised via the TCA cycle. Alternatively, lactate can be converted to
  lactoyl-CoA, which then participates in the lactylation of both histones and non-histone
  proteins. MCT: Monocarboxylate transporter; LDHA: lactate dehydrogenase A; LDHB:
  lactate dehydrogenase B; PDH: pyruvate dehydrogenase; GLUD: glutamate dehydrogenase;
  GOT: glutamateoxaloacetate transaminase; GPT: glutamate-pyruvate transaminase; GLS:
  glutaminase; AACS: acetoacetyl-CoA synthetase; ACSS2: acetyl-CoA synthetase 2; HDAC:
  histone deacetylases; LGSH: lactoylglutathione'
