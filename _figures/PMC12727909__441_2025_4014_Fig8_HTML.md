caption: Modulation of cell death pathways by Coxiella burnetii. Several T4BSS effector
  proteins of C. burnetii are known to influence host cell survival pathways on many
  different levels. These include the inhibition of the intrinsic apoptosis pathway
  (CaeB) and the downregulation of pro-apoptotic genes (AnkG and CaeA). Other cell
  death-related pathways modulated by Coxiella effector proteins include the reduction
  of oxidative stress (MceF) and the reduction of ER stress (CaeB). Interestingly,
  ER stress is not only inhibited by C. burnetii but is also induced by ElpA. This
  highlights the double-edged relationship of C. burnetii with the manipulation of
  host cell apoptosis. In addition, the C. burnetii infection results in modulation
  of transcription of anti-apoptotic genes, modulation of PKA/Akt, Erk1/2, and STING
  pathways, which results in either inhibition or activation of host cell death
