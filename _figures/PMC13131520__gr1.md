caption: 'Process of uric acid biosynthesis. The synthesis of UA consists of de novo
  pathway and salvage pathway, which involve two purine nucleotide pathways: adenine
  and guanine metabolism. De novo synthesis requires the consumption of amino acids
  and ATP, and when the cell is in a state of high demand for purine nucleotides,
  the cell initiates de novo synthesis to replenish purines. The salvage pathway has
  a lower energy requirement, and most normal cells preferentially synthesize purines
  by recycling degraded bases. After a complex series of enzymatic reactions, PRPP
  is converted to IMP. IMP subsequently can be metabolized into AMP and GMP, which
  are converted to hypoxanthine and guanine under the catalysis of PNP, ultimately
  forming xanthine. Xanthine can be oxidized to UA by XOR, regardless of its origin.
  ADSL: Adenylosuccinate lyase; ADSS1: Adenylosuccinate Synthase 1; AMP: Adenosine
  monophosphate; APRT: Adenine phosphoribosyltransferase; GMP: Guanosine monophosphate;
  FAICAR: 5-Formamidoimidazole-4-carboxamide ribotide; HRPT: Hypoxanthine-guanine
  phosphoribosyltransferase; IMP: Inosine monophosphate; PNP: Purine nucleoside phosphorylase;
  PRPP: Phosphoribosyl pyrophosphate; XMP: xanthosine monophosphate; XOR: Xanthine
  oxidoreductase; 5′- NT: 5′- Nucleotidase'
