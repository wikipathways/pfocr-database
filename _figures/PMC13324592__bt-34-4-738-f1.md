caption: Neuroprotective effects of EP through multiple mechanisms in CNS diseases.
  EP exerts neuroprotective effects through multiple protective mechanisms against
  neurons, astrocytes, and microglia. In neurons, EP scavenges ROS, restores intracellular
  ATP and NAD⁺ levels, and suppresses excitotoxicity and apoptosis. In astrocytes,
  EP activates the Nrf2 pathway, leading to the upregulation of antioxidant enzymes,
  HO-1 and GSH, and the release of GDNF, thereby enhancing neuronal survival. In microglia,
  EP inhibits NF-κB and NLRP3 inflammasome activation, reducing the expression of
  pro-inflammatory mediators, such as HMGB1, COX-2, IL-1β, TNF-α, and IL-6. Multiple
  EP mechanisms promote neuroprotection and functional recovery in CNS diseases by
  suppressing neuroinflammation, oxidative stress, anti-excitotoxicity, and energy
  depletion. EP-ethyl pyruvate; CNS-central nervous system; ROS-reactive oxygen species;
  ATP-adenosine triphosphate; NAD-nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide; Nrf-2-nuclear
