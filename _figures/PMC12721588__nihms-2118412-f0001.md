caption: A) Machine learning modelling can aid in the prediction and generation of
  signal peptides. Signal peptide sequences are extracted from secreted protein sequences
  annotated on public databases like Uniprot. The sequences are then tokenised for
  the training of either predictive or generative deep learning models. Adapted from
  Wu et al. 202045 and Teufel et al. 202221. B) Protein engineering methods can guide
  efforts to improve secretability in mammalian cells by identifying cryptic transmembrane
  domains. The likelihood of the presence of transmembrane domains is calculated on
  sliding windows. in silico mutagenesis is then applied to mutate and remove the
  identified domains. Adapted from Wang et al. 202322. C) Systems biology approaches
  can be used to identify and model the host cell machinery in the secretion pathway.
  This can support identification and mitigation of interactions between the secreted
  protein and the cellular microenvironment that can impact secretion. Data from the
  literature are integrated with new information derived from omics approaches to
  update the parameters of a genome scale model enabling secretion cost calculations.
  Adapted from Gutierrez et al. 202018 and Masson et al. 202447
