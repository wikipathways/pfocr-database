caption: Cytokine storm and its immunopathogenesis of DBV infection(A) DBV infection
  upregulates miR-146a/b in macrophages, promoting M2-like polarization via STAT pathway
  inhibition. Meanwhile, CD56dimCD16+ NK cells become hyperactivated, secreting IFN-γ
  and TNF-α during the acute phase, contributing to cytokine storm.(B) NSs bind to
  TPL-2/ABIN2, activating the MEK-ERK pathway and upregulating IL-10, which suppresses
  T cell function, promotes IL-6 release, and inhibits TGF-β production. NSs also
  activates the IKKβ-NF-κB axis, inducing pro-inflammatory cytokines (e.g., IL-1β
  and IL-6). Additionally, DBV-induced mitochondrial damage releases oxidized mtDNA,
  which activates the NLRP3 inflammasome and caspase-1, promoting IL-1β maturation
  and amplifying the inflammatory response.(C) DBV impairs CSR by invading germinal
  centers of B cell follicles, and causes T cell activation followed by depletion.(D)
  Excessive cytokines damage vascular endothelium, creating a positive feedback loop
  that drives SIRS, culminating in MOF involving the cardiovascular, neurological,
  respiratory, and hematologic systems
