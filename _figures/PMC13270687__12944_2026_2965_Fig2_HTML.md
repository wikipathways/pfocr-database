caption: 'Cav-1 and endothelial cells. ① Cav-1 mediates LDL transendothelial transport,
  promoting lipid deposition. ② Inflammatory cytokines (e.g., IL-1β) upregulate the
  expression of Cav-1 and Cavin-1 by activating the NF-κB pathway, promoting caveolae
  formation and maturation, thereby enhancing LDL transcytosis across the endothelium
  and leading to subendothelial lipid deposition. ③ Dynamic Interplay Between Cav-1
  and eNOS: Under physiological conditions, Cav-1 compartmentalizes and stabilizes
  eNOS within caveolae, regulating NO homeostasis. Under pathological stimuli, dysregulated
  eNOS activity can induce post-translational modifications and degradation of Cav-1,
  disrupting caveolae structure and endothelial barrier function, thereby exacerbating
  oxidative stress and inflammation. ④ Cav-1 Modulates FFA/CD36 Signaling and the
  Endothelial Barrier: Free fatty acids activate Src kinase via CD36, leading to phosphorylation
  of Cav-1 and destabilization of caveolae. Concurrently, CD36 cooperates with Cav-1
  to activate nSMase2, increasing ceramide production and altering exosome secretion,
  which compromises endothelial barrier integrity and promotes lipid retention and
  inflammation'
