caption: Proposed model illustrating how plasma membrane glycosylated sterols (GS)
  modulate cold sensitivity in tomato. An increased ratio of glycosylated to free
  sterols (GS/FS) enhances membrane structural integrity and functionality, promoting
  jasmonic JA biosynthesis and signaling, which activates the CBF–COR cold-response
  pathway, antioxidant defenses, and polyamine biosynthesis. Collectively, these responses
  mitigate chilling injury. In contrast, a low GS/FS ratio impairs membrane structure
  and stress signaling, resulting in elevated cold sensitivity
