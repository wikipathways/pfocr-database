caption: 'The energy coupling mechanisms of the axon-myelin unit. A Lactate is transferred
  from myelin to axon via MCT1 and MCT2, where it is converted into pyruvate by LDH,
  resulting in ATP production. B In glucose metabolism, glucose is translocated via
  GLUT1 and GLUT3 from myelin to the axon, where it is transformed into pyruvate by
  glycolysis, resulting in ATP production. C In EV signalling, myelin releases MVE,
  and then the exosome-derived SIRT2 protein increases ATP production via deacetylation
  in axonal mitochondria. D In lipid metabolism, very long-chain fatty acids (VLCFA)
  undergo peroxisomal β-oxidation in the paranodal loop, after which the resultant
  short-chain fatty acids are delivered to axonal mitochondria by a carnitine-dependent
  pathway for complete β-oxidation to generate ATP. MCT1: monocarboxylate transporter
  1; MCT2: monocarboxylate transporter 2; LDH: lactic dehydrogenase; GLUT1: glucose
  transporter 1; GLUT3: glucose transporter 3; EV: extracellular vesicle; MVEs: multivesicular
  endosomes; SIRT2: sirtuin-2; VLCFA: very long chain fatty acids'
