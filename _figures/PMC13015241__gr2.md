caption: 'Transforming growth factor beta (TGF-β) receptor 3 (TGFBR3)-mediated canonical
  (left) and non-canonical (right) TGF-β signaling pathways. In the canonical pathway,
  TGFBR3 binds and presents TGF-β ligands to TGFBR2, which then recruits and phosphorylates
  TGFBR1. This activates mothers against decapentaplegic homolog (Smad)-dependent
  signaling cascades to regulate downstream gene transcription. Additionally, the
  extracellular domain of TGFBR3 can be proteolytically cleaved to generate a soluble
  form (soluble TGFBR3 (sTGFBR3)), which antagonizes TGF-β signaling by competitively
  binding TGF-β ligands. In the non-canonical pathway, the cytoplasmic domain of TGFBR3
  interacts with Galpha-interacting protein (GAIP)-interacting protein, C terminus
  (GIPC) and β-arrestin 2 to modulate phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase (PI3K), mitogen-activated
  protein kinase (MAPK), and nuclear factor-kappaB (NF-κB) signaling pathways. P:
  phosphorylated; Cdc42: cell division cycle 42; RhoA: Ras homolog gene family member
  A'
