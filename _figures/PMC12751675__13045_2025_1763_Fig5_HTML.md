caption: Inflammasome-associated immune dynamics in the TME. TMEM176B inhibition and
  low adropin concentrations enhance macrophage antitumor activity. APOC3 and ARF1
  ablation increase cytotoxicity and CD8 + T cell infiltration in the tumor microenvironment.
  Vaccine adjuvants hyperactivate aged DCs with CD4 + Th1 activity. Bone marrow stromal
  cells with high inflammasome activity inhibit B-cell malignancy. High NLRP3 expression
  in tumor cells causes Th2-type inflammation and immunocompetent TME to become immunosuppressive
  by increasing IL-1 and PD-L1. Increased SPHK1 expression inhibits CD8 + T cell cytotoxicity
  and promotes TAM infiltration. TREM2 depletion activates the NLRP3/NF-κB/IL-1β signaling
  pathway, resulting in proinflammatory macrophages that aid tumor growth. Nanoparticle
  delivery of Alu RNA and NR1D1 deficiency activate the NLRP3 inflammasome to promote
  EMT. NLRP3 transcriptional repression by ERRα inhibits inflammasome-mediated pyroptosis
  of cancer cells. DC, dendritic cell; Mφ, macrophage; SPHK1, sphingosine kinase 1;
  TAM, tumor-associated macrophage; TME, tumor microenvironment; EMT, epithelial mesenchymal
  transition; ERRα, estrogen-related receptor alpha
