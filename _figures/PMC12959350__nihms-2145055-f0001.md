caption: CYP11A1 converts cholesterol to pregnenolone. On the left, the pregnane pathway
  yields progesterone (via 3β-HSD), then DHP (via 5α-reductase), allopregnanolone
  (via 3α-HSD), and allopregnanediol (via 20α-HSD); in parallel, progesterone forms
  DOC (via CYP21A2), which is reduced to DHDOC (via 5α-reductase) and then to THDOC
  (via 3α-HSD). On the right, the androstane pathway begins with CYP17A1 to form DHEA,
  which converts either to androstenedione (via 3β-HSD) then testosterone (via 17β-HSD),
  or to androstenediol (via 17β-HSD) then testosterone (via 3β-HSD). 5α-reductase
  yields androstanedione and DHT, which 3α-HSD converts to androsterone and 3α-androstanediol.
  A regulatory branch sulfates or desulfates pregnenolone and DHEA via SULT2A1 and
  STS, forming pregnenolone sulfate and DHEA sulfate. The yellow box highlights pregnanes;
  the blue box highlights androstanes.Abbreviations. CYP11A1, cholesterol side-chain
  cleavage enzyme; CYP17A1, 17α-hydroxylase/17,20-lyase; CYP21A2, 21-hydroxylase;
  3β-HSD, 3β-hydroxysteroid dehydrogenase; 5αR, 5α-reductase; 3α-HSD, 3α-hydroxysteroid
  dehydrogenase; 20α-HSD, 20α-hydroxysteroid dehydrogenase; 17β-HSD, 17β-hydroxysteroid
  dehydrogenase; SULT2A1, hydroxysteroid sulfotransferase; STS, steroid sulfatase;
  PREGS, pregnenolone sulfate; DHEA, dehydroepiandrosterone; DHEAS, dehydroepiandrosterone
  sulfate; DOC, deoxycorticosterone; DHDOC, dihydrodeoxycorticosterone; THDOC, tetrahydrodeoxycorticosterone;
  DHP, dihydroprogesterone; AP, allopregnanolone; DHT, dihydrotestosterone
