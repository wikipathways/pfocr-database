caption: 'The intermittent hypoxia response pathway mediates parallel disruptions
  in secretory profiles and metabolic homeostasis across obesity and SDBs pathophysiologies.
  Intermittent hypoxia activates HIFs to drive systemic metabolic dysregulation through
  three interconnected mechanisms: promoting insulin-resistant adipocyte remodeling,
  inducing SREBP-1/SCD-1-mediated hepatic steatosis, and triggering ROS-dependent
  mitochondrial dysfunction. The cyclical nature of CIH in SDB generates ROS while
  depleting antioxidant defenses, creating a state of oxidative stress that damages
  multiple organ systems. These pathological changes collectively increase susceptibility
  to metabolic syndrome, progressive liver disease, and cardiovascular complications.
  Abbreviations: FFA: free fatty acid; HIF: hypoxia-inducing factor; OS: oxidative
  stress; ROS: reactive oxygen species'
