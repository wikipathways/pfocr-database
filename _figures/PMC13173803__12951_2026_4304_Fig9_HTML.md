caption: Schematic illustration of the mechanism underlying PSNP-induced hepatic lipid
  accumulation. PSNP exposure impairs hepatic autophagy, thereby blocking the autophagic
  degradation of NCoR1 and leading to its pathological accumulation. Simultaneously,
  PSNPs activate the mTOR/S6K2 pathway, promoting NCoR1 phosphorylation and nuclear
  translocation. Nuclear-localized NCoR1 binds to PPARα, suppressing transcription
  of fatty acid β-oxidation genes and reducing lipid catabolism, ultimately resulting
  in hepatic lipid accumulation
