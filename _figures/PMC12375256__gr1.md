caption: 'ThecGAS-MITA/STING-TBK1-IRF3signaling pathway.(A) A schematic representation
  of the functional domains of human MITA.(B) A diagrammatic overview illustrating
  the cGAS-MITA-TBK1-IRF3 signaling cascade. Upon detecting cytosolic double-stranded
  DNA, cGAMP synthesized by cGAS binds to the ligand-binding domains (LBDs) of the
  MITA dimer. This interaction is accompanied by a 180° clockwise rotation of the
  LBDs relative to the transmembrane region. Such a conformational change creates
  a surface topology conducive to lateral MITA oligomerization. The activation of
  the MITA polymer induces COPII-mediated vesicular transport through the ERGIC to
  the Golgi. Consequently, MITA recruits TBK1 through C-terminal tails (CTTs) and
  facilitates the trans-phosphorylation and activation of TBK1. Once activated, TBK1
  phosphorylates the MITA CTT, generating a docking site for IRF3, which is subsequently
  phosphorylated by TBK1. Phosphorylated IRF3 undergoes dimerization and translocates
  to the nucleus, where it initiates the transcription of interferon genes (IFN).
  The COPI complex promotes the retrograde transport of MITA back to the ER, during
  which SURF4 functions as an adaptor.Abbreviations: CTT, C-terminal tail; ER, endoplasmic
  reticulum; ERGIC, ER–Golgi intermediate compartment'
