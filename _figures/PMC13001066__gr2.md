caption: The role of SUMOylation in the regulation of mitochondrial. SUMOylation maintains
  mitochondrial mass homeostasis and cellular adaptability via multi-dimensional mechanisms.
  In the regulation of stress responses, SUMOylation influences the mitochondrial
  localization and function by modifying proteins such as HK2, MURF1, and SIRT3, thereby
  affecting mtROS production. Simultaneously, the accumulation of mtROS can feedback-regulate
  the SUMOylation states of proteins such as P300 and SENP3, establishing a dynamic
  balance. Additionally, SUMOylation participates in the repair of mtDNA damage by
  modulating the activities of DNA repair-related proteins, including PSO2, and enzymes
  involved in the SUMO cascade reaction, such as ULP2 and SIZ1. Concerning mitochondrial
  dynamics, SUMOylation directly acts on fission proteins (e.g., DRP1 and FIS1), regulating
  their expression, mitochondrial localization, and protein interactions. It also
  modulates the SUMOylation status of mitochondrial fusion proteins (e.g., MFN1/2),
  altering mitochondrial morphology. Furthermore, SUMOylation contributes to PINK1-PARKIN
  pathway-dependent mitochondrial autophagy by targeting proteins such as NDP52 and
  MUL1, ensuring selective clearance of damaged mitochondria and maintaining mitochondrial
  quality control and cellular homeostasis
