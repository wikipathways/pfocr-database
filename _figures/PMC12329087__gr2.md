caption: Regulation of SERCA activity by post-translational modifications. Growth
  factor signaling activates PI3K/AKT pathway, leading to the production of PI(3,4,5)P3
  and subsequent AKT activation. Activated AKT phosphorylates PLN, relieving its inhibitory
  effect on SERCA and enhancing Ca²⁺ reuptake into the ER. Additionally, AKT modulates
  SERCA activity through the phosphorylation of GSK3β, further regulating Ca2+ homeostasis.
  SOCE, mediated by STIM1/2 and ORAI channels, facilitates extracellular Ca2+ influx
  into the cytosol. The resulting increase in Ca²⁺ activates CaMK, which phosphorylates
  PLN to further enhance SERCA function and prevent intracellular Ca2+ overload. In
  addition, c-Jun N-terminal kinase 2 (JNK2) directly phosphorylates SERCA, increasing
  its activity. Post-translational modifications, including SUMOylation and acetylation,
  further fine-tune SERCA function, while ATP depletion (↓ ATP/ADP) disrupts Ca2+
  homeostasis and contributes to autophagy. Mitochondrial Ca2+ uptake via the MCU
  is also depicted, along with the generation of ROS and production of ATP. The intricate
  interplay between ER Ca2+ regulation, mitochondrial function, and autophagy highlights
  the critical role of Ca2+ homeostasis in metabolism and cellular stress responses
