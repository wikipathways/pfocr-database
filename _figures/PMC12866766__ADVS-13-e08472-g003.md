caption: Schematic illustration of MDSPL NPs for targeted delivery of Lut to CD74+
  macrophages in OA treatment. i) The DS‐PLGA NPs were engineered to encapsulate Lut
  and surface‐modified with MIF‐mimetic peptide sequences to target CD74+ macrophages.
  ii) MDSPL was administered via IA injection into the OA joint of mouse induced by
  DMM surgery. iii) The MIF‐mimetic peptides on MDSPL surface specifically recognize
  and bind to CD74 receptors highly expressed on CD74+ macrophages, facilitating efficient
  cellular internalization through CD74‐mediated endocytosis. ix) Following cellular
  internalization, the elevated ROS levels in inflammatory macrophages trigger the
  cleavage of diselenide bonds, leading to Lut release. The released Lut binds to
  the intracellular domain of CD74, subsequently downregulating CD74 expression. This
  downregulation reduces CEBPB levels and disrupts its interaction with P65, thereby
  inhibiting P65 nuclear translocation and NF‐κB pathway activation. The suppression
  of this inflammatory cascade ultimately attenuates macrophage‐mediated inflammation
  and ameliorates OA progression
