caption: Identification and Functional Characterization of Lipotoxicity-Related Genes
  in Thyroid Carcinoma (THCA). a Volcano plot of differentially expressed genes (DEGs)
  in THCA compared to control. The x-axis represents the log2 fold change (FC), and
  the y-axis represents -Log10(p-value). Each point corresponds to a gene, with horizontal
  and vertical reference lines indicating thresholds at -Log10(0.05) = 1.3 and log2
  FC = ± 0.5, respectively. Genes in the top-right quadrant (orange) are upregulated
  DEGs, while those in the top-left quadrant (blue) are downregulated. Non-significant
  genes are represented in grey. The top 10 upregulated and downregulated genes, based
  on the largest absolute log2 FC values, are highlighted. b Heatmap of DEGs between
  THCA and normal tissue samples. c Venn diagram showing the intersection of DEGs
  and lipotoxicity-related genes (L-RGs). The orange circles represent DEGs, while
  the blue circles represent L-RGs. d Protein–protein interaction (PPI) network illustrating
  interactions among candidate L-RGs. e Gene Ontology (GO) enrichment analysis of
  candidate L-RGs. This analysis identifies 1,546 enriched biological functions, including
  1,439 biological processes (BP), 34 cellular components (CC), and 73 molecular functions
  (MF). f Kyoto Encyclopedia of Genes and Genomes (KEGG) pathway enrichment analysis
  of candidate L-RGs
