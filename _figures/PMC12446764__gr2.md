caption: 'Signaling pathways in cervical cancer cells and their impact on cellular
  behavior. Note: This figure illustrates two major signaling pathways in cervical
  cancer cells and their roles in regulating cell proliferation, differentiation,
  migration, invasion, stemness, EMT, and drug resistance. The left pathway involves
  ERα, PI3K, AKT, and mTOR, influencing gene transcription and cellular behaviors.
  The right pathway involves ERα, Ras, Raf, MEK, and ERK, similarly regulating gene
  transcription and cellular responses. The Wnt/Frizzled/LRP pathway modulates β-catenin
  stability via Dishevelled, GSK-3β, CK1α, Axin, and APC, thereby affecting gene transcription
  and cellular behavior. Aberrant activation of these pathways is closely associated
  with cervical cancer progression'
