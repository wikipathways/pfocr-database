caption: 'Activation mechanisms and structure basis of the cGAS‐STING signaling pathway.
  (A) Upon stimulation by dsDNA from pathogens, dead cells, tumor cells, endogenous
  micronuclei, or mitochondria, cGAS catalyzes the formation of 2’3’‐cGAMP, which
  acts as a second messenger to induce STING activation. Then STING translocates from
  the ER to the ERGIC and the Golgi apparatus, thereafter, activating IRF3 and NF‐κB
  transcription activity through TBK1 and IKKε. The downstream cascade ultimately
  promotes the expression of IFNs and ISGs, thus triggering inflammatory responses;
  (B) cGAS (PDB ID code 4LEY) has three DNA‐binding sites: site A, B, and C; (C) cGAMP
  (PDB ID code 4KSY) contains 2′−5′ and 3′−5′‐bonds between AMP and GMP; (D) STING
  (PDB ID code 6NT5) is a transmembrane protein featured by the LBD (cGAMP binding
  domain) and the TMD. cGAMP, 2’3’‐cyclic GMP‐AMP; cGAS, cyclic GMP‐AMP synthase;
  dsDNA, double‐stranded DNA; ER, endoplasmic reticulum; ERGIC, ER‐Golgi intermediate
  compartment; IFNs, interferons; IKKε, IkappaB kinase epsilon; IRF3, interferon regulatory
  factor 3; ISGs, interferon‐stimulated genes; LBD, ligand binding domain; mtDNA,
  mitochondrial DNA; NF‐κB, nuclear transcription factor‐kappa B; p, phosphorylation;
  STING, stimulator of interferon gene; TBK1, TANK‐binding kinase 1; TMD, transmembrane
  domain'
