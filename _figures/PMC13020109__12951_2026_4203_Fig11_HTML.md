  for metastatic adrenal cortical carcinoma (ACC).The CG/MC/U-M nanoassembly releases
  Cu²⁺ to induce cuproptosis and mitochondrial damage, leading to mtDNA release into
  the cytosol. Concurrently, released Mn²⁺ acts as a cGAS cofactor, enhancing cGAS-STING
  pathway activation. This promotes type I interferon production and dendritic cell
  maturation, synergistically reshaping the immunosuppressive tumor microenvironment
  and boosting antitumor immunity
