caption: DOX@Fe-Gal@Fe-Zol-cGAMP Nanodrug Coordinates Pyroptosis and cGAS-STING Activation.The
  nanodrug releases galangin and DOX, inducing caspase-3-mediated GSDME cleavage and
  pyroptosis, releasing DAMPs (HMGB1, ATP). Concurrently, cGAMP activates the cGAS-STING
  pathway, promoting IFN-β production and DC maturation. Pyroptosis-derived DAMPs
  amplify immune activation, while STING signaling further promotes inflammatory cell
  death, creating a positive feedback loop that enhances antitumor immunity against
  primary and metastatic tumors
