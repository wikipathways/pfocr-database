caption: 'Mediation of the Childhood Trauma Questionnaire (CTQ) → functional movement
  disorder (FMD) association by affective symptoms. Bayesian structural equation model
  illustrating indirect and direct pathways between childhood trauma and FMD symptom
  burden. The latent variable CTQ represents the five Childhood Trauma Questionnaire
  subscales: EA = emotional abuse, EN = emotional neglect, PA = physical abuse, PN = physical
  neglect, and SA = sexual abuse. The latent variable DEPANX captures affective symptoms,
  indexed by the Beck Depression Inventory–II (BDI-II) and the State–Trait Anxiety
  Inventory – Trait form (STAI). The latent variable FMD reflects functional symptom
  severity, measured by the Subjective Motor Symptom Severity Scale (SMSS), Cognitive
  Complaints Questionnaire (QPC), Fatigue Severity Scale (FSS), and Pain. Standardized
  path coefficients are shown adjacent to arrows. The direct path from CTQ to FMD
  is negligible, whereas the indirect path via DEPANX is robust, indicating full mediation
  by affective symptoms'
