caption: Alpha-fetoprotein exerts complex regulatory effects on cancer cell pathways
  through its interaction with AFPR. This binding initiates diverse signaling cascades,
  notably triggering PI3K-mediated conversion of PIP2 to PIP3, leading to Akt pathway
  activation. Subsequent downstream signaling through mTOR enhances cellular survival
  mechanisms, while EpCAM, MMP9, MMP2, and CXCR4 mediate invasive and metastatic processes.
  Cellular calcium dynamics are modulated through ion channel regulation, affecting
  NFkB signaling, while AFP's interaction with TNF/TNFR system influences inflammatory
  and immune responses. Additionally, AFP modulates apoptotic pathways through regulation
  of Bcl-2, Bax, Bak, and XIAP proteins, ultimately affecting Caspase-3 activation
  and cell survival
