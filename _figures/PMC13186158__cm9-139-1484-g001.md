caption: 'The role of cannabinoids in the immune system. (A) Immune cells apoptosis.
  CM induces B cell apoptosis by upregulating caspase. THC can trigger mice macrophage
  and T cell apoptosis by inhibiting Bcl-2 and increasing caspase activity. Additionally,
  THC treatment induces NF-κB-regulated apoptosis gene transcription, inducing dendritic
  cell apoptosis. CBD has been proven to generate ROS and activate caspase-8 and caspase-3,
  resulting in CD4+ and CD8+ T cell as well as mice thymocytes apoptosis. Besides,
  CBD acts on mitochondria, inducing ROS generation by opening the MPTP, leading to
  monocyte apoptosis. (B) Immune cells proliferation. THC could inhibit intracellular
  calcium homeostasis in thymocyte and inhibit its proliferation. Furthermore, THC
  also has an inhibitory effect on the proliferation of T cells, helper T cells and
  Ly2 lymphocytes. Apart from THC, endogenous cannabinoid AEA can restrain the proliferation
  of T cells and B cells stimulated by mitogen. (C) Pro-inflammatory cytokine production.
  Natural cannabinoids can significantly inhibit LPS-induced elevation of pro-inflammatory
  cytokine levels such as IL-1β, IL-6, and TNF-α. Besides, CBD and THC can prevent
  the elevation of IL-6, IL-8, and TNF-α levels caused by LPS through regulating the
  NLRP3 inflammasome and STAT3 signaling pathway. Furthermore, CBD alone can also
  significantly decrease the plasma levels of pro-inflammatory cytokines IFN-γ and
  TNF-α, while increasing anti-inflammatory cytokines IL-4 and IL-10. (D) Other immune
  regulatory functions. In the tumor microenvironment of non-small cell lung cancer,
  activated CB2 promotes tumor growth by inhibiting the activity of CD8 T cells and
  NK cells. Additionally, THC can also induce mouse macrophages pyroptosis through
  CB2-mediated p38 MAPK activation. Besides, cannabinoids could directly induce the
  conversion of B cell antibodies from IgM to IgE. 2-AG: 2-arachidonyl glycerol; Akt:
  Protein kinase B; Bcl-2: B-cell lymphoma-2; CBD: Cannabidiol; CM: Cannabinoid mixture;
  DAGL-α: Diacylglycerol lipase α; IFN-γ: Interferon-γ ; IgE: Immunoglobulin E; IgM:
  Immunoglobulin M; IL: Interleukin; JNK/c-Jun: c-Jun N-terminal kinase; LPS: Lipopolysaccharide;
  MAPK: Mitogen-activated protein kinase; MPTP: Mitochondrial permeability transition
  pore; NF-κB: Nuclear factor κB; NK cell: Natural killer cell; NLRP3: Nucleotide-binding
  oligomerization domain, leucine-rich repeat and pyrin domain-containing 3; PI3K:
  Phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase; ROS: Reactive oxygen species; STAT3: Signal transducer
  and activator of transcription 3; THC: Δ-9-trans-tetrahydrocannabinol; TNF-α: Tumor
  necrosis factor-α'
