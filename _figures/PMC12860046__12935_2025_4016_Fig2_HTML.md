caption: Role of lncRNAs in the VEGF and Akt Pathway in RCC. Oncogenic lncRNAs, including
  HOTAIR, TCL6, SPRY4-IT1, ZNF180-2, and GPRC5D-AS1, promote tumor progression by
  activating the Src/Akt pathway, driving EMT, a critical process for metastasis.
  lncRNAs such as DUXAP9 and DANCR activate the Akt-GSK3β-Snail Pathway, leading to
  GSK3β inhibition and Snail stabilization. AGAP2-AS1, MALAT1, HOTAIR, SNHG1, and
  PANTR1 are upregulated in RCC and contribute to tumor angiogenesis, cell survival,
  proliferation, and metastasis via regulating the VEGF and Akt pathways. AGAP2-AS1
  and SNHG1 enhance VEGF signaling. MALAT1 activates VEGF-A/VEGFR and Akt pathways.
  HOTAIR activates PI3K/Akt. PANTR1 regulates VEGF-A expression
