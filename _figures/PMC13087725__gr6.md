  skeletal muscle injuryLIPUS alleviates AECS-induced injury by suppressing ferroptosis.
  This anti-ferroptotic effect of LIPUS is mediated through the coordinated activation
  of the Nrf2/Gpx4 pathway and downregulation of p53. These actions collectively reduce
  oxidative stress and iron-dependent lipid peroxidation, thereby mitigating the accumulation
  of lipid ROS, which eventually triggers ferroptosis. Abbreviations: AECS, acute
