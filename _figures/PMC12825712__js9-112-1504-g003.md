caption: 'HVEPs-induced ICD transiently alters the immunosuppressive TME in PC. PC
  is known as a “cold tumor” due to its immunosuppressive microenvironment. After
  undergoing HVEP ablation, cells in the marginal zone experience regulated cell death,
  while cells in the center of the ablation zone undergo IRE, which induces ICD. The
  death of cells results in the release of DAMPs. On the one hand, DAMP induces imDC
  to migrate to the TdLN, mature, undergo MHC presentation, bind to T cells, and promote
  their differentiation. CD8+ T cells differentiate into CTLs, which release IFN-γ
  and granzyme B to attack tumor cells. HVEP also promotes stromal regulation through
  pathways such as enhancing vascular permeability, softening the stroma, and mitigating
  hypoxia. After the phenomenon that occurs in b, the immunosuppressive microenvironment
  of PC is alleviated, and it transiently becomes a “hot tumor.” Abbreviations: APC:
  antigen-presenting cell; ATP, adenosine triphosphate; CAFs, cancer-associated fibroblasts;
  CD, cluster of differentiation; CRT, calreticulin; CTLs, cytotoxic T lymphocytes;
  DAMP, damage-associated molecular pattern; DC, dendritic cell; HMGB1, high-mobility
  group protein 1; ICD, immunogenic cell death; IFN-γ, interferon-γ; IL, interleukin;
  IRE, irreversible electroporation; MDSC, myeloid-derived suppressor cell; MHC, major
  histocompatibility complex; MVD, micro vessel density; NK cell, natural killer cell;
  RE, reversible electroporation; TAA, tumor-associated antigen; TCR, T cell receptor;
  TLR, toll-like receptor; TME, tumor microenvironment; TNF-α, tumor necrosis factor-α.

  tumors: immune effects induced by HVEP section.)'
