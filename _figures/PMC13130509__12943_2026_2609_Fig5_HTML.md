caption: Emerging biological, engineered, and device-assisted strategies for ICI sensitization.
  Overview of novel approaches beyond conventional pharmacologic agents that aim to
  enhance ICI responsiveness. (1) Cancer vaccination, including personalized neoantigen
  vaccines and viral vector–based systems, promotes tumor antigen presentation and
  primes CD8⁺ T cells. (2) Cell- and gene-based therapies, such as bispecific antibodies,
  CAR-T platforms, and oncolytic or adenoviral vectors, reshape the TIME and sustain
  progenitor-like T-cell populations. (3) Chemically engineered or molecularly targeted
  approaches, including PROTAC-mediated degradation of immune checkpoints (e.g., PD-L1)
  and NR4A1 reprogramming, rewire immunosuppressive pathways to potentiate antitumor
  immunity. (4) Sonodynamic therapy (SDT) employs ultrasound-triggered ROS generation
  to induce immunogenic cell death and enhance local immune infiltration. (5) Gut
  microbiome modulation, via dietary fiber or short-chain fatty acids (SCFAs), regulates
  systemic immune tone and augments ICI efficacy. Collectively, these biological,
  engineered, and biophysical strategies represent promising next-generation modalities
  that complement conventional ICI therapy and may enable more adaptive and safer
  sensitization frameworks
