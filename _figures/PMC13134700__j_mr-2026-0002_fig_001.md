caption: 'Key genetic regulatory pathways in pulmonary hypertension (PH). Genetic
  mechanisms in PH prominently feature two major regulatory axes: (1) disruption of
  the bone morphogenetic protein receptor Type 2 (BMPR2) signaling pathway, characterized
  by impaired ligand–receptor signaling and downstream transcriptional control, leading
  to endothelial dysfunction, smooth muscle cell proliferation, and maladaptive right
  ventricular remodeling. (2) Dysregulation of SOX17 and related angiogenic pathways,
  in which reduced endothelial regulatory capacity and angiogenic gene defects promote
  mitochondrial dysfunction, aberrant vascular cell proliferation or apoptosis, and
  progressive vascular remodeling'
