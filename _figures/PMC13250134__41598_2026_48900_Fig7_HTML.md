caption: Schematic representation of the role of TMEM45B in LC progression through
  the MET signaling pathway. Elevated TMEM45B expression is associated with increased
  MET protein levels and enhanced phosphorylation of downstream effectors AKT and
  ERK. Enhanced MET/AKT/ERK signaling is associated with increased cellular proliferation,
  invasion, and epithelial–mesenchymal transition (EMT) in vitro. In vivo, TMEM45B
  knockdown reduced tumor growth and tumor weight and shifted EMT marker expression
  (decreased vimentin and increased E-cadherin) in xenograft tumors. This model illustrates
  that TMEM45B may drive LC malignancy, at least in part, through MET signaling
