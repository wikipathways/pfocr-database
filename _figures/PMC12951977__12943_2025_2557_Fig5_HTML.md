caption: Potential targets for CSCs-based therapies. CD133 activates Src (phosphorylated
  at Y825) and PI3K (P85/P110), with Src phosphorylating FAK (involved in ubiquitination
  or JNK activation) and PI3K driving mTORC2-dependent phosphorylation of PDK1 and
  AKT (modulated by intracellular pH). CD44 regulates β-catenin signaling through
  HDAC6 and p-Smad2, leading to the translocation of β-catenin to the nucleus to bind
  TCF/LEF, while also influencing TGF-β/Smad2/3/4 signaling to induce EMT-related
  transcription factors (Twist, ZEB, Snail). Notch activation generates NICD, which
  interacts with COT to activate the MEK/ERK pathway; here, ERK phosphorylates MNK
  and c-Jun and cross-talks with p-Smad2. CD24 engages the RAS/RAF/MEK/ERK cascade,
  with RAS cycling between GTP and GDP states to activate Raf, MEK, and ERK. Meanwhile,
  ALDH1 modulates p-Smad2 signaling, and SOX2 activates PI3K (P85/P110) to drive mTORC2,
  PDK1, and AKT activation (influenced by intracellular pH), with phosphorylated AKT
  also targeting c-Jun. Collectively, these cascades, initiated by CSCs-specific markers,
  converge to promote epithelial-mesenchymal transition, transcription factor activation,
  and the sustained stemness of colorectal CSCs
