caption: Jablonski diagram summarizing the decay pathways of near-UV photoexcited
  DNP in aqueous solution. Broad colored arrows indicate the branching for the competing
  pathways following photoexcitation to two different bright states. The fast pathways
  (orange/pink broad arrows) describe relaxation on the singlet manifold and capture
  both relaxation via the S1(ππ*)/S0 ESIPT conical intersection and the S1(nπ*)/S0
  conical intersections. The slow pathway (blue/green arrows) involves intersystem
  crossing into the triplet manifold, deprotonation to form the nitrophenolate anion,
  and reprotonation at a rate determined by solution pH. The rapid electronic deexcitation
  of the photoexcited DNP- anion is shown on the Right. Dashed arrows indicate indirect
  internal conversion pathways, dotted arrows vibrational cooling, and wiggly arrows
  photoexcitation. Although IC and ISC conserve internal energy, they are represented
  with downward-pointing arrows capturing the subsequent relaxation by vibrational
  energy transfer
