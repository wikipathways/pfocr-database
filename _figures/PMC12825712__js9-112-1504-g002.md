caption: 'Apoptosis, necroptosis, and pyroptosis induced by HVEPs. Apoptosis, necroptosis,
  and pyroptosis are recognized as the primary programmed cell death pathways induced
  by HVEPs. Apoptosis has traditionally been viewed as the dominant mechanism, evidenced
  by significantly increased levels of cleaved/active caspases-3, -7, and -9 post-HVEPs.
  Pyroptosis, a regulated inflammatory cell death pathway dependent on cysteine proteases
  (caspases), involves caspase-1 (or −4, −5, −11) activation within the inflammasome.
  Necroptosis, another regulated pathway, is typically initiated by RIPK3 activation,
  which subsequently phosphorylates the key effector MLKL. Abbreviations: DAMP, damage-associated
  molecular pattern; TNF-α, tumor necrosis factor-α. Created with MedPeer (medpeer.Cn).
