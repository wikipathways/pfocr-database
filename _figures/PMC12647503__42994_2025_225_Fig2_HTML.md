caption: Chemical interaction between rice and other organisms within the soil. The
  fungus Gibberella fujikuroi produces gibberellins that affect rice growth. Rice
  strigolactone biosynthesis is largely resolved and studies with mutants demonstrate
  their role in the recruitment of beneficial microbes, including arbuscular mycorrhizal
  fungi (AMF), and are used as a host-presence cue by the parasitic plant Striga.
  Biosynthetic pathway elucidation and mutant generation demonstrated that certain
  diterpenoid oryzalexins, phytocassanes, and momilactones are inhibitors of pathogenic
  fungi and nematodes, as well as being allelopathic and promote recruitment of beneficial
  microbes
