caption: 'Engineering a self-sustaining FBP in plants.(A) Schematic of the engineered
  metabolic pathway. Enzyme origins are indicated by mushroom (fungal) and plantlet
  (plant) symbols. In the native plant phenylpropanoid pathway, caffeic acid is synthesized
  from phenylalanine and serves as the substrate for the heterologous fungal bioluminescence
  cycle. This cycle is catalyzed by four enzymes: hispidin synthase (HispS), hispidin-3-hydroxylase
  (H3H), luciferase (Luz), and caffeylpyruvate hydrolase (CPH). CPH enables substrate
  recycling, thereby establishing a self-sustaining luminescence system that functions
  without exogenous luciferin. Enzyme abbreviations: PAL, phenylalanine ammonia-lyase;
  C4H, cinnamate 4-hydroxylase; 4CL, 4-coumarate: CoA ligase; HCT, hydroxycinnamoyl-CoA:
  shikimate/quinate hydroxycinnamoyltransferase; C3′H, coumaroyl shikimate/quinate
  3′-hydroxylase; CSE, caffeoyl shikimate esterase. Metabolites: Mal-CoA, malonyl-CoA;
  CoA, coenzyme A; NAD(P)H, nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide phosphate.(B) Luminescence
  phenotype of plants expressing the FBP module, demonstrating their capacity to emit
  visible light'
