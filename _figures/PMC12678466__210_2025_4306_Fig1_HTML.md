caption: 'Synthetic pathway and structure of GABAergic neurosteroids. Progesterone
  (PROG), deoxycorticosterone (DOC), and testosterone (TESTO) are metabolized by 5α-reductase
  or 5β-reductase into their intermediates, which are then metabolized by 3α-hydroxysteroid
  dehydrogenase (3α-HSD), respectively, into the following neurosteroids (indicated
  in light blue): 3α,5α-tetrahydroprogesterone (3α,5α-THPROG or allopregnanolone,
  Allo-P), 3α,5β-THPROG (prognanolone, P), 3α,5α-tetrahydrodeoxycorticosterone (3α,5α-THDOC
  or Allo-THDOC), 3α,5β-THDOC (THDOC), and 3α,5α-androstanediol (5α-Adiol). These
  neurosteroids possess a common C3α-OH steroid structure essential for the action
  of GABAA receptors'
