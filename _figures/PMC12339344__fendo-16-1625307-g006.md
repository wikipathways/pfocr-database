caption: Exosomes interfere with DN progression through miRNA intervention. In DN
  patients, urinary exosome-derived miR-615-3p and miR-516b-5p are significantly upregulated.miR-615-3p
  expression positively correlates with Scr, urea, PCR, 24-hour urinary protein, cystatin
  C, and TGF-β1 levels, while negatively correlating with eGFR and albumin. miR-516b-5p
  upregulation significantly increases IL-18, IL-1β, caspase-1, and NLRP3 activity
  while inactivating the SIRT3/AMPK signaling pathway. SIRT3 is a direct target of
  miR-516b-5p, and either SIRT3 overexpression or miR-516b-5p silencing reverses these
  pathological effects. Additionally, urinary exosomal miR-145-5p in DN patients promotes
  podocyte apoptosis by suppressing Srgap2 and subsequently activating the RhoA/ROCK
  pathway. This effect is reversed by either miR-145-5p inhibitors or Srgap2 overexpression
