caption: 'QM directly targets FabD, an essential enzyme that catalyzes the transfer
  of malonyl groups from malonyl-CoA to acyl carrier protein (ACP), initiating fatty
  acid biosynthesis and subsequent mycolic acid production. Inhibition of FabD impairs
  mycolic acid synthesis, disrupting the integrity of the mycobacterial cell wall.
  This structural damage leads to intracellular pH imbalance and proton motive force
  (PMF) collapse, ultimately exerting bactericidal effects. The schematic illustrates
  the Mtb cell envelope, including labeled components (mycolic acid, peptidoglycan,
  and arabinogalactan) and relevant biosynthetic pathways. Arrows indicate regulatory
  effects: sharp-ended arrows denote stimulation or upregulation, while blunt-ended
  arrows represent inhibition or downregulation, with arrows indicating up- or downregulation
  of specific pathways or components'
