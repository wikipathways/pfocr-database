caption: 'Neuroprotective mechanism of Litchi chinensis against Alzheimer''s disease.
  The mechanism was schematically illustrated using Microsoft PowerPoint (2013 version),
  with graphical assets and figures sourced from the following publicly available
  repositories: https://smart.servier.com/, https://www.biorender.com/, https://www.pngwing.com/,
  and https://www.pngegg.com/. The figure illustrates the neuroprotective effects
  of Litchi chinensis on neuronal health through a pathway diagram, showing its influence
  on cellular processes. Litchi chinensis downregulates GSK‐3β/CDK5, reducing tau
  hyperphosphorylation and enhancing synaptic plasticity, while also decreasing APP/BACE1
  activity to lower amyloid‐β production. It upregulates Bcl‐2 and downregulates Bax,
  caspase‐3, and caspase‐9, promoting neuronal survival and inhibiting apoptosis.
  ROS contributes to oxidative and neuronal damage, but Litchi chinensis mitigates
  this by downregulating NF‐κB, which reduces the production of pro‐inflammatory cytokines
  TNF‐α, IL‐1β, and IL‐6, thereby decreasing neuroinflammation and microglia activation.
  The converging pathways highlight a neuroprotective effect, supporting neuronal
  health and function'
