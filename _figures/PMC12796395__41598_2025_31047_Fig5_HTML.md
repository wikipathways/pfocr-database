caption: The promising pathway. The regulation of MITF is complex, with multiple upstream
  pathways converging on MITF; MAPK and PI3K pathways(represented by the right and
  left routes respectively) have some known transcription factors that bind to the
  MITF promoter and regulate its transcription. These two pathways act on the RXRα
  and increase its phosphorylation, which can block the formation of RAR/RXR heterodimers
  that bind specific DNA sequences in RARE or RXRE on MITF. Retinoic Acid (RA, ATRA)
  binds and activates members of the RARα family. α-Mangostin can increase the expression
  of total RXR and RAR and, conversely, inhibit the formation of RAR/RXR heterodimers,
  which may be linked to the phosphorylation levels of RXR
