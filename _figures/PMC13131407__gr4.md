caption: 'Role and molecular mechanisms of mtDNA methylation in atherosclerosis. In
  human umbilical artery VSMCs, PDGF-BB triggers DNMT1 translocation to mitochondria,
  catalyzing 5mC methylation of mtDNA D-loop to suppress respiratory chain genes (ND4,
  COX I, ATP6), reducing ATP synthesis and VSMC contractility to promote atherosclerosis.
  In a mouse carotid ligation model, Glut10 enhances mitochondrial DHA transport,
  converting to vitamin C to activate TET2/3 for 5hmC demethylation of mtDNA D-loop,
  increasing ATP production and oxygen consumption to drive VSMC phenotypic switch
  from synthetic (PCNA/MMP2) to contractile (αSMA/SM22α), inhibiting neointimal formation.
  Squared-shaped VSMCs (synthetic phenotype) suppress nuclear cPLA2, leading to reduced
  activation of the PI3K/AKT signaling pathway and promoting DNMT1 mitochondrial translocation
  for high mtDNA D-loop 5mC methylation, suppressing respiratory chain complex genes
  (I/IV), reducing ATP and contractility to accelerate atherosclerosis. In plaque-associated
  macrophages, METTL4 expression is upregulated, which catalyzes 6mA methylation at
  MT-ATP6 promoter, inhibiting TFAM binding, reducing ATP synthase activity, inducing
  mitochondrial dysfunction and NLRP3 inflammasome activation to exacerbate atherosclerosis.
