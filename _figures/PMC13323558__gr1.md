caption: Lung innervation and inhibitory non-adrenergic non-cholinergic (iNANC)-mediated
  signaling pathways. Schematic overview of neural circuits innervating the lung,
  highlighting sensory afferents and autonomic efferents converging on the pulmonary
  plexus. Parasympathetic efferents arising from the brainstem (medulla oblongata)
  travel via the vagus nerve and relay in parasympathetic ganglia, whereas sympathetic
  efferents originate from the spinal cord and project through thoracic ganglia. Sensory
  inputs return to the central nervous system via vagal ganglia and dorsal root ganglia.
  The inset illustrates signaling in iNANC nerves, where action potential–evoked Ca²⁺
  influx activates neuronal nitric oxide synthase (nNOS) to generate nitric oxide
  (NO) from L-arginine, leading to soluble ornithine cyclase activation, conversion
  of guanosine triphosphate (GTP) to cyclic guanosine monophosphate (cGMP), and downstream
  Ca²⁺-dependent responses in target airway tissues. Carbon monoxide (CO) may also
  participate as a gaseous signaling mediator in nonadrenergic noncholinergic pathways
