caption: Aerobic exercise - mediated Nrg4/STING pathway in regulating hepatic - adipose
  tissue metabolic crosstalk. In adipose tissue (right), exercise triggers growth
  factor binding to EGFR, activating an intracellular cascade that promotes PPARγ
  nuclear translocation where PPARγ then drives Nrg4 transcription and secreted Nrq4
  binds to hepatocyte ErbB4 receptors. In hepatocytes (left), Nrg4-ErbB4 signaling
  activates the PI3K-AKT axis, which phosphorylates and suppresses cGAS activation
  to in turn inhibit the cGAS-STING pathway and downstream IRF3/NF-κB inflammatory
  signaling such as reducing IL − 6, TNF-α, and IL-8, while Nrg4-induced signaling
  also modulates lipogenic pathways through phosphorylated STAT5a binding RXR to inhibit
  LXR-mediated SREBP-1c and Fasn transcription, thereby curbing hepatic lipogenesis
