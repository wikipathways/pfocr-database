caption: 'Mechanisms of tumor aerobic glycolysis regulating DNA damage repair networks
  in bladder urothelial carcinoma aerobic glycolysis enhances DNA repair and chemoresistance
  through four integrated mechanisms. First, antioxidant defense and ferroptosis inhibition:
  PPP-derived NADPH fuels the GSH/GSSG and Trx/TrxR antioxidant systems to reduce
  oxidative DNA damage. G3P and LRRC45 stabilize NRF2 by disrupting KEAP1 binding,
  which promotes SLC7A11 transcription and cystine uptake for GSH synthesis. This
  enables GPX4 to suppress lipid peroxidation and inhibit cisplatin-induced ferroptosis.
  Second, apoptosis suppression: HK2–VDAC1 interaction prevents Bax-mediated DNA fragmentation,
  while nuclear PKM2 suppresses p53-PUMA pro-apoptotic signaling. Third, DNA repair
  pathway enhancement: repair capacity is augmented through ALDOA activation of ATM
  and DNA-PKcs for DSB repair, nucleotide derived from glycolysis precursors for DNA
  synthesis, and lactate-mediated promotion of both homologous recombination via MRE11
  lactylation and non-homologous end joining via HDAC inhibition. Fourth, cell cycle
  checkpoint reinforcement: checkpoint control is strengthened through PKM2-cyclin
  D1/CDK4/6 activation, lactate-induced p21/p27 expression, and ATM-CHK2 signaling
  for damage surveillance. Generated using adobe Illustrator (version 26.3)'
