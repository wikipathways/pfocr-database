caption: Activation mechanism of the TRPM2 ion channel. A. Oxidative stress induces
  DNA damage and activates the PARP/PARG pathway, generating ADPR that binds to the
  MHR1/2 and NUDT9-H domains of TRPM2. B. On binding of ADPR to the channel, trans
  inter-subunit interaction between NUDT9-H and MHR1/2 undergoes conformational changes,
  with a rotation of 27°, priming the channel for activation. Subsequently, binding
  of intracellular Ca2+ near S2-S3 domains and TRP helix further rotates the structure
  by 15°, that causes the TRP H1 helices to tilt and the S6 domain to twist, ultimately
  opening the channel
