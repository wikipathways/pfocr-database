caption: Model for HBsAg folding and secretion pathways(A) HBsAg and Dane particle
  formation under normal conditions. When DNAJB12 and DNAJB14 are present, L- and
  S-HBsAg isoforms are properly folded (here, only L-HBsAg is represented). After
  folding, HBsAg assembles to form either circular SVPs secreted through the ERGIC
  pathway or filamentous SVPs and Dane particles secreted through the MVB pathway.(B)
  HBsAg and Dane particles formation under DNAJB12 or DNAJB14 KD. When DNAJB12 is
  KD, both L- and S-HBsAg isoforms are misfolded and sent to proteasomal degradation,
  leading to no SVP formation and secretion and no Dane particle or infectious HDV
  virion (not represented here) formation and secretion. When DNAJB14 is KD, HBsAg
  isoforms are still properly folded and assemble to form SVPs secreted through the
  ERGIC pathway. Conversely, no Dane particle nor infectious HDV virions (not represented
  here) were observed
