caption: 'Immunological Phenomena with miRNA: Overview of the signalling processes
  involved in DM-related cardiac inflammation. Enhanced leukocyte migration in the
  myocardium is a feature of DM. Pathological stressors such as high blood sugar,
  high cholesterol, increased RAAS, and AGEs cause leukocytes to secrete pro-inflammatory
  chemicals, adhesion molecules, and DAMPs. Additionally, these catalysts cause endothelial
  dysfunction brought on by ROS, which aids in heart remodelling. Pro-inflammatory
  cytokines are secreted, and when they bind to receptors, including the TLR-4-MyD88
  complex, RAGE, and IL-1R, they initiate intracellular signalling cascades. Inflammatory
  cytokines and the NLRP3 inflammasome are transcriptionally upregulated due to the
  activation of NF-κB by these pathways. Inflammasome assembly follows the activation
  of NF-κB and oxidative stress, which trigger the maturation of IL-1β and IL-18 and
  the induction of pyroptosis'
