caption: Mechanisms of the central-peripheral neural axis and local breast innervation
  in regulating breast cancer. A Sympathetic nerves release norepinephrine (NE), which
  binds to β-adrenergic receptors (β-AR) on CD8⁺T cells to activate the cAMP/PKA/CREB
  pathway, inhibiting CD8⁺T cells infiltration; NE also promotes VEGF expression in
  endothelial cells, enhancing angiogenesis and breast cancer cell proliferation.
  B Vagus nerve-derived acetylcholine (ACh) activates α7nAChR on TAMs, suppressing
  the NF-κB pathway to reduce IL-6/IL-1β secretion and promote M2 polarization; cytokines
  from immune cells reciprocally regulate neural activity. C Sensory nerve-secreted
  substance P (SP) binds to TACR1 on breast cancer cells, inducing partial cell death
  and single-stranded RNA release, which activates pro-metastatic genes in surviving
  cells. D Sensory nerve-derived CGRP binds to endothelial cell receptors to promote
  proliferation/migration (facilitating angiogenesis) and increases vascular permeability
  via VE-cadherin phosphorylation, enabling breast cancer cell intravasation and metastasis
