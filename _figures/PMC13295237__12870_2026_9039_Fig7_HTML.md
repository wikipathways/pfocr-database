  metabolism under Cr stress. In each pathway: Black text indicates key metabolites
  at critical nodes of the pathway; Blue text denotes key enzymes involved in upstream
  and downstream processes; Genes regulating the synthesis and metabolism of the corresponding
  enzymes are presented as heatmaps adjacent to each enzyme. The heatmaps display
  expression patterns of these genes, with the left three columns representing control
  samples and the right three columns representing Cr-treated samples. Color intensity
  reflects relative gene expression levels; Core metabolites in each pathway are marked
  with a red star (★). Detailed information regarding gene names, identifiers, and
  expression patterns for each pathway is provided in Table S8 and Table S9'
