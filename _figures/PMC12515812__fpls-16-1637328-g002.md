  remodeling under abiotic stress. The NADPH produced in central carbon metabolism

  with other metabolic pathways demonstrates the flexibility of central carbon metabolism
  under abiotic stress. (A) Glycolysis pathway. (B) Pentose phosphate pathway. (C)
