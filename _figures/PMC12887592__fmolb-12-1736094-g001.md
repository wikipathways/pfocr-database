caption: 'The Wnt signaling pathway was first linked to cancer when the Wnt1 gene
  was found to be activated by the mouse mammary tumor virus in tumor development.
  Aberrant Wnt/β-catenin activation occurs in approximately 90% of sporadic cases
  of CRC, and it has also been associated with other solid tumors, including kidney,
  liver, and gastric cancer. Loss-of-function mutations within genes belonging to
  the destruction complex (APC, AXIN, and AMER1), or gain-of-function mutations in
  CTNNB1, resulted in constitutive signaling. Both the canonical and non-canonical
  pathways of Wnt pathways are involved in tumor growth, invasiveness, and ultimately,
  metastasis (Reactome Pathway ID: R-HSA-4791275)'
