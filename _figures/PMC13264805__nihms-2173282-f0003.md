caption: Blue nodes show a canonical upstream signalling pathway in which adenine
  inhibits AMP-kinase, which results in inhibition of mTORC1 and stimulation of S6
  kinase 1 (S6K1). Activation of s6K1 leads to increased expression of a large number
  of genes. Genes that were upregulated in proximal tubule cells in at least one patient
  with diabetic kidney disease (DKD) were mapped to ribosomal biosynthesis pathways
  (orange)18. This analysis shows that multiple components of the ribosomal complex
  were upregulated in patients with DKD. This upregulation could potentially contribute
  to the cellular hypertrophy and fibrosis that is observed in DKD
