caption: Lipidomic and metabolomic evidence of mitochondrial reprogramming after induction
  of differentiation. (A-E) Each sub-plot shows representative metabolites from specific
  pathways while the arrows show known directionality of metabolic flux between interconnected
  pathways. Glycolysis (A), pentose phosphate pathway (B), lipid β-oxidation (C, D),
  lipid biogenesis (E, F), and TCA cycle (G) show characteristic changes upon induction
  of differentiation. Bar plots show the level of various metabolites in NPCs at different
  time points after induction of differentiation as normalised values to the level
  of these metabolites at T0. The last column in each plot (Dyn/2) shows the level
  of these metabolites in dynarrestin-treated NPCs after 2 h of differentiation relative
  to the level of the metabolites after 2 h of differentiation in control cells. Scatter
  plots show the level of various lipids in NPCs after induction of differentiation
  with an experimental set-up equivalent to that described for the metabolites. n = 3
  biological replicates. (H) Electron photomicrographs (top) show expansion of smooth
  endoplasmic reticulum (right, pseudo-coloured red) in differentiating NPCs. Scale
  bars from left to right 1 μm, 0.2 μm, 0.5 μm. Bottom left inset is a summary of
  metabolic reprogramming of NPCs during differentiation with the metabolic flux from
  the upper glycolysis pathway being shunted to lipogenesis
