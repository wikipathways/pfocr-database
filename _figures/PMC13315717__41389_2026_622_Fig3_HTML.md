caption: Prolonged cytokine stimulation engages central protein mediators—mTORC1,
  MYC, SREBP, and MEF2C—that act as hubs coordinating NK cell metabolic programs.
  These pathways integrate nutrient and cytokine cues to drive glycolysis, mitochondrial
  biogenesis, and the citrate/malate shuttle enabling the increase of mitochondrial
  oxidative phosphorylation (OXPHOS), while regulating amino acid-dependent pathways
  (i.e., leucine, arginine) to sustain mTORC1 activity, lipid biosynthesis, and polyamine
  production. Serine uptake supports one-carbon metabolism and sphingolipid biosynthesis.
  Together, this network enables NK cell proliferation, IFNγ and granzyme B production,
  and cytotoxic function
