caption: Overview of plastidial isoprenoid metabolism. The methylerythritol 4‐phosphate
  (MEP) pathway produces geranylgeranyl diphosphate (GGPP) for carotenoid, chlorophyll,
  tocopherol, plastoquinone and phylloquinone biosynthesis. Some intermediates are
  shared by several pathways. Phytyl diphosphate (phytyl‐PP) for tocopherol and phylloquinone
  biosynthesis can be produced from GGPP reduction (direct pathway) or from the phosphorylation
  of phytol released from chlorophylls (indirect pathway). In leaves, both direct
  and indirect pathways supply phytyl‐PP for phylloquinone biosynthesis, but only
  the indirect pathway is involved in tocopherol biosynthesis under normal conditions.
  Homogentisic acid (HGA) for tocopherol and plastoquinone biosynthesis is produced
  from chorismate, an intermediate that is also used in the phylloquinone biosynthetic
  pathway. Dashed arrows represent multiple steps. The steps targeted by fosmidomycin
  (FSM) and norflurazon (NFZ) are indicated in red. Enzymes are indicated in bold
  and their Arabidopsis accession numbers are listed in Table S1
