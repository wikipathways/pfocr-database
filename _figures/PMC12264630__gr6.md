caption: The multiple potential metabolic pathways of selenite in Rhodococcus qingshengii
  PM1. The sulfate transport system (CysAWTP) facilitates the uptake of Se(IV) from
  the extracellular environment into the cytoplasm. The pentose phosphate pathway
  is activated under selenium treatment, generating NADPH or NADH for selenium reduction.
  NADPH or NADH is then transported to sulfate reductase (CysNDCHIJ) to reduce Se(IV)
  to Se⁰. SeNPs produced in the cytoplasm are released into the extracellular space
  through cell lysis or the efflux ABC transporter. Se(IV) is reduced to Se⁰ through
  various enzymatic reductions, non-enzymatic reductions, or antioxidant enzymes.
  Non-enzymatic reduction of Se(IV) to Se⁰ involves agents like iron siderophores,
  glutathione, and sulfide. Iron-sulfur cluster biosynthesis genes (SufBCDSE) enhance
  the strain's selenium reduction capability
