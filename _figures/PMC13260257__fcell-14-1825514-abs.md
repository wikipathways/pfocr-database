caption: Schematic overview of let-7f functions in the tumor microenvironment, metabolic
  reprogramming, and cancer hallmark suppression (A) Let-7f regulates multiple components
  of the tumor microenvironment (TME), including stromal cells, angiogenesis, immune
  cells, and metabolism (B) Let-7f is also involved in tumor metabolic reprogramming.
  It affects glucose metabolism (ATP production), fatty acid/lipid metabolism, and
  amino acid metabolism, thereby modulating the metabolic adaptation of tumor cells.
  Through these regulatory actions, let-7f influences key signaling pathways (C) and
  directly targets specific genes such as ADAMTS1 and MYH9 (D), ultimately leading
  to the inhibition of tumor cell proliferation, stemness, invasion, and metastasis,
  exerting broad tumor-suppressive effects across various cancer hallmarks
