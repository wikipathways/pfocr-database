caption: Interactions between different AAV serotypes and cell surface glycans. (A)
  AAV2 primarily binds to HSPGs triggering endocytosis via either the clathrin-mediated
  uptake route47 or the clathrin-independent carriers / GPI-anchored-protein-enriched
  endosomal compartment (CLIC/GEEC) pathway35. The preferential method of endocytosis
  depends on the charge of the AAV capsid, with positively-charged capsids binding
  to surface HSPGs and activating clathrin-mediated uptake while neutral capsid AAVs
  prefer the CLIC/GEEC pathway. (B) AAV6 and AAV9 primarily bind to complex glycans
  with expressed SA and galactose, respectively. Galactose is typically located below
  a SA cap within glycans, so a method to expose the sugars is through SA cleavage
  with NM
