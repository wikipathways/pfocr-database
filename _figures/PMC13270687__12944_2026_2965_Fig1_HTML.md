caption: Cav-1 and macrophages. The PPARγ and LXR pathways are activated. Intracellular
  cholesterol accumulation upregulates ABCA1, whose membrane localization and function
  are stabilized by Cav-1, thereby promoting cholesterol efflux to ApoA-I for HDL
  generation. Concurrently, TLR4 and MAMP within the inflammatory milieu can activate
  the NF-κB pathway, a process modulated by Cav-1 via regulating TLR4 signaling. This
  illustrates the pivotal role of Cav-1 at the intersection of cholesterol metabolism
  and inflammatory responses
