caption: Crosstalk Between Lipid Metabolism and Oncogenic Signaling Pathways in CSCs.
  This schematic summarizes how lipid metabolic pathways intersect with key oncogenic
  signaling cascades—including Notch, Wnt, Hippo, and Hedgehog—to regulate cancer
  stem cell (CSC) properties such as self-renewal, therapy resistance, epithelial–mesenchymal
  transition (EMT), and metabolic plasticity. In the Notch pathway, SCD1-mediated
  lipid desaturation supports Notch1–Hes1 signaling, maintains redox balance through
  NADPH production, and enhances FAO via upregulation of CPT1A and ACOX1. Wnt signaling
  is amplified by SCD1-driven MUFA synthesis, which stabilizes LRP5/6 and boosts canonical
  β-catenin signaling, while non-canonical Wnt signaling activates the PI3K–AKT–YAP/TAZ
  axis, contributing to resistance. Hippo signaling is modulated through SCD1-dependent
  lipid composition, which stabilizes nuclear YAP/TAZ activity; feedback from LATS2/p53
  suppresses cholesterol synthesis via SREBP inhibition, and the mevalonate (MVA)
  pathway enhances YAP/TAZ signaling. Hedgehog signaling relies on cholesterol-mediated
  activation of SMO and downstream Gli1 transcription; depletion of cholesterol impairs
  this process, and non-canonical SMO signaling activates AMPK, suppressing lipid
  biosynthetic regulators like FASN and SREBP1. These pathways collectively illustrate
  the mechanistic basis by which lipid metabolism reinforces CSC functions and contributes
  to tumor aggressiveness
