caption: 'The G protein signalling pathway of Neurospora crassa. In N. crassa, the
  G protein-mediated cAMP pathway is essential for vegetative growth and asexual development.
  The pheromone receptors PRE-1 and GPR-1 transmit external signals for sexual development
  via GNA-1, initiating this process. Additionally, Asm-1, the homolog of Aspergillus
  nidulans StuA, is a transcription factor regulating reproductive development in
  N. crassa. GNA-1, acting with its upstream carbon source receptor GPR-4, also plays
  a key role in regulating biomass accumulation during vegetative growth. Gα: GNA-1,
  GNA-2, and GNA-3; Gβ: GNB-1; Gγ: GNG-1; Cr-1 is adenylate cyclase. The dotted line
  represents the speculated result, and the solid line represents the verified result.
