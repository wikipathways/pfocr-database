  WT strain. A Chitin degradation pathway within amino sugar and nucleotide sugar
  metabolism (chitinase, EC 3.2.1.14; β-N-acetylhexosaminidase, EC 3.2.1.52). B D-glucose
  biosynthetic pathway within starch and sucrose metabolism (α-amylase, EC 3.2.1.1;
  β-amylase; EC 3.2.1.2; endo-1,4-β-mannosidase, EC 3.2.1.133; α-glucosidase, EC 3.2.1.3;
  oligo-1,6-glucosidase, EC 3.2.1.10; trehalose-phosphate synthase, EC 2.4.1.35; β-glucosidase,
  EC 3.2.1.21; cellulase, EC 3.2.1.4; cellobiose phosphorylase, EC 2.4.1.20). C Tyrosine
  biosynthesis pathway within isoquinoline alkaloid biosynthesis (tyrosine aminotransferase,
  EC 2.6.1.5; aspartate transaminase, EC 2.6.1.1; tyrosine decarboxylase, EC 4.1.1.80).
  Red indicates genes downregulated at both developmental stages (2 and 6 weeks);
  green, only during the early infection stage (2 weeks); and blue, only during the
  fruiting body formation stage (6 weeks)
