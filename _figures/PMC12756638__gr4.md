caption: The Legionella effector kinases exhibit distinct host-targeting activities.
  LegK1 phosphorylates IκB, resulting in NF-κB pathway activation. LegK2 phosphorylates
  the ARP2/3 complex to inhibit actin polymerization. LegK3 phosphorylates caspase-3,
  caspase-7, and caspase-9, thereby blocking apoptosis and pyroptosis. LegK4 impairs
  global protein synthesis by phosphorylating Hsp70. LegK7 hijacks the Hippo signaling
  pathway via MOB1 phosphorylation. HSP, heat shock protein
