caption: Proposed mechanisms underlying the anti-lymphoma effects of galangin and
  ACA from galanga. Galangin and ACA exert anti-proliferative and pro-apoptotic effects
  on Raji and Daudi lymphoma cells through modulation of multiple signaling pathways.
  Both compounds downregulate c-Myc and p-c-Myc (Ser62), as identified by network
  analysis implicating PI3K-Akt and MAPK signaling as upstream regulators. These molecular
  alterations lead to cell cycle arrest—at the S phase in Raji cells, the G0/G1 phase
  in Daudi cells, and an increase in the sub G1 population in both cell lines–followed
  by activation of apoptosis-related pathways, including aspace-3 (Casp3)-mediated
  apoptosis. Collectively, these events contribute to suppression of lymphoma cell
  growth and induction of programmed cell death
