caption: Pathway for the degradation of Petroleum hydrocarbons by Microbacter sp.
  EMBS2025. The pathway shows the biodegradation of petroleum hydrocarbons by Microbacter
  sp. EMBS2025. Bio-emulsifiers facilitate hydrocarbon uptake, followed by transport
  via ABC transporters. n-Alkanes undergo terminal oxidation by alkB (P450), leading
  to alcohol, aldehyde, and fatty acid formation, which enter β-oxidation to produce
  acetyl-CoA for the TCA cycle. Aromatic hydrocarbons are degraded via dioxygenation
  by benABC, forming diols, which are further processed into catechol and protocatechuate,
  eventually funneling into central metabolism via acetyl-CoA
