caption: Identification of downregulated pain signalling pathways in nerve terminals.
  (A) Dot plot summarizing the top 20 Gene Ontology (GO) biological processes terms
  for downregulated proteins in peripheral nerve terminals of wild type relative to
  NaV1.9 KO mice. The x-axis represents the fold enrichment, and the y-axis represents
  the enriched pathway for biological processes. The colour scale indicates different
  thresholds of the P-value, and the size of the dot indicates the number of genes
  corresponding to each pathway (log2FC ≤ −1, q < 0.05). (B–F) STRING analysis of
  proteins upregulated in wild type relative to NaV1.9 KO nerve terminals. Each node
  represents a protein, and each edge represents an interaction including either physical
  or functional associations. Protein components relevant to mitochondria (B), mRNPs
  (C), ER-mitochondria Ca2+ axis (B, D, E, and F), translational machinery (G). Only
  interactions with a high confidence score (0.7) are shown. KO, knockout
