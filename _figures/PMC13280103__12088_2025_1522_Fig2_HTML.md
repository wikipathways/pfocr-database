caption: When N-acetylglucosamine-1-phosphate synthesis from glucose is inhibited,
  excess glucose enters the glycolytic pathway, leading to increased pyruvate production.
  Pyruvate is converted into acetyl coenzyme A via pyruvate dehydrogenase. Acetyl
  coenzyme A then enters the TCA cycle, generating NADH and FADH2—both of which facilitate
  electron transfer in the respiratory chain and ultimately lead to ATP and H2O production.
  Simultaneously, this process leads to an increase in the generation of ROS as respiratory
  chain byproducts
