caption: 'Molecular mechanisms linking toxic heavy metals to neurological disorders.
  (A). These metals disrupt the blood-brain barrier and trigger multiple molecular
  events, including neuroinflammation mediated by pro-inflammatory cytokines such
  as increased expression of IL and TNF-α, and increased oxidative stress. (B) This
  alters multiple signaling pathways, such as disruption in the AMPK signaling, increase
  NFᴋB, reduce transcriptional factors regulating cellular energy metabolism and mitochondrial
  proteins (such as ULK1, PGC-1α), thus impairing autophagy and promoting neuroinflammation.
  These metals also suppress the SIRT1 pathway, leading to increased deacetylation
  of p53, inhibiting NF-κB, which promotes β-secretase activity, causing the accumulation
  of non-amyloidogenic amyloid precursor protein and misfolded proteins like tau.
  Reactive oxygen species (ROS) induced by heavy metals can oxidize key cysteine residues
  in proteins. This can alter the MAPK signaling pathway by activating upstream kinases
  (MAP3Ks and MAP2Ks), thus enabling them to phosphorylate (P) their downstream targets.
  Metals can modulate the mTOR pathway, leading to decreased autophagy and increased
  Th17 cells that could lead to autoimmune attacks. (C) Alterations in these pathways
  have been implicated to lead to loss of dopaminergic neurons in the substantia nigra,
  amyloid beta formation, and multiple demyelinating plaques, implicated in Parkinson’s
  disease, Alzheimer’s disease, and multiple sclerosis, respectively. (D) Metals such
  as Pb enhance genetic and epigenetic changes, suggesting a mechanism through which
  it may alter gene expression. Abbreviations: AMPK: AMP-activated protein kinase,
  CAT: catalase, cJNK: c-Jun N-terminal Kinase, DNMT1: DNA methyltransferase 1, GPx:
  Glutathione peroxidase, GSH: Glutathione, IL: Interleukins, NF-ᴋB: Nuclear Factor
  kappa B, NAD: Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide, NOX2: NADPH oxidase 2; MAPK: mitogen-activated
  protein kinase; MAPK3s: MAP kinase kinase kinases; MAPK2s: MAP kinase kinases; mTOR:
  mechanistic target of rapamycin, SIRT1: Sirtuin 1; SOD: Superoxide dismutase, PKC:
  Protein kinase C; TNF-α: Tumor Necrosis Factor-alpha, ULK1: Unc-51-like kinase 1'
