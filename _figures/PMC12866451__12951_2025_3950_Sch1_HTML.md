caption: Schematic illustration of MPRH nanoplatform formation and MPRH for cGAS-STING
  pathway activation and anti-tumor immunity. (A) The constructed Mn-ZIF8 nanoparticles,
  loaded with RRX-001 and surface-modified with PEG-HA, form MRPH nanoparticles that
  can degrade in acidic environments to exert anti-tumor effects. (B) MRPH releases
  Mn²⁺ and RRX-001 in the tumor acidic microenvironment, with the latter releasing
  NO to amplify the cGAS-STING pathway activated by Mn²⁺, forming a NO-mtDNA-Mn²⁺
  ternary cascade. This cascade triggers ICD, reduces CD47 expression, and enhances
  macrophage phagocytic function. Additionally, it promotes DC maturation, increases
  CD8⁺ T cell infiltration into tumor tissues, and contributes to tumor vascular normalization,
  thereby achieving the effect of gas-amplified metalloimmunotherapy
