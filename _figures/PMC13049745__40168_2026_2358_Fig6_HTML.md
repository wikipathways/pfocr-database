caption: Summary schematic of early risperidone exposure impairs cognitive function.
  Early risperidone exposure disrupts the gut microbiota (1), leading to alteration
  of bile acid metabolism and tyrosine metabolism (2), thereby attributable to the
  reduction of bile acid metabolite TUDCA and elevation of tyrosine metabolite p-cresol
  in brain, to trigger the ER stress-PTP1B signaling pathway and decrease the synaptic-related
  proteins (3) and thereafter synapse impairment (4) and cognitive impairment (5).
  Therefore, early risperidone exposure leads to an adverse impact on brain function
  by modulating the gut microbiome and bile acids/tyrosine-PTP1B axis. By Figdraw
