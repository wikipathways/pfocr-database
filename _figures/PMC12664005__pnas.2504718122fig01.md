caption: Schematic illustration of the spatiotemporal immunotherapy strategy. (A)
  Using scRNA-seq and spatial transcriptomics of primary and metastatic PDAC samples,
  we identified multiple subpopulations of TAMs as key regulators of disease progression.
  These subpopulations coexpress MRC1 and suppress antitumor immune responses. (B)
  PMMB was developed as an advanced macrophage-reprogramming platform leveraging spatiotemporal
  immune engineering and STING-mediated phase separation. By incorporating a CD80+
  macrophage cell membrane coating and MRC1-targeting peptides, PMMB achieves precise
  delivery to MRC1+ TAMs. BLZ945 in PMMB modulates CSF-1R signaling in MRC1+ TAMs,
  potentially driving their phenotypic shift toward CD80+ TAMs. Concurrently, H2O2
  triggers MnO2 degradation, liberating Mn2+, which activates the STING pathway and
  induces IFN production. Mn2+ induces STING phase separation, mitigating excessive
  inflammation and immune toxicity. This spatiotemporal immunotherapy strategy synergistically
  combines CSF-1R inhibition and STING activation, leading to sustained TAM reprogramming
  and robust antitumor immune responses
