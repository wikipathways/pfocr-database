caption: 'Schematic model of the mechanism by which l-lactate drives lysosomal dysfunction
  and GSDME-dependent pyroptosis via ATP6V1B2 lactylation. HDM induces intracellular
  l-lactate accumulation, triggering lactylation at ATP6V1B2 K108/K109 sites, leading
  to disruption of V-ATPase assembly and lysosomal membrane permeabilization (LMP).
  LMP initiates two downstream pathways: (1) lysosomal Cathepsin B leakage activates
  the tBid-Bax axis, resulting in mitochondrial damage; (2) initiation of a non-canonical
  Caspase-8/3 cascade reaction, specifically cleaving GSDME to execute pyroptosis,
  thereby exacerbating airway inflammation'
