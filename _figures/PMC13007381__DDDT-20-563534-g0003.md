caption: Mechanism of Ferroptosis in CIRI. Ischemia and hypoxia activate HIF-1α, which
  promotes TfR1-mediated iron uptake. Upon reperfusion, ferritinophagy and BBB disruption
  synergistically cause intracellular iron overload, providing an abundant substrate
  for the Fenton reaction and leading to ROS generation.97–99 Restoration of blood
  flow during reperfusion further induces massive ROS production through mitochondrial
  dysfunction, activation of XO and PLA2, and neutrophil infiltration.100,101 ROS
  then drives lipid peroxidation via enzymatic pathways, such as the oxidation of
  PUFAs by ALOX, and non-enzymatic mechanisms, such as the Fenton reaction.102–104
  Concurrently, ATP depletion disrupts ionic homeostasis and suppresses System xc-mediated
  cystine uptake, thereby impairing GSH synthesis and reducing GPX4 activity. Consequently,
  lipid peroxides cannot be effectively cleared, culminating in ferroptosis.105–107
