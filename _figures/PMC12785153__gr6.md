caption: 'SlBES1 and SlBZR1 function differently in flavonoid and carotenoid pathways,
  as revealed by genome-wide RNA-seq and DAP-seq analyses.Color blocks with distinct
  backgrounds represent separate pathways. Both carotenoid and flavonoid metabolic
  pathways originate from phosphoenolpyruvate in the shikimate pathway. Carotenoid
  biosynthesis proceeds through the plastidial MEP pathway, which supplies the C5
  precursor metabolites isopentenyl diphosphate (IPP) and dimethylallyl diphosphate
  (DMAPP). The flavonoid pathway uses NarCh as its precursor, derived from phenylalanine.
  Shikimate pathway: G3P, glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate. MEP pathway: MEP, methylerythritol
  4-phosphate; GGPP, geranylgeranyl diphosphate; DXS, 1-deoxy-D-xylulose 5-phosphate
  synthase; GGPPS, GGPP synthase. Carotenoid pathway: PSY1, phytoene synthase 1; PDS,
  phytoene desaturase; ZISO, ζ-carotene isomerase; ZDS, ζ-carotene desaturase; CRTISO,
  carotenoid isomerase; LCY-E, lycopene εε-cyclase; LCY-B, lycopene β-cyclase. Flavonoid
  pathway: PAL, phenylalanine ammonia-lyase; 4CL, 4-coumarate:CoA ligase; CHS1, chalcone
  synthase 1; CHS2, chalcone synthase 2; CHI, chalcone isomerase; F3H, flavanone-3-hydroxylase;
  FLS, flavonol synthase; F3′H, flavonoid 3′-hydroxylase. Transcriptional regulators:
  SlBZR1, SlBES1, and SlMYB12. Small circles represent differentially expressed genes
  co-regulated by SlBES1 and SlBZR1, identified through genome-wide RNA-seq and DAP-seq
  analysis. From left to right, the three circles indicate gene expression ratios
  of bzr1:WT at the MG stage, bes1:WT at the B stage, and bzr1 bes1:WT at the P (pink)
  stage. Red shading of the circles denotes upregulated expression, while blue indicates
  downregulation'
