caption: Wnt/β-catenin signaling pathway regulation of HCC biological behaviors. (A)
  Biomolecules targeting the Wnt/β-catenin signaling pathway modulate HCC proliferation,
  invasion, and apoptosis. (B) Role of Wnt/β-catenin signaling pathway in immunity.
  Wnt ligands within the tumor microenvironment impair DC function, thereby suppressing
  the priming and activation of CD8+ cytotoxic T cells and preventing their anti-tumor
  activity. β-catenin in CD8+ T cells reduces phospholipase C-gamma 1 activity, which
  is essential for T cell activation. Furthermore, Wnt/β-catenin activation in CD4+
  regulatory T cells (Tregs) induces RORγt expression, promoting immune evasion. (C)
  Contribution of Wnt/β-catenin signaling pathway to tumer resistance
