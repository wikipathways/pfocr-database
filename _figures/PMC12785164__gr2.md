caption: An updated metabolic network for biosynthesis of the high-value compound
  fucoxanthin in Phaeodactylum tricornutum.Strategies, including precursor enhancement
  via overexpression of MEP-pathway and carotenogenic genes, PAP-mediated xanthophyll
  sequestration, and manipulation of transcriptional regulators, have been used to
  improve fucoxanthin accumulation. The updated biosynthetic network reflects recent
  advances, although critical catalytic steps remain to be characterized. Engineered
  enzymes (highlighted in red) enhance fucoxanthin biosynthesis. Dashed lines indicate
  steps in which the enzymes are uncharacterized. MEP, methylerythritol phosphate
  pathway; GGPP, geranylgeranyl diphosphate; PSY, phytoene synthase; PDS, phytoene
  desaturase; ZDS, ζ-carotene desaturase; CRTISO, carotenoid cis/trans isomerase;
  LCYB, lycopene cyclase; BCH, carotene hydroxylase; ZEP, zeaxanthin epoxidase; VDE,
  violaxanthin de-epoxidase; VDL, violaxanthin de-epoxidase-like protein; ABA4, function-unknown
  protein in P. tricornutum; PAP, plastid lipid-associated protein; HSF, heat shock
  factor; MED, mediator subunit; CryP, cryptochrome
