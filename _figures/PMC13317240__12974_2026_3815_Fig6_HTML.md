caption: 'Therapeutic modalities and CNS translational constraints of cGAS–STING targeting.
  A Interventions are organized along the canonical cGAS–STING cascade by druggable
  nodes, including upstream DNA-stress control, cGAS catalytic inhibition, ENPP1 targeting,
  STING pocket antagonism or trafficking blockade, inhibition of palmitoylation-dependent
  signaling, and enhancement of termination pathways. Representative compounds or
  approaches are listed for each modality, and a delivery bar highlights enabling
  technologies for CNS exposure. B Major barriers to translation include blood–brain
  barrier penetration, disease-stage dependency, dose-limiting toxicities, risks of
  prolonged innate immune suppression, dependence on baseline neuroinflammatory state,
  and human STING allelic variation. C Conceptual intervention windows are aligned
  to disease progression rather than development phase. Early-stage strategies emphasize
  source control; mid-stage strategies prioritize pathway-node inhibition to dampen
  glial amplification; late-stage strategies focus on limiting propagation and restoring
  homeostasis. These stage-linked windows are intended as conceptual hypotheses rather
  than treatment recommendations, and should be interpreted as context- and biomarker-dependent.Abbreviations:
  BBB, blood–brain barrier; ENPP1, ectonucleotide pyrophosphatase/phosphodiesterase
  1; ER, endoplasmic reticulum. Created with BioRender.com'
