caption: 'Schematic of serotonin (5-HT) signaling pathways in triple-negative breast
  cancer (TNBC). This figure depicts the autocrine/paracrine effects of 5-HT in TNBC.
  5-HT is synthesized by TNBC cells (via upregulated TPH1) and released by platelets,
  then taken up by TNBC cells via the serotonin transporter (SERT) to form an autocrine
  loop. 5-HT binds to 5-HT₇ receptors on TNBC cells, activating two major pathways:
  the JAK1/STAT3/ERK1/2 pathway promotes glycolysis, while the adenylate cyclase/PKA
  pathway enhances mitochondrial biogenesis, both supporting tumor proliferation and
  energy metabolism. 5-HT₇ signaling also upregulates cathepsin S (CTSS) via HSP90/PI3K/Akt/NF-κB,
  facilitating extracellular matrix (ECM) remodeling and breast cancer stem cell (BCSC)
  self-renewal. In the immune microenvironment, 5-HT modulates T cell cytokine secretion
  and macrophage phagocytosis via specific receptors. Selective serotonin reuptake
  inhibitors (SSRIs) block SERT, altering extracellular 5-HT levels to affect tumor
  progression'
