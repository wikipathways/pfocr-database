caption: (LEFT) Wild-type p53 induces the transcription of mir-34a, a small non-coding
  RNA that negatively regulates the transcription of the WNT genes. The oncogene WNT
  is produced when both alleles of TP53 are mutant and cell division and tissue destruction
  result in the induction of cytokines such as IL-1b. This leads to the accumulation
  of γδ and Th-17 T-cells, which secrete IL-17 and IL-1b. This tumor microenvironment
  influences pre-adipocytes to undergo reprogramming into CAAs and increases the number
  of MDSCs. Collectively, this induces an inflammatory state. Cancerous cells that
  have TP53 mutations can be reprogramed by demethylation CpG dinucleotides permitting
  the expression of Alu and LINE-1 RNA sequences, activating the gas-sting pathway
  resulting in interferon production. (Right side of figure). CAA cancer-associated
  adipocyte, IL interleukin, MDSC myeloid-derived suppressor cell
