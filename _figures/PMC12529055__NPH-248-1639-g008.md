caption: 'Li+ stress‐induced Ca2+ signalling simultaneously blocks Li+ uptake and
  induces removal of cytosolic Li+. (a) Ca2+ blocks Li+ uptake through nonselective
  cation channels (NSCCs) during Li+ stress. Blunt‐ended arrow represents Ca2+ inhibition
  of NSCCs, purple arrow represents Li+ transport, and black arrow with red cross
  indicates blocked transport. (b) Salt Overly Sensitive 1 (SOS1) is activated by
  the Ca2+‐mediated SOS pathway: SOS3 binds SOS2 after sensing Ca2+, SOS2 activates
  SOS1 by phosphorylation, and SOS1 then likely exports Li+ out of the cell. The thick
  arrow represents SOS1 phosphorylation by SOS2 using ATP. White and purple arrows
  represent H+ and Li+ direction of transport, respectively. (c) Extracellular Li+
  is sensed by glycosyl inositol phosphorylceramide (GIPC) sphingolipids, whose production
  requires Monocation Activated [Ca2+] Increases 1 (MOCA1). These GIPC sphingolipids,
  after interacting with Li+, activate unidentified plasma membrane Ca2+ channels
  to start cytosolic Ca2+ signalling. Thick, faint arrow represents Ca2+ transport,
  and black arrow with red cross indicates blocked transport. Arrows from Ca2+ to
  (a) and (b) represent that, after entering the cell through these channels, Ca2+
  has those roles in Li+ stress signalling. Created in BioRender (https://BioRender.com/amz28p4)'
