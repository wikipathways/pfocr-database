caption: Cell type‐specific sesquiterpene biosynthesis in Artemisia argyi leaves.
  (a) Quantification of three sesquiterpenoids in GTs and NGTs. (b) Expression profiles
  of genes in the TPS‐a subfamily genes across different cell types in A. argyi leaves.
  The functionally characterised genes in this study are highlighted in red. (c) Co‐expression
  network of MVA pathway and TPS‐a genes in GT, with edges indicating strong correlations
  (r > 0.5). Yellow and blue denote positive and negative correlations, respectively.
  (d) ML phylogenetic tree constructed using protein sequences of the candidate TPSs
  and functionally characterised TPSs. (e–g) GC–MS chromatograms showing the enzymatic
  products of AarTPS52 (e), AarTPS7 (f), AarTPS95 and AarTPS96 (g). 1, β‐elemene;
  2, β‐caryophyllene; 3, α‐guaiene; 4, Unknown1; 5, (E)‐β‐farnesene; 6, α‐humulene;
