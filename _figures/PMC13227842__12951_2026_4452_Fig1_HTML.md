caption: Schematic illustration of the Fe₃O₄@MnSiO₃-based vaccine platform fabricated
  through carrier synthesis, antigen loading, and functionalization. Upon subcutaneous
  injection and uptake by antigen-presenting cells, the nanovaccine releases Cap protein
  and Mn²⁺ within acidic lysosomes. Mn²⁺ activates the cGAS-STING pathway, boosting
  type I interferon production and facilitating dendritic cell (DC) maturation and
  antigen presentation. Processed antigens are presented via MHC-I and MHC-II to activate
  CD8⁺ and CD4⁺ T cells, respectively, eliciting humoral and cellular immune responses.
  These responses drive the generation of specific antibodies and sensitized T lymphocytes,
  which collectively clear virus-infected cells and establish potent immune protection
