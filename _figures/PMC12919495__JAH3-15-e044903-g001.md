caption: The de novo synthesis of Cer16:0 promotes IR through multiple mechanisms.
  It activates PPs (eg, PP1 or PP2A), leading to AKT dephosphorylation, and stimulates
  PKCζ to impair AKT phosphorylation, collectively suppressing the PI3K/AKT signaling
  pathway. Activation of the MAPK pathway further exacerbates IR. IR disrupts the
  MSRN, resulting in impaired cholesterol efflux, foam cell formation, and subsequent
  atherosclerosis progression. Additionally, IR reduces hepatic VLDL secretion, further
  accelerating atherosclerosis development. × represents inhibition. atherosclerosis

  protein phosphatase; and VLDL, very low‐density lipoprotein
