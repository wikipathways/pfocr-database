caption: Schematic representation of the CXCR12/CXCR4 signaling pathway. Upon CXCL12
  binding, CXCR4 activates G protein–dependent signaling pathways including PI3K/AKT,
  Ras/RAF/ERK, PLC/Ca2+/PKC, and Rho GTPases (Rac, Rho, Cdc42), regulating cell survival,
  migration, and chemotaxis. It also inhibits adenylate cyclase (AC) to reduce cAMP.
  CXCR4 can additionally signal via G protein–independent pathways such as JAK/STAT
  and GRK/β-arrestin. This figure was drawn by Figdraw
