caption: 'Roles of parthanatos in atherosclerosis (AS). In vascular endothelial cell
  (VEC)/vascular smooth muscle cell (VSMC)/foam cell, diverse stimuli cause DNA extensive
  damage, poly (adenosine diphosphate (ADP)-ribose) polymerase 1 (PARP1) overactivation
  and NAD+-depended poly (ADP-ribose) (PAR) formation. This accumulation subsequently
  facilitates the translocation of PAR, promoting the release of apoptosis-inducing
  factor (AIF) from the mitochondria into cytoplasm, where AIF combines with macrophage
  migration inhibitory factor (MIF), and the AIF/MIF complex translocates into the
  nuclear, resulting in chromatin condensation, extensive DNA fragmentation and parthanatos.
  Moreover, activated PARP1 upregulates arginase II (Arg II), which competes with
  endothelial nitric oxide synthase (eNOS), causing decreased nitric oxide (NO) production
  and VEC dysfunction. In macrophages, under hyperglycemic conditions, PARP1 mediates
  calcification and inflammation by promoting VSMC phenotype transition from a contractile
  to a synthetic phenotype,and macrophages differentiation into pro-inflammatory and
  osteoblast-like types via signal transducer and activator of transcription 1 (STAT1)
  -Runt-related transcription factor 2 (RUNX2) pathway. ROS: reactive oxygen species;
  ox-LDL: oxidized low-density lipoprotein; Ang II: angiotensin II; TNF-α: tumor necrosis
  factor-α; NF-κB: nuclear factor-κB'
