caption: 'TRP metabolite-driven neuroplasticity imbalance: a dual modulatory network
  in brain disorders. Upper regulatory axis: Neurotoxic metabolites (QUIN, 3-HK, XA)
  induce mitochondrial apoptosis and autophagy suppression via oxidative stress, mGlu2/3
  inhibition, and ETC disruption. Conversely, protective agents (KYNA, IL-4/BDNF,
  IPA/IAA) counterbalance toxicity through anti-inflammatory signaling, ROS scavenging,
  and synaptic/NSC regeneration. This bidirectional tension governs neuronal fate.
  Lower regulatory axis: Astrocyte (AC) dysfunction (e.g., AQP4 depolarization→Ca²?
  overload) and disrupted ERK1/2-mTOR-PPARγ pathways impair hippocampal autophagy/plasticity,
  amplifying neuronal damage. Collectively, a “metabolite–glia–neuron” cascade drives
  neurodegeneration through convergent oxidative, inflammatory, and bioenergetic failure.
  Neural stem cells: NSC; metabotropic glutamate: mGlu; major depressive disorder:
  MDD; Alzheimer’s disease: AD; mammalian/rapamycin mechanistic target: mTOR; brain-derived
  neurotrophic factor: BDNF; tumor necrosis factor alpha: TNF-α; Interleukin: IL;
