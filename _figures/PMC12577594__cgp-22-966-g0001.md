caption: Schematic model illustrating the cooperative regulation of EGFR signaling
  by SCAMP3 and EPS8 in enzalutamide-resistant prostate cancer cells. Upon EGF stimulation,
  EGFR forms a complex with EPS8 and SCAMP3, promoting downstream activation of STAT3,
  AKT, and ERK signaling pathways. This effect is more prominent in enzalutamide-resistant
  LNCap (LNCap–Enz-R) cells compared to parental LNCap cells. Genetic inhibition using
  shRNA targeting SCAMP3 or EPS8 suppresses this pathway, whereas overexpression via
  pcDNA transfection enhances EGFR signaling activity. These findings suggest that
  SCAMP3 and EPS8 act synergistically to modulate EGFR-mediated resistance signaling
  in advanced prostate cancer
