caption: Schematic representation of RNA-binding proteins (RBPs) regulating the Hippo
  pathway. RBP MSI 2 binds to the transcripts of MST 1/2, SAV1, and LATS 1/2 and decreases
  their stability and stimulates their degradation. Also, this RBP binds to Mob1 transcripts
  and inhibits their translation. Thereby, MSI2 inhibits cell proliferation and survival
  in cancer cells by suppressing the Hippo pathway. RBP QKI5 inhibits TAZ expression
  by regulating the stability and splicing of TAZ transcripts. RBPs YTHDF1 and ALKBH5
  promote YAP stability and translation, thereby stimulating the Hippo pathway. RBP
  RBFOX2 increases the expression of TEAD1 by regulating its splicing. Activated Hippo
  pathway causes the transcription of genes involved in tumor behaviors such as proliferation,
  invasion, apoptosis, etc
