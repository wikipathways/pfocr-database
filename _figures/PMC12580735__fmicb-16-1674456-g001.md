caption: Flowchart of the mechanism by which mesenchymal stem cells inhibit Pseudomonas
  aeruginosa through ERK-GM-CSF immune signaling. The upregulation of CD69 expression
  in adipose tissue-derived mesenchymal stem cells (ASCs) was markedly enhanced during
  the infection by Pseudomonas aeruginosa, which subsequently enabled CD69 to recognize
  Pseudomonas aeruginosa. Following this recognition, the activation of the ERK1 pathway
  by CD69 led to increased secretion of granulocyte-macrophage colony-stimulating
  factor (GM-CSF) from ASCs, ultimately contributing to the mitigation of Pseudomonas
  aeruginosa pneumonia
