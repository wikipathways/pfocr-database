caption: Signaling pathway of BDNF-OE-OEC-Exos crossing the BBB to inhibit neuronal
  ferroptosis. BDNF-overexpressing olfactory ensheathing cell-derived exosomes (BDNF-OE-OEC-Exos)
  deliver BDNF and miR-21 across the BBB. BDNF binds TrkB receptors, activating PI3K-AKT.
  miR-21 inhibits PTEN, enhancing AKT activation. AKT phosphorylates and inhibits
  AMPK, preventing its over-activation. AKT also phosphorylates BECN1 at Ser234, reducing
  its pro-autophagic activity and releasing inhibition of SLC7A11. These events restore
  System Xc⁻ activity, increasing cysteine uptake for GSH synthesis, supporting GPX4
  function and suppressing ferroptosis
