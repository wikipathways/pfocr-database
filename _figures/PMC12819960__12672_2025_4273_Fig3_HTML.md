caption: Mechanisms of immune modulation through SPP1-receptor interactions in the
  tumor microenvironment. Blocking antibodies targeting CD44, integrin, PD-L1, and
  VEGFR impact SPP1-mediated signaling pathways. Inhibition of CD44 or integrin reduces
  SPP1-receptor interactions, leading to decreased SPP1 signaling. Lower PD-L1 expression
  correlates with reduced SPP1 expression, resulting in a shift from M2 to M1 macrophage
  polarization. VEGFR blockade decreases angiogenesis and MMP9 secretion, limiting
  tumor proliferation, invasion, and migration. Increased IFN-γ enhances CXCL9 expression,
  which suppresses SPP1 expression and modulates the JAK-STAT signaling pathway. Additionally,
  IFN-γ upregulates T-bet, thereby promoting Th1 cell differentiation
