caption: 'Red line: pro-inflammatory pathway; Green line: anti-inflammatory pathway;
  Blue line: PI3K/Akt signaling pathway inhibition. Diagram of TNF-α signaling pathway.
  After TBI, the innate immune response elevates TNF-α expression [36]– [37]. TNF-α
  binds to TNF-R1: can activate caspase8/FADD, RIPK1/RIPK3/MLKL [54], classical NF-κB
  [43], MAPK/JNK/p38 signaling pathways, leading to apoptosis and necrosis. TNF-α
  binding to TNF-R2: activates PI3K/Akt and non-classical NF-κB signaling pathways
  [57], the former inhibits caspase8/FADD and RIPK1/RIPK3/MLKL signaling pathways,
  which can be anti-inflammatory, anti-apoptotic, and enable cell survival [59]'
