caption: Bidirectional interaction between Trp metabolism and gut microbiota. In the
  gastrointestinal system, gut microbiota regulate Trp metabolism through two primary
  pathways. These include direct catabolism of Trp into indole and its derivatives,
  and modulation of Trp flux via microbial metabolites like SCFAs and LPS, which affect
  enzymes such as IDO1. Conversely, Trp and its metabolites reciprocally shape the
  composition of gut microbial communities. The extra-intestinal regulatory effects
  of gut microbiota-mediated Trp metabolism involve multiple mechanisms. These include
  vagal nerve stimulation for neural signaling, AhR-mediated modulation of T cell
  differentiation, mitigation of neuronal damage by controlling neurotoxic metabolites
  like QA, and systemic effects through the circulatory spread of microbial Trp-derived
  metabolites
