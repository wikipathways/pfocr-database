caption: 'Mechanism of angiogenesis inhibition: The figure illustrates the pathways
  targeted by angiogenesis antagonists, such as angiostatin, axitinib, bevacizumab,
  ranibizumab, semaxanib, and everolimus. Angiostatin blocks ATP synthase, angiomotin,
  and integrin (αvβ3), leading to inhibition of ATP production, cell migration, adhesion,
  and promotion of endothelial cell apoptosis. Axitinib inhibits vascular endothelial
  growth factor (VEGFR)−1, VEGFR-2, and VEGFR-3 tyrosine kinases, suppressing angiogenesis.
  Bevacizumab, ranibizumab, and everolimus degrade VEGF, blocking VEGF receptor activation,
  with everolimus additionally inhibiting the mammalian target of rapamycin (mTOR)
  pathway to suppress cell proliferation. Semaxanib specifically inhibits VEGFR-2,
  leading to suppression of angiogenesis. Created with Biorender'
