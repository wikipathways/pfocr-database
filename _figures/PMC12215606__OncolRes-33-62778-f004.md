caption: 'Mechanistic view of garlic against breast cancer: Garlic extract fights
  against breast cancer by inducing apoptosis via DNA fragmentation and lowering S
  phase occupancy, which inhibits DNA synthesis. It triggers cell cycle checkpoints
  and apoptotic pathways, comprising the Bax, cytochrome c, and caspase cascades,
  resulting in cell death. Furthermore, garlic extract alters the epigenetic landscape
  by suppressing histone deacetylases, which increases anti-cancer gene expression
  [Bax = Bcl-2-associated X protein, APAF-1 = Apoptotic Protease Activating Factor
