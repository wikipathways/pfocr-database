caption: 'Prediction of miRNA‐mediated regulation of the IFN Beta node and regulators
  over time in response to IFNβ‐1a and pegIFNβ‐1a. (A) Longitudinal activation of
  the IFN Beta node (i.e., role of IFNβ and IL‐6 together) or regulatory network over
  time using miRNA‐regulated protein targets and their response to IFNβ‐1a treatment
  over time. Maximum activation (yellow colored star symbol) by IFNβ‐1a was observed
  at 0.33, 1.33, 4, and 5 days, respectively. (B) Graphical illustration of the IFN
  Beta node or in this instance the IFNβ‐1a product (plus IL‐6) as the master regulator
  and 18 other regulators with specific activation states as predicted by the protein
  targets of one or more of the 10 miRNAs differentially expressed by IFNβ‐1a treatment.
  For the 10 IFNβ‐1a impacted miRNAs: miR‐21‐5p was predicted to regulate the expression
  of TNF, IL1B, STAT3, and NFKB (complex); miR‐223‐3p regulates the expression of
  STAT3, RELA, and IL1B; miR‐101‐3p regulates the expression of EZH2, HIF1A, and IL1B;
  miR.27a.3p regulates the expression of IFNG, JUN, and MYC; and miR.423.5p regulates
  the expression of IRF1, CEBPB, and NATC2. (C) Longitudinal activation of the IFNβ
  group node and regulatory network over time using miRNA‐regulated protein targets
  and their response to pegIFNβ‐1a treatment with significant and strong activation
  (z‐score > 2) occurring from 0.67 days (16 h) through 13 days. The maximum activation
  (yellow colored star symbol) by pegIFNβ‐1a was observed at 13 days. (D) Graphical
  illustration of the IFN Beta node or in this instance pegIFNβ‐1a product (plus IL‐6)
  as the master regulator and 22 other regulators with specific activation states
  as predicted by the protein targets of one or more of the 13 miRNAs differentially
  expressed by pegIFNβ‐1a treatment. Of the 13 pegIFNβ‐1a impacted miRNAs, miR‐223‐5p
  was predicted to regulate the expression of TNFSF10 and STAT1, while miR‐223‐3p
  regulated the expression of STAT3 and STAT1. Figures were generated using QIAGEN
  Ingenuity Pathway Analysis (IPA). CEBPB, CCAAT enhancer binding protein beta; CITED2,
  Cbp/p300 interacting transactivator with Glu/Asp rich carboxy‐terminal domain 2;
  CTNNB1, catenin beta 1; EZH2, enhancer of zeste 2 polycomb repressive complex 2
  subunit; FOS, Fos proto‐oncogene; FOXO1, forkhead box O1; HIF1A, hypoxia inducible
  factor 1 subunit alpha; IFN Beta, interferon‐beta 1; IFNG, interferon gamma; IL1B,
  interleukin 1 beta; IRF1, interferon regulatory factor 1; IRF9, interferon regulatory
  factor 9; JUN, jun proto‐oncogene; MAPK1, mitogen‐activated protein kinase 1; MYC,
  MYC proto‐oncogene; NFATC2, nuclear factor of activated T cells 2; NFKB, nuclear
  factor kappa B; NFKB1, nuclear factor kappa B subunit 1; NFKBIA, NFKB inhibitor
  alpha; NR3C1, nuclear receptor subfamily 3 group C member 1; NR4A1, nuclear receptor
  subfamily 4 group A member 1; PPARG, peroxisome proliferator activated receptor
  gamma; RELA, RELA proto‐oncogene; STAT1, signal transducer and activator of transcription
  1; STAT2, signal transducer and activator of transcription 2; STAT3, signal transducer
  and activator of transcription 3; TNF, tumor necrosis factor; TNFSF10, TNF superfamily
  member 10; TP53, tumor protein p53; VDR, vitamin D receptor'
