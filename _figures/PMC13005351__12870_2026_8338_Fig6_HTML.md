caption: 'Integrated transcriptomic and metabolomic analysis of plant hormone signal
  transduction pathways following CaCl₂ treatment. The left panel demonstrates endogenous
  hormone levels including abscisic acid (ABA), total gibberellin (GA), zeatin, indole-3-acetic
  acid (IAA), jasmonic acid (JA), and salicylic acid (SA) across treatment groups.
  * P < 0.05, ** P < 0.01, ns = not significant (Student’s t-test). The main panel
  displays hormone signal transduction pathways for eight hormones: ethylene, ABA,
  GA, cytokinin, auxin, JA, SA, and brassinosteroid. Colored heatmaps beneath each
  pathway represent the transcript abundance of corresponding genes across treatment
  groups. Symbols indicate regulatory mechanisms: “ + P” = phosphorylation; “ + u” = ubiquitination;
  “⊣” = inhibition. The right panel shows key physiological processes regulated by
  each hormone pathway. The top-right insert shows metabolite abundance of S-adenosylmethionine
  (AdoMet) and methionine (precursors in plant hormone biosynthesis) across treatment
  groups. Gene abbreviations in the hormone signaling pathways follow standard plant
  nomenclature. Full gene names are provided in Table S5'
