caption: Schematic illustration of the mechanism by which a CAR T-cell e.g., anti-CD19,
  recognises and eliminates CD19 (+ve) B-cells. Binding of anti-CD19 CAR T-cell to
  CD19 on the targeted B-cell, triggers CAR T-cell activation. Activation results
  to the release of perforins and granzyme B. Perforins form pores on the B-cell-membrane,
  facilitating Granzyme B entry into the cell, which in-turn activates intrinsic apoptotic
  pathways. Engagement of the FASL on the CAR T-cell with FASR on the B-cell activates
  apoptotic pathways via the receptor-mediated extrinsic pathway [20]. “Created in
