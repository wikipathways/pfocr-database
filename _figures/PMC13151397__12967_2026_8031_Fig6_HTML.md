caption: CeY-B1 induces apoptosis in SiHa and HeLa cells via ceramide accumulation
  and JNK signaling pathway. CeY-B1 treatment leads to increased ceramide levels in
  SiHa cells, which in turn activates the JNK signaling pathway. Phosphorylated JNK
  promotes mitochondrial dysfunction by downregulating BCL-2 expression. At the same
  time, the drug reduces PI3K expression and inhibits AKT phosphorylation, and the
  two effects work together to induce cell apoptosis. This schematic diagram shows
  the possible mechanism by which CeY-B1 inhibits the PI3K/AKT pathway and links apoptosis
  through ceramide accumulation and JNK pathway activation. (Created with BioRender.com)
