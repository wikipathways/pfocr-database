caption: Receptor–ligand interactions (gray arrows) of immune checkpoints from the
  TNF-TNFR superfamily (A) and other protein families (B) between T cells or NK cells
  and tumor cells or TAM. In most cases, these interactions induce co-stimulatory
  signals (green arrows) predominantly via NF-κB pathway. Immune checkpoints from
  other families could influence TCR signaling (black arrows) or maintain functions
  of immunosuppressive cells (green arrows). Multiple negative effects of kynurenine
  on APC functions are shown by black arrows with red circles. BTLA and LAG-3, which
  interact with HVEM and Gal-3, respectively (gray dotted arrows), belong to IgSF
  and are presented in Figure 1
