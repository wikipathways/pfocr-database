caption: Inositide-dependent signaling pathways. A Phospholipase Cβ (PLCβ) is activated
  by the Gαq, hydrolyzing the PI(4,5)P2 IP3 and DAG second messengers; IP3diffuses
  and interacts with the endoplasmic reticulum (ER) membrane receptors, elevating
  the cytosolic calcium level; along the cell membrane, DAG diffuses and targets many
  kinases, calcium-sensitive enzymes, and ion channels, enabling further biological
  processes. B After GPCR receptor activation, G-proteins activate PI3K to convert
  PIP2 to PIP3, which recruits PDK1 and one of the mTOR complexes (mTOR complexes
  (mTOR complex 2 (mTORC2)). Thereby, activating Akt to phosphorylate essential cytoplasmic
  proteins and two tumor suppressor proteins, PRAS40 and TSC2, hence activating the
  other complex of mTOR (mTOR complex 1 (mTORC1)), which is responsible for regulating
  cell metabolism and growth proteins
