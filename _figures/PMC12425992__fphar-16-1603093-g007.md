  mTOR pathway, thereby inhibiting the malignant progression of TNBC. In detail, shikonin

  shikonin downregulates the mRNA expression of MTOR and CCND1, inhibits the activity
  of mTOR protein through hydrophobic bond and π–π bond binding, thereby inhibiting
  the activity of mTOR pathway, blocking the cell cycle, and inhibiting the malignant
  progression of TNBC in the cytoplasm
