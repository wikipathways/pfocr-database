caption: Schematic illustration of the Pickering emulsion-based cascade bioreactor
  (PEBR) for the precision treatment of GA. One-step ultrasonic self-assembly produces
  squalene-in-water droplets whose interface is densely packed with URI and CAT. This
  creates a self-cascade catalytic surface that rapidly dissolves MSU crystals while
  scavenging H2O2. Mannose ligands conjugated to DSPE-PEG direct the droplets to CD206-expressing
  inflammatory macrophages. The oil core co-delivers MTX inhibits ROS generation and
  NF-κB/NLRP3 signaling pathways, thereby down-regulating pro-inflammatory cytokines
  and promoting polarization toward the reparative M2 phenotype. Integrated cascade
  catalysis, targeted uptake, and macrophage reprogramming transform the GA microenvironment,
  providing a safe and clinically translatable platform for GA treatment
