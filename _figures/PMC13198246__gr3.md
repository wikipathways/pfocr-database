caption: The regulation of the gut microbiome on ferroptosis in cardiovascular degenerative
  diseases. SCFAs inhibited the expression of ATF3 and reduced iron overload, thereby
  mitigating lipid peroxidation and ferroptosis in cardiomyocytes. Butyrate promoted
  the transcriptional activation of NRF2 through P300, protecting against endothelial
  dysfunction. In contrast, TMAO inhibited the NRF2–GPX4 pathway, leading to enhanced
  neointimal hyperplasia and advanced plaque formation. Additionally, drugs such as
  dapagliflozin, canagliflozin, atorvastatin, and nicorandil alleviated the progression
  of cardiovascular degenerative diseases through their effects on gut metabolites
  and ferroptosis. Created in https://BioRender.com
