caption: a, 5′ capping. RNA processing during early transcription by RNA polymerase
  II (RNAP II) involves the cap-binding complex (comprised of CBP20 (also known as
  NCBP2) and CBP80 (also known as NCBP1)), RNAP II, and the recruitment of capping
  enzymes RNGTT, RAM (also known as RAMAC), RNMT, CMTR1, and CMTR2 to the nascent
  transcript. b, Ribonucleosomes. Heterogeneous nuclear ribonucleoprotein (HNRNP)
  complexes package RNA into large RNA–protein assemblies known as ribonucleosomes;
  these were historically proposed to occur as repeating units of ~700 nucleotides,
  although recent studies show that periodicity is not general and, instead, the assembly
  of ribonucleosomes depends on RNA sequence. c, Spliceosome assembly. Co-transcriptional
  and post-transcriptional assembly of the spliceosome, depicting the sequential binding
  of small nuclear ribonucleoproteins (snRNPs; U1, U2, U4, U5, and U6) and the role
  of RNAP II. d, Cleavage and polyadenylation. Cleavage and polyadenylation of the
  pre-mRNA 3′ end mediated by cleavage stimulation factors (CSTFs), cleavage and polyadenylation
  specificity factors (CPSFs), and poly(A) polymerase (PAP), followed by association
  of poly(A) binding protein nuclear 1 (PABPN1) with the poly(A) tail. e, The exon-junction
  complex. The exon-junction complex (EJC) assembles on spliced mRNAs, involving proteins
  such as RBM8A, MAGOH, and EIF4A3, alongside the cap-binding complex and PABPN1,
  with subsequent recruitment of the transcription-export (TREX) complex — including
  ALYREF, the THO complex, and UAP56 — which facilitates mRNA compaction and nuclear
  export via NXF1–NXT1. N6-methyladenosine (m6A) writers may also be involved. f,
  Translation initiation. Translation initiation for cytoplasmic mRNAs involves binding
  of translation initiation factors (EIF4E, EIF4G, EIF4A, and EIF4B) and cytoplasmic
  poly(A)-binding protein (PABPC1) to EJC-remodeled mRNAs, with recruitment of the
  43S pre-initiation complex (including EIF3A) and ribosome assembly. g, Nonsense-mediated
  decay. The nonsense-mediated mRNA decay (NMD) pathway is triggered by premature
  stop codons through stepwise assembly of the SURF and DECID complexes. Key factors
  include UPF proteins (UPF1, UPF2, and UPF3), SMG proteins, decay complexes (SMG5–SMG7,
  SMG6, and CCR4–NOT), and ribosome disassembly stages, which facilitate endonucleolytic
  cleavage and mRNA degradation
