caption: The dual role of p53 in regulating ferroptosis. p53 exerts opposing effects
  on ferroptosis through distinct molecular mechanisms. On one hand, it can promote
  ferroptotic cell death by repressing the expression of SLC7A11 and cystathionine
  beta-synthase (CBS), or alternatively by activating the expression of SAT1 and GLS2.
  Conversely, p53 can suppress ferroptosis through alternative pathways, either by
  inhibiting DPP4 activity or via the induction of CDKN1A/p21 and Parkin expression
