caption: Identical cellular and molecular mechanisms underlie both chemobrain and
  neurodegenerative disorders driving cognitive decline. A. Chemotherapeutic agents
  disrupt DNA repair pathways, known as the DDR, including BER and NER, causing SSBs,
  DSBs, and ICLs, which ultimately lead to parthanatos. B. Mitochondrial dysfunction
  caused by chemotherapy increases ROS production, causing oxidative stress and reduced
  ATP production, which disrupts synaptic function and leads to cognitive decline.
  C. Chemotherapy-induced increase in ROS generation leads to oxidative stress, damaging
  cellular components such as proteins, lipids, and the blood-brain barrier (BBB).
  D. NAD+ metabolism pathway showing that chemotherapy reduces NAMPT levels, leading
  to NAD+ depletion and triggering parthanatos
