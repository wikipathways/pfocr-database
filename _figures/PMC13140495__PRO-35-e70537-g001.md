caption: Biogenesis pathway of mitochondrial carrier proteins. Following translation,
  hydrophobic precursor carrier proteins are bound by the cytosolic chaperone complex
  comprised of a heat shock protein 90 (HSP90) dimer, heat shock protein 70 (HSP70),
  and TOMM34 and directed to the translocase of the outer mitochondrial membrane (TOM)
  complex. HSP90 binds to the TOMM70 receptor to facilitate transfer of the precursor
  to the TOM complex and subsequent translocation through the outer mitochondrial
  membrane (OMM) via TOMM40. Hexameric complexes of TIMM9/10A bind the precursor and
  facilitate transfer to the TIMM9/10A/10B hexamer docked at the translocase of the
  inner membrane 22 (TIM22) complex. The precursor is threaded through the lateral
  cavity of the TIMM22 subunit to facilitate transfer into the inner mitochondrial
  membrane (IMM) in a membrane potential‐dependent manner. A mature canonical SLC25
  carrier protein with six transmembrane domains is depicted. The TIM22 complex also
  imports other mitochondrial carrier proteins including the mitochondrial pyruvate
  carriers (with three transmembrane domains) and the sideroflexins (with four or
  five transmembrane domains). Alongside carrier proteins, the TIMM family members
  TIMM17A/B, TIMM23, and TIMM22 are imported by the TIM22 complex. Arrows denote carrier
  precursor pathway. OMM, outer mitochondrial membrane, IMS, intermembrane space,
  IMM, inner mitochondrial membrane, ∆ψ, mitochondrial membrane potential
