caption: 'NOTCH3 pathway in PASMCs and therapeutic targeting of the NOTCH3 pathway.
  In PASMCs, Jag‐1 binds to the NOTCH3 receptor, triggering γ‐secretase to cleave
  NOTCH3 and release the intracellular domain NICD3. NICD3 then enters the nucleus
  and activates the gene HES‐5, which promotes PASMC proliferation. In contrast, DLL‐4
  inhibits this process. Several strategies have been developed to inhibit NOTCH3
  signaling: sJag‐1 blocks Jag‐1 binding; anti‐NOTCH3 Ab 28042 prevents Jag‐1 interaction;
  and γ‐secretase inhibitors (GSIs) stop NOTCH3 cleavage. In addition, other pathways
  such as S1P/S1PR2 and mTOR can be targeted using JTE‐013, verteporfin, or rapamycin
  to indirectly suppress NOTCH3 signaling. DLL‐4: Delta‐like ligand 4; sJag‐1: soluble
  Jag‐1'
