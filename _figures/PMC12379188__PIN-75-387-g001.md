caption: The fatty acid metabolic pathway and its key inhibitors. Fatty acids are
  synthesized from acetyl‐CoA, which also serves as a substrate for histone acetylation,
  influencing tumor growth and T‐cell function. Both ACC and FASN are frequently upregulated
  in various cancer types, with FASN recognized as a promising therapeutic target.
  Additionally, CD36, a fatty acid translocase, has emerged as a therapeutic target,
  and neutralizing antibodies against CD36 have been shown to reduce tumor growth
  and metastasis
