caption: 'The upper panel depicts the murine periodontitis model induced by silk ligature
  placement around the maxillary second molar, facilitating food impaction and polymicrobial
  dysbiosis that elicits inflammatory alveolar bone loss. The lower left panel shows
  the healthy gingival state: epithelial cells express high levels of N4BP1, which
  sustains mucosal barrier integrity by preserving tight junction protein occludin
  (OCLN); the LDN-1 neutrophil subset remains transcriptionally quiescent with negligible
  epithelial interaction. The lower middle panel illustrates periodontitis (PD): epithelial
  N4BP1 is downregulated by dysbiotic challenge, resulting in OCLN disruption and
  compromised barrier function. In contrast, LDN-1 expands and upregulates N4BP1,
  establishing pathogenic crosstalk with epithelium through two distinct, N4BP1-restricted
  axes: suppression of the pro-inflammatory chemokine axis CXCL1/6–CXCR1/2 and concomitant
  inhibition of the anti-inflammatory ANXA1–FPR1/FPR2 resolution pathway. The lower
  right inset conceptualizes N4BP1 as a cell-type-specific molecular rheostat that
  simultaneously dampens neutrophil-driven inflammation and endogenous pro-resolving
  signals, thereby fine-tuning immune–epithelial homeostasis during periodontitis
  progression. Created with BioRender.com'
