caption: The bidirectional immune effects of radiotherapy on TIME. This diagram illustrates
  the dual immune-modulating effects of radiotherapy on the TIME. Radiotherapy induces
  immunogenic cell death (ICD) in tumor cells, releasing damage-associated molecular
  patterns (DAMPs) and tumor-associated antigens (TAAs). Cytosolic DNA activates the
  STING pathway, promoting dendritic cell (DCs) maturation and subsequent T cell priming.
  Conversely, radiotherapy simultaneously exerts immune-inhibitory effects by recruiting
  myeloid-derived suppressor cells (MDSCs), which establish a robust immunosuppressive
  network that can counteract the anti-tumor immune response and limit therapeutic
  efficacy
