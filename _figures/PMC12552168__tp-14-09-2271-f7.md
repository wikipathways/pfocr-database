caption: The overall view of the single ventricle pathway is depicted. Patients with
  left ventricular outflow tract obstruction are directed to a Norwood or Hybrid Norwood
  procedure. Those without systemic outflow obstruction progress towards a bidirectional
  Glenn and may require pulmonary artery banding or a PDA stent or shunt depending
  on the status of their pulmonary blood flow. Patients are re-directed towards mechanical
  support or transplant depending on their clinical status throughout the entire pathway.
  Once palliated with a Fontan procedure, there is an expected attrition of this population
  over time with patients receiving more advanced support with assist devices or transplantation.
  APS, aorto-pulmonary shunt; BDG, bidirectional Glenn; CS2, comprehensive stage 2;

  ductus arteriosus; PDAS, patent ductus arteriosus stenting; PGE, prostaglandin E;
  VAD, ventricular assist device
