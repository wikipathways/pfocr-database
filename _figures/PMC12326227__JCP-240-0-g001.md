caption: The RIPO model for rDNA transcription regulation by the ARF‐TTF1‐lncRNA pathway.
  (A) LncRNA Induced Promoter Occlusion (RIPO) is suppressed by TTF1 arrest of Spacer
  Promoter‐driven transcription to maintain maximal 47S pre‐rRNA synthesis. (B) Accumulation
  of p14/p19ARF leads to displacement of TTF1 from the rDNA, allowing transcription
  from the Spacer Promoter to readthrough the 47S gene promoter. This leads to promoter
  interference or occlusion by displacing the RPI initiation complex and in this way
  suppresses 47S pre‐rRNA synthesis
