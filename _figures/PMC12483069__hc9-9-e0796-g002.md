caption: 'Analytical approach overview. Blood samples were collected from participants
  with biliary atresia (BA), alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency (A1AT), and Alagille syndrome
  (ALGS); liver stiffness measurement (LSM) was assessed using VCTE; protein expression
  was measured using the SomaScan aptamer-based platform. A cohort of healthy pediatric
  participants evaluated for constipation without evident liver disease was recruited
  as normal pediatric controls for the proteomic analyses. Protein expression was
  correlated with LSM. Network analysis was conducted for each disease using WGCNA.
  LSM was predicted using machine learning regression. Informative proteins were characterized
  by enriched pathways, protein–protein interaction networks, and cell-type annotation.
  The expression of selected proteins was confirmed by ELISA. Association of proteins
  and clinical features with liver transplant outcome was evaluated using survival
  analysis. Created in BioRender https://BioRender.com/47unsns). Abbreviations: APRI,
  aspartate aminotransferase to platelet ratio index; GGTP, gamma-glutamyl transpeptidase;
  GPR, gamma-glutamyl transpeptidase to platelet ratio; INR, international normalized
  ratio; KNN, k-nearest neighbor; RF, random forest; SVM, support vector machine;
  VCTE, vibration-controlled transient elastography; WGCNA, weighted gene correlation
  network analysis'
