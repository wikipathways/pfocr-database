caption: Pro-inflammatory effects of M1 microglia. Activated M1 microglia drive inflammation
  primarily through DAMP, IFNγ signaling, and NOX2 activation. DAMPs engage TLR2/4,
  triggering the MAL/NF-κB pathway and inducing cytokines such as TNFα and IL-6. These
  mediators enhance neutrophil activation via ICAM-1 on endothelial cells of the BBB,
  promoting NET release, microvascular occlusion, and secondary thrombosis. NF-κB
  also upregulates matrix metalloproteinases (MMP2, MMP9), which degrade occludin,
  releasing toxic fragments that induce endothelial apoptosis and compromise BBB integrity.
  Meanwhile, CD4+ Th1 cells secrete IFNγ, reinforcing M1 activation and establishing
  a feedback loop in which ROS-induced mitochondrial dysfunction amplifies NLRP3 inflammasome
  signaling, further sustaining neuroinflammation. The figure was created using Biorender.com,
