caption: The relationship between the endothelial NF‐κB signaling pathway, the ADAMTS13‐vWF
  axis, and inflammation in CTEPH. The local activation of inflammatory cells in the
  thrombus releases inflammatory factors, leading to the activation of the NF‐κB pathway
  in endothelial cells and an increase in vWF levels, resulting in increased platelet‐endothelial
  cell (EC) adhesion. Reduced plasma ADAMTS13 levels can disrupt the balance of the
  ADAMTS13‐vWF axis. Once platelets are activated, they continue to adhere to immune
  cells and participate in the inflammatory response. At the same time, the upregulation
  of inflammatory factors in endothelial cells maintains the continuous recruitment
  of inflammatory cells
