caption: An extrinsic cell‐death‐inducing signaling pathway comprised of p53, Reprimo,
  FAT1/FAT4/CELSR1/CELSR2/CELSR3, YAP/TAZ, and p73. RPRM expression is activated by
  p53. Reprimo protein is secreted from cells, where it specifically interacts with
  the protocadherin family members FAT1, FAT4, CELSR1, CELSR2, and/or CELSR3. This
  causes a decrease in the phosphorylation of YAP, which translocates to nucleus.
  YAP‐p73 then modulates the expression of apoptosis‐related genes leading to cell
  apoptosis
