caption: Diagram of canonical and non-canonical Wnt signaling pathways. Activation
  of the canonical Wnt pathway recruits DVL to the membrane, disrupting the destruction
  complex (AXIN1/2, APC, GSK3β, CK1α). Stabilized β-catenin accumulates in the cytoplasm
  and translocates to the nucleus to regulate gene transcription. Binding of DKK1
  to LRP inhibits Wnt signaling by preventing Wnt ligand–receptor interaction, resulting
  in β-catenin phosphorylation and subsequent proteasomal degradation. The non-canonical
  Wnt pathway activates cytoskeletal remodeling and gene transcription via the PCP
  (ROCK/JNK) and Ca2+ (PKC/NFAT) signaling branches. This figure was drawn by Figdraw
