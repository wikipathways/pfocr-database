caption: Pathways modulated by six lncRNAs identified by intersection of their role
  in endocrine resistance in breast cancer and unfolded protein response. H19 promotes
  EnR homeostasis by increasing activity of PI3K/AKT/mTOR and XBP1 signaling. MALAT1
  acts as an miR‐195a‐5p sponge to upregulate HMGA1 expression and promote UPR‐induced
  apoptosis. GAS5 inhibits growth and induces apoptosis by upregulating DDIT3. UCA1
  and LINP1 attenuate EnR‐induced apoptosis. UPR upregulates SOX2OT expression and
  SOX2OT decreases the expression of GRP78 and PERK protein. The LncRNA downregulated
  in endocrine resistant samples are shown in red and LncRNAs upregulated in endocrine
  resistant samples are shown in green. EnR, endoplasmic reticulum; lncRNAs, long
  noncoding RNAs; HMGA1, high mobility group AT‐hook1; UPR, unfolded protein response
