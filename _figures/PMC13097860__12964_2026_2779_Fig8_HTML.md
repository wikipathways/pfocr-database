caption: 'Working model of the transgenerational inheritance of the downregulation
  of innate immunity induced by PS-NPs. Parental exposure to PS-NPs inhibits the IRE-1/XBP-1
  branch of the UPRER and downregulates collagen gene expression, compromising both
  molecular and structural immunity. PS-NPs also reduce germline histone H3K36me3
  levels via MES-4, establishing an epigenetic mark that transmits transcriptional
  repression of UPRER and collagen-related genes to F1-F2 progeny. These events collectively
  result in heritable immunosuppression independent of DNA methylation and ncRNA pathways.
  Abbreviations: PS-NPs, polystyrene nanoplastics; UPRER, endoplasmic reticulum unfolded
  protein response; H3K36me3, histone H3 lysine 36 trimethylation; MES-4, histone
  lysine N-methyltransferase MES-4; ncRNA, non-coding RNA; P0, parental generation;
  F1, first filial generation; F2, second filial generation'
