caption: This schematic illustrates the three core pathways of sphingolipid metabolism,
  centered on the pivotal role of Cer. ​1) The de novo synthesis pathway​ (top right)
  begins in the endoplasmic reticulum with the condensation of serine and palmitoyl-CoA
  by SPT, proceeding through several intermediates to generate Cer. ​2) The salvage
  pathway​ (left) recycles degradation products like sphingosine, which can be re-acylated
  to Cer or phosphorylated to S1P—a key signaling molecule degraded by SPL. This pathway
  also interfaces with GSL metabolism. ​3) The SMase pathway​ (bottom) shows the rapid
  hydrolysis of SM to Cer, which can be reversibly converted to SM or to C1P
