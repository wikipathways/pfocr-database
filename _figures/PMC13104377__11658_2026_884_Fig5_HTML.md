caption: Mechanism of mitophagy. A Ubiquitin-dependent mitophagy pathway. Upon mitochondrial
  damage under diverse stresses, a loss of membrane potential (ψ) recruits PINK1 to
  the outer mitochondrial membrane (OMM). PINK1 then recruits and phosphorylates ubiquitin
  and the E3 ubiquitin ligase Parkin, triggering the ubiquitination of OMM proteins,
  such as VDAC1 (voltage-dependent anion channel 1 protein) and MFN2 (mitofusin 2).
  These polyubiquitin chains interact with autophagy receptors (such as OPTN and NDP52)
  to facilitate the recruitment of damaged mitochondria to autophagosomes. Ubiquitinated
  mitochondria are subsequently engulfed in autophagosomes and eventually degraded
  in autolysosomes. B Ubiquitin-independent mitophagy pathway. Many other receptors
  (e.g., BNIP3, NIX, and FUNDC1) are directly recruited to the OMM and bind to LC3,
  thereby promoting the formation of autophagosomes. The cargoes and damaged mitochondria
  are engulfed within autophagosomes and subsequently degraded in autolysosomes. Created
