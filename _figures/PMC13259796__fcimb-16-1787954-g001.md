caption: Gut and tissue-associated microbiome as a metabolic organ with systemic influence.
  The gut and tissue-associated microbiome functions as a metabolic organ that produces
  bioactive metabolites, including short-chain fatty acids (SCFAs), amino acids, bile
  acids, and polyamines. These metabolites enter the systemic circulation, are processed
  by the liver, and subsequently influence the tumor microenvironment. Circulating
  metabolites also reach peripheral tissues, where similar microbial-derived metabolites
  are present locally. Together, this bidirectional pathway highlights the systemic
  metabolic influence of the microbiome across organs and tissues
