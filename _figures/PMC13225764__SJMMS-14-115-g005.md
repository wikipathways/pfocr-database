  effects of olaparib. Olaparib resistance is associated with PPAR-γ downregulation,
  SIRT1 upregulation, and SASP-driven cellular senescence (left panel). Pioglitazone

  the SIRT1 signaling pathway, thereby increasing the sensitivity of ovarian cancer
  cells to olaparib (right panel)
