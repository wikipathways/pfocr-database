caption: The roles of PRR genes in the circadian clock and output pathways.(A) The
  circadian clock genes PRR9/7/5 regulate plant growth, development, and metabolism
  through multiple molecular mechanisms.(B) PRR9/7/5 control flowering time in various
  plant species, including Arabidopsis, pear, sugar beet, chrysanthemum, barley, rice,
  and soybean. Dashed lines represent putative regulatory relationships.(C) PRR9/7/5
  are essential for plant responses to environmental stresses such as heat, cold,
  salinity, and drought by directly or indirectly regulating the expression of stress-response
  genes, including CBF genes, HKT2;1, and ABF3, in multiple species such as Arabidopsis,
  rice, and soybean.(D) TOC1 regulates diverse aspects of plant growth and development
  by directly or indirectly controlling the expression of genes involved in hypocotyl
  elongation, thermomorphogenesis, the cell cycle, autophagy, the Krebs cycle, fatty
  acid metabolism, and auxin signaling.(E) TOC1 is crucial in the regulation of plant
  responses to abiotic and biotic stresses by modulating the expression of stress-related
  genes and integrating circadian clock signals with stress-response mechanisms
