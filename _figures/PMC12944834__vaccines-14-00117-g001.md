caption: (A) MR1-dependent MAIT cell activation. TCR-dependent MAIT cell activation
  involving MR1-mediated presentation of antigen. MAIT cell co-stimulation can occur
  by CD28-CD80/86 interactions and by cytokines from the APC in response to the pathogen,
  including the inflammatory cytokine response to TLR ligands. (B) MR1-independent
  MAIT cell activation. TCR-independent (cytokine-dependent) MAIT cell activation
  requires the action of multiple cytokines, which can be generated in response to
  the phagocytosis of bacteria and/or viruses, and TLR ligands. (C) Formation of the
  prototypical MR1-dependent MAIT cell agonist, 5-OP-RU (1), occurs in situ from the
  reaction of 5-A-RU (2), an intermediate in the riboflavin biosynthesis pathway,
  and methylglyoxal (MG, 3), a by-product of glycolysis
