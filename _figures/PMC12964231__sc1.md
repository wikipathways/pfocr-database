caption: Schematic illustration of BTZ-conjugated TiN NPs via reversible boronate
  ester bonds for synergistic unfolded protein response (UPR) activation through combination
  of proteasome inhibition and sonodynamic therapy (SDT). a) Discovery of carminic
  acid (CA) for BTZ loading though pH- and US-responsive dynamic covalent boronate
  ester bonds. After screening from polyphenol compound library, CA was found to interact
  to BTZ through reversible boronate ester bonds, and controlled drug release can
  be realized by the acidic environment and US irradiation. b) Preparation of BSA-CA
  coated TiN NPs and BTZ loading based on the intermolecular coordination between
  BTZ and CA. c) ROS and additional inhibition of proteasomal β2 active site by SDT
  enable more accumulation of ubiquitinated proteins triggering enhanced ER stress
  after the combination treatment with BTZ and SDT. ER stress-induced calcium overload
  and ROS elevation lead to mitochondrial dysfunction. More importantly, the severe
  ER stress induced by the combination therapy triggers the activation of terminal
  UPR pathways including PERK/eIF2α/ATF4, ATF6, IRE1/JNK leading to cell death, with
  the evidence of upregulation of CHOP and caspase-12 activation
