caption: Proposed immunopathological processes linking systemic inflammation to neuroinflammation
  and the development of late-life depression (LLD). Peripheral chronic low-grade
  inflammation, exacerbated by aging and comorbidities, communicates with the brain
  via humoral and neuronal pathways, compromising the blood-brain barrier (BBB) integrity.
  In the CNS, this triggers microglial activation leading to increased inflammatory
  cytokines and reactive oxygen species (ROS) release, reduction of neurotrophic support,
  and altered neurotransmission. Taken together, these events may underlie the LLD
  onset and progression. BDNF, brain-derived neurotrophic factor; IL-1β, interleukin-1β;
