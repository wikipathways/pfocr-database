caption: Dendritic cells secrete IL-6 and Type I interferons that bind to their receptors
  on CD4+ T cells, thereby activating JAK/STAT signaling to induce BCL6, CXCR5, and
  PD-1 expression. STAT3 also activates transcription of BCL6 and upregulates c-Maf,
  both of which enhance IL-21 production. IL-12 from dendritic cells and macrophages
  activate STAT4 and induce T-bet and IFN-γ expression, skewing Tfh cells toward a
  Th1-like profile. Tfh differentiation is restrained by CTLA-4 and IL-10 produced
  by T follicular regulatory (Tfr) cells. Additionally, the PI3K-FOXO1 pathway negatively
  regulates BCL6 expression and Tfh development
