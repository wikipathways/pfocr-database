caption: Integrated overview of interconnected mitochondrial dysfunction pathways
  in renal IRI-AKI. Ischemia/reperfusion triggers Ca2+ overload and mtROS surges,
  promoting ΔΨm collapse, mPTP opening, MQC imbalance (biogenesis–dynamics–mitophagy),
  and release of mtDAMPs (e.g., mtDNA/mtRNA and cardiolipin). These signals activate
  innate sensors (e.g., TLR9, cGAS–STING, and NLRP3) and amplify inflammation and
  regulated cell death; multiple therapeutic entry points include antioxidants, biogenesis
  activators, fission/fusion modulators, mitophagy tuning, and mtDAMP-pathway inhibition.
  (Red upward arrows indicate activation of the process, while downward arrows indicate
  inhibition.)
