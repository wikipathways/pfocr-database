caption: Modulation of cell death pathways by Rickettsia spp. Several effector proteins
  of Rickettsia spp. are translocated via the T4SS into the host cell and are involved
  in the manipulation of apoptotic signaling (RapL and RARP2) and the inhibition of
  bacterial degradation (bacterial methylases). Other pathways, which Rickettsia spp.
  interfere with, include NF-κB and caspase-11 signaling, as well as ROS reduction.
  Importantly, ER damage is also induced during the infection, highlighting a pro-apoptotic
  aspect of the infection
