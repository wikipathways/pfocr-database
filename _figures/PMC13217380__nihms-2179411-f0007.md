caption: 'A. U87 (wild-type) cells: Capsaicin treatment activates TRPV1 and CACNB1
  expression, leading to MAPK pathway activation and phosphorylation of key transcription
  factors, including MSTRG.66879, MYC, and HSF1. Induction of DUSP family proteins
  provides negative feedback to the MAPK cascade, thereby maintaining cellular homeostasis.
  HSF1 activation promotes the expression of heat shock proteins (HSPs), supporting
  protein folding and protection against capsaicin-induced stress. Genes such as MSTRG.66879,
  MYC, and DUSP1 show specific expression in U87 cells. B. In TRPV1 knockout (TRPV1-KO)
  U87 cells, capsaicin exposure appears to induce compensatory activation of alternative
  calcium channels, such as CACNA1D and RGS6, leading to increased intracellular Ca2+
  influx. The elevated cytosolic Ca2+ interacts with calmodulin, activating Ca2+/calmodulin-dependent
  kinase 1G (CaMK1G) signaling. Excess cytosolic calcium is also taken up by mitochondria,
  resulting in elevated mitochondrial Ca2+ levels and subsequent generation of reactive
  oxygen species (ROS). To counteract this oxidative and metabolic stress, transcription
  factors such as GABPα, and EIF1 are upregulated, promoting transcription of genes
  encoding components of mitochondrial electron transport chain complexes I-IV. However,
  this compensatory mitochondrial activation contributes to cellular dysregulation,
  triggering intrinsic apoptotic signaling characterized by strong induction of the
  pro-apoptotic gene HRK (Harakiri, 5.16-fold increase) and consequent inhibition
  of cell proliferation'
