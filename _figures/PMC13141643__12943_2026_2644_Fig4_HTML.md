caption: 'The connection between ROS and chronic inflammation: A Cuproptosis leads
  to mitochondrial oxidative stress, increase in the degree of ROS, activation of
  NLRP3 inflammasome and caspase-1, and subsequent IL-12 processing and maturation
  of IL-18. B The presence of a bidirectional interaction between cuproptosis and
  inflammation exists. Prolonged inflammation, which is usually characterized by the
  existence of myeloid cells is linked to the release of ROS and pro-inflammatory
  mediators. This will then contribute to cuproptosis, further increase ROS generation,
  and eventually increase inflammatory reactions. In turn, cuproptosis also can be
  caused by established chronic inflammation when the overproduction of ROS compounds
  the oxidative injury and regulates the NF-kaB signaling pathway to release inflammatory
  factors to maintain the chronic inflammatory phenotype'
