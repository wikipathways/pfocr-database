caption: Schematic illustration of the protective mechanism of PHB2 against IVDDUnder
  inflammatory conditions in IVDD, activation of the TNF receptor (TNFR) triggers
  the RIPK1/RIPK3/MLKL signaling cascade, leading to necroptosis and mitochondrial
  damage. PHB2, in coordination with the PINK1/Parkin pathway, promotes mitophagy
  through the recruitment of LC3 and subsequent fusion of autophagosomes with lysosomes.
  This process facilitates the clearance of damaged mitochondria, thereby inhibiting
  necroptosis. Collectively, PHB2-mediated mitophagy preserves mitochondrial homeostasis
  and protects NP cells from degeneration
