caption: Interaction of platelets with the complement system. First, P-selectin (CD62L)
  expressed on the surface of activated platelets, which may act as a receptor for
  C3b, initiates the complement alternative pathway activation by forming the C3bBb
  complex, which ultimately leads to an increase of the C5b-9 complexes formation
  on the platelet surface. Second, the receptor protein gC1qR/P33 on the platelets
  may initiate activation of the complement classical pathway by binding to C1q. Third,
  the terminal complement C5b-9 complex (membrane attack complex [MAC] or terminal
  complement complex [TCC]) alters the direction of platelet membrane potential through
  depolarization, leading to a procoagulant lipid surface that provides binding sites
  for the prothrombin activator, thereby further promoting thrombin generation. C3aR,
  complement component 3a receptor; gC1qR, globular complement C1q receptor
