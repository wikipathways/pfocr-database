caption: Dual roles of CCA1 and LHY in the activation and repression of output pathways.(A
  and B) LHY and CCA1 regulate growth, development, metabolism, and yield in multiple
  plant species, including Arabidopsis, rice, maize, and Medicago, by modulating the
  expression of key genes involved in lipid and starch biosynthesis, vernalization,
  cell elongation, tillering, and other processes. Black arrows and bars indicate
  transcriptional activation and repression, respectively. Thicker arrows indicate
  activation of molecular modules that correspond to phenotypic outcomes, whereas
  thicker bars indicate repression of such modules.(C) The circadian regulators CCA1
  and LHY are important for the uptake of essential nutrients in plants.(D) CCA1 and
  CCA1/LHY-like genes control floral timing across species.(E and F) The transcription
  factors CCA1 and LHY enhance plant resilience to biotic and abiotic stresses by
  regulating essential stress-response genes
