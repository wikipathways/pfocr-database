caption: 'Canonical and noncanonical AHR signaling pathways. (a) In the canonical
  pathway, xenobiotic ligands bind to the cytoplasmic AHR complex (HSP90/p23/AIP/c-Src),
  promoting AHR activation, nuclear translocation, heterodimerization with ARNT, and
  the transcription of XRE/DRE-driven target genes such as AHRR, CYP1A1, and TIPARP.
  (b) In the noncanonical pathway, physiological ligands derived from host metabolites,
  microorganisms, or dietary sources elicit alternative signaling. Activated AHR engages
  cytosolic c-Src–mediated cascades and recruits additional transcription factors
  (TFs) to non-XRE/DRE regulatory elements, influencing gene expression programs including
  KLF6 and NF-κB. Abbreviations: AHR, aryl hydrocarbon receptor; ARNT, AHR nuclear
  translocator; AHRR, aryl hydrocarbon receptor repressor; AIP, AHR-interacting protein;
  HSP90, heat shock protein 90; XRE, xenobiotic response element; DRE, dioxin response
  element; CYP1A1, cytochrome P4501A1; TIPARP, TCDD-inducible poly­(ADP-ribose) polymerase;
  KLF6, kruppel-like factor 6; NF-κB, nuclear factor kappa B; TFs, transcription factors'
