caption: 'Dysregulation of histone methylation modifiers in IDH1-mutant U-87 cells:
  The upregulation of HMTs and the downregulation of HDMs result in transcriptional
  repression and knockdown in the expression levels of downstream target genes: silencing
  of epithelial genes and tumor suppressor genes for the activation of downstream
  effectors and signaling pathways related to cancer progression and growth. Specific
  inhibitors of EZH2 and SMYD2 may serve as valuable therapeutic strategies for the
  treatment of IDH-mutated gliomas. HMTs: histone methyltransferases; HDMs: histone
  demethylases; EZH2: enhancer of zeste homologue 2; KDMs: lysine demethylases; SMYD3:
  lysine methyltransferase; EMT: epithelial–mesenchymal transition'
