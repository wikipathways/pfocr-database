caption: Mechanism of fibrosis and common pathways activated by proteases and antiproteases
  resulting in lung remodelling in ILD. Based on a plethora of murine models of IPF,
  proteases and antiproteases are regarded as both “profibrotic” and “antifibrotic”.
  The damage to the epithelial/endothelial cells with subsequent recruitment and activation
  of immune cells stimulates the release of proteases and antiproteases that can initiate
  fibrosis by triggering the TGF-β/SMAD2-3 pathway and WNT β-catenin pathway. Consequently,
  epithelial and endothelial mesenchymal transition, fibroblast proliferation, and
  fibroblast differentiation to myofibroblasts result in extracellular matrix deposition.
  EMT, epithelial-mesenchymal transition; EndMT, endothelial-mesencyhmal transition;
  TTSP, type II transmembrane serine proteases (TTSP)
