caption: Role of key genes in tumorigenesis. (A) This pathway is initiated by MAPK
  cascades and facilitated. ERK, JNK, and p38 constitute three distinct groups of
  MAPKs. ERK is activated by MEK1 and MEK2, JNK by MKK4 and MKK7, and p38 by MKK3
  and MKK6. Once activated, MAPKs regulate the transcription of downstream transcription
  factors FOSB and JUN genes while enhancing the expression of AP-1 complex. When
  activated by AP-1 and other related cytokines, they are essential for controlling
  diverse physiological processes such as gene expression regulation, cell proliferation
  and death promotion, facilitation of cell differentiation and movement in response
  to cytokines or growth factors or harmful stimuli; additionally inducing oncogenic
  transformation. (B) Cancer cells primarily rely on glycolysis for energy production,
  facilitated by LDHC, which also leads to excessive lactate generation. This lactate
  is exported to TME through MCT, enabling cancer cells to maintain an acidic microenvironment
  that promotes immunosuppression and immune evasion. FH, fumarate hydratase; FOSB,
  FBJ murine osteosarcoma viral oncogene homolog B; IDH, isocitrate dehydrogenase;
  LDHC, lactate dehydrogenase C; MCT, monocarboxylate transporter 1; SDH, succinate
  dehydrogenase; TME, tumor microenvironment
