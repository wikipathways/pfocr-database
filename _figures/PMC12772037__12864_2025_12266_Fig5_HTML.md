caption: Expression heatmap of genes in the lignin biosynthesis pathway in linalool-type
  and borneol-type camphor trees. The pathway was constructed based on the ‘Monolignol
  biosynthesis’ pathway (M00039) from the KEGG Pathway Database (https://www.kegg.jp/pathway/map00940).
  Enzymatic products are depicted as nodes and gene symbols are highlighted in azure.
  Panels 1–4 and 5–8, designated as LS1-4 and BS1-4, represent S1-S4 developmental
  stages of linalool-type and borneol-type camphor trees, respectively. Genes identified
  exclusively in the WGCNA analysis of the linalool or borneol chemotype were highlighted
  in orange and purple, respectively, while those common to both chemotypes were highlighted
  in azure. Dotted lines denote the two non-conserved bypass reactions within the
  plant kingdom, including the bypass of the second HCT [15, 40] and the bypass of
  the Caffeoyl shikimate pathway [8]. PAL, phenylalanine ammonia-lyase; C4H, Cinnamate
  4-hydroxylase; 4CL, 4-coumarate–CoA ligase; HCT, shikimate O-hydroxycinnamoyltransferase;
  C3'H, 5-O-(4-coumaroyl)-D-quinate 3'-monooxygenase; CSE, caffeoylshikimate esterase;
  CCoAOMT, caffeoyl-CoA O-methyltransferase; CCR, cinnamoyl-CoA reductase; CAD, cinnamyl-alcohol
  dehydrogenase; F5H, ferulate-5-hydroxylase; COMT, caffeic acid 3-O-methyltransferase
