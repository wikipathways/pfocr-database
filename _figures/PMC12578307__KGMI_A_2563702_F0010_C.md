caption: A proposed mechanism of endocytic LPS transport during acute LCFA absorption
  and chronic feeding of a Western-style diet in the jejunum. In general, macromolecules
  such as LPS do not cross the small intestinal epithelium. Changes in tight junction
  structure and function increase ion permeability (blue circles), which is accompanied
  by the reduction of TEER known as ‘pore’ pathway, followed by macromolecule entry
  through the widened paracellular space, known as the ‘leak’ pathway.58 In contrast,
  LPS are transcellularly transported during acute LCFA absorption via the lipid raft/CD36/Cav-1
  pathway (green dots).16 Furthermore, chronic feeding of a Western-style diet enhances
  LPS and FD4 transport via an upregulated CD36/Src/Cav-1 pathway, as shown in this
  study. The absorbed LPS exits the basolateral membrane and enters the PV. LPS, lipopolysaccharide;
  FD4, FITC-dextran 4000; TEER, transepithelial electrical resistance; LCFA, long-chain
  fatty acid; Cav-1, caveolin-1; Src, Src kinase; CD36, cluster-of-differentiation
  36; PV, portal vein
