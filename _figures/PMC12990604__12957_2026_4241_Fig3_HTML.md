caption: 'Molecular mechanism of ferroptosis and its inhibitors. Ferroptosis is initiated
  by the peroxidation of PUFA-containing phospholipids in membranes, facilitated by
  ACSL4-mediated PUFA incorporation and amplified by excess labile iron via the Fenton
  reaction. Cellular defense relies on two key systems: the canonical GPX4-GSH axis,
  which detoxifies lipid hydroperoxides, and the parallel FSP1-CoQ10-CoQ10H₂ axis,
  which functions as a GSH-independent radical-trapping antioxidant pathway. As a
  compensatory response to oxidative stress, the Nrf2 pathway can be activated to
  upregulate downstream antioxidant genes (e.g., HO-1 and SLC7A11). Ferroptosis inhibitors
  include AS-252,424, TEMPO, artemisinin, and ferrostatin-1. Abbreviation: CoQ10:
  Coenzyme Q10, DFP: Deferiprone, GPX4: Glutathione Peroxidase 4, GSH: Glutathione,
  HO-1: Heme Oxygenase-1, Nrf2: Nuclear Factor Erythroid 2-related Factor 2, PUFA:
  Polyunsaturated Fatty Acid, ROS: Reactive Oxygen Species, SLC7A11: Solute Carrier
  Family 7 Member 11, TEMPO: 2,2,6,6-tetramethylpiperidine-1-oxyl'
