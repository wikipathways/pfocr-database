caption: Schematic illustration of LOCoF2 nanoreactors that unleash a self-amplifying
  pyroptosis and cGAS-STING activation cycle via lactate depletion for a potent catalytic
  immunotherapy. The LOCoF₂ nanoreactor consumes lactate and generates ROS through
  cascade catalysis, depleting an immunosuppressive metabolite while inducing oxidative
  stress. This triggers caspase-1/GSDMD-mediated pyroptosis, characterized by mitochondrial
  damage and release of mitochondrial DNA (mtDNA) into the cytosol. The liberated
  mtDNA acts as a potent cGAS agonist, activating the STING pathway. Concurrently,
  Co²⁺ ions released from the nanoreactor synergistically enhance STING signaling
  by promoting TBK1/IRF3 phosphorylation. The resulting type I interferon production
  amplifies pyroptosis, creating a self-reinforcing loop that drives DC maturation,
  cytotoxic T-cell infiltration, and reversal of immunosuppression
