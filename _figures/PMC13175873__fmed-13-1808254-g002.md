caption: ER stress and apoptosis. In the ER stress-induced apoptotic pathway, ATF6,
  IRE1, and ATF4 bind and activate the CHOP promoter under ERS conditions. CHOP activation
  promotes the translocation of caspase-12 into the cytoplasm, initiating a caspase
  cascade that leads to ERS-dependent apoptosis. CHOP also upregulates the expression
  of death receptors DR4 and DR5, activating caspase-8 and promoting cell death via
  the death receptor pathway. In addition, CHOP suppresses the anti-apoptotic expression
  of BCL2, BCL-XL, and MCL-1, while promoting the expression of pro-apoptotic proteins
  BAX, BAK, and BH3-only proteins. This facilitates mitochondrial outer membrane permeabilization
  (MOMP), resulting in the release of cytochrome c and apoptosis-inducing factor (AIF),
  ultimately leading to programmed cell death
