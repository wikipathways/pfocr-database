caption: Effects of the classical and nonclassical RAAS pathways on the vasculature.
  In the classical pathway, angiotensin I undergoes conversion into angiotensin II
  along with its immediate metabolites, angiotensin III and angiotensin IV, as illustrated
  on the left side of the image, denoted by red dotted arrows. Angiotensin II exerts
  vasoconstrictive, inflammatory, and salt retention effects, exacerbating cardiovascular
  pathologies. The alternately produced peptides of the renin angiotensi –aldosterone
  system (RAAS), including angiotensin 1–7 (Ang 1–7) and DAA-I, serve to mitigate
  the detrimental effects of angiotensin II (Ang II), as illustrated on the right
  side of the image, indicated by a green dotted arrow. Aspartate (Asp), arginine
