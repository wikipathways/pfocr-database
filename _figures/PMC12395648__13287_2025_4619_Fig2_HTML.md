caption: Mitochondrial transfer pathways. (a) Mitochondria move through TNTs, which
  are made of F-actin and microtubules, and Miro with TRAKs/Milton link them to motor
  proteins to drive their movement along microtubules. TNF-α–mediated NF-κB signaling,
  ROS-activated PI3K/AKT/mTOR, and reduced MICAL2PV all boost mitochondrial transfer
  in TNTs. But excessive ROS can damage mitochondria. (b) Donor cells package mitochondria
  into EVs and release them into the extracellular space, where they are taken up
  by other cells through endocytosis. Higher ROS levels can promote EV-mediated mitochondrial
  transfer. (c) Cx43 forms gap junctions that mediate mitochondrial transfer, and
  transfer increases when ROS levels rise and cells face oxidative stress. (d) When
  mitochondria fail inside a cell, TMEM16F drives PS to the outer membrane, which
  promotes cell fusion so donor cells can transfer healthy mitochondria and restore
  function
