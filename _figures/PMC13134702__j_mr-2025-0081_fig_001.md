caption: 'Current animal models and pathogenic mechanisms of cholangitis and their
  translational relevance to CCA modeling. (a): Schematic illustration of established
  animal models for cholangitis with their associated signaling pathways. The depicted
  models recapitulate key pathological features of human cholangitis through mechanisms
  involving TLR/NF-κB, ROS, and TGF-β/smad signaling. These inflammatory pathways
  drive persistent biliary injury and create a tumor-promoting microenvironment. (b):
  Summary of current experimental models for CCA development. The models are categorized
  by carcinogen-induced model, tumor implantation model, genetic engineering model,
  surgical animal model. The arrows indicate the likelihood of transformation between
  model methods. The sequential relationship between (a) and (b) indicates that there
  are many similarities between the modeling methods of inflammation model and cancer
  model, and the cholangitis model provides an important pathophysiological background
  for studying the inflammation-driven occurrence of CCA. CCA, Cholangiocarcinoma;
  ROS, reactive oxygen species'
