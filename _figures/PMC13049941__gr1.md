caption: 'O-glycosylation Pathway: Mucin type O-glycosylation is initiated in the
  ER and Cis-Golgi by ppGalNAcTs to form the Tn antigen and completed in the Golgi
  after initial action of T-synthase to generate the Core 1 O-glycan. Generation of
  active T-synthase requires Cosmc, a unique molecular chaperone in the ER required
  for correct folding of T-synthase, and encoded by human Xq24 and mouse Xc3. In GI
  tract epithelia, Core 3 GnT (β3GnT6) can also act on Tn antigen to generate Core
  3 O-glycans. Both Core 1 and Core 3 can be further extended or branched to generate
  Core 2 and Core 4 O-glycans, respectively. In some circumstances the Tn antigen
  may be modified by ST6GalNAc-I to generate the STn. STn, sialylTn antigen'
