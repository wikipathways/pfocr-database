caption: The diversity and biosynthesis of taxanes.The central circle illustrates
  the MEP and MVA pathways, which catalyze the formation of GGPP. The upper section
  represents the MEP pathway, synthesizing GGPP from pyruvate, whereas the lower section
  depicts the MVA pathway, synthesizing GGPP from acetyl-CoA. The first ring displays
  the 11 known taxane skeletons, from types I to XI. The second ring shows potential
  intermediates involved in the synthesis of representative compounds. The third (outermost)
  ring presents representative compounds derived from the corresponding skeletons.
  In the color bar, blue indicates taxane skeletons with known and well-defined metabolic
  pathways, such as the biosynthesis of paclitaxel; white indicates unknown taxane
  biosynthetic pathways
