caption: Schematic illustration of the preparation and biological function of DSPM.
  (A) Schematic diagram of the preparation routes of DSPM. (B) Schematic illustration
  of DSPM‐mediated radiosensitization and STING pathway‐dependent antitumor immunity.
  After cellular uptake of DSPM, pH stimuli‐responsive PEG‐polyphenol disassembled
  and released DSNPs and Mn2+. Upon X‐ray irradiation, DSNPs induced cancer cells
  death and resulted in the generation of cytosolic dsDNA. Meanwhile, the released
  Mn2+ sensitized cGAS to cytosolic dsDNA for STING‐mediated IFN‐β production in both
  cancer cells and DCs, resulting in DC maturation and robust antitumor immunity.
