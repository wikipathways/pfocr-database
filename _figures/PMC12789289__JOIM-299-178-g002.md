caption: Somatic mutations in primary aldosteronism (PA). Schematic illustration depicting
  different somatic variants and their consequence in aldosterone‐producing tumors.
  Somatic mutations in KCNJ5, ATP1A1, ATP2B3, MCOLN3, CLCN2, and SLC30A1 alter ion
  transport, resulting in membrane depolarization and elevated calcium concentrations,
  increasing aldosterone synthesis. Calcium influx is deregulated by activating variants
  in voltage‐gated calcium channels, CACNA1D and CACNA1H. CTNNB1 mutations activate
  the WNT pathway, increasing CYP11B2 expression and cell proliferation. CADM1 mutations
  disrupt cell–cell communication through gap junctions, increasing aldosterone production
