caption: 'Immunoregulatory mechanisms of itaconate and its derivatives in macrophages.
  Itaconate and its derivatives modulate both pro-inflammatory and anti-inflammatory
  signaling pathways in activated macrophages. The derivative 4-OI stabilizes NFE2L2
  via electrophilic alkylation of KEAP1, promoting its nuclear translocation and transcriptional
  activation of antioxidant and anti-inflammatory genes. Concurrently, itaconic acid
  and DMI suppress IL6 production by inhibiting the NFKBIZ/IκBζ–ATF3 transcriptional
  axis. 4-OI covalently modifies STING1, inhibiting the STING1–IRF3 pathway and attenuating
  type I interferon signaling. Similarly, both itaconate and 4-OI alkylate TBK1, impairing
  its dimerization and consequently suppressing type I interferon responses. 4-OI
  also targets JAK1 through S-itaconylation, thereby inhibiting downstream STAT activation.
  In addition, 4-OI inhibits NLRP3 inflammasome activation, promotes TFEB nuclear
  translocation to enhance lysosomal biogenesis, and competitively inhibits TET2 demethylase
  activity, influencing epigenetic regulation. In contrast, endogenous itaconate can
  activate type I IFN signaling through two distinct mechanisms. First, inhibition
  of SDH by itaconate promotes mtRNA release via VDAC; cytosolic mtRNA is sensed by
  RIG-I and MDA5, leading to MAVS-dependent recruitment of TBK1, phosphorylation of
  IRF3, and induction of IFNB1. Second, non-covalent inhibition of PRDX5 increases
  mitochondrial ROS, which activate the cGAS–STING1 pathway to induce IFNB1 expression.
  Collectively, these findings highlight the dual, context-dependent roles of itaconate
  in immune regulation.4-OI: 4-Octyl itaconate; ATF3: Activating transcription factor
  3; cGAS: Cyclic GMP-AMP synthase; DMI: Dimethyl itaconate; IFN: Interferon; EIF2S1:
  Eukaryotic translation initiation factor 2 subunit alpha; GSDMD: Gasdermin D; IFNB1:
  Interferon-beta1; IL6: Interleukin 6; IRF3: Interferon regulatory factor 3; JAK1:
  Janus kinase 1; KEAP1: Kelch-like ECH-associated protein 1; MAVS: Mitochondrial
  antiviral signaling protein; MDA5: Melanoma differentiation–associated protein 5;
  mtRNA: Mitochondrial RNA; NEK7: NIMA-related kinase 7; NETosis: Neutrophil extracellular
  trap–mediated cell death; NF-κB: Nuclear factor-κB; NFE2L2: NFE2-like bZIP transcription
  factor 2; NFKBIZ/IκBζ: Nuclear factor of κ light polypeptide gene enhancer in B-cells
  inhibitor ζ; NLRP3: NLR family pyrin domain containing 3; PADI4: Peptidyl arginine
  deiminase 4; PRDX5: Peroxiredoxin 5; RIG-I: Retinoic acid-inducible gene I; ROS:
  Reactive oxygen species; SDH: Succinate dehydrogenase; STING1: Stimulator of interferon
  response cGAMP interactor 1; STAT1: Signal transducer and activator of transcription
  1; TBK1: TANK-binding kinase 1; TFEB: Transcription factor EB; TET2: Tet methylcytosine
