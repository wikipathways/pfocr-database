caption: Displayed is a synthetic myocyte. Store-operated Ca2+ entry (SOCE) is mediated
  through CRAC channels encoded by STIM and Orai. The store-independent pathway is
  mediated through the ARC channel mediated by the same proteins (see text for details).
  The CRAC channel is activated by STIM1 upon ER Ca2+ store depletion by IP3-generating
  agonists. Ca2+ entry through CRAC channels stimulates signaling pathways, including
  NFAT, CaMKIIẟ2, and CREB (the latter likely through Ca2+-dependent activation of
  Adenylyl cyclase 8 and subsequent production of cAMP and activation of PKA) to stimulate
  fibroproliferative gene expression. The ARC channel is activated by arachidonic
  acid (produced downstream receptor activation by either PLA2 or sequential activation
  of PLC and DAG lipase) and more robustly by its downstream metabolite, leukotrieneC4
  (LTC4). ARC channel activation leads to inhibition of AKT signaling to GATA-6 and
  inhibition of contractile gene expression. Created using BioRender
