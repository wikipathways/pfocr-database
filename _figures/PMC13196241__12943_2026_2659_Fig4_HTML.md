caption: Interplay between KRAS and MYC in metabolic reprogramming (Created with BioRender.com).
  A KRAS and MYC synergistically regulate glycolysis to meet elevated energy requirements
  and adapt to metabolic stress. MYC functions as a downstream effector of activated
  KRAS/JAK1/STAT6 and MEK/ERK signaling, rewiring glycolysis in KRAS-driven cancers.
  In KRAS-mutant CRC, high MYG1 expression promotes PKM2 phosphorylation, transcriptionally
  activates MYC, and enhances MYC-driven glycolysis, which is associated with poor
  prognosis. B The role of KRAS and MYC cooperation in lipid metabolism. In LUAD and
  PDAC, PITPNC1 acts as a mediator linking KRAS to MYC via JNK1/2 and MEK1/2 signaling.
  KRAS also alters transcription of metabolic pathways, including oxidative phosphorylation
  and arachidonic acid metabolism, through MNK/P-eIF4E signaling. In addition, KRAS
  activates Wnt/MYC signaling to increase unsaturated fatty acyl groups in phospholipids
  and promote conversion of triacylglycerols to phospholipids. C The role of KRAS
  and MYC cooperation in glutamine metabolism. KRAS induces MYC gene expression by
  activating the RAS/PI3K/ERK pathway and enhances MYC and ARF6 G4-mRNA translation
  by upregulating eIF4A activity
