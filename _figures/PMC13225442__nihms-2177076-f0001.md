caption: Tanycyte EVs regulate feeding rhythms. Choi et. al.4 report that α-tanycytes,
  which line the third ventricle of the hypothalamus, release EVs in a circadian manner,
  increased release in the light phase and decreased release in the dark phase. Tanycyte
  EVs target POMC neurons in the arcuate nucleus (ARC) via prepro-insulin (ppIns)
  displayed on the surface of EVs, which is recognized by the insulin receptor on
  the neuron. Once internalized by the neuron the EV cargo, which includes mTOR pathway
  components like Rictor, signal to induce a satiety signal in the hypothalamus. This
  EV-based mechanism supports healthy rhythmic feeding to maintain body weight and
  metabolic balance
