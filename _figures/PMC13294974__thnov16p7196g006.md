caption: The roles of GRP78 in infectious diseases. In viral infections, csGRP78 assists
  in viral entry as well as subsequent replication, assembly, and maturation. By activating
  the UPR and regulating viral components, it disrupts immune homeostasis, and meanwhile
  connects host signaling pathways to participate in disease initiation and progression.
  In bacterial infections, GRP78, on the one hand, mediates the retrograde transport
  of bacterial toxins into the nucleus; on the other hand, it regulates related signaling
  pathways, inducing Helicobacter pylori-associated mitochondrial damage and Salmonella-induced
  inflammation and immune disorders, respectively
