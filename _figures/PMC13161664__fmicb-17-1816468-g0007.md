  hormone signaling pathways and transcription factors to produce secondary metabolites
  through a complex cross-talk mechanism. While the integrative model provides a conceptual
  framework, future validation requires incorporation of quantitative network analysis
  approaches such as co-occurrence networks, correlation-based microbial interaction

  identify keystone taxa, quantify interaction strengths, and resolve unresolved aspects

  communities needs to be experimentally validated to improve predictive modeling
