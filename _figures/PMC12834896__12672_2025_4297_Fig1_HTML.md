caption: Diagrammatic representation of epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition (EMT).
  Epithelial cells with apical-basal polarity and adherens junctions lose polarity
  and intercellular adhesion under the influence of EMT-inducing transcription factors
  (Snail, Slug, Zeb1/2, Twist), microRNAs, and key signaling pathways (TGF-β, WNT,
  Notch, Hippo). This transition drives the acquisition of a mesenchymal phenotype
  characterized by enhanced motility, invasiveness, and resistance to apoptosis, ultimately
  contributing to cancer progression, metastasis, and CSC-like properties [29]
