caption: 'The physiological role of TLR2/1 signaling in modulating reproductive competence
  and its strong potential for enhancing assisted reproductive technologies (ARTs).
  The Toll-like receptor 2/1 (TLR2/1) signaling pathway functions as a critical regulator
  across key phases of bovine reproduction, offering multiple approach targets for
  ARTs. 1. Enhancing uterine receptivity: Following artificial insemination (AI),
  sperm, semen extender (EX), and components within the seminal plasma (SP) interact
  with bovine endometrium via TLR2/1 to elicit a controlled, weak inflammatory response.
  This physiological reaction is essential for uterine clearance and prepares the
  endometrium for subsequent embryo implantation. 2. Improving gamete and embryo competence:
  In in vitro fertilization (IVF) models, sperm-TLR2 activation (achieved using a
  specific agonist like PAM3CSK4-PAM3) enhances the spermʼs functional capacity, leading
  to improved cleavage and blastocyst rates. 3. This outcome has been demonstrated
  in models such as Wagyu Japanese Black (JB) cattle, highlighting the increased IVP
  yield and offering substantial economic benefits for the commercial embryo transfer
  (ET) industry. 2. Furthermore, direct TLR2 stimulation in early embryos significantly
  increases blastocyst efficiency and developmental competence. Collectively, TLR2
  modulation provides a mechanism to optimize both the maternal environment and the
  essential quality of the gametes and embryos for improved ART outcomes'
