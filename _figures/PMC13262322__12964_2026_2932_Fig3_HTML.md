caption: Mechanistic framework and clinical translation of PARP inhibitor–based combinatorial
  synthetic lethality strategies. Platinum agents and topoisomerase I inhibitors synergize
  with PARP trapping to overwhelm DNA repair in HRD cells; overlapping myelosuppression
  requires optimized dosing. Anti-angiogenic therapy induces tumor hypoxia and downregulates
  HR genes to enhance PARPi sensitivity. The PAOLA-1 trial confirmed prolonged progression-free
  survival in HRD ovarian cancer patients. PARPi-triggered DNA damage activates the
  cGAS–STING pathway, elevates T-cell infiltration and neoantigen presentation. The
  MEDIOLA and TOPACIO trials verified durable efficacy in BRCA-mutant ovarian and
  triple-negative breast cancers. Inhibition of these DDR kinases abolishes the G2/M
  checkpoint and exacerbates replication catastrophe. The CAPRI trial showed this
  combination resensitizes PARPi-resistant high-grade serous ovarian cancer. Epigenetic
  drugs and PI3K inhibitors induce a functional “BRCAness” phenotype to enhance PARPi
  sensitivity in tumors without canonical BRCA mutations
