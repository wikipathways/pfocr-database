  of DEHP/MEHP spans the protein lifecycle: (1) Induction of endoplasmic reticulum
  stress, disrupting proper protein folding; (2) Oxidative stress leading to aberrant
  post-translational modifications (e.g., carbonylation); and (3) Impairment of both
  major degradation systems-the ubiquitin-proteasome system and the autophagy-lysosomal
  pathway. These disruptions converge to cause organelle dysfunction, corrupted signaling
  networks, and ultimately, apoptotic cell death'
