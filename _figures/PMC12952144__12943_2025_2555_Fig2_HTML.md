caption: The dual role of Notch signaling in tumor development. The Notch signaling
  pathway plays both oncogenic and tumor-suppressive roles in the tumor microenvironment.
  (A) The oncogenic functions of the notch pathway. MYBBP1A activates the Notch pathway
  by inhibiting IGFBP4, thereby promoting the EMT of hepatocellular carcinoma. CD146
  upregulates the expression of JAG2 by activating the NF-κB signaling pathway, ultimately
  enhancing the self-renewal ability and chemotherapy resistance of liver cancer cells.
  Ntoch2 promotes tumor cell proliferation by activating TCF7L2 and downstream target
  gene MYC and JUN. CRTC1 enhances the tumor immunosuppressive effect through the
  Notch1/Akt signaling pathway. (B) Tumor suppressor functions of the notch pathway.
  FRMD3 inhibits breast cancer by promoting the degradation of Disheveled-2 and activating
  the Notch signaling pathway. Notch1 inhibits the malignant proliferation of keratinocytes
  by negatively regulating ROCK1/2 and MRCKα. Notch1 inhibits skin cancer by suppressing
  the β-catenin signaling pathway and preventing the continuous expression of Gli2
