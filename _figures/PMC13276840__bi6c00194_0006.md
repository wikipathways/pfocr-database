caption: Proposed β-mannan degradation pathway in B. cellulosilyticus. Polymeric β-mannans
  are depolymerized on the surface of B. cellulosilyticus by the endomannanase BcGH26B,
  and the products are imported into the cell by the SusC-SusD-like transporter proteins.
  In the periplasm, the galactosyl and acetyl decorations are removed by the α-galactosidase
  BcGH36 and the acetyl esterases BcCE7 and BcCE25. The mannanase BcGH26A generates
  mannobiose or short mannooligosaccharides, and the exoacting glucosidase BcGH3 removes
  glucose residues. Mono- and disaccharides are further transported into the cytosol
  by a sugar transporter. Mannobiose is epimerized to mannosyl-glucose by the epimerase.
  The phosphorylase BcGH130_1 phosphorolyses mannosyl-glucose to mannose-1-phosphate
  and glucose. The monosaccharides then enter glycolysis. The specific roles of GH5_2
  and GH5_7 are not displayed due to the lack of functional information
