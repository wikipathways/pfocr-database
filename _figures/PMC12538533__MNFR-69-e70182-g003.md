caption: Overview of the de novo synthesis pathway of sphingolipids. The de novo synthesis
  pathway starts with the condensation of the amino acid serine with palmitoyl‐CoA,
  resulting in 3‐keto‐sphinganine. After further processing, sphinganine is generated.
  Next, ceramide synthases 1–6 add isoform‐specific fatty acids with different chain
  lengths (C14–C32) to the sphingosine backbone, leading to a multiple dihydroceramide
  species. After desaturation, ceramides are produced. The image was created with
