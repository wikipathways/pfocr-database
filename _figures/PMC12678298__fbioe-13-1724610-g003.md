  Sr2+ activates the CaSR, which subsequently triggers downstream signaling cascades,
  including the Wnt/β-catenin, TGFβ/SMAD, and MAPK/ERK pathways, to promote osteogenic
  differentiation of cells. Furthermore, by activating the AMPK/mTOR/LC3B-II signaling
  pathway, Sr2+ enhances cellular autophagy, thereby effectively delaying cellular
