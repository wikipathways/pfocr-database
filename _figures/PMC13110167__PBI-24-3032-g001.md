caption: Aglycone yield improvement and overview of metabolic rewiring. (a–e) Final
  yield of diosgenin and tigogenin, expressed as percentages of dry weight. (a) Diosgenin
  and (b) tigogenin yields in S. nigrum green fruits, and (c) diosgenin and (d) tigogenin
  yields in VIGS‐treated leaves (eGFP, TRV2‐eGFP infiltrated plants; SnGAME25, TRV2‐SnGAME25
  infiltrated plants). (e) Diosgenin yields of S. americanum green fruits of Sagame4
  and TKO (Triple knockout mutant of SaGAME4, SaGAME25‐1 and SaGAME25‐2). The data
  represent the mean concentration (± SD) from five biological replicates, except
  Sagame4 green fruits of which biological replicates are three. Statistical significance
  was determined using a two‐tailed unpaired t‐test. (*, p < 0.05; ***, p < 0.001;
  ****, p < 0.0001; ns, not significant). (f) Schematic representation of metabolic
  pathway rewiring leading to enhanced diosgenin biosynthesis in S. nigrum
