caption: The illustration diagram showing the role of DAO in muscle cell behavior
  and signaling pathways. Mechanical stimulation activates Fbln1 in the extracellular
  matrix (ECM), which influences cell adhesion and migration through integrin receptors
  on the cell membrane. The binding of Fbln1 to integrins triggers the activation
  of Focal Adhesion Kinase (FAK). Once activated, FAK phosphorylates and recruits
  downstream effectors like RhoA/ROCK, leading to cytoskeletal rearrangements and
  mechanical signal transduction. These processes are crucial for maintaining mechanical
  stability, cell adhesion, migration, and differentiation, ultimately affecting muscle
  cell fusion and function. But when DAO combined and inhibite the function of Fbln1,
  the whole mechanical conduction pathway is disturbed
