caption: 'AChR Autoantibody‐Mediated Disruption of Neuromuscular Signaling in MG.
  At the skeletal muscle neuromuscular junction, the ACh‐AChR pathway proceeds as
  follows: Neurotransmitter Release: Nerve impulses arriving at the axon terminal
  activate voltage‐gated Ca2+ channels (VGCC). Ca2+ influx triggers synaptic vesicle
  fusion with the presynaptic membrane, releasing ACh into the synaptic cleft. Receptor
  Activation: ACh diffuses to the postsynaptic membrane (endplate membrane) and binds
  to AChRs (ligand‐gated ion channels). Conformational changes in the receptors open
  the channels, allowing Na+ influx and K+ efflux, generating an endplate potential
  (local depolarization). Action Potential Initiation: The endplate potential propagates
  to adjacent sarcolemma, activating voltage‐gated sodium channels (VGSC). Massive
  Na+ influx triggers a sarcolemmal action potential. Muscle Contraction Initiation:
  The action potential passes through the T‐tubule, dihydropyridine receptor (DHPR)
  undergoes conformational change, activating ryanodine receptors (RyR) on the sarcoplasmic
  reticulum (SR). RyR opening releases Ca2+ into the cytosol. Elevated cytosolic Ca2+
  binds troponin, initiating filament sliding and ultimately muscle contraction. Anti‐AChR‐Ab
  inhibit the ACh‐AChR pathway, prevent action potential generation, and result in
  muscle weakness. AChR antibody triggers AChR endocytosis, CaMKII regulates vesicle
  recycling to the postsynaptic membrane. Skeletal muscle cells express 5‐HT receptors,
  including 5‐HT2AR, which mediates the Gq/11 signaling pathway. 5‐HT binding induces
  receptor conformational changes, recruiting and activating Gq/11 proteins. Activated
  Gq/11 stimulates phospholipase C (PLCβ), catalyzing phosphatidylinositol 4,5‐bisphosphate
  (PIP2) hydrolysis into inositol trisphosphate (IP3) and diacylglycerol (DAG). IP3
  binds IP3 receptors (IP3R), triggering calcium ion release from SR. 5‐HT transporter
  (serotonin transporter, SERT) drive reuptake of 5‐HT and is the target of widely
  used medications to treat neuropsychiatric disorders'
