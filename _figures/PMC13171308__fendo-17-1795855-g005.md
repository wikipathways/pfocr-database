  cohort and should not be interpreted as evidence of a validated human pituitary
  mechanism. Arrows “↑” and “↓” indicate an increase/upregulation or decrease/downregulation,

  E2; dark blue, FT4/T4; orange, TSH; green, T3. Dark red highlights the E2-activated
  cascade, including E2–ER signaling and downstream Dio2/DIO2 and Oatp1c1/OATP1C1-related
  processes. Dio2/DIO2, type II deiodinase; Dio3/DIO3, type III deiodinase; E2, estradiol;
