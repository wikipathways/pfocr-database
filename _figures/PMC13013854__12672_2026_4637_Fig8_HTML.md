caption: The schematic diagram of AFP activating the PI3K/AKT signaling pathway leading
  to liver cancer cells' tolerance to lenvatinib. AFP activates the PI3K/AKT signaling
  pathway, stimulating the expression of glucose metabolism reprogramming key enzymes,
  LDHA, PKM2, HK2 and PFK1 regulated by hypoxia-inducible factor-1α(HIF-1α), and promoting
  the production of lactic acid in HCC cells. AFP can also promote the expression
  of P-pg and Bcl-2. Lactic acid, synergizes with P-gp and Bcl-2, inhibits the killing
  effect of lenvatinib on liver cancer cells
