caption: Schematic representation of the autophagy-lysosome pathway (ALP) in mammals.
  First, the ULK1 complex initiates autophagy, followed by the activation and recruitment
  of the PI3K III complex to promote the phagophore formation. Second, ATG2 and ATG9
  mediate the membrane expansion of autophagosomes. ATG7 (E1), ATG3 (E2), and the
  ATG12-ATG5-ATG16L1 (E3) complex catalyze the conjugation of LC3-I to phosphatidylethanolamine
  (PE), forming LC3-II. Third, LC3-II facilitates the formation of autophagosomes.
  Finally, mature autophagosomes fuse with lysosomes to form autolysosomes, which
  selectively or nonselectively degrade different cargoes, including protein aggregates
  and damaged organelles, thereby releasing the nutrients into the cytoplasm for recycling.
  ULK1, unc51-like kinase 1; PI3K, phosphoinositide-3 kinase; LC3, microtubule-associated
  protein light chain 3. Created with BioRender.com
