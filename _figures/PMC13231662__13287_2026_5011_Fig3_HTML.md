caption: 'T lymphocytes need DC-derived co-stimulatory signals for tumoricidal properties
  (A). It is believed that the PD-1/PD-L1 pathway is directly integral to anti-tumor
  activity in T cells. Tumor cells can exploit PD-1/PD-L1 pathway by upregulating
  PD-L1, which binds PD-1 on T cells, leading to the inhibition of downstream effectors
  such as RAS, PI3K, AKT, and ERK. Along with these changes, the production and release
  of cytokines were diminished, and the tumoricidal properties of T cells (B). By
  the control of ICIs, the mutual interaction between PD-1 and PD-L1 is damped, followed
  by restoring tumor-specific T cell function. TAMs: Tumor-associated macrophages;
  MDSCs: Myeloid-derived suppressor cells; APCs: Antigen-presenting cells; TME: Tumor
  microenvironment; CSC: Cancer stem cells; TCR: T-cell receptor. Reproduced with
  Permission [54]. Biomedicine & Pharmacotherapy. 2024'
