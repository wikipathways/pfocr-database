caption: Some relevant intermediates in the degradation of oxidised guanine. Molecular
  oxygen reacts with guanine to form 8‐oxoG, which can undergo deamination to form
  uric acid for export or undergo further reactions to form dehydroguanidinohydantoin
  (DGh). DGh resembles allantoin, another by‐product of metabolic accidents, that
  is degraded to urea, ammonium and bicarbonate, directly or through various intermediates.
  In one pathway, DGh can degrade to form guanidine and parabanic acid (see text),
  but the stereoisomers and routes remain poorly described, as reflected in inconsistent
  metabolite naming
