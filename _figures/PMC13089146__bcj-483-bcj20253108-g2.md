caption: Stimulation of RTK by insulin or growth factors leads to activation of PI3K,
  that can generate PIP3 from PIP2 at the plasma membrane. Next, PIP3 recruits inactive
  AKT form from cytoplasm to membrane via the binding to AKT PH domain. Once at the
  plasma membrane, AKT is subjected to a series of phosphorylations at Thr308 and
  Ser473 by PDK1 and mTORC2 complex, respectively, resulting in full activation. This
  activation signal is terminated by PIP3 phosphatase PTEN and protein phosphatases
  PP2A and PHLPP
