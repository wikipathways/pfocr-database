caption: The endocytosis and nuclear translocation mechanism of HER3. following activation
  by the ligand NRG on the cell membrane, HER3 forms heterodimers and is internalized
  via a clathrin-independent mechanism. In the absence of ligands, HER3 can also be
  internalized through a clathrin-dependent pathway, which is independent of its phosphorylation
  status. Subsequently, importin β1 recognizes and binds to the NLS of HER3, guiding
  it to the nucleus. At the nuclear envelope, HER3 interacts with nucleoporin 358
  (NUP358) to facilitate its translocation through the nuclear pore. Once inside the
  nucleus, HER2/HER3 together with Stat3 binds to the chromatin and acts as a co-activator
  of the cyclin D1 promoter to exert transcriptional regulatory functions. Ultimately,
  exportin XPO1 mediates the nuclear export of HER3 back to the cytoplasm. HER3 can
  also be internalized in the absence of the ligand in a clathrin-dependent manner
