caption: In tumor cells, FABP4 drives the EMT process through different signaling
  pathways. FABP4 induces the expression of CD36, which indirectly activates the non-canonical
  TGF-β pathway (without affecting the canonical TGF-β/Smad pathway) by mediating
  lipid signaling. This leads to the upregulation of EMT-related transcription factors
  such as Snail and Twist, promotes the expression of mesenchymal markers (including
  N-cadherin, Vimentin, and α-SMA), and is concomitant with the downregulation of
  the epithelial marker E-cadherin, thereby collectively promoting EMT. Furthermore,
  FABP4 overexpression activates the AKT signaling pathway, resulting in increased
  levels of phosphorylated AKT (p-AKT). This activation further upregulates invasion-related
  proteins such as Snail and matrix metalloproteinases MMP-2/9, while downregulating
  E-cadherin, which together facilitate the occurrence of EMT
