caption: Proposed mechanism of TORC1-directed endocytosis. In nutrient-deprived conditions
  or during rapamycin-mediated inhibition, TORC1 is repressed, leading, through an
  unknown pathway, to the TORC2-directed activation of Ypk1. Active Ypk1 facilitates
  α-arrestin–directed CME of Ste2, which ultimately traffics to the vacuole. During
  the pheromone response, TORC2 is also required for endocytosis, after which Atg8
  promotes trafficking of the receptor to the lumen of the vacuole. CME, clathrin-mediated
