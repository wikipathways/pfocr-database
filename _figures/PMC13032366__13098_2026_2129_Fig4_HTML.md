caption: 'Renal protective effects of therapeutic strategies targeting the histone
  modification network in DN. (A) Anti-inflammatory strategies via restoring repressive
  chromatin at pro-inflammatory loci. In DN, increased histone acetylation at inflammatory
  gene promoters contributes to sustained inflammatory transcription. Apelin-13 upregulates
  HDAC1, thereby reducing renal pro-inflammatory histone H3 acetylation marks (H3K9ac,
  H3K18ac, H3K23ac) and ameliorating glomerular dysfunction and inflammatory injury.
  In parallel, inhibition of HDAC6 mitigates DN by suppressing NLRP3-mediated pyroptosis.
  Histone methylation homeostasis also constrains inflammation: hyperglycemia-induced
  loss of the CREB/KMT5A axis reduces the repressive mark H4K20me1, derepressing PTP1B
  and IRAK1, which promotes NF-κB signaling and endothelial inflammation; thus, restoring
  KMT5A/H4K20me1 is predicted to reinforce inflammatory gene silencing. Finally, SUV39H1-dependent
  H3K9me3 may represent a compensatory repressive program in human DN tubulointerstitium,
  suggesting stage-dependent opportunities for sustaining inhibitory methylation marks.
  (B) Anti-fibrotic strategies targeting histone deacetylation, methylation, and ubiquitination
  to suppress pro-fibrotic programs and EndMT. A central anti-fibrotic approach is
  to stabilize or activate protective sirtuins. In mesangial cells, AGEs/RAGE downregulate
  the deubiquitinase USP22, accelerating SIRT1 ubiquitination/degradation; restoring
  USP22–SIRT1 preserves repression of fibronectin (FN) and TGF-β1 expression. In proximal
  tubules, the Nampt–SIRT6 axis functions as another protective pathway; Nampt loss
  lowers SIRT6, and SIRT6 deficiency is sufficient to recapitulate a fibrotic phenotype,
  supporting SIRT6 as a key anti-fibrotic effector. Repressive methylation programs
  centered on EZH2/H3K27me3 restrain fibrosis: lncRNA GAS5 recruits EZH2 to deposit
  H3K27me3 at the MMP-9 promoter, limiting MMP-9 expression and fibrosis; GAS5 loss
  derepresses MMP-9, while GAS5 restoration alleviates fibrosis. Conversely, high
  glucose–induced miR-101b reduces EZH2 (and concomitantly increases demethylases
  JMJD3/KDM6A), shifting chromatin toward H3K27me3 loss and depression of pro-fibrotic/inflammatory
  transcriptional programs. Endothelial-to-mesenchymal transition (EndMT) is promoted
  by reduced KMT5A/H4K20me1, which cooperates with transcriptional programs (e.g.,
  ENO1 and ETS1/PFN2 axes) to drive EndMT; thus, restoring KMT5A is a rational anti-EndMT
  strategy. In addition, drug repurposing highlights histone ubiquitination–methylation
  crosstalk: in STZ-diabetic rats, aspirin reduces the H2A deubiquitinase Mysm1, increasing
  H2AK119ub, which downregulates the pro-fibrotic methyltransferase SET7, thereby
  preventing renal fibrosis. More broadly, altered H2AK119ub/H2BK120ub can modulate
  downstream methyltransferases (e.g., SET7/9 and SUV39H1) to reshape fibrotic chromatin
  states. (C) Epigenetic interventions for podocyte protection through sirtuin activation
  and maintenance of EZH2-dependent repression. Podocyte survival is supported by
  both non-cell-autonomous and cell-intrinsic epigenetic mechanisms. Early diabetes
  downregulates SIRT1 in proximal tubules, weakening tubule-to-podocyte metabolic
  support via reduced nicotinamide mononucleotide (NMN) secretion and compromising
  the glomerular filtration barrier. Within podocytes, SIRT6 is decreased in DN; SIRT6
  restoration promotes a repressive chromatin state via H3K9 deacetylation, suppressing
  pathogenic Notch1/4 and uPAR, while activating PI3K/AKT signaling to enhance cytoskeletal
  stability, autophagy, and anti-apoptotic responses. A selective SIRT6 agonist (UBCS039)
  improves renal injury in db/db mice, supporting druggability. Maintenance of EZH2-mediated
  H3K27me3 is necessary for podocyte survival under diabetic stress. Multiple DN signals
  converge to reduce EZH2/H3K27me3 and derepress injury genes: miR-101 suppresses
  EZH2, derepressing Pax6 and inducing TxnIP; AGEs suppress NIPP1, reducing EZH2 and
  H3K27me3; and LPA/LPAR1 signaling decreases EZH2/H3K27me3 at the Egr1 promoter,
  activating NLRP3 inflammasome-dependent pyroptosis. Pharmacologic inhibition of
  LPAR1 blocks this pathogenic cascade'
