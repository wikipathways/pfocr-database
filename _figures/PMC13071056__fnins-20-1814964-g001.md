  mediates mechanotransduction in response to ECM stiffness. Microglial NF-κB pathway
  drive inflammatory cytokine production. PI3K/AKT signaling exhibits phase-dependent
  effects, contributing to early neuroprotection but potentially promoting fibrosis
  during chronic stages. Crosstalk among inflammatory, mechanical, and proliferative
  pathways shapes scar architecture and regenerative potential
