  process is the synthesis of homogentisic acid (HGA) from methanol via three endogenous
  modules, including the central carbon metabolic pathway (Module I), shikimate pathway

  oxidation and polymerization of HGA into pyomelanin. Xu5P, xylulose-5-phosphate;
