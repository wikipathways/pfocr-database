caption: Biogenesis and classification of EVs. This schematic illustrates the primary
  formation mechanisms of EVs. (a) Exosomes are formed via the endocytic pathway,
  originating as ILVs within MVBs, and are released upon fusion of MVBs with the plasma
  membrane. (b) Ectosomes, including oncosomes, are formed by direct budding and shedding
  from the plasma membrane. (c) EVs interact with recipient cells through various
  mechanisms, including endocytosis, receptor-ligand binding, or direct membrane fusion,
  to deliver their molecular EV-cargo (e.g., proteins, miRNAs) and modulate cellular
  functions. Other EVs subtypes are detailed in Table 1
