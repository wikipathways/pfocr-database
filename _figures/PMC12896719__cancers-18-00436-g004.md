caption: Mechanisms of action of COX-2 inhibitors in the arachidonic acid pathway.
  Ultraviolet (UV) radiation induces arachidonic acid metabolism and upregulation
  of cyclooxygenase-2 (COX-2), leading to increased levels of prostaglandin E2 (PGE2).
  PGE2 promotes cell proliferation, apoptosis, tumor angiogenesis, immune responsiveness,
  and metastasis via VEGF-c. COX-2 inhibitors, such as celecoxib and aspirin, block
  this pathway, thereby modulating these processes and potentially reducing cancer
  progression
