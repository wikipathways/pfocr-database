caption: The biogenesis and functional pathway of microRNAs. The process is categorized
  into three distinct, sequentially linked stages. (A) In the nucleus, RNA polymerase
  II transcribes pri-miRNA, which is processed by the drosha-DGCR8 complex into precursor
  miRNA (pre-miRNA). (B) The pre-miRNA is exported to the cytoplasm via the RanGTP-exportin-5
  complex, where it is enzymatically cleaved by dicer to yield a mature miRNA duplex.
  (C) A mature guide strand is incorporated into the RNA-induced silencing complex
  (RISC), which binds to target mRNA sequences to mediate post-transcriptional gene
  silencing
