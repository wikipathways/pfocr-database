caption: 'The Role of LncRNAs in RCC Progression via the MAPK/ERK, PI3K-Akt and Notch
  Pathways: Insights into Tumor Aggressiveness, Resistance, and Therapeutic Opportunities.
  MAPK/ERK Pathway; oncogenic lncRNAs (e.g., FGD5-AS1, PVT1) promote proliferation
  and metastasis, while tumor-suppressive lncRNAs (e.g., SDPR-AS) inhibit tumor growth.
  Notch Pathway; RP11-567G11.1 enhances tumor progression, while NONHSAT113026 suppresses
  Notch signaling to reduce aggressiveness. p53 Pathway; tumor-suppressive lncRNAs
  (e.g., MEG3) restore p53 activity, while oncogenic lncRNAs (e.g., NEAT1) impair
  its function, driving resistance and progression. Ubiquitin-Proteasome Pathway;
  Both RCAT1 and DUXAP9 influence the ubiquitin-proteasome system, which is crucial
  for degrading HIF-1α under normoxic conditions. Their oncogenic roles in RCC contribute
  to hypoxia-like conditions, further driving HIF-1 synthesis and activity. RCAT1
  alters the ubiquitin-proteasome pathway (UPP), potentially disrupting VHL-mediated
  degradation of HIF-1α, leading to its stabilization. DUXAP9 stabilizes EGFR by inhibiting
  its degradation, activating pathways like PI3K/AKT, which promote HIF-1 synthesis'
