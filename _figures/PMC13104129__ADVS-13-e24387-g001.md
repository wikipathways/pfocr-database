caption: Generation of AA‐containing PLs through PL remodeling. In mammalian cells,
  AA‐containing PLs (AA‐PLs), a major class of PUFA‐PLs, are generated through the
  PL remodeling pathway known as the Lands’ cycle. In the first step, phospholipase
  A2 (PLA2) family enzymes cleave newly synthesized PLs to generate lysophospholipids
  (LPLs) and free fatty acid (FFA). In the second step, AA is activated to AA‐CoA
  primarily by ACSL4, and then re‐esterified into LPLs by LPLATs, such as LPCAT3 or
  MBOAT7, to produce AA‐PLs, which sensitize cells to ferroptosis. This figure was
