caption: Antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects of cynaroside. Regarding antioxidant
  effect, cynaroside increased HO-1 activity and reduced ROS and lipid peroxidation
  by upregulating Nrf2, and favored Fe2+ release. In relation to anti-inflammatory
  effects, cynaroside effectively inhibited the production of NO and PGE2 and inhibited
  the expression of the corresponding enzymes, like iNOS and COX-2, which was associated
  with downregulation of the TLR4/Myd88/NF-κB/AP-1 pathway. Cynaroside blocks IL-1β
  secretion and inhibits the activation of NLRP3 inflammasome by increasing ICEBERG
  (caspase-1 inhibitor) levels. Cynaroside inhibits the expression of inflammation-related
  target genes by inhibiting the damage of STAT3 nuclear translocation, thereby antagonizing
  the JAK/STAT3 pathway activated by IL-22 and IL-6 and reducing the expression of
  inflammation molecules such as ICAM-1, VCAM-1, TNF-α, IL-1β and COX-2
