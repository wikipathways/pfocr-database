caption: Schematic representation of the m6A regulatory network in retinal pathophysiology.
  This model illustrates the central role of m6A RNA modification, mediated by writers
  (W), erasers (E) and readers (R), in coordinating key biological processes within
  the retina. The dynamic interplay of these regulatory proteins influences a network
  of downstream pathways, including epitranscriptomic regulation, inflammatory response,
  angiogenesis and cell survival, which collectively contribute to retinal disease
  pathogenesis. The convergence of these pathways highlights the potential of targeting
  the m6A machinery for therapeutic intervention. m6A, N6-methyladenosine; W, writers;
  E, erasers; R, readers; WTAP, WT1-associated protein; YTHDC1, YTH domain-containing
