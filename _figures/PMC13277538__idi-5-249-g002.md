caption: 'Hypothesis on the mechanisms of HBsAg clearance in HIV/HBV coinfection.
  This diagram compares the immune pathways leading to HBsAg loss in HIV/HBV coinfection
  (left) and HBV monoinfection (right). In coinfection, HIV-driven CD4+ T cell depletion
  disrupts HBV-specific tolerance, and after ART, immune reconstitution clears HBV-infected
  hepatocytes, leading to transient hepatitis flares and eventual HBsAg clearance.
  In monoinfection, steroid priming followed by Peg-IFN-α reactivates the immune system,
  resulting in immune activation, hepatitis flares, and HBsAg clearance. Created with
  BioRender (https://biorender.com/) and permission has been obtained. HBsAg: Hepatitis
  B surface antigen; HBV: Hepatitis B virus; HIV: Human immunodeficiency virus; CD4+
  T cells: Cluster of differentiation 4 positive T lymphocytes; ART: Antiretroviral
  therapy; Peg-IFN-α: Pegylated interferon alpha'
