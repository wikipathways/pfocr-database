caption: a, PmB binds to the OM, causing minor permeabilization, regardless of bacterial
  metabolic activity (insufficient damage to provide PmB with access to the periplasm).
  b, In metabolically active bacteria, PmB triggers LPS release via a process that
  requires ATP-dependent synthesis (Raetz pathway) and transport of LPS (further boosted
  by novobiocin (Nov)). c, The shedding of LPS compromises the integrity of the OM,
  resulting in surface protrusions, and enables ingress of the antibiotic into the
  periplasm where it targets LPS in the IM, leading to permeabilization. d, The loss
  of integrity of both the OM and IM results in bacterial killing
