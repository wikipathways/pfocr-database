caption: Alternative anticancer mechanisms with TTM treatment. Intracellular copper
  transport proteins (Atox1 and ATP7A/B) mediate cellular efflux of cisplatin (CDDP)
  (A). Binding of TTM to these proteins increases intracellular cisplatin level by
  reducing its efflux (B). TTM also inhibits the activity of MAPK kinase kinase-1
  (MEK1) (C) and MAPK/ERK-induced cell proliferation (D). This enhances the effects
  of BRAF-kinase inhibitors (e.g., vemurafenib; Vfb), which inhibit the MAPK pathway
  to suppress the progression of BRAFV600E-induced cancers (E). Atox1, antioxidant
  1 copper chaperone; ATP7A, ATPase copper transporting alpha; ERK, extracellular
  signal-regulated kinase; RAS, rat sarcoma
