caption: 'Direct regulation mechanism of microglial LDs.Homeostasis of microglial
  lipid droplets is regulated through four core direct pathways. In the fatty acid
  uptake process, the fatty acid transporter CD36 on the cell surface recognizes and
  binds extracellular free fatty acids (FFA), internalizing them via endocytosis to
  provide substrates for lipid droplet (LD) synthesis. In the triglyceride (TG) regulatory
  pathway, internalized FFAs are catalyzed by acyl-CoA synthetase (ACSL) to generate
  fatty acyl-CoA, which is then esterified with glycerol-3-phosphate to synthesize
  TGs stored in the LD core. The LD-coating protein PLIN2 anchors to the LD monolayer
  phospholipid membrane via its N-terminal domain, forming a physical barrier, while
  its C-terminal structure binds to comparative gene identification-58 (CGI-58), inhibiting
  the formation of the adipose-triglyceride lipase (ATGL)-CGI-58 complex and thereby
  suppressing lipolysis initiation. When energy mobilization is required, ATGL, hormone-sensitive
  lipase (HSL), and monoacylglycerol lipase (MGL) sequentially catalyze the hydrolysis
  of TGs into diacylglycerol (DAG), monoacylglycerol (MAG), and ultimately release
  FFAs and glycerol (Gly). The autophagy-lysosome pathway selectively degrades LDs:
  the autophagosome membrane protein microtubule-associated protein 1 light chain
  3 (LC3) binds to the LC3-interacting region on the LD surface protein PLIN2, driving
  the encapsulation of LDs by autophagosomes. Subsequently, autophagosomes fuse with
  lysosomes, and lysosomal acid lipase (LAL) is activated in the acidic environment
  to hydrolyze TGs into FFAs and glycerol. In the cholesterol transport process, intracellular
  free cholesterol is reverse-transported out of the cell via the transporters ATP-binding
  cassette transporter A1 (ABCA1) and ABCG1, binding to apolipoprotein A-I (ApoA-I)
  to form nascent high-density lipoprotein (HDL) or to mature HDL particles, respectively,
  thereby completing cholesterol efflux.(TG, Triglyceride; CE, Cholesterol ester;
  PLIN2, Perilipin 2; CGI-58, Comparative gene identification-58; LC3, Microtubule-associated
  protein 1 light chain 3; LAL, Lysosomal acid lipase; ATGL, Adipose-triglyceride
  lipase; HSL, Hormone-sensitive lipase; MGL, Monoacylglycerol lipase; DAG, Diacylglycerol;
  MAG, Monoacylglycerol; Gly, Glycerol; FFA, Free fatty acids; ACSL, Acyl-CoA synthetase;
  ABCA1, ATP-binding cassette transporter A1; ABCG1, ATP-binding cassette transporter
  G1; ApoA-I, Apolipoprotein A-I; HDL, High-density lipoprotein; CD36, Cluster of
