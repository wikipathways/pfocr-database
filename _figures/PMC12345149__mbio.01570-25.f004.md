caption: TLR3 signaling is essential for HSV1 immunity. Schematic representation of
  the TLR3 signaling pathway required for HSV1 immunity. Detection of viral RNA by
  TLR3 results in the recruitment of TRIF, TRAF3 ubiquitination, and recruitment of
  TBK1 and IKK. TBK1 and IKKε phosphorylate IRF3, which translocates and induces expression
  of IFN-β. IFN-β then induces the expression of anti-viral ISGs required for anti-HSV1
  immunity. Loss-of-function mutations in TLR3, TRIF, TRAF3, TBK1, and IRF3 all lead
  to HSV1 susceptibility
