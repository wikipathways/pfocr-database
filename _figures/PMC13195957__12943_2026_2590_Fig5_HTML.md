caption: The dual role of damps in ferroptosis-mediated tumor immunity. This figure
  illustrates the dual immunomodulatory roles of DAMPs released during ferroptosis
  in the tumor microenvironment. On the pro-immunogenic side, DAMPs such as HMGB1
  and calreticulin activate the cGAS/STING pathway and promote antigen presentation,
  M1 macrophage polarization, and CD8⁺ T cell activation. SAPE-OOH is recognized by
  TLR2 on macrophages, enhancing phagocytosis of ferroptotic cells. In contrast, under
  certain conditions, DAMPs such as 8-OHG activate STING signaling and promote M2
  polarization, contributing to tumor progression. Tumor-intrinsic pathways also modulate
  ferroptosis and immune evasion, including TGF-β signaling activated by lncRNA YTHDF3-DT
  in LUAD and ANO1 in gastrointestinal tumors. Meanwhile, IFN-γ released by CAR-T
  cells enhances ACSL4 expression and ferroptosis sensitivity, offering a synergistic
  therapeutic opportunity
