caption: A visual diagram of the DDX41 DNA sensing pathways in HSV-1 infection. DDX41
  modulates the state of cytosolic DNA by unwinding dsDNA and annealing ssDNA. This
  is very important in regulating cGAS activation. Like cGAS, DDX41 can also activate
  the STING-TBK-IRF3 pathway. miR-H2-3p targets DDX41, preventing it from activating
  the STING pathway and reducing the cell’s immune response. DDX41 also binds with
  Receptor-Interacting Serine/Threonine-Protein Kinase 3 (RIPK3) to activate the Mixed
  Lineage Kinase Domain-Like protein (MLKL), which translocates to the cell membrane
  and forms pores, disrupting the cell’s ion balance to cause necroptosis
