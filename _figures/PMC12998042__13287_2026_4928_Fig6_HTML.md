caption: NAMPT-enriched EVs confer superior mitochondrial recovery compared to EV-GFP
  in both in vitro and in vivo models of cisplatin-induced tubular injury. A Schematic
  representation of the EV engineering process. Human ucMSCs were transduced with
  a NAMPT-expressing lentiviral vector, leading to transcription and translation of
  NAMPT and release of NAMPT-enriched EVs through the multivesicular body pathway.
  B Mechanisms of action in RPTECs. After tubular cell uptake, NAMPT-enriched EVs
  increase intracellular NAMPT and elevate NAD⁺ levels via the salvage pathway, where
  NAMPT converts nicotinamide (NAM) into nicotinamide mononucleotide (NMN), which
  is subsequently converted into NAD⁺. This leads to increased expression and activation
  of PGC1α, which promotes mitochondrial biogenesis. Furthermore, NAD+-dependent SIRT3
  upregulation enhances mitochondrial energy metabolism and antioxidant defenses by
  stimulating ATP5i and SOD2, ultimately supporting mitochondrial recovery and cellular
  resilience. Drawings were created using BioRender (https://www.biorender.com/)
