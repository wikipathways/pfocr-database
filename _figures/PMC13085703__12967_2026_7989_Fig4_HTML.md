caption: 'This schematic illustrates the pathogenic role of sphingolipid imbalance
  in four distinct disorders, with Cer as a central mediator. A) AD:​​ SM deficiency
  and Cer accumulation disrupt lipid raft integrity, promoting BACE1 activation and
  Aβ production. Cer-driven neuroinflammation (via NF-κB/IL-1β) and apoptosis exacerbate
  tau pathology and neuronal loss. B) PD:​​ Cer elevation induces mitochondrial dysfunction
  and oxidative stress, contributing to α-synuclein aggregation and dopaminergic neuron
  degeneration. Two pathways converge: one involving nSMase-mediated Cer generation
  and another linking lysosomal impairment to neuroinflammation. C) IBD:​​ Intestinal
  inflammation upregulates Cer synthesis, disrupting epithelial barrier function and
  activating immune cells (e.g., macrophages). Cer amplifies pro-inflammatory signaling
  (NF-κB/TNF-α), perpetuating mucosal damage. D) NAFLD:​​ Hepatic Cer accumulation
  promotes lipotoxicity, insulin resistance, and hepatocyte apoptosis. Cer-driven
  oxidative stress and inflammasome activation exacerbate steatosis, inflammation,
  and fibrosis progression'
