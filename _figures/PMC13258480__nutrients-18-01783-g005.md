  dietary patterns (high in saturated fat, fructose, and ultra-processed foods) drive
  steatosis, lipotoxicity, and oxidative stress, thereby fueling fibrogenic and oncogenic
  signaling. Plant-forward patterns (Mediterranean, polyphenol-rich, coffee) attenuate

  interactions, whereas black arrows indicate pathway progression or biological associations.
