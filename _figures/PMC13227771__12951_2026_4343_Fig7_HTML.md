caption: Schematic of Bio-Cy’s photoimmunotherapy, depicting a self -amplifying cascade
  of pyroptosis and cGAS-STING activation fueling a potent antitumor immune response.
  The unimolecular photosensitizer Bio-Cy induces lysosomal rupture via Type I photodynamic
  action, leading to calcium-mediated mitochondrial damage. This activates caspase-3,
  which cleaves GSDME to trigger pyroptosis. Pyroptotic cell death releases mtDNA,
  which activates the cGAS-STING pathway, driving type I interferon production. Lysosomal
  disruption concurrently inhibits protective autophagy, preventing clearance of damaged
  mitochondria and further amplifying both pyroptosis and STING signaling. This self-reinforcing
  cycle enhances DC maturation and T-cell infiltration, effectively converting “cold”
  tumors into “hot” ones
