caption: 'Correlation between ferroptosis and organelle dysfunction in dry eyeROS
  often plays a pivotal role in ferroptosis, organelle dysfunction, and dry eye. Oxidative
  stress serves as a crucial link between ferroptosis and dry eye, while inflammation
  and oxidative stress are key characteristics of both organelle dysfunction and dry
  eye. Ferroptosis and mitochondrial dysfunction are mutually causal. Moreover, enhanced
  lysosomal activity in phagocytosing lipid droplets can induce ferroptosis through
  GPX4/TFRC/LPO pathways. Leakage of mtDNA can activate cGAS-STING-related inflammation,
  contributing to damage of the ocular surface. Abbreviations: LPO, lipid peroxidation;
  mtDNA, mitochondrial DNA; cGAS, cyclic GMP-AMP synthase; STING, stimulator of interferon
  genes'
