caption: 'The schematic diagram demonstrates the summary of the general roles of S100
  family proteins in regulating cell function. Left: S100 family proteins act intracellularly
  by binding to and modulating the function of a wide range of binding partners, including
  those involved in regulating cytokine and reactive oxygen species (ROS) production
  and release, calcium transport (both through directly binding Ca2+ channels and
  Ca2+ regulators), cell cycle, apoptosis, and adhesion molecule expression, as well
  binding to cytoskeletal components. Right: Secreted S100 proteins can also act as
  DAMPs to signal via an array of cell surface receptors. This binding will activate
  signaling pathways to affect changes in cytokine/ROS production, cell cycle, apoptosis,
  adhesion molecule expression, and changes in cytoskeletal reorganization and migration'
