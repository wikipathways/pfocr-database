caption: Hypothetical mechanisms between inhibition of p38α MAPK and biomarker-related
  changes in cerebral tissue. Amyloid plaques and tau pathology can stimulate microglial
  and astrocyte responses, which in turn lead to tau hyperphosphorylation and neuronal
  damage through the p38α MAPK pathway [15]. In our study, neflamapimod, a selective
  inhibitor of the p38α MAPK, increased short-term reactive astrogliosis via CSF GFAP
  compared to placebo, and lowered CSF p-tau levels compared to baseline within the
  neflamapimod group. This might suggest an effect of reactance in which blocking
  the p38α MAPK pathway might exacerbate the response of astrocytes. Figure created
  using the Mind the Graph platform. CSF refers to cerebrospinal fluid and PET to
  positron emission tomography
