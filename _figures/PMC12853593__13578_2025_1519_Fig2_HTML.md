caption: 'Transcription and Activity of PGC-1α. A PGC-1α Isoform Structure and Transcriptional
  Origin: Transcription from an upstream alternative promoter (AP) of the PGC-1α gene
  leads to the inclusion of new exons 1b or 1b’ (e1b, e1b’). These encode two distinct
  N-termini (aa sequence in pink and light pink) that differ from the canonical exon
  1a (e1a; aa sequence in green), which follows the proximal promoter (PP). An additional
  promoter located in intron 2 (LP) regulates expression of a variant in human liver,
  which includes the novel exon 1L (e1L). A set of brain-specific PGC-1α isoforms
  are expressed under the control of a promoter region (BP) located 587 kb upstream
  of e1a. B Regulation of PGC-1α Transcription: The PGC-1α promoter contains binding
  sites for transcription factors such as myocyte enhancer factor 2 (MEF2), forkhead
  box class-O (FOXO1), activating transcription factor 2 (ATF2), and cAMP response
  element-binding protein (CREB), all of which enhance PGC-1α transcription. These
  factors are modulated by various signaling pathways: insulin activates Akt, leading
  to cytoplasmic sequestration and inhibition of FOXO1; cytokines and exercise activate
  p38-mitogen-activated protein kinase (p38-MAPK), which phosphorylates and activates
  MEF2 and ATF2; exercise also stimulates Ca2⁺ signaling, inducing CREB and MEF2-mediated
  PGC-1α transcription via calmodulin-dependent protein kinase IV (CaMKIV) and calcineurin
  A (CnA); and cold activates β3-adrenergic receptors (β3-AR) in muscle and brown
  fat, leading to protein kinase A (PKA)-mediated activation of CREB. IRS: insulin
  response sequence; GLGN-R: glucagon receptor; P: phosphate; CRE: cAMP response element.
  C Post-Translational Modifications of PGC-1α: p38-MAPK, AKT, GSK3β, and PKA phosphorylate
  PGC-1α, enhancing its transcriptional activity and mitochondrial biogenesis. E3
  ubiquitin ligases, such as the Skp1/Cullin/F-box-cell division control 4 complex
  (SCF), ubiquitinate PGC-1α, targeting it for proteasomal degradation. PRMT1-mediated
  arginine methylation modulates PGC-1α’s coactivator recruitment, affecting gluconeogenic
  gene expression. Nutrient-sensitive O-GlcNAc transferase (OGT) modifies PGC-1α,
  linking cellular glucose levels to its transcriptional output. GCN5 acetylates PGC-1α
  at multiple lysine residues, reducing its interaction with transcription factors
  and suppressing its transcriptional activity. The NAD+-dependent deacetylase SIRT1
  reverses GCN5-mediated acetylation, restoring PGC-1α activity during fasting or
  exercise to promote mitochondrial biogenesis and oxidative metabolism. D Cell Metabolism
  Regulated by PGC-1α: PGC-1α functions as a transcriptional coactivator by interacting
  with multiple nuclear receptors and transcription factors, including PPARs, ERRs,
  NRFs, FOXO1, HNF4α, and GR. Through these interactions, PGC-1α orchestrates diverse
  cellular metabolic processes and physiological adaptations'
