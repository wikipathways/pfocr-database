caption: WRKYs are modulated by post-translational modifications in response to diverse
  signals.(A) WRKY33 is induced by various biotic stress signals and modified through
  phosphorylation (red “P”) and SUMOylation (yellow “S”). WRKY33 is phosphorylated
  and SUMOylated by different MAPK cascades and their associated SUMOylation modules,
  either promoting the expression of defense-related genes such as PAD3 (camalexin
  biosynthesis) or inhibiting anthocyanin biosynthesis genes such as DFR.(B) WRKYs
  are induced by different signals and undergo three types of ubiquitination (dark
  yellow circles labeled “U”) to regulate specific secondary metabolic pathways. (1)
  MdWRKY40 is ubiquitinated and subsequently interacts with MYB TFs to promote anthocyanin
  biosynthesis. (2) Ubiquitin-mediated degradation of VvWRKY24 reduces its binding
  affinity to the promoters of proanthocyanidin biosynthesis genes, resulting in decreased
  proanthocyanidin accumulation. (3) SlWRKY71 interacts with an E3 ubiquitin ligase,
  leading to its degradation, thereby reducing its inhibitory effect on lycopene biosynthesis.
  WRKYs are depicted as gray ovals, interacting with MAPK proteins (rectangular boxes)
  and other signaling molecules (ovals). Arrows indicate activation, while black lines
  ending in bars indicate repression
