caption: IQGAP3-driven activation of Hedgehog and Wnt signaling cascades resulting
  in increased stemness, and cell growth. a The schematic illustrates the role of
  IQGAP3 in regulating GLI1 stability and transcriptional activation of GLI1 target
  genes. IQGAP3 stabilizes GLI1 and promotes its nuclear transcriptional activity,
  while loss of IQGAP3 leads to GLI1 phosphorylation, SUFU binding, and proteasomal
  processing into the repressor form (GLI1R). Nuclear GLI1 activates the transcription
  of downstream target genes, including stemness-associated transcription factors
  NANOG and OCT4, thereby enhancing proliferative and self-renewal capacities. b The
  figure illustrates the dual role of IQGAP3 in regulating β-catenin activity through
  direct interaction with the β-catenin destruction complex and via the CDC42–PAK1
  pathway. (Created in https://BioRender.com)
