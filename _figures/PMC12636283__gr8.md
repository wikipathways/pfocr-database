caption: Schematic diagram illustrates the proposed working model for the anti-complement
  and podocyte-protective effect of MC5R melanocortinergic signaling in THSD7A-associated
  MNIn THSD7A-associated MN, binding of circulating anti-THSD7A antibodies to THSD7A
  expressed on glomerular podocytes triggers complement pathways, where the complement
  amplification loop propagates the signal to sustain a robust complement response.
  Fixation of the terminal complement membrane attack complex C5b-9 on the podocyte
  plasma membrane induces sublytic injury to podocytes and impairs the glomerular
  filtration barrier, leading to glomerular damage and massive proteinuria. MC5R is
  expressed on podocytes and can be activated by various melanocortins, including
  pan-MCR agonists such as ACTH and NDP-MSH, as well as the selective MC5R agonist
  PG-901. MC5R signaling activates PPARγ, which in turn suppresses the upregulated
  expression of CFB and CFD—key mediators of the complement amplification loop—in
  injured podocytes, resulting in an anti-complement effect that protects against
  podocyte injury in MN. This beneficial effect could be abolished by the PPARγ antagonist
  GW9662. In MC5R KO mice, the anti-complement activity of melanocortins is blunted
  but can be partially restored by podocyte-specific MC5R reconstitution or by the
  PPARγ agonist rosiglitazone (RSG)
