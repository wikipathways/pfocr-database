caption: Mechanism of dopamine receptor agonists in enhancing antitumor immune responses.
  Low-dose D1-like receptor agonist (such as SKF-38393) binds to D1-like receptors,
  activating the cAMP-PKA-CREB signaling pathway, which enhances the cytotoxicity
  of NK cells and improves their ability to lyse tumor cells. Meanwhile, low physiological
  concentration (10 nM) D3R agonist (such as 7-OH-DPAT) and D4R agonist (such as PD168077)
  reduce the expression of PD-1 on the surface of T cells by inhibiting the transcription
  of PDCD1 gene, promote T cell proliferation, and enhance the killing activity of
  T cells against tumor cells
