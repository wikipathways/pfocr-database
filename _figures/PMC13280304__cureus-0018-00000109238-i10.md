  signalling through PLCγ2 and NF-κB pathways, promoting B-cell survival and retention
  within lymphoid tissues. BTK inhibitors such as ibrutinib and acalabrutinib block
  this pathway, resulting in impaired adhesion and chemokine signalling. Consequently,
  malignant B cells redistribute from lymph nodes into the peripheral blood, leading
  to transient lymphocytosis.PLCγ2: phospholipase C gamma 2; NF-κB: nuclear factor
