caption: Canonical and non-canonical NF-κB signaling as inflammatory transcriptional
  pathways. The canonical pathway can be activated by inflammatory stimuli such as
  TLR, TNFR, and IL-1R signaling, leading to p65/p50-mediated transcription of inflammatory
  mediator genes. The non-canonical pathway involves NIK-dependent processing of p100
  to p52 and RelB/p52-mediated transcriptional regulation. In this review, NF-κB is
  discussed as a glial inflammatory transcriptional coordinator relevant to AD neuroinflammation,
  rather than as a clinically validated stand-alone therapeutic target
