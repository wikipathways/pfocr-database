caption: AMPK - centered molecular pathways of aerobic exercise - mediated fatty acid
  metabolism regulation. There are three interconnected cascades (a, b, c) activated
  by aerobic exercise. In cascade (a), exercise - induced cAMP elevation activates
  protein kinase A to phosphorylate CREB and phosphorylated CREB drives Cdo1 expression
  and together with Camkk2 activates AMPK to promote fatty acid flux toward utilization.
  In cascade (b), the AMP/ATP ratio increase from exercise directly activates AMPK
  which then phosphorylates ACC to reduce malonyl - CoA and relieve CPT1 inhibition,
  boost mitochondrial β - oxidation of fatty acids and mitigate insulin resistance.
  And in cascade (c), the AMP/ATP rise activates AMPK to suppress the SREBP1c - SCAP
  complex, decrease SREBP1c - mediated Scd1 expression and curb fatty acid synthesis
