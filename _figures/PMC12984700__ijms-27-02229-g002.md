caption: 'Dual-pathway model of mitophagy in cochlear hair cells. Damaged mitochondria
  are cleared via two parallel pathways: (1) Ubiquitin-dependent: Depolarization stabilizes
  PINK1, recruiting Parkin to ubiquitinate OMM proteins. Receptors (OPTN, NDP52) bind
  ubiquitin and LC3 to recruit mitochondria to autophagosomes. (2) Ubiquitin-independent:
  OMM receptors (BNIP3L/NIX, FUNDC1) and IMM receptor PHB2 directly bind LC3 via LIR
  motifs. Both pathways require Drp1-mediated fission and culminate in lysosomal degradation,
  crucial for preventing oxidative stress and ototoxicity'
