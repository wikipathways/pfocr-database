caption: Nplp1+ cells in the imaginal disc respond to warm temperatures by increasing
  Nplp1-RC expression. Nplp1-ALH, the Nplp1-RC-specific peptide, suppresses the PERK/ATF4/Yki/Dilp8
  pathway in imaginal disc cells, allowing ecdysone signaling and promoting normal
  organ growth. In Nplp1 mutant larvae, warm temperatures activate the PERK/ATF4/Yki/Dilp8
  pathway. This then suppresses ecdysone synthesis, cell growth, and proliferation,
  while also activating apoptosis, resulting in smaller imaginal discs with developmental
  retardation. Thor has been implicated to inhibit cell growth previously, but our
  study found no changes in cell size of Nplp1 mutant imaginal discs
