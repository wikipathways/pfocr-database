caption: The NRF2-mediated ferroptosis signaling pathway. In the pathway illustrated
  on the left, mTORC1, p62, and DPP9 interact to modulate NRF2 activity by regulating
  Keap1. NRF2 functions as a key transcription factor that associates with sMaf and
  binds to the Antioxidant Response Element (ARE), thereby activating the expression
  of its downstream target genes. The activity of this pathway is antagonized by BACH1,
  while it is positively regulated by oncogenic signals such as KRASG12D, RAF V619E,
  and MycERT2. NRF2 activation induces the expression of HO-1 (heme oxygenase 1),
  which subsequently influences the Fenton reaction - an iron-dependent process driving
  lipid peroxidation - thereby contributing to the regulation of ferroptosis
