caption: 'The potential mechanisms for CBD antioxidant activity. The left panel delineates
  three primary mechanisms of CBD antioxidant action in vitro: (a) direct radical
  scavenging, (b) suppression of pro-oxidant enzyme activity, and (c) chelation of
  metal ions, which is associated with the Fenton reaction. Conversely, the right
  panel depicts the mechanisms by which CBD modulates the Nrf2/Keap1/ARE pathway in
  cellular contexts. •OH, hydroxyl radicals; O2−•, superoxide; H2O2: hydrogen peroxide;
  ETC: electron transport chain; NOX, NADPH oxidases; XO, Xanthine oxidase; Nrf2,
  Nuclear factor erythroid 2-related factor 2; Keap1, Kelch-like ECH-associated protein
  1; Cul3: Cullin-3; AMPK: AMP-activated protein kinase; SIRT3: Sirtuin-3; ROS: Reactive
  oxygen species; Mafs, small Maf protein; ARE, antioxidant response element; NQO1,
  NAD(P)H dehydrogenase [quinone] 1; GSH-Px, Glutathione peroxidase; SOD, Superoxide
  dismutase; GSH, glutathione; CAT, catalase; HO-1, heme oxygenase-1. Closed arrows
  indicate inhibition; open arrows indicate activation'
