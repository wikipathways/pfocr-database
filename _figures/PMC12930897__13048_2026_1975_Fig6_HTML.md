caption: Effect of SS-31 supplementation on metabolome in aged oocytes. A Orthogonal
  partial least squares discriminant analysis (OPLS-DA) of oocytes from aged mice.
  Red circles represent samples from aged oocytes (n = 7), and green circles represent
  samples from SS-31 + aged oocytes (n = 6). B Volcano plot showing differential metabolites
  in SS-31 + aged oocytes compared with aged ones. C KEGG enrichment analysis of differential
  metabolites in SS-31 + aged oocytes compared with aged ones. D Heatmap illustration
  showing differential metabolites in the Beta-alanine metabolism pathway in SS-31 + aged
  oocytes compared with aged ones. E Heatmap illustration showing differential metabolites
  in the arginine and proline metabolism pathway in SS-31 + aged oocytes compared
  with aged ones. F Violin plot displayed levels of Spermidine, D-glucose, deoxyribose
  and Glutathione in SS-31 + aged oocytes compared with aged ones. G HMDB Primary
  pathways analysis displays that SS-31 treatment promote Glutathione metabolism.
  Compared with the aged group, red indicates that the metabolite content is significantly
  up-regulated in the SS-31 + aged group, whereas blue indicates that the metabolite
  content is significantly down-regulated in the SS-31 + aged group
