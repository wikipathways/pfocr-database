caption: Kv10.1 channel regulates the hypoxia-induced migration and -EMT in breast
  cancer cells. Schematic overview of Kv10.1 channel-mediated signaling pathways in
  MDA-MB-231 cell. At the center of this interplay, a positive loop between HIF-1α
  and Kv10.1 channel. HIF-1α translocating into the nucleus it induces expression
  of pro-invasive genes and EMT transcription factors. Additional signaling aggressiveness
  pathways include a Kv10.1-driven activation of ZEB-1 affecting EMT via nuclear translocation
  and a separate signaling pathway via integrin-mediated activation of PFAK, engaging
  PERK to promote cell migration. A direct signal to PAKT is also illustrated. The
