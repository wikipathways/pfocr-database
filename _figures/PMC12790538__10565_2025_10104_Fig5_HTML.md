caption: Mechanisms of pyroptosis in breast cancer. This diagram illustrates canonical
  and non-canonical signaling cascades that activate pyroptosis in breast cancer.
  The NLRP3 inflammasome, assembled with ASC and procaspase-1 and regulated by MEG3,
  cleaves and activates caspase-1, while caspase-4/5/11 provide non-canonical input.
  Activated caspases cleave gasdermin D (GSDMD), releasing its N-terminal fragment
  that forms membrane pores to execute pyroptosis. In parallel, mitochondrial ROS
  accumulation and cytochrome c release, regulated by STAT3, Bak, and BCL-2, activate
  caspase-9 and caspase-8, leading to caspase-3 activation and subsequent GSDMD cleavage.
  Together, these pathways highlight pyroptosis as an immunogenic cell death modality
  with potential therapeutic relevance in TNBC
