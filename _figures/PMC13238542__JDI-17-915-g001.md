caption: Proposed model linking c‐Jun N‐terminal kinase 3 (JNK3) to glucagon‐like
  peptide‐1 (GLP‐1) receptor signaling competence and β‐cell protection. In β‐cells,
  GLP‐1 receptor (GLP‐1R) activation stimulates the canonical Gs–cyclic adenosine
  monophosphate (cAMP) pathway, resulting in cAMP response element‐binding protein
  (CREB) phosphorylation and insulin receptor substrate 2 (IRS2) induction, with downstream
  effects on incretin‐potentiated insulin secretion and protection against cytokine‐induced
  apoptosis. The proposed model suggests that JNK3 acts as a permissive regulator
  of this signaling system by maintaining GLP‐1R expression and supporting the intracellular
  signaling competence required for effective GLP‐1 action
