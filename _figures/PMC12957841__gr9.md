caption: 'Mechanism of farnesylation in bone metabolic diseases. In OP, FTI-277 inhibits
  the farnesylation of prelamin A and HDJ-2, prevents osteoblast differentiation,
  and finally induces OP. In RA, FTI-276 inhibits protein farnesylation, downregulates
  MMP-1, JNK and NF-κB, and ultimately relieves RA. In OS, NZ inhibits Ras farnesylation
  and downregulates the Akt/mTOR and ERK1/2/HIF-1α signaling pathways, thereby increasing
  the expression of ABCA1 and decreasing the expression of ABCB1, accelerating cell
  apoptosis and ultimately relieving OS. HDJ-2: human dnaJ homolog 2. ERK1/2: extracellular-signal-regulated
  kinase 1/2. MMP-1: matrix metalloproteinase-1. ABCB1: ATP binding cassette transporter
  B1. ABCA1: ATP binding cassette transporter A1'
