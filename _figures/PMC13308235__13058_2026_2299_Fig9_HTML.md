caption: Kaiso suppresses AR expression in TNBC. This schematic illustrates the canonical
  AR signaling pathway and highlights a potential regulatory role of Kaiso in modulating
  AR activity. Under normal conditions, Kaiso represses AR expression and limits AR
  nuclear signaling. In the absence of Kaiso, AR expression levels increase and promotes
  nuclear translocation. Elevated AR activity is associated with the reduced expression
  of the AR-responsive gene, ACSL4. Enzalutamide inhibition of AR is shown for context,
  demonstrating some of the points at which it can block AR signaling. Created in
