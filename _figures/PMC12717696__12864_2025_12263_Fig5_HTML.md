caption: 'Hypothesis of the regulatory pattern of exogenous melatonin on the hair
  follicle anagen phase in Inner Mongolia cashmere goats. Solid arrows represent the
  normal hair follicle growth cycle, transitioning from the anagen phase to the catagen
  phase and then to the telogen phase. Dashed arrows (M) indicate the secondary shedding
  process influenced by melatonin, which may accelerate or alter this cycle. Nodes
  represent differentially expressed genes in the anagen phase, with bar plots displaying
  the average log₂FoldChange from DESeq2 analysis for the S1, S2, and S3 stages. Red
  and light red bars indicate differentially upregulated genes at the respective stages,
  blue bars indicate differential downregulation, and gray bars indicate no differential
  expression at the corresponding stage. Connections between nodes represent gene
  interactions based on the STRING database, with line thickness reflecting confidence
  levels (minimum required interaction score: 0.700). Clusters are highlighted by
  colored circles: pink (hair follicle and epithelial tissue development), purple
  (WNT signaling pathway), green (skin morphogenesis), blue (ECM receptor interactions),
  and gray (second shell interactors)'
