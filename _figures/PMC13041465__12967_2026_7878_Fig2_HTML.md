caption: 'Mechanisms of action of MSC in microglia: a,b MSCs can convert M1-type inflammatory
  cytokines, such as inducible nitric oxide synthase (iNOS) and CD16, into M2-type
  anti-inflammatory factors, such as arginase-1 (arg-1) and CD206; inhibition of the
  HMGB1/TLR4 signaling pathway by HucMSC - Exos through miR-126 reduces the expression
  of HMGB1 and its downstream target protein, NLRP3 inflammatory vesicles, NF-κB p65,
  and inflammatory factors, such as caspase-1, IL-1β, and IL-18, in retina, thus slowing
  down the inflammatory response of diabetes. HucMSC-exos inhibited the HMGB1/TLR4
  signaling pathway through miR − 126, which reduced the expression of HMGB1 and its
  downstream target protein NLRP3 inflammatory vesicles, NF-κB p65, and inflammatory
  factors such as caspase-1, IL-1β and IL-18 in the retina, thus slowing down inflammatory
  responses in the diabetic retina; and BMSC-exos inhibited the expression of TLR4
  by activating the TLR4/NF-κB signaling pathway via miR-486-3p, which suppressed
  oxidative stress, inflammation, and apoptosis of the retinal cells; c:anti-apoptotic
  process of MSCs; lncRNA SNHG7-derived MSCs inhibit endothelial-mesenchymal transition
  and neovascularization in human retinal microvascular endothelial cells through
  the miR-34a-5p/XBP1 axis, attenuating diabetes-induced retinal neuropathy and vasculopathy;overexpression
  of Sirt3 protects the RPE through the FOXO3a/PINK1-parkin pathway to inhibit apoptosis.
  d: inhibition of neddylation expression by MSC-exo led to activation of the nuclear
  factor erythroid 2-related factor (Nrf2) signaling pathway via miR-143-3p, which
  reduced reactive oxygen species (ROS) and decreased retinal inflammation, thereby
  reducing diabetic retinopathy. MSC-exo inhibited the expression of neddylation in
  human retinal microvascular endothelial cells, which reduced diabetic neuropathy
  and vasculopathy'
