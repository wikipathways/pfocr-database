caption: Scheme illustrating the synthesis of A@MCP nanoparticles and their mechanism
  of action, which induces pyroptosis and activates the cGAS-STING pathway to potentiate
  antitumor immunity and enhance the efficacy of radioimmunotherapy.In the acidic
  tumor microenvironment, A@MCP nanoparticles release Ca²⁺, Mn²⁺, and the demethylating
  agent Azacitidine. Azacitidine upregulates GSDME expression, enabling caspase-3-mediated
  pyroptosis upon calcium overload-induced mitochondrial damage. Pyroptosis releases
  mtDNA, which activates the cGAS-STING pathway, with Mn²⁺ acting as a potent STING
  agonist to amplify TBK1/IRF3 phosphorylation and type I interferon production. This
  dual activation synergizes with radiotherapy to promote DC maturation and CTL priming,
  overcoming immunosuppression
