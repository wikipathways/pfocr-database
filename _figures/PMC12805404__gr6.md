caption: 'Sensory neuronimmune crosstalk in the skin and lung. In the skin, TRPV1⁺
  sensory neurons release SP, which drives mast cell degranulation and primes Th2
  cells via CD301b⁺ dendritic cells. In the lung, sensory neurons rapidly activate
  ILC2s through NMU signaling. They also promote humoral immunity by inducing SP-mediated
  IgE class switching in B cells. Concurrently, FcεRI⁺ vagal nociceptors enhance Th2
  cytokine responses upon detecting IgE–allergen complexes. Furthermore, lung-innervating
  neurons can suppress excessive type 2 inflammation via the JAK1–CGRPβ pathway, highlighting
  the bidirectional regulation by sensory nerves in atopic disease. CGRPβ: Calcitonin
  gene-related peptide β; DCs: Dendritic cells; FcεRI: Fc epsilon receptor I; JAK1:
  Janus kinase 1; IgE: Immunoglobulin E; IL-5: Interleukin-5; IL-13: Interleukin-13;
  ILC2: Type 2 innate lymphoid cell; MRGPRA1: Mas-related G protein-coupled receptor
  A1; MRGPRB2: Mas-related G protein-coupled receptor B2; Nav1.8: Voltage-gated sodium
  channel 1.8; NMU: Neuromedin-U; NMUR1: Neuromedin-U receptor 1; SP: Substance P;
  Tacr1: Tachykinin receptor 1; Th2: T helper 2 cell; TRPV1: Transient receptor potential
  vanilloid 1. This figure was created with BioRender.com'
