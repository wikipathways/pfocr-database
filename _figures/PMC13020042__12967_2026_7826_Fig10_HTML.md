caption: 'Graphical illustration of mechanism mediating anti-hyperlipidemia effects
  of Limonin. The possible mechanisms by which limonin alleviates dyslipidemia are
  as follows: (1) Reduced relative abundance of BSH-producing microbes induced by
  Limonin. (2) Boosted conjugated BAs, particularly TCA, T-αMCA, and T-βMCA acting
  in antagonistic manner on intestinal FXR, (3) The increased conjugated BAs boosted
  elimination of BAs via feces, and also as endogenous signaling molecules that inhibited
  activation of intestinal FXR causing FGF15’s reduced production in the distal ileum.
  (4) The FGF15 through the enterohepatic circulation to enter mice liver, and regulates
  liver CYP7A1 expression via negative feedback of FGF15-FGFR4 pathway to promoting
  the synthesis of TC to BA, thereby reducing TC'
