caption: The FXR activation pathway. Conjugated and unconjugated bile acids are able
  to activate the FXR receptor, with the first utilising IBAT, whereas the latter
  can pass through the membrane. Upon activation, FXR binds together with RXR to induce
  the transcription of SHP, IBABP, and FGF19. The presence of SHP inhibits both the
  formation of the NLRP3 complex and the production of bile acids. The activation
  of FXR also leads to the inhibition of IBAT, the inhibition of macrophage infiltration,
  and the expression of tight junctions (TJs). Created in BioRender. Te velde, A.
