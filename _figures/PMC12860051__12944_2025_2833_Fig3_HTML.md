caption: 'FABP4 and the nuclear receptor PPARγ engage in a bidirectional feedback
  loop within the nucleus: FABP4 transports fatty acids to the nucleus to activate
  PPARγ, which in turn transcriptionally upregulates FABP4 expression. FABP4 cooperatively
  promotes the expression of itself and other fatty acid transporters (e.g., CD36,
  FATP), enhancing cellular fatty acid uptake. It also directs fatty acids to specific
  organelles: to mitochondria for β-oxidation and energy production, to the endoplasmic
  reticulum for lipid synthesis, or back to the nucleus to activate nuclear receptors
  and regulate gene expression. In tumor cells, aberrant lipid metabolism generates
  reactive oxygen species (ROS) and lipid intermediates. Enhanced β-oxidation contributes
  to ROS accumulation and oxidative stress. FABP4 alleviates this stress by promoting
  free fatty acid (FFA) storage in lipid droplets, thereby reducing lipid peroxidation.
  Furthermore, ROS can activate the HIF-1α and NF-κB pathways, promoting the transcription
  of pro-angiogenic factors like VEGF and PDGF. Studies in uterine leiomyoma indicate
  that adipocytes, via FABP4-mediated fatty acid transfer and the secretion of factors
  such as IL-1β and TNF-α, synergistically activate the NF-κB pathway in tumor cells,
  promoting inflammation and angiogenesis'
