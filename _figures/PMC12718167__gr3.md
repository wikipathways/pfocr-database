caption: 'Proteoglycan-mediated regulation of autophagy and angiogenesis in the tumor
  vascular microenvironment(A) Decorin exhibits dual regulatory functions through
  VEGFR2 binding: it promotes autophagy via AMPK activation and mTOR inhibition, while
  simultaneously exerting anti-angiogenic effects through disruption of multiple growth
  factor signaling pathways.(B) Endorepellin, the C-terminal fragment of perlecan,
  triggers autophagy through VEGFR2-mediated signaling, leading to AMPK activation
  and mTOR inhibition, while inhibiting angiogenesis.(C) Full-length perlecan acts
  as an anti-autophagic molecule by activating mTOR and promotes angiogenesis by facilitating
  VEGFA-VEGFR2 binding.(D) Endostatin induces autophagy through α5β1-integrin interaction
  while inhibiting angiogenesis by preventing VEGFA-VEGFR2 binding. This dual action
  involves modulation of downstream signaling pathways including ERK, FAK, and p38/MAPK.(E)
  Biglycan induces autophagy through TLR4 and CD44-mediated signaling in macrophages,
  antagonizes endostatin’s effects, and promotes angiogenesis by enhancing VEGFA-VEGFR2
  signaling'
