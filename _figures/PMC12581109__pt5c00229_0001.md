caption: mTOR interacting with IR, AMPK and AMP. Triggering growth factors and insulin
  stimulate receptors on the cell membrane, activating Ras and PI3K. These pathways
  reduce TSC1/2 dephosphorylation and inactivation of mTORC1. The Ras pathway results
  in inhibitory activity of ERK and RSK on TSC1/2, while the PI3K/AKT pathway can
  be independently activated by mTORC2. TSC1/2 is responsible for the deactivation
  of mTORC1 by dephosphorylating and dissociating Rheb, which remains reversibly available.
  DNA damage and reduced intracellular energy stores accelerate the inhibitory activity
  of TSC through p53 and direct activity on AMPK. mTORC1 modulates expression of proteins
  responsible for cell growth and proliferation
