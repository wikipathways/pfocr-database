caption: Mechanism of mucosa-associated lymphoid tissue translocation gene 1 (MALT1)
  in glioblastoma (GBM) and its role in nuclear factor kappa-B (NF-κB) activation.
  MALT1 is a paracaspase involved in regulating the NF-κB signaling pathway, which
  plays a crucial role in GBM progression. MALT1 mediates the proteolytic cleavage
  of TNFAIP3 (A20), CYLD, and V-Rel reticuloendotheliosis viral oncogene homolog B
  (RelB), promoting the activation of NF-κB. The activation of NF-κB leads to the
  upregulation of genes involved in immunity, inflammation, cell proliferation, differentiation,
  and apoptosis, contributing to tumor proliferation and invasion
