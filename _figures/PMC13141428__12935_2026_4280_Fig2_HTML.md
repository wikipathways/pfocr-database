caption: 'Integrated schematic of EGFR-dependent oncogenic signaling pathways and
  their regulation by microRNAs in NSCLC proliferation and survival. The diagram illustrates
  three major intracellular cascades activated downstream of the epidermal growth
  factor receptor (EGFR): (i) the PI3K/AKT/mTOR pathway, (ii) the MAPK/ERK pathway,
  and (iii) the JAK/STAT pathway. Although represented as separate modules for clarity,
  all three pathways initiate from the same upstream EGFR dimer, which is shown once
  but functionally serves as the shared receptor for each signaling axis. Ligand binding
  induces EGFR activation, leading to downstream phosphorylation events that drive
  proliferation, survival, metabolic adaptation, and transcriptional reprogramming.
  The miRNAs positioned directly adjacent to the EGFR receptor — shown in pink to
  distinguish them from pathway-specific regulators — modulate EGFR expression or
  activation itself. Arrowheads indicate activation, and blunt ends indicate inhibition.
  EGFR, Epidermal growth factor receptor; RTK, Receptor tyrosine kinase; PI3K, Phosphoinositide
  3-kinase; PIP2, Phosphatidylinositol-(4,5)-bisphosphate; PIP3, Phosphatidylinositol-(3,4,5)-trisphosphate;
  PTEN, Phosphatase and tensin homolog; PDK1, Phosphoinositide-dependent kinase 1;
  AKT, Protein kinase B; FOXO1/3, Forkhead box protein O1/3; mTOR, Mammalian target
  of rapamycin; FAK, Focal adhesion kinase; GRB2, Growth factor receptor–bound protein
  2; SOS1, Son of sevenless homolog 1; NRAS, Neuroblastoma RAS viral oncogene homolog;
  KRAS, Kirsten RAS viral oncogene homolog; ABC-RAF, RAF proto-oncogene serine/threonine-protein
  kinase (A-, B-, C-RAF); BCL-2, B-cell lymphoma 2; MEK1/2, Mitogen-activated protein
  kinase kinase 1/2; ERK1/2, Extracellular signal-regulated kinase 1/2; IL-6, Interleukin-6;
  IL-6R, Interleukin-6 receptor; JAK2, Janus kinase 2; STAT3, Signal transducer and
  activator of transcription 3'
