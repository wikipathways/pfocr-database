caption: The role of SUMOylation in the regulation of the ER. SUMOylation plays a
  pivotal role in maintaining ER homeostasis by precisely modulating the three core
  pathways of the UPR. (A) In the PERK pathway, PERK can sense ERS abnormalities.
  SUMOylation influences the degradation of DRP1 via the regulation of SENP3-mediated
  SUMOylation on DRP1. Additionally, by modifying key proteins such as EIF2α, ATF4,
  and TEX264, it participates in ER-associated degradation processes, thereby preserving
  ER homeostasis. (B) In the ATF6 pathway, under physiological conditions, ATF6 is
  bound to BIP. Upon ERS, ATF6 dissociates from BIP and is transported to the Golgi
  apparatus for cleavage and activation. SUMOylation dynamically regulates the activation
  process of ATF6. Specifically, SENP2-mediated SUMOylation of MAGI1 influences the
  localization and function of ATF6. Moreover, PIAS1-mediated SUMOylation of ATF6(N)
  affects its responsiveness to ERS. (C) In the IRE1α-XBP1 pathway, upon activation
  of IRE1α, XBP1 mRNA undergoes splicing. SUMOylation plays a bidirectional regulatory
  role in this process. Specifically, the SUMOylation of xbp1u mediated by PIAS2 facilitates
  its degradation, whereas SENP1-mediated deSUMOylation of XBP1s enhances its stability,
  thereby precisely modulating the expression of ERS-related genes
