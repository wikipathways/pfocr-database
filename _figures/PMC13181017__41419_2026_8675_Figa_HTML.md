caption: Schematic model demonstrated that Plasmodium infection suppressed autophagy
  in lung cDC2 via the TLR3/7-STAT3-IRF4 signaling pathway. Autophagy deficiency caused
  abnormal accumulation of cDC2 in lungs, impairing their phagocytosis capacity and
  clearance of malaria parasites. IL-12 secreted by cDC2 drove Th1-biased T-cell differentiation,
  which exacerbated pro-inflammatory responses and increased immunopathological damage
  in lung tissues. Inhibition of STAT3 or knockout of IRF4 within cDC2 restored cDC2
  autophagy, reduced aberrant accumulation, re-established the phagocytic function
  of cDC2, and attenuated pro-inflammatory responses of Th1 cells, ultimately alleviating
  lung inflammatory damage
