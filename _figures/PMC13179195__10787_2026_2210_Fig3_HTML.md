caption: 'Il-6 signaling pathways in the pathogenesis of epileptogenesis. This diagram
  details how interleukin-6 (IL-6) acts as a pivotal mediator of the long-term neurological
  shifts seen in epilepsy. Triggered by stressors and cytokines like TNF-α and IL-1β,
  activated microglia and neurons secrete IL-6, which then engages the JAK/STAT3 signaling
  pathway. This activation precipitates a triple threat of pathological changes: it
  disrupts synapses by ramping up glutamate release, reshapes brain structure through
  gliosis and impaired neurogenesis, and compromises the blood-brain barrier via CCL2.
  Together, these mechanisms transform a localized inflammatory response into a broader
  state of network hyperexcitability and cognitive decline'
