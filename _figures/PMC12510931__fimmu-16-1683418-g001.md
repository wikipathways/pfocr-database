caption: Proposed mechanisms of DNT cell development. Four proposed theories exist
  on the origin of DNT cells. Theory 1 suggests that CD4+ or CD8+ T cells in the periphery
  may downregulate their co-receptors, possibly through demethylation, leading to
  the formation of DNT cells. Theory 2 proposes that an incomplete transition from
  DN4 to C stages in the thymus results in DNT cells migrating to the periphery. Theory
  3 posits that HSCs directly mature into DNT cells in the periphery. Theory 4 indicates
  that CLPs migrate to the periphery and develop into DNT cells utilizing an alternate
  thymic pathway. DN, double negative stage; DP, double positive; HSCs, hematopoietic
  stem cells; CLPs, common lymphoid progenitors
