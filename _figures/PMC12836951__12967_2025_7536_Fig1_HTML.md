caption: 'Molecular regulatory mechanisms of the cGAS-STING pathway. (A) Canonical
  pathway: Cytosolic dsDNA derived from pathogens, dead cells, or mitochondrial injury
  is recognised by cGAS, which catalyses the conversion of ATP and GTP into the cyclic
  dinucleotide cGAMP. cGAMP binds to the ER-resident adaptor STING, triggering its
  conformational activation and translocation to the ER-Golgi intermediate compartment
  (ERGIC) and Golgi apparatus. Activated STING recruits TBK1 and IRF3, leading to
  the phosphorylation of IRF3 and the subsequent activation of NF-κB. (B) Non-canonical
  regulation: (a) Translational regulation: Upon activation, STING stimulates the
  PERK-eIf2α-ATF4 axis on the er membrane, selectively promoting ATF4 translation
  and linking cGAS-STING signaling to the integrated stress response. This event occurs
  upstream of TBK1–IRF3 activation and independently of the unfolded protein response.
  (b) Autophagy regulation: STING activation can induce autophagy via both canonical
  and non-canonical pathways. Non-canonical STING-dependent autophagy involves a direct
  interaction with LC3 through its LIRs, which is dependent on WIPI2 (Controversy
  exists) and the ATG5–ATG12–ATG16L1 complex'
