caption: Molecular and metabolic features of the MPS1and MPS2 subtypes. MPS1 cell
  lines, characterized by frequent mutations in the PI3K and RTK-RAS pathways, are
  sensitive to inhibitors of lipid synthesis such as C75. Activation of the PI3K pathway
  could drive nutrient uptake and enhance lipid synthesis in tumor cells. In the MPS2
  subtype, metabolic reprogramming is driven by genomic alterations that enhance glycolysis.
  Key therapeutic targets include the glucose transporter SLC2A1 and LDHA, whose inhibition
  disrupts glycolytic flux and promotes cancer cell apoptosis. Furthermore, LDHA blockade
  enhances anti-tumor immunity by increasing CD8+ T and NK cell infiltration, thereby
  sensitizing tumors to anti-PD-1 immunotherapy
