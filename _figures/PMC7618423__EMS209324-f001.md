caption: 'A. Modulation of mismatch repair status can occur through specific inhibitors
  or extrinsic factors such as hypoxia, inflammation, regulatory RNAs, pathogens,
  genotoxic metabolites, and therapeutic pressure. These influences may induce a transition
  from MMR proficiency to deficiency, promoting immune activation via the cGAS–STING
  pathway and enhancing the recruitment of T and NK cells. B. Therapeutic strategies
  targeting MMR deficiency exploit the immunogenic landscape of MMRd tumors and the
  vulnerabilities arising from disrupted DNA repair mechanisms. These tumors are amenable
  to targeted interventions, including WRN helicase inhibition, NMD blockade and both
  personalized and shared neoantigen-based vaccines aimed at boosting immune recognition
  and improving response to ICB. PTC: premature termination codons. A and B Created
