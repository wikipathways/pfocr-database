caption: Schematic representation of the PI3K/AKT/mTOR signaling pathway. Activation
  of RTKs or GPCRs by extracellular ligands (e.g., IGF, EGF) leads to phosphorylation
  of PI3K via IRS, resulting in the conversion of PIP₂ to PIP₃. PIP₃ facilitates the
  membrane recruitment and phosphorylation of AKT by PDK1 and mTORC2. Activated AKT
  inhibits TSC1/2, activating Rheb and promoting mTORC1 signaling, which enhances
  cell growth and protein synthesis. Negative regulation occurs via PTEN and AMPK,
  which suppresses mTORC1 via TSC1/2. mTORC2 also reinforces AKT activity, establishing
  a positive feedback loop. This pathway is critical in regulating ovarian cancer
  cell survival, growth, and proliferation
