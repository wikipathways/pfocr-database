caption: (A) Preparation procedure of H-CM EVs@INS. H-CM EVs@INS platform was proposed
  by fusing S-CM EVs@INS and I-CM EVs@INS using PEG as inducer to facilitate close
  contact and dehydration of lipid bilayers, combining the advantages of both incubation
  and sonication methods; (B) I-CM EVs@INS exhibited low drug encapsulation efficiency
  but intact membrane integrity thereby high transcytosis across the intestinal epithelial
  barrier after oral administration; (C) Although S-CM EVs@INS had a higher drug encapsulation
  efficiency, their transmembrane efficiency was significantly reduced due to the
  disruption of EV membrane integrity caused by the sonication method; (D) H-CM EVs@INS
  demonstrated both superior drug encapsulation and drug delivery capability. Compared
  with I-CM EVs@INS and S-CM EVs@INS, H-CM EVs@INS showed higher uptake by epithelial
  cells and fulfilled transepithelial transport via ER-Golgi transport pathway, demonstrating
  excellent oral bioavailability; (E) H-CM EVs@INS enabled efficient oral insulin
  delivery and hypoglycemic effects, owing to the high drug encapsulation capacity
  and intact membrane functionality
