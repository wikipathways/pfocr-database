caption: A working model illustrating the role of MaCDSP32 in regulating abiotic stress
  tolerance and flowering time in Arabidopsis. Under abiotic stress, the ROS signaling
  pathway and the activities of antioxidant enzymes (SOD, POD, and APX) are inhibited
  in Atcdsp32 knock-down mutant lines. This inhibition further impairs plant survival
  rate, seed germination, and seedling growth. Conversely, MaCDSP32 overexpression
  enhances these physiological processes, thereby improving plant drought and osmotic
  tolerance as well as seed stress adaptation. In addition, under normal growth conditions,
  MaCDSP32 could be involved in regulating the expression of flowering-related genes
  AtTF and AtSOC1; MaCDSP32 overexpression promotes early flowering in transgenic
  Arabidopsis. Solid green arrows indicate enhancing effects; red dotted T-shaped
  lines denote inhibitory effects
