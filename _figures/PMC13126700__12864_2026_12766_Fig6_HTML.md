caption: 'Reconstruction of the lignin biosynthetic pathway and expression profiles
  of key enzyme-coding genes during glume development. The lignin biosynthetic pathway
  was reconstructed to illustrate the sequential enzymatic reactions from L-phenylalanine
  to the major lignin monomers. Key enzymes include PAL (Phenylalanine ammonia-lyase),
  C4H (Cinnamate-4-hydroxylase), 4CL (4-coumarate: CoA ligase), C3H (p-coumarate 3-hydroxylase),
  C3′H (p-coumaroyl shikimate 3′-hydroxylase), HCT (Hydroxycinnamoyl-CoA: shikimate/quinate
  hydroxycinnamoyl transferase), CSE (Caffeoyl shikimate esterase), CCR (Cinnamoyl-CoA
  reductase), COMT (Caffeic acid O-methyltransferase), CCoAOMT (Caffeoyl-CoA O-methyltransferase),
  F5H (Ferulate 5-hydroxylase), and CAD (Cinnamyl alcohol dehydrogenase). Arrows indicate
  the reaction direction.Colored heatmaps adjacent to each enzyme denote normalized
  expression levels across three developmental stages (0, 5, and 10 days post-heading)
  in tenacious glume wheat (Syn-SAU-93) and mutant. The color gradient (blue–white–red)
  represents relative expression intensity from low to high'
