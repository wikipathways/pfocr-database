caption: (A) Molecular mechanisms of ferroptosis. Ferroptosis arises from the interplay
  of toxic effects caused by intracellular free iron accumulation, decreased glutathione
  levels, and lipid peroxidation damage to bio membranes. Cells can resist this process
  through multiple defense systems, including the SystemXc−-dependent glutathione
  synthesis and GPX4 antioxidant pathway, the FSP1-mediated NADPH-CoQ10 reduction
  system axis, the GCH1/DHFR/BH4 axis, and the DHODH/CoQ10 axis. These protective
  mechanisms collaboratively regulate cellular redox balance, effectively preventing
  iron-dependent cell death. (B) Ferroptosis in osteosarcoma. Ferroptosis-related
  factors control lipid peroxidation levels in osteosarcoma cells by regulating iron
  metabolism, ferroptosis-related axes, and key ferroptosis protein expression, thereby
  modulating ferroptosis
