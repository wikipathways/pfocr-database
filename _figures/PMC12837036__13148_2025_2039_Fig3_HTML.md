caption: MicroRNA networks balancing fibrotic and protective signaling in the TM.
  A schematic comparison of miRNA networks in physiological versus glaucomatous TM
  is presented. In the physiological TM (left panel), protective miRNAs, such as the
  miR-29 family (miR-29b/c), miR-200c-3p, miR-181a, miR-1298, miR-483-3p, and miR-137,
  inhibit TGF-β/Smad and Src–YAP/TAZ signaling pathways, thereby limiting the expression
  of ECM genes. This regulation ensures balanced ECM turnover, normal TM stiffness,
  and standard outflow resistance and IOP. Conversely, in glaucomatous TM (right panel),
  there is an upregulation of profibrotic and pro-contractile miRNAs, including miR-143/145,
  miR-21-5p, and miR-210-3p. This upregulation enhances TGF-β/Smad and RhoA/ROCK signaling
  pathways, promoting the expression of collagen and fibronectin. Consequently, this
  leads to TM fibrosis, increased tissue stiffness, and elevated outflow resistance
  and IOP
