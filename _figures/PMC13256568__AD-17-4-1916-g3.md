caption: Activation Mechanism of the TLR4/Rac1/NLRP3 Pathway in AD. In AD, Aβ specifically
  binds to TLR4, inducing conformational changes in TLR4 that trigger a downstream
  signaling cascade. This cascade activates Rac1, which potentiates the activity of
  the NLRP3 inflammasome. The NLRP3 inflammasome recruits ASC and caspase-1 to form
  a functional complex. Activated caspase-1 then cleaves the precursor cytokines pro-IL-1β
  and pro-IL-18 into their mature forms, IL-1β and IL-18, driving neuroinflammation.
