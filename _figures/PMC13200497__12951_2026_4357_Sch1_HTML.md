caption: Biomarker-guided acute kidney injury (AKI) treatment using supramolecular
  ertiprotafib assemblies (SENs). (A) Chemical structure of SENs. Conjugation of ertiprotafib
  with FRRG enables spontaneous self-assembly through intermolecular interactions,
  selectively releasing the active ingredient via cathepsin B–mediated enzymatic cleavage.
  (B) Mechanism of biomarker-guided AKI therapy. In an AKI mouse model, intravenously
  administered SENs accumulate in the inflamed kidney by penetrating the interstitium
  facilitated by tight junction disruption during disease onset followed by enzymatic
  activation by the AKI biomarker cathepsin B to precisely deliver ertiprotafib into
  injured tubular cells. Ultimately, ertiprotafib inhibits PTP1B-dependent Src/ERK
  signaling pathways, thereby significantly improving inflammatory responses, renal
  barrier integrity and histopathological outcomes
