caption: Awakening of dormant cancer cells. DTCs can be awakened by microenvironmental
  disturbances, particularly inflammation (red zone) and aging. Inflammation may arise
  from chemotherapy agents such as cisplatin, doxorubicin, and bleomycin, as well
  as lipopolysaccharide exposure, tobacco smoking, or respiratory viral infections,
  including influenza A and SARS-CoV-2. The mechanisms underlying inflammation-induced
  awakening include the release of NE and MMP9 from neutrophil extracellular traps,
  which can be promoted by senescent fibroblasts, and the production of EGFR ligands
  by M2 macrophages. In aged lungs, fibroblasts secrete sFRP1, which suppresses non-canonical
  Wnt signaling and facilitates the awakening of dormant cancer cells. DTCs, disseminated
  tumor cells; EGFR, epidermal growth factor receptor; MMP9, matrix metalloproteinase
  9; NE, neutrophil elastase; NETs, neutrophil extracellular traps; sFRP1, secreted
  frizzled-related protein 1; Wnt, wingless/integrated signaling pathway
