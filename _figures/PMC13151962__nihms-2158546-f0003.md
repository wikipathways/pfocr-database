caption: 'Exposure of macrophages to TMAO promotes their differentiation into distinct
  functional phenotypes: foam cell–like macrophages: characterized by increased lipid
  uptake through scavenger receptors such as CD36, leading to foam cell formation
  commonly associated with atherosclerosis. Oxidative stress–associated macrophages:
  marked by elevated production of reactive oxygen species (ROS) and activation of
  inflammasome signaling pathways, which drives secretion of proinflammatory cytokines
  such as IL-1β. Antitumor macrophages: exhibit polarization toward an antitumor phenotype,
  with increased expression of immunostimulatory markers, including activation of
  the type I interferon response, which is associated with enhanced activation of
  CD8+ T cells. IFN: interferon; IL-1β: interleukin-1β; mtROS: mitochondrial reactive
  oxygen species; TMAO: trimethylamine-N-oxide; TNF-α: tumor necrosis factor-α; AHR:
  aryl hydrocarbon receptor; VEGF: vascular endothelial growth factor'
