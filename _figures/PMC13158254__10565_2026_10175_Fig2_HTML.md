caption: Critical role of m6A modification in Th2-driven allergic responses. Modification
  of m6A is vital for regulating immune cell growth, differentiation, polarization,
  migration, and activation in Th2-driven allergic responses. Downregulating m6A modification
  induces DCs maturation, activates M2 macrophages, and modulates epithelial barrier
  function, facilitating innate immune responses. Moreover, m6A modification serves
  as a key regulatory mechanism across diverse T-cell subsets, modulating the functional
  properties of distinct T-cell populations and influencing the expression of genes
  involved in naïve T-cell differentiation pathways. In downstream cells of the Th2-driven
  allergic responses, m6A RNA modification is indispensable for mast cell activation
  and functional regulation, as well as eosinophil infiltration
