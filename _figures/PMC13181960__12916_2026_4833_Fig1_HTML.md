caption: 'Hallmarks of senescence in systemic and pulmonary immunity. The figure summarizes
  key age-related alterations in innate and adaptive immunity in aged individuals.
  Innate immune cell changes: Macrophages. Exhibit heightened pro-inflammatory cytokine
  production and excessive ROS release, defective phagocytosis, and impaired antigen
  presentation. Neutrophils. Display impaired chemotaxis, aberrant migration, and
  dysfunctional NET formation. Adaptive immune cell changes: T cells. Phenotypic shifts
  from naïve to memory T cells occur, with a Th1/Th2 imbalance favoring Th2 predominance,
  increased Th17 cells, and decreased Tregs, which can reverse upon stimulation. Senescent
  T cells lack costimulatory molecules (CD27/CD28) but express markers like KLRG-1
  and CD57, along with upregulated p16 and p21 and immune checkpoint molecules (Tim-3,
  TIGIT, CTLA-4). The cells overproduce proinflammatory cytokines (IFN-γ, TNF-α) and
  cytotoxic mediators (granzyme B, perforin), contributing to aberrant immune responses,
  functional suppression, tissue damage, defective B cell help, and mitochondrial
  dysfunction. In the aged lung, IFN-γ signaling further drives T cell activation.
  B cells. Immunosenescence is characterized by reduced output of mature B cells,
  expansion of proinflammatory age-associated B cells and double negative B cells,
  declined CSR and SHM, and impaired germinal center function, resulting in defective
  antibody production and function. Inflammaging microenvironment. The SASP, activated
  via p21Cip1, triggers the p38 MAPK and JAK signaling pathways, leading to NF-κB
  activation and secretion of pro-inflammatory cytokines (IL-1β, IL-6, TNF-α), growth
  factors (VEGF, TGF-β), chemokines (CXCL1, CXCL8, CCL2), and MMPs (MMP-2, MMP-9).
  This disrupts the lung microenvironment, promoting chronic inflammation and tissue
  damage. ROS reactive oxygen species, NET neutrophil extracellular traps, Th T helper
  cell, Treg regulatory T cell, CD cluster of differentiation, KLRG-1 killer cell
  lectin-like receptor subfamily G member 1, Tim-3 T cell immunoglobulin and mucin
  domain-containing protein 3, TIGIT T cell immunoreceptor with Ig and ITIM domains,
  CTLA-4 cytotoxic T lymphocyte-associated protein 4, IFN-γ interferon gamma, TNF-α
  tumor necrosis factor alpha, CSR class switch recombination, SHM somatic hypermutation,
  SASP senescence-associated secretory phenotype, MAPK mitogen-activated protein kinase,
  JAK Janus kinase, NF-κB nuclear factor kappa-light-chain-enhancer of activated B
  cells, IL interleukin, VEGF vascular endothelial growth factor, TGF-β transforming
  growth factor beta, CXCL C-X-C motif chemokine ligand, CCL C-C motif chemokine ligand,
  MMP matrix metalloproteinase'
