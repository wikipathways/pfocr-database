caption: Repressive roles of the evening complex (EC) in plant growth, flowering,
  and stress responses.(A) The evening complex (EC), which includes LUX, ELF3, and
  ELF4, regulates hypocotyl elongation, photoperiodic growth, starch accumulation,
  seed dormancy, heading date, and shoot architecture by directly controlling the
  expression of key genes.(B) In diverse plant species, including Arabidopsis, soybean,
  barley, rice, maize, and Brachypodium, ELF3, ELF4, and LUX are critical regulators
  of flowering time via direct repression of essential flowering genes such as FT,
  E1, PRR37, Ghd7, CCT, COL, and VRN1.(C) In Arabidopsis and rice, EC components are
  essential for regulating plant stress responses and defense pathways by directly
  modulating the expression of genes related to autophagy, nitrogen deficiency, cold
  response, UV-B sensitivity, and salicylic acid and jasmonic acid signaling
