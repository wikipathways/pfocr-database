caption: Reactions catalyzed by BBE-like enzymes. (A) Typical oxidative dehydrogenation
  and oxidative cyclization reactions catalyzed by BBE-like enzymes from plants. (B)
  BBE-like enzymes from Moraceae plants in the biosynthesis pathway of D-A type natural
  products. BBE-like oxidocyclases from Morus alba (MaOCs) catalyze oxidative cyclization
  of moracin C (1) to yield benzopyran-type natural product moracin D (2) while the
  BBE-like diene synthases (MaDSs) catalyze the oxidative dehydrogenation of moracin
  C (1) to yield diene 3, which further undergoes an enzymatic intermolecular Diels–Alder
  (D-A) reaction with morachalcone A (4) to yield the D-A type natural product chalcomoracin
  (5) in the presence of BBE-like Diels–Alderases (MaDAs)
