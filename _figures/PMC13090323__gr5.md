caption: Roadmap for the development and clinical translation of antifibrotic mRNA
  nanomedicinesKey considerations are organized into three domains. (A) Delivery system
  design, highlighting strategies to improve targeting accuracy and safety, including
  high-throughput screening approaches, stimuli-responsive control mechanisms, multimodal
  targeting, non-toxic and biodegradable carrier materials, low-immunogenic lipid
  components, and alternative RNA formats.201,202 (B) Therapeutic strategies, emphasizing
  combinatorial and co-delivery approaches with RNA interference (RNAi), small-molecule
  agents, CRISPR/sgRNA systems, proteins, and multi-mRNA formulations to enable coordinated
  pathway modulation. (C) Clinical translation, outlining factors critical for implementation,
  including scalable manufacturing, patient-compatible administration routes (e.g.,
  inhalation, microneedle patches, subcutaneous micropumps), and evaluation of safety,
  durability, and accessibility.20
