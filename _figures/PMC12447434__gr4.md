caption: 'Evolutionary divergence in tryptophan (Trp)-derived secondary metabolic
  pathways between Arabidopsis and Brassica crops.WRKY33 interacts with MYB51 to regulate
  the biosynthesis of indole-3-acetaldoxime (IAOx), a common precursor of indolic
  glucosinolates (IGSs), camalexin, and 4-hydroxy-indole-3-carbonylnitrile (4OH-ICN).
  In Arabidopsis, thick black arrows indicate the full preservation of these biosynthesis
  branches. In contrast, thick blue arrows represent the redirected metabolic flux
  in Brassica crops, where evolutionary processes have favored the retention of the
  IGS pathway as the primary defense route, while the camalexin and 4OH-ICN branches
  have been partially lost, as indicated by gray arrows with prohibition symbols.
  Despite this divergence, WRKY33 continues to regulate the IGS pathway in Brassica
  species, exemplified here by broccoli (Brassica oleracea var. italica), highlighting
  the conserved regulatory role of this transcription factor. Red arrows represent
  transcriptional activation; black lines ending in bars indicate repression. Abbreviations:
  IGS, indolic glucosinolates; 4OH-ICN, 4-hydroxy-indole-3-carbonylnitrile; camalexin,
  3-thiazol-2′-yl-indole; CYP, cytochrome P450; IAOx, indole-3-acetaldoxime; IAN,
  indole-3-acetonitrile; I3M, 3-indolylmethyl glucosinolate; PAD3, PHYTOALEXIN DEFICIENT
  3; ICN, indole-3-carbonylnitrile; 4OH-I3M, 4-hydroxy-I3M; 4MO-I3M, 4-methoxy-3-indolylmethyl
  glucosinolate'
