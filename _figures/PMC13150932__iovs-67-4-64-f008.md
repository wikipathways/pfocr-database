caption: Scheme of the detailed mechanism by which TRPV1 promoted pathological neovascularization.
  The deficiency of endothelial TRPV1 inhibited pathological neovascularization in
  the CNV model. Mechanistically, elevated LA levels in the aqueous humor of patients
  with wAMD activated TRPV1, which promoted angiogenesis by triggering the NF-κB pathway
  in HUVECs. Meanwhile, the secretion of pro-inflammatory cytokines in TRPV1-activated
  HUVECs induced microglia to secrete both pro-inflammatory and angiogenic factors,
  which in turn facilitated angiogenesis. Therefore, targeting the TRPV1/NF-κB pathway
  may offer a novel therapeutic approach for treating pathological neovascular diseases
