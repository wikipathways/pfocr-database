caption: Schematic diagram for NDV exploitation of ATP11c–CDC50A-mediated PS asymmetry
  to facilitate viral replication cycle. The ATP11c–CDC50A complex maintains inner-leaflet
  PS asymmetry, enabling NDV M protein oligomerization at PS-rich domains to drive
  membrane curvature and virion assembly. In ATP11c-KO or CDC50A-mutant (D193G/K319E)
  cells, disrupted flippase activity reduces PS flipping efficiency, impairing M recruitment
  and viral budding. Dashed lines indicate mechanistic disruptions observed in functional
  assays. Colocalization of M with PS and reduced viral titers in KO models highlight
  the pathway’s necessity for NDV replication. M, M protein; NDV, Newcastle disease
  virus; PS, phosphatidylserine
