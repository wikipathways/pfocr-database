  for cellular entry. Individual EV-D68 particles enter cells via CME following receptor
  attachment, whereas membrane-associated EV-D68 fails to utilize this pathway. Short-term
  inhibition of CME using chemical inhibitors does not trigger compensatory cellular
  responses; however, prolonged inhibition—achieved by overexpression of dominant-negative
  Eps15 or siRNA-mediated knockdown—leads to enhanced macropinocytosis, thereby facilitating
  EV-D68 entry
