caption: The role of Wnt/β‐catenin signalling in pulmonary vascular remodelling. The
  canonical Wnt pathway, when activated, stabilises β‐catenin and causes it to move
  into the nucleus, where it triggers the expression of genes important for cell cycle
  progression and extracellular matrix remodelling. In HAPH, this signalling cascade
  is hijacked, resulting in the uncontrolled growth and migration of PASMCs. Evidence
  from HPAH models shows that key pathway components (e.g., ROCK, Rho, PCNA) are upregulated
  and its inhibition can reverse vascular pathology
