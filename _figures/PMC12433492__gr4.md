caption: A schematic diagram of the regulation of inter-connected nutrient-sensing
  pathways by H2O2. (a) High nutrient levels activate the IIS and mTORC1 signaling,
  but inhibit AMPK activity. mTORC1 is activated downstream of the IIS, and negatively
  modulated by AMPK. All these pathways are modulated by post-translational cysteine
  oxidation of critical kinases and phosphatases. (b) The IIS pathway contains H2O2-sensing
  kinases insulin receptor and AKT, as well as H2O2-targeting phosphatases PTEN and
  PTP1B. (c) H2O2 suppresses mTORC1 kinase activity by modifying Cys1483 of mTOR.
  (d) AMPK can be oxidized at multiple cysteines in the α subunit, which differentially
  affects its kinase activity
