caption: Mechanisms of tumor aerobic glycolysis regulating EMT and autophagy in bladder
  urothelial carcinoma (a) glycolytic enzymes can promote epithelial-mesenchymal transition
  (EMT) through redistribution to the nucleus, and can also enhance autophagy via
  multiple signaling pathways. Furthermore, glycolysis generates large amounts of
  lactate, which facilitates autophagy by promoting histone lactylation and lactylation
  of core autophagy proteins. Additionally, lactate contributes to EMT by activating
  the TGF-β pathway and mediating lactylation-related crosstalk with stromal and endothelial
  cells. (b) hypoxia, ROS, and low glucose promote glycolysis to induce autophagy
  through various mechanisms, whereas high glucose, through the YAP1/TAZ pathway,
  triggers glycolysis that ultimately inhibits autophagy. Generated using adobe Illustrator
