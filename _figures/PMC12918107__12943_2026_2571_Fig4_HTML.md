caption: Mitochondrial mechanisms underlying cancer progression. Mitochondrial quality
  control, mtDNA integrity, ion homeostasis, and mitochondrial interactions within
  the TME exert critical influences on cancer pathogenesis. Neoplastic cells regulate
  mitochondrial quantity and function to support proliferation, with mitochondrial
  biogenesis and fission promoting tumor growth, while fusion often suppresses tumors,
  depending on the cancer subtype. The role of mitophagy remains complex, with context-specific
  effects. Mitochondrial transfer from immune cells to cancer cells enhances aggressiveness
  and weakens antitumor immunity. The UPRmt aids tumor survival by alleviating stress.
  mtDNA damage drives oncogenesis via multiple pathways, including genomic instability,
  immune dysregulation, and metabolic rewiring. Mitochondrial calcium flux supports
  biogenesis and metastasis, yet its overload induces cell death. Ferroptosis presents
  a vulnerability in cancer cells. Copper has dual effects as both a pro- and antitumor
  agent. Epigenetic modifications of DNA, RNA, histones, and mitochondrial structures
  contribute to cancer development by disrupting mitochondrial homeostasis. (Created
