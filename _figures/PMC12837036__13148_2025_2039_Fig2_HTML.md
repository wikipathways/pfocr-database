caption: Mechanism of GDF7 hypomethylation in glaucoma pathogenesis. The hypomethylation
  of the GDF7 promoter, mediated by TET enzymes, augments both the transcription and
  translation of GDF7. Once secreted, GDF7 activates the BMPR2/Smad signaling pathway,
  which is implicated in the upregulation of fibrotic gene expression, including α-SMA,
  FN, and Collagen I, thereby contributing to TM fibrosis. It is hypothesized that
  a positive feedback loop, maintained by GDF7-induced TET activation, perpetuates
  this signaling pathway. The application of a GDF7-neutralizing antibody (nGDF7)
  disrupts this cascade by preventing GDF7 from binding to its receptor
