caption: 'The leptin receptor family consists of six distinct isoforms: one long isoform
  (Ob-Rb), four short isoforms (Ob-Ra, Ob-Rc, Ob-Rd, and Ob-Rf), and one soluble form
  (Ob-Re). Leptin primarily exerts its classical effects through activation of the
  JAK2/STAT3 signaling pathway via phosphorylation. However, recent studies have uncovered
  a broader spectrum of leptin-induced post-translational modifications, including
  phosphorylation, glycosylation, acetylation, and ubiquitination. Additionally, leptin
  has been implicated in modulating DNA and histone modifications, as well as promoting
  the formation of novel adipokines'
