  dendritic cells (mDCs) accumulate in interfollicular regions and at the T-B border,
  creating antigen-rich niches outside germinal centers (GCs). In these non-GC environments,
  allergen-specific B cells receive IL-4/IL-13 help from non-GC Tfh cells, undergo
  IgE class-switch recombination, and subsequently engage in clonal expansion, somatic
  hypermutation (SH), and antibody-secreting cells under conditions of sustained allergen
  stimulation, providing a mechanism for IgE affinity maturation despite the scarcity
  of IgE+ cells within GCs
