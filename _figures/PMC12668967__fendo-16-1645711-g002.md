caption: FSH suppresses mitophagy to prevent follicular atresia. FSH prevents follicular
  atresia by deploying a dual strategy in granulosa cells. Activation of the PI3K-AKT-mTOR
  pathway suppresses autophagic activity, while inhibition of the PINK1-Parkin pathway
  dampens mitophagy, thereby alleviating oxidative damage and preventing excessive
  mitochondrial loss. This coordinated regulation thus enhances granulosa cell vitality
  and forestalls the onset of atresia
