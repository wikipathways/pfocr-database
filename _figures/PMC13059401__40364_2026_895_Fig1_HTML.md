caption: 'CAR structure and its evolution. The CAR consists of three domains: an extracellular
  domain, a transmembrane domain, and an intracellular domain. First-generation CARs
  contain only one intracellular signaling component, CD3ζ. Second-generation CARs,
  built upon the first-generation design, incorporate one additional costimulatory
  molecule in the cytoplasmic domain. Third-generation CARs, evolving from the first-generation
  framework, integrate two costimulatory molecules into their cytoplasmic domain.
  Fourth-generation CARs, derived from second-generation constructs, introduce an
  inducible domain to drive targeted cytokine secretion. Fifth-generation CARs, based
  on the second-generation architecture, incorporate the IL-2Rβ domain to activate
  the JAK-STAT signaling pathways. IL-2Rβ: interleukin-2β-chain receptor; JAK: Janus
  kinase; STAT: signal transducer and activator of transcription'
