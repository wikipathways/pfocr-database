caption: 'Simplified graphical representation of: (a) the photosynthetic C1 pathway
  including 5,10‐methylene‐THF production from serine conversion to glycine and the
  oxidative C1 pathway via the formaldehyde–THF reaction in the chloroplast, as well
  as cytosolic methionine synthesis and the downstream S‐methylmethionine (SMM) cycle
  involved in phloem loading and methyl group transport to sink tissues; and (b) photorespiration,
  including 5,10‐methylene‐THF production from glycine decarboxylation and the oxidative
  C1 pathway via the formaldehyde–THF reaction. Also shown are three common microbial
  pathways for C1 compound assimilation: (c) the serine cycle, (d) acetogenesis, and
  (e) the ribulose monophosphate (RuMP) cycle. Key enzymes involved in C1 metabolism
  are highlighted by functional category: oxidative C1 pathway enzymes (orange), THF‐dependent
  enzymes (light blue), and non‐THF‐dependent enzymes shared between photorespiration
  and the microbial serine cycle (pink). Enzymes marked with an asterisk denote those
  for which protein sequences were used in the phylogenetic analysis across plants,
  algae, cyanobacteria, and methylotrophic bacteria (see Fig. 4)'
