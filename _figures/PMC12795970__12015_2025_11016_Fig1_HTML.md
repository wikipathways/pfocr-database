caption: 'H3K4me3/H3K27ac modifications open the chromatin, facilitating the binding
  of transcription factors (TFs, such as HOXA9/Meis1/RUNX1). After binding, genes
  like MYC and BCL2 are activated to maintain the self - renewal of leukemia stem
  cells (LSCs). DNA methylation and H3K27me3 close the chromatin, preventing the binding
  of TFs. Gene silencing (e.g., restricted expression of CDKN1A) leads to the loss
  of LSC stemness and their entry into a differentiation state.Under hypomethylated
  conditions, enrichment of H3K27ac at the promoter or enhancer regions of the ACSL4
  gene in LSCs activates its transcription and expression. Elevated ACSL4 expression
  increases the content of PUFAs in the LSC membrane, rendering the cells more susceptible
  to lipid peroxidation and thereby enhancing their sensitivity to ferroptosis.Conversely,
  under hypermethylated conditions, enrichment of H3K27ac in the regulatory regions
  of the GPX4 gene promotes its expression. High levels of GPX4 efficiently eliminate
  lipid peroxides and reactive oxygen species within LSCs, thereby enhancing their
  resistance to ferroptosis.When MYC/BCL2 are activated, they enhance the expression
  of HOXA9 in a reverse manner, further consolidating the stemness state and forming
  a vicious cycle. HOXA9 can activate MYC to promote stemness. However, excessive
  MYC may activate inhibitory epigenetic modifications (such as DNA methylation),
  thus reducing the expression of HOXA9 and preventing abnormal proliferation.Under
  normal circumstances, when MYC over - activates cell proliferation, the p53 pathway
  may be activated, which inhibits the expression of MYC and prompts LSCs to enter
  the differentiation or apoptosis stage. Abbreviation: LSC, leukemia stem cell; MYC,
  v - myc avian myelocytomatosis viral oncogene homolog; BCL-2, B-cell lymphoma 2;
  HOXA9, Homeobox A9; RUNX1, Runt-related transcription factor 1; Meis1, Meis homeobox
  1; H3K4me3, Histone H3 lysine 27 trimethylation; H3K27ac, Histone H3 lysine 27 acetylation;
  CDKN1A, Cyclin Dependent Kinase Inhibitor 1 A; GATA2, GATA binding protein 2; TFs,
  transcription factors; KMD6A, Lysine (K)-specific demethylase 6 A; GPX4, Glutathione
  peroxidase 4;ACSL4, Acyl-CoA synthetase long-chain family member 4;ROS, Reactive
  Oxygen Species; PUFA-CoA, Polyunsaturated fatty acid–Coenzyme A'
