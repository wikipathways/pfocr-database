caption: 'Schematic of the cGAS-STING pathway regulating T cell responses and anti-tumor
  immunity. Tumor/damage-derived double-stranded DNA (dsDNA) activates cyclic GMP-AMP
  synthase (cGAS) to produce cyclic GMP-AMP (cGAMP), triggering stimulator of interferon
  genes (STING). a For Treg cells: They exert immunosuppressive effects by secreting
  IL-10 and TGF-β. In the cGAS-STING regulatory network, they interact with CD4⁺ T
  cells and MDSCs to promote tumor progression; meanwhile, they affect cDC₁ function
  via suppressive cytokines, hindering immune activation. In therapeutic strategies,
  cGAMP-virus-like particles and PD-1 blockers can target cDC₁/cGAMP, antagonize Treg’s
  immunosuppressive effects, and enhance cytotoxic T lymphocyte (CTL) responses. Moreover,
  the cGAS-STING pathway also participates in regulating Treg function and MHC-I expression;
  in radiotherapy-related immune regulation, Tregs interact with CD4⁺ T cells, cDC₁,
  etc., influencing Th1 differentiation and CD8⁺ T cell activation, together forming
  a complex regulatory network in tumor immunity. b For CD4⁺ T cells: Abnormal Kras
  or interactions with regulatory T cells (Tregs)/myeloid-derived suppressor cells
  (MDSCs) modulate differentiation; therapies (cGAMP-virus-like particles, PD-1 blockers)
  target conventional DC₁ (cDC₁)/cGAMP to enhance cytotoxic T lymphocyte (CTL) responses.
  In radiotherapy contexts, CD4⁺ T cells secrete IL-2/IFN-γ, activating cGAS-STING
  in cDC₁ to prime CD8⁺ T cells, with Tregs/T follicular helper 1 (Tfh1) also involved.
  c. dsDNA activates cGAS-STING, driving IFN-I production via IRF3 to activate DCs
  and CD8⁺ T cells. STING also promotes stem-like CD8⁺ T cells via AKT/TCF1. Trex1
  negatively regulates this pathway by degrading cytosolic dsDNA. Induced by high-dose
  radiation, Trex1 limits STING activation: low-dose radiation evades Trex1, enabling
  robust CD8⁺ T cell priming, while high-dose radiation upregulates Trex1 to suppress
  anti-tumor immunity. d For γδ T cells: cGAS-STING (via post-translational modification/TBK1)
  induces IFN-γ, TNF-α, IL-17/IL-4 secretion, with TGF-β modulating responses. Tregs
  further regulate immunity via IL-10/TGF-β, and cGAS-STING in cells also modulates
  MHC-I expression'
