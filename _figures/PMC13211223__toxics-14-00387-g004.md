  leads to the accumulation of reactive oxygen species (ROS), which in turn upregulates
  autophagy-related proteins, including LC3-I/II, Beclin-1, ATG5, ATG12, and ATG16L1,
  thereby activating autophagy; (Right): PS-NPs bind to TSC2, disrupting the TSC1/2
  complex and consequently activating mTORC1. This suppresses the nuclear translocation
  of TFEB, leading to impaired autophagy–lysosome pathway (ALP) activity and blocked
  autophagic flux. Image created with BioRender.com. Wan, F. (https://BioRender.com/by15qsx)
