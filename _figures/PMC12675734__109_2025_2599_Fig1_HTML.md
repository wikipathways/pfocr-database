caption: 'Molecular mechanism and regulatory pathways of ferroptosis. The storage,
  release, import, and export of iron impact the ferroptosis sensitivity of cells.
  Disruption of iron homeostasis and intracellular iron overload can lead to the generation
  of ROS through the Fenton reaction, thereby promoting membrane lipid peroxidation
  and triggering ferroptosis. Ferroptosis is primarily regulated by three antioxidant
  axes: the system Xc−-GSH-GPX4 axis, the FSP1-CoQ-NADPH axis, and the GCH1-BH4-DHFR
  axis, all of which rely on NADPH as electron donors. ROS, reactive oxygen species;
  GSH, glutathione; GPX4, glutathione peroxidase 4; FSP1, ferroptosis suppressor protein1;
  CoQ, coenzyme Q; NADPH, nicotinamide adenine phosphate dinucleotide; GCH1, guanosine
  triphosphate cyclohydrolase 1; BH4, tetrahydrobiopterin; DHFR, dihydrofolate reductase;
  ACSL4, acyl-CoA synthetase long-chain family member 4; TfR1, transferrin receptor1;
  NTBI, non-transferrin-bound iron; NCOA4, nuclear receptor coactivator 4; MVB, multivesicular
  bodies; GCL, glutamate-cysteine ligase; GSR, glutathione reductase; LPCAT3, lysophosphatidylcholine
  acyltransferase 3; ALOX, lipoxygenase; POR, cytochrome P450 oxidoreductase; CISD1,
  CDGSH iron-sulfur domain 1'
