caption: Integrated analysis between 13 types of flavonoids and four genes in the
  flavonoid biosynthesis pathway. (a) Summary of flavonoid biosynthesis pathway. Heatmaps
  show the abundance of 13 types flavonoids and four genes of flavonoid biosynthesis
  during Fusarium oxysporum f. sp. fordiis infection. Epigallocatechin, catechin and
  (−)‐epicatechin are marked with blue font. The two ANR genes are marked with red
  font. (b) Correlation analysis between 13 types of flavonoids and two ANR genes
  in the flavonoid biosynthesis pathway. *p < 0.05, **p < 0.01, ***p < 0.001. (c)
  The reverse transcription‐quantitative PCR validation of the ANR (A008279) response
  to infection in Vernicia montana (M) and Vernicia fordii (F). M0 and F0, 0 days
