  (LKR) and saccharopine dehydrogenase (SDH) domains of the bifunctional aminoadipic
  semialdehyde synthase (AASS) enzyme. Deficiency of antiquitin, encoded by the ALDH7A1
  gene, results in pyridoxine-dependent epilepsy (PDE), causing an accumulation of
