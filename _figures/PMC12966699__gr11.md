caption: Schematic diagram of potential mechanism of OA ameliorating central cognitive
  impairment. OA interacts with PKC and PI3Kγ to activate downstream signaling cascades
  of mAChRs, thereby promoting ACh synthesis and release to potentiate hippocampal
  cholinergic neurotransmission. Additionally, OA activates MEK, which propagates
  signaling through the ERK/CREB/BDNF axis; this cascade enhances synaptic plasticity,
  as well as memory formation and consolidation. Furthermore, OA modulates IP3R to
  maintain intracellular Ca2+ homeostasis, alleviating ROS-induced neuronal damage
  and mitigating the oxidative stress state in the hippocampus. Collectively, OA may
  exert a multi-pathway regulatory effect to attenuate core pathological processes
  linked to cognitive impairment, thereby effectively ameliorating SCOP-induced central
  cognitive impairment. Red arrows in the diagram indicate the potential molecular
  targets of OA. (For interpretation of the references to colour in this figure legend,
  the reader is referred to the web version of this article.)
