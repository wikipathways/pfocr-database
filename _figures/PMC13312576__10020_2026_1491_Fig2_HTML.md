caption: GPCRs pathway signaling. (i) a G-protein-dependent manner, where the ligand
  binds to the extracellular domain of the receptor, converting Gα-GDP (inactive state)
  to Gα-GTP (active state), which causes dissociation of the heterotrimer into Gα
  and the Gβγ dimer. Each Gα isoform and the Gβγ dimer regulates distinct signaling
  pathways by activating specific second messengers; (ii) regulatory and scaffolding
  roles of G-protein-independent manner (arrestin- dependent), upon receptor activation,
  G protein–coupled receptor kinases (GRKs) are recruited, which subsequently resulting
  in C-terminal phosphorylation, leading to two distinct functions of arrestin-pathway,
  blocking the engagement of G-protein to halt signaling activation of the activated
  receptors and initiating the machinery of the clathrin-dependent endocytic (initiates
  receptor endocytosis), in which receptors are degraded via lysosomes or recycled
  to cell surface. Arrestins also function as scaffolding proteins for downstream
  signaling pathways
