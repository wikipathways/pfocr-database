caption: 'This figure presents a visual summary of most important metabolic pathways,
  which distinguish adenocarcinoma patients (A), squamous cell carcinoma patients
  (B-D) and small cell lung cancer patients (E). Those pathways were highlighted via
  Mummichog metabolic functional enrichment analysis due to the most hits in the respective
  group comparison. Database exact mass assignment (Schymanski evidence level (4 &
  5) lead to the assignment of putative compounds which showed a P value <0.05 in
  their group comparison. Among them various fatty acids, hydroxyproline, L/D-proline,
  L-glutamate 5-semialdehyde, succinate, succinate semialdehyde, glyoxylate and alanine.
  Compounds like pyruvate and lactate (Warburg effect) were also detected but showed
  no significant differences in exhaled breath between groups. The following metabolic
  mechanisms are presented: TCA, tricarboxylic acid cycle; A, de-novo fatty acid biosynthesis;
  B, glycin, serine, alanin, threonin metabolism; C, alanin and aspartat metabolism;
  D, glyoxylate & dicarboxylate metabolism; E, arginine and proline metabolism'
