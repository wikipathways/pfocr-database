caption: Oxidative modification of specific cysteine residues regulates the proteostasis
  network. (a) Oxidation of Cys115 in ribosomal protein RPL40A mediates the inhibitory
  effect of H2O2 on protein synthesis. (b) During oxidative stress, H2O2-mediated
  cysteine oxidation converts Get3, Prxs, and HSP70 family members into ATP-independent
  holdases to prevent protein aggregation. (c) (d) The two major protein degradation
  pathways in eukaryotes, UPS and the autophagy lysosomal pathway, are both redox-regulated.
  (c) Glutathionylation of Cys76 and Cys221 in the 20S proteasome subunit α5 promotes
  gate opening of 20S CP. (d) Cysteine oxidation modulates multiple proteins involved
  in autophagy and lysosomal degradation. For example, H2O2 post-translationally suppresses
  several ATG proteins and the lysosomal protein cathepsin S. By contrast, oxidation
  of Cys105 and Cys113 in the autophagy receptor p62 promotes its oligomerization
  and enhances autophagy activity. In all panels, peptides and proteins are shown
  in dark yellow
