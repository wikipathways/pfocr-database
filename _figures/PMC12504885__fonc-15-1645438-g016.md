caption: An illustration of the Hippo pathway's significance in ER+ breast cancer.
  Overexpression of ZNF367 promotes metastasis and stimulates Hippo/YAP signaling
  via blocking LATS. Overexpression of USP9x promotes cell proliferation by deubiquitinating
  and stabilizing YAP1.NE and EPI suppress breast cancer by rapidly phos-phorylating
  YAP and retaining it in the cytoplasm. ZEB1, a transcriptional activator, interacts
  with YAPI to in-crease transcription. The connection between LATS1 and CRABP2 decreases
  LATS1 ubiquitination, which sup-presses cell invasion. The STARD13-correlated ceRNA
  network controls TAZ distribution and may decrease the stem cell nature of breast
  cancer by upregulating LATS1/2 [248]
