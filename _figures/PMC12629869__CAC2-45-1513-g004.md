caption: CIN‐induced acute and chronic STING stimulation dynamically regulate tumor
  progression. (A) Acute drug‐induced CIN promotes the antitumor activity of APCs,
  T cells, and NK cells through STING‐induced IRF3 and NF‐κB transcription via TBK1
  and IKKε recruitment, thus promoting the expression of IFNs and ISGs; (B) However,
  cancer‐inherited chronic CIN triggers downstream rewiring of STING and upregulation
  of immunosuppressive factors. Non‐canonical NF‐κB signaling promotes cancer cell
  survival through IL‐6/STAT3 signaling and facilitates metastasis. Chronic CIN also
  triggers ER stress response (including PERK, IRE1, and ATF6 signaling pathway),
  promoting metastasis and leading to an immunosuppressive TME (enriched with M2‐like
  macrophages, dysfunctional CD8+ T cells, and MDSCs), which is also induced by the
  upregulation of immunosuppressive PD‐L1, IDO, and adenosine by chronic IFN response.
  APCs, antigen‐presenting cells; ATF6, activating transcription factor 6; CIN, chromosomal
