caption: Biogenesis of hepatocyte apoptosis bodies. 1. The interaction between death
  ligands and their corresponding death receptors initiates the extrinsic apoptotic
  pathway. 2. The formation of a death-inducing signaling complex occurs when death
  domains, death effector domains, and procaspases 8 and 10 come together. The continuous
  transmission of death signals resulting from ligand-receptor interactions increases
  the concentration of procaspase 8, ultimately leading to its autocleavage. 3. Once
  active, caspase 8 cleaves Bid into tBid, 4. which in turn activates Bax and Bak,
  prompting the permeabilization of the mitochondrial outer membrane. Bax and Bak
  are also activated by intrinsic apoptotic signals detected by BH3-only proteins.
  5. The release of mitochondrial intermembrane proteins, such as cytochrome c, interacts
  with apoptotic protease-activating factor 1 to form an apoptosome, thus initiating
  the activation of caspase 9. 6. Active caspase 9 subsequently activates caspases
  3 and 7, leading to 7. membrane blebbing, organelle sequestration, nuclear fragmentation,
  and the contraction of actin and myosin, resulting in 8. the formation of ApopBDs
