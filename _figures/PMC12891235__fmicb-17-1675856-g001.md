caption: The 2-methylcitrate cycle in bacterial propionic acid catabolism. The pathway
  is structurally and enzymatically similar to the tricarboxylic acid (TCA) cycle.
  The key enzymes specific to the 2-methylcitrate cycle are 2-methylcitrate synthase
  (PrpC), 2-methylcitrate dehydratase (PrpD), and 2-methylisocitrate lyase (PrpB),
  highlighted in cyan, green, and yellow, respectively. In many bacteria, including
  B. thuringiensis, the genes encoding these enzymes (prpC, prpD, and prpB) are located
  in the same prp operon. The chemical structure of the intermediate 2-methylcitrate
  is shown in the upper right corner
