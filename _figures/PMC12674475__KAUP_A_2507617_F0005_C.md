caption: Schematic model describing a putative role of DDX11 in autophagy pathway
  regulation. DDX11 exerts its function as a DNA helicase in the nuclear compartment.
  Within the cytoplasm, DDX11 cooperates with SQSTM1 and ATG16L1 to promote phagophore
  formation as well as the autophagic flux (bold green and blue dotted arrows, respectively;
  left side). Absence of DDX11 affects intracellular localization of the ATG16L1 autophagy
  precursor, which in turn impairs LC3 lipidation and autophagosome fusion with lysosomal
  compartment, ultimately reducing the autophagic flux (thin green and blue dotted
  arrows, respectively; right side)
