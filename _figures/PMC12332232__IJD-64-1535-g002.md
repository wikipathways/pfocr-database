caption: Schematic view of methotrexate's (MTX) effects. MTX is taken up into the
  cell by reduced folate carrier 1 (RFC1), then it inhibits dihydrofolate reductase
  (DHFR), which catalyzes the conversion of dihydrofolate (DHF) into tetrahydrofolate
  (THF), a key component of nucleotide synthesis. MTX also inhibits the enzyme AICAR
  transformylase (ATIC), which results in adenosine accumulation and downregulation
  of immune cells. A new emerging effect is the inhibition of the JAK/STAT pathway
  via inhibiting Janus kinase 1 (JAK1), Janus kinase 2 (JAK2), and Tyrosine kinase
  2 (Tyk2). AICAR, 5‐aminoimidazole‐4‐carboxamide‐1‐β‐D‐ribofuranoside; FAICAR, 5‐formamidoimidazole‐4‐carboxamide
  ribotide
