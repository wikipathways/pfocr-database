caption: Metabolite levels and transcripts coding for enzymes putatively involved
  in the two proposed vanillin biosynthesis pathways in pods at 1.5, 2, 4, 6, and
  8 MAP. The ferulate pathway is depicted on the left, and the benzoate pathway is
  shown on the right. The transcriptome heatmap scale represents transcript abundance
  between pairs of stages, with a white to blue color gradient indicating low and
  high expression levels, respectively. For the metabolomic data, the upper bar corresponds
  to the nontargeted method, illustrating metabolite relative abundance, where a color
  scale ranging from white to red indicates metabolite levels. The lower bar represents
  compound quantitation, displaying their concentration at each developmental stage,
  with a gradient color scheme from white to green. All data were based on five biological
  replicates
