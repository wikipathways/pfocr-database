caption: Mitochondrial metabolic reprogramming and cancer. Mitochondrial metabolic
  reprogramming is one of the characteristics of cancer cells. Activation of oncogenes
  (e.g. MYC) and inactivation of tumor suppressor genes (e.g. TP53) can promote tumor
  metabolic reprogramming through multiple pathways, including PI3K/Akt, mTOR, AMPK,
  and HIF. Metabolic reprogramming includes increased glycolysis under aerobic conditions,
  weakened oxidative phosphorylation, and changes in lipid and protein metabolism.
  Metabolites affect the expression of nuclear genes through epigenetic modifications
  and also affect cancer progression in various ways such as providing nutrient substrates
  and affecting the tumor microenvironment (TME). Hypoxia is also one of the characteristics
  of tumor tissue due to the increase in oxygen consumption caused by rapid tumor
  growth. The HIF pathway is activated at this time, promoting ROS generation and
  tumor progression via various mechanisms. (Created in Biorender)
