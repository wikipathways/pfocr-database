caption: The schematic summary of the molecular mechanisms of SNORA58 facilitating
  radioresistance and anisomycin as a radiosensitizer in ESCC. In ESCC patients, SNORA58
  exhibits heterogeneous expression patterns. Its aberrant overexpression stabilizes
  CTCF by inhibiting ubiquitin‐mediated degradation, leading to the attenuation of
  JNK1‐mediated ferroptosis induced by radiotherapy through disruption of intracellular
  iron homeostasis, and ultimately contributes to radioresistance. Administration
  of the JNK signaling pathway activator could effectively overcome SNORA58‐induced
  resistance
