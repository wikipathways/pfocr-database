caption: EP’s organic matters (mainly polysaccharides and proteins) undergo •OH/•O2--mediated
  degradation into small molecules (e.g. sugar, reducing sugar, peptides/amino acids).
  Sugar or reducing sugar may undergo dehydration, cyclization, carboxylation and
  Maillard reaction by free radical attack to form furfurals, glyoxal, and pyranone derivatives etc.
  Peptides/amino acids mainly undergo carbonyl-amino condensation to form N-heterocyclics.
  These precursors may be reacted by HAT/RAF/SET, further generating •R that polymerized
  into FLA. The cartoon schematics of EP and FLA in the figure were made in PowerPoint,
  and the chemical structures in the figure were drawn using ChemDraw. Related GC-MS
  and Py-GCMS data are provided in Table S4, 5
