caption: 'Multiple Mechanisms of H₂S in Regulating Placental Angiogenesis. H₂S modulates
  placental vascular function and trophoblast invasion through multiple pathways,
  facilitating adaptive adjustments in placental blood flow. In early pregnancy, CBS-mediated
  H₂S production increases, which downregulates sFlt-1 and sEng expression, thereby
  enhancing PlGF levels and promoting trophoblast invasion, facilitating placental
  implantation and early development. In late pregnancy, H₂S regulates VEGF release
  by inhibiting miR-200c targeting VEGF mRNA and suppressing miR-20a and miR-20b targeting
  VEGF protein. This process enhances VEGF release, promotes placental vascular dilation,
  and optimizes placental blood flow supply. Overexpression of miR-21 inhibits CSE
  and SP1 transcription factors, reducing placental H₂S production. Additionally,
  miR-21 promotes the proliferation of VSMCs while inhibiting their differentiation,
  thereby affecting placental vascular function. H₂S primarily enhances NOS3 phosphorylation
  through the AKT1-NOS3 signaling pathway, which promotes NO production, leading to
  vasodilation and improved blood perfusion. The PI3K-specific inhibitor Wortmannin
  blocks NOS3 phosphorylation, whereas the MAPK3/1-specific inhibitor U0126 does not
  affect NOS3 activity. Moreover, H₂S activates the MAPK3/1 signaling pathway, enhancing
  placental endothelial cell proliferation and migration, further promoting placental
  angiogenesis. The H₂S donor NaHS significantly enhances these effects. Notes: sFlt-1:
  Soluble fms-like tyrosine kinase-1; sEng: Soluble endoglin; PlGF: Placental growth
  factor; VEGF: Vascular Endothelial Growth Factor; PE: Preeclampsia; SP1: Specificity
  Protein 1; NO: Nitric Oxide; miR-21: miRNA-21; VSMCs: Vascular Smooth Muscle Cells;
  AKT1: AKT Serine/Threonine Kinase 1; NOS3: Nitric Oxide Synthase 3; MAPK3/1: Mitogen-Activated
  Protein Kinase 3/1'
