caption: 'Effect of rm‐GALC on autophagy regulation. This schematic illustrates the
  hypothesized impact of rm‐GALC dosing on autophagy regulation. Administration of
  a supra‐physiological dose may overwhelm the autophagy flux, impairing the proper
  degradation of materials within the pathway. Conversely, our results indicate that
  administering a physiological dose of rm‐GALC sufficient to restore normal enzymatic
  activity supports the recovery of autophagy flux. This promotes the clearance of
  abnormally accumulated materials typically observed in TWI cells. These findings
  underscore the importance of precise dosing in therapeutic strategies aiming to
  restore GALC activity and normalize cellular processes in KD tissues, strongly supporting
  the role of nanomedicine‐based ERT strategies. A schematic nanovector representation
  is shown: stabilized reverse micelle (SRMs; right) and polymeric nanoparticles (PLGA‐NPs;
  left). RmGALC = recombinant murine Galactosylceramidase; TWI = Twitcher; ERT = enzyme
  replacement therapy'
