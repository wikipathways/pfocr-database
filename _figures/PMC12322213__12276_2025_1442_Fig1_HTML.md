caption: 'A DSB is introduced into a functional (smiley face) locus of human genomic
  DNA designated as YFG, which temporarily inactivates it (frowny face). The chromosomal
  DSB can be repaired by either of two end joining pathways: either C-NHEJ, which
  requires the factors Ku, DNA-PKcs and LIG4, or via A-NHEJ, which requires the factors
  POLQ and LIG1/3. Because both of these pathways can be error prone, they can generate
  insertions and deletions (‘indels’, red bar) that cause out-of-frame mutations and
  result in the permanent inactivation of the gene (frowny face) following completion
  of repair'
