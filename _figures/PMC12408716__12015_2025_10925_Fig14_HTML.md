  ES could activate the cAMP pathway by stimulating adenylyl cyclase. In addition,
  rolipram could prevent degradation of the cAMP by inhibiting the PDE4. The activated
  cAMP pathway leads to increased expression of cytoskeleton proteins, neurite outgrowth,
  and nerve regeneration. Also, the ES improves Ca2+ influx, which can improve neuronal
  differentiation. Additionally, the cAMP pathway upregulates BDNF expression, enhancing
  cell viability and promoting neuronal differentiation. The Figure is created using
