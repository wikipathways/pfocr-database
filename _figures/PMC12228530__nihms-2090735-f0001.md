caption: 'Mechanism of tissue regeneration by mRNA translational control. mRNA translation
  is controlled mainly by two key signaling pathways: mTOR and ISR. Activation of
  mTOR pathway and inhibition of ISR engender proregenerative processes at early stages
  of regeneration through the induction of protein synthesis and ribosome biogenesis.
  The secretion of growth factors (e.g. IGFs, VEGF) and nutrients (e.g. amino acids
  [AAs]) released from cells at injury sites enhances mTORC1 activity and suppresses
  ISR to promote tissue repair. Small molecules with potential regenerative application
  highlighted in green boxes'
