  propionate synthesis in the Oat1 KO microbiome. The KEGG pathway: ko00640 (Propanoate
  metabolism) was plotted with the results of the DESEQ2 analysis. Red highlights

  Oat1 KO gut bacteria for an enzymatic step. Thickness of the highlight indicates

  < 0.05). Minor propionate synthesis pathways, via 1,2-propanediol are also significantly
