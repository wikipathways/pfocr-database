caption: Schematic illustration of Co-TB COF-M sonosensitizers enabling potent antitumor
  immunotherapy via coordinated innate immune activation and vascular normalization.
  (a) Synthesis process of Co-TB COF-M. (b) The enhanced sonodynamic performance,
  driven by electron transfer between the Wurster-type COF and cobaloxime, generates
  a ROS surge that triggers GSDMD-mediated pyroptosis in cancer cells. (c) In the
  tumor microenvironment, the ROS generated by Co-TB COF-M prompts the release of
  STING agonists, which synergize with dsDNA from dying cells to robustly activate
  the STING-mediated innate immune pathway. (d) Ultimately, Co-TB COF-M treatment
  enhances T-cell infiltration and response to immunotherapy by synergistically coordinating
  innate immunity with blood vessel normalization
