caption: 'Main signaling pathways involved in the in vitro differentiation of hPSCs
  into spiny projection neurons (SPNs). TGF-β (green) and BMP (blue): Members of the
  transforming growth factor (TGF)-β and bone morphogenetic protein (BMP) superfamily
  are key regulators of human pluripotent stem cells (hPSC) pluripotency maintenance
  and self-renewal capacity. Pathway activators: Activin A. Pathway inhibitors: SB431542,
  Dorsomorphin, LDN193189. WNTs (orange): Regulate mesoendodermal differentiation
  and maintain pluripotency. Activators: CHIR 99021. Inhibitors: DKK1, XAV939, IWR-1.
  Hedgehog (magenta): Mainly focused on SHH, are critical for specification of certain
  neural subtypes including ventral telencephalon. Activator: SHH, SAG, Purmorphamine.
  Inhibitor: Cyclopamine. Neurotrophic factors (red and dark blue): BDNF, GDNF, and
  IGF further support SPN specification and maturation. FGF (pink): MEK/ERK signaling
  downstream of FGF is essential for hPSC proliferation and maintenance. Other trophic
  factors may also converge on MEK/ERK and PI3K. Notch (brown): activated through
  interaction with Delta/Jagged ligands, release the NICD and influences neural subtype
  specification. Inhibitor: DAPT. RA (cyan): retinoic acid plays a pivotal role in
  neural differentiation and patterning. Epigenetics (yellow): histone and DNA modifications,
  including acetylation and methylation, modulate gene expression and are essential
  for reprogramming. Histone deacetylase inhibitors: valproic Acid, valpromide. CDK4/6
  (light green): regulates cell cycle progression through the G1 phase, influencing
  proliferation and timing of differentiation. Inhibitor: PD 0332991. RHO/ROCK (purple):
  regulates cytoskeletal dynamics, cell migration, and apoptosis; its activation leads
  to anoikis in hPSCs lacking cadherin/integrin signaling. Inhibitors: Y-27632. AKT
  protein kinase B, ALK anaplastic lymphoma kinase, APC adenomatous polyposis coli,
  AXIN axis inhibition protein, BDNF brain-derived neurotrophic factor, β-cat beta-catenin,
  BMP bone morphogenetic protein, BMPβRI/II BMP type I/II receptor, CDK 4/6 cyclin-dependent
  kinase 4 and 6, CycloP cyclopamine, DAPT (N-[N-(3,5-difluorophenacetyl)-L-alanyl]-S-phenylglycine
  t-butyl ester), DKK1 dickkopf-1, DSH dishevelled, ERK1/2 extracellular signal-regulated
  kinase 1/2, FGF fibroblast growth factor, FGFR fibroblast growth factor receptor,
  FZD frizzled receptor, GLI glioma-associated oncogene homolog, GSK3 glycogen synthase
  kinase 3, IGF insulin growth factor, IGFIR insulin-like growth factor 1 receptor,
  IWR1 inhibitor of Wnt response 1, LDN LDN193189, LRP5/6 low-density lipoprotein
  receptor-related protein 5/6, MEK mitogen-activated protein kinase kinase 1/2, mTOR
  mechanistic target of rapamycin, NICD Notch intracellular domain, PI3K phosphoinositide
  3-kinase, PTC patched receptor, RA retinoic acid, RAF rapidly accelerated fibrosarcoma
  kinase, RAS rat sarcoma GTPase, RHO Ras homologous GTPase, ROCK Rho-associated kinase,
  SAG smoothened agonist, SHH Sonic hedgehog, SMAD mothers against decapentaplegic
  homolog, SMO smoothened receptor, TGF-β transforming growth factor beta, TGFβRI
  TGF-β receptor I, TNKS tankyrase, TrkB tropomyosin receptor kinase B, VPA valproic
  acid, WNT wingless/integrated, γ-SEC γ secretase'
