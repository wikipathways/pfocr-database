caption: Pannexin 1 activation of inflammasomes, apoptosis, and pyroptosis. Upon activation,
  pannexin (Panx) 1 channels open to release ATP, which stimulates purinergic receptors,
  inducing Ca²⁺ influx and ER Ca²⁺ release. Intracellular Ca²⁺ overload establishes
  a positive feedback loop that further activates Panx1 and triggers the mitochondrial
  apoptosis pathway. Concurrently, Panx1-mediated K⁺ efflux activates inflammasomes,
  recruiting Caspase-1 to cleave gasdermin D (GSDMD) and pro-inflammatory cytokines
  IL-1β/18, thereby initiating inflammatory responses and pyroptosis. Additionally,
  Panx1 enhances Caspase-11-mediated cleavage of GSDMD through purinergic receptor
  activation, leading to membrane perforation and pyroptosis
