caption: PKM2 facilitates the release of cytokines, such as CXCL1 and MIF, from tumor
  cells, which can bind to the CXCR2/CXCR4 receptors on the surface of MDSCs. This
  interaction activates relevant signaling pathways within MDSCs, including the STAT3
  signaling pathway. Upon activation of the STAT3 signaling pathway. Activated STAT3
  functions as a transcription factor that binds to cathepsin (Cat) gene promoters
  and recruits transcriptional coactivators including histone acetyltransferases (HATs).
  HAT-mediated histone acetylation induces chromatin relaxation, thereby potentiating
  cathepsin gene transcription
