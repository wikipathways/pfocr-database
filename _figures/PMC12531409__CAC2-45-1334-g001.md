caption: 'IL‐6 promotes cancer cachexia‐associated apathy via the neuro‐immune axis.
  Under physiological conditions, a low concentration of IL‐6 crosses the BBB and
  binds to IL‐6R in the ArP. A tightly regulated neural circuit comprising the NAc,
  VTA, SNpr, and PBN DA release to maintain normal feeding behavior and metabolic
  homeostasis. In the cachectic state, cancer‐induced elevation of IL‐6 disrupts this
  circuitry. Excessive IL‐6 over‐activates the ArP, suppressing DA release from the
  VTA to the NAc. This dysregulation contributes to hypophagia and apathy. Notably,
  therapeutic interventions including IL‐6 blockade, IL‐6R KD, DA receptor agonists,
  and VTA‐NAc pathway stimulation have demonstrated efficacy in reversing these symptoms,
  suggesting the promising translational strategies for cachexia management (created
  with bioRender.com). Abbreviations: IL‐6, Interleukin‐6; IL‐6R, Interleukin‐6 receptor;
  ArP, area postrema; BBB, blood‐brain barrier; DA, dopamine; NAc, nucleus accumbens;
  PBN, parabrachial nucleus; SNpr, substantia nigra pars reticulata; VTA, ventral
  tegmental area; KD, knockdown'
