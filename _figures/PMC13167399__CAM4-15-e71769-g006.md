caption: The Ang1/Tie2 signaling pathway and its downstream related factors. The Ang1
  binds to the Tie2 receptor and inhibits the expression of NF‐κB, thereby reducing
  inflammatory responses. Additionally, it can activate downstream Ras/MEK proteins
  to promote tumor proliferation and activate PI3K/AKT proteins to influence the expression
  of factors such as Bad, Caspase‐9, FOXO‐1, eNOS, and Survivin. Activated FOXO‐1
  may increase the expression of the cytoplasmic Ang2 factor, which is then secreted
  into the extracellular space, disrupting the Ang1/Tie2 signaling pathway
