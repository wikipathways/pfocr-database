caption: (A) Chemical structures of the heme species in the heme a pathway. Heme a
  is produced from the heme b (also known as protoheme) precursor in a two-step reaction
  mediated by Cox10 (heme o synthase) and Cox15 (heme a synthase) enzymes. The reaction
  involves the formation of a short-lived heme o intermediate. (B) Mitochondrial heme
  b distribution and heme a route. Ferrochelatase (FECH) metalates protoporphyrin
  IX (PPIX), yielding heme b, which is rapidly routed to subcellular locations via
  an unknown mechanism. In the heme a pathway, heme b is trafficked via Cox10 and
  Cox15, which modify it to heme a. Heme a is inserted into maturing CcO by assembly
  factors Coa2 and Shy1. The assembly factor Pet117 appears to be also involved in
  this process. IM, inner mitochondrial membrane; IMS, intermembrane space; OM, outer
  mitochondrial membrane
