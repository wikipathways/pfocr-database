caption: Gene–gene interaction network for CDKN2A, ITGA3, SMAD4 and SIRT7. The functional
  association network of CDKN2A, ITGA3, SMAD4 and SIRT7 genes, using GeneMANIA, displayed
  physical interaction between ITGA3 and SIRT7 and co-expression of CDKN2A and SMAD4.
  The 4 central nodes represent our target genes surrounded by 20 nodes representing
  genes that greatly correlated with the target genes in terms of co-expression, prediction,
  pathway, and shared protein domains. The top five genes displaying the greatest
  correlations with these targets were SMAD1, SMAD2, FOXO3, CDK4 and ITGB1. With regard
  to other human genes, ITGA3 showed physical interaction and co-expression with CD151
  and ITGB1, and also co-localisation and shared protein domain with the latter. CDKN2A
  demonstrated physical interaction with CDK6, CDK4, CDKN2B and CDKN2C; co-expression
  with CDK6 and CDKN2C; shared pathway with CDK4 and CdK6; shared protein domain with
  CDKN2B and CDKN2C. SMAD4 displayed physical interaction with SMAD1, SMAD2, SMAD3,
  SKI, USP9X, TRIM33, FOXO3 and TGIF2; shared pathway with SMAD1, SMAD2, SMAD3, SKI,
  FOXO3 and TGIF2; co-expression with SMAD2, USP9X and TGIF2; gene interaction with
  USP9X; shared protein domain with SMAD1, SMAD2 and SMAD3. The figure was created
  with GeneMANIA
