caption: '(A) The BAFF/APRIL pathway. BAFF and APRIL are type II transmembrane proteins
  and exist in soluble and membrane trimeric cytokines. BAFF and APRIL are capable
  of binding to TACI and BCMA; at the same time, BAFF molecule can also interact with
  a third receptor, BAFF-R. All of these receptors are predominantly expressed by
  B cells at different stages of B cell differentiation, and activation of these 3
  receptors results in an increase in B cell survival, maturation, and differentiation
  and also increases antibody production and plasma cell survival. (B) The role of
  telitacicept. Telitacicept, a dual inhibitor, effectively binds with BAFF and APRIL
  molecules and interfere in binding with type III transmembrane protein receptors
  such as BAFF-R, TACI, and BCMA; significantly decreases B cell survival, maturation,
  and differentiation; and decreases antibody production and plasma cell survival.
  Abbreviations: APRIL, a proliferation-inducing ligand; BAFF, B cell activating factor;
  BAFF-R, B cell activating factor receptor; BCMA, B cell maturation antigen; ERK,
  extracellular signal-regulated kinase; JNK, c-Jun N-terminal kinase; NF B1, nuclear
  factor B1; P38MAPK, p38 mitogen-activated protein kinase; TACI, transmembrane activator
  and CAML interactor'
