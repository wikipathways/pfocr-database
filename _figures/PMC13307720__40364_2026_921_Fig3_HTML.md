caption: 'Downstream pathways of ZDHHC5. A. ZDHHC5 catalyzes the S-palmitoylation
  of Frizzled-5, a modification that is potentiated upon HFS or cLTP. These stimuli
  elevate endogenous Wnt7a levels. This palmitoylation reduces the endocytosis of
  Frizzled-5 and promotes its plasma membrane localization. B. The palmitoylation
  of NCX1, mediated by plasma membrane-localized ZDHHC5, results in its functional
  inactivation. C. ZDHHC5-catalyzed palmitoylation of Gαs and Gαi enhances their membrane
  anchoring and stability, which promotes AC activation, cAMP accumulation, and subsequent
  PKA signaling, thereby ultimately increasing cardiomyocyte contractility. D. ZDHHC5
  catalyzes the palmitoylation of lamin A. The level of lamin a palmitoylation is
  negatively correlated with myoblast differentiation: low levels promote differentiation,
  whereas high levels inhibit it. E. ZDHHC5 activates the PI3K/AKT/mTOR signaling
  pathway, thereby inhibiting autophagy and exacerbating the senescence of MSCs. F.
  Following autophagy induction, ZDHHC5 undergoes clathrin-mediated endocytosis and
  is trafficked to intracellular compartments, where it catalyzes the S-palmitoylation
  of ATG9A. Impaired ATG9A S-palmitoylation results in defects in autophagy initiation
  and autophagosome formation. G. GOLGA7 forms a mutually stabilizing complex with
  ZDHHC5 at the plasma membrane, which plays a critical role in a novel non-apoptotic
  cell death pathway induced by the small molecule CIL56. CIL56 blocks anterograde
  trafficking from the golgi to the plasma membrane, whereas the ZDHHC5–GOLGA7 complex
  mediates retrograde trafficking. This imbalance leads to protein accumulation and
  mislocalization, triggering a unique form of cell death. H. Golga7b acts as an accessory
  subunit that stabilizes the plasma membrane localization of ZDHHC5 by preventing
  its clathrin/AP2-mediated endocytosis. The ZDHHC5-Golga7b complex interacts with
  desmosomal components, including Dsg2 and Pkp3, which are palmitoylated by ZDHHC5.
  Loss of either ZDHHC5 or Golga7b disrupts the membrane localization of Dsg2 and
  desmosome reassembly, impairing intercellular adhesion in cardiac and epithelial
  tissues. I. ZDHHC5 mediates the palmitoylation of PCDH7. The two proteins co-localize
  at the cell surface during metaphase and at the cleavage furrow during cytokinesis.
  This spatiotemporal targeting enables PCDH7 to maintain RhoA activity and ROCK-dependent
  phosphorylation of myosin light chain (pMLC), thereby enhancing the actomyosin contractility
  required for successful abscission and ensuring the faithful completion of cytokinesis'
