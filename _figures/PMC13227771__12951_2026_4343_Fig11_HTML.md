caption: Schematic of a therapeutic strategy using zinc-phenolic nanocapsules to potentiate
  immunotherapy in triple-negative breast cancer through the coordinated activation
  of pyroptosis and the STING pathway.The RMP@Cap nanocapsule induces pyroptosis via
  the NLRP3-Caspase-1-GSDMD pathway, leading to mitochondrial damage and mtDNA release.
  Liberated mtDNA activates the cGAS-STING pathway, driving type I interferon production.
  STING activation further promotes pyroptosis, while pyroptosis-derived mtDNA continuously
  reinforces STING signaling, establishing a cyclic-cascade amplification loop that
  synergistically enhances antitumor immunity
