caption: 'The G protein signalling pathway of Saccharomyces cerevisiae and Cryptococcus
  neoformans. Studies have indicated that mating requires the G protein and its downstream
  MAPK pathway in both yeast-like fungi. In addition, other biological processes,
  such as virulence expression and vegetative growth, are regulated by the cAMP signalling
  pathway. Interestingly, in C. neoformans, the cAMP pathway mediated by Gpa1 also
  influences mating. Mat2, Znf2, and Ste12 are transcription factors for modulating
  the mating in S. cerevisiae and C. neoformans; Sxi1α/Sxi2a are specific recognition
  factors essential for sexual development after cell fusion during heterothallic
  mating of C. neoformans. G protein-coupled receptors: Gpr-1, GPR2, GPR4, Ste2, and
  Ste3; Gα: Gpa1, Gpa2, and Gpa3; Gβ: Gpb1 and Ste4; Gγ: Ste18; Cac1, adenylyl cyclase;
  PKA, protein kinase A. The dotted line represents indirect regulation, and the solid
  line represents direct regulation. This figure was created with BioGDP.com'
