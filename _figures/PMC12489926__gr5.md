caption: 'FKBP51-Mediated Pathways in Alzheimer''s PathogenesisA: Regulation of SA
  by FKBP51 and SKA2. The SA pathway involves TRIM16/SEC22B-mediated cargo (e.g.,
  IL-1β) delivery to LC3B + autophagosomes. SEC22B (R-SNARE) then drives fusion with
  the plasma membrane by assembling with Q-SNAREs SNAP23/29 (Qbc) and STX3/4 (Qa),
  enabling extracellular cargo release. FKBP51 facilitates SA by promoting TRIM16-SEC22B
  complex stability and SNARE complex assembly, enhancing fusion efficiency (positive
  regulator). Conversely, SKA2 suppresses SA by inhibiting SNARE complex formation
  during fusion, acting as a gatekeeper (negative regulator).B: FKBP51/HSP90 Complex
  Drives Pathogenic Tau Processing. Tau binding by the FKBP51/HSP90 complex orders
  tau and inhibits 20S proteasomal degradation, resulting in the accumulation of neurotoxic
  tau oligomers'
