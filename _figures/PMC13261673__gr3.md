caption: 'Response mechanisms of deepwater rice (escape strategy) and Sub1A rice (quiescence
  strategy) to flooding stress.Escape strategy (left): upon ethylene accumulation,
  the GA biosynthesis pathway is activated through ABA suppression, which relieves
  ABA-mediated repression of GA signaling, and through the upregulation of GA biosynthetic
  genes (e.g., SD1 and GA20ox2). This results in substantial accumulation of active
  GA (e.g., GA4), driving rapid internode elongation under flooding. Quiescence strategy
  (right): SUB1A protein inhibits ethylene and GA signaling pathways and promotes
  the accumulation of DELLA repressors (e.g., SLR1/SLRL1) of GA signaling, resulting
  in growth arrest of plants under flooding. This hormonal reconfiguration restricts
  starch catabolism and shoot elongation, preserving resources until floodwaters recede.
  The red arrow represents upregulation of the indicated genes. GA20ox, gibberellin
  20-oxidase; SD1, semidwarf 1; SLR1/SLRL1, slender rice-1/SLR1 like-1; Sub1A, submergence
  1A'
