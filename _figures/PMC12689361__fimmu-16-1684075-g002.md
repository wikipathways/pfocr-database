caption: Polarization and metabolic alterations of M1 and M2 macrophages. M1 and M2
  macrophages exhibit distinct functional phenotypes shaped by specific stimuli. M1
  polarization is driven by LPS, IFN-γ, and TLRs, leading to production of pro-inflammatory
  mediators (e.g., IL-1β, IL-12, IL-23, ROS, TNF-α). The metabolic changes of M1 macrophages
  were characterized by enhanced glycolysis, disruption of the TCA cycle, and damage
  to OXPHOS, accompanied by downregulation of FAO. M2 polarization mainly depended
  on the complete TCA cycle, OXPHOS, and FAO. M2 polarization is primarily induced
  by IL-4, IL-13, etc., and characterized by anti-inflammatory/pro-fibrotic responses,
  exemplified by IL-10 and TGF-β1 release. IL, lnterleukin; MHC-II, major histocompatibility
