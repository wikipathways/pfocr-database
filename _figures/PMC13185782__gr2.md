caption: Schematic of the noncanonical pyroptosis pathwayUpon entering the cytosol,
  LPS derived from Gram-negative bacteria directly induces the autoactivation of caspase-4/5
  in humans or caspase-11 in mice, leading to the cleavage of GSDMD and the release
  of its pore-forming N-terminal fragment (N-GSDMD). Oligomerized N-GSDMD inserts
  into the plasma membrane to form transmembrane pores, triggering lytic and proinflammatory
  pyroptotic cell death. The resulting membrane permeabilization drives K+ efflux
  and other ionic fluxes, which secondarily activate the NLRP3-ASC-pro-caspase-1 complex.
  Activated caspase-1 subsequently processes pro-IL-1β and pro-IL-18 into their mature
  forms, thus amplifying the canonical inflammasome pathway. Although this pathway
  initiates independently of the inflammasome assembly, it converges with the canonical
  pathway at downstream signaling steps. The figure was created with BioRender.com.LPS,
  lipopolysaccharide; GSDMD, gasdermin D
