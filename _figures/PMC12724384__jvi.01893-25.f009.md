caption: Working model of the distinct roles of TRIM31 in the replication of IAV and
  other RNA viruses. In cells infected with RNA viruses, such as SeV and VSV, TRIM31
  promotes the MAVS-mediated IFN-I response and inhibits viral replication. Conversely,
  in IAV-infected cells, the PB1, PA, and HA proteins of IAV exploit TRIM31 to promote
  their stability and competitively bind to TRIM31 with MAVS, and thus counteract
  TRIM31’s antiviral effect on the IFN-I response. Blue arrows indicate MAVS-mediated
  type I IFN signaling pathway; green arrows indicate the process of TRIM31-mediated
  stabilization and activation of MAVS and viral proteins; and the dashed lines indicate
  the low-level interaction
