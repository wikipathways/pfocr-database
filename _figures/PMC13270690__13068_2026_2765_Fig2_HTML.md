caption: 'Physiological tolerance mechanisms, metabolic pathways, and transporter
  diversity involved in D-xylose utilization by yeasts. A Tolerance to environmental
  and process-related stresses: autochthonous yeasts, such as Candida subhashii, exhibit
  tolerance to industrial stresses associated with lignocellulosic hydrolysates, including
  inhibitors (acetic acid, furfural, and phenolic compounds). Defense systems involve
  the upregulation of transcription factors (Ace2p, Sfp1p) and antioxidant responses
  (increased CTT1 and CTA1; decreased HSR12) triggered by xylitol production. Adaptive
  responses include thermotolerance (37 °C), resistance to low pH, and metabolic versatility
  under oxygen-limited conditions (OTR control) and mixed sugar streams (glucose,
  xylose, and cellobiose). B Central metabolic pathway for d-xylose and glucose fermentation:
  integration of xylose and glucose assimilation. Xylose enters via specific transporters
  and is processed through the oxidoreductive pathway (Xyl1, Xyl2) or isomerase route
  (XylA) to xylulose, which is phosphorylated (Xks1) to enter the pentose phosphate
  pathway (PPP). These pathways connect to glycolysis, the TCA cycle, and ethanol
  formation. C Native yeasts as sources of efficient pentose transporters: yeasts
  from diverse environments (forest floor, compost, bovine feces) provide a library
  of transporters (MFS, Hxt, Hgt, SUT1, XUT3-E538K, CS4130) with improved xylose affinity
  and reduced glucose inhibition, serving as essential modules for metabolic engineering.
  Sugar metabolism abbreviations: G-6-P, glucose-6-phosphate; Gn-6-P, glucono-1,5-lactone-6-phosphate;
  6-P-G, 6-phosphogluconate; Ru-5-P, ribulose-5-phosphate; Ri-5-P, ribose-5-phosphate;
  X-5-P, xylulose-5-phosphate; F-6-P, fructose-6-phosphate; F-1,6-BP, fructose-1,6-bisphosphate;
  Ga-3-P, glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate; DHAP, dihydroxyacetone phosphate; PEP, phosphoenolpyruvate;
  PYR, pyruvate; Oaa, oxaloacetate; α-KG, alpha-ketoglutarate; Suc, succinate; Fum,
  fumarate; Mal, malate; Glo, glyoxylate. Enzyme abbreviations: HXK1,2, hexokinase;
  PGI1, phosphoglucose isomerase; PFK1, phosphofructokinase; FBP1, fructose-1,6-bisphosphatase;
  FBA1, fructose-bisphosphate aldolase; TPI1, triose-phosphate isomerase; ZWF1, glucose-6-phosphate
  dehydrogenase; GND, 6-phosphogluconate dehydrogenase; RKI1, ribose-5-phosphate isomerase;
  TKL1,2, transketolase; TAL1, transaldolase; Xyl1, xylose reductase; Xyl2, xylitol
  dehydrogenase; XylA, xylose isomerase; Xks1, xylulokinase; PYK1, pyruvate kinase;
  PCK1, phosphoenolpyruvate carboxykinase; PYC1, pyruvate carboxylase; PDA1/PDB1,
  pyruvate dehydrogenase complex; PDC1, pyruvate decarboxylase; ALD4, aldehyde dehydrogenase;
  ADH1, alcohol dehydrogenase; CIT1,2, citrate synthase; ACO1, aconitase; IDH1,2,
  isocitrate dehydrogenase; KGD1,2, alpha-ketoglutarate dehydrogenase; SDH1,2,4, succinate
  dehydrogenase; FUM1, fumarase; MDH1, malate dehydrogenase; ICL1, isocitrate lyase;
  MLS1, malate synthase. General abbreviations: OTR, oxygen transfer rate; PPP, pentose
  phosphate pathway; TCA, tricarboxylic acid cycle'
