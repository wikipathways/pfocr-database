caption: Hypothetical role of the WIP–WASp–DOCK8 complex in IL-6–mediated STAT3 phosphorylation
  in T cells. DOCK8 has been shown to associate with WASp and WIP to form a molecular
  complex in T lymphocytes. In this study, we demonstrate that WASp deficiency impairs
  STAT3-Y705 phosphorylation following IL-6 stimulation in T cells. These findings
  support a speculative model in which the WIP–WASp–DOCK8 complex contributes to IL-6/STAT3
  signaling. The precise mechanism by which the WASP/WIP/DOCK8 complex regulates the
  IL-6/STAT3 signaling pathway remains to be elucidated. This figure has been created
