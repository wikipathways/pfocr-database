caption: The role of SUMOylation in the regulation of the Golgi apparatus, endosomes,
  and lysosomes. SUMOylation regulates the functions of the Golgi apparatus, endosomes,
  and lysosomes via multi-level molecular mechanisms. In the Golgi transport system,
  SUMOylation modifies key proteins such as SORTILIN, P155, and CTF, thereby precisely
  regulating the localization and transport efficiency of critical membrane proteins
  like VEGFR2 and GLUT1/4, which influences cellular material transport and signal
  transduction. Regarding the regulation of endosomal sorting, SUMOylation dynamically
  modulates the SUMOylation status of substrates such as HNRNPA1 through enzymes like
  SENP1/6, affecting their sorting and subcellular localization within the endosomal
  sorting system. In the lysosomal degradation pathway, SUMOylation not only affects
  degradation efficiency by regulating the oligomerization state and interactions
  of proteins such as α-SYN, IL-1R1, and TOLLIP but also influences lysosome-related
  degradation processes by modulating the SUMOylation state of key factors such as
  RAB7
