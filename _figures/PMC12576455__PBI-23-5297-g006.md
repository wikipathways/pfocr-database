caption: Schematic model illustrating the involvement of MsNAC2a in salt stress tolerance
  regulation in alfalfa. Upon experiencing salt stress, MsNAC2a is induced and directly
  binds to specific cis‐elements in the MsCOX11 promoter, leading to downregulation
  of MsCOX11 transcription and subsequent ROS accumulation. Moreover, MsNAC2a binds
  directly to the MsOASA1 and MsLCD1 promoters, leading to upregulation of MsOASA1
  and downregulation of MsLCD1, which ultimately results in a reduction of H2S levels.
  The reduced H2S levels further improve salt stress susceptibility in alfalfa. Notably,
  MsEREBP1 directly interacts with MsNAC2a and influences its transcriptional activity,
  suggesting that the MsNAC2a–MsEREBP1 module may act as a central hub for the regulation
  of stress‐responsive pathways, precisely controlling the expression of downstream
  genes involved in stress tolerance. Taken together, MsNAC2a is likely involved in
  regulating the trade‐off between stress resistance and normal growth by controlling
  the expression of ROS‐scavenging genes and H2S homeostasis‐related genes, leading
  to ROS accumulation and H2S degradation, ultimately reducing the salt tolerance
  of alfalfa
