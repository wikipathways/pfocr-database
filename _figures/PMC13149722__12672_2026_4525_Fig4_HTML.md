caption: The chemical structural diversity of natural dietary polyphenols and its
  association with biological activity. This figure systematically reveals the classification
  of the chemical structures of natural dietary polyphenols. The common structural
  feature is the presence of one or more phenolic rings, and they are classified as
  flavonoids (such as EGCG, quercetin) and non-flavonoids (such as resveratrol, chlorogenic
  acid). Flavonoids share the core skeleton of benzopyranone, and the differences
  in their hydroxylation, methoxylation and glycosylation patterns determine their
  unique biological activity, solubility and bioavailability. These intricate chemical
  structures collectively form the molecular basis for polyphenols to regulate multiple
  signaling pathways and exert anti-liver cancer effects
