caption: HIF-1A and TGF-β/SMAD Signaling in uterine fibroids. This schematic illustrates
  how under hypoxic conditions, stabilization of HIF-1α promotes activation of TGF-β3
  signaling and downstream SMAD2/3–SMAD4 transcriptional complexes in uterine fibroid
  cells. This pathway induces expression of extracellular matrix–related genes (e.g.,
  COL1A1, COL3A1, FN1, CTGF), contributing to excessive matrix deposition and fibrosis.
  Progressive ECM accumulation impairs oxygen diffusion, reinforcing local hypoxia,
