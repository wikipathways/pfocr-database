caption: Interaction of CAFs with tumor cells and immune cells in HCC. CAFs in TME
  secreted TGF-β1, SDF1, CLCF1, exosomes, etc., up-regulated the AKT/STAT3 signaling
  pathway, AXIN1/β catenin axis, and MV cadherin, and facilitated the proliferation,
  metastasis, and VM formation of tumor cells in HCC. In addition, CAFs produce CCL5,
  IL-6, IL-8, and CCL-2, which increase the expression of PD-1 and affect the function
  of naïve cells and CD8 + T cells, leading to the resistance of the efficacy of PD-1/PD-L1
  in the treatment of HCC. In addition, CAFs prevent CD8 + T cell infiltration by
  secreting IL-34, which promotes the recruitment of Treg. IL-6, MIP, CD36, and CXCL12
  produced by CAFs activate STAT3 in macrophages, leading to macrophage polarization
  to tumor-facilitating types (M2 macrophages), and secretion of SPP1 to down-regulate
  the function of CD8 + T cells. CAF also upregulates PD-L1 expression and activates
  the STAT3 signaling pathway in TAN via IL-6, TGF-β, CLCF1, and CXCL6 to facilitate
  differentiation into N2 types, leading to immunosuppression. Created with BioRender.com
