caption: Mechanism for multidrug resistance in OC caused by Hh. A GLI1 downregulates
  the epithelial gene E-cadherin and upregulates mesenchymal genes N-cadherin and
  vimentin while interacting with HGF to promote EMT in tumors. In drug-resistant
  cancer, the expression of EMT-related transcription factors such as Snail (SNAI1),
  Slug (SNAI2), Twist, Zeb1, and Zeb2 is upregulated. B Platinum–DNA adducts activate
  NER by interfering with the normal structure and function of DNA. C GLI1 regulates
  the expression of ABC transporters ABCB1/MDR-1 and ABCG2, leading to increased efflux
  of chemotherapeutic drugs in tumors, reduced intracellular drug concentration, and
  enhanced drug resistance. D GLI1 remodels the tumor microenvironment to inhibit
  autophagy, enhance the anti-apoptotic ability of tumor cells, and promote the activation
  and proliferation of CAFs. E OC spheroids in patient ascites exhibit varying sizes
  and morphologies and demonstrate stronger drug resistance compared to single cells.
  F CA-MSCs and CA-SCs interact with OCSCs through the Hh signaling pathway
