  begins with the condensation of serine and palmitoyl-CoA, catalyzed by serine palmitoyltransferase.
  Ceramide can be phosphorylated to form ceramide-1-phosphate. Additionally, ceramide
  can be metabolized to sphingomyelin and glycosphingolipids through the actions of
  sphingomyelin synthases and glycosphingolipid synthases, respectively. Alternatively,
  ceramide can be hydrolyzed to sphingosine
