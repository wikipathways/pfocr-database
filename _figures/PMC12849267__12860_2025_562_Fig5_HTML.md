caption: Model of Gα1 and Gα2 signal transduction pathway interactions. Previous studies
  have indicated that cAMP stimulates cAMP receptors (CARs) and the Gα2 subunit and
  that conditioned medium factor (CMF) might stimulate the receptor activating the
  Gα1 subunit. The Gα2 subunit mediates chemotactic responses and transcription factor
  (GtaC) translocation. Results of this study suggest that the Gα1 subunit functions
  in a separate pathway but that there might be common binding sites on other signaling
  proteins such as regulators of G protein signaling (RGS) or other downstream regulatory
  proteins that could deplete Gα2 subunits from mediating GtaC translocation in the
  absence of the Gα1 subunit. The increased translocation of GtaC in gα2− cells upon
  the loss of Gα1 function implies that another pathway can mediate GtaC translocation
  in response to cAMP stimulation of cAMP receptors or cAMP receptor-like receptors
  (CRLs). This alternative pathway has reduced GtaC translocation compared the Gα2
  mediated pathway and must be inhibited by the Gα1 and Gα2 subunits
