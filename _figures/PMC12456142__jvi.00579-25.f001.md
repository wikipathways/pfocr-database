caption: 'Phosphatidylcholine lipids and their synthesis. A simplified PC lipid model
  emphasizing the major structural components: two fatty acyl tails, glycerol backbone,
  and the phosphocholine head (left). Since the tails can vary in their length (number
  of carbons) and degree of unsaturation (number and placement of double bonds), they
  are depicted as gray bars. In humans, three metabolic pathways can generate PC lipids
  (right). The de novo PC pathway starts with choline and uses DAG. The phosphatidylethanolamine
  N-methyltransferase pathway uses phosphatidylethanolamine lipids and modifies the
  head to generate PC. The LPCAT pathway, as part of the Lands Cycle, uses one-tailed
  lysophosphatidylcholine as a substrate and adds a second tail to generate PC'
