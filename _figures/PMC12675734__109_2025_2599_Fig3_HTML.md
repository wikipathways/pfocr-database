caption: Crosstalk between ferroptosis and apoptosis, necroptosis, autophagy, and
  pyroptosis in MASLD/MASH. (a) Apoptosis. The amplification loop of liver damage
  induced by the interplay between ferroptosis and apoptosis. (b) Necroptosis. Iron
  overload and GPX4 degradation can regulate both necroptosis and ferroptosis. (c)
  Autophagy. Dysregulated autophagy, including ferritinophagy, mitophagy, and lipophagy,
  mediates iron overload, ROS generation, and lipid peroxidation to induce ferroptosis
  in MASH/MASLD. (d) Pyroptosis. The DAMPs generated from pyroptosis activate the
  cGAS-STING pathway to induce ferroptosis, which in turn generates more DAMPs. RIPK,
  receptor-interacting protein kinase; MLKL, mixed-lineage-like kinase; 4-HNE, 4-hydroxynonenal;
  MDA, malondialdehyde; DAMP, damage-associated molecular pattern; mtDNA, mitochondrial
