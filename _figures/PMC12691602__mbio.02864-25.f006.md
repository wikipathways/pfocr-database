caption: Schematic model proposed for signal perception through QseGE. In the typical
  signal transduction pathway, histidine kinase (HK) is activated via sensing external
  stress, binding signal molecules, or stimulation by auxiliary proteins. Once activated,
  HK transfers a phosphate group to the response regulator (RR), triggering downstream
  gene expression regulation (left panel). Under standard culture conditions, QseG
  is anchored to the outer membrane and is an endogenous protein activator, persistently
  interacting with QseE (HK). This interaction keeps QseE in a constitutively active
  state (right panel)
