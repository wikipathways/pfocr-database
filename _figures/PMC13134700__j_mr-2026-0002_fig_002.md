caption: 'Metabolic reprogramming and bioenergetic abnormalities in pulmonary hypertension
  (PH). The metabolic landscape of PH is defined by coordinated bioenergetic dysregulation
  across six interconnected domains: (1) glucose metabolism with a warburg-like glycolytic
  shift. (2) Lipid and amino acid remodeling, including impaired fatty acid oxidation,
  altered eicosanoid balance, and glutamine and serine reprogramming. (3) Mitochondrial
  dysfunction characterized by abnormal fission–fusion dynamics, defective mitophagy,
  and impaired biogenesis. (4) Oxidative stress driven by ROS overload, eNOS uncoupling,
  and redox-sensitive signaling disruption. (5) Regulated cell death pathways such
  as pyroptosis, ferroptosis, and senescence. (6) Bioactive mediators including divergent
  prostaglandin signaling and the estrogen paradox'
