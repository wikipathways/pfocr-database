caption: Neurovascular dysfunction induced by MPs exposure. MPs entering circulation
  can cross the BBB and directly damage BMECs, leading to mitochondrial injury, tight‐junction
  disruption (claudin‐5, ZO‐1, occludin), and increased BBB permeability. Excessive
  ROS generation activates the NF‐κB pathway, promoting inflammation (TNF‐α, IL‐6)
  and endothelial apoptosis. Within the brain, MPs and inflammatory cytokines trigger
  microglial M1 polarization and neuroinflammation, releasing IL‐1β, IL‐18, NO, and
  ROS, which further injure neurons and astrocytes. Endothelial dysfunction (eNOS),
  up‐regulated ICAM‐1/VCAM‐1, and impaired vascular autoregulation together result
  in cerebral microvascular constriction, perfusion imbalance, and stroke susceptibility.
