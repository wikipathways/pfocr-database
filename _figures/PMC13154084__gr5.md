caption: 'Conceptual Illustration of Preferential Conduction in an Intramural Ventricular
  Arrhythmia(A) Schematic diagram showing the spatial relationship between the intramural
  ventricular arrhythmia (VA) origin and two endocardial recording sites: the junction
  of the left and right coronary cusps (Site 1) and the septal perforator vein (Site
  2). (B) Conceptual activation sequence during spontaneous PVC. Site 2, located closer
  to the VA origin, records earlier activation (52 ms before QRS onset). In contrast,
  Site 1, positioned farther from the VA origin, demonstrates less prematurity relative
  to the QRS (35 ms before QRS onset). (C) During pace mapping, the stimulus-to-QRS
  latency is longest at Site 1, the most remote endocardial site from the breakout
  point, reflecting conduction through a preferential intramural pathway. ABL = ablation
  catheter; LAD = left anterior descending artery; LCC = left coronary cusp; PVC =
  premature ventricular contraction; VA = ventricular arrhythmia'
