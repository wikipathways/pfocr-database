caption: The hypothesized role of the Dexter SLC45A2 variant in the melanogenic pathway.
  Early melanogenesis involves activation of MITF and thus the expression of TYRP1
  and TYR. (a) TYRP1 and TYR are trafficked to an acidic stage II melanosome. As the
  melanosome matures, the wildtype SLC45A2 cotransporter (b) exports protons, increasing
  the internal pH of the melanosome. This pH shift allows copper to bind tyrosinase,
  activating the enzyme to catalyze the initial steps of melanin synthesis. The Dexter
  SLC45A2 variant (c) fails to regulate pH, and subsequently reduces tyrosinase activity
  (d), ultimately preventing the enzymatic conversion of tyrosine into melanin intermediates
  (e). Created in BioRender. Fuller, A. (2025) https://BioRender.com/yjfzjrs
