caption: 'Integrated model of trait-associated gene modules in F. carica.The central
  illustration shows an F. carica tree, with the surrounding annulus divided into
  four colored quadrants that summarize key gene modules underlying major biological
  traits. Syconium formation (blue): upregulation of class A/E MADS-box genes (AP1,
  FUL, AP2, and SEP1/AGL6) and class B/C/D genes (AP3, AG, STK, and PI) orchestrates
  development of the enclosed inflorescence. Anthocyanin biosynthesis (purple): co-induction
  of the structural genes FcANS and FcCHS drives the sarcocarp transition from green
  to red. Psoralen biosynthesis (green): elevated expression of the root-specific
  gene FcMS correlates with increased psoralen accumulation. Drought responsiveness
  (yellow): stress triggers a regulatory cascade involving FcJA2, FcPP2C5, and FcP5CS
  (proline biosynthesis), thereby activating drought-response pathways. Red arrows
  indicate transcript upregulation or metabolite accumulation associated with each
  trait module'
