caption: Schematic diagram illustrating the induction of autophagy in Laodelphax striatellus
  by rice stripe virus (RSV) glycoprotein through activation of the Toll7 immune pathway.
  This study elucidates a novel molecular mechanism wherein RSV glycoprotein Gc interacts
  with the TIR domain of Toll7, leading to the activation of the Toll7 receptor. This
  interaction subsequently inhibits the intracellular PI3K‐Akt‐TOR signalling cascade,
  thereby inducing autophagy. This autophagic response plays a critical role in inhibiting
  persistent RSV infection in L. striatellus. The schematic was created using Figdraw
  software
