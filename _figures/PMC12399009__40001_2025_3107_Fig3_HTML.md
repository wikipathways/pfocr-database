  IL-1β and IL-6 through the NF-κB signaling pathway; HIF-1α binds to the IL-10 and
  IFN-γ promoters at the HRE to upregulate IL-10 and IFN-γ; Inhibition of IL-8 expression
  by HIF-1α is associated with downregulation of nuclear factor erythroid 2-related
  factor 2 (Nrf2) By activation of retinoid-related orphan nuclear receptor γt (RORγt),
  or in conjunction with RORγt, HIF-1α upregulates IL-17 through p300 recruitment
  and histone acetylation. List of abbreviations: HIF-1α: Hypoxia Inducible Factor-1,
