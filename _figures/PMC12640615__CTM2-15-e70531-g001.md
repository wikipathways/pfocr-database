caption: Treatment approaches targeting the cGAS–STING pathway in urological malignancies.
  Based on the dual regulatory role of the cGAS–STING pathway in urological malignancies,
  we have systematically summarised relevant therapeutic strategies. In prostate cancer,
  various carriers (such as RC@RMPs and LNPs) activate the cGAS–STING pathway, promote
  interferon release and subsequently activate CD8⁺ T cells and NK cells to enhance
  anti‐tumour immunity. In RCC, PARPi induces the cGAS–STING signalling via DNA damage,
  triggering an immune response. Additionally, 2‐BP inhibits palmitoylation of the
  STING protein, promotes the interaction between VDAC2 and GRP75, enhances calcium
  influx, and thereby induces tumour cell death. In BCa, metabolic intervention promotes
  the accumulation of the STING protein, activating its downstream signalling pathway.
  The inhibitor DCPIB suppresses the proportion of STING‐mediated SLC14A1⁺ CAFs, thereby
  inhibiting tumour progression
