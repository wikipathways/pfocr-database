caption: 'The mechanism by which alliin exerts its anti-aS effects. This diagram summarizes
  the molecular pathways through which alliin mitigates atherosclerosis: (1) PCSK9-LDLR
  pathway: alliin suppresses PCSK9 activity, preventing LDL receptor dissolution and
  lowering circulating LDL-cholesterol; (2) AMPK/SREBP-2 pathway: alliin activates
  AMPK, which regulates SREBP-2, leading to increased LDLR synthesis in the Golgi
  apparatus and enhanced cellular cholesterol uptake, thereby reducing plasma LDL-cholesterol
  levels; (3) AMPK/SREBP-1c pathway: alliin suppresses SREBP-1c expression, reducing
  lipid accumulation in hepatocytes induced by free fatty acids'
