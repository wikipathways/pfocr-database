caption: 'Regulation of VEGF expression by key oncogenic drivers in epithelial ovarian
  cancer (EOC). A Loss of TP53 function: In normal cells, wild-type p53 represses
  the VEGF promoter. In EOC, TP53 mutations or loss remove this repression, leading
  to VEGF overexpression and angiogenesis B Oncogenic pathway activation: Mutations
  in KRAS/BRAF or loss of PTEN hyperactivate the RAS/MAPK and PI3K/AKT pathways, which
  stabilize and enhance HIF-1α transcriptional activity. Stabilized HIF-1α binds the
  Hypoxia Response Element (HRE) in the VEGF promoter, driving VEGF expression even
  in normoxia Together, these genetic alterations converge on VEGF overexpression,
  establishing a potent and sustained pro-angiogenic program essential for EOC progression
  and metastasis'
