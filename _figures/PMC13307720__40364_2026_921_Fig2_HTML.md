caption: Upstream regulatory pathways of ZDHHC5. A. The transcriptional activation
  of ZDHHC5, resulting in its overexpression, is mediated by mutant p53, which forms
  a functional complex with NF-Y and directly binds to the ZDHHC5 promoter. B. Furthermore,
  src promotes ZDHHC5 transcription by interacting with the transcription factor p53
  and binding to its promoter region. C. Elevated miR-96-5p directly targets the 3’
  untranslated region (3‘UTR) of ZDHHC5 mRNA, thereby suppressing the expression of
  both ZDHHC5 transcripts and protein. D. ZDHHC5 forms a complex with PSD-95 and Fyn.
  Fyn phosphorylates ZDHHC5, which prevents the recruitment of the clathrin adaptor
  protein AP-2 and thereby blocks endocytosis. E. Fatty acid binding to CD36 activates
  the kinase LYN. Subsequently, LYN-mediated phosphorylation of ZDHHC5 inhibits its
  palmitoyltransferase activity, compromising its function in sustaining CD36 palmitoylation.
  F. Under conditions of enhanced synaptic activity, ZDHHC5 is sequentially phosphorylated
  by CDK5 and PLK2. This phosphorylation cascade triggers the degradation of ZDHHC5
  via the ubiquitin-proteasome pathway, thereby leading to a dynamic spatial reorganization
  of substrate palmitoylation. G. The palmitoyltransferase ZDHHC20 mediates the palmitoylation
  of ZDHHC5, which enhances its membrane association and promotes its interaction
  with the sodium pump. Furthermore, OGT catalyzes the O-GlcNAcylation of ZDHHC5,
  further strengthening this membrane binding. Collectively, these modifications facilitate
  the palmitoylation of PLM. H. TRIM7 competitively binds to ZDHHC5 and promotes its
  ubiquitination and proteasomal degradation. This process disrupts the ZDHHC5-dependent
  palmitoylation of P2 × 7, which impairs its membrane localization and copper efflux
  function
