caption: Mechanisms of ferroptosis regulation. The core regulatory network of ferroptosis
  involves a balance between driving forces and antioxidant defenses. The primary
  defense is the System Xc–GSH-GPX4 axis, which reduces toxic lipid hydroperoxides,
  with parallel pathways like FSP1-CoQ10 and GCH1-BH4 providing additional protection.
  Key drivers include intracellular iron, which catalyzes peroxidation via the Fenton
  reaction, and the enzymatic incorporation and oxidation of PUFAs into membrane phospholipids.
  The process culminates in the execution phase, where excessive lipid peroxide accumulation
  causes membrane damage and cell death, a fate that can be counteracted by membrane
  repair systems like ESCRT-III
