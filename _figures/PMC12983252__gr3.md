caption: 'Core metabolic network of the chloroplast.Carbon assimilation and the photosynthetic
  electron transport chain provide the primary energy (e.g., ATP) and carbon skeletons
  for metabolism. Key precursor molecules—including 3-phosphoglyceric acid (PGA),
  glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate (G3P), and erythrose-4-phosphate (E4P)—are channeled
  into three major biosynthetic pathways: fatty acid synthesis, the methylerythritol
  phosphate (MEP) pathway, and the shikimate pathway. The MEP pathway, in particular,
  supplies the essential precursors (IPP/DMAPP) for the heterologous synthesis of
  high-value products such as artemisinin, taxadiene, and astaxanthin. PEP, phosphoenolpyruvic
  acid; RuBP, ribulose-1,5-bisphosphate; IPP, isopentenyl pyrophosphate; DMAPP, dimethylallyl
  pyrophosphate; NADPH, nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide phosphate'
