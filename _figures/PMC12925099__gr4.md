caption: 'Glutamine metabolism involves anaplerosis (entry into the TCA cycle) and
  cataplerosis (removal as malate). Malate is converted to OAA and then to PEP via
  PEPCK. PEP turns into pyruvate, entering the TCA cycle as acetyl-CoA or converting
  to alanine. Glyceroneogenesis forms 3-Glycerol-P for triglyceride synthesis, balancing
  OAA entry and intermediate removal. DHAP is reduced to 3-Glycerol-P, providing the
  glycerol backbone for new triglycerides. Solid arrows indicate anaplerosis pathways;
  red arrows show glyceroneogenesis pathways; dotted arrow represents mitochondrial
  cataplerosis. 3FA: 3 Fatty acids; 3-Glycerol-P: l-glycerol-3-phosphate; CoA: Coenzyme
  A; DHAP: Dihydroxyacetone phosphate; GDP: Guanosine diphosphate; GTP: Guanosine
