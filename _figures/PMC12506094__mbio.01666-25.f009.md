caption: The mechanisms by which 3D proteins of picornaviruses target JAK1, impeding
  the activation of the JAK-STAT signaling cascade and facilitating viral replication.
  Upon SVA infection of host cells, the viral 3D protein interacts with JAK1 and recruits
  RNF125, promoting the K48-linked polyubiquitination of JAK1 at specific lysine residues
  (K205 and K249) and leading to its degradation. This results in the suppression
  of JAK-STAT signaling pathway activation, which counteracts the innate immune response
  and bolsters viral replication. EMCV-3D, EMCV-3D, and EV-71-3D also inhibit JAK-STAT
  signaling pathway activation for facilitating viral replication
