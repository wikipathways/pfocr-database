caption: 'Control points hexokinase, phosphofructokinase-1, and pyruvate kinase M2
  regulate flux while intermediates feed the pentose phosphate pathway, hexosamine
  synthesis, serine/glycine, and glycerol-3-phosphate for lipid production. Numerous
  bypasses (dashed arrows) generate pyruvate and lactate independently of pyruvate
  kinase M2. Note that while ADP can relieve some ATP inhibition, classically, AMP
  is the stronger allosteric activator of phosphofructokinase-1. The effect of hexosamines
  on phosphofructokinase-1 is non-allosteric and occurs via glycosylation. 1,3BPG
  1,3-bisphosphoglycerate, 3PG 3-phosphoglycerate, 2PG: 2-phosphoglycerate, ALDO aldolase,
  DHAP dihydroxyacetone phosphate, ENO enolase, FBP Fructose-1,6-bisphosphatase, Frc
  fructose, Frc-6-P fructose-6-phosphate, Frc-1,6-P fructose-1,6-bisphosphate, Frc-2,6-P
  fructose-2,6-bisphosphate, Gal galactose, G3P glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate, G6PC glucose-6-phosphatase,
  GAPDH glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate dehydrogenase, Glc-1-P glucose-1-phosphate, Glc-6-P
  glucose-6-phosphate, GLUTs glucose transporters, GK glycerol kinase, GPD glycerol-3-phosphate
  dehydrogenase, GSH glutathione, HK hexokinase, PEP phosphoenolpyruvate, PFK-1 phosphofructokinase-1,
  PFK-2 phosphofructokinase-2, PGI phosphoglucose isomerase, PGAM phosphoglycerate
  mutase, PGK phosphoglycerate kinase, PLs phospholipids, PKM2 pyruvate kinase M2,
  pyr pyruvate, TGs triacylglycerols. The enzyme triose isomerase is omitted from
  the figure to reduce clutter. Created in BioRender. Chinopoulos (2025) https://BioRender.com/i27v503'
