caption: Mechanisms of action of lncRNA inhibitors. siRNAs and shRNAs silence lncRNAs
  via the RNA interference pathway. Designed siRNAs or shRNAs with sequence complementarity
  to the target lncRNA are processed, and the guide strand is incorporated into the
  RNA-induced silencing complex (RISC). The RISC–guide strand complex binds to the
  complementary lncRNA, leading to transcript degradation and reduced expression,
  thereby inhibiting cancer cell proliferation. ASOs complementary to the target lncRNA,
  form DNA–RNA hybrids upon binding. These hybrids are recognized by RNase H, which
  cleaves the RNA strand and promotes degradation of the lncRNA, suppressing cancer
  growth. Small-molecule inhibitors also modulate oncogenic lncRNAs; For example-
  Niclosamide activates GSK3β leading to reduction in MALAT1 levels, AC1NOD4Q blocks
  HOTAIR–EZH2 interaction, destabilizing HOTAIR, and YK-4-279 directly downregulates
  HULC. Natural compounds such as curcumin suppress oncogenes like c-Myc, leading
  to decreased H19 expression and reduced cancer cell proliferation
