  RAS/MAPK, and JAK-STAT to promote tumor proliferation and survival. When a certain
  pathway is inhibited, HER3 can adopt bypass signaling to facilitate tumor growth,
  thereby inducing therapeutic resistance. This fully demonstrates the flexibility
  and complexity of the HER3 signaling network
