caption: 'MAs (including TMA/DMA/MMA) are imported into the cell via MA transporters.
  Intracellular MAs trigger activation of the transcription of oxidative demethylation
  pathway genes and secR1 gene expression through an unidentified sensor system. SecR1
  subsequently activates transcription of the serine cycle genes driven by Psga and
  Pppc promoters. Additionally, SecR1-mediated activation of Pppc enhances secR2 expression.
  SecR1 and SecR2 collectively form a regulatory pool (SecR pool) that co-activates
  Psga, Pppc and PsecR1. This cascade rapidly upregulates MA oxidation and serine
  cycle pathways, converting MAs into one-carbon units for biomass synthesis. Notably,
  SecR proteins are intrinsically unstable and undergo rapid degradation in the cytoplasm.
  Orange-red bold arrows: activation of transcription; Dashed lines: hypothetical
  components or reactions'
