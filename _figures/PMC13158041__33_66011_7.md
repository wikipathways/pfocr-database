caption: 'BAIBA, which is secreted from skeletal muscle during physical exercise,
  acts on vascular endothelial cells (currently, it is unknown whether MRGPRD functions
  as a receptor for BAIBA in these cells), upregulating the expression of PGC-1β and
  ERRα. Increased PGC-1β levels lead to enhanced phosphorylation of both AKT and AMPK,
  subsequently resulting in the phosphorylation and activation of eNOS. Furthermore,
  increased PGC-1β suppresses the TNFα-NFκB pathway, which leads to the reduced expression
  of various inflammation-related molecules (e.g., E-selectin and VCAM-1). This suppression
  may be partially attributed to inhibition of IKK activity via AMPK activation. Meanwhile,
  the increased PGC-1β and ERRα also synergistically boosted the levels of molecules
  involved in the NFκB degradation pathway, including PDLIM2 and COMMD1, which contribute
  to an anti-inflammatory effect.Abbreviations: AKT, protein kinase B; AMP-activated
  protein kinase; BAIBA, β-aminoisobutyric acid; COMMD1, Copper metabolism Murr1 domain-containing
  protein 1; eNOS, endothelial nitric oxide synthase; ERRα, estrogen-related receptor
  alpha; IKK, IκB kinase; MRGPRD, MAS-related G protein-coupled receptor, member D;
  NFκB, nuclear factor-kappa B; PDKIM2, PDZ and LIM domain protein; PGC-1β, peroxisome
  proliferator-activated receptor gamma coactivator 1-beta; TNFα, tumor necrosis factor
  alpha; VCAM-1, vascular cell adhesion molecule-1'
