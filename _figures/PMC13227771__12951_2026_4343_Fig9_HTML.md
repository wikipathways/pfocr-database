caption: Ultrasound-propelled erythrocyte-cloaked Pt(II)-LDH triggers cGAS-STING-driven
  immunogenic cell death to reinforce PD-1 inhibitor immunotherapy in triple-negative
  breast cancer. Under ultrasound irradiation, the RmPLH nanoplatform generates ROS
  and platinum ion-mediated DNA damage, inducing mitochondrial dysfunction and caspase-3/GSDME-dependent
  pyroptosis. Pyroptosis releases mtDNA and DAMPs, which activate the cGAS-STING pathway,
  enhancing type I interferon production. This coordinated activation promotes DC
  maturation, cytotoxic T-cell infiltration, and potentiates PD-1 checkpoint blockade
  therapy, converting “cold” triple-negative breast tumors into “hot” ones
