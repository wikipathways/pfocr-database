caption: Bidirectional crosstalk of the gut-lung axis in ALI/ARDS. This overview illustrates
  the primary pathways (immune, metabolic, neural, barrier) through which the gut
  and lungs communicate. dashed arrow indicates a process of migration. Gut dysbiosis
  promotes lung injury via microbial metabolites, bacterial translocation, and immune
  cell activation. In turn, systemic inflammation and cytokine release impair gut
  barrier integrity, increasing permeability and fueling a feedback loop of inflammation.
  Lymphatic transport of immune factors further contributes to lung injury. Thus,
  maintaining gut microbiota balance is essential for protecting lung function
