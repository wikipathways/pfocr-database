caption: 'Mitochondrial dynamics participate in regulating PH disease in a spatiotemporal
  manner. In the early stage of PH (PAH/HPH), SENP1 nuclear translocation promotes
  calcium mitochondrial efflux, leading to stabilization of lung endothelial cell
  function, and promotes the interaction between FIS1 and MFN2, inhibits DRP1 expression,
  promotes mitochondrial fusion, facilitates ATP production, inhibits ROS production,
  alleviates OS, inhibits apoptosis of lung epithelial cells, and mitigates the development
  of PH (PAH/HPH). In the late stage of PH(PAH/HPH), activation of DRP1 is promoted
  while MFN2 expression is inhibited, leading to an increase in mitochondrial fission.
  Migration of lung endothelial cells was promoted by promoting the expression of
  PCNA and cell cycle protein A/B1 and inducing abnormal fatty acid metabolism; inflammation
  was promoted by promoting ROS production and increasing the expression of NLRP3;
  and proliferation and migration of PASMCs was promoted by promoting the activation
  of MTOP, S6, and the PDGF-RF-1-ERK1/2 pathway. Together, these factors may exacerbate
  PH (PAH/HPH) deterioration. ATP: Adenosine triphosphate; DRP1: Dynamin-related protein
  1; FIS1: Fission protein 1; FUNDC1: FUN14 domain-containing protein 1; MFN2: Mitofusin2;
  NLRP3: Nod-like receptor family pyrin domain-containing; PASMCs: Pulmonary vascular
  smooth muscle cells; PAECs: Pulmonary arterial endothelial cells; PH: Pulmonary
  hypertension; PCNA: Proliferating cell nuclear antigen; PGC-1a: Peroxisome proliIerators-activated
  receptor γ coactivator lalpha; ROS: Reactive oxygen species; SENP1: SUMO specific
  peptidase 1'
