caption: 'Mechanisms of action of MSCs in endothelial cells in diabetic retinopathy:
  MSC can reduce inflammatory responses by inhibiting neuronal precursor cell-expressed
  developmentally downregulated 4 activation of the PTEN/AKT signaling pathway; VEGF
  promotes the survival of pancreatic islet microvascular endothelial cells through
  activation of the PI-3 K/AKT/m-TOR/eNOS and p38/MAPK signaling pathways; MSCs attenuate
  oxidative stress and apoptosis in endothelial cells through the Sirt1/AMPK/PGC-1α
  pathway; MSC-exo reduced hyperglycemia-induced retinal damage and decreased hyperglycemia-induced
  retinal apoptosis, inflammation, and angiogenesis by activating the HIF-1α/EZH2/PGC-1α
  signaling pathway via miR-5068 and miR-10228; MSC JAK/STAT signaling pathway induced
  phosphorylation of the Nrf2, which resulted in the activation of the Keap1-Nrf2-ARE
  signaling pathway to activate can resist oxidative stress and inflammatory response;
  MSC-Exos activates STAT5 signaling pathway through miR-222, which can significantly
  improve bFgF and IL-3, and inhibit neovascularization; MSC-Exos attenuates inflammation
  by activating P65 through the signaling pathway of NF-κB; MSC activates P65 through
  the MAP3K1/MAPKK/MAPKK/SIR pathway, thereby inhibiting NF-κB p65 phosphorylation
  to attenuate the inflammatory response in DR; MSC-exos, through miR-22-3p, can activate
  the signaling pathway of NF-κB and reduce the levels of inflammatory factors such
  as NLRP3, ASC, caspase-1, and IL-1β in retina'
