caption: IAV infection results in the production of Z-RNA, which is sensed by ZBP1.
  Once activated by binding Z-RNA, ZBP1 interacts with RIPK3 via RHIMs found in both
  proteins. RIPK3 can then induce either apoptosis via RIPK1-Fas-associated protein
  with death domain (FADD)-caspase-8 signaling, or necroptosis via mixed lineage kinase
  domain-like protein (MLKL). ZBP1-RIPK3 signaling can also activate the NLRP3 inflammasome
  to induce pyroptosis, via caspase-1 cleavage and activation of GSDMD. IAV infection
  can also induce intrinsic apoptosis via BAX/BAK/caspase-9 activation. Both PB1-F2
  and NS1 proteins have been implicated in mediating mitochondrial cytochrome c release
  to trigger this pathway. Additionally, IAV infection can result in the initiation
  of extrinsic apoptosis via Fas ligand (FasL) and TRAIL-driven death receptor activation
  in lung cells. Created with BioRender.com
