caption: CD5+ B cells’ function in immunomodulation and tumour growth. IL-6 binds
  to CD5 in CD5+ B cells, activating gp130 and Jak2, which triggers the activation
  of STAT3, which raises CD5 expression. Additionally, BTG2 facilitates the activation
  of the STAT3 signalling pathway. Therefore, by inhibiting BTG2 expression, cellular
  CD5 expression may be increased through STAT3 activation. Recombinant granzyme B
  (GzmB), which has immunomodulatory effects, is released when IL-21, secreted by
  follicular helper T cells (Tfh) and invariant natural killer T cells (iNKT cells),
  interacts with IL-21R on the surface of CD5+ B cells
