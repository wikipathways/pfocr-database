caption: Central role of System Xc⁻/GSH and amino acid-mediated ferroptosis regulation.
  System Xc⁻, a cystine/glutamate antiporter formed by SLC7A11 and SLC3A2, imports
  extracellular cystine in exchange for intracellular glutamate. Once inside the cell,
  cystine is rapidly reduced to cysteine (Cys), the rate-limiting precursor for glutathione
  (GSH) synthesis. GSH serves as a major intracellular antioxidant and an essential
  cofactor for glutathione peroxidase 4 (GPX4), facilitating the reduction of lipid
  hydroperoxides and preventing ferroptosis. In addition to GSH synthesis, Cys can
  be generated from methionine via the transsulfuration pathway and further used to
  produce coenzyme A (CoA) and hydrogen sulfide, which regulate mitochondrial metabolism
  and redox homeostasis. GPX4 is a selenoprotein that requires selenocysteine (Sec)
  incorporation during translation, a process dependent on isopentenyl pyrophosphate
  from the mevalonate (MVA) pathway. Meanwhile, glutamine is taken up by specific
  transporters and transported into mitochondria, where glutaminase (GLS) converts
  it to glutamate and α-ketoglutarate (α-KG). α-KG fuels the tricarboxylic acid (TCA)
  cycle and supports lipid homeostasis, thereby modulating ferroptosis sensitivity
