caption: Mechanisms of mtROS-induced damage in periodontitis and CVD. mtROS produced
  by mitochondrial electron transport promote mtDNA damage and mPTP opening, causing
  calcium overload and dysfunction (50). Released mtDNA activates inflammatory pathways,
  increasing cytokines such as IL-1β, IL-6, and TNF-α, which further amplify mtROS
  generation (51). In periodontitis (left), pathogens increase oxidative stress, impair
  hPDLCs function, and activate NF-κB, thereby stimulating bone loss (46). In CVD
  (right), factors like TLR4 activation induce ROS, oxidize LDL, and promote foam
  cell formation in early atherosclerosis (52). (Created in BioRender)
