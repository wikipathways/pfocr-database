caption: Mechanistic Model of HIF2α-Mediated GBM Malignancy. The model illustrates
  how HIF2α, under hypoxic conditions, upregulates DNMT1, which leads to miR-652-5p
  promoter hypermethylation and subsequent inhibition of miR-652-5p expression. Reduced
  miR-652-5p levels increase SDC1 and TGFβ2 expression, promoting MMP9 activity and
  sSDC1 generation. TGFβ2 is secreted via exosomes under the control of SDC1 and binds
  to extracellular matrix components. SDC1 and TGFβ2 interact, possibly through the
  HS chains of SDC1, to activate pERBB4 and the PI3K-AKT pathway, driving GBM cell
  proliferation, migration, and invasion through autocrine and paracrine signalling
  mechanisms
