caption: Prenols and prenoic acids metabolism in mammals. The figure illustrates the
  mevalonate (MVA) pathway and the synthesis of polyprenyl diphosphates (both shown
  in brown arrows), together with the metabolic routes involved in prenoic acid biosynthesis
  (green), prenol/isoprenoid aldehyde salvage (purple), and prenylated protein degradation
  (blue). Enzymes and enzymatic processes are represented in boxes, whereas metabolites
  are shown in regular font. Line-ending arrows indicate regulatory steps within the
  pathway, and red arrows denote enzymatic inhibition by drugs or metabolites. The
  symbol “?” represents steps catalyzed by unknown enzymes. Yellow numbers and their
  corresponding legends (below) indicate biological processes in which specific enzymes
  participate, although they are not directly related to prenol or prenoic acid metabolism.
  Dashed arrows indicate multiple enzymatic steps, whereas solid arrows represent
  single enzymatic reactions. ADH1A, alcohol dehydrogenase 1A; AKR1B10, aldo-keto
  reductase family 1 member B10; AKR1C3, aldo-keto reductase family 1 member C3; CYP3A4,
  cytochrome P450 3A4; FPP, farnesyl diphosphate; FPPS, farnesyl diphosphate synthase;
  GGPP, geranylgeranyl diphosphate; GGPPS, geranylgeranyl diphosphate synthase; GPP,
  geranyl diphosphate; MAO-B, monoamine oxidase B; PDP1/PPAPDC2, pyrophosphatase/phosphatase
  domain-containing protein 2; PolK, polyprenol kinase; PolPK, polyprenyl phosphate
