caption: 'The schematic diagram shows the effect of the Piezo1 channel on pre-sympathetic
  PVN neurons in the PVN-RVLM-IML pathway. Under normal physiological conditions,
  the Piezo1 channel mediates the passage of cations including Ca2+. Microinjection
  of Dooku1 or GsMTx4 into PVN significantly increased sympathetic nerve activity
  (SNA), which, in turn, affected arterial blood pressure (ABP). This may be related
  to the calcium activation channels such as SK and BK. Green channel: Piezo1 (open).
  Green channel with red dot: Piezo1 (closed). Red dot: Represents the Piezo1 channel
  (closed state) blocked by Dooku1 or GsMTx4. Blue circle: SK channel. Orange circle:
  BK channel. Solid black arrows: Direction of cation influx. Question marks: Proposed
  downstream signaling pathways. Connecting lines and arrows: Represent the projection
  pathway from PVN to RVLM, illustrating the structure of the PVN-RVLM pathway. [Color
  figure created in BioRender. A, R. https://BioRender.com/vjjoxqv (accessed on 23
  October 2025)]'
