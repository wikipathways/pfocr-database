caption: 'Evolutionary convergence of circadian-aging pathways. Schematic representation
  of the main interconnected pathways linking aging and the circadian system in long-lived
  species. In pink, the naked mole-rat exhibits a robust insulin/glucose signaling
  pathway, downregulated mTORC1 and upregulated mTORC2 pathways, and a strong circadian
  system. In blue, cavefish display enhanced DNA repair activity and dampened circadian
  rhythms due to constant external conditions. However, their circadian system remains
  highly plastic, likely due to an enhanced sensory system. In yellow, whales exhibit
  increased DNA repair capacity, robust PI3K/AKT and insulin/IGF-1 signaling pathways,
  and high behavioral plasticity associated with rapid Bmal1 evolution. The central
  gear symbolizes conserved mechanisms potentially involved in lifespan extension
  and regulated by the circadian system: insulin/IGF-1 signaling, glucose metabolism,
  and mTOR pathway modulation'
