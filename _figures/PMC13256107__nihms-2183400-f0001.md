caption: Schematic representation of central metabolism and its branch pathways. G6P,
  glucose-6-phosphate; PEP, phosphoenolpyruvate. This figure provides an overview
  of the glycolytic and tricarboxylic acid (TCA) cycles, along with key branch pathways
  relevant to trehalose and 4-hydroxycoumarin (4HC) biosynthesis. It highlights the
  positions of intermediates such as glucose, glucose-6-phosphate (G6P), phosphoenolpyruvate
  (PEP), and pyruvate, illustrating how carbon flux is distributed through various
  biosynthetic and energy-generating routes. UDP-sugar and shikimate pathways are
  outlined to contextualize the carbon entry points for trehalose and 4HC production,
  respectively. The diagram serves to emphasize the importance of controlling carbon
  distribution at central metabolic nodes to optimize the biosynthesis of target compounds
  through engineered regulation strategies
