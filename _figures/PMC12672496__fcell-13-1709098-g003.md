  and therapeutic targeting. This schematic integrates established and emerging concepts,
  illustrating how three GPCR subfamilies-orphan receptors, chemokine receptors, and
  hormone-responsive receptors-orchestrate prostate cancer progression. The diagram

  the androgen receptor (AR) axis, extensive remodeling of the tumor microenvironment
