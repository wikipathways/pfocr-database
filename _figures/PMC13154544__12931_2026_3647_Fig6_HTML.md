caption: Schematic diagram summarizing the findings. Under hypoxic conditions, circATXN2
  expression is upregulated in PASMCs. circATXN2, which is formed by the back-splicing
  of ATXN2 exons, acts as a ceRNA to sponge miR-138-5p. This sequestration of miR-138-5p
  relieves its suppression of SMURF1 mRNA, leading to increased SMURF1 protein expression.
  Elevated SMURF1, in turn, promotes the degradation of R-SMADs, thereby impairing
  the anti-proliferative BMP signaling pathway. The disruption of this pathway results
  in enhanced PASMC proliferation and migration, which contributes to pulmonary vascular
  remodeling and the pathogenesis of PAH
