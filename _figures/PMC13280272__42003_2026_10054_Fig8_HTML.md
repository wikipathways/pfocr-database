  recruitment of SHOC2 scaffold proteins, resulting in differential BRAF activation
  between heat-sensitive C. gigas and heat-tolerant C. angulata. The divergent signaling
  subsequently causes species-specific phosphorylation of ERK1/2 at T187 and Y189

  an energy supply critical for survival under thermal stress. The figure was created
