  in regulating bladder urothelial carcinoma drug resistance this model proposes that
  in bladder urothelial carcinoma, the key glycolytic components PKM, lactate, and
  HK function as core regulatory nodes of tumor aerobic glycolysis, synergistically
  governing five major resistance mechanisms through interconnected pathways. It illustrates

  mechanisms, regulation of epigenetic changes, induction of tumor immune evasion,
  activation of resistance-promoting downstream mechanisms and increased drug efflux.
