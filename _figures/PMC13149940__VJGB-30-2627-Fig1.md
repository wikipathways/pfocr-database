caption: 'The ETC components in the inner mitochondrial membrane are shown: from the
  ubiquinone pool (UQ/UQH2) electrons are transferred via the alternative pathway
  (AP) catalyzed by alternative oxidase (AOX) and via the cytochrome pathway through
  complex III, cytochrome c, and complex IV. GGP denotes GDP-L-galactose phosphorylase.
  L-galactono-1,4-lactone (GL), synthesized through the L-galactose pathway and transported
  from the cytosol into the mitochondrial intermembrane space (IMS), is oxidized to
  ascorbate by L-galactono-1,4-lactone dehydrogenase (GLDG), which transfers electrons
  to cytochrome c. Electron transport along the ETC is coupled with proton pumping
  into the intermembrane space and the “return” of protons into the matrix via ATP
  synthase, driving ATP production'
