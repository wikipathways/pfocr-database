caption: Model for 4456dh activity in fungal cells. Trehalose biosynthesis in Cryptococcus
  and Candida is initiated by Tps1, which converts G6P and UDPG into T6P. In the second
  step of the trehalose biosynthesis pathway, T6P is dephosphorylated by Tps2, and
  trehalose is generated. As shown, Tps1 from C. neoformans forms a homotetramer (31).
  4456dh binds the exposed substrate-binding pockets of Tps1. Additionally, 4456dh
  may have an additional target that is likely another glycosyltransferase in some
  fungal organisms
