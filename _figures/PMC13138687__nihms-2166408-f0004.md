caption: Macrophage-specific Hippo-YAP signaling. Activation of YAP/TAZ in macrophages
  occurs in response to several pro-inflammatory factors (e.g. LPS, IL-1β) and mechanical
  force, whereas resolving molecules (e.g. IL-4, IL-13) inhibit YAP/TAZ function.
  YAP/TAZ-TEAD stimulate inflammatory and fibrotic responses, and polarize macrophages
  toward an inflammatory phenotype through increased expression of pro-inflammatory
  cytokines and chemokines. YAP/TAZ also inhibit expression of resolving genes through
  HDAC3-NCor1-mediated transcriptional repression. Hippo pathway activation (i.e.
  RASSF1A-MST1) reduces macrophage-mediated cardiac inflammation through YAP/TAZ inhibition,
  as well as inhibitory phosphorylation of 5-LOX and attenuated production of LTB4.
