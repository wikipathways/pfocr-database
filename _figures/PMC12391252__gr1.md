caption: Mechanism of taurine regulation of bone formationThe image illustrates the
  interaction of several key pathways in cell signaling and their mechanisms of action
  in bone metabolism. The left side of the figure illustrates the pathways through
  which MSCs and hematopoietic stem cells (HSCs) differentiate to become osteoblasts
  and osteoclasts, respectively. The middle section provides a detailed exposition
  of the roles of the Wnt signaling pathway and β-catenin in osteoblast differentiation.
  This includes the activation of ERK1/2 and β-catenin upon binding of the Wnt ligand
  to the receptor. In turn, this regulates gene transcription. The right side of the
  figure demonstrates the roles of the RANKL/RANK pathway and NF-κB in osteoblast
  differentiation. This includes the activation of the NF-κB signaling pathway upon
  binding of RANKL to RANK, which in turn promotes osteoblast differentiation
