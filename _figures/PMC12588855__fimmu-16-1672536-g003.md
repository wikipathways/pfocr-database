caption: The role of CaSR-mediated autophagy in cardiac remodeling. The activation
  of CaSR promotes the autophagy process in cardiac fibroblasts, and autophagy further
  aggravates myocardial fibrosis. AngII can enhance the expression of CaSR and, by
  activating the Raf/MEK/ERK pathway, upregulates autophagy-related proteins (such
  as LC3B, P62). After MI, CaSR in macrophages further drives autophagy and activation
  of the inflammatory body through inhibiting the CaMKKβ-AMPK-mTOR pathway, thereby
  exacerbating ventricular remodeling. Calhex 231, as an antagonist of CaSR, can inhibit
  autophagy and alleviate inflammation, fibrosis, and hypertrophy
