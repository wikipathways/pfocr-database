caption: A schematic description of the interaction between ncRNAs and signaling cascades
  in hemangioma (HA). The dysregulation of miRNAs, lncRNAs, and circRNAs may play
  a significant role in the development and progression of HA. Several miRNAs, including
  miR-382, miR-205, miR-15a, miR-200c-3p, miR-203a-3p, miR-143, miR-199a-5p, miR-4458,
  miR-139-5p, and miR-556-3p via targeting PTEN, AKT, Notch, VEGFA, Bcl-2, HIF-1α,
  IGF-1R, and VEGFC could regulate cell proliferation, invasion, migration, angiogenesis,
  and apoptosis of endothelial cells, thus affecting the course of HA. Moreover, miR-424,
  by targeting AKT, ERK1/2, VEGFA, VEGFR-2, and FGFR1, acts as a pivotal miRNA in
  the progression of HA through regulating critical cellular processes such as cell
  growth, angiogenesis, viability, and apoptosis. On the other hand, lncRNA MALAT1,
  via directly sponging miR-424 on its target Mitogen-activated protein kinase kinase
  kinase 3 (MEKK3), regulates the IKK/NF-kB signaling pathway and promotes cell proliferation,
  viability, migration, invasion, and tube formation ability of hemangioma endothelial
  cells while increasing their apoptosis. Moreover, LINC00152 could promote cell proliferation
  and reduce apoptosis by inducing AKT/mTOR and the Notch1 axis. Additionally, Circ-0000915
  could reduce propranolol sensitivity by transcriptionally inducing the IL6/STAT3
  pathway and upregulating RNF187 by sponging miR-890, thus leading to propranolol
  resistance in hemangioma endothelial cells. The blue arrow indicates downregulation,
  while the red arrow indicates upregulation
