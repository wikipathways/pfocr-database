caption: Schematic summary. Aging triggers the activation of the PERK signaling pathway
  in lacrimal gland acinar cells, leading to endoplasmic reticulum stress (ERS), characterized
  by elevated BiP and CHOP expression. Sustained ERS subsequently promotes ferroptosis,
  evidenced by lipid peroxidation, iron accumulation, downregulation of GPX4 and SLC7A11,
  and characteristic mitochondrial ultrastructural abnormalities. Ferroptosis further
  contributes to cellular senescence and impaired lacrimal gland function. A reciprocal
  feed-forward loop between ERS and lipid accumulation amplifies the pathological
  cascade. Importantly, the PPARγ agonist Rosiglitazone (Rosi) protects against age-related
  damage by enhancing fatty acid oxidation (FAO) via upregulation of ACOX1 and CPT1A,
  thereby reducing lipid accumulation and alleviating both ERS and ferroptosis
