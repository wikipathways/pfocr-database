caption: 'A) Construction of PEA nanozymes from EGCG and L‐arginine (L‐Arg) via oxidative
  polymerization, Schiff‐base condensation, and Michael addition. Nitrogen atoms from
  L‐Arg optimize PEA‐catalyzed oxygen reduction: first, markedly increasing the abundance
  of semiquinone radicals; then, providing N‐centered active sites that promote efficient
  O2 adsorption and activation, thereby enhancing the precision and efficiency of
  semiquinone‐mediated electron/proton transfer; and finally strengthening the ubiquinone‐like/SOD‐like
  cascade catalysis to release dose‐controllable H2O2 in an N‐dependent manner. B)
  Intradermal delivery of PEA to telogen‐phase alopecic mice using microneedles enable
  in situ H2O2 generation within cells of the hair‐follicle microenvironment, inducing
  oxidative eustress, activating the Wnt/β‐catenin pathway and down‐regulate the Calcium
  signaling pathways, stimulating the release of factors such as Shh and removing
  follicular quiescence restriction, which together accelerate the transition from
  telogen to anagen[ 25, 26 ] and promote hair regeneration'
