caption: Mechanistic pathway linking gut dysbiosis to atrial fibrillation. Gut dysbiosis
  leads to increased levels of proarrhythmic metabolites (TMAO, LPS, indoxyl sulfate),
  which activate the NLRP3 inflammasome and stimulate proinflammatory cytokines (IL-1β,
  IL-6, TNF-α). These mediators promote structural remodeling via atrial fibrosis
  and electrical remodeling via connexin downregulation and RyR2 phosphorylation,
  together enhancing susceptibility to atrial fibrillation. α-SMA = alpha smooth muscle
  actin; IL-1β = interleukin-1β; IL-6 = interleukin-6; RyR2 = ryanodine receptor 2;
  TMAO = trimethylamine N-oxide; TNF-α = tumor necrosis factor α
