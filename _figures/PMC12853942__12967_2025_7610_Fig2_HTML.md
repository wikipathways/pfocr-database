caption: Schematic illustration of diverse lysosome mediated and autophagy lysosome
  pathway dependent targeted protein degradation strategies. The diagram depicts that
  LYTACs recruit LTRs at the cell membrane to form ternary complexes with POI, which
  then undergo mesh protein mediated endocytosis, traffic to endosomes, and are ultimately
  delivered to lysosomes for degradation. The AbTACs mediate the formation of EGFR-AbTAC-POI
  ternary complexes, these complexes are internalized into endosomes and subsequently
  directed to lysosomes. Additionally, a pathway involving K63-linked polyubiquitination
  of POI allows recognition by autophagy receptors, triggering autophagosome formation
  and subsequent delivery to lysosomes for degradation
