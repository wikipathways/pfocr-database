caption: α9‐nAChR signal transduction pathways in breast cancer cells. Various signaling
  pathways are activated by α9 nAChR in different breast cancer cell lines, contributing
  to cancer progression. These pathways include activation of the EGFR/ERK1/2 pathway,
  leading to cell cycle progression via Src, and Akt‐mediated up‐regulation of Bcl‐2
  to enhance cell survival. Furthermore, α9‐nAChR activation triggers MAP kinase p38
  pathways, involving PKC‐mediated NF‐kB activation, and promotes STAT3 phosphorylation,
  facilitating its nuclear translocation and increasing transcription of the anti‐apoptotic
  lectin Gal‐3 gene. Created with BioRender.com
