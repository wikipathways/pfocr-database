caption: 'Environmental pathogens like Burkholderia thailandensis (Bt; blue) and Chromobacterium
  violaceum (Cv; purple) are successfully detected, triggering pyroptosis. Host-adapted
  pathogens like Shigella flexneri (Sf; red), Salmonella enterica serovar Typhimurium
  (ST; orange), and Mycobacterium tuberculosis (Mtb; brown) evade pyroptosis. Cytosol-invasive
  bacteria like Bt and Sf expose LPS to the cytosol, activating CASP4/5/11. However,
  Sf inhibits this pathway using several translocated effectors: OspC3 to inhibit
  the caspases, while IpaH9.8 to inhibit guanylate binding proteins that may be upstream
  of LPS sensing. The NAIP/NLRC4 inflammasome detects type III secretion systems successfully
  during infection by environmental pathogens Bt and Cv, and after oral (but not systemic)
  infection by ST, detecting the ST SPI1 T3SS. ST evades NLRC4 during systemic infection
  by using its SPI2 T3SS. Similarly, Sf evades NLRC4 detection using OspF. Sf also
  inhibits GSDMD using IpaH7.8, which also attacks GSDMB (not shown). Mtb also evades
  the NLRP3 inflammasome using mechanisms that require PknF and Zmp1, however other
  publications indicate its T7SS and EST12 lead to detection by NLRP3. Mtb also inhibits
  the GSDMD pore using PtpB. Sf and Cv also inhibit the TNF signaling pathway using
  OspC1 and CopC, respectively, to attack apoptotic caspases'
