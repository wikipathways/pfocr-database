caption: Mechanistic diagram of shared roles of CCR1 and CCL19 in COPD and H. pylori
  infection. In COPD, CCR1 activates chemokine signaling via Gi/o-type G proteins
  and mediates inflammation through the JAK/STAT3/NF-κB pathway. In H. pylori infection,
  CCR1 binds its ligand CCL3 to activate the JAK/STAT pathway, promoting pro-inflammatory
  cytokine secretion and exacerbating tissue damage. CCL19 recruits and directs CCR7-expressing
  inflammatory cells, facilitating their migration into the lungs in COPD, while in
  H. pylori infection, it regulates dendritic cell–mediated inflammatory recruitment.
  Together, CCR1 and CCL19 drive pathological progression in both diseases
