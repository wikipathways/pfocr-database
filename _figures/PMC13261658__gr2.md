caption: RLKs and PUBs function in plant immunity.(A) The OsSDS2–OsSPL11 feedback
  loop orchestrates RLCK-dependent ROS biosynthesis during rice immune responses.
  OsSDS2 promotes immunity by phosphorylating OsRLCK118, thereby activating RbohB-dependent
  ROS production. In a feedback loop, OsSDS2-mediated phosphorylation of OsSPL11 enables
  OsSPL11 to ubiquitinate OsSDS2, marking it for 26S proteasomal degradation.(B) AtBAK1-mediated
  phosphorylation of AtPUB12/13 drives AtFLS2 ubiquitination and immune attenuation.
  AtBAK1 phosphorylates AtPUB12/13, enhancing their interaction with AtFLS2 and promoting
  its ubiquitination and degradation, thereby preventing immune overactivation.(C)
  AtCERK1 phosphorylation balances PUB-dependent immune activation and attenuation.
  AtCERK1 phosphorylates AtPUB4 to promote immune responses, whereas AtPUB12 interacts
  with AtCERK1 to negatively regulate signaling intensity.(D) The OsCERK1–OsCIE1 feedback
  circuit maintains immune homeostasis in rice (Oryza sativa). Under homeostasis,
  OsCIE1 prevents autoimmunity by ubiquitinating OsCERK1. Upon chitin perception,
  activated OsCERK1 phosphorylates and inhibits OsCIE1, leading to OsRLCK185-mediated
  immune signaling and OsPUB20-mediated activation of the jasmonic acid (JA) pathway,
  thereby enhancing disease resistance.(E) AtPUB13-dependent ubiquitination of AtLYK5
  ensures chitin signaling homeostasis. In the absence of chitin, AtPUB13 targets
  AtLYK5 for proteasomal degradation, suppressing immune signaling. Upon chitin perception,
  dissociation of AtPUB13 stabilizes AtLYK5, enabling CERK1-dependent immune responses.(F)
  SlPUB12/13-mediated ubiquitination regulates PSKR1-dependent immune signaling. Under
  basal conditions, SlPUB12/13 constitutively target SlPSKR1 for proteasomal degradation,
  suppressing basal defense. PSK perception disrupts this interaction, leading to
  SlPSKR1 stabilization and enhanced defense activation against Botrytis cinerea.(G)
  OsPID2 coordinates dual PUB-mediated E3 ligase pathways to regulate blast resistance
  in rice. OsPIE3 suppresses immunity by ubiquitinating and degrading OsPID2, whereas
  OsPID2 activates OsPUB15 to promote ROS production and defense gene expression against
  Magnaporthe oryzae.(H) AtPUB2/4-mediated scaffolding regulates PTI signaling through
  coordination of the AtFLS2 complex. AtPUB2/4 scaffold AtFLS2 with AtBIK1 and AtRbohD
  to enhance ROS production and the activation of defense genes. In the absence of
  PAMPs, PUB4/25/26 degrade non-activated AtBIK1. PAMP perception suppresses PUB25/26
  activity, whereas PUB4 promotes the accumulation of activated BIK1 and thereby enhances
  downstream immune signaling.(I) Coordinated regulation of plant immunity. PUB E3
  ligases act both as scaffolds for RLKs and as regulators of CRCK3 stability. The
  AtPUB22/23/24 triplet attenuates PTI responses, whereas AtPUB5 and AtPUB44 antagonistically
  regulate CRCK3 protein homeostasis to modulate autoimmunity. A. brassicicola, Alternaria
