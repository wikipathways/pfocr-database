caption: 'Mechanisms underlying the development of subretinal neovascularization in
  VLDLR knockout mice.VLDLR deficiency results in reduced fatty acid uptake and activates
  Ffar-1, which inhibits cellular glucose uptake via GLUT1. Dual fuel deficiency leads
  to reduced synthesis of the key energy metabolism molecule α-ketoglutarate, which
  fails to activate PHD with oxygen, the enzyme that tags Hif1a for degradation by
  proline hydroxylation. This results in the stabilization of Hif1a and ultimately
  leads to increased levels of VEGFA. WNT pathway under ADAM 17 and PMA can accelerate
  the shedding of VLDLR into soluble fragments, which helps maintain the inhibition
  of the WNT pathway. In contrast, TAPI -1 and anoxia conditions can inhibit this
  shedding process, potentially leading to WNT pathway activation. VLDLR deficiency
  leads to the loss of inhibitory effects of soluble VLDLR fragments on the WNT pathway,
  which results in WNT pathway overactivation and subsequent upregulation of fibrotic
  factors such as CTGF. Ffar-1: Free Fatty Acid Receptor 1; GLUT1: Glucose Transporter
  1; PHD: prolyl-hydroxylase dehydrogenase; VEGFA: Vascular Endothelial Growth Factor
  A, CTGF: Connective Tissue Growth Factor; ADAM 17: A disintegrin and metalloprotease
  17; PMA: phorbol 12-myristate 13-acetate, TAPI -1: tumor necrosis factor alpha protease
  inhibitor 1'
