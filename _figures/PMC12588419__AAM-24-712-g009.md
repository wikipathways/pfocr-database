caption: Proposed mechanism of red ginger extract (RGE) in acetaminophen (APAP) cell
  induction. The cytochrome P450 (CYP450) is the gate where APAP enters the human.
  The human body converts APAP to a toxic substance known as N-acetyl-p-benzoquinone
  imine (NAPQI) by the P4502E1 enzyme. During a drug overdose, CYP450 enzymes convert
  more APAP into NAPQI, causing liver impairment by processes such as mitochondrial
  (MC) damage, oxidative stress, and reactive oxidative stress. The JNK pathway could
  translocate into mitochondria (MC) and nucleus. In MC, it binds with Sab protein
  and activates the Cytochrome C (CyC). On the other hand, in nucleus it produces
  pro-caspase-9 (Casp-9), then it is cleaved into active Casp-9 by CyC from MC. Then
  Casp-9 activated the Casp-3 resulting in apoptosis. APAP = Acetaminophen, NAPQI
  = N-acetyl-p-benzoquinone imine, ROS = Reactive oxygen species, IL = Interleukin,
  LDH = Lactate dehydrogenase
