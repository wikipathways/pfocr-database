caption: oHSV combination with chemotherapy for solid tumors(A) oHSV therapy targets
  multiple components of DNA damage response (DDR) and thus synergizes with poly (ADP-ribose)
  polymerase (PARP) inhibitors by sensitizing cancer cells to PARP inhibition. (B)
  Gemcitabine, an antimetabolite agent, in addition to interfering with DNA synthesis,
  reduces the number of MDSCs in the tumor, enhancing virus-induced killing. (C) Topoisomerase
  inhibitors induce DDRs that result in enhanced virus replication and cytotoxicity.
  (D) Platinum-based agents, including cisplatin, inhibit DNA synthesis through the
  generation of DNA adducts. The resulting DNA damage leads to the activation of cGAS-STING,
  which in turn enhances antigen presentation, augmenting the antitumor immune response.
  oHSV synergizes with cisplatin therapy by modulating the exosomal pathway, therefore
  preventing drug efflux. (E) Alkylating agents such as cyclophosphamide (CPA) synergize
  with oHSV therapy by preventing immune cell infiltration, allowing for enhanced
  virus replication and spread. (F) HDAC inhibitors synergize with oHSVs by inhibiting
  antiviral responses, including interferon, therefore allowing for enhanced viral
  replication and efficacy
