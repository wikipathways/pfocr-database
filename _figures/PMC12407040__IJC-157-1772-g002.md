caption: The significance of CD86 expression in the survival of myeloma cells. In
  a significant proportion of cases of multiple myeloma, the expression of CD86, along
  with CD28, has been observed on the surface of malignant plasma cells. They enhance
  myeloma cell survival via activation of the NF‐kB signaling pathway and play a role
  in increasing the expression of surface integrin (e.g., ITGβ1‐CD29). Moreover, the
  impact of these molecules extends to the myeloma microenvironment, where they regulate
  T cells through the release of soluble CD86 (sCD86), reducing their anti‐tumor and
  increasing their tumor‐promoting effects. VLA‐4, very late activation antigen 4;
  IRF4, interferon regulatory factor 4; sCD86, soluble isoform of CD86
