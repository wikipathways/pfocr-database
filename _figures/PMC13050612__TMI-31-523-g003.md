caption: Interconnected mechanisms in the tuberculosis‐diabetes mellitus, glycated
  haemoglobin pathology. Pathophysiological effects of diabetes mellitus and persistent
  high blood glucose (HBG) to tuberculosis pathology. Persistent HBG can (1) increased
  the release of AGE and RAGE, which leads to the activation of the polyol pathway;
  (2) reduced intake of NADPH amidst increased release of reactive oxygen species;
  (3) increased sorbitol activity, activation of NF‐kB, reduced NO signalling (4)
  leading to increased protein glycation and oxidation; The consequences of these
  events are impairment of the biological metabolome leading to compromised immunity,
  making such individuals vulnerable to opportunistic infections such as tuberculosis
