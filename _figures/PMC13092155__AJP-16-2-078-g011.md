caption: The apoptotic and anti-proliferative mechanism of wortmannin in Oral cancer
  cells as an inhibitor of PI3K, AKT, and mTOR. The inhibition of these genes affects
  the cellular proliferation. In addition, BAD and BAK promotes apoptosis. Wortmannin
  showed activity on ROS and MMP also causing apoptosis of the cell. Inhibition of
  ERK 1/2 and STAT3 genes lead to the inhibition of cellular proliferation which may
  have cross-talk with PI3K-AKT pathway. Cell cycle progress is arrested at the Sub
  G1 phase resulting in the inhibition of cell proliferation. [Genes present in yellow-coloured
