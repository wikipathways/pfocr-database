caption: Pathway enrichment analysis and immunoblot data. A, KEGG pathway diagram
  demonstrating the downregulation of multiple enzymes involved in fatty acid degradation
  following linagliptin treatment. Proteins highlighted in red demonstrate downregulation.

  lactate metabolism. Linagliptin-treated swine showed downregulation of both pathways.
  Again, proteins highlighted in red represent downregulation. C, Representative immunoblots
  of proteins involved in glycolysis and the citric acid cycle. Linagliptin-treated
  swine demonstrated nuanced regulation of proteins involved in glycolysis and upregulation
  of several enzymes within the TCA cycle. MPC2, Mitochondrial pyruvate carrier 2;
  IDH2, isocitrate dehydrogenase 2
