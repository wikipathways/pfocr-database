caption: The immunostimulatory role of necroptosis in cancer. The necroptosis pathway
  can induce anti-cancer immunity through three primary mechanisms. First, necroptotic
  cancer cells release MLKL-mediated lytic DAMPs. Second, RIPK1-mediated activation
  of NF-kβ promotes the transcription of cytokines such as IL-1β, TNF-α and CCL-4,
  which enhances CD8 + T cell cross-priming induced by DAMPs. Finally, activated RIPK3
  phosphorylates TRIM28, relieving its inhibition of NF-kβ and SOX9, leading to increased
  cytokine expression and dendritic cells maturation. Mature dendritic cells subsequently
  activate antitumor CD8+ T cells via CD80/CD28 interactions and MHC-mediated antigen
  presentation, thereby stimulating the anti-tumor immune response
