caption: a Schematic representation of the circular material lifecycle. The fluorinated
  diol (HDF-OH) is first functionalized with allyl isocyanate to yield the alkene-functionalized
  monomer (HDF-ENE), which is then crosslinked with pentaerythritol tetrakis(3-mercaptopropionate)
  (PETMP) via thiol–ene photopolymerization to form a fluorinated network (HDF-X).
  Upon exposure to NaOH, selective cleavage of urethane and ester bonds enables recovery
  of the fluorinated monomer (HDF-OH), which is re-functionalized for subsequent printing
  cycles. b Chemical structure of the thiol–ene crosslinked network (HDF-X), highlighting
  the cleavable urethane and ester bonds (red), and the corresponding degradation
  pathway under alkaline conditions leading to HDF-OH and byproducts. c LC–MS analysis
  of pristine and recycled HDF-OH confirming molecular identity and successful recovery
  of the fluorinated monomer after degradation
