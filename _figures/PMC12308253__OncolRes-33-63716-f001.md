caption: Pyruvate is a key point in regulating energy metabolism in tumor cells. The
  glycolytic pathway begins glucose metabolism by breaking down glucose into pyruvate.
  In anaerobic conditions, approximately 85% of pyruvate is converted to lactate in
  the cytoplasm by lactate dehydrogenase, which maintains a continuous energy supply
  from glycolysis. Conversely, in aerobic conditions, approximately 5% of pyruvate
  enters the mitochondrial matrix, There, it undergoes oxidative decarboxylation by
  pyruvate dehydrogenase to form acetyl-CoA. and then enters the tricarboxylic acid
  cycle and interacts with the electron transport chain for efficient energy conversion
