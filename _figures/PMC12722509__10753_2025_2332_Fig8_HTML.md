caption: Panoramic comparison of the immune microenvironment in vitiligo and melanoma.
  (A) The features of vitiligo lesions include inflammatory infiltration, increased
  fragility of melanocytes, heightened secretion of IL-17 by CD4 + T cells leading
  to enhanced cytotoxicity, upregulation of interleukin receptors and chemokine receptors,
  augmented antigen presentation ability of langerhans cells, and pro-inflammatory
  characteristics exhibited by mono. phagocytes. Additionally, fibroblasts and KRTs
  demonstrate heightened activity in the IFN-γ and JAK-STAT pathways under oxidative
  stress, resulting in increased secretion of pro-inflammatory factors like CXCL9/10
  and facilitation of CD8 + T cell recruitment. (B) In contrast, melanoma lesions
  exhibit inert infiltration, increased resistance of melanocytes, heightened immune
  suppressive function of Treg cells, decreased cytotoxicity, migration, and activation
  functions of CD8 + T cells, reduced antigen presentation function of langerhans
  cells, and anti-inflammatory characteristics displayed by mono. phagocytes. Both
  fibroblasts and KRTs remain under low levels of stress
