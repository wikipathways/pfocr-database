  the TGFβ/Smad signaling pathway. This process is inhibited by the BET inhibitor
  JQ1. The combination of PD-0332991 and JQ1 may suppress pancreatic tumor cell growth,
  migration, invasion, and EMT. BET: Bromodomain and extra-terminal; CDK4/6: cyclin-dependent
  kinases 4 and 6; EMT: epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition'
