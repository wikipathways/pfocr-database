caption: Plasma membrane damage–dependent senescence molecular mechanism. Diverse
  types of cellular stress can trigger PMD, repaired by the ESCRT‐III complex machinery,
  which initiates cellular senescence through both Ca2+‐dependent and Ca2+‐independent
  pathways. Upon PMD, an influx of extracellular Ca2+ activates the p53/p21 axis,
  leading to senescence, a mechanism that is distinct from the classical DDR pathway.
  Ca2+‐independent outcome contributes to PMD‐Sen through the upregulation of p16.
  PMD‐Sen has significant implications in physiological and pathological processes
