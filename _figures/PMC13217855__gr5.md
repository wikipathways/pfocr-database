caption: 'Exploration of the potential mechanisms of aqueous extract of xylem in the
  root of Polygala tenuifolia (PTX) (PTXE)-induced central nervous system- and cardiovascular
  system-related symptoms. (A) During myocardial injury, microRNA-122 (miR-122) is
  upregulated and miR-140 is downregulated. They respectively inhibit the expression
  of dual specificity phosphatase 4 (DUSP4), laminin subunit gamma 1 (LAMC1), glycogen
  synthase 1 (GYS1), and filamin B (FLNB), and promote the expression of YES proto-oncogene
  1 (YES1), protein phosphatase 2 regulatory subunit B''epsilon (PPP2R5E), and Rho
  GTPase activating protein 5 (ARHGAP5), thereby inhibiting cell metabolism, actin
  polymerization, and cell survival, promoting mitochondrial apoptosis, and inducing
  myocardial injury. The specific manifestations include myocardial cell damage, elevated
  serum creatine kinase, and an elevated ST segment in the electrocardiogram. During
  the cardiac repair phase, miR-28, miR-326, miR-184, miR-122, and lethal-7c (let-7c)
  are downregulated, while miR-590 is upregulated, which regulates their target genes
  and induces cardiac repair through multiple pathways. The core pathway is phosphatidylinositol
  3-kinase (PI3K)-protein kinase B (AKT) which promotes cell survival, cell metabolism,
  cell proliferation, and DNA repair by enhancing the expression of proteins that
  activate this pathway and/or reducing the expression of proteins that inhibit it.
  (B) Upregulation of miR-590 inhibits the expression of glutamate ionotropic receptor
  alpha-amino-3-hydroxy-5-methyl-4-isoxazolepropionic acid (AMPA) type subunit 2 (GRIA2),
  partially attenuating the antidepressant effect. Downregulation of miR-326 promotes
  the expression of dopamine D2 receptor (DRD2), exerting an antidepressant effect.
  (C) PTXE can induce depressive- and anxiety-like mood changes in rats. Administration
  of 2-amino-5-phosphonopentanoic acid (AP5) via lateral habenula (LHb) cannula injection
  can counteract the depressive-like mood changes induced by PTXE. This figure was
  created using Figdraw. CLIC5: chloride intracellular channel 5; PPP2R2C: protein
  phosphatase 2 regulatory subunit Bgamma; HOPX: homeodomain-only protein homeobox;
  HOMER1: homer scaffold protein 1; PPP2R3A: protein phosphatase 2 regulatory subunit
  B″alpha; FBXO28: f-box protein 28; WAPL: wings apart-like protein homolog; ORC2:
  origin recognition complex subunit 2; ALDH2: aldehyde dehydrogenase 2; PDK1: 3-phosphoinositide-dependent
  protein kinase 1; LAMC2: laminin subunit gamma 2; ERBB4: erb-B2 receptor tyrosine
  kinase 4; GNG5: G protein subunit gamma 5; F1R: insulin like growth factor 1 receptor;
  CCND2: cyclin D2; E2F2: E2F transcription factor 2; Glu: glutamate; NMDAR: N-methyl-D-aspartic
  acid receptor; VTA: ventral tegmental area; DRN: dorsal raphe nucleus; PFC: prefrontal
  cortex; LHb: lateral habenula; SPT: sucrose preference test; FST: forced swim test;
  OFT: open field test; EPM: elevated plus-maze test; CK: creatine kinase'
