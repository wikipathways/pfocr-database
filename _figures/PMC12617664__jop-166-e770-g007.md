caption: Identification of upregulated pain signalling pathways in nerve terminals.
  (A) Dot plot summarizing the top 20 Gene Ontology (GO) biological processes terms
  for upregulated proteins in peripheral nerve terminals of wild type relative to
  NaV1.9 KO mice. The x-axis represents the fold enrichment, and the y-axis represents
  the enriched pathway for biological processes. The colour scale indicates different
  thresholds of the P-value, and the size of the dot indicates the number of genes
  corresponding to each pathway (log2FC ≥ 1, q < 0.05). (B–F) STRING analysis of proteins
  upregulated in wild type relative to NaV1.9 KO nerve terminals. Each node represents
  a protein, and each edge represents an interaction including either physical or
  functional associations. Protein components relevant to MAPK/ERK pathway (B), Arp2/3
  complex (C), cytoskeleton organisation (D and F), and translational machinery (E).
  Only interactions with the high confidence score (0.7) are shown. ERK, extracellular
  regulated kinase; KO, knockout; MAPK, mitogen-activated protein kinase
