caption: Non-coding RNAs modulate tumor radiosensitivity and radioresistance through
  regulation of DNA damage response (DDR) pathways. Representative microRNAs, long
  non-coding RNAs, and circular RNAs, implicated in radiosensitization (blue) and
  radioresistance (purple) are depicted. Radiosensitizing ncRNAs such as miR-101,
  miR-182, miR-34a, LINC-PINT, and lnc-RI enhance radiation response by suppressing
  DNA repair effectors or checkpoint regulators, whereas radioresistance-promoting
  ncRNAs such as HOTAIR, LINP1, and NORAD sustain DDR signaling and survival pathways.
  These ncRNAs collectively orchestrate cellular outcomes to ionizing radiation across
  diverse tumor types. Mechanisms depicted are supported by evidence from in vitro
  cellular models
