caption: The mechanism underlying MEKi+RT treatment-mediated activation of anti-tumor
  immunity. In KRAS-mutated lung cancer, the administration of trametinib in combination
  with RT results in the inhibition of the downstream MEK-ERK signaling pathway, impacts
  CHK2 phosphorylation, and induces increased DNA damage. Consequently, the generation
  of additional double-stranded DNA may facilitate the activation of the cGAS-STING
  pathway. This activation stimulates the transcription factor NF-κB, leading to an
  upregulation of CXCL10 expression. CXCL10 promotes the infiltration of T cells into
  the tumor microenvironment, thereby enhancing anti-tumor immunity. CHK2, checkpoint
  kinase 2; cGAS-STING, cyclic GMP–AMP synthase-stimulator of interferon genes; CXCL,
  C-X-C motif chemokine ligand; MEK inhibitor; RT, radiotherapy
