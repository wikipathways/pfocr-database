caption: MR Study Design Assessing the Impact of Education on HFTwo-step analysis
  process was employed in this MR study. In step 1, we evaluated the causal relationships
  of various SES factors (education, income, and TDI), intelligence, and cognitive
  function with HF outcomes using UVMR and MVMR. This step revealed that among these
  factors, only education had an independent causal effect on HF outcomes, even after
  adjusting for income, TDI, intelligence, and cognitive function. In step 2, we conducted
  a 2-step MR analysis to identify potential mediators (N = 88), including common
  lifestyle factors and various diseases, and quantified their mediation proportions
  in the causal pathway linking education with HF outcomes. BMI = body mass index;
  BWMR = Bayesian-weighted Mendelian randomization; COPD = chronic obstructive pulmonary
  disease; HF = heart failure; IVW = inverse-variance weighted; MR = Mendelian randomization;
  MV-IVW = multivariable inverse-variance weighted; MVMR = multivariable Mendelian
  randomization; NAFLD = nonalcoholic fatty liver disease; SES = socioeconomic status;
  TDI = Townsend deprivation index; UVMR = univariable Mendelian randomization; WHR
  = waist-to-hip ratio
