caption: Before LH surge, Follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) regulates E2 and antioxidants
  with a redox balance in granulosa cells. FSH binds to its receptor (FSHR) in granulosa
  cells, activating the protein kinase A signaling pathway, which subsequently regulates
  β-catenin (CTNNB1). CTNNB1 promotes the expression of aromatase, an enzyme responsible
  for converting androgens into E2. E2 directly promotes ROS production, leading to
  the activation of the NF-κB signaling pathway. Concurrently, by binding to ER, E2
  enhances antioxidant capacity, thus maintaining a balance between ROS and antioxidants
  to ensure cellular stability and function
