caption: 'Analysis of calycosin and calycosin-7-O-β-D-glucoside (CG) biosynthesis.
  (A) Upper: Pathway Ⅰ shows the previously proposed route to CG. Lower: Pathway Ⅱ
  depicts the novel route identified here that uses AmOMT2 to convert liquiritigenin
  to 4′-methoxyliquiritigenin prior to IFS. IFS, isoflavone synthase; I3′H, isoflavone
  3′-hydroxylase; IOMT, isoflavone O-methyltransferase; HID, 2-hydroxyisoflavanone
  dehydratase; UGT, UDP-glucose: calycosin-7-O-glucosyltransferase. (B) Proposed MS
  fragmentation pattern of 4′-methoxyliquiritigenin (m/z 270). (C) HPLC profile of
  the 4′-methoxyliquiritigenin product generated by AmOMT2 with liquiritigenin as
  substrate. Liq, liquiritigenin. (D) The MS2 spectrum of the product of 4′-methoxyliquiritigenin'
