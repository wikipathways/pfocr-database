caption: Multi-immune modulation with rilzabrutinib. Rilzabrutinib acts across the
  innate and adaptive immune system by inhibiting activation of B cells; potentially
  reducing autoantibody production; blocking Fcγ receptor – mediated phagocytosis
  in macrophages in spleen and liver; inhibiting NLRP3 inflammasome activation; and
  modulating key drivers of thromboinflammation, including NETosis, ROS production,
  and CLEC-2–mediated platelet activation. Additional effects on other immune cells
  include inhibition of inflammatory pathways in antigen presenting cells and neutralization
  of IgE autoantibody responses by FcεR pathway inhibition in mast cells and basophils
