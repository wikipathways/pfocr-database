caption: 'Key intracellular signaling pathways activated by innate immune receptors.
  This schematic summarizes the three major signaling cascades triggered by pattern
  recognition PRR engagement: the NF-κB, MAPK, and JAK-STAT pathways. The NF-κB pathway
  is activated via canonical and noncanonical routes, leading to the nuclear translocation
  of NF-κB dimers and induction of inflammatory genes. The MAPK pathway includes ERK1/2,
  JNK, and p38 branches, each activated through a kinase cascade in response to stressors
  and inflammatory cytokines. The JAK-STAT pathway is initiated by cytokine receptors,
  culminating in STAT activation and transcriptional regulation. Together, these pathways
  orchestrate gene expression programs that drive inflammation, immune activation,
  and tissue responses. ASK1, apoptosis signal-regulating kinase 1; BAFF, B-cell activating
  factor; BAFFR, BAFF receptor; BCR, B-cell receptor; CD40L, CD40 ligand; CpG DNA,
  cytosine-phosphate-guanine-rich DNA; DLK, dual leucine zipper-bearing kinase; EGFR,
  epidermal growth factor receptor; ERK, extracellular signal-regulated kinase; IFN,
  interferon; IFN-α, interferon alpha; IFN-β, interferon beta; IFN-γ, interferon gamma;
  IFN-R, interferon receptor; IκB, inhibitor of NF-κB; IKK, IκB kinase; IL, interleukin;
  IL-1β, interleukin-1 beta; IL-1R, interleukin-1 receptor; IL-2R, interleukin-2 receptor;
  IL-3R, interleukin-3 receptor; IL-6R, interleukin-6 receptor; JAK, Janus kinase;
  JNK, c-Jun N-terminal kinase; LIGHT, TNF ligand superfamily member 14; LTα/β, lymphotoxin
  alpha/beta; LTβR, lymphotoxin beta receptor; MAPK, mitogen-activated protein kinase;
  MAPKK, MAPK kinase; MAPKKK, MAPK kinase kinase; MEKK1/4, MAP/ERK kinase kinase 1/4;
  MKK1/2, MAPK kinase 1/2; MKK3/6, MAPK kinase 3/6; MKK4/7, MAPK kinase 4/7; MLK3,
  mixed-lineage kinase 3; NF-κB, nuclear factor kappa B; NIK, NF-κB-inducing kinase;
  p38, p38 MAP kinase; RANKL, receptor activator of NF-κB ligand; STAT, signal transducer
  and activator of transcription; TAK, transforming growth factor-β-activated kinase
  1; TCR, T cell receptor; TGF-α, transforming growth factor alpha; TLR, toll-like
  receptor; TNF-α, tumor necrosis factor alpha; TNFR, tumor necrosis factor receptor;
  UDP-Sugars, uridine diphosphate-sugars'
