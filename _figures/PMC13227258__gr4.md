caption: CircRNAs regulate Wnt/β-catenin signaling in cardiovascular diseases (By
  Biorender). CircRNA employs multiple strategies to regulate the Wnt/β-catenin pathway,
  driving cardiovascular pathologies. Wnt binding to Frizzled (Fzd) activates Dishevelled
  (Dvl), recruiting LRP and Axin to phosphorylate β-catenin. Released β-catenin enters
  the nucleus, displacing Groucho from TCF to activate transcription of genes involved
  in migration, inflammation, and calcification in atherosclerosis, hypertension,
  and myocardial ischemia–reperfusion injury
