  energy stress and activating catabolic pathways. Under conditions of low energy
  availability, AMPK promotes autophagy by upregulating TSC2, an upstream inhibitor
  of mTORC1, and by directly phosphorylating Raptor, a key mTORC1 subunit. Through
  these mechanisms, AMPK suppresses mTORC1 activity, thereby limiting cell growth

  energy while maintaining cellular homeostasis. Image credits: Figure created by
