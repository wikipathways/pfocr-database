caption: Schematic illustration of the lock-equipped six-helix DNA bundle (L6HB) for
  targeted siSTING delivery and modulation of the cGAS-STING pathway in Alzheimer’s
  disease. L6HB nanostructure was engineered to encapsulate siSTING and remain structurally
  stable during circulation. Upon reaching Alzheimer’s disease (AD) lesions, the nanostructure
  responds to elevated CD64 expression by undergoing conformational unlocking, enabling
  controlled siSTING exposure. Through LRP-1-mediated ANG targeting, the nanocarrier
  effectively traverses the blood-brain barrier and delivers siSTING to microglia,
  thereby suppressing aberrant cGAS-STING signaling. This targeted silencing shifts
  microglial polarization from the proinflammatory M1 phenotype to the anti-inflammatory
  M2 phenotype, facilitating Aβ clearance, alleviating neuroinflammation, and promoting
  neuroprotection in AD model mice
