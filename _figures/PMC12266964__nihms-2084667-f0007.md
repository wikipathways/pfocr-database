caption: 'Working hypothesis: Schematic depicting presumptive pathway of nNOS regulation
  via ‘CHIP-HSP70-nNOS-ubiquitination Heterocomplex’ in the PVN of CHF rats. Binding
  of PIN to nNOS in cytosol destabilizes nNOS dimers, which renders nNOS catalytically
  inactive, either by interfering with the assembly or dimer stability. The nNOS dimerization
  inhibitors like PIN or BH4 depletion increased nNOS as monomeric form that recruits
  HSP70 and CHIP to forms the ‘CHIP-HSP70-nNOS Heterocomplex’ that allows nNOS monomer
  inactivation. Inactive nNOS monomers are susceptible to ubiquitination and subsequent
  proteasomal degradation. This results in decreased levels of nNOS as well as NO
  production in the PVN during CHF causing an increase in sympathetic nerve activity
  (SNA). However, HSP90 doesn’t form nNOS-Heterocomplex with CHIP, and HSP90 is not
  involved in the CHIP-nNOS ubiquitination mediated nNOS regulation in the PVN'
