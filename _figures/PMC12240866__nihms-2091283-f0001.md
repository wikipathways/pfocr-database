caption: 'GPCR signaling is organized in subcellular compartments as exemplified based
  on the βAR signaling pathway. FRET-based biosensors have been pivotal in defining
  the spatial aspects of GPCR signaling. cAMP nanorulers are targeted FRET-based biosensors
  that allow to record cAMP gradients at defined nanometer distances from a target
  protein. Fluorescent nanobody (Nb) and miniG (mG) protein biosensors have been key
  for detecting active GPCRs at distinct subcellular sites. Conventional and enhanced
  bystander BRET has proven useful in detecting GPCR signaling at distinct organelles.
  The ONE-GO biosensors are BRET-based reporters in which the modified Ga subunit
  needs to be expressed only in trace amounts, minimally interfering with endogenous
  signaling. ATP: adenosine triphosphate; AC: adenylyl cyclase; GDP: guanosine diphosphate;
  GTP: guanosine triphosphate; AKAP: A-kinase anchoring protein; PKA: protein kinase
  A (with regulatory (R) and catalytic (C) subunits); PDE: phosphodiesterase; YFP:
  yellow fluorescent protein; CFP: cyan fluorescent protein; EPAC: exchange protein
  directly activated by cAMP; Nb-FP: fluorescent protein-tagged nanobody; Rluc: Renilla
  luciferase; FP: fluorescent protein (can be GFP (e.g. as acceptor for conventional
  bystander BRET) or rGFP (for ebBRET); Nluc: nano luciferase'
