caption: 'The role of LONP1 in reproductive diseases, musculoskeletal diseases, and
  cancer. A Reproductive diseases: Artemisinin ameliorates PCOS by enhancing the LONP1–CYP11A1
  interaction. Additionally, downregulation of LONP1 triggers nuclear translocation
  of AIFM1, leading to oocyte death. B Musculoskeletal diseases: Under muscle disuse
  conditions, downregulation of LONP1 triggers the UPRmt and FGF21 secretion, leading
  to bone loss. Resveratrol antioxidant therapy suppresses LONP1 knockout-induced
  ROS production and MAPK pathway activation to alleviate chondrocyte death. Furthermore,
  ASIC1a promotes LONP1 expression, ultimately resulting in chondrocyte death. C Cancer:
  LONP1 triggers an increase in ROS, which subsequently activates the MAPK and Ras/ERK
  signaling pathways to promote NSCLC cell proliferation, while LONP1 inhibits caspase-3-mediated
  apoptosis. Obtusilactone A and sesamin effectively inhibit NSCLC cell proliferation
  by inhibiting the protease activity of LONP1. H. pylori promotes gastric cancer
  cell proliferation by regulating the HIF-1 α/LONP1 axis. HBx enhances the UPRmt
  by upregulating LONP1 in mitochondria, while downregulating LONP1 in cytoplasm to
  activate the PINK1/Parkin axis. The dual pathways synergistically induce mitochondrial
  autophagy, ultimately promoting HCC cell proliferation. Furthermore, LONP1 induces
  mitochondrial autophagy by increasing the LC3-II/I ratio and PINK1/Parkin content,
  thereby promoting cervical cancer cell proliferation. LONP1 promotes ferroptosis
  in pancreatic cancer cells by inhibiting the Nrf2/Keap1/GPX4 axis. Additionally,
  LONP1 promotes the proliferation of pancreatic cancer cells by regulating the JNK/MMP
  axis. In terms of treatment, LMPt can inhibit the proliferation of pancreatic cancer
  cells by enhancing the combination of LONP1 with POLG and TFAM, degrading POLG and
  TFAM, and thus blocking POLG- and TFAM-mediated mtDNA replication. LONP1, Lon protease
  1; PCOS, polycystic ovary syndrome; CYP11A1, cytochrome P450 family 11 subfamily
  A member 1; AIFM1, apoptosis inducing factor mitochondria associated 1; FGF, fibroblast
  growth factor; UPRmt, mitochondrial unfolded protein response; ROS, reactive oxygen
  species; MAPK, mitogen activated protein kinase; ASIC1a, acid-sensing ion channel
  1a; ERK, extracellular signal-regulated protein kinase; NSCLC, non-small cell lung
  cancer; HIF, hypoxia-inducible factor; HBx, HBV X protein; PINK1, PTEN-induced putative
  kinase 1; Nrf2, nuclear factor erythroid 2-related factor 2; GPX4, glutathione peroxidase
  4; JNK, c-Jun N-terminal kinase; MMP, matrix metalloproteinase; LMPt, miriplatin-loaded
  liposomes; POLG, polymerase gamma; TFAM, mitochondrial transcription factor A'
