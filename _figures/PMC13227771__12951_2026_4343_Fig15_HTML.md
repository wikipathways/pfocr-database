caption: 'Building on clinical evidence that identifies a distinct subset of GSDME-positive
  hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC), we developed a biomimetic nanoplatform (CRRPE NPs)
  to leverage this unique target. This system enables highly efficient intracellular
  delivery of platinum (Pt), shifting the dominant cell death mode from slow apoptosis
  to prompt and potent pyroptosis. The proposed mechanism is illustrated schematically:
  when the intracellular concentration of co-delivered CDDP exceeds a threshold, it
  activates the systemic STING pathway, initiating adaptive immunity via GSDME cleavage-induced
  pyroptosis. Simultaneously, the complementary component RRx-001 downregulates CD47,
  repolarizing tumor-associated macrophages (TAMs) to an M1 phenotype for pro-inflammatory
  mediator release, thereby further enhancing the adaptive immune response and offering
  a combined innate and adaptive immunotherapeutic strategy for HCC'
