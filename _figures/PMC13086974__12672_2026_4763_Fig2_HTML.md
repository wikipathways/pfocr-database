caption: LncRNA regulation of IR-induced DDR pathway proteins. LncRNAs influence DNA
  repair by modulating core DDR effectors. LncRNA LINP1 promotes NHEJ via Ku70/Ku80–DNA-PKcs,
  while LINC-PINT and LINC00312 inhibit DNA-PKcs. Lnc-RI suppresses RAD51 via miR-193a
  and stabilizes Ligase 4, shifting repair toward NHEJ. LINC00665, LINC00261, and
  PCAT-1 support HR factors (RAD51, BRCA2). HOTAIR and NORAD regulate ATR/CHK1 checkpoint
  signaling. Dysregulation alters HR/NHEJ balance, impacting radiosensitivity. Mechanisms
  depicted are supported by evidence from in vitro cellular models
