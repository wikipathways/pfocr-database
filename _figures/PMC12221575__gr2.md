caption: The role of ion channels in IVDD. When activated by mechanical stress and
  extracellular H+, ion channels distributed in IVD cell membrane including PIEZO1,
  ASIC and TRPV4 subsequently open and mediate the influx of Ca2+ and Fe3+. Intracellular
  iron overload brought on by the Fe3+ influx ultimately results in ferroptosis as
  a stress response. The influx of Ca2+ activates the NF-κB pathway to mediates senescence,
  apoptosis and pyroptosis of IVD cells via transcriptionally upregulating downstream
  proteins and pro-inflammatory cytokines. P2X7R distributed on the cell nuclear membrane
  can also bind to NLRP3 to promote the release of IL-1β
