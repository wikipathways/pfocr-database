caption: 'The scheme of dual blockade of the VEGF-A/VEGFR2 pathway using antibody-based
  agents. VEGF-A binding to VEGFR2 promotes angiogenesis, immune-cold TME, and tumor
  proliferation. BEV-BS and RAM suppress VEGFR2 signaling but RAM induces excess VEGF-A.
  Excess VEGF-A can bind to VEGFR1 or NRP-1 and may promote detrimental effects via
  alternative signaling. Combination of BEV-BS and RAM may strongly suppress these
  signalings. Arrow color indicates signaling strength: red = signal transduction,
  cream = residual signal transduction, and blue = suppressed signal transduction.
  BEV-BS, bevacizumab biosimilar; NRP1, neuropilin-1; RAM, ramucirumab; TME, tumor
  microenvironment, VEGF-A, vascular endothelial growth factor-A; VEGFR1, vascular
  endothelial growth factor receptor 1; VEGFR2, vascular endothelial growth factor
  receptor 2'
