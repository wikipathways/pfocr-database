caption: IL-15 promotes immune escape, invasion, and metastasis of pancreatic cancer
  by upregulating PD-L1 expression via the JAK1/STAT1/IRF1 pathway. In the immune
  microenvironment of pancreatic cancer, IL-15 upregulates PD-L1 expression in pancreatic
  cancer cells by activating the JAK1/STAT1/IRF1 pathway within them. The PD-L1 on
  the surface of pancreatic cancer cells binds to the PD-1 receptors on CD8⁺ T cells
  in the microenvironment, which in turn inhibits the proliferation, activation, secretion,
  and tumor-killing ability of CD8⁺ T cells. This aggravates the immunosuppression
  of the microenvironment and thereby indirectly promotes the malignant progression
  of pancreatic cancer cells such as invasion and metastasis
