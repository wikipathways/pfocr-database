caption: Functional heterogeneity of TAMs across BRCA subtypes. TAMs interact differently
  with each BRCA subtype through subtype-specific molecular pathways. In the luminal
  BC cell overlap, TAM secrete BMP-2 or CCL12, binding with BMPR and CCR2 respectively,
  further activating PI3K/p-AKT/mTOR signaling; furthermore, CXCL12 from the TME bind
  with CXCR4 of TAM membrane, activating MAPK signaling. In HER2+ BC cell overlap,
  TAM-secreted IL-8 activates the Src/STAT3/ERK1/2 signaling pathway, and TAM-derived
  factors, such as matrix metalloproteinase 11 (MMP11), activate ERK1/2-JNK phosphorylation
  and up-regulate MMP9 via the CCR2 signaling axis. In TNBC cell overlap, TAMs secrete
  CCL22 and activate CCR4 signaling in hypoxic microenvironments, secrete IL-10 and
  IL-6 to activate the JAK2/STAT3 axis, and promote EMT and cell migration through
  pathways involving YAP1, MCP-1/CCR2
