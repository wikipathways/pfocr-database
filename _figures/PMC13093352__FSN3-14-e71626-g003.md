caption: Proposed mechanism of MP‐mediated analgesia in CFA‐induced inflammatory pain
  at the spinal level. CFA‐induced peripheral inflammation elevates levels of pro‐inflammatory
  cytokines (TNF‐α, IL‐6), leukocytes, and lymphocytes. These factors cross the blood‐nerve
  barrier, activating spinal astrocytes (as indicated by increased GFAP) and upregulating
  the expression of NLRP3, a key component of the inflammasome pathway. Based on the
  established role of this pathway in neuroinflammation, we propose a model in which
  increased NLRP3 may lead to inflammasome assembly and subsequent caspase‐1‐dependent
  release of IL‐1β and IL‐18, contributing to neuronal sensitization and pain hypersensitivity.
  Our data suggest that the freshwater‐derived MP attenuates this pain signaling cascade
  by suppressing peripheral cytokine production and subsequent astrocyte activation
  and NLRP3 expression, ultimately alleviating pain behavior
