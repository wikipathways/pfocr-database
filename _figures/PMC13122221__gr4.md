caption: When the stiffness of the ECM increases, integrins on the cell membrane are
  activated and recruit FAK, Vinculin, and Talin to form a complex. This complex further
  regulates the assembly of actin filaments. Subsequent changes in the actin cytoskeleton
  affect Lamin A/C within the nucleus, which in turn inhibits the Hippo signaling
  pathway. Under this condition, YAP/TAZ is not phosphorylated; instead, it translocates
  into the nucleus, binds to the transcription factor TEAD to form a complex, regulates
  the expression of related genes, and ultimately promotes cell migration. Created
