caption: Mechanism of action and structure of HER3. A This graph illustrates the dimerization
  interactions between HER3 and HER2/ EGFR, highlighting the biological significance
  of HER3 in both its functionally active and inactive states. In its functional state,
  HER3 heterodimerizes with other family members to transduce downstream signals through
  adaptor molecules. Notably, when the ligand NRG is present, the interaction of HER2
  and HER3 results in the AKT pathway's activation, thereby promoting cell survival
  and proliferation. B Structure of HER3 Receptor. HER3 possesses an extracellular
  region consisting of four domains responsible for ligand binding and dimerization.
  Its intracellular kinase domain is catalytically inactive, while the C-terminal
  tail contains multiple tyrosine phosphorylation sites that serve as docking motifs
  for downstream signaling adaptors
