caption: Mechanisms of resistance to PARP inhibitors. (1) alterations in the PARP1
  signaling pathway disrupt the synthetic lethality associated with PARPi. Mutations
  in PARP1 can lead to reduced efficacy of standard inhibitors or result in a diminished
  capacity for DNA trapping (a). The loss of PARG or ARH3, which are enzymes responsible
  for removing PARylation modifications, can restore the normal signaling pathways
  mediated by PARP1 (b). Additionally, the formation of stable complexes between PARP1
  and KAT6A and APEX1 or HMGB3 decreases the amount of PARP1 that becomes trapped
  at DNA single-strand break sites (c). (2) HR restoration can occur through the reactivation
  of HRR genes (d), restoration of end resection at DSBs (e), upregulation of RAD51
  (f), and remodeling and modification of chromosomes (g). The 53BP1–RIF1-Shieldin
  complex, along with CST, plays a critical role in stabilizing the ends of DSBs,
  thereby preventing degradation mediated by MRE11. ATMIN, DYNLL1, and TLK serve as
  regulators that enhance the functionality of the 53BP1–RIF1-Shieldin-CST complex;
  conversely, TRIP13 inhibits its formation. Ku and DNA PKcs participate in repairing
  end excision while concurrently inhibiting HRR mechanisms, a process facilitated
  by ERCC6L2. Additionally, miR-622 and Cxorf56 act to inhibit this mechanism. DSBs
  facilitate the recruitment of ATM, which subsequently phosphorylates Syk and CtIP,
  thereby inducing degradation via MRE11. HLEB inhibits MRE11. (3) a stalled RF generates
  one-ended DSBs through the actions of SMARCAL1, ZRANB3, and HLTF, the absence of
  which confers resistance against PARPi (h). MRE11 plays a critical role in degrading
  stalled RF; its recruitment is enhanced by PTIP-MLL3/4 methyltransferase, which
  modifies H3K4me1/3, thus facilitating the recruitment of the nucleosome remodeling
  factor CHD4. The UFMylation of PTIP by UFL1 represents a crucial process; loss of
  UFL1 or overexpression of the UFM1 protease UFSP2 leads to resistance. Furthermore,
  EZH2 mediates MUS81 nuclease recruitment through H3K27 modification, thereby contributing
  to the degradation (i). A dotted-line box indicates a decrease in activity, while
  a solid-line box signifies an increase in activity. PARPi, PARP inhibitors; HRR,
  homologous recombination repair; DSBs, double-strand breaks; RF, replication fork
