caption: 'Tregs attenuate astrogliosis and facilitate neurological recovery by modulating
  the IL-10/STAT3/PKC/vimentin pathway in astrocytes. A proposed mechanism for Treg-mediated
  modulation of astrocyte activation: Treg-derived IL-10 activates the STAT3 signaling
  in astrocytes, which in turn suppresses PKC phosphorylation and downregulates vimentin
  expression, thereby maintaining astrocytes in an appropriate activation state. Through
  this IL-10/STAT3/PKC/vimentin pathway, Tregs attenuate astrogliosis, reduce CSPG
  deposition, and ultimately facilitate axonal remodeling and functional recovery
  after SCI'
