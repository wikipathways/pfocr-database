caption: Mechanisms and pathways of plant immune inducers.Plant immune inducers activate
  signaling pathways that lead to the induction of plant immunity. After plant immune
  inducer recognition by specific receptors, MAPK signaling cascades and transcriptional
  reprogramming are initiated to promote disease resistance. Simultaneously, hormone
  signaling pathways, such as the SA signaling pathway, are activated, leading to
  an SAR response. ROSs are also induced, resulting in an ROS burst and a hypersensitive
  response (HR), which collectively contribute to resistance against pathogen infection.
