caption: The role and mechanism played by Irisin in heart failure. Irisin plays a
  pivotal role in augmenting cardiac output in heart failure through three distinct
  mechanisms. First, Irisin upregulates miRNAs, resulting in the reduction of ROS
  production and subsequent attenuation of apoptosis. Second, Irisin promotes the
  upregulation of PGC‐1α, troponin C and troponin T2D, thereby enhancing myocardial
  function. Finally, Irisin activates the AMPK‐ULK1 signalling pathway, leading to
  the induction of protective autophagy. The figure was created using Figdraw
