caption: Interplay of CK1 in multiple cellular pathways. This figure illustrates how
  CK1 plays a dual role in two core pathways that determine cell fate. In necroptosis
  (left panel), when the necrosome complex is formed, CK1 directly phosphorylates
  (P) and activates RIPK3. The activated RIPK3 then phosphorylates its downstream
  protein, MLKL, and the phosphorylated MLKL creates pores in the cell membrane (25,
  107, 108). In middle panel (canonical WNT pathway), when WNT ligand is absent, CK1
  phosphorylates β-catenin, promoting its degradation. In this process, CK1 acts as
  a negative regulator. When the WNT ligand is present, CK1 phosphorylates Dishevelled
  (Dvl), which interacts with the β-catenin destruction complex. This stabilizes β-catenin,
  allowing it to move to the nucleus and induce cell proliferation (109). Right panel,
  In the non-canonical WNT pathway, CK1 functions as a positive regulator, independent
  β-catenin. CK1 phosphorylates DVL, which activates downstream signals that regulate
  cell movement and shape. This can manifest as either cell contraction or cell expansion
  and can also transcription factors like NF-κB to regulate inflammatory responses
  (110)
