caption: Molecular mechanisms promoting BBB disruption and transmigration in BCBM.
  Breast cancer cells disrupt the BBB through coordinated actions of secreted factors,
  EVs, and non coding RNAs. Tumor derived LCN2 cooperates with MMP9 to remodel the
  ECM and promote EMT. ENPP1 interferes with endothelial insulin signaling and alters
  the AKT-GSK3β-β catenin pathway, leading to junctional destabilization. Hypoxia
  driven EVs enriched in ITGB3 activate VEGFR2 signaling and induce angiopoietin 2
  expression, promoting angiogenesis and vascular permeability. Exosomal miR105 and
  lncRNA GS1 600G8.5 disrupt tight junction proteins including ZO-1, claudin-5 and
  N cadherin, whereas migrasome mediated ATF6 signaling reduces VE cadherin expression.
  Additional mediators including HBEGF, ST6GALNAC5, SEMA4D, GBP1 and CAFs further
  facilitate tumor cell transmigration across the barrier and promote brain metastasis
  formation
