caption: Palmitoylation-mediated modulation of BCR signaling and apoptotic regulation
  in B cells. a Palmitoylation facilitates FCRL4 recruitment to membrane lipid rafts,
  enabling its phosphorylation through Src-family kinase interaction and subsequent
  activation of the NF-κB pathway, which culminates in CpG-induced IL-10 production.
  Similarly, HGAL requires palmitoylation for lipid raft partitioning and Src-kinase-mediated
  amplification of BCR signaling. Furthermore, BCR-CD19/CD21/CD81 coengagement triggers
  dynamic CD81 palmitoylation that sustains BCR signaling through lipid raft localization,
  whereas 2BP abrogates these processes by displacing lipid raft-associated components.
  b GNA13 palmitoylation suppresses BCL2 expression to promote apoptosis and to inhibit
  lymphoma progression, while GNA13-deficient cells exhibit increased sensitivity
  to Venetoclax, a BCL2 inhibitor. Furthermore, pharmacological activation of ZDHHC21
  by lanatoside C enhances FASN palmitoylation to promote its degradation, thereby
  suppressing FA biosynthesis and amplifying anti-tumor responses. c miR-138 and miR-424
  suppress APT1/APT2 expression, thereby impairing depalmitoylation capacity to enhance
  CD95 palmitoylation, which stabilizes its membrane localization and potentiates
  apoptosis signaling cascades
