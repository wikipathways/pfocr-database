caption: Pathogenic CD70-CD27 signaling in cancer. In hematological malignancies,
  CD70-CD27 signaling activates canonical Wnt, JAK/STAT, Hedgehog, and TGF-β pathways,
  which maintain cellular stemness and an immature phenotype. CD27 crosslinking via
  protein kinase C or β-catenin-dependent mechanisms, along with CD70 reverse signaling,
  drives malignant cell proliferation. This axis sustains survival through synergistic
  regulation of MEK pathway kinases and transcription factor AP-1, coupled with β-catenin-dependent
  Wnt pathway activation. Within solid tumors, CD70 signaling fosters cancer stem
  cell characteristics and epithelial-mesenchymal transition by upregulating EMT-associated
  transcriptional regulators such as SOX2, CD44, Vimentin, Snail, Slug, and β-catenin
  while concurrently activating MAPK signaling and inducing RhoE overexpression. Hypoxia
  emerges as a critical modulator of CD70 expression, directly influencing tumor stemness,
  migration, and invasive potential
