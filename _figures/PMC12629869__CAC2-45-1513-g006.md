caption: STING‐induced noncanonical autophagy and lysosome biogenesis. The cGAS‐STING‐mediated
  noncanonical autophagy involves regulators, including WIPI2, ATG5, V‐ATPase, and
  ATG16L1, which induce LC3 lipidation. STING also inhibits canonical autophagy through
  competing with PI3P for binding to WIPI2. STING has recently been shown to function
  as a proton channel. The increased proton flux and pH in the Golgi apparatus and
  acidic vesicles, followed by STING activation, are essential for LC3 lipidation.
  Also, the lipidation of the GABARAPs abrogates the inhibition of mTORC2 on the transcriptional
  activity of TFEB, TFE3, and MITF, thus promoting lysosomal biogenesis. The non‐canonical
  autophagy and lysosome biogenesis are essential for cell survival and host defense
  and considered the primordial function of the cGAS‐STING pathway, prior to the emergence
  of STING‐NF‐κB/IRF3‐IFN‐dependent functions in antimicrobial defense and immune
  surveillance (recently adapted functions). ATG16L1, autophagy‐related protein 16‐like
  1; ATG5, autophagy related 5; cGAMP, 2’3’‐cyclic GMP‐AMP; cGAS, cyclic GMP‐AMP synthase;
  GABARAP, gamma‐aminobutyric acid receptor‐associated protein; IFNs, interferons;
  IRF3, interferon regulatory factor 3; LC3, light chain 3; MITF, microphthalmia‐associated
  transcription factor; mTORC2, mechanistic target of rapamycin complex 2; NF‐κB,
  nuclear transcription factor‐kappa B; PI3P, phosphatidylinositol 3‐phosphate; STING,
  stimulator of interferon gene; TBK1, TANK‐binding kinase 1; TFEB, transcription
  factor EB; WIPI2, WD repeat domain, phosphoinositide‐interacting protein 2
