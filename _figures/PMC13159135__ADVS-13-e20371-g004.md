caption: Working model depicts that IAV‐induced ferroptosis via the MVP‐IRF1‐FSP1
  pathway. During IAV infection, MVP interacts with TRAF6 to destabilize its interaction
  with IRF1. As a result, non‐ubiquitinated IRF1 translocates to the cell nucleus,
  suppressing FSP1 at the transcription level (blue arrow). On the other hand, IAV
  infection induces interactions between IRF1 and NMT2 and between IRF1 and TRIM21,
  thereby releasing MVP and FSP1. As a result, non‐ubiquitinated and non‐myristoylated
  FSP1 do not translocate to the membrane, leading to ferroptosis
