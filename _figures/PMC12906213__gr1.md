  under low oxygen tension. Figure created using BioRender.Legend: The schematic depicts
  the uptake of circulating NO2− into red blood cells and its reduction to bioactive
  NO by nitrite-reductase systems, including deoxyhemoglobin and, where relevant,
  xanthine oxidoreductase. This oxygen-independent NO-generating pathway becomes increasingly
  active when oxygen availability is limited for NOS-dependent NO synthesis, such
  as during intense physical exercise (Part A). The integration of nitrite-derived
