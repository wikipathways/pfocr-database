caption: Glucose is the precursor of glycolysis, the pentose phosphate pathway, and
  the hexosamine pathway. Upon glucose starvation, lung cancer cells display reduced
  hexosamine pathway flux, leading to impaired protein N-glycosylation, and subsequent
  activation of the unfolded protein response (UPR). These specific effects of glucose
  withdrawal are rescued with exogenous mannose. Through activation of the PERK arm
  of the UPR and concurrent activation of the MEK-ERK pathway, likely by cancer-cell
  intrinsic oncogenic signaling pathways, synthesis and secretion of the LIF cytokine
  is elevated. Cancer cell-derived LIF then acts as a paracrine factor to mediate
  local angiogenesis, immune suppression, and lung colonization and tumorigenesis.
