caption: 'The regulatory mechanism of mitochondrial biosynthesis is centered on PGC-1α.
  PGC-1α is a master modulator of mitochondrial function and is regulated by diverse
  physiological and pathological stimuli, such as DNA damage, oxidative stress, cold,
  exercise, fasting, metabolic stress, hemodynamic stress, and nutrient deprivation.
  Multiple transcription factors, including CREB, FOXO1, MEF2, p53, and HEY2, bind
  directly to the PPARGC1A promoter to regulate its transcription. CREB and FOXO1
  strongly induce PGC-1α expression under conditions such as fasting, cold, exercise,
  and energy stress. MEF2 mediates calcium signaling-dependent regulation, whereas
  p53 suppresses the expression of PGC-1α upon oxidative stress. HEY2 recruits HDAC1
  to reduce histone acetylation and inhibit the transcription of PGC-1α. Epigenetically,
  PGC-1α is modulated by histone modifications: Under metabolic stress, SMYD1 increases
  promoter activity in the heart via H3K4me3 enrichment, and SIRT1 decreases H3K9ac
  levels at the promoter and directly deacetylates the PGC-1α protein. Additionally,
  p38/mitogen-activated protein kinase (MAPK) or AMPK regulates PGC-1α phosphorylation
  under nutrient deprivation conditions. By interacting with transcription factors
  such as ERRs, NRFs, and PPARs, PGC-1α coordinates downstream pathways involving
  glucose homeostasis, fatty acid oxidation, and mitochondrial biogenesis. Arrows
  (→): Activation; Blunted lines (⊥): Inhibition; Double-headed arrows (↔): Interactions.
  Ac: Acetylation; AMP: Adenosine monophosphate; AMPK: Adenosine 5′-monophosphate
  (AMP)-activated protein kinase; ATP: Adenosine triphosphate; cAMP: Cyclic adenosine
  monophosphate; CREB: Cyclic AMP-response element binding protein; ERRs: Estrogen-related
  receptors; FA: Fatty acids; FAO: Fatty acid oxidation; FOXO1: Forkhead box O1; H3K27ac:
  Histone H3 lysine 27 acetylation; H3K4me3: Trimethylation of histone H3 at lysine
  4; H3K9ac: Histone H3 lysine 9 acetylation; HDAC1: Histone deacetylase 1; HEY2:
  Hes related family bHLH transcription factor with YRPW motif 2; MAPK: Mitogen-activated
  protein kinase; MEF2: Myocyte enhancer factor 2; NAD+/NADH: Nicotinamide adenine
  dinucleotide; NRFs: Nuclear respiratory factors; P: Phosphorylation; p53: Tumor
  protein p53; PGC-1α: Proliferator-activated receptor gamma coactivator 1-alpha;
  PKA: Protein kinase A; PPARGC1A: Peroxisome proliferator-activated receptor gamma
  coactivator 1-alpha; PPARs: Peroxisome proliferator-activated receptors; SIRT1:
  Sirtuin 1; SMYD1: SET and MYND domain containing 1'
