caption: In the circulatory system, in cardiomyocytes, pathological activation of
  TAS2R or disruptions in its signaling pathway inhibit the nuclear translocation
  of Nrf-2, thereby downregulating the synthesis and secretion of protective and detoxifying
  enzymes. Concurrently, TAS2R activation promotes the expression of inflammation-related
  genes via the CaMK II and NF-κB/IκB signaling pathways and produces inflammatory
  factors like IL-1β and IL-18 through the NLRP3 pathway, further amplifying the inflammatory
  response and ultimately inducing neuroinflammation. Both of these effects influence
  immune cells in cardiac capillaries. In vascular smooth muscle cells, dextromethorphan
  activates TAS2R, leading to a significant increase in IP3 and Ca2+ levels, which
  in turn activates myosin light chain kinase (MLCK), resulting in an increase in
  the phosphorylated form of MLC (pMLC) and promoting the contraction of pulmonary
  artery smooth muscle, thereby affecting vascular contraction status and blood flow
  regulation. In monocytes/macrophages, TAS2R activation by absinthin reduces ROS
  and RNS levels via signaling pathways, providing antioxidant and antimicrobial effects
