caption: Network analysis of significantly enriched KEGG pathways in Ogg1Tgskeletal
  muscle. Protein–protein interaction networks (A–C) of significantly enriched KEGG
  pathways using the differentially expressed genes from the Ogg1Tg skeletal muscle
  and their closely connected nodes. Pathways in cancer (A) and the MAPK signaling
  pathway (B) show a strong representation of immune signature genes and nutrient
  deprivation. The Ribosome (C) pathway shows upregulation of ribosome biogenesis.
  The pathways have a false discovery rate <0.01 and p < 0.05. The networks had a
  confidence (score) cutoff of 0.8 and a degree cutoff of 1. The color indicates the
  fold change gradient from −1 to 1, and the node size indicates the degree of interaction
  with other nodes. KEGG, Kyoto Encyclopedia of Genes and Genomes; MAPK, mitogen-activated
