caption: The dual role of Notch signaling in esophageal cancer. A Notch signaling
  promotes esophageal cancer progression via two key mechanisms. The Notch1–USP5–STAT3
  axis drives angiogenesis and proliferation by stabilizing STAT3, which increases
  the secretion of pro-angiogenic factors. Meanwhile, the TGFβ–ZEB1 pathway suppresses
  Notch3, inducing epithelial–mesenchymal transition (EMT) and generating tumor-initiating
  cells. B. Mechanism of Jag1/2-mediated Notch signaling in maintaining esophageal
  epithelial homeostasis and suppressing tumorigenesis. Under normal conditions, Jag1/2
  activates Notch signaling to maintain tissue homeostasis by balancing cell proliferation
  and differentiation. In contrast, Jag1/2 deficiency impairs Notch signaling, leading
  to uncontrolled proliferation, defective differentiation, disruption of tissue architecture,
  and ultimately driving tumorigenesis. (Created by biorender.com)
