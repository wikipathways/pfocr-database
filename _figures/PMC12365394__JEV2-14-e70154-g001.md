  and RTN4‐rich sEV was taken up by tumour cells, which promoted tumour growth and
  lung metastasis and regulated EMT through the NF‐κB pathway. RTN4 modulated the
  immunosuppressive microenvironment by upregulating PD‐L1 expression and suppressing
  T‐cell infiltration. Blockade of RTN4 can sensitize patients to the immunotherapy
  response of PD‐1, thus inhibiting TNBC progression. EMT, epithelial‐to‐mesenchymal
