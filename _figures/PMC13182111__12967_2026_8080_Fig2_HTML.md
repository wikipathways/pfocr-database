caption: 'Mechanism network of cartilage metabolism regulatory myokines in OA progression.
  This figure illustrates the signaling networks and effects of five key cartilage
  metabolism-regulating myokines (Irisin, IGF-1, Decorin, FGF-21, and IL-7) in osteoarthritis
  (OA). Arrows (→) indicate activation or promotion, while perpendicular lines (⊥)
  indicate inhibition or blockade. Solid lines represent direct upstream effects,
  and dashed lines represent indirect downstream effects. Irisin reduces chondrocyte
  programmed death and inflammation, promotes extracellular matrix (ECM) synthesis
  and deposition, and maintains cartilage integrity by inhibiting NLRP3/caspase-1,
  PI3K/Akt/NF-κB, Wnt/β-catenin, MAPK, and JNK signaling pathways, while enhancing
  mitochondrial biogenesis. IGF-1 attenuates inflammation and matrix degradation by
  inhibiting AMPK and Src/PI3K/AKT signaling and activating the ERK pathway, thereby
  regulating cartilage repair. Decorin stabilizes the cartilage matrix network and
  delays cartilage degeneration by enhancing aggrecan retention in the ECM. FGF-21
  exhibits concentration-dependent effects: at low/moderate concentrations, it promotes
  TFEB nuclear translocation, enhancing chondrocyte autophagy and inhibiting apoptosis
  and ECM degradation; at high concentrations, it may suppress chondrocyte proliferation
  and differentiation. IL-7 upregulates MMP-13 expression by activating the JAK/STAT
  signaling pathway and PYK2 phosphorylation, promoting cartilage matrix degradation
  and accelerating OA pathology'
