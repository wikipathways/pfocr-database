caption: Schematic illustration of astrocytic PTP1B-driven cognitive impairment following
  chronic T. gondii infection. Chronic T. gondii infection upregulates PTP1B in astrocytes,
  activating the NF-κB pathway and driving a pro-inflammatory A1-like neurotoxic reactive
  astrocytes. These reactive astrocytes exhibit increased C3 secretion, elevated pro-inflammatory
  cytokine release, and downregulation of the glutamate transporter EAAT2, leading
  to glutamate accumulation and excitotoxicity. These disorders in neuron-astrocyte
  crosstalk further induce neuronal senescence, reduce neuronal complexity and impair
  synaptic ultrastructure, ultimately resulting in cognitive decline. This graphical
