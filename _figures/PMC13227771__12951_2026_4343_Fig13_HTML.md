  Pyroptosis-Boosted cGAS-STING Activation.The bimetallic nanovaccine induces pyroptosis
  through both classical (caspase-1/GSDMD) and nonclassical (caspase-3/GSDME) pathways,
  releasing immunogenic factors (HMGB1, CRT) and exposing cytosolic dsDNA. The dsDNA,
  together with Mn²⁺ released from the nanovaccine, activates the cGAS-STING pathway,
  enhancing type I interferon production. This positive feedback loop amplifies DC
  maturation, CTL priming, and antitumor immunity
