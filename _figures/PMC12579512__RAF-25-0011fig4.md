caption: Graphical depiction of the classical map kinase (MAPK) pathway and its proposed
  signaling cascade in the morula and blastocyst stage embryos. At each stage, the
  genes representing messages that were increased or decreased (FDR < 0.05) in the
  FLI-supplemented group are indicated. At the morula stage, an increase in FGFR expression
  leads to an increase in DUSPs, negative regulators of the ERK pathway. The downstream
  increase in ELK and subsequently FOS drives proliferation and differentiation at
  this stage. At the blastocyst stage, an increase in endogenous FGF4 drives a signaling
  cascade characterized by a decrease in both PKA and ERK, possibly driven by the
  increase in DUSPs at the earlier stage. The decrease in ERK leads to decreased expression
  of both MNK and MYC, but no difference in CREB or FOS, both of which drive proliferation
  and differentiation of the cells
