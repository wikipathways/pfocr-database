caption: Role of PI3K/Akt/mTOR, Wnt/β-catenin, and JAK/STAT pathways in RCC. PI3K/AKT/mTOR
  Pathway; HOTAIR, MALAT1, GAS5, and LINC00460 modulate this pathway to either promote
  or inhibit RCC progression. For example, HOTAIR and MALAT1 enhance tumor growth
  and metastasis, whereas GAS5 acts as a suppressor that indirectly enhances the expression
  of PTEN by directly inhibiting the expression of miR-21a-5p. WNT/β-catenin Pathway;
  HOTAIR, MALAT1, and TUG1 activate this pathway, promoting cell proliferation and
  migration. Conversely, OTUD6B-AS1 inhibits WNT/β-catenin signaling, reducing tumor
  aggressiveness. JAK/STAT Pathway; SNHG1 influences immune escape and tumor microenvironment
  through STAT activation. SNHG1 enhances immune evasion via the STAT3/PD-L1 axis
