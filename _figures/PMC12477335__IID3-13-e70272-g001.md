caption: Functions of NOD2 during mycobacterial infection. (1) Macrophages/monocytes
  phagocytose mycobacteria, which are digested into MDP following the fusion of phagosomes
  and lysosomes. NOD2 recognizes MDP and produces effectors (TNF, IL‐1β, IL‐6, IL‐12,
  IL‐32, type I IFNs, and NO) via the RIP2–NF‐κB/MAPK/IRF5 pathway. MDP‐dependent
  IL‐32 induces the rapid differentiation of monocytes into DCs. (2) NOD2‐dependent
  autophagy reduces the survival of hidden mycobacteria in MSCs. (3) The NOD2 and
  TLR4 signaling pathways enhance T‐cell initiation in DCs. (4) BCG vaccines enhances
  cellular bactericidal efficacy through NOD2‐trained immunity
