caption: Hypothetical enniatin biosynthetic pathway. The first step involves the desamination
  of l‐valine to 2‐ketoisovaleric acid, followed by its NAD(P)H‐dependent reduction
  to d‐hydroxyisovaleric acid catalyzed by the ketoisovalerate reductase (KivR) enzyme,
  encoded by the kivr gene. d‐Hydroxyisovalerate is then activated and incorporated
  by the nonribosomal peptide synthetase (NRPS) ESYN1, which consists of two modules.
  Each module contains a set of functional domains, including adenylation (A), thiolation
  (T), condensation (C), N‐methyltransferase (M), and a final cyclization (C3) domain.
  These modules enable the stepwise assembly of the hexadepsipeptide compound. Adapted
  from Richter et al. (2014), Süssmuth et al. (2010), and Gautier et al. (2020)
