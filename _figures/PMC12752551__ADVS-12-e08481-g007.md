caption: Schematic illustration of RFWD3‐mediated pro‐tumor immunity. (Left) In lung
  epithelial cells exposed to clean air, TREX1 undergoes TRIM24‐mediated proteasomal
  degradation, leading to accumulation of dsDNA, activation of cGAS‐STING signaling,
  and production of IFN‐I, such as IFNβ. (Middle) Tobacco and PM2.5 induce upregulation
  of RFWD3 via the BaP‐AhR pathway, resulting in stabilization of TREX1, inactivation
  of cGAS‐STING signaling, upregulation of PD‐L1,[ 37 ] and an immunosuppressive tumor
  microenvironment. (Right) Inhibition of RFWD3 restores TREX1 degradation, dsDNA
  accumulation, cGAS‐STING activation, and IFN production. Combined use of RFWD3 inhibition
  and ICI exerts synergistic therapeutic efficacy. Created with BioRender.com
