caption: A proposed cooperative model of PagDMG6341, PagWD40, and PagPOLD4 in the
  base excision repair (BER) pathway of ‘84K’ poplar under 5‐AU stress. (A) During
  the S phase, 5‐AU, as a pyrimidine base analog, is incorporated into newly synthesised
  DNA strands, resulting in mismatched base pairs, single‐base damage, and replication
  arrest. (B) PagDMG6341 recognises the mismatched base and recruits PagWD40, initiating
  the BER process. (C) PagDMG6341 excises the mismatched base. (D) PagPOLD4, a component
  responsible for repairing AP sites, is recruited by PagWD40
