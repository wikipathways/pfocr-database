caption: Model of Hippo kinase LATS1-dependent regulation of IFN-I in cytosolic nucleic
  acid sensing PRR signaling pathways. Under basal conditions, YAP operates as a steady-state
  negative regulator of TBK1-IRF3 signaling in part by associating with TBK1. Upon
  the detection of RNA or DNA virus genomes by distinct cytosolic PRRs, the Hippo
  kinase LATS1 undergoes phosphorylation to trigger the phospho-inhibition of YAP,
  abrogating its ability to antagonize TBK1. Activated LATS1 further associates with
  TBK1 and licenses TBK1 dependent signaling to IRF3, resulting in the subsequent
  induction of IFN-I. Figure created with BioRender
