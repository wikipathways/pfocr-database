caption: 'Proposed Mechanism for TWP-Mediated Reversal of Cisplatin Resistance in
  Ovarian Cancer. The alkaloid components of TWP act as multi-target inhibitors, engaging
  upstream regulators including EGFR, Cytokine Receptors (via JAK1/JAK2), and the
  critical signal integrator PTPN11/SHP2. This coordinated inhibition blocks signal
  transduction through three major oncogenic pathways: the JAK-STAT, RAS-ERK-MAPK,
  and PI3K-AKT cascades. Consequently, this leads to the suppression of pro-survival
  outputs such as proliferation and stemness, while promoting apoptosis, ultimately
  restoring cellular sensitivity to cisplatin'
