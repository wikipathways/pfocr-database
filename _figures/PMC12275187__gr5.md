caption: Proposed synthetic mechanism for CPG2. Based on previous genetic (6, 12)
  and biochemical (13) studies, we propose the following pathway for CPG2 synthesis.
  CpgB functions as a ceramide kinase that uses ATP and ceramide to produce ceramide
  1-phosphate (C1P). CgpC, through a yet unidentified mechanism, converts C1P to CPG.
  CpgD uses CTP and phosphoglycerate to produce CDP-glycerate, which is then a substrate
  for CpgA to convert CPG to CPG2. Steps supported by biochemical evidence are highlighted
  in magenta, and those with genetic evidence are highlighted in green
