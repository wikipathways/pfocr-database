caption: Proposed model of HMGB1 signal transduction pathway in cardiovascular diseases.
  Extracellular dsHMGB1 binds to the membrane receptors TLR4 and RAGE expressed on
  cells such as monocytes, endothelial cells, and vascular smooth muscle cells. Binding
  of dsHMGB1 to TLR4 activates the MyD88-dependent pathway, leading to NF-κB activation,
  and involves interaction with MD-2 to form the HMGB1/TLR4/MD-2 complex. Similarly,
  dsHMGB1 engagement with RAGE triggers activation of MAPK pathways. These signaling
  pathways induce the production of pro-inflammatory cytokines (e.g., TNF-α, IL-1α,
  IL-1β, IL-6, and IFN-γ), thereby contributing to inflammatory heart disease. In
  contrast, the extracellular frHMGB1/CXCL12 complex binds to CXCR4 receptors, primarily
  expressed on mesenchymal stem cells and leukocytes, promoting inflammatory cell
  recruitment to damaged sites and facilitating tissue repair. CXCL12, C-X-C motif
  chemokine ligand 12; CXCR4, C-X-C motif chemokine receptor 4; dsHMGB1, disulfide
  high mobility group box 1; frHMGB1, fully reduced high mobility group box 1; IL-1α,
  interleukin-1α IL-1β, interleukin-1β IL-6, interleukin-6; IFN-γ, interferon-γ MAPK,
  mitogen-activated protein kinases; MD-2, myeloid differentiation factor 2; MyD88,
  Myeloid differentiation primary response 88; NF-κB, nuclear factor-κB; RAGE, receptor
  for advanced glycation end-products; TLR4, toll-like receptor 4; TNF-α, tumor necrosis
  factor-α
