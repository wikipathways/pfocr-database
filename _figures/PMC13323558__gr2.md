caption: 'Reciprocal signaling mechanisms driving neurogenesis and nerve–tumor crosstalk
  in lung cancer. (A) Neurotrophic induction of tumor progression: Lung cancer cells
  secrete neurotrophins (e.g., NGF, BDNF) that bind to cognate receptors (such as
  TrkA and TrkB) on peripheral nerve terminals. This signaling not only triggers axonogenesis
  and nerve infiltration, but also activates paracrine loops that promote tumor cell
  proliferation and angiogenesis. (B) Dysregulation of axon guidance cues in NSCLC:
  Downregulation of the chemorepellent SEMA3A dismantles the endogenous inhibitory
  barrier, thereby permitting the unrestricted ingress of sympathetic and parasympathetic
  fibers into the tumor bed. Concurrently, overexpression of Netrin-1 orchestrates
  a dual-role mechanism: it guides nerve terminal extension via DCC and UNC5 receptors
  while simultaneously enhancing the invasive and migratory capacity of NSCLC cells.
  These effects are mediated through the activation of PI3K/AKT and ERK-driven epithelial–EMT
  pathways. (C) Synaptic hijacking in SCLC: SCLC cells exhibit neuroendocrine plasticity,
  enabling the formation of functional, synapse-like structures with infiltrating
  neurons. By hijacking synaptic neurotransmission mediated by NMDA and GABA(A) receptors,
  SCLC cells convert neural electrical and chemical signals into metabolic and proliferative
  advantages, fueling aggressive tumor progression. AKT, Protein kinase B; BDNF, Brain-derived
  neurotrophic factor; DCC, Deleted in colorectal carcinoma; EMT, Epithelial-mesenchymal
  transition; ERK, Extracellular signal-regulated kinase; GABA, Gamma-aminobutyric
  acid; GABAAR, Gamma-aminobutyric acid type A receptor; NGF, Nerve growth factor;
  NMDA, N-methyl-D-aspartate; NMDAR, N-methyl-D-aspartate receptor; NSCLC, Non-small
  cell lung cancer; p75NTR, p75 neurotrophin receptor; PI3K, Phosphoinositide 3-kinase;
  SCLC, Small cell lung cancer; SEMA3A, Semaphorin 3A; TrkA, Tropomyosin receptor
  kinase A; TrkB, Tropomyosin receptor kinase B; UNC5, Uncoordinated-5'
