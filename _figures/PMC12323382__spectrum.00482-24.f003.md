caption: 'The potential pathway by which gut microbes and metabolites influence atherosclerosis.
  ①②③④TMAO, LPS, and other metabolites primarily regulate the signaling pathways including
  JNK, NLRP3, ER-stress, and mitochondrial oxidation, resulting in the production
  of inflammatory factors and adhesion molecules that exacerbate disease progression.
  On the contrary, metabolites such as short-chain fatty acids (SCFAs) yield opposite
  effects⑤⑥⑦. Additionally, bile acids like CDCA and UDCA regulate the TGR5 signaling
  pathway to suppress inflammatory responses⑧. Notes: SCFAs, short chain fatty acids;
  TMAO, trimethylamino oxide; LPS, lipopolysaccharide; CDCA, chenodeoxycholic acid;
