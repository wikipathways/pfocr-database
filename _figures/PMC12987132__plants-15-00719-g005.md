caption: 'The schematic highlights differences in physiological regulation related
  to growth-associated and stress-related traits across environmental gradients. (a)
  Carex parvula, (b) Carex moorcroftii, and (c) Poa litwinowiana. Each species exhibits
  distinct regulatory patterns along the stress–growth trade-off continuum under combined
  warming and drought conditions. The diagram highlights three major physiological
  pathways: ROS scavenging, osmotic adjustment, and hormonal signaling. Within each
  pathway, the numbers in parentheses (e.g., (1), (2)) represent the number of response
  indicators (e.g., proline, soluble sugars, specific hormones, antioxidant enzymes)
  whose variation is predominantly explained by temperature (green arrows) or precipitation
  (yellow arrows), based on sensitivity analysis. Arrows represent directional responses
  to environmental drivers, with green pathways primarily induced by warming and yellow
  pathways by drought'
