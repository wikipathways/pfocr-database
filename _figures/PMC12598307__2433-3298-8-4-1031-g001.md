caption: 'Effects of visual conditions and ON/OFF pathway alterations on axial elongation.
  This schematic illustrates how visual and genetic factors disrupt ON/OFF signaling
  and promote axial elongation. Hyperopic defocus impairs ON pathway signaling and
  disrupts feedback control of axial growth. Nyx and Lrit3 mutations impair ON signaling
  and increase myopia susceptibility. Mesopic light sustains rod-driven ON input,
  while high contrast enhances OFF activation. These changes disrupt emmetropization
  feedback and drive ocular elongation.PR: Photoreceptor; OS: outer segment; IS: inner
