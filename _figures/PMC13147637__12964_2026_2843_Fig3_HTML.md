caption: Mitochondria-lysosome interplay and mitochondrial quality control. Mitochondrial
  quality control mechanisms maintain organelle integrity through coordinated processes,
  including mitochondrial dynamics process, proteostasis, and PINK1/Parkin-dependent
  mitophagy, which collectively maintain cellular homeostasis. Recent studies have
  identified alternative monitoring pathways, including PINK1/Parkin-independent mitophagy,
  mitochondrial-derived vesicle (MDV) degradation, mitocytosis, and mitolysosome exocytosis.
  Mitochondrial fusion enables the functional complementation of defective mtDNA and
  proteins by mixing them with healthy mitochondria. Conversely, fission isolates
  damaged segments for selective degradation, preventing the spread of mitochondrial
  dysfunction throughout the mitochondrial network. The canonical mitophagy pathway
  involves PINK1/Parkin-mediated recruitment of autophagy receptors (for instance,
  OPTN and NDP52) to the depolarized mitochondria. These receptors interact with LC3
  proteins to promote the mitochondrial engulfment by autophagosomes. Hypoxic conditions
  activate alternative pathways via FUNDC1- and BNIP3/NIX-mediated mitophagy, thereby
  expanding the regulatory repertoire. VAMP1, an ER protein, participates in autophagosome
  formation by regulating the release of isolation membranes from the ER through its
  interaction with SERCA. Furthermore, MIGA modulates autophagy by regulating the
  fusion of the autophagosome and the lysosome. MDVs selectively transport damaged
  mitochondrial components to lysosomes for their degradation. PINK1-Parkin pathway
  mediates the ubiquitination and phosphorylation of mitochondrial proteins, which
  are then targeted for incorporation into MDVs. DRP1 coordinates vesicle formation,
  whereas the SNARE complex (STX17/SNAP29/VAMP7) promotes subsequent MDV-lysosome
  fusion, exposing the cargo to hydrolytic enzymes. GDAP1-LAMP1 interaction directly
  links mitochondria and lysosomes, whereas MFN2-RAB7 promotes three-dimensional interactions
  between mitochondria, ER, and endosomes. TBC1D15 binds FIS1 to hydrolyze RAB7-GTP,
  disrupting mitochondria-lysosome contacts. Mitochondria marked by Bcl-2-associated
  X protein (Bax) and nipsnap homolog 2 (GBAS) undergo direct lysosomal engulfment,
  a process governed by VAMP2 and STX4
