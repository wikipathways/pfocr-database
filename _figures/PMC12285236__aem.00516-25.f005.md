caption: Regulatory model for intersecting XynR and XylR regulons. Transport of linear
  xylan is performed by XynUVW, catabolism of xylooligosaccharides (XOS) is performed
  by Xyl3A, and then free xylose is converted to xylulose-5-phosphate by the xylose
  isomerase-xylulose pathway. XylR represses the expression of xynUVW and xylA, while
  XynR controls xyl3A and XylB. The interconnected regulation on the four-step transport
  and conversion of XOS highlight the importance of these two transcription factors
  for xylan metabolism
