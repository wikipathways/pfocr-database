caption: Hypoxia-induced epigenetic mechanism driving TM fibrosis in glaucoma. The
  proposed model elucidates the mechanism by which hypoxia may facilitate fibrosis
  within the TM. Under hypoxic conditions, Hypoxia-Inducible Factor 1-Alpha (HIF-1α)
  is stabilized, which has been documented to upregulate the expression of TGF-β1.
  The upregulation of TGF-β1 is correlated with increased expression of DNMT1, leading
  to hypermethylation and subsequent silencing of the anti-fibrotic gene RASAL1. The
  suppression of RASAL1 is hypothesized to result in persistent activation of Ras-GTP
  and heightened cellular proliferation. In conjunction with TGF-β1-induced stimulation
  of ECM deposition, this sequence of events is proposed to culminate in fibrosis
  of the TM, potentially augmenting resistance to aqueous humor outflow and increasing
  IOP. Proposed therapeutic interventions include the use of TGF-β1 small interfering
  RNA (siRNA) or the DNMT inhibitor 5-azacytidine as strategies to mitigate this pro-fibrotic
  pathway
