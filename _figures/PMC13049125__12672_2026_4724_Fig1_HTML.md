caption: Therapeutic landscape and angiogenic mechanisms in gastric cancer. The left
  panel depicts the current clinical management of gastric cancer, which relies on
  systemic chemotherapy (e.g., 5-FU, Capecitabine) and targeted agents like HER2 inhibitors
  (Trastuzumab) and VEGFR inhibitors (Ramucirumab), yet still faces challenges with
  limited survival benefits and high recurrence rates in advanced stages. The right
  panel details the molecular dependence on the VEGF pathway, where tumor cells and
  hypoxic macrophages secrete elevated levels of VEGF-A. This ligand binds to VEGFR-2
  on endothelial cells to drive angiogenesis via PI3K/AKT and MAPK/ERK signaling,
  while simultaneously promoting tumor cell proliferation through autocrine loops
  and triggering epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition (EMT) to facilitate invasion
  and metastasis
