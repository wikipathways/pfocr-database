caption: SynNotch-regulated CAR-NK cells for conditional IL-15 expression. (A) Conventional
  CAR-NK cells constitutively express IL-15 to promote NK cell survival, proliferation,
  and cytotoxic function. However, continuous IL-15 expression can induce off-target
  toxicity due to the undesired activation of host NK cells. (B) SynNotch CAR-NK cells
  use scFv-A to recognize antigen A and mediate cytotoxicity, while scFv-B detects
  antigen B to activate the SynNotch pathway. Upon antigen B binding, a transcription
  factor is released to induce IL-15 expression. Once antigen B is no longer present,
  IL-15 production stops, thereby enhancing safety by limiting cytokine release to
  dual-antigen recognition. scFv, single-chain variable fragment
