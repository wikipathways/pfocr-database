caption: Clinical decision pathway. The DL model based on ResNet18 established in
  this study classifies patients as suitable for hyperfractionated radiotherapy or
  conventional fractionated radiotherapy. For patients identified by the model with
  a score >0.5, the study considers that this group exhibits higher aggressiveness
  and is therefore more suitable for hyperfractionated radiotherapy regimens. For
  patients identified by the model as having a score ≤0.5, the study considers that
  this group exhibits relatively lower aggressiveness, with no significant difference
  between the two treatment regimens. However, conventional radiotherapy demonstrates
  a lower incidence of acute adverse events. BID, twice daily; DL, deep learning;
  QD, once daily
