caption: 'The hexosamine biosynthetic pathway (HBP) relies on carbohydrate, amino
  acid, fatty acid, and nucleotide metabolism in order to produce UDP-GlcNAc which
  is used by O-GlcNAc Transferase (OGT) to catalyse the addition of an O-GlcNAc moiety
  onto a serine/threonine residue. O-GlcNAcase (OGA) hydrolyses O-GlcNAc residues.
  Yellow elements represent a lipid bilayer, whereas the blue, pink, and green elements
  represent transmembrane transport proteins. Enzyme abbreviations include: PGI (phosphoglucose
  isomerase), GFAT1 (glutamine-fructose-6-phosphate transaminase 1), GNK (N-acetylglucosamine
  kinase), GNA1/GNPNA1 (Glucosamine-6-phosphate N-acetyltransferase1), PGM3/AGM1 (phosphoglucomutase
  3), UAP/AGX1 (UDP-N-acetylglucosamine pyrophosphorylase 1), MGAT (alpha-1,3-mannosyl-glycoprotein
  2-beta-N-acetylglucosaminyltransferase). The red arrows represent the HBP, the black
  arrows represent any reactions feeding into or deviating from the HBP'
