caption: Schematic of cucurbitacin B-induced TNF-R1 ectodomain shedding. Multiple
  ADAM proteases cleave full-length TNF-R1 at a membrane-proximal site. The resultant
  soluble TNF-R1, consisting of the extracellular domain, is released into the culture
  medium. The membrane-bound intracellular region of TNF-R1 then appears to undergo
  lysosomal degradation. Cucurbitacin B selectively activates the ERK pathway via
  the RAF1–MEK–ERK axis, while exerting minimal effects on the activation of p38 MAPK
  and JNK. ERK may contribute to the activation of ADAM proteases involved in TNF-R1
  shedding. TNF-R1 ectodomain shedding induced by cucurbitacin B is attenuated by
  the metalloprotease inhibitor TAPI-2 and the MEK inhibitor U0126
