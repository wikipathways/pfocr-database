caption: Gene–metabolite interaction network associated with intramuscular fat (IMF)
  in LYB chicken breast. (A) WGCNA-derived network of key genes and differential metabolites
  significantly correlated with IMF (|r| > 0.6, P < 0.05). Node shape denotes feature
  type (circle, gene; diamond, metabolite; square, KEGG pathway). (B) Glycerophospholipid
  metabolism (Kennedy pathway) with heatmap showing log2-fold change of DEGs in CR
  vs FR comparisons across four ages. (C) Acylcarnitine-mediated mitochondrial fatty
  acid transport and β-oxidation pathway. (D) Signaling pathways involved in glycerophospholipid
  metabolites. Correlation network constructed from WGCNA; DEGs identified by DESeq2
  (Fold change >1.5, adjusted P < 0.05). Metabolite data from OPLS-DA (VIP > 1)
