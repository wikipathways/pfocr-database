caption: HDAC-mediated regulation of secondary-metabolite pathways under external
  stimuli. External stimuli activate intracellular signaling networks. At secondary-metabolite
  gene promoters, HDACs reduce H3/H4 acetylation and repress pathway genes under basal
  conditions, whereas stress or HDAC inhibitor treatment increases histone acetylation
  and de-represses genes involved in phenylpropanoid and terpenoid biosynthesis. SIR2-type
  HDACs also regulate HMGS through enzyme deacetylation, thereby increasing MVA pathway
  flux. HDAC regulation of alkaloid biosynthetic genes remains putative and requires
  further validation
