caption: 'This panel illustrates distinct sphingolipid metabolic perturbations across
  four pathologies: A) CHB:​​ HBV infection induces hepatocyte apoptosis via mitochondrial
  pathways and dysregulates CD8⁺ T-cell responses (IFN-γ/TNF-α), leading to altered
  sphingolipid metabolism (↑SM, ↓Cer) and sustained hepatic inflammation. B) Ctr:​​
  Intracellular Chlamydia effector IncD binds CERT at ER-inclusion membrane contact
  sites, hijacking Cer transport to promote bacterial replication and elevate Cer/Sph
  levels. C) LN:​​ Vascular dysfunction involves RhoA signaling, neutrophil infiltration,
  and complement activation, driving SMase-dependent mitochondrial apoptosis, ROS
  generation, and renal cell death. D) MS:​​ Activated microglia and T cells produce
  ROS via NADPH oxidase, enhancing lipid peroxidation (LPO/F2-isoprostanes) and activating
  aSMase to degrade SM into pro-apoptotic Cer'
