caption: FGG functions as a critical molecule in cholestatic liver fibrosis by driving
  HSCs activation and facilitating M2 macrophage polarization. During cholestasis,
  accumulated bile acids induce HSCs activation, leading to a substantial increase
  in FGG secretion. The released FGG not only further sustains HSC activation in an
  autocrine manner but also acts as a key paracrine signal to polarize macrophages
  toward an M2 phenotype. This FGG-mediated communication establishes a vicious cycle
  where in M2 macrophages and activated HSCs engage in reciprocal crosstalk, collectively
  accelerating fibrotic progression. To therapeutically target this pathway, we developed
  AEAA@LNP-siFGG, a targeted delivery system that markedly enhances siRNA uptake in
  HSCs. Treatment with this system efficiently knocked down FGG expression, resulting
  in suppressed HSCs activation, disrupted profibrotic HSC and macrophage crosstalk,
  and ultimately significant attenuation of liver fibrosis
