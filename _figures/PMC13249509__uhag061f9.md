caption: Metabolic flux regulation downstream of p-coumaroyl-CoA in Lithocarpus litseifolius.
  Metabolic flux was mainly pulled and driven into the DHC pathway through highly
  expressed LlCCR and high catalytic efficiency of phloretin glucosyltransferase in
  tender leaves, and primarily entered into lignin pathway through highly expressed
  LlHCT in growing stems. The flux was indicated by the line width of the arrows.
  HCT, hydroxycinnamoyl-CoA:shikimate/quinate hydroxycinnamoyl transferase; C3H, coumarate
  3 hydroxylase; CSE, caffeoyl shikimate esterase; 4CL, 4-coumaric acid:CoA ligase;
