caption: 'Strategies for the modulation of OsTOC1 transcriptional activity for rice
  breeding.(A) The transcriptional activity of core clock components is influenced
  by multiple factors, including histone modification–driven chromatin remodeling,
  dynamic binding of clock components to promoter regions, pre-mRNA alternative splicing,
  post-translational modifications (e.g., phosphorylation and ubiquitination), and
  protein–cofactor interactions. The thick arrows indicate the steps in each pathway.(B)
  OsTOC1 transcriptional activity can be modulated through precision breeding strategies,
  including domestication and allele screening. Furthermore, integration of artificial
  intelligence–driven algorithms with CRISPR-Cas9 biotechnology enables nucleotide
  sequence optimization for precise engineering of genetic elements to improve OsTOC1
  transcriptional regulation.(C) TOC1 expression can be regulated through two biotechnological
  strategies: promoter substitution via genetic engineering and transcript suppression
  via RNA interference (RNAi).(D)OsTOC1 overexpression lengthens the circadian period,
  whereas Ostoc1 mutants exhibit shortened periods with reduced amplitude. OsTOC1
  activates OsIAA27 and OsTGAL3b in a dose-dependent manner, potentially promoting
  rice growth and pathogen resistance. It may also indirectly integrate sugar and
  strigolactone signaling via OsCCA1 to modulate plant architecture, including tiller
  number. Therefore, OsTOC1 not only sustains circadian oscillations but also regulates
  agronomic traits. Consequently, the identification of OsTOC1 variants with optimal
  activity supports the improvement of rice breeding strategies'
