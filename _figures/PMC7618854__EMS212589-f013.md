caption: Bone metastases exhibit enhanced glycolysis, tricarboxylic acid (TCA) cycle
  and pentose phosphate pathway (PPP) activity, which is in part supported by bone-marrow
  adipocytes. These adipocytes also provide lipids and promote fatty acid oxidation
  (FAO) in cancer cells. To successfully progress as a metastasis, cancer cells need
  to remodel the extracellular matrix (ECM), either by hydroxyapatite-activated secretion
  of matrix metalloproteinases (MMP) or activation of osteoclasts by serine and lactate.
  GLUT1, Glucose transporter 1; HK2, Hexokinase 2; ENO2, Enolase 2; PSAT1, Phosphoserine

  protein kinase; FABP4, Fatty acid binding protein 4; CD36, Cluster of differentiation
  36
