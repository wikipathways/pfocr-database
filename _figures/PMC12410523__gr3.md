caption: This figure illustrate the different targeting and repairing mechanisms mediated
  by the different membrane proteins. Macrophage membrane contains Lymphocyte Function-associated
  Antigen 1 (LFA-1) and Cluster of Differentiation 11b (CD11b) which binds with the
  Intercellular Adhesion Molecule 1 (ICAM 1) and Vascular Cell Adhesion Molecule 1
  (VCAM 1) (A). Neutrophil membrane possesses C-X-C Motif Chemokine Receptor 2 (CXCR2)
  which binds with the C-X-C Motif Chemokine Ligand 2 (CXCL2) and C—C Motif Chemokine
  Receptor 2 (CCR2) which binds with C—C Motif Chemokine Ligand 2 (CCL2) (B). Platelet
  membrane surface protein P-selectin interacts with P-selectin glycoprotein ligand-1
  (PSGL-1) at the inflamed site and platelet membrane also upregulates mucin protein
  (MUC 2) which restores the mucus membrane (C). CD44 present on the stem cell membrane
  directly interacts with the inflamed site. Stem cells release growth factors like
  Wnt proteins, epidermal growth factor (EGF) that activate the Wnt/β-Catenin Pathway
  and mucin protein (MUC-1) that leads to the epithelial regeneration (D). Bacterial
  membrane contains lipoproteins (LP) & lipoteichoic acid (LTA) which are recognized
  by toll-like receptor (TLR 2, TLR 6) (E)
