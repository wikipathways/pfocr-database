caption: UBASH3B mediated tumor suppressive signaling. Loss of UBASH3B leads to increased
  phosphorylation of CBL, relieving its inhibition and promoting receptor tyrosine
  kinase (RTK) signaling. UBASH3B deficiency dephosphorylates the BCR-ABL fusion protein
  (p210), contributing to oncogenic signaling in leukemia. The p210 fusion protein
  also triggers downstream signaling pathways, including JAK/STAT, PI3K/Akt/mTOR,
  and Ras/Raf/MEK/ERK. UBASH3B loss also disrupts its interaction with MRPL12, impairing
  mitochondrial metabolic regulation. Collectively, these alterations drive cancer
  progression by enhancing UBASH3B tumor suppressive signaling. Figure created with
