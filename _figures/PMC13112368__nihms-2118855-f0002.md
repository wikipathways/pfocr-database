caption: Cellular signaling pathways that have or are being evaluated as targetable
  in FP-RMS include those active at the plasma membrane, including RTKs and downstream
  signaling members, organic cation transporters (OCTs) that facilitate metformin
  transport, and developmental pathway receptors for TGF-beta, Smoothened, Notch,
  and Wnt. In principle, PAX3::FOXO1 activity itself can be modified by targeting
  epigenetic modifiers, transcriptional inhibitors, enzymes that impact PAX3::FOXO1
  post-translational modifications, or DNA damage/repair pathways. Finally, PAX3::FOXO1
  mRNA or protein could in principle be directly targeted for degradation by siRNAs/ASOs
  or PROTACs/bioPROTACs, respectively. Transcriptional targets of PAX3::FOXO1 can
  also be targeted, although to date they have not shown durable activity. Created
  in BioRender. Turco, G. (2025) https://BioRender.com/ty4sufs
