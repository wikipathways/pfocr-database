caption: Antitumor activity of natural compounds in HNC. This figure illustrates the
  mechanisms by which phytotherapeutic agents induce autophagy, pyroptosis, necroptosis,
  and cytotoxic effects in cancer cells. Zanthoxylum bungeanum and Zingiber officinale
  promote autophagy by modulating LC3-I/II conversion and key regulators like ATG14L,
  Vps34, and BECLIN1. Acetyl-11-keto-beta-boswellic acid also contributes to this
  process. Various caspases (caspase-4/5/11, caspase-1, caspase-8, and caspase-3)
  mediate pyroptosis via gasdermin proteins (GSDMD, GSDMC, and GSDME). Triptolide
  and neobractatin enhance GSDME-mediated pyroptosis. Tumor necrosis factor (TNF)
  signaling through TNFR1 and downstream molecules (TRADD, RIP1, RIP3, MLKL) triggers
  necroptosis. Shikonin promotes this cell death pathway.Various plant-derived compounds
  (e.g., pinicol B, Momordica charantia, Berberis aristata, sulforaphane, Cratoxylum
  formosum, and Dioscorea sansibarensis) exhibit cytotoxic activity against cancer
  cells, targeting multiple oncogenic pathways
