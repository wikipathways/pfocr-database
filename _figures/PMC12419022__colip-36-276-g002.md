caption: Schematic depiction of the SREBP pathway and its activation. (Left) SREBP
  and Scap are retained in the ER membrane by INSIG when the ER membrane cholesterol-content
  is high. This limits transcription of SREBP target genes. (Right) A drop in the
  cholesterol content of the ER membrane leads to release of INSIG from the SREBP-Scap
  complex, and to COPII-mediated anterograde transport of the complex to the Golgi.
  In the Golgi, SREBP undergoes sequential cleavage by S1P, a step licensed by SPRING,
  and by S2P. The released transcriptionally competent N-terminal domain translocates
  to the nucleus where it activates the transcription of SRE-containing genes
