caption: 'Lysosome serves as a cellular degradative endpoint. Multiple vesicle trafficking
  processes terminate at lysosomes through oncoming vesicle fusion, including autophagy,
  endocytosis, LC3-associated endocytosis, phagocytosis and crinophagy. Few other
  processes such as microautophagy and chaperone-mediated autophagy (CMA) directly
  capture cargo without vesicle fusion. A list of degradative cargo for each process
  is listed to the right. In macroautophagy, the microtubule associated protein LC3
  is conjugated to the double membranes (outer and inner) of the autophagosome, while
  in LAP and LANDO, LC3 conjugation happens only on the cytosolic face of the single
  membrane of LAPosome/LANDOsomes. Autophagosomes engulf primarily cytosolic contents
  including mitochondria/ER with or without the help of selective autophagy receptors
  (selective or bulk autophagy, respectively). LAP and LANDO engulf extracellular
  material such as pathogens, apoptotic debris often via specialized phagocytic receptors.
  Endocytosis uptakes either extracellular bulk fluid phase cargo via the plasma membrane,
  or it can be receptor-mediated (selective). An early-to-late endosome switch coincides
  with a change in tubulovesicular morphology, increased acidification, swapping of
  Rab proteins (Rab5→Rab7) and membrane tethers (CORVET→HOPS), and formation of ILVs
  with the help of ESCRT complex. ILVs act as a sink for lysosomal hydrolases and
  cholesterol coming via the endocytic pathway, and can be secreted extracellularly
  as exosomes. In microautophagy, smaller cargo is directly internalized by the lysosomal
  membrane, which can include mitochondria-derived vesicles, micronucleus and lipid
  droplets. In CMA, KFERQ-motif containing proteins are bound to the chaperone protein
  Hsc70 and internalized by LAMP2A. Immature secretory granules undergo homotypic
  fusion to produce mature granules which are either secreted, undergo macrosecretophagy
  (autophagic degradation of secretory granules) or, in the crinophagy pathway, directly
  fuse with lysosomes to form crinosomes for degradation. Abbreviations: AP: autophagosome,
  EE: early endosome, Hsc70: Heat Shock Cognate 70 kDa, iSG: immature secretory granule,
  KFERQ: Lys-Phe-Glu-Arg-Gln, LANDO: LC3-associated endocytosis, LAP: LC3-associated
  phagocytosis, LD: lipid droplets, LE: late endosome, Lys: lysosome, MDV: mitochondria-derived
  vesicle, MN: micronucleus, mSG: mature secretory granule, MVB: multivesicular body,
  Ub: ubiquitin'
