  pathway of GPCR mediated by G proteins involves a conformational change of G proteins
  and subsequent release of GDP upon activation by agonists. This event facilitates
  the rapid binding of GTP to G proteins, resulting in the dissociation of Gα subunits
  from both the receptor and Gβγ subunits. Active Gα subunits further activate various
  intracellular signaling pathways, including the adenylyl cyclase pathway and changes
  in intracellular Ca2+ concentration, leading to diverse cellular biological effects
