caption: Schematic overview of the proposed XoxF-CBB pathway for methanol metabolism
  in Bradyrhizobium and Sinorhizobium. La3+ is transported into the cell and complexes
  with apo-XoxF. XoxF (pink) oxidizes methanol to formaldehyde in the periplasm. Formaldehyde
  is oxidized to formate in the cytoplasm by the multi-step GSH-linked oxidation pathway
  (brown). Formate is oxidized to CO2 by an NAD+-dependent formate dehydrogenase (red).
  CO2 can be assimilated via the Calvin-Benson cycle (green). For clarity, only the
  most relevant steps of the CBB cycle are depicted. Dashed lines represent hypothesized
  steps based on similarity with other systems. Solid lines show experimentally supported
  steps from this study. XoxF, lanthanide-dependent methanol dehydrogenase; Gfa, glutathione-dependent
  formaldehyde activating enzyme; FrmA, S-(hydroxymethyl)glutathione dehydrogenase;
  FrmB, S-formylglutathione hydrolase; FDH, NAD+-dependent formate dehydrogenase;
  3PGA, 3-phosphoglycerate; G3P, glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate; RuBP, ribulose 1,5-bisphosphate.
