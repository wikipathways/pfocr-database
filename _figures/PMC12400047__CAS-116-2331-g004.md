caption: SETDB1‐mediated gene regulation. SETDB1 stabilized by ATF7IP and works with
  TRIM28 for H3K9me3 mediated gene suppression (top panel). In SETDB1‐depleted cells,
  aberrant activation of endogenous retroviral sequences (ERVs) leads to the generation
  of double‐stranded RNAs or DNA (dsRNA/dsDNA), resulting in the activation of the
  cGAS‐STING pathway or Mda5/RIG‐I‐like receptor (RLR) signaling pathway. This promotes
  type I interferon‐driven inflammatory responses and interferon‐stimulated genes,
  eventually leading to apoptosis. Figures were created with BioRender.com
