caption: 'This study uncovers the therapeutic mechanism of RRx-001 in hepatocellular
  carcinoma (HCC): by inhibiting G6PD, it reduces NADPH levels, leading to redox imbalance
  and F-actin contraction, thereby inducing disulfidptosis—a process that can be partially
  reversed by TCEP. Meanwhile, RRx-001 promotes the release of DAMPs, activating immunogenic
  cell death (ICD). In vivo, RRx-001 significantly inhibits tumor growth, enhances
  T-cell infiltration, promotes M1 macrophage polarization, downregulates PD-L1 expression,
  and strengthens anti-tumor immunity through T cell-related pathways. With both metabolic
  and immunomodulatory effects, RRx-001 provides a basis for novel HCC therapies,
  and future research could explore its synergistic effects with immune checkpoint
  inhibitors'
