caption: Diagram of the primary molecular mechanism through which NaB inhibits CRC.
  On the left, butyrate induces a phenotypic shift in macrophages from an M2-like
  to an M1-like phenotype and downregulates the expression of PD-L1+ macrophages.
  On the right, the TLR/MyD88 signaling pathway plays a crucial role in the modulation
  of macrophage polarization. Activated TLR4 binds to MyD88, stimulating the activation
  of IκB kinase (IKK) and subsequently leading to the activation of the NF-κB signaling
  pathway. This reversal facilitates the binding of tumor-associated antigens to T
  cells, leading to T cell activation through antigen-presenting cells. The activated
  T cells release various cytokines, which recruit macrophages to attack tumor cells,
  thereby exerting an anticancer effect
