caption: Lactylation regulates key functional pathways in neutrophils. H3K18la enhances
  the transcription of MICU3, and the resulting MICU3–VDAC1 interaction promotes mitochondrial
  autophagy, activating neutrophil extracellular trap (NET) formation. In hypoxic
  inflammatory lesions, increased glycolytic activity in CD71⁺ neutrophils elevates
  lactate accumulation and lysine lactylation (Kla) levels, which upregulate Arg1
  expression and suppress T cell activation by depleting local arginine. Moreover,
  activation of the G protein–coupled receptor GPR37 elevates H3K18la levels, inducing
  the expression of chemokines CXCL1 and CXCL5, which recruit neutrophils to inflammatory
  sites and amplify local inflammation
