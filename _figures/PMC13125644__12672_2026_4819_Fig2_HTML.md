caption: Regulation of TGF-β/Smad signaling modulated by GM3 ganglioside. Schematic
  representation showing the TGF-β/Smad signaling pathway and its modulation by the
  ganglioside GM3. Upon ligand-receptor interaction, TGF-β receptor type II (TGF-β
  RII) recruits and phosphorylates of TGF-β receptor I (TGF-β RI/ALK5), leads to the
  activation of SMAD2 and SMAD3. SMAD2/3 form a complex with SMAD4, and translocate
  into the nucleus where they regulate the key transcription factors such as snail,
  slug, twist and ZEB1/ZEB2 that drive EMT and ultimately involved in cell-migration,
  invasion, and metastasis. Modified Images provided by Servier Medical Art (https://smart.servier.com),
