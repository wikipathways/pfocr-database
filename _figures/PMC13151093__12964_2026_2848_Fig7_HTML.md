caption: Working model diagram. Cisplatin can induce senescenc of NPC cells, thereby
  recruiting NK cells and inhibiting their function and the tumor cell killing mediated
  by them, thereby promoting the progression of NPC cells. Mechanistically, cisplatin-induced
  senescence of NPC cells upregulates EBI3 expression through the mtDNA-cGAS-STING
  signaling pathway, thereby promoting IL-35 secretion to activate the STAT1-NKG2A
  signaling axis and ultimately inhibiting NK cell-mediated anti-tumor immunity. Therefore,
  targeting EBI3 is expected to improve the efficiency of cisplatin for NPC and provide
  a new approach for the treatment of NPC (Created in https://BioRender.com)
