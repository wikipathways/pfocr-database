caption: circRNA-mediated regulatory networks involved in hepatocellular carcinoma
  (HCC). A Schematic representation of the circRNA-miRNA signaling axes converging
  on the PI3K/AKT/mTOR pathway in HCC. Dysregulated circRNAs, such as circ100381 and
  circ103489, were upregulated, whereas circ101764 was downregulated, collectively
  modulating members of the miR-181 family and influencing PI3K/AKT signaling. The
  circRNA circZKSCAN1 encodes a secretory peptide (circZKSaa) that negatively regulates
  mTOR, thereby linking circRNA translation to metabolic signaling control. In parallel,
  the circCCAR1/miR-127-5p/WTAP axis, facilitated by hnRNPA2B1-dependent circRNA export,
  contributes to immune evasion by stabilizing PD1 expression in CD8+ T-cells, thereby
  promoting resistance to immunotherapy. B Oncogenic circRNAs, including circ0001459,
  circ104348, circ0051040, and circRHOT1, promote HCC progression through distinct
  regulatory axes involving miR-6165-IGF1R, miR-187-3p-RTKN2, Wnt/β-catenin signaling,
  miR-569-ITGAV-mediated EMT, and NR2F6 activation. In contrast, the tumor-suppressive
  circRNA circITCH inhibits proliferation, migration, and invasion by sponging miR-184
  expression. Together, these networks highlight the context-dependent oncogenic and
  suppressive roles of circRNAs in HCC biology and therapeutic resistance
