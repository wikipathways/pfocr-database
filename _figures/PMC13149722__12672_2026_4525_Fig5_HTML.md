caption: Mechanisms of dietary polyphenols in modulating glycolipid metabolism to
  suppress HCC. This schematic illustrates the coordinated mechanisms through which
  dietary polyphenols concurrently regulate the interconnected glucose and lipid metabolic
  pathways to suppress hepatocellular carcinoma. Polyphenols ameliorate glucose metabolism
  by enhancing insulin sensitivity through the IRS/PI3K/AKT signaling axis and inhibiting
  gluconeogenesis via downregulation of G6Pase and PEPCK. Concurrently, they reshape
  lipid homeostasis by activating AMPK/SIRT1 to suppress SREBP-1c-mediated lipogenesis
  and key enzymes including ACC and FASN, while promoting fatty acid β-oxidation through
  the PPARα/CPT-1 pathway. Additional modulation of cholesterol metabolism occurs
  through FXR activation and LDLR upregulation. Collectively, these multi-targeted
  actions restore metabolic balance, further supported by the activation of the Nrf2-mediated
  antioxidant response and suppression of NF-κB/NLRP3-driven inflammation, ultimately
  inhibiting HCC progression
