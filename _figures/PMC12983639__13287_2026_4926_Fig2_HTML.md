caption: Mechanisms of stem cell-based therapies for alopecia areata. a Inhibit inflammation
  and oxidative stress. Stem cells can not only inhibit the infiltration of inflammatory
  cells and the secretion of pro-inflammatory cytokines, but also reduce the production
  of ROS by enhancing the activity of antioxidant enzymes and secreting antioxidant
  proteins. b Regulate immune responses. Stem cells have potent immunomodulatory effects
  on both innate and adaptive immune cells; they can regulate the proliferation and
  function of immune cells through cell-to-cell contact and paracrine activity. c
  Regulate the hair follicle cycle. Stem cells can activate the Wnt/β-catenin signaling
  pathway and promote the proliferation of HFSCs by secreting a variety of bioactive
  molecules, promoting the transition from telogen to anagen phase and the regeneration
  of hair follicles. d Promote angiogenesis. Stem cells can promote scalp angiogenesis
  by secreting various growth factors, which increase the blood supply to the hair
  follicle, thus promoting hair regeneration. (Created in https://BioRender.com).
  ROS reactive oxygen species, PGE2 prostaglandin E2, VEGF vascular endothelial growth
  factor, IGF-1 insulin-like growth factor-1, HFSCs hair follicle stem cells, FGF
  fibroblast growth factor, HGF hepatocyte growth factor, PDGF platelet-derived growth
  factor
