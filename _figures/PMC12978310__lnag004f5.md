caption: Endocrine regulation of liver regeneration via metabolic and cytokine-mediated
  signaling. Norepinephrine released from sympathetic neurons and the adrenal medulla
  promotes hepatocyte proliferation indirectly by activating α1-AR and enhancing HGF
  and EGF synthesis. Insulin enters the liver through the portal vein and supports
  regeneration by enhancing hepatocyte lipid accumulation via the Akt-SREBP1c pathway,
  thereby providing metabolic energy for proliferation. Adiponectin secreted by adipose
  tissue improves hepatic insulin sensitivity and promotes STAT3 activation, facilitating
  regeneration. Transient hepatic steatosis during early liver regeneration results
  from adipose-derived fat uptake, providing ATP through fatty acid oxidation to fuel
  regenerative processes. Duodenum-derived EGF reaches the liver via portal circulation
  and stimulates hepatocyte proliferation. α1-AR, α1-adrenergic receptors; HGF, hepatocyte
  growth factor; EGF, epidermal growth factor; STAT3, signal transducer and activator
  of transcription 3; IRS, insulin receptor substrates. The figure was created with
