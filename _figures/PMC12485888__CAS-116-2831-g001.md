caption: Mechanism of TP‐0903 action in MDS‐L. In MDS‐L, TP‐0903 induced G2/M arrest
  via DNA damage response and TGF‐β/SMAD3 signaling. Aurora A dephosphorylation reduces
  Cyclin A/CDK1 and BORA expression and suppresses PLK1 activation. Inhibition of
  the PI3K/AKT and JAK/STAT3 pathways further contributed to reduced cell proliferation
  and apoptosis, partly by decreasing their interactions with MET. Gene profiling
  showed the upregulation of HMOX1 and transferrin, along with ROS and Fe2+ accumulation,
  suggesting HMOX1‐driven activation of an atypical ferroptosis pathway
