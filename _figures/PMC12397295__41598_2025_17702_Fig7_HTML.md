caption: Scheme showing the localization of CTL1, the metabolic pathway of choline
  and the mechanism of the choline uptake inhibitor hemicolinium-3 (HC-3) in human
  OSCC cells. Proteins are biosynthesized in the rough ER and Golgi apparatus in the
  cell and transported to other organelles and the plasma membrane via vesicular transport,
  membrane contact, and other pathways. In contrast, the synthesis and transport of
  CTL1 increases in cancer cells with active cell division and cell proliferation,
  suggesting that the Kennedy pathway, which synthesizes large amounts of intracellular
  choline into PtdCho and sphingomyelin, which are components of the cell membrane,
  is promoted. Furthermore, choline needs to pass through the mitochondria in order
