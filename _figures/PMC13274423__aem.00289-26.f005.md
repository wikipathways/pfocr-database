caption: '(A) Proposed biosynthetic pathway for brGDGT synthesis via brGDGT and brGTGT
  analogs. (i) DAG formation and headgroup addition: monomers of iso-C15-based DAG
  with or without a Phosphoglycerol (PG) head group attached would be condensed by
  the activity of Mss (ii) during the MSL formation. The detection of brGTGT analogs
  suggests that the activity of Mss occurs before or after the formation of ether
  bonds (iii), and that it could either be interrupted or proceed after the etherification
  process or happen simultaneously. We speculate that the etherification process (iii)
  proceeds via the activity of PlsA and/or Ger (see main text for details). Branched
  GDGTs, such as brGDGT-Ia or brGTGT-Ia, have not been detected in the analyzed samples,
  but we speculate here that they could be formed by the subsequent activity of Ger
  from brGDGT analog 3, or the brGTGT analogs detected by the activity of a yet-unknown
  enzyme (indicated as a question mark). We also propose an alternative pathway (B)
  starting with DAG formation and headgroup addition (ii), followed by etherification
  reactions (iii) on iso-C15-based DAG monomers, which would then be condensed via
  Mss (iv). Here, we have drawn all brGDGT analogs with an anti-parallel configuration,
  but it should be noted that we have not determined whether this is the correct configuration.
  For all compounds, R = PG head group. The blue and pink backgrounds indicate the
  brGDGT and brGTGT analogs detected in this study, being those with the blue background
  the ones with MSL formation, while the ones with a pink background have experienced
  ether lipid reduction to a certain extent'
