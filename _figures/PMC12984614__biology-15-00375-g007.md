caption: Transcriptomic analysis of the phenylpropanoid and lignin biosynthesis pathways
  under salt stress in Brassica napus is presented. The diagram illustrates the metabolic
  pathway from phenylalanine and tyrosine to major phenylpropanoid derivatives, including
  monolignols and lignin subunits (H, G, and S lignins), under control (CK) and salt
  stress conditions. Gene expression changes are depicted as z-score-based heatmaps
  for each annotated gene, with red indicating upregulation and blue indicating downregulation
  in response to salt stress treatment. Key enzymes, such as phenylalanine ammonia-lyase
  (PAL), cinnamate-4-hydroxylase (C4H), 4-coumarate-CoA ligase (4CL), ferulate-5-hydroxylase
  (F5H), caffeic acid O-methyltransferase (COMT), cinnamoyl-CoA reductase (CCR), cinnamyl
  alcohol dehydrogenase (CAD), and peroxidase (PER) genes, exhibit substantial upregulation
  under salt stress. Furthermore, UDP-glucosyltransferase (UGT) genes involved in
  the glycosylation of phenolic compounds also showed enhanced expression. These transcriptional
  changes suggest a stress-induced upregulation of lignin biosynthesis and phenolic
  metabolism, contributing to cell wall reinforcement and mitigating oxidative stress
