caption: Proposed working model for OsMBL1 mediated salt resistance. A working model
  for the involvement of OsRMT1‐OsMBL1 in the salt stress response pathway. Under
  salt stress, the transcription level of OsRMT1 decreases, which provides evidence
  for the rapid up‐regulation of OsMBL1. Consequently, the ubiquitination‐mediated
  degradation of OsMBL1 by OsRMT1 is attenuated, leading to increased interaction
  between OsMBL1 and OsERF040, thereby facilitating the expression of downstream salt‐stress‐related
  genes for regulating rice tolerance to salt stress
