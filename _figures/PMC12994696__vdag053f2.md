caption: 'Schematic of MAPK pathway activation and therapeutic inhibition in NF1/PTPN11-mutated
  glioma. Under physiological conditions, MAPK signaling is tightly regulated. NF1
  negatively regulates RAS activity, and SHP2 (encoded by PTPN11) remains autoinhibited
  until activated by upstream RTKs (left panel). In tumors with NF1 loss and activating
  PTPN11 mutations, RAS becomes constitutively active, leading to sustained MAPK signaling;
  MEK inhibition by trametinib reduces ERK activation and impairs downstream survival
  signaling (right panel). Abbreviations: RTK, receptor tyrosine kinase; MAPK, mitogen-activated
  protein kinase; NF1, neurofibromin 1; PTPN11, protein tyrosine phosphatase non-receptor
  type 11; SHP2, Src homology region 2-containing protein tyrosine phosphatase-2.
