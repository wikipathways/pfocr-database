caption: Proposed model for monocyte differentiation into macrophages versus dendritic
  cells. Monocytes differentiate into macrophages by a default pathway promoted by
  the activation of mTORC1 and MAFB expression. Microbe sensing via TLRs activates
  mTORC1 and favors macrophage differentiation. Upon exposure to TNF, monocytes upregulate
  miR‐155, ETV3 and ETV6 which can actively repress MAFB expression thereby licensing
  monocytes for dendritic cell differentiation. The dendritic cell differentiation
  program is induced by the activation of AhR and IL‐4 signaling, resulting in the
  expression of IRF4. Retinoic acid can inhibit IRF4 expression in tumors and favor
  macrophage differentiation. Other transcription factors involved in dendritic cell
  differentiation include NCOR2, BLIMP‐1, ZNF366 and MAFF
