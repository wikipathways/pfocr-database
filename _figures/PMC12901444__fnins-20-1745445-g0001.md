caption: Schematic illustration of the VLO in the neural pathways in chronic pain
  and comorbid depression. (A) Activation of the pathway in purple results in an analgesic
  effect, activation of the pathways in blue leads to alleviation of chronic pain-induced
  depressive-like behaviors, and activation of the pathway in red results in both
  analgesic and anti-depressant effects. The dotted line represents the pathway, by
  which chronic pain leads to a decrease in the activity of 5-HTergic neurons in the
  DRN. (B) The anti-depression mechanisms mediated by 5-HT1A and 5-HT2A receptors
  in the VLO and ACC. ACC, anterior cingulate cortex; CeA, central amygdala; DRN,
  dorsal raphe nucleus; LHb, lateral habenular nucleus; PAG, periaqueductal gray;
