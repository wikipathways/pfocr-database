caption: 'Lactate-driven angiogenesis and lactylation in endothelial cells (ECs).
  Left panel (light blue): Lactate promotes angiogenesis by stabilizing hypoxia-inducible
  factor-1α (HIF-1α) and N-myc downstream-regulated gene 3 (NDRG3), inducing vascular
  endothelial growth factor (VEGF), and activating rapidly accelerated fibrosarcoma
  (Raf)/extracellular signal-regulated kinase (ERK), nuclear factor κB (NF-κB)/interleukin-8
  (IL-8), and phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase (PI3K)/protein kinase B (Akt) signaling
  pathways. Lactate also reduces oxidized nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide (NAD+)
  levels, decreasing VEGF poly-ADP-ribosylation (PAR) and enhancing its bioactivity.
  Conversely, lactate signaling via G protein-coupled receptor 81 (GPR81) and heat
  shock protein A12B (HSPA12B) downregulates vascular endothelial cadherin (VE-cadherin),
  claudin-5, and zonula occludens-1 (ZO-1), increasing endothelial permeability. Right
  panel (pink): Lactate-induced lactylation of Snail activates transforming growth
  factor-beta (TGF-β)/SMAD family member 2 (Smad2) signaling, driving endothelial-to-mesenchymal
  transition (EndMT) and fibrosis. By contrast, the exercise-induced lactylation of
  methyl-CpG binding proteins 2 (MeCP2) suppresses ETS-related gene (Erg)/mitogen-activated
  protein kinase (MAPK) signaling, attenuating atherosclerosis (AS). Oxidized low-density
  lipoprotein (oxLDL) enhances glycolysis and histone H3 lysine 18 (H3K18) lactylation,
  exacerbating EndMT and AS progression. NADH: NAD, reduced form; HUVECs: human umbilical
  vein endothelial cells; MCT1: monocarboxylate transporter 1; PHD: proline hydroxylase;
  ROS: reactive oxygen species; VEGFR2: vascular endothelial growth factor receptor
  2; Ang-1: angiopoietin-1; TIE2: tyrosine kinase with immunoglobulin-like and EGF-like
  domains 2; Gas6: growth arrest specific 6; VHL: von Hippel-Lindau; MI: myocardial
  infarction; la: lactylation; eNOS: endothelial nitric oxide (NO) synthase. Mcp-1:
  monocyte chemoattractant protein-1; SNAI1: Snail family transcriptional repressor
  1'
