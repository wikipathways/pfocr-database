caption: Inhibition of the NF-κB Pathway by Agarwood. Inhibition of NF-κB Pathway
  by Agarwood. Upon pro-inflammatory stimulation, IKK (IκBα Kinase) is activated,
  leading to the phosphorylation and subsequent degradation of IκBα. This degradation
  allows NF-κB (p65/p50 dimer) to translocate from the cytoplasm to the nucleus, where
  it initiates the transcription of pro-inflammatory genes. Agarwood chromone compounds
  effectively interfere with this process by inhibiting IκBα phosphorylation, thereby
  preventing IκBα degradation. This action sequesters NF-κB in the cytoplasm, blocking
  its nuclear translocation and ultimately inhibiting pro-inflammatory gene transcription
