caption: Under normal conditions, Yap enters the cell nucleus and binds to TEAD to
  promote the transcription and translation of COX2. COX2 plays a crucial role in
  lipid metabolism and helps maintain normal cell functions. When stimulated by neomycin,
  the Hippo signaling pathway is activated, leading to the phosphorylation and subsequent
  degradation of Yap in the cytoplasm. As a result, the transcription and translation
  of COX2 are reduced, leading to lipid metabolism disorders, abnormal accumulation
  of lipid droplets, and ultimately, cell death
