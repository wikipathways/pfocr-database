caption: 'MYC-driven resistance to KRAS inhibitors (Created with BioRender.com). A
  MYC-driven genetic resistance to KRAS inhibitors: (1) mutations or amplifications
  of upstream RTKs (such as EGFR and FGFR); (2) secondary mutations on KRAS alleles;
  (3) activating mutations in downstream effectors pathways, such as PIK3CA, BRAF,
  MEK or MYC; B MYC-driven non-genetic resistance to KRAS inhibitors: (1–4) Mutant
  KRAS will continuously activate downstream pathways. The overactivity of this pathway
  can inhibit the activity of upstream receptors (EGFR, FGFR, etc.) through a negative
  feedback mechanism. After treatment with KRAS inhibitors, the feedback inhibition
  may be reduced, activating the rapid rebound of the upstream RTK receptor or other
  downstream effectors. Rebound signaling limits the efficacy of drug treatment. MYC
  acts as a downstream mediator of KRAS signaling pathways, mediating resistance to
  KRAS-targeted therapies'
