caption: 'Mechanism of sulfhydration in bone metabolic diseases. In OA, GYY4137 accelerates
  H2S release, promotes NF-κB p65 cysteine sulfhydration, downregulates NLRP3, caspase1
  and ROS, alleviates mitochondrial dysfunction, and ultimately relieves OA. In RA,
  H2S promotes Sp1 sulfhydration, downregulates HDAC6, MyD88 and NF-κB, and ultimately
  alleviates RA. In OP, GYY4137 accelerates H2S release. The released H2S alleviates
  OP in the following three ways. Firstly, H2S increases Ca2+ influx, promotes cysteine
  sulphhydration, upregulates the Wnt/β-catenin signaling pathway, and ultimately
  promotes osteogenic differentiation. Secondly, H2S promotes sulfhydration of SIRT3,
  improves mitochondrial function and inhibits BMSC senescence. Thirdly, H2S promotes
  the sulfhydration of ERp57, and increases intentional calcium and phosphorus absorption.
  In OS, HA-Lsdox accelerates H2S release, promotes the sulfhydration of Pgp, induces
  endoplasmic reticulum stress and apoptosis, and ultimately relieves OS. NLRP3: NOD-like
  receptor protein 3. NF-κB: nuclear factor kappa–B. caspase1: cysteinyl aspartate
  specific protease-1. Sp1: specificity protein 1. ROS: reactive oxygen species. HDAC6:
  histone deacetylase 6. MyD88: myeloid differentiation primary response 88. BMSC:
  bone marrow mesenchymal stem cell. ERp57: ER protein 57. HA-Lsdox: hyaluronylated
  liposomes. Pgp: P-glycoprotein'
