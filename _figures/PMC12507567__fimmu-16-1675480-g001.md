caption: The lactate-driven lactylation modification network in tumors. EGFR activation
  triggers the ERK pathway, which phosphorylates ACSS2, promoting its nuclear translocation
  and complex formation with KAT2A. This complex converts lactate into lactyl groups
  to modify both histones and non-histone proteins. This process activates pro-tumorigenic
  genes, enhances DNA repair and immunosuppressive pathways, thereby mediating chemoresistance
  and immune evasion. Conversely, delactylases exert anti-tumor effects by removing
  these modifications, suppressing glycolysis and oncogenic signaling
