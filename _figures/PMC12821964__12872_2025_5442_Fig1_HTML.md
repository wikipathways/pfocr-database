caption: Specific mechanisms by which exercise improves mitochondrial dysfunction
  in HF. Exercise promotes mitochondrial biogenesis by activating key signalling pathways
  such as AMPK/PGC-1α, thereby finely regulating the dynamic equilibrium between mitochondrial
  fusion and fission to optimise network architecture. It also facilitates the timely
  clearance of dysfunctional organelles through enhanced mitochondrial autophagy (e.g.,
  via the PINK1/Parkin pathway). Internally, exercise effectively restores electron
  transport chain activity and enhances the flow of multiple substrates—including
  fatty acids and amino acids—into the tricarboxylic acid cycle. This facilitates
  efficient ATP production, thereby reversing the energy crisis and contractile dysfunction
  in failing hearts
