caption: 'Overview of the ocular immune landscape in the healthy human eye. (a) Cornea:
  Dendritic cells and T lymphocytes reside predominantly in the basal epithelial layer,
  while stromal immune cells consist mainly of macrophages. (b) Bulbar Conjunctiva:
  This mucosa-associated lymphoid tissue contains diverse immune cells, including
  intraepithelial myeloid cells and stromal T and B lymphocytes. (c) Choroid: As a
  highly vascularized loose connective tissue, it harbors resident dendritic cells,
  macrophages, and mast cells. (d) Retina: In this neural tissue, resident microglia
  are predominantly located in the synaptic layers and ganglion cell layer. Autophagy
  plays an essential role in the ocular immune system by maintaining physical barrier
  integrity, precisely regulating immune cell functions, and facilitating the clearance
  of harmful substances while suppressing inflammatory pathways. DC, Dendritic Cell;
  T cells, T Lymphocytes; Mast cell, mast Cell; Macrophage, macrophage; ILM, inner
  limiting membrane; NFL, nerve fiber layer; GCL, ganglion cell layer; IPL, inner
  plexiform layer; INL, inner nuclear layer; OPL, outer plexiform layer'
