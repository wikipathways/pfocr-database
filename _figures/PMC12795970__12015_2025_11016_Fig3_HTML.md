caption: 'Extrinsic apoptosis pathway: Activation via Fas/TRAIL - R receptors leads
  to the initiation of apoptosis by Caspase − 8. LSC evasion mechanism: Overexpression
  of FLIP prevents the activation of Caspase − 8. Inhibition of Caspase-8 activates
  DR5, leading to ROS accumulation and lipid peroxidation, which in turn promotes
  ferroptosis.Intrinsic apoptosis pathway: The release of cytochrome C from mitochondria
  activates Caspase − 9 to induce apoptosis. LSC evasion mechanism: Overexpression
  of BCL − 2 and MCL − 1 inhibits BAX/BAK, preventing the release of Cyt - c. High
  expression of BCL-2 and MCL-1 blocks mitochondrial outer MOMP, thereby impairing
  Caspase activation and causing mitochondrial ROS accumulation, which promotes ferroptosis.Meanwhile,
  p53 can activate BAX/BAK to induce apoptosis; additionally, p53 suppresses SLC7A11
  expression, reducing GSH synthesis and leading to ROS accumulation that promotes
  ferroptosis.LSC drug - resistance strategies: Activation of anti - apoptotic genes
  such as BCL − 2 and XIAP by NF - κB enables continuous survival. Mutation or inactivation
  of p53 makes it unable to trigger apoptosis. The autophagy pathway is exploited
  to degrade apoptotic signals and avoid cell death. Abbreviation: BCL-2, B-cell lymphoma
  2; MCL-1, Myeloid Cell Leukemia-1 Protein; Cyt – c, Cytochrome c; BAX/BAK, Bcl-2
  Associated X Protein/Bcl-2 homologous antagonist/killer; Fas/TRAIL – R, Fas cell
  surface death receptor/Tumor Necrosis Factor-Related Apoptosis-Inducing Ligand Receptor;
  FLIP, FLICE-like inhibitory protein; DR5, death receptor 5; PUFA, Polyunsaturated
  fatty acid; MOMP, membrane permeabilization'
