caption: An overview of the cGAS-STING signaling pathway. The process begins when
  microbial DNA, genomic DNA, or mtDNA trigger cGAS, resulting in the creation of
  the second messenger, cGAMP. cGAMP subsequently stimulates STING, thereby activating
  the cGAS-STING-TANK-TBK1-IRF3/NF-κB signaling cascade. This activation results in
  the expression of type I IFNs and pro-inflammatory cytokines. In addition to cGAMP,
  cyclic di-adenosine monophosphate (c-di-AMP) or cyclic di-guanosine monophosphate
  (c-di-GMP) can directly activate STING. Furthermore, cGAMP can activate STING in
  neighboring cells through gap junctions
