caption: Metabolism exerts manifold functions including the generation of energy such
  as adenosine triphosphate (ATP) from the breakdown of nutrients such as carbohydrates,
  amino acids or lipids (bioenergetics), the assembly of macromolecules as cellular
  building blocks such as nucleic acids, membranes and proteins (anabolism), the maintenance
  of a redox homeostasis through generation and usage of cofactors and the influence
  on signaling and communication by acting as ligands, tuning the activity of signal
  pathways, regulating expression, posttranslational modification (PTM, e.g., phosphorylation,
  P) or degradation. These functions can act on all steps of metastasis formation
  such as invasion and migration, survival in the circulation, tumor dormancy, interaction
  with cells in the tumor microenvironment and outgrowth at a distant site
