caption: The mechanism of action of sotatercept involves the binding and inhibition
  of Pro-proliferative activin-class ligands (Activin A, GDFs 8 and 11) which intrinsically
  promote cell growth and myocyte proliferation. The inhibition of activin and GDFs
  by sotatercept leads to downstream prevention and deactivation of ActRIIA/B and
  the pSmad2/3 pathway. This in turn causes downregulation of endogenous BMP antagonists,
  Gremlin-1 and noggin. This causes a reflex which allows the support of growth-inhibiting
  pathways via the anti-proliferative BMPs to dominate causing activation of BMPR-II
  thereby reversing vascular remodeling and decreasing elevated pulmonary arterial
  pressure. Reprinted/adapted with permission from Ref. [120]
