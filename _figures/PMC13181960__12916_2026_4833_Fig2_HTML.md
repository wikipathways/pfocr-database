caption: Immunosenescence and its correlation to disease characteristics in COPD.
  Macrophages. Alveolar macrophages in COPD patients exhibit decreased levels SIRT1
  and Klotho, along with elevated p21CIP1 levels, indicating the presence of macrophage
  senescence. These macrophages have decreased Nrf2 expression as well as increased
  release of MMP-9, IL-6, IL-8, and TNF-α through the activation of the NF-kB pathway,
  thereby promoting airway inflammation and tissue damage in COPD in some degree.
  T Cells. Peripheral blood T cells from patients with COPD show more CD28null cells.
  CD8+CD28null T cells, and CD8+CD28null NKT-like cells secrete increased amounts
  of perforin and granzyme, promoting tissue damage, as well as higher IFN-γ and TNF-α,
  contributing to lung inflammation. Other features of T cell senescence include decreased
  proportion of naïve T cells, with a higher fraction of senescent naïve T cells expressing
  senescence and late differentiation markers (KLRG1+CD57+PD1+). Additionally, CD8+
  naïve T cells predominantly shifted toward TEMRA cells. Expression levels of GCR
  and SIRT1 declined, correlating with the secretion of proinflammatory factors (IFN-γ
  and TNF-α) and corticosteroid resistance. Additionally, CS exposure induces increased
  expression of RORγt and IL-13, indicating heightened Th17 and Th2 responses, which
  contribute to lung inflammation and iBALT formation. COPD chronic obstructive pulmonary
  disease, SIRT Sirtuin, Nrf2 nuclear factor erythroid 2-related factor 2, MMP matrix
  metalloproteinase, IL interleukin, TNFα tumor necrosis factor alpha, NF-kB nuclear
  factor kappa-light-chain-enhancer of activated B cells, NKT natural killer T cell,
  IFN-γ interferon-gamma, KLRG1 killer cell lectin-like receptor subfamily G, PD1
  programmed cell death protein 1, TEMRA terminally differentiated effector memory
  T cell, GCR glucocorticoid receptor, CS cigarette smoke; RORγt, retinoic acid-related
  orphan receptor gamma t; Th, T-helper; iBALT, inducible bronchus-associated lymphoid
  tissue
