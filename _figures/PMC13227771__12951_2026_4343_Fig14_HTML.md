caption: MHN Nanogenerators Induce Cascade Tumor Pyroptosis and cGAS-STING Activation.
  Under ultrasound irradiation, the MnO₂-based MHN nanogenerator releases NO, inducing
  mitochondrial dysfunction and caspase-1 /GSDMD-mediated pyroptosis. Pyroptosis releases
  cytoplasmic dsDNA, which, in combination with Mn²⁺ from degraded MnO₂, activates
  the cGAS-STING pathway. This results in type I interferon production, promoting
  DC maturation and CTL infiltration, thereby remodeling the immunosuppressive microenvironment
