caption: Regulation of autophagy in neutrophils. Autophagy in neutrophils can be initiated
  through multiple signaling pathways. In addition to common autophagy inducers such
  as nutrient starvation, neutrophils, as phagocytic cells, can also have autophagy
  induced by infection. With the combined action of various autophagy-related proteins,
  phagophores are generated from organelles such as the endoplasmic reticulum. These
  phagophores then expansion and close to become mature autophagosomes. After the
  autophagosomes fuse with lysosomes, the cargo is degraded and nutrients are recycled,
  completing the entire process of autophagy in neutrophils. mTOR, mammalian target
  of rapamycin; AMPK, adenosine monophosphate-activated protein kinase; ULK1, unc-51-like
  autophagy-activating kinase; FIP200, family kinase-interacting protein of 200 kDa;
  PI3K, phosphoinositide 3-kinase; PI3P, phosphatidylinositol-3-phosphate; LC3, light
  chain 3; PE, phosphatidylethanolamine
