caption: Mechanism of cellular senescence induction:- Cellular response is a mechanism
  by which cells respond to different intra and extracellular stimulations including
  stresses. A Cellular senescence can have a role at the early development stage and
  mainly via the PI3 K/FOXO and TGF-b/SMAD pathway activating p21. B Oncogenic activation
  can result in cellular senescence through the RAS-PI3 K-mTOR and RAS-p38MAPK pathways,
  and direct activation of the PI3 K-mTOR (loss of PTEN, mutation on PI3 K, mutation
  of AKT) activating the p53/p21 pathway and blocking CDK2. Oncogenic activation can
  activate ARF which activates the p53/p21 pathway by blocking MDM2 (murine double
  minute 2). C DNA damage responses, resulting from damages caused by chemotherapy,
  ROS, radiation telomere alteration, and mitochondrial dysfunction recruit ATM and
  ATR phosphorylating CHK2 and CHK1 respectively, and leading to the activation of
  the p53/P21 pathway that blocks CDK2 and halts the cell cycle. ATM and ATR also
  inhibit p62, which in turn upregulates GATA4, leading to increased activity of NF-kB,
  resulting in cellular senescence through SASP production. Mitochondrial dysfunction
  can also reduce the NAD+/NADH balance, leading to a decrease in ATP which activates
  AMPK pathway-dependent activation of p53 and induces cellular senescence. D Other
  stress like epigenetic modification can activate p16INK4b and p16INK4 A and block
  CDK4/6 resulting in cellular senescence. All pathways result in the RB protein remaining
  attached to the E2 F blocking the cells'transition from the G1 phase to the S phase
  resulting in cellular senescence
