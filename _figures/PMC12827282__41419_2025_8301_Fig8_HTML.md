caption: A In cells with normal PRPF40B expression (SH-SY5Y WT), BDNF interacts with
  the TRKB-FL receptor, triggering a cascade of signaling events through the MAPK/ERK
  and PI3K/AKT pathways to activate key genes essential for proper neuronal differentiation,
  synaptogenesis, and plasticity. B In cells where PRPF40B expression is silenced
  (SH-SY5Y G2), TRKB-T1 receptor expression is upregulated during differentiation.
  BDNF binds to TRKB-T1 without activating the classical signaling cascades due to
  the absence of the kinase domain, thereby reducing BDNF availability for TRKB-FL
  and impairing differentiation and synaptic maturation
