caption: VvERF045 integrates ET to modulate grape pigmentation in an ABA‐ and BR‐dependent
  manner. Under basal conditions, VvERF045 activates the transcription of anthocyanin
  biosynthetic genes (VvUFGT and VvDFR), BR biosynthetic genes (VvDWF4 and VvCPD),
  and ABA biosynthetic genes (VvZEP/ABA1). This regulatory activity contributes to
  maintaining the homeostasis of metabolites and phytohormones. Upon exposure to exogenous
  ETH, the activation of the ET signalling pathway significantly upregulates the expression
  of VvERF045. Additionally, VvMYB62 enhances the VvERF045‐mediated transcriptional
  activation of target genes (VvUFGT, VvDFR, VvDWF4, VvCPD, and VvZEP/ABA1). The coordinated
  action of the VvERF045‐VvMYB62 module facilitates anthocyanin accumulation potentially
  through both BR‐dependent and ABA‐dependent pathways, thereby promoting berry pigmentation.
  Solid arrows indicate activation. Proteins are represented by ellipses; genes are
  denoted by rectangles; metabolites are depicted as coloured boxes, with box size
  proportional to the relative concentrations of the corresponding metabolites. The
  intensity of berry colour reflects the level of anthocyanin accumulation
