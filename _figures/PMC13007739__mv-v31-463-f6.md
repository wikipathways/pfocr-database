caption: 'Mechanisms of proteostasis dysregulation and pathogenesis in pseudoexfoliation.
  Normal State: In a healthy cellular environment, the ubiquitin proteasome system
  (UPS) and autophagy function in a coordinated manner to degrade misfolded or damaged
  proteins, preventing their accumulation. The equilibrium between protein synthesis
  and degradation maintains proteostasis, ensuring proper cellular function. Compromised
  State in PEX Pathogenesis: Under pathological conditions, such as PEX, impairments
  in protein degradation pathways result in proteostasis dysregulation. Dysfunctional
  UPS and autophagy lead to the accumulation of extracellular matrix (ECM) proteins
  and intracellular protein aggregates. This proteotoxic stress contributes to increased
  oxidative damage, mitochondrial dysfunction, and cellular stress, ultimately driving
  PEX pathogenesis. Red arrows highlight the points of dysregulation in the degradation
  pathways, leading to proteostasis imbalance. The tilted scale depicts the imbalance,
  symbolizing the shift toward protein aggregation and ECM accumulation, hallmarks
  of PEX pathology'
