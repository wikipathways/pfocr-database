caption: Schematic diagram of the proposed signaling pathway for PC-induced lipolysis
  and apoptosis in adipocytes. Extracellular phosphatidylcholine (PPC) acts as a noncanonical
  agonist of Toll-like receptor 4 (TLR4) on the adipocyte membrane. Upon activation,
  TLR4 initiates the downstream inflammatory signaling cascade, leading to the translocation
  of NF-κB and the subsequent upregulation of TNF-α. This signaling axis ultimately
  increases both lipolysis and apoptosis in adipocytes
