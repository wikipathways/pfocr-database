caption: Anticancer Effects of CBDA and THCA. This figure demonstrates the mechanisms
  through which cannabidiolic acid (CBDA) and tetrahydrocannabinolic acid (THCA) exert
  their anticancer effects. Both compounds promote apoptosis, inhibit cell cycle progression,
  and reduce metastasis while also suppressing inflammation and engaging cannabinoid
  receptors. CBDA disrupts the RhoA signaling pathway by inhibiting COX-2 and reducing
  c-Fos/AP-1 levels, which limits tumor cell motility and promotes angiogenesis, invasion,
  and epithelial-mesenchymal transition (EMT). On the other hand, THCA lowers levels
  of TNF-α, COX-2, and pro-angiogenic mediators, while also activating peroxisome
  proliferator-activated receptor gamma (PPARγ) and inhibiting NF-κB and AP-1. These
  combined actions of THCA and CBDA shows their potential as complementary anticancer
  agents by targeting key hallmarks of cancer
