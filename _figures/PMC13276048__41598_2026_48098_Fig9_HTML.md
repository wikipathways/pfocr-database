caption: Proposed role of ANO6 in CRC under high-glucose conditions in the presence
  of CML. High glucose and CML synergistically enhance ANO6 expression in CRC cells.
  Increased ANO6 activity promotes activation of the Smad2/3 signaling pathway and
  downregulation of E-cadherin, thereby facilitating EMT. In parallel, high glucose
  suppresses ANO6-associated pyroptotic signaling, contributing to enhanced cell survival.
  Collectively, these molecular events promote CRC cell proliferation, increased migratory
  capacity, spheroid outgrowth, and tumor progression under hyperglycemic conditions
