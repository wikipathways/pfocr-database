caption: Schematic illustration of MTHFD2-mediated NADPH homeostasis in maintaining
  mitochondrial function and mitophagy in NSCs after ICH. Following ICH, hippocampal
  NSCs experience endoplasmic reticulum stress (ERS), which activates the PERK/ATF4
  signaling pathway. Activation of ATF4 leads to the transcriptional upregulation
  of MTHFD2, a key mitochondrial enzyme essential for NADPH production. MTHFD2 maintains
  intracellular NADPH levels and supports redox homeostasis within NSCs. By sustaining
  adequate NADPH supply, MTHFD2 preserves mitochondrial integrity and morphology,
  promotes mitophagy, and enhances NSC survival under ICH-induced stress conditions.
  This schematic highlights the crucial role of MTHFD2-mediated metabolic regulation
  in maintaining NSC function and mitigating cognitive deficits following brain injury
