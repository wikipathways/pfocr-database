caption: Blood biomarkers in patients with calcific aortic valve disease (with examples
  listed within parentheses) can arise from the diseased valve, appear in blood as
  a result of flow disturbance from AS, and be released from the myocardium in response
  to pressure overload from AS (black arrows). Additionally, other sources related
  to diseased vasculature (eg, atherosclerosis), adipose tissue, liver, and kidney
  disease—especially in patients with comorbidities—may also contribute to the blood
  pool of biomarkers (gray arrows). Some of the blood biomarkers can act on the diseased
  valve to cause further damage (red arrow). After SAVR, due to the resection of diseased
  leaflets and relief of valve obstruction, all sources of biomarkers can continue
  to contribute to the blood pool except for the aortic valve and flow disturbance
  from AS (blue pathway). In contrast to SAVR, similar changes in biomarker sources
  occur in TAVR except that some biomarkers may still be released from the diseased
  aortic valve (*) as the valve leaflets were not resected but rather just immobilized
  by the bioprosthetic valve (purple pathway). In both cases after SAVR and TAVR,
  the myocardium may still contribute some levels of blood biomarkers (eg, BNP) in
  certain patients despite relief of valve obstruction (**). AS indicates aortic stenosis;
  BNP, B‐type natriuretic peptide; CaxP, calcium‐phosphorus product; CBA, conjugated
  bile acids; eHb, extracellular hemoglobin; FGF‐23, fibroblast growth factor‐23;
  IL‐1β, interleukin‐1 beta; IL‐6, interleukin‐6; Lp(a), lipoprotein (a); Lp‐PLA2,
  lipoprotein‐associated phospholipase A2; mCRP, monomeric C‐reactive protein; MPO,
  myeloperoxidase; oxLDL, oxidized low‐density lipoprotein; SAVR, surgical aortic
  valve replacement; sST2, soluble form of ST2, a member of the interleukin‐1 receptor
  family; TAVR, transcatheter aortic valve replacement; TNF‐α, tumor necrosis factor‐alpha;
  and vWF, von Willebrand factor. Created in BioRender. Ho, Y. (2025) https://BioRender.com/ojvruwm
