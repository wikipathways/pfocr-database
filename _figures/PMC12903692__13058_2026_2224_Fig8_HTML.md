caption: 'Schematic Model of TRPS1–PDE4D Axis-Mediated Paclitaxel Resistance and Reversal
  Mechanism. (Left) In TRPS1-low TNBC cells, PDE4D is minimally expressed, allowing
  paclitaxel to stabilize microtubules and induce apoptosis. (Right) In TRPS1-high
  TNBC cells, TRPS1 upregulates PDE4D, promoting resistance through two synergistic
  mechanisms: (1) Survival signaling activation – PDE4D degrades cAMP, suppresses
  PKA, and activates the PI3K–AKT pathway; (2) Microtubule destabilization – PDE4D
  binds α-tubulin, locally depleting cAMP and blocking paclitaxel-induced microtubule
  stabilization. Activated AKT reinforces PDE4D–microtubule binding, forming a feedforward
  loop. Roflumilast restores cAMP levels, inhibits AKT, and detaches PDE4D from microtubules,
  re-sensitizing TRPS1-high cells to paclitaxel'
