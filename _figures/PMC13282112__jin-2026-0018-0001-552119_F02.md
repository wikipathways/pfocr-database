caption: Metabolic basis of neutrophil dysfunction and neutropenia in GSD-Ib. Deficiency
  of G6P transporter 1 disrupts import of both G6P and 1,5-AG6P in the ER. Accumulation
  of 1,5-AG6P in the cytosol inhibits HK, thereby reducing glycolytic flux and activity
  of the oxPPP. Attenuated NADPH production limits NOX2-dependent ROS generation,
  while reduced glycolytic intermediates restrict UDP-sugar synthesis, impairing ER
  protein glycosylation and inducing ER stress. The toxic accumulation of 1,5-AG6P,
  together with decreased metabolic activity and energy production, leads to neutrophil
  dysfunction, such as impaired NET formation, chemotaxis, and phagocytosis, and ultimately
  results in neutropenia. Figure created with BioRender (BioRender.com). NOX2, NADPH
  oxidase complex 2; oxPPP, oxidative pentose phosphate pathway
