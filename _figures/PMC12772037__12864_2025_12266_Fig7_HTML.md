caption: 'Differentially expressed genes (DEGs) in the terpenoid biosynthesis pathway
  in linalool-type and borneol-type camphor trees. The transcriptional cascade of
  terpenoid backbone biosynthesis, including the MVA and MEP pathways, is depicted
  with enzymatic products as nodes according to the ‘terpenoid backbone biosynthesis’
  pathway (ko00900) from the KEGG Pathway Database (https://www.kegg.jp/kegg/pathway.html).
  Functional enzymes displayed as gene symbols are highlighted in azure and enzyme
  expression patterns are represented by logFPKM values. The left four and right four
  columns represent S1-S4 developmental stages of linalool-type and borneol-type camphor
  trees, respectively. The full name of each enzyme is listed as following: ACAT,
  acetyl-CoA C-acetyltransferase; HMGCS, hydroxymethylglutaryl-CoA synthase; HMGCR,
  hydroxymethylglutaryl-CoA reductase; MVK, mevalonate kinase; MVD, diphosphomevalonate
  decarboxylase; IPK, isopentenyl phosphate kinase; DXS, 1-deoxy-D-xylulose-5-phosphate
  synthase; DXR, 1-deoxy-d-xylulose-5-phosphate reductoisomerase; ispE: 4-diphosphocytidyl-2-C-methyl-D-erythritol
  kinase; gcpE, (E)−4-hydroxy-3-methylbut-2-enyl-diphosphate synthase; ispH, 4-hydroxy-3-methylbut-2-en-1-yl
  diphosphate reductase; IDI, isopentenyl-diphosphate delta-isomerase; GPPS, geranyl
  diphosphate synthase; FPPS, farnesyl diphosphate synthase; GGPPS, geranylgeranyl
  diphosphate synthase; LIS, S-(+)-linalool synthase; BPPS, (+)-borneol diphosphate
  synthase; KS, ent-kaurene synthase; KO, ent-kaurene oxidase; KAO, ent-kaurenoic
  acid oxidase; CYP71A9, Cytochrome P450 71A9; SQLE, squalene monooxygenase; GA2ox,
  gibberellin 2beta-dioxygenase; GA3ox, gibberellin 3beta-dioxygenase; GA20ox, gibberellin
  20 oxidase; MAS, momilactone-A synthase; G8H, geraniol 8-hydroxylase'
