caption: Involved molecular mechanism of AFP promoting MDR in cancer cells. AFP can
  activate the PI3K/AKT signaling pathway, and the activation of this pathway further
  promotes the activation of NF-κB. The activated NF-κB, such as p65, can translocate
  into the nucleus. After p65 translocates into the nucleus, on the one hand, it can
  stimulate the transcription of drug resistance genes, thereby increasing the efflux
  of chemotherapeutic drugs; on the other hand, it can promote the expression of anti-apoptotic
  genes, thus enhancing the survival ability of cancer cells
