caption: 'Schematic diagram of VLDLR-mediated viral entry and intracellular release.Viral
  binding modes: Direct binding (Alphavirus, Rhinovirus): Viral surface proteins interact
  with VLDLR, initiating receptor-mediated endocytosis. Lipoprotein-dependent pathway
  (hepatitis C virus (HCV)): Viruses bind to VLDL, and VLDLR facilitates viral entry
  through VLDL uptake. Both mechanisms depend on VLDLR-mediated endocytosis. Intracellular
  release mechanisms: Vesicle fusion (A, C): Genomic RNA is released via membrane
  fusion from late endosomes. Protein pore formation (B, Rhinovirus): Viral genetic
  material is released through pore formation and membrane lysis. In both cases, the
  acidic endosomal pH induces VLDLR conformational changes, promoting viral or lipoprotein
  release. By Figdraw'
