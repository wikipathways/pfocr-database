caption: Modeling of ubiquitin-dependent and non-dependent mitochondrial autophagy
  pathways.Mitochondrial stress stabilizes PINK1 on the outer mitochondrial membrane
  (OMM) [16]. PINK1 is activated by autophosphorylation and phosphorylates Parkin(an
  E3 ubiquitin ligase [17]) and ubiquitin, both of which activate the E3 ligase activity
  of Parkin [18]. Parkin ubiquitinates several OMM proteins, and the resulting polyubiquitin
  chains act as additional phosphorylation targets for PINK1, forming a feed-forward
  loop [18]. The phosphorylated polyubiquitin chains trigger the recruitment of the
  ubiquitin-binding interface proteins OPTN, NDP52,and p62,which directly bind the
  autophagosomal light chain 3 (LC3) protein through its LC interaction region motif,
  thereby initiating autophagosome formation [48]. The affinity of OPTN for ubiquitin
  chains is enhanced by its phosphorylation and TANK-binding kinase 1 (TBK1) [48].
  Receptor-mediated autophagy is dependent on various OMM proteins, including BNIP3,
  NIX, and FUNDC1, which directly interact with LC3 to mediate autophagosome formation
  [39]
