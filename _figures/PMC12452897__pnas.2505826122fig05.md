caption: Model of dsDNA-induced phase separation of HMGB1 activating the RAGE-mediated
  inflammatory signaling pathway in macrophages. dsDNA (derived from bacteria, viruses,
  and apoptotic cells) induces HMGB1 to undergo LLPS. The HMGB1-DNA complex then interacts
  with the RAGE receptor on the macrophage plasma membrane, triggering RAGE activation.
  Condensed phase of RAGE assembly facilitates the recruitment of the adaptor protein
  SLP76, which also undergoes LLPS in the cytoplasm, forming signaling condensates.
  SLP76 integrates downstream signaling molecules, such as Grb2, into these condensates,
  promoting the activation of the MAPK pathway. This cascade leads to the release
  of proinflammatory cytokines like TNF-α and IL-1β, thereby initiating an immune
  response to pathogenic infection. Created with BioRender.com
