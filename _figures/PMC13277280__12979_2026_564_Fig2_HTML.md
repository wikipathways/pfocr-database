caption: ABC activation and functions. In the absence of DAMPs and PAMPs, SWEF proteins
  can block IRF4/5 binding to DNA motifs, which results in less binding of T-bet to
  DNA. In the absence of SWEF and presence of stimulatory signals, including TLR7/9,
  IL-21, and IFN-γ, IRF5 recruits T-bet to its target motifs, which results in activation
  of ABCs. ABC migration is species-dependent; mouse ABCs utilize CCR7 for T-B border
  localization, whereas human ABCs (particularly the DN2 subset) follow an extrafollicular
  (EF) pathway characterized by a CXCR3high CCR7/CXCR5low profile
