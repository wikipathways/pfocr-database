caption: Depiction of the canonical GPX4 pathway where cystine is transported into
  the cell by the cystine/glutamate antiporter (xCT) system. Cystine is subsequently
  converted to cysteine, then to Glu‐Cys, and then converted to GSH by glycine. GPX4
  facilitates the conversion of GSH to GSSG and LOOH to LOH. The accumulation of LOOH
  triggers ferroptosis and the Fenton reaction also enhances the production of LOOH
  in the presence of disfunctioning GPX4, thereby enhancing LOOH and triggering ferroptosis.
  Glu‐Cys, glutamate–cysteine; GPX4, glutathione peroxidase 4; GR, glutathione reductase;
  GSH, glutathione; GSSG, oxidized glutathione; LOH, lipid alcohols; LOOH, lipid hydroperoxides;
  NADP, nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide phosphate; NADPH, reduced form of nicotinamide
  adenine dinucleotide phosphate
