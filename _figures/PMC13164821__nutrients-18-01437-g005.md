caption: Hormetic signaling and mitochondrial adaptation induced by dietary polyphenols.
  Polyphenols such as resveratrol, quercetin, and epigallocatechin gallate activate
  adaptive stress-response pathways including the SIRT1–PGC-1α axis, promoting mitochondrial
  biogenesis and metabolic flexibility. Moderate reactive oxygen species (ROS) act
  as signaling mediators that stimulate mitochondrial adaptation, whereas excessive
  oxidative stress may impair anabolic signaling. The physiological impact of redox
  modulation differs between pediatric muscle, where oxidative stress may disrupt
  growth-related signaling, and aging muscle, where mitochondrial dysfunction and
  redox imbalance contribute to sarcopenia
