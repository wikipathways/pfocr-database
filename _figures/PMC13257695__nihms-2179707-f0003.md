caption: Other exposures. (A) Left, Alcohol ethoxylates (nonionic surfactants present
  in dishwasher rinse aid) disrupt tight junctions and induce proinflammatory mediators
  in gut epithelial cells. Right, Toothpaste component. SDS promotes esophageal epithelial
  hyperplasia; epithelial IL-33 induction; and CD4+ T cell, eosinophil (Eos), and
  neutrophil (Neu) infiltration—pathology with some similarities to human eosinophilic
  esophagitis (EoE). (B) Left, Inhaled PVC NPs are engulfed by airway macrophages,
  in which they reduce expression of RNase H, leading to accumulation of R loops,
  then activation of cGAS-STING pathway, culminating in cytokine production and ultimately
  exacerbation of AHR and remodeling in model of allergen-induced asthma. Right, Inhaled
  CuO nanoparticles (NPRs) and polystyrene (PS) MPs induce death in lung epithelial
  cells via TXNIP and STING, respectively. Epithelial death is linked to neutrophilic
  exacerbation of allergen-induced asthma (CuO NPRs) and lung fibrosis (PS MPs)
