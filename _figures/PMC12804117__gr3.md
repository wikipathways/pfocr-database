caption: 'The biosynthetic pathway consists of three modules: M-I includes atoB, mvaS
  and mvaE; M-II includes mvk, pmd and ipk; M-III includes idi, ispA and CcBOS. Dashed
  arrows indicate multiple enzymatic steps. The enzymes encoded by these genes are
  as follows: atoB, acetyl-CoA acetyltransferase; mvaS, HMG-CoA synthase; mvaE, HMG-CoA
  reductase; mvk, mevalonate kinase; pmd, phosphomevalonate decarboxylase; ipk, isopentenyl
  phosphate kinase; idi, isopentenyl diphosphate isomerase; ispA, farnesyl pyrophosphate
  synthase; CcBOS, (-)-α-bisabolol synthase; gltA, citrate synthase; acnAB, aconitate
  hydratase; icd, isocitrate dehydrogenase; sucAB, α-ketoglutarate dehydrogenase;
  sucCD, succinyl-CoA synthetase; sdh, succinate dehydrogenase; fum, fumarase; madh,
  malate dehydrogenase; slaA, α-acetolactate decarboxylase; poxB, pyruvate dehydrogenase;
  ldhA, d-lactate dehydrogenase; adhE, aldehyde/alcohol dehydrogenase; pflB, pyruvate
  formate lyase; pdh,pyruvate dehydrogenase. Metabolite abbreviations: G6P, glucose-6-phosphate;
  PEP, phosphoenolpyruvate; HMG-CoA, 3-hydroxy-3-methylglutaryl coenzyme A; MVA, mevalonate;
  MVAP, phosphomevalonate; IP, isopentenyl phosphate; IPP, isopentenyl diphosphate;
  DMAPP, dimethylallyl diphosphate; GPP, geranyl diphosphate. Transcription factors
  encoded by genes: iclR encodes isocitrate lyase repressor; arcA encodes aerobic
  respiration control protein A. Genes targeted for knockout are highlighted in red
  cross, and the effects of knocking out arcA and iclR genes on the metabolic pathway
  are shown with up-down triangles'
