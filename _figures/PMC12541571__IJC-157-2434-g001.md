caption: 'Mechanisms of the estrogen signaling pathway. ❶ Classical genomic estrogen
  signaling pathway: Estrogen and nuclear‐localized ER (E‐nER) along with their recruited
  co‐regulator bind to the estrogen response element of estrogen‐responsive genes,
  leading to the subsequent activation of gene transcription and regulating cell growth
  and development. ❷ Nonclassical genomic estrogen signaling pathway: The E‐nER complex
  activates estrogen‐responsive gene transcription through the involvement of specific
  transcription factors, such as SP‐1, AP‐1, and NF‐κB. ❸ Nongenomic estrogen signaling
  pathway: Upon the binding of estrogen with membrane‐localized ER (E‐mER), the E‐mER
  complex engages in interaction with membrane‐associated proteins, such as G protein‐coupled
  receptors or membrane‐based ion channels that later trigger the activation of downstream
  intracellular signaling pathways, such as PI3K/AKT/mTOR and RAS/RAF/MEK/ERK pathway.
  Subsequently, these activated pathways phosphorylate mER, transcription factors,
  or co‐regulators to regulate cellular growth, differentiation, and other estrogen‐mediated
  effects. ❹ Ligand‐independent estrogen signaling pathway: coupling with growth factor
  and its tyrosine kinase receptors on common downstream signaling pathways, PI3K/AKT/mTOR
  and RAS/RAF/MEK/ERK pathways disturb estrogen signaling and abnormal expression
  of estrogen‐responsive gene, resulting in uncontrolled cellular proliferation or
  developing resistance to estrogen‐targeted treatment. ER, estrogen receptor α; NF‐κB,
  nuclear factor κB; SP‐1, stimulating protein‐1'
