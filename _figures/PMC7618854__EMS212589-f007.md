caption: In the blood stream, cancer cells are exposed to reactive oxygen species
  (ROS), which needs to be buffered by antioxidant metabolic pathways that produce
  the cofactor nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide phosphate (NADPH) to regenerate glutathione
  (GSH) from its oxidized form (GSSH). In contrast, the metabolic environment in the
  lymphatic system protects from oxidative cell death. Dashed arrows indicate that
  not all steps of this pathway are shown. Grey dashed arrow between lipids and NADPH
  indicate that they are linked via an unknown mechanism. PPP, Pentose phosphate pathway;
  G6PD, Glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase; ME, Malic enzyme; IDH2, Isocitrate dehydrogenase
  2; FAO, Fatty acid oxidation; PHGDH, Phosphoglycerate dehydrogenase; THF, Tetrahydrofolate;
  SHMT2, Serine hydroxymethyltransferase 2; MTHF, Methylenetetrahydrofolate; MTHFD1,
  Methylenetetrahydrofolate dehydrogenase; ALDH1L2, Aldehyde dehydrogenase 1 L2
