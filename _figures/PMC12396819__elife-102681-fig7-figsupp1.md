caption: 'Pathway map of RAS Initiative with highlighted DEGs in ΔPr-A9 cells vs parental
  wild-type cells. DEGs are mainly distributed and clustered in two main branches
  of the RAS pathway: MAPK signaling branch and PI3K/AKT branches. DEGs were derived
  from RNA-seq data at a cutoff of adjusted p-value≤0.01 and fold change >1.5 for
  both up- and downregulated genes (indicated by red and blue arrows, respectively)
  between ΔPr-A9 and WT CaSki cells using three common analysis methods (DESeq2, edgeR,
  and limma-voom) and in-house BRB analysis pipeline from NCI. Highlighted genes in
  the pathway map are derived as DEGs in at least three of the analysis methods, although
  in most cases, these DEGs behaved consistently across all four methods. MAPK signaling
  branch is highlighted in a transparent green box, and PI3K/AKT branch is highlighted
  in a transparent yellow box within the pathway map of RAS signaling pathway, which
  was collectively collated from community inputs that were organized and stimulated
  by RAS Initiative and maintained as a common knowledge basis here: https://www.cancer.gov/research/key-initiatives/ras/ras-central/blog/2015/ras-pathway-v2,
  which was also described in a recent review (Figure 1 in Nissley and McCormick,
  2022). Reference (where the Ras pathway map has been described)'
