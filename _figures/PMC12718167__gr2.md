caption: 'Signaling networks regulating autophagy in tumor endothelial cellsMultiple
  stress-responsive pathways converge to regulate autophagy: (A) amino acid sensing
  machinery at the lysosomal surface, including RAG complexes, RAGULATOR, v-ATPase,
  SLC38A9, and KICSTOR, mediates mTORC1 activation.(B) Growth factor signaling operates
  through the PI3K-AKT axis to regulate mTORC1 activity via dual mechanisms.(C) VEGF
  triggers autophagy through VEGFR2-mediated AMPK activation.(D) Hypoxic stress induces
  autophagy through three parallel pathways: AMPK activation, HIF-1α stabilization,
  and ER stress induction.(E) ER stress response involves UPR-mediated activation
  of IRE1 and PERK pathways, while calcium signaling triggers autophagy through DAPK1.(F)
  Oxidative stress-generated ROS modulates autophagy through GSK-3β and p38/p53 signaling
  cascades. Gln: glutamine; Arg: arginine; Leu: leucine'
