caption: Mechanisms of necroptosis in breast cancer. Ligand engagement of death receptors
  (TNFR, TRAILR, FAS) assembles Complex I (TRADD–TRAF2/5–cIAP1/2–RIPK1). When caspase-8
  is inhibited or low, signaling shifts to Complex IIa (necrosome), where RIPK1 and
  RIPK3 phosphorylate and activate MLKL, and to Complex IIb (RIPK1–RIPK3–FADD–caspase-8/FLIPL).
  Phospho-MLKL oligomerizes and translocates to the plasma membrane to execute necroptosis,
  a process amplified by mitochondrial ROS and PGAM5. In parallel, TLR3/4–TRIF–RIPK3
  and nucleic-acid sensor ZBP/DAI–RIPK3 pathways converge on RIPK3–MLKL, providing
  apoptosis-independent routes to regulated necrotic death
