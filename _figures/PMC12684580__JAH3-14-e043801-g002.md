caption: 'The figure systematically summarizes the functional roles and molecular
  mechanisms of histone (H3K18la, H4K12la, H4K5la) and nonhistone (Snail1, Titin K1897,
  Mecp2 K271, NLRP3 K245) lactylation modifications across major CVDs. In MI, early‐phase
  H3K18la in monocytes/macrophages upregulates reparative genes (Lrg1, Vegf‐α, IL‐10)
  via the GCN5‐mediated IL‐1β/IL‐1R1 pathway to promote myocardial salvage, while
  lactate‐induced and p300/CBP‐mediated Snail1 lactylation exacerbates fibrosis through
  TGF‐β/Smad2‐dependent EndoMT. I/R injury exhibits dual regulation: HSPA12A confers
  cardioprotection by stabilizing HIF‐1α to maintain glycolytic metabolism, whereas
  YTHDF2 and NLRP3 K245 lactylation promotes cellular damage. Fibroblast‐derived SA3K–K351la
  activates cardioprotective RISK/SAFE pathways while inhibiting Wnt signaling. In
  HF, Titin K1897la stabilizes cardiac structure by maintaining the Titin–α‐MHC complex.
  For atherosclerosis and vascular calcification, exercise‐induced Mecp2 K271la improves
  endothelial function by modulating EGFR–MAPK signaling, while NR4A3/TRAP1‐driven
  glycolysis elevates H3K18la/H4K12la to activate EndoMT and SASP. PH involves HIF‐1α–PDK1/2‐mediated
  metabolic reprogramming that enhances H3K18la/H4K5la to drive vascular remodeling.
