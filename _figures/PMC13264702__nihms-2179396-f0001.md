caption: Lipin catalyzes a key branch point in the glycerol-3-phosphate pathway. Lipin
  is the only peripheral membrane protein in this pathway. The other members, glycerol
  phosphate acyltransferase (GPAT), acylglycerol phosphate acyltransferase (AGPAT),
  and diacylglycerol acyltransferase (DGAT) are integral membrane proteins and use
  one molecule of fatty acyl coenzyme A (FA-CoA) to generate their product. Lipin
  is a magnesium-dependent enzyme that generates diacylglycerol (DAG) from the hydrolysis
  of phosphatidic acid (PA). DAG is a starting material for the de novo biosynthesis
  of major membrane phospholipids phosphatidylcholine (PC), phosphatidylethanolamine
  (PE), and phosphatidylserine (PS). PA is a precursor for other lipids such as phosphatidylinositol
  (PI) and cardiolipin (CL)
