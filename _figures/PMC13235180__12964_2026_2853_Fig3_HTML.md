caption: 'Schematic representation of the major microenvironmental and intracellular
  signaling pathways that converge to regulate ABCG2 expression and autophagy in cancer
  stem cells (CSCs). Pro-inflammatory cytokines (TNF-α, IL-1) activate IL-1R/TNFR
  signaling, leading to NF-κB (p50/p65) nuclear translocation and transcription of
  ABCG2, LC3B, Beclin-1, and inflammatory cytokines. Estrogen (E2) activates ERα through
  both genomic and non-genomic pathways (SRC/AMPK and RAS–RAF–MEK–ERK), promoting
  ABCG2 expression and autophagy via FOXO3A. Hypoxia stabilizes HIF-1α, which induces
  ABCG2 and autophagy-related genes through hypoxia response elements. Endoplasmic
  reticulum (ER) stress activates the PERK–eIF2α–ATF4–CHOP axis, leading to the transcription
  of ATG genes (ATG5, ATG7, ATG10, ATG12), LC3, and p62. Oxidative stress activates
  Nrf2, which upregulates ABCG2 and autophagy-associated genes (ATG4, ATG5, ATG7,
  p62). In parallel, PPARγ/RXRγ and C/EBPβ signaling enhances ABCG2 and ATG4B expression,
  while FOXO1/FOXO3A transcription factors promote autophagy through induction of
  ULK, VPS34, Beclin-1, ATG5, and LC3. These pathways collectively converge on the
  autophagic machinery (phagophore formation, autophagosome maturation, and lysosomal
  fusion) and ABCG2-mediated drug efflux, thereby promoting CSC survival, stemness,
  and therapy resistance. The figure was created with https://www.biorender.com. Abbreviations:
  TNF-α: Tumor necrosis factor alpha, IL-1R: interleukin-1 receptor, E2: 17-β-estradiol,
  ERα: estrogen receptor alpha, NF-κB: Nuclear factor kappa-light-chain-enhancer of
  activated B cells, HIF-1α: hypoxia inducible factor-1 alpha, ER stress: endoplasmic
  reticulum stress, Nrf2: nuclear factor erythroid 2–related factor 2, ATF4: Activating
  transcription factor 4, ATG: autophagy-related protein, PERK: Protein Kinase R-like
  ER Kinase, PPAR-γ: Peroxisome proliferator-activated receptor gamma, C/EBPβ: CCAAT/enhancer-binding
  protein β, FOXO: forkhead box transcription factors'
