caption: 'Conceptual map of the metabolic alterations in HIV/TB coinfection and the
  effect of ART. This figure illustrates key metabolic pathway alterations identified
  in this study. The diagram integrates both mitochondrial and non-mitochondrial cellular
  processes, with affected metabolites highlighted in red. Annotations next to highlighted
  metabolites indicate their primary biological classification as gut microbiota (GM),
  amino acid (AA), energy metabolism (EM), or carbohydrate metabolism (CM), to aid
  interpretation. Metabolites shown correspond to statistically significant differences
  reported in Table 2, and for each metabolite, the corresponding line graph depicts
  the group-mean concentration derived from our study dataset: HIV+/TB+/Tx- (red),
  HIV+/TB+/Tx+ (blue), and HIV-/TB-/Tx- (green). The pathway arrangement is intended
  for conceptual clarity and does not represent a direct mechanistic map; arrows indicate
  general metabolic relationships rather than measured reaction fluxes'
