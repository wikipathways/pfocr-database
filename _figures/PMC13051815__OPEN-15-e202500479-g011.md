caption: Hsp70–Bim–TOMM20 axis diverts Bim from apoptosis toward Parkin‐mediated mitophagy [3].
  Under cellular stress, cytosolic Hsp70 binds the BH3‐only protein Bim and shuttles
  it to the mitochondrial outer membrane, where the complex docks on the import receptor
  TOMM20. The Hsp70–Bim platform recruits the E3 ubiquitin ligase Parkin (blue), which
  ubiquitinates TOMM20. Ubiquitylated TOMM20 triggers PINK1/Parkin‐dependent mitophagy,
  promoting selective removal of damaged mitochondria (green recycling symbol), and
  thereby suppressing Bax/Bak‐driven apoptosis. This noncanonical pathway explains
  how tumor cells can tolerate high Bim levels while escaping mitochondrial outer‐membrane
  permeabilization, adapted with premission [3]. Chemical‐shift perturbations map
  a hydrophobic triad in Bim (Leu94, Ile97, Phe101) that inserts into a pocket formed
  by Hsp70 Phe92 and Leu139, while the acidic side chains Glu90 and Asp98 form bidentate
  hydrogen bonds with the chaperone's Lys71 and Lys732023, Springer
