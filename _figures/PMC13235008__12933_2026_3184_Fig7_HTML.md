caption: 'Schematic illustration of GULP1-mediated alleviation of DCM via activation
  of the IKIP/NF-κB Pathway. In the healthy heart, GULP1 binds to IKIP, limiting the
  interaction between IKIP and IKKβ, thereby facilitating IKKβ-dependent activation
  of NF-κB and promoting OPA1 expression. This maintains mitochondrial morphology,
  preserves MRCS, supports oxidative phosphorylation and FAO, and limits oxidative
  stress and apoptosis. In DCM, cardiomyocyte GULP1 expression of GULP1 is reduced,
  weakening its interaction with IKIP and favoring IKIP-IKKβ binding. This inhibits
  IKKβ-mediated activation (phosphorylation) of NF-κB-dependent Opa1 transcription,
  disrupts mitochondrial morphology, downregulates mitochondrial complexes, impairs
  oxidative phosphorylation and fatty acid β-oxidation, and enhances oxidative stress,
  apoptosis, and myocardial lipid accumulation. Restoration of GULP1–IKIP–NF-κB–OPA1
  signaling improves mitochondrial structure and function, promotes FAO, reduces lipid
  overload, and ultimately improves cardiac function and prevents DCM progression.
  GULP1: PTB-domain engulfment adapter protein 1, IKIP: Inhibitor of nuclear factor
  kappa-B kinase-interacting, DCM: diabetic cardiomyopathy, MRCS: mitochondrial respiratory
  chain complexes, FAO: fatty acid β-oxidation'
