caption: 'MsMDR-catalyzed reactions characterized in this work. (a) Canonical cinnamyl
  alcohol dehydrogenase (CAD)-like activity of MsMDRs, catalyzing the conversion between
  cinnamyl alcohol (CAL) and cinnamaldehyde (CA). (b) Reactions converting 8-hydroxygeraniol
  (HYD) to 8-oxogeraniol (OXO) and 8-oxogeranial (OXA), part of the strictosidine
  biosynthetic pathway. (c) Reported downstream pathway in MIA metabolism, catalyzed
  by characterized MsMDRs: MsDCS1 converts strictosidine aglycone to 20R-dihydrocorynantheine
  and 20S-dihydrocorynantheine; MsHYS converts strictosidine aglycone to ajmalicine,
  tetrahydroalstonine, and mayumbine; and MsTHAS converts strictosidine aglycone to
  tetrahydroalstonine. All reactions accept NAD(P)H/NAD(P)+ as marked, except for
  the oxidation of HYD to OXO catalyzed by MsTHAS, which requires NADPH but not NADH'
