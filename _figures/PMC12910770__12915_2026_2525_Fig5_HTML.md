  early embryos, aTPST2 interacts with MED8 and activates TPST2 in cis. TPST2 catalyzes

  which promotes phosphorylation of RAP1B and then leads to activation of the ERK
  signaling pathway. This molecular cascade ultimately regulates proper preimplantation
  embryonic development
