caption: 'Diagram of the pathogenesis of memory loss in PE patients. Path diagram
  of the mediation analysis of potential serum biomarkers on the relationship between
  MAP and the total AVLT-H score (A). This study found that the mechanism of memory
  decline in PE patients includes three pathways (B): ① The increase of MAP directly
  leaded to increased serum Aβ1-42 level and indirectly leaded to memory decline,
  ② The increase of MAP directly leaded to increased serum P-tau181 level, which indirectly
  damaged memory by increasing serum Aβ1-42 level, and ③ The increase of MAP can directly
  damage memory, and has little effect compared with the previous two pathways. Note:
  serum Aβ1-42 and P-tau181 can promote each other and have a synergistic effect on
  memory decline in pathway 2'
