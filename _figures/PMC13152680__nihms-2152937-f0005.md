caption: Depicted are known associations between gut microorganisms and microbial
  pathways with host gene expression and pathways across various body sites—including
  the oral cavity, lungs and airways, skin, gut, cervicovaginal region, and breast
  milk—and in different health conditions. These associations vary by body site and
  disease state, suggesting that niche-specific microbes may both influence and respond
  to host gene expression through diverse biological pathways. The section labeled
  ‘Breast milk and infant gut’ refers to associations between gene expression in milk
  and microbial abundance in the infant gut. An overview of the body sites and diseases
  studied in the papers whose findings were used to generate this network figure is
