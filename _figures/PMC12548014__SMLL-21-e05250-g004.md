caption: Schematic illustration of p‐ZNPs as an adjuvant for cancer vaccines, enhancing
  cellular immunity (Created in BioRender.com). After cell uptake, Zn2⁺ released from
  p‐ZNPs activate TLR2, TLR4, and TLR6, initiating Myd88‐TRAF6 signaling. This activation
  triggers MAPK and NF‐κB pathways, leading to the secretion of pro‐inflammatory cytokines.
  Meanwhile, ROS generation induces endosomal rupture, further amplifying TLR downstream
  signalling and reinforcing immune activation. The released cytokines drive the differentiation
  of CD4⁺ T cell into Th1 cells, which, along with activated CD8⁺ cytotoxic T cells,
  promotes a potent antitumor immune response
