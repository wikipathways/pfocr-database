caption: Interaction between the cGAS-STING and TLR signaling pathways. Upon sensing
  extracellular or damage-derived nucleic acids, TLR3 and TLR4 activate distinct adaptor
  proteins to initiate downstream signaling. In as, this cascade amplifies NF-κB signaling,
  leading to the release of cytokines such as TNF-α, IL-6, and IL-1β. MyD88, however,
  exerts an inhibitory effect on TLR3 signaling by selectively suppressing IKKε-dependent
  IRF3 activation, preventing uncontrolled inflammation (balancing of pro-inflammatory
  and anti-inflammatory). Notably, RBAP attenuates TLR4 activity, indirectly dampening
  cGAS-STING-mediated inflammatory signaling and reducing foam cell formation
