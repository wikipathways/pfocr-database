caption: The role of sphingolipids and their metabolites in CNS diseases. (A) S1P
  and ceramide bind to β‐secretases and γ‐secretases and BACE1, facilitating the conversion
  of APP to Aβ. Elevated GM1 levels form GM1‐Aβ complexes, promoting the conversion
  of Aβ monomers into toxic oligomers. S1P enhances the PI3K pathway, while ceramide
  activates the PP2A pathway, impacting the downstream Akt/GSK‐3β pathway and ultimately
  affecting tau phosphorylation. Tau protein stimulates SMase, promoting ceramide
  accumulation, leading to further aggregation of p‐tau. (B) Mutant glucocerebrosidase
  1 (GBA1) synthesizes mutant β‐GCase, disrupting lysosomal clearance of α‐synuclein
  aggregates. As a result, α‐synuclein monomers and oligomers accumulate within the
  cell. (C) Sites of action of S1P in the CNS in MS
