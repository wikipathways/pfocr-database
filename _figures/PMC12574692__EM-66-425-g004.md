caption: A schematic diagram overviewing the methods of this analysis. They included
  (1) dataset generation and acquisition, (2) random forest modeling to identify understudied
  chemicals with properties more similar to chemicals with a putative association
  with breast cancer, and (3) pathway analysis run on informative genes from the random
  forest modeling and assessment of expression patterns of these genes in a human
  populations. The green check indicates chemicals that have been tested but do not
  show elevated risk for breast cancer risk, the pink tumor indicates chemicals that
  do have a known association with increased breast cancer risk, and the yellow question
  mark indicates chemicals that have not been thoroughly screened for breast cancer
  related outcomes
