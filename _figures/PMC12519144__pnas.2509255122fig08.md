caption: Schematic diagram of histone Kcr-mediated resistance to EGFR-TKI therapy
  in lung cancer. In EGFR-TKI-resistant lung cancer cells, a decrease in ACSS2 expression
  induced by a high pHi leads to reduced levels of histone Kcr. Furthermore, the reduced
  H3K56cr suppresses HNF1A transcription, and thereby activates the PI3K/AKT pathway,
  which contributes to cancer cell survival and resistance to EGFR-TKI therapy. In
  cells exposed to S67, the resistant phenotype can be reversed, resulting from the
  restored histone Kcr levels and transcriptional upregulation of HNF1A
