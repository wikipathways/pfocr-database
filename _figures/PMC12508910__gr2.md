caption: 'Organelle disorders in dry eyeWhen ocular surface cells are exposed to dry
  eye risk factors, cellular organelles undergo structural and functional impairments.
  Mitochondria exhibit swelling, rupture, and depolarization, accompanied by extensive
  mPTP opening. There is a decrease in mitochondrial Ca2+, ATP, and mtDNA levels,
  alongside a decline in mitochondrial oxygen uptake capacity, and a sharp increase
  in mitochondrial ROS levels. Mitochondrial autophagy mediated by BNIP3L/NIX and
  PINK1 is initiated. Endoplasmic reticulum cisternae dilate, leading to a significant
  rise in endoplasmic reticulum stress characterized by the upregulation of stress-related
  proteins (BiP/GRP78, GRP94, XBP1, CHOP, and ATF4) and activation of the PERK-CHOP
  pathway of the UPR. Lysosomes show anomalies, including reduced count and increased
  engulfment of LDs. These result in impaired cell polarity, barrier function, reduced
  apical mucin 16 expression, decreased cell viability, increased apoptosis and oxidative
  stress, and diminished neural innervation. Abbreviations: mPTP, mitochondrial permeability
  transition pore; ATP, adenosine triphosphate; mtDNA, mitochondrial DNA; ER, endoplasmic
  reticulum; BiP/GRP78, heavy-chain binding protein/78-kDa glucose-regulated protein;
  GRP94, glucose-regulated protein 94; XBP1, X-box binding protein 1; CHOP, C/EBP-homologous
  protein; ATF4, activating transcription factor 4; UPR, unfolded protein response;
  LDs, lipid droplets'
