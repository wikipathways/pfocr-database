caption: NF-κB-targeted therapeutics in inflammatory diseases and cancer. Abnormal
  activation of NF-κB is linked to inflammatory diseases and cancer, making this pathway
  an attractive therapeutic target. A Targeting the canonical and noncanonical NF-κB
  pathways with various small-molecule inhibitors or receptor-blocking monoclonal
  antibodies. B Incorporating the signaling domain of 4-1BB or OX40 into chimeric
  antigen receptors (CARs) in CAR-T-cell therapy promotes the activation of NF-κB,
  especially the NIK-dependent noncanonical NF-κB pathway, and improves the efficacy
  of cancer immunotherapy. The NIK pathway promotes the survival and metabolic fitness
  and reduces the exhaustion of CAR-T cells. The images were created with BioRender
