caption: The roles of intestinal amino acid (AA) metabolic enzymes and AA-derived
  metabolites in IBD. In intestinal cells, tryptophan (Trp) is primarily metabolized
  through the kynurenine (Kyn) pathway, followed by the serotonin pathway. The product
  kynurenic acid (Kna) activates the aryl hydrocarbon receptor (AhR) or G protein-coupled
  receptor 35 (GPR35) in epithelial cells or T cells, which ameliorating IBD. In the
  serotonin pathway, Trp is finally metabolized into 5-hydroxytryptamine (5-HT), which
  exerts diverse effects on IBD by acting on several 5-HT receptors (5-HTRs). Arginine
  (Arg) is converted into spermidine and spermine. Spermidine reduces colitis by maintaining
  a protective gut microbiota, suppressing α-defensin expression, and promoting epithelial
  proliferation and macrophage differentiation. This Figure was created by MedPeer
