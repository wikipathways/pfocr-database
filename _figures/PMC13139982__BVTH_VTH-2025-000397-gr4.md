  adult platelets. Blue indicates pathway enrichment in neonatal platelets. The x-axis

  or adult enrichment with panel B. (D) Krebs cycle comparison in human and mouse
  platelets. Red indicates higher protein abundance in neonatal platelets, and blue
  indicates higher protein abundance in adult platelets. (E) GPVI pathway comparison
  in human and mouse platelets. Red indicates higher protein abundance in neonatal
