caption: Ginsenosides suppress hepatic inflammation to prevent liver fibrosis. Ginsenosides
  attenuate NOX4 expression, thereby inhibiting NLRP3 inflammasome activation and
  subsequent cleavage of pro-IL-1β and pro-IL-18 into their mature forms. Additionally,
  ginsenosides suppress P2X7R-mediated inflammasome activation through the activation
  of ERRα and LXR. Activated LXR downregulates pro-inflammatory cytokine expression.
  Furthermore, ginsenosides activate the Keap1-Nrf2 pathway, which suppresses pro-inflammatory
  cytokine production via NF-κB pathway inhibition and prevents inflammasome activation
  by reducing ROS levels. These integrated effects collectively decrease cytokine
  production and suppress HSC activation, thereby attenuating hepatic fibrosis
