caption: Overview of human N-glycosylation pathway. A Building of N-glycans starts
  in the endoplasmic reticulum (EndoR), flipped to the EndoR lumen by oligosaccharyltransferase
  (OST) complex, then added to growing polypeptide chain. Glucose residues are then
  removed during protein folding check involving calnexin and calreticulin. B First
  mannose residue is removed by EndoR α-mannosidase (MAN1B1), before glycosylated
  protein is sent to Golgi complex. C Three more mannose residues are removed by Golgi
  α-mannosidase I (includes MAN1A1, MAN1A2, and MAN1A3/MAN1C1) before hybrid N-glycans
  can be formed by N-acetylglucosaminyltransferase 1 (MGAT1). If mannose residues
  are not trimmed, only high mannose N-glycans are formed. D After formation of hybrid
  N-glycans, they can be further differentiated by adding various sugars to GlcNAcylated
  arm. E Instead of hybrid N-glycan differentiation, glycans can have further mannose
  residues trimmed by Golgi α-mannosidase II (MAN2A1, MAN2A2), forming complex N-glycan
  by MGAT2. Complex N-glycans can be further branched and differentiated. Enzymes
  shown using their gene name. Glycans represented in Symbol Nomenclature for Glycans
  (SNFG) format
