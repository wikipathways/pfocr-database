caption: 'cGAS-STING Pathway Mechanism in Innate Immunity to Cytoplasmic dsDNA. Cytoplasmic
  dsDNA (genomic, viral, mitochondrial, bacterial) activates cGAS, which synthesizes
  cGAMP from ATP and GTP. Bacterial cyclic dinucleotides (CDNs) act on STING, while
  ENPP1 hydrolyzes CDNs and cGAMP to negatively regulate STING. cGAMP binds STING
  on the endoplasmic reticulum (ER), inducing its conformational change and COPII-mediated
  transport to the ER-Golgi intermediate compartment (ERGIC)/Golgi, where STING undergoes
  palmitoylation. At ERGIC/Golgi, STING recruits TBK1 to phosphorylate IRF3 (which
  homodimerizes), and activates the IKK complex to release NF-κB (which homodimerizes).
  Phosphorylated IRF3 and NF-κB translocate to the nucleus: IRF3 drives transcription
  of type I interferons (IFNs) and interferon-stimulated genes (ISGs), while NF-κB
  induces proinflammatory cytokine transcription. To restrict excessive signaling,
  activated STING undergoes ESCRT-driven microautophagy for lysosomal degradation;
  degradation of IKK complex components also regulates the pathway'
