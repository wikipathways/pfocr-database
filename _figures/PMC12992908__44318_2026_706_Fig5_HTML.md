caption: (A) When DMSP concentration is low, the DmdR dimer binds to dmd box sequences
  in the promoter region of dmdA-acuI (including dmdEF in many cases), resulting in
  repression of DMSP demethylation, oxidative stress protection and downstream DMSP
  cleavage (catalyzed by enzyme AcuI), ensuring conditions that favor its intracellular
  accumulation. (B) When DMSP concentration increases, ddd gene expression is increased,
  leading to enhanced DMSP cleavage and accumulation of the DmdR effector acryloyl-CoA
  through the action of PrpE. DmdR binds to its acryloyl-CoA effector. Holo-DmdR with
  lower affinity to dmd boxes dissociates from the dmdA-acuI promoter derepressing
  transcription. This upregulates expression of the demethylation pathway and the
  AcuI enzyme of the DMSP cleavage pathway, that detoxifies acryloyl-CoA and enables
  DMSP’s use as a carbon and sulfur source. Enhanced DMSP demethylation results in
  reactive oxygen species (ROS) production and oxidative stress, which can be alleviated
  via the action of the peroxidase DmdF and, potentially, DmdE and DinB, whose expression
  is also derepressed
