caption: Our study demonstrates upregulated levels of TNFSF10, TNFSF11, and TNFSF12
  in the circulating plasma in ASD, with B cells, CD4 T cells, and NK cells likely
  contributing to these upregulations. In CD8 T cells, TRAIL and TWEAK signaling pathways
  are inhibited, ultimately leading to impaired cell fate regulation and a reduced
  inflammatory response. Although RANKL signaling is induced in both NK cells and
  CD4 T cells leading to an enhanced inflammatory response, the TWEAK signaling pathway
  is disrupted in NK cells resulting in inhibited inflammation in another population
  of this cell type. The green arrow indicates cell types producing a specific ligand,
  while the red arrow denotes the proposed signaling effect on their specific receptor
