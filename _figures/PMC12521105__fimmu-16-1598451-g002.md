caption: DAMPs promote testicular inflammation by regulating spermatogenesis through
  the activation of the inflammasome pathway (IP), particularly the NLRP3 inflammasome
  (from Figdraw, www.figdraw.com). DAMPs released by testicular cells activate the
  NLRP3 inflammasome via the TLR/MYD88/NF-κB signaling pathway, leading to the release
  of pro-inflammatory cytokines such as IL-1α, IL-1β, and TNF-α. Additionally, the
  NLRP3 inflammasome enhances the expression of Caspase-1 and IL-1β. Furthermore,
  DAMPs induce mitochondrial damage within cells, resulting in ROS accumulation and
  ATP efflux, thereby exacerbating testicular inflammation
