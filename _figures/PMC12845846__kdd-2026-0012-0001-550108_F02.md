caption: Complement activation pathways and corresponding complement pathway inhibitors.
  The CP is initiated when it binds to the antibody-antigen complex, subsequently
  causing the activation of C1r, C1q, and C1s and then converting C4 and C2 into C3
  convertase (C4bC2a). The lectin pathway is triggered by the binding of mannose residues
  on the surface of foreign pathogens to mannose-binding lectin (MBL), which then
  binds to and activates MBL-associated serine protease (MASP)-1 and MASP-2. By recruiting
  MASP to induce the division of C2 and C4, C3 invertase (C4bC2a) is formed. The alternative
  pathway is activated by the self-hydrolysis of C3 to form C3b, while MASP-3 continuously
  cleaves C3 at a low level, causing rapid self-amplification of the signal. Factor
  B is activated by factor D to form Bb, and then Bb combines with the activated C3
  to form C3bBb (C3 invertase). Then C3bBb cleaves C3 to form C3b, which then combines
  with C4b2a to form C5 invertase (C4b2a3b). By cutting C5 into fragments C5a and
  C5b. C5b combines with C6–C9 to form C5B-9, namely, the membrane attack complex
  (MAC). MAC inserts into the cell membrane, causing cell damage, activating the complement
  pathway, and inducing an inflammatory response
