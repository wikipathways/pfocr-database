caption: The role of HIV proteins in disrupting molecular and signaling pathways.
  The figure depicts how HIV and HPV activate complementary molecular pathways that
  collectively promote cervical carcinogenesis. HIV proteins particularly Tat, Nef,
  and Vpu downregulate CD4, stimulate PI3K/AKT, MAPK, and NF-κB signaling, and enhance
  transcriptional activity through Tat-mediated TAF recruitment. These effects reduce
  apoptosis, increase metabolic activation, and drive sustained IL-6 and TNF-α release.
  At the same time, HPV oncoproteins E6 and E7 inactivate the tumor suppressors p53
  and Rb, leading to uncontrolled cell-cycle entry and impaired apoptotic responses
