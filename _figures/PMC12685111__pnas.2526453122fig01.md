  of the IL-12-IL-12Rβ2-STAT4-T-bet-Il12rb2 and IFN-γ-STAT1-T-bet-Ifng pathways. The
  Adm–RAMP3/CRLR–cAMP–PKA–CREB pathway (highlighted), triggered by persistent TCR
  activation, fuels the IL-12-IL-12Rβ2-STAT4-mediated positive feedback loops at a
  late stage of Th1 cell differentiation. Solid arrows indicate signal flow and/or
  positive regulation. Dashed arrows indicate a possible direct gene induction yet
