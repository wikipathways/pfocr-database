caption: The physiological functions of ZDHHC5. A. ZDHHC5 mediates S-palmitoylation
  of NOD1/2, enabling their membrane trafficking to phagosomes. This promotes the
  recognition of phosphorylated derivatives of peptidoglycan (such as iE-DAP and MDP)
  by NODs and the subsequent activation of the NF-κB/MAPK pathways, thereby initiating
  inflammatory responses against intracellular bacteria. B. ZDHHC5, enriched in axons,
  mediates Gp130 palmitoylation to facilitate JAK/STAT3 activation in retrograde injury
  signaling, promoting neuronal survival and regeneration. C. ZDHHC5 promotes oligodendrocyte
  differentiation and (re)myelination by mediating STAT3 palmitoylation, enabling
  its phosphorylation and transcriptional activation. D. ZDHHC5 regulates excitatory
  synapse formation and maintenance. E. Palmitoylation of Flotillin-2 by ZDHHC5 is
  essential for neurite outgrowth. F. ZDHHC5 palmitoylates NCX1, promoting its inactivation
  to reduce Ca2 + efflux and regulate cardiac contractility. G. ZDHHC5 modulates GPCR
  signaling by palmitoylating Gαs and Gαi subunits, synchronizing downstream cAMP
  production and calcium oscillations to regulate myocardial contractility
