caption: 'Programming of sugar metabolism during macrophage polarization. Upon activation,
  macrophages undergo metabolic reprogramming aligned with their functional phenotype.
  Proinflammatory M1 macrophages shift to aerobic glycolysis (the Warburg effect),
  characterized by high glucose uptake, lactate production, and breaks in the TCA
  cycle at IDH and SDH, leading to citrate/succinate accumulation. In contrast, anti-inflammatory
  M2 macrophages primarily rely on OXPHOS, supported by an intact TCA cycle and FAO.
  α-KG: α-Ketoglutarate; Acetyl-CoA: Acetyl-coenzyme A; CPT1: Carnitine palmitoyltransferase

  PDH: Pyruvate dehydrogenase; PKM2: Pyruvate kinase M2; PPP: Pentose phosphate pathway;
