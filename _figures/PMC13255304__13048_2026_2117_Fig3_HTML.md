caption: 'Schematic of TGF-β-induced canonical (SMAD-dependent) and noncanonical (SMAD-independent)
  signaling pathways. Inactive TGF-β is secreted into the ECM and sequestered within
  the LLC. The integrin-mediated activation of the LLC and subsequent release of TGF-β
  can be modulated by fibrillin. Upon activation, TGF-β binds to TGFR2, leading to
  the recruitment and phosphorylation of TGFR1, thereby forming a heteromeric TGFR
  complex that initiates either the SMAD-dependent or non-SMAD signaling pathway.
  In the SMAD-dependent pathway, the activated TGFR complex phosphorylates receptor-regulated
  SMADs, which then form a complex with the common-mediator SMAD. This complex translocates
  into the nucleus and promotes the transcription of fibrosis-related genes, including
  CTGF, α-SMA, MMPs, and TIMPs. In the non-SMAD pathway, the activated TGFR complex
  engages various downstream signaling components—such as the SHC adaptor protein
  (SHC)/growth factor receptor bound protein 2 (GRB2)/Son of sevenless homolog (SOS),
  TNF receptor associated factor (TRAF)4/6, par-6 family cell polarity regulator (PAR6),
  and phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase (PI3K) cascades—to mediate alternative cellular
  responses. Abbreviations: α-SMA Alpha-Smooth Muscle Actin, Co-SMAD Common-mediator
  Small Mothers Against Decapentaplegic, CTGF Connective Tissue Growth Factor, ECM
  Extracellular Matrix, GRB2 Growth Factor Receptor-Bound Protein 2, LCC Latency-Associated
  Complex, LTBP Latent Transforming Growth Factor-β Binding Protein, MMP Matrix Metalloproteinase,
  PAR6 Partitioning Defective 6 Homolog, PI3K Phosphatidylinositol 3-Kinase, SHC Src
  Homology 2 Domain-Containing Transforming Protein 1, SMAD Small Mothers Against
  Decapentaplegic, SOS Son of Sevenless, TGFR1 Transforming Growth Factor-β Receptor
  1, TGFR2 Transforming Growth Factor-β Receptor 2, TGF-β Transforming Growth Factor-β,
  TIMP Tissue Inhibitor of Metalloproteinases, TRAF4/6 Tumor Necrosis Factor Receptor-Associated
  Factor 4/6'
