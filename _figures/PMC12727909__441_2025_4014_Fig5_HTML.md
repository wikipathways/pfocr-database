caption: Modulation of cell death pathways by Ehrlichia spp. Ehrlichia spp. manage
  to avoid the degradation of their replicative compartment by the cellular machinery
  using different T4SS effector proteins, such as Etf-3-dependent inhibition of lysosomal
  recruitment and Etf-2 to prevent vacuole maturation. Interestingly, Etf-1 is not
  involved in these pathways, but rather in the inhibition of the intrinsic apoptosis
  pathway. The T1SS is also employed to secrete effector proteins, such as TRP47,
  TRP32, and TRP120, to ensure host cell survival
