caption: Mechanism diagram of mitochondrial oxidative stress, apoptosis, calcium homeostasis,
  DAMPs. ROS in mitochondrial oxidative stress is mainly produced by superoxide anions
  generated during the etc process and cleared by the enzyme defense system (sod,
  cat, gr, and GPx) and the non-enzyme defense system (vitamin e, C, and GSH), mitochondria-induced
  apoptosis is initiated by the pro-apoptotic protein Bax, which triggers MOMP. This
  event promotes the massive release of pro-apoptotic factors from mitochondria, subsequently
  initiating the caspase cascade. The activation of this cascade leads to oligonucleosomal
  DNA fragmentation, ultimately resulting in cellular apoptosis. Bcl-2, bcl-xL, and
  IAPs can suppress apoptotic processes. Smac/Diablo and Omi/HtrA2 are capable of
  reversing the apoptosis-inhibiting effect exerted by IAPs, mitochondria take up
  Ca2+ via VDACs localized on the OMM and MCU on the IMM, and extrude Ca2+ through
  ion exchange mechanisms, thereby dynamically regulating mitochondrial Ca2+ concentration,
  genetic materials (mtDNA, mtRNA), metabolites (ATP, succinate, and ROS), and proteins
  (NFPs, cyt C) released during mitochondrial lytic cell death can regulate cellular
  functions through immune, inflammatory, and other pathways
