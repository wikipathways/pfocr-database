caption: Schematic of the peptidoglycan biosynthetic pathway. PG precursor synthesis
  begins in the cytoplasm with the conversion of UDP-GlcNAc to UDP-MurNAc, followed
  by the sequential addition of amino acids onto MurNAc. The UDP-MurNAc-pentapeptide
  is added onto a lipid carrier in the cytoplasmic membrane, where it receives GlcNAc,
  and the lipidated disaccharide pentapeptide (lipid-II) is flipped into the periplasm
  to be polymerized by the action of glycosyltransferases (GTase) and transpeptidases
  (TPase). D-cycloserine depletes D-ala−D-ala by inhibiting alanine racemase and D-Ala
  ligase. TPase activity is the target of β-lactam antibiotics
