caption: NAT10-mediated ac4C modification in human non-cancer diseases. A In MASLD
  and obesity, NAT10 enhances hepatic lipogenesis and adipogenesis by stabilizing
  key metabolic transcripts, including Srebf1, Scap, Srebp-1c, and KLF9; B In sepsis,
  NAT10 drives multi-organ injury by activating GABABR1-ROS/NLRP3 signaling and inducing
  neutrophil pyroptosis. In the lung, it further promotes ferroptosis via TFRC, pyroptosis
  via ULK1–STING pathway, and mitochondrial dysfunction through HMGB1; C In autoimmune
  diseases, NAT10 stabilizes PTX3 to promote fibroblast-like synoviocyte invasion
  in RA, regulates immune–metabolic networks in SLE, and is reduced in AS; D In cardiovascular
  pathologies, NAT10 facilitates cardiac hypertrophy, fibrosis, and ferroptotic injury,
  yet also contributes to heart regeneration following injury; E In aging, NAT10 preserves
  nuclear and mitochondrial integrity, while its inhibition alleviates HGPS defects
