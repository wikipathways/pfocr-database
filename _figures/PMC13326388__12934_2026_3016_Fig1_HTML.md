caption: 'Microbial engineering for shikimic acid synthesis. Shikimic acid is synthesized
  from PEP and E4P through series of enzymatic reactions. To enhance shikimic acid
  synthesis, several strategies were conducted: (i) replacing PTS system by introducing
  a Zymomonas mobilis-derived non-phosphorylative glucose uptake system to enhance
  PEP supply; (ii) blocking pyruvate bypass for reducing byproduct formation and enhance
  precursor availability; (iii) enhancing the PP and shikimic acid pathways for promoting
  its synthesis by enhancing metabolic flux through these interconnected pathways;
  and (iv) optimizing fermentation conditions for altering substrate utilization.
  Genes: glf, glucose-stimulated protein; glk, glucokinase; aroG, 3-deoxy-d-arabino-heptulosonate-7-phosphate
  synthase; aroB, 3-dehydroquinate synthase; aroD, 3-dehydroquinate dehydratase; aroE,
  shikimic acid dehydrogenase; ldhA, lactate dehydrogenase; poxB, pyruvate dehydrogenase;
  adhE, alcohol dehydrogenase; pta, phosphate acetyltransferase; tktA, transketolase;
  talB, transaldolase; zwf, glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase. G6P glucose-6-phosphate,
  F6P fructose-6-phosphate, G3P glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate, Ru5P ribulose-5-phosphate;
  Xu5P xylulose-3-phosphate, R5P ribose-5-phosphate, S7P sedobertulose-7-phosphate,
  DAHP 3-deoxy-d-arabino-heptulosonate-7-phosphate, DHQ 3-dehydroquinate, DHS 3-dehydroshikimic
  acid'
