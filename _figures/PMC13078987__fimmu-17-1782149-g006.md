caption: The pathological mechanisms of the inflammation-immune axis within the muscle
  microenvironment. GCs induce a state of chronic low-grade inflammation, distinctly
  marked by M1/M2 macrophage imbalance and elevated circulating cytokines (such as
  TNF-α and IL-6). This chronic inflammatory state continuously activates the NF-κB
  and FoxO signaling pathways, which in turn strongly upregulate muscle-specific E3
  ubiquitin ligases (MuRF1 and MAFbx/Atrogin-1). The robust activation of these ligases
  executes rapid proteolysis, leading to substantial muscle protein loss, decreased
  cross-sectional area, and definitive muscle degeneration. Solid arrows indicate
  direct molecular activation or pathological progression. Upward and downward arrows
  (↑/↓) denote upregulation or downregulation
