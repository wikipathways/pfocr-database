caption: Lipid mediators of the arachidonic acid signaling cascade. The enzymes lipoxygenases,
  cyclooxygenases, and cytochrome P450 epoxygenases can metabolize diverse fatty acids
  and fatty acid-containing lipids, and their preferred substrate is arachidonic acid.
  These enzymes and their downstream pathways enable the synthesis of a wide spectrum
  of lipid mediators, several of which possess biological activity. Lipid mediators
  participate in both autocrine and paracrine signaling and are thereby involved in
  various cellular functions, homeostasis, and inflammation. cPLA2α, phospholipase
  A2α; DHETs, dihydroxyeicosatrienoic acids; DiHETEs, dihydroxyeicosatetraenoic acids;
  EET, epoxyeicosatrienoic acids; HETEs, hydroxyeicosatetraenoic acids; KETEs, oxo-eicosatetraenoic
  acids; PG, prostaglandin; TX, thromboxane
