caption: Mechanisms of microbiome-mediated modulation of host signaling. (a) Microbial
  metabolites like SCFAs, tryptophan, and polyamines bind to host GPCRs and regulate
  epigenetic changes, immune responses, and barrier function. (b) MAMPs such as LPS,
  peptidoglycan, and flagellin are recognized by host PRRs (e.g., TLR2, TLR4, NODs,
  etc.), activating NF-κB and MAPK pathways, leading to inflammatory cytokine release,
  immune defense, and pyroptosis. (c) Bacterial extracellular vesicles (BEVs) carry
  bioactive molecules influencing gut-brain communication and activating TLR2/TLR4
  on epithelial cells. This changes host gene expression and triggers IL-10 production
  by CD4+ T cells, helping maintain homeostasis. (d) Microbial enzymes (proteases,
  glycosidases) and effectors modulate host immune signaling, inflammation, and cell
  survival
