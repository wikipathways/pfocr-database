caption: The biosynthetic gene cluster from N. carnea contains enzymes capable of
  catalyzing the production of nocardichelin. A. The production of HSC by NcdI, NcdJ,
  and NcdE-ATx has been demonstrated here. B. A possible route to HTC has not yet
  been defined, although the involvement of the fatty acyl ligase NcdN, carrier protein
  NcdK, dehydrogenase NcdL, and acyltransferase NcdM are candidate proteins. C. The
  salicylate synthase NcdB, adenylating enzyme NcdA, and NRPS NcdE are proposed to
  produce the PCP-bound salicyl-oxazoline intermediate. D. Final conversion of the
  intermediates to nocardichelin is proposed to use the NIS synthetase NcdF and the
  aminolysis reaction catalyzed by the free-standing condensation domain NcdH, which
  can occur through alternate pathways that differ in the order of the two reactions
