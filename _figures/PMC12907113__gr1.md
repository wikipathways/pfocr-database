caption: The signaling pathway involved in keratinocyte proliferation in psoriasisProinflammatory
  cytokines such as IL-17A and TNF-α bind to their receptors to activate the PI3K/AKT
  and NF-κB signaling pathways, affecting CDK and cyclin D1 expression to induce cell
  proliferation. The IL-22 receptor 1/IL-10 receptor 2 (IL-22R1/IL-10R2) heterodimer
  activates STAT3 by phosphorylating tyrosine kinase 2 (TYK2) and JAK1 and induces
  proliferation. Activation of Wnt5a signaling increases the phosphorylation level
  of GSK3β within this complex, reducing its inhibitory effect on β-catenin. This
  leads to the translocation of β-catenin into the nucleus, where it regulates downstream
  targets such as cyclin D1 and c-Myc, thereby modulating keratinocyte proliferation
