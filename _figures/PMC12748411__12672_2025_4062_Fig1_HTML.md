caption: The figure illustrates the molecular mechanisms of ferroptosis across six
  panels (A–F) A Iron uptake via transferrin receptor 1 (TFR1) increases intracellular
  Fe³⁺, which is reduced to Fe²⁺, contributing to the labile iron pool and promoting
  ferroptosis through reactive oxygen species (ROS) generation. B The system xc⁻ (SLC7A11/SLC3A2)
  imports cystine in exchange for glutamate (Glu). Cystine is reduced to cysteine,
  which is used to synthesize glutathione (GSH) via glutamate-cysteine ligase (GCL)
  and glutathione synthetase (GSSG). GPX4 activity to reduce lipid peroxidation, thereby
  inhibiting ferroptosis. C ACSL4 incorporates polyunsaturated fatty acids (PUFAs)
  into phospholipids, which are oxidized by lipoxygenases (LOXs) to form PUFA-OOH,
  driving lipid peroxidation and ferroptosis. GPX4 counteracts this by reducing PUFA-OOH
  to PUFA-OH using GSH. D The FSP1-CoQ10 pathway reduces CoQ10 to CoQH2 using NAD(P)H,
  acting as a lipid radical scavenger (VK/VKH2) to suppress lipid peroxidation and
  ferroptosis. E Nrf2 upregulates SLC7A11, enhancing cystine uptake and GSH synthesis,
  which in turn supports GPX4 activity to reduce lipid peroxidation and inhibit ferroptosis.
  F p53 downregulates SLC7A11, reducing GSH levels, while upregulating p21 or SOD,
  which can either promote or inhibit ferroptosis depending on the cellular context
  by affecting ROS and iron levels
