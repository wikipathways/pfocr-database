caption: Schematic diagram of PAC1R-mediated signaling pathways regulating neuroblastoma
  cell proliferation and migration. Upon binding of PACAP38, PAC1R activates multiple
  G protein pathways, including Gαs, Gα12/13, and Gαq/11. Activation of Gαs promotes
  cell proliferation via the cAMP/EPAC pathway, which stimulates ERK and AKT signaling
  independently of PKA. Concurrently, activation of Gαq/11 and Gα12/13 drives cell
  migration through the RhoGEF/RhoA/ROCK signaling axis. The PAC1R-selective antibody
  AMG-301 effectively blocks PACAP38-induced G protein activation, as well as downstream
  proliferative and migratory responses in neuroblastoma cells. The figure was created
  using BioRender.com
