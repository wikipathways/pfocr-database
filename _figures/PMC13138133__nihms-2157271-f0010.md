caption: In SS rats, an increase in dietary salt intake produces an increase in arterial
  pressure, afferent arteriolar autoregulatory dysfunction, and associated kidney
  injury. The transcription factor, ETS-1, participates directly in hypertension-associated
  kidney injury and afferent autoregulatory dysfunction in SS rats [15, 16] and is
  activated by cyclic stretch [61]. As shown by other groups, CCR2 is the primary
  receptor for CCL2 [51, 77] and is active in vascular smooth muscle cells [24]. The
  present studies showed that the introduction of cyclic stress increases Ccr2 and
  Ccl2 mRNA, promoting secretion of this chemokine (CCL2), by kidney microvascular
  smooth muscle cells (KMSMC). Activation of CCR2 alters the vascular biology of KMSMCs
  during stress by promoting reduction in Notch3/MLCK/MLC pathway activity through
  Notch3. This effect alters KSMSC tone and autoregulation to promote hypertension-associated
  kidney injury. Loss of Ccl2 prevented these findings in SS rats
