caption: Outer membrane protein stimulates the clearance of oxygen species produced
  by mitochondria. The reactive oxygen species (ROS) produced by Gram-negative bacterial
  outer membrane proteins can activate mitochondrial permeability transition (PT)
  pore opening through adenine nucleotide transferase (ANT) and voltage-dependent
  anion channel (VDAC) sensitive proteins, promote mitochondrial membrane rupture,
  and release apoptotic proteins Bax and Bcl-2. In addition, mitochondrial fission
  protein 1 (Fis1) senses ROS and mediates covalent dimerization of Fis1 through its
  cysteine disulfide bond, recruiting mitochondrial dynamics-related protein 1 (Drp1),
  leading to mitochondrial fission and clearance of damaged mitochondria. The clearance
  of ROS mainly involves the reversible transformation of sulfur groups (such as superoxide
  dismutase and thioredoxin), the action of peroxidases in the body, and activating
  the Nrf2 signal pathway of antioxidant enzyme-related genes. Created in https://BioRender.com
