caption: Biosynthesis of agarwood sesquiterpenes and fungal regulation. (a) Sesquiterpenes
  are synthesized via the plastidial MEP pathway and cytosolic MVA pathway, producing
  IPP and DMAPP, which condense to GPP and FPP. The cyclization of FPP yields linear,
  mono-, bi-, and tricyclic sesquiterpenes, which are further diversified by hydroxylation
  and action of CYP450 enzymes. (b) Phaeoacremonium rubrigenum modulates sesquiterpene
  biosynthesis in Aquilaria sinensis callus through phosphorylation of MYB, WRKY,
  and bZIP TFs. Promoters of biosynthetic genes contain cis-elements (G-box, W-box,
  GCC-box) for TF binding. Correlation analysis links phospho-TFs to enzymes; the
  FPS genes SesTPS1 and SesTPS2 are upregulated in response to fungal infection
