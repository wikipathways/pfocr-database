caption: A schematic illustration of circ-0001313’s role in colon and lung cancer
  development. Circ-0001313 acts as a sponge for miR-510-5p in colon cancer (left),
  increasing AKT2 levels and the P13K/AKT pathway activation. This activation affects
  Bcl-1, Bcl-2, and Bcl-3, as well as Bad, promoting cell proliferation while reducing
  apoptosis via mTOR signaling. Circ-0001313 sponges miR-452 in lung cancer (right),
  leading to the overexpression of HMGB3, which enhances the phosphorylation of the
  Ras/Raf/MEK/ERK signaling cascade, thereby boosting tumor cell motility and invasion
  in response to extracellular signals
