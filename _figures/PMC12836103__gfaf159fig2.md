caption: Pathway of care for patients with ADPKD-associated PLD. The liver should
  be examined in existing images of all ADPKD patients. Once PLD has been identified,
  the patient should be informed about this manifestation accompanied by obtaining
  information on potential PLD-associated symptoms. We suggest that patients with
  asymptomatic PLD should be invited for routine follow-up, e.g. after 2–3 years.
  All patients with symptomatic PLD should be referred to expert centres initiating
  structured assessment of symptoms, signs of malnutrition/sarcopenia and liver volume.
  These factors are the basis for individualized selection of treatment options in
  a shared decision-making process with the patient. In any case, further support
  involving psychologists, physiotherapy and dietary counselling may be needed. Created
