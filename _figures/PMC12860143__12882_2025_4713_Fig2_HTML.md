caption: 'Lipid–immune crosstalk in MN: a vicious cycle. Conceptual diagram illustrating
  the bi-directional and self-perpetuating nature of immunometabolic crosstalk. Immune
  arm: autoantibody binding is facilitated by cholesterol-rich lipid rafts, which
  act as nucleation sites. Subsequent oxidized lipids (oxLDL) amplify MAC formation
  via the alternative complement pathway. Metabolic Arm: Podocyte injury from MAC
  and inflammation disrupts cholesterol efflux (ABCA1/ApoE), leading to intracellular
  lipotoxicity (oxLDL, Ceramides). This lipotoxicity directly feeds back (red arrows)
  to enhance inflammation, promote T-cell and B-cell pathogenicity, and further compromise
  podocyte function, thereby perpetuating the glomerular damage cycle'
