caption: In vivo terpenoid production in engineered E. coli and proposed cyclisation
  pathways for the main products produced by bCinS and previously produced variants.
  The C5 isoprenoid precursors isopentenyl diphosphate (IPP) and dimethylallyl diphosphate
  (DMAPP) are supplied by a heterologous mevalonate (MVA) pathway and converted to
  geranyl diphosphate (GPP) and farnesyl diphosphate (FPP), the substrates for mono‐
  and sesquiterpenoids, by a heterologous GPPS and endogenous FPPS (IspA). The platform
  can be expanded to diterpenoid production by replacing GPPS with IspAM22 (D2G, C155G),
  which is capable of producing the C20 substrate geranylgeranyl diphosphate (GGPP)
