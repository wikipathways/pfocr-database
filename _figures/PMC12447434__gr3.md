caption: 'Regulation of carotenoid biosynthesis and turnover by WRKY TFs.Endogenous
  carotenoid biosynthesis occurs in plastids and is supplied by precursors from the
  methylerythritol 4-phosphate (MEP) pathway, whose flux is mainly controlled by 1-deoxy-D-xylulose
  5-phosphate synthase (DXS). The rate-limiting step in the carotenoid biosynthesis
  pathway is catalyzed by phytoene synthase (PSY). Key proteins influencing chromoplast
  differentiation are indicated. After several desaturation and isomerization steps,
  lycopene is produced and subsequently converted via cyclization into the α-carotene
  and β-carotene branches. A wide range of carotenoids are degraded by carotenoid
  cleavage dioxygenases (CCDs) or 9-cis-epoxycarotenoid dioxygenases (NCEDs) to produce
  apocarotenoids. Metabolites are shown in bold and colored according to their natural
  pigment, whereas non-pigmented intermediates are in black. Enzymes and regulators
  are presented in non-bold font. WRKY TFs involved in the regulation of carotenoid
  metabolism are highlighted in gray boxes. Red arrows represent activation; black
  lines ending in bars indicate repression. Abbreviations: IPP, isopentenyl diphosphate;
  DMAPP, dimethylallyl diphosphate; IPI, isopentenyl diphosphate isomerase; GGPP,
  geranylgeranyl diphosphate; GGPPS, GGPP synthase; PSY, phytoene synthase; PDS, phytoene
  desaturase; Z-ISO, ζ-carotene isomerase; ZDS, ζ-carotene desaturase; CRTISO, carotenoid
  isomerase; LCYE, lycopene ε-cyclase; LCYB, lycopene β-cyclase; CHYB, β-carotene
  hydroxylase; CYP97C, cytochrome P450 monooxygenase 97C; ZEP, zeaxanthin epoxidase;
  VDE, violaxanthin de-epoxidase; NXS, neoxanthin synthase; CCD, carotenoid cleavage
  dioxygenase; NCED, 9-cis-epoxycarotenoid dioxygenase'
