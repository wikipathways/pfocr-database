  class I gene expression and antigen presentation. It also modulates inflammatory
  signaling pathways, including suppression of NF-κB and negative regulation of type
  I interferon responses. In metabolic disease contexts, NLRC5 is associated with
  lipid-metabolic regulation, obesity, diabetes-related antigen presentation, and
  diabetic nephropathy. NLRC5 cooperates with PPARγ to regulate lipid-metabolic gene
  expression and influences renal inflammation and fibrosis through TGF-β/Smad signaling
