  stress, and inflammatory signals, initiate profibrotic signaling cascades. These
  inputs engage multiple receptor systems (PDGFR, TGFR, BMPR, Wnt, TLR, and IL-1R/TNFR),9,22,23,24,25

  fibroblast activation, extracellular matrix remodeling, and proinflammatory responses
