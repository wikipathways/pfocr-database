caption: Avutometinib plus defactinib mechanism of action. Avutometinib is a RAF/MEK
  clamp that potently inhibits MEK while also suppressing the compensatory reactivation
  of MEK by upstream raf [67]. However, this inhibition induces compensatory activation
  of FAK and potentially other parallel tumor growth pathways (eg, PI3K, YAP, and
  RhoA), bypassing RAS blockade. Defactinib is a selective inhibitor of FAK, targeting
  a key adaptive resistance mechanism to the RAS/MAPK pathway [68,69]
