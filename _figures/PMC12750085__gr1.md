caption: The proposed model for LySR activation and regulation. In C. elegans, RNAi
  of specific lysosomal v-ATPase subunits (e.g., vha-6 RNAi) activates a beneficial
  lysosomal surveillance response (LySR) that is controlled by the GATA transcription
  factor ELT-2. The role of ELT-2 in LySR is dependent on CBP-1-mediated histone acetylation
  at H3K27Ac. Activation of LySR is typified by the up-regulation of a large panel
  of lysosome/proteolysis-related genes (e.g., cpr-5 and cpr-8) and thereby improves
  proteostasis, reduces protein aggregates, and extends the lifespan/healthspan in
  normal aging worms and in several worm models of neurodegenerative diseases. Dietary
  restriction partially hijacks the LySR pathway to promote longevity
