caption: The transduction of TGF-β pathway signaling and mechanisms of drug action.
  (A) Extracellular microfibrils usually bind to large latent complexes composed,
  including TGFβ, latency-associated peptide (LAP), and latent transforming growth
  factor-b binding proteins (LTBPs). This interaction is proposed to suppress the
  release of free and active TGFβ. MFS leads to abnormal microfiber and failure of
  large latent complex sequestration and subsequent activation of TGF-β signaling
  cascades (right side). Free and active TGFβ interacts with its cell surface receptor
  and phosphorylation (P) of the R-Smad proteins (R-Smads 2 and 3), which form heteromeric
  complexes with Smad4 and translocate from cytoplasm to nucleus and mediate TGFβ-induced
  transcriptional responses. (B) Losartan acts on angiotensin receptors(ATR) to reduce
  TGFβ signaling activation. Methotrexate inhibits TGFβ activity. Doxycycline and
  indomethacin indirectly modulate the TGFβ signaling pathway through MMPs, thereby
  regulating extracellular matrix stability and TGFβ release
