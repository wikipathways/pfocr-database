caption: 'The schematic representation illustrates the ethylene and abscisic acid
  (ABA) signaling pathways under salt stress conditions in Brassica napus. This figure
  integrates gene expression profiles into the principal regulatory pathways involved
  in the response to salt stress. On the left, the ethylene signaling cascade is depicted,
  involving RAN1, ETR/ERS, CTR1, MKK9, MPK3/6, and transcription factors like EIN2,
  EIN3/EIL, and ERF1, culminating in the activation of downstream defense genes, such
  as CHI-B and PDE1.2. On the right, the ABA signaling pathway is initiated by PYR/PYL
  receptors, leading to PP2C inhibition, SnRK2 activation, and subsequent activation
  of downstream kinases (MPK17, MKK3, MPK10/14) by an unknown mechanism of activation
  (?), which contribute to stress adaptation in plants. The activation of MPK6 also
  results in H2O2 signaling and CAT1 expression. Heatmaps display gene expression
  changes between control (CK) and salt-stressed plants based on z-scores: red signifies
  upregulation, and blue indicates downregulation. Each heatmap box represents a specific
  B. napus gene annotated in the pathway'
