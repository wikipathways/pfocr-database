caption: A Both TIGIT and PD-1 inhibit signaling from the immune-activating receptor
  CD226. PD-1 binds to PD-L1 and recruits SHP2, ultimately leading to the inhibition
  of intracellular signaling from CD226. TIGIT accomplishes this extracellularly by
  competing with CD226 and disrupting the structure of the CD226 dimer. B Using antibodies
  to inhibit the PD-1/PD-L1 pathway increases TIGIT expression, leading to immune
  inhibition. C Combination of anti-TIGIT and anti-PD-1 antibodies can release the
  inhibition of CD226 signaling and activate T cells. D Bispecific antibodies (bsAbs)
  inhibit both TIGIT and PD-1/PD-L1 and bridge immune cells and tumor cells. Created
