caption: 'The ASF axis and its regulated hepatic DNL process. A: The ASF axis constitutes
  the core regulatory pathway governing hepatic lipid metabolism. Phosphorylated AMPK
  modulates the nuclear translocation of SREBP1, thereby regulating the expression
  of FASN and subsequent DNL processes, which is critical for maintaining hepatic
  lipid homeostasis. B: DNL refers to the process by which hepatocytes (as well as
  adipose tissue) convert excess non-lipid precursors such as carbohydrates into fatty
  acids. These fatty acids can ultimately be synthesized into triglycerides for storage.
  Notably, the excess accumulation of long-chain fatty acids, typified by hexadecanoic
  acid, exhibits potential lipotoxicity. ASF: AMPK-SREBP1-FASN; AMPK: AMP-activated
  protein kinase; Acaca: Acetyl-CoA carboxylase alpha; Cab39: Calcium binding protein
  39; Camkk2: Calcium/calmodulin dependent protein kinase kinase 2; Cbr4: Carbonyl
  reductase 4; DNL: De Novo Lipogenesis; FASN: Fatty acid synthase; Hsd17b8: Hydroxysteroid
  17-beta dehydrogenase 8; Htd2: Hydroxyacyl-thioester dehydratase type 2; Irs1: Insulin
  receptor substrate 1; LKB1: Liver kinase B1; MAP3K7: Mitogen-activated protein kinase
  kinase kinase 7 ; Mcat: Malonyl CoA: ACP acyltransferase (mitochondrial); Mecr:
  Mitochondrial trans-2-enoyl-CoA reductase; Olah: Oleoyl-ACP hydrolase; Oxsm: 3-Oxoacyl-ACP
  synthase, mitochondrial; Pdpk1: 3-Phosphoinositide dependent protein kinase-1; PI3K:
  Phosphoinositide 3-kinase; Pkcl: Protein kinase C iota; Srebp1: Sterol regulatory
  element-binding protein 1; Stradb: STE20-related kinase adaptor beta'
