caption: Starvation induces a metabolic rate depressionSchematic integrative map representing
  the interconnection of the eight differentially represented pathways involved in
  the energetic metabolism, out of the 21 identified (FELLA). Pink boxes represent
  metabolites accumulated in the starved condition, while green boxes represent those
  in the fed condition. Pink and green gene identifiers correspond to genes respectively
  over-represented or under-represented in the starved condition by comparison to
  the fed condition at 0 h post-cohabitation. Full arrows represent the direct conversion
  of one metabolite from another, while the dotted lines represent indirect conversion.
  The electron transport chain (ETC) followed by the oxidative phosphorylation (OxPhos)
  are the link with the pathways and the energetic production in a eukaryotic cell
  in a classical aerobic state
