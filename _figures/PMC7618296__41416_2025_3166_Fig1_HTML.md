caption: Pathway 1 is the currently recommended CA125-USS sequential pathway in England (a).
  In the risk-based triage pathways (b), we used Ovatools risk <1%, 1–3% and ≥3% in
  Pathway 2, or their equivalent age-adjusted CA125 thresholds in Pathway 3, to indicate
  low, moderate and high risks, respectively, in base-case analysis. In pathways with
  concurrent CA125 and USS (c), the abnormal CA125 threshold is Ovatools risk ≥3%
  (Pathway 4), its equivalent age-adjusted CA125 threshold (Pathway 5), or CA125 ≥ 35 U/mL
  (Pathway 6). This model assumed a one-time testing pathway based on initial symptomatic
  presentation. False negative cases were assumed to re-enter the primary care pathway
  once and be diagnosed within 1 year. CA125, cancer antigen 125; OC, ovarian cancer;
  USS, pelvic/transvaginal ultrasound scan
