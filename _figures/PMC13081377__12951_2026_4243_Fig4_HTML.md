  osteoinductive mechanisms of BP@Gel-CD[SA] hydrogel in addressing lung cancer-induced
  osseous metastases. The photothermal and photodynamic properties of BP nanosheets
  enable hyperthermia and ROS-induced mitochondrial impairment, concurrently augmenting
  antitumor immunity via immunogenic cell death (ICD). The localized administration
  of SA further stimulates neoplastic cells to secrete IFNβ, facilitating the recruitment
  of immature dendritic cells (DCs) and subsequent activation of the adaptive immune
  response. Over an extended period, BP nanosheets foster osteogenesis at sites of
  tumor-mediated bone degradation, thereby enhancing bone integrity in the affected
