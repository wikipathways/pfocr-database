caption: tNIR therapy restores glutamatergic synapse and retrograde endocannabinoid
  signaling disrupted by chronic SD. (A) KEGG pathway map of the glutamatergic synapse
  (mmu04724) showing proteins upregulated in the tNIR group compared to SD in red
  (p ​< ​0.05) or pink (p ​≈ ​0.05). Purple highlights indicate concurrent upregulation
  in calcium signaling. (B) Z-score plot comparing key proteins and metabolites across
  SD, tNIR-treated, and control groups. tNIR reverses SD-induced deficits in glutamatergic
  and calcium signaling components, including Gria2/3, Shank proteins, cAMP, and l-glutamine.
  (C) KEGG map of retrograde endocannabinoid signaling (mmu04723). Red/pink denotes
  tNIR-upregulated proteins; green/light blue indicates downregulation post-tNIR.
  Adjacent box plots show group-wise expression of key regulators, highlighting tNIR-mediated
  normalization of cannabinoid, GABAergic, and cAMP signaling components
