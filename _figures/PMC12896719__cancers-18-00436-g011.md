caption: Mechanisms of action of tirbanibulin in the prevention of non-melanoma skin
  cancer (NMSC). Tirbanibulin inhibits microtubule polymerization and Src kinase-dependent
  signaling in atypical keratinocytes within actinically damaged skin. Disruption
  of microtubule dynamics induces mitotic arrest and apoptosis, while Src kinase inhibition
  suppresses downstream pathways involved in keratinocyte proliferation and survival.
  These combined effects result in selective elimination of dysplastic keratinocyte
  clones with a limited inflammatory response, contributing to control of field cancerization
  and reduction in NMSC risk
