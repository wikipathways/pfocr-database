  pathway of anthocyanin biosynthetic and branching pathways. This pathway is constructed
  based on the KEGG pathway and involves Flavonoid biosynthesis, Flavone and flavonol
  biosynthesis, Isoflavonoid biosynthesis, and Anthocyanin biosynthesis. In the network,
  red arrows represent the main pathway of anthocyanin biosynthesis, while black arrows
  indicate other branches. Red font indicates the high accumulation of this metabolite
  in ZNX litchi, blue font indicates the high accumulation of this metabolite in GYL
  litchi, and black font indicates no significant change. Heat map section, where
  red indicates upregulation and blue indicates downregulation. (For interpretation
