caption: KEGG global metabolic pathways for genes linked to community growth efficiency
  (CGE). a Pathways represented by genes positively associated with CGE, and b pathways
  represented by genes negatively associated with CGE. Associations were defined from
  partial least squares regression (PLSR) with CGE as the response variable and metatranscriptome
  gene transcript abundances as predictor variables (top 10%, VIP > 1.6). The direction
  of association was determined by the sign of the predictor variable coefficient.
  Colored boxes highlight KEGG pathways on the global metabolism map and labels show
  pathway names (red, nucleotide; tan, xenobiotics; purple, energy; blue, carbohydrate;
  green, lipid; orange, amino acid; and magenta, vitamin and cofactor metabolism).
  Pathway names indicate if genes represent primarily biosynthesis or degradation;
  otherwise, it is shown as “metabolism.” Pent., pentose; TCA, tricarboxylic acid
