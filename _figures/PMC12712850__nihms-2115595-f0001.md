caption: Aging induces qualitative changes in various niches in the heart. In particular,
  that associated with large vessels (the vascular niche, niche 7 according to Rodriguez
  Morales et al4), containing smooth muscle cells (SMCs), macrophages (MPs) and fibroblasts,
  becomes hotspots of senescent cells, Lyve1− MPs are recruited, and inflammation
  and fibrosis are stimulated. At the molecular level, the C3:C3ar1 interaction promotes
  fibroblast-MP interaction and MP polarization. The niche activates the immune inhibitory
  (don’t eat me) pathway, including Cd47 and signal regulatory protein α (Sirpa),
  to prevent phagocytosis of senescent cells by MPs. Other cytokines secreted from
  senescent cells may promote inflammaging and pathological remodeling. Although senescent
  hotspots are not abundant, niches containing cardiomyocytes (CMs) (niches 3, 4,
  5, 8 and 9) exhibit age-dependent changes in genes involved in circadian regulation
  and hepatic stellate cell activation (Acta2, Cygb and Rps6ka1), the contributions
  of which to cardiac aging remain to be elucidated
