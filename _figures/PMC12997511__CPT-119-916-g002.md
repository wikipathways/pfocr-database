caption: Diagrammatic representation of the intracellular metabolism of thiopurine
  drugs. Azathioprine, mercaptopurine, and thioguanine are all inactivated by thiopurine
  methyltransferase (TPMT). All three agents share the same active thioguanine nucleotides
  (TGN) metabolites. Thioguanine mono‐, di‐, and triphosphate (TGMP, TGDP, and TGTP,
  respectively) collectively constitute TGNs, with TGTP having the greatest activity.
  NUDT15 prevents TGTP incorporation into DNA (as DNA‐TG) by dephosphorylating this
  cytotoxic metabolite into inactive TGMP. Pathway images and data are available under
