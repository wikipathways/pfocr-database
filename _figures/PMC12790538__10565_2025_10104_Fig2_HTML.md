caption: Mechanisms of apoptosis in breast cancer. Death ligands (TNF-α, FasL, TRAIL)
  engage TNFR/Fas/DR4-5 to assemble TRADD–FADD–TRAF2/RIP, activate caspase-8, and
  cleave caspase-3 (extrinsic pathway). Mitochondrial stress releases cytochrome c,
  forming Apaf-1–caspase-9 to activate caspase-3 (intrinsic pathway). Bcl-2/Bcl-xL
  restrain mitochondrial permeabilization, whereas p53 promotes apoptosis partly by
  repressing Bcl-xL. Pro-survival NF-κB/STAT3 signaling counteracts these effects.
  Representative agents (e.g., berberine/curcumin; APR-246/CX-5461; inhibitors of
  NF-κB/STAT3) modulate these nodes to restore apoptotic sensitivity
