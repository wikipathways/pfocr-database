caption: 'Panel A illustrates the mechanisms of midzone and peripheral fission. Panel
  B depicts the fate of daughter mitochondria resulting from each fission type. Red
  arrows denote pathways associated with peripheral fission (<25% from the tip), and
  black arrows indicate midzone fission (central 50% region). Dynamin-related protein
  1 (Drp1) coordinates both types of fission. Midzone fission (Panel A, left): The
  endoplasmic reticulum (ER) anchors to mitochondria via PDZD8 contact sites. Inverted
  formin 2 (INF2)-dependent actin polymerization drives preconstriction. Mitochondrial
  fission factor (MFF) forms high-intensity clusters (2× brighter than the periphery)
  that recruit Drp1. Peripheral fission (Panel A, right): Lysosomes dock via TBC1D15
  at mitochondrial tips. The mitochondrial fission 1 protein (Fis1) is enriched at
  damage sites to recruit Drp1 and Parkin. Fate of daughter mitochondria (Panel B):
  Midzone fission generates functional mitochondria with intact mitochondrial DNA
  (mtDNA) and membrane potential (ΔΨm), supporting biogenesis. Peripheral fission
  produces small fragments that sequester damaged components with low ΔΨm and increased
  reactive oxygen species/calcium (ROS/Ca²⁺) levels. These components are targeted
  by autophagosomes but cannot be degraded because of impaired lysosomal clearance'
