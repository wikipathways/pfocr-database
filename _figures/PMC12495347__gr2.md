caption: Reactive‑oxygen-species (ROS) pathway triggered by natural phenolics in bacterial
  cells.Redox-active phenolics—illustrated here with gallic acid, BDMC and EGCG—undergo
  autoxidation or metal-phenolic (Fenton-like) reactions to generate O₂−, H₂O₂ and
  •OH. The accumulated ROS initiate lipid-peroxidation cascades that weaken the membrane,
  oxidise key enzymes, and produce DNA lesions (e.g., 8-OHdG), collectively driving
  stress responses and cell death. DCFH-DA fluorescence is shown as the standard in
  vitro validation of ROS elevation
