caption: Proposed mechanism of light-mediated circadian regulation of rice immunity
  enhancement. Constant light exposure significantly alleviated rice blast symptoms,
  while constant dark exposure exacerbated disease progression. Mechanistically, persistent
  light remodels the circadian network by altering the phase and amplitude of core
  clock genes. Furthermore, constant light exposure specifically activated bidirectional
  regulation of NAD⁺ metabolism by promoting de novo NAD⁺ biosynthesis and enhancing
  its hydrolysis pathway. Notably, NMN, the metabolic product of this pathway, effectively
  alleviated rice blast severity
