caption: Viral immune evasion mechanisms targeting innate immune pathwaysThe figure
  depicts representative strategies used by natural viruses to interfere with host
  innate immune defenses at multiple levels. Shown are viral proteins that inhibit
  cytosolic RNA sensing by RIG-I, suppress activation or stability of interferon regulatory
  factors, and block downstream interferon signaling through the JAK-STAT pathway.
  Viral modulation of the ISR is illustrated through inhibition of PKR activation
  and eIF2α phosphorylation, thereby limiting stress-induced translational arrest.
  The figure also depicts viral interference with humoral innate immunity, including
  inhibition of complement activation and membrane attack complex formation. Together,
  these panels summarize distinct viral mechanisms that antagonize cellular, stress-induced,
  and extracellular innate immune responses
