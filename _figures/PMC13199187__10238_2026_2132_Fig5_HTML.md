caption: In terms of sugar metabolism, LMP1 activates the mTORC1/NF-κB signaling pathway
  to enhance the transcription of GLUT1. LMP1 can also induce the expression of key
  enzymes for glycolysis such as HIF-1α and hexokinase 2 (HK2). The EBV-encoded miRNA
  is also involved in this process. miR-BART1-5p promotes aerobic glycolysis and tumor
  cell proliferation by activating the AMPK/mTOR/HIF1 pathway. LMP1 promotes lactate
  production. LMP2A increases the expression of HIF-1α and VEGF through the PI3K signaling
  pathway, and the Annexin A3/PI3K/HIF-1α axis is also involved in EBV-induced tumor
  vascular malformation and angiogenesis. The EBV-encoded miR-BART1-5p can promote
  the acquisition of an endothelial-like phenotype by tumor cells through activating
  the PI3K/Akt/mTOR pathway and regulating the expression of Sprouty2 protein, thereby
  forming vascular pseudo-generation (VM)
