  TLR/IL-1R recruits the adapter protein MyD88, which assembles a Myddosome complex
  with IRAK-4 and IRAK-1/2. This complex activates TRAF6, thereby triggering the NF-κB
  and MAPK signaling pathways. IRAK-M exerts an inhibitory effect on the NF-κB and
  MAPK signaling pathways; however, it can also activate the NF-κB pathway in a context-dependent
