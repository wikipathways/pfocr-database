caption: A working model illustrating the pGhFAD2‐1 involved in regulating cottonseed
  C18:2 content and seed size. pGhFAD2‐1 interacts with GhHDT1 and GhRPS12, thereby
  affecting cottonseed C18:2 content and seed size. Over‐expressing pGhFAD2‐1 recruits
  more GhHDT1, resulting in stronger GhHDT1‐mediated inhibition on GhFAD2‐1 expression
  at the transcriptional level. Moreover, at the translational level, pGhFAD2‐1 inhibits
  GhFAD2 protein synthesis by competitively binding to RPS12 with GhFAD2‐1 mRNA, ultimately
  disrupting C18:2 biosynthesis during cotton seed development. At the same time,
  more abundant GhHDT1 inhibits the expression of the genes in the ABA synthesis pathway,
  leading to reduced ABA accumulation and larger seeds. Knocking‐out of pGhFAD2‐1
  decreases the level of GhHDT1 binding to the promoter of GhFAD2‐1, resulting in
  a higher expression of GhFAD2‐1 at the transcriptional level. And, GhFAD2 protein
  synthesis increases due to the lack of pGhFAD2‐1 to competitively bind to RPS12.
  The reduced abundance of GhHDT1 also promotes the expression of the genes in the
  ABA synthesis pathway, leading to increased accumulation of ABA and smaller seeds.
  GhABA2, abscisic Acid Deficient 2; GhDGAT2, diacylglycerol O‐Acyltransferase 2;
  GhFAD2‐1, fatty acid desaturase 2; GhHDT1, Histone deacetylase 1; GhNCED9, 9‐cis‐epoxycarotenoid
  dioxygenase 9; GhPDAT2, diacylglycerol acyltransferase 2; RPS12, 40S Ribosomal Protein
  S12
