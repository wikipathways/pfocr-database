caption: 'Stage-specific palmitoylation sequentially regulates NLRP3 inflammasome
  assembly and GSDMD-mediated pyroptosis. In macrophages, LPS triggers NF-κB signaling
  to upregulate NLRP3 and DHHC5/9 expression, initiating a tightly regulated inflammasome
  assembly process. NLRP3 undergoes sequential palmitoylation by distinct DHHC enzymes:
  initial modification by DHHC1/3/5/7 drives its cytosolic-to-TGN translocation and
  oligomerization priming, while subsequent DHHC5/17-mediated palmitoylation stabilizes
  NLRP3 oligomers, enables NEK7 interaction, and facilitates inflammasome complex
  formation. This activation pathway is counterbalanced by DHHC12-dependent palmitoylation,
  which promotes NLRP3 degradation via CMA to prevent inflammatory overactivation.
  Concurrently during pyroptosis, DHHC5/7/9 palmitoylates GSDMD, inducing conformational
  changes that prime its pore-forming capacity and enhance caspase-1 cleavage efficiency.
  Caspase-1-processed GSDMD-NT fragments then oligomerize into stable plasma membrane
  pores and execute pyroptotic death'
