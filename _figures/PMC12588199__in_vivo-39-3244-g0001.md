caption: Proposed pathway of toll-like receptor 3 (TLR3) activation by polyinosinic:polycytidylic
  acid (poly I:C), leading to the expression of tripartite motif-containing 22 (TRIM22)
  and C-C motif chemokine ligand 5 (CCL5) in rheumatoid fibroblast-like synoviocytes
  (RFLS). In RFLS, poly I:C induces TRIM22 expression, a process involving interferon
  β (IFNB) induction and nuclear factor kappa B (NF-κB) activation. TRIM22 positively
  regulates CCL5 expression and enhances IFN-β expression through a positive feedback
  loop. CCL5 upregulation may subsequently promote leukocyte chemotaxis, leading to
  synovial inflammation. Therefore, TRIM22 may contribute to rheumatoid synovial inflammation,
  at least in part, by enhancing TLR3-mediated CCL5 expression
