caption: 'Pathogenesis of AMAN pathway (1) Campylobacter jejuni infection presents
  antigen at the gut epithelium expressing LOS mimicking neuronal gangliosides. (2)
  LOS binds TLR4/NLR on APCs, inducing IL-1β, IL-6, and TNF-α release and activates
  innate immunity. (3) Molecular mimicry: Adaptive immunity produces IgG antibodies
  against gangliosides (GM1, GD1a, GT1a). (4) Anti-ganglioside autoantibodies disrupt
  and cross the blood–brain barrier. (5) Antibody-mediated activation of complement
  system and MAC complex. (6) MAC complexes target motor axolemma at nodes of Ranvier
  and motor end terminals. MAC and macrophages disrupt Na+ channel clusters, causing
  axonal degeneration. (7) Prolonged axonal damage leads to Wallerian degeneration
  distal to the axons, i.e., pure motor paralysis with preserved sensation due to
  selective motor axon damage'
