caption: Integrated model of peppermint's metabolic response to increasing salt stress.
  Coordinated physiological and metabolic changes are observed in peppermint under
  salt stress. Increasing NaCl concentrations lead to impaired photosynthesis, which
  in turn causes a limitation of precursor molecules (e.g., triose phosphates). This
  limitation drives a metabolic reallocation. In the terpenoid pathway, genes encoding
  upstream enzymes are upregulated while the final steps leading to menthol are downregulated,
  creating a strategic bottleneck and redirecting metabolic flux toward menthofuran.
  Concurrently, the plant's flavonoid defence shifts from general, high‐volume phenolic
  production to a targeted increase in specific flavonoid conjugates, which serve
  as specialized antioxidants and photoprotectants. This integrated response demonstrates
  the plant's strategic management of compromised resources for survival
