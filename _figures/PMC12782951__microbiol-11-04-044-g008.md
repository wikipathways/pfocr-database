caption: An integrative model of EHEC infection, inhibition of autophagy via an EspF-ANXA6-dependent
  mechanism, and suppression of ATG16L1 to induce inflammation. EspF interacts with
  the host ANXA6 protein, leading to a downregulation of LC3B-II expression and an
  increase in p62 protein levels, thereby inhibiting autophagy. Furthermore, the interaction
  between EspF and ANXA6 results in the downregulation of the ATG16L1 protein. EHEC
  may disrupt the intestinal barrier via ATG16L1, enabling its entry into the host
  and subsequent activation of the NF-κB signaling pathway, which promotes the release
  of IL-1β. These effects contribute to the onset of inflammation
