caption: In the endocrine system, TAS2R activation in thyroid cells leads to an increase
  in intracellular calcium ion concentration, potentially affecting the absorption
  and utilization of iodine ions and influencing the synthesis of thyroid hormones
  (T3 and T4). In the choroid plexus epithelial cells, TAS2R activation by DB triggers
  a calcium response via the PLCβ₂ and IP₃ signaling pathways and activates cation
  flow mediated by the TRPM5 channel, which may play a role in the chemical surveillance
  and detoxification functions of the blood-brain barrier. Upon bitter activation
  of TAS2R, intracellular calcium levels rise, further promoting the secretion of
  glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) and CCK. In skin-infiltrating lymphocytes, TAS2R
  is activated by bitter agonists, potentially influencing lymphocyte migration and
  distribution through intracellular signaling pathways, though the specific mechanisms
  remain unclear
