caption: Schematic illustration of engineering and mechanism of Piezo1-exosome scaffold
  therapy for ONFH. (a) Workflow illustrating the electroporation-based preparation
  of P-Exos and their incorporation into HAMA hydrogel-coated titanium scaffolds (P-Exos@HAMA/Ti)
  for sustained, controlled exosome release. (b) Sustained release of P-Exos from
  the scaffold promotes coordinated regeneration, including osteogenic differentiation
  of BMSCs characterized by upregulated OCN, RUNX2, and Osterix expression, and angiogenic
  activation of VECs accompanied by enhanced VEGFA and CD31 levels. (c) Mechanistic
  pathway showing P-Exos-induced Ca2+ influx and Piezo1 upregulation, activating YAP1
  and β-catenin signaling cascades. (d) In a rat model of ONFH, implantation of P-Exos@HAMA/Ti
  scaffolds facilitates trabecular bone regeneration, enhances neovascularization,
  and mitigates necrotic progression, underscoring their therapeutic efficacy
