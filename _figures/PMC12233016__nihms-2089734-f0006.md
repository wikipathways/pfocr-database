caption: 'Schematic representation of ABCF1’s diverse roles in innate immune sensing,
  cytokine production, and transcription of virus genome, and viral RNA degradation.
  A. ABCF1 participates in cytosolic DNA sensing by directly binding dsDNA or by regulating
  HMGB2 [140], IFI16/IFI204 [147], and potentially the cGAS-STING pathway to activate
  TBK1-IRF3 and NF-κB, leading to the production of interferons and pro-inflammatory
  cytokines. B. In HBV-infected hepatocytes, ABCF1 binds cccDNA through its LCD and
  undergoes phase separation. ABCF1-cccDNA condensate formation [147] impairs viral
  replication by inhibiting transcriptional complex assembly on cccDNA and enhancing
  immune recognition. C. In response to dsRNA, ABCF1 binds 2–5An through its NBDs
  [158], and may modulate the degradation of viral and host RNAs by RNase L. Note:
  The “NBD” depicted in the figure represents the two NBDs in ABCF1 protein'
