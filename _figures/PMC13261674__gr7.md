caption: A proposed model for the contribution of SlBAP31s to chloroplast accumulation
  of SlPSBW2.Preproteins such as PsbWs, which contain an N-terminal chloroplast transit
  peptide (CTP) recognized by TOC receptors, are translocated across the outer envelope
  through the TOC75 channel, a process referred to as the conventional CTP-mediated
  chloroplast protein import pathway. In this study, SlBAP31s are induced by heat
  stress and interact with both the chloroplast translocon component SlTOC75 and SlPSBW2.
  These results raise the possibility that SlBAP31s may promote the chloroplast accumulation
  of SlPSBW2, but not SlPSBW1, via SlTOC75, although direct experimental evidence
  for this pathway is still lacking (indicated by “?”). The greater accumulation of
  SlPSBW2 likely plays a major role in maintaining the stability of PSII–LHCII supercomplexes
