caption: PDG stimulates TLR2 which utilizes NFκB to induce miR-146a-3p expression.
  miR-146a-3p binds and activates TLR8. Downstream of TLR8, MAPK (p38 and ERK) and
  p65 NFκB drive 1) the IL-8 chemokine response; 2) the uric acid-mediated NLRP3 inflammasome-mediated
  IL-1β response, and 3) production of mediators of membrane weakening, MMP1 and PGE2.
  Independent of p65 NFκB and MAPK signaling, PDG mediates FM MMP9 production putatively
  through another TLR8-activating DAMP
