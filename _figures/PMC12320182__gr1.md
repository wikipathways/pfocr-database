caption: Schematic diagram of the N-PMIplus@CA design and its antitumor mechanism.
  N-PMIplus@CA incorporates a composite peptide, N-PMIplus, that links an optimized
  β-catenin H1 helix combined with the MDM2 antagonist peptide and the Wnt signaling
  pathway inhibitor carnosic acid (CA), which self-assembles to form nanoparticles.
  N-PMIplus@CA exerts its antitumor effects by simultaneously targeting MDM2 and β-catenin,
  thereby restoring p53 levels in tumor tissues and suppressing the Wnt signaling
  pathway, resulting in a synergistic antitumor outcome
