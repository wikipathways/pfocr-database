caption: The TREM2/PI3K/Akt/mTOR pathway drives central sensitization in VM rats.
  NTG combined with rotational stimulation upregulates TREM2 expression, which consequently
  suppresses autophagic flux via activation of the PI3K/Akt/mTOR pathway. Autophagic
  dysfunction attenuates the negative regulation of the NLRP3 inflammasome, leading
  to the inflammasome’s hyperactivation and excessive IL-1β release, ultimately driving
  neuronal central sensitization in VM. Targeted inhibition of TREM2 (e.g., IA9) or
  autophagy activation via RAPA-mediated mTOR signaling blockade effectively blocks
  this pathological cascade and alleviates VM-related phenotypes
