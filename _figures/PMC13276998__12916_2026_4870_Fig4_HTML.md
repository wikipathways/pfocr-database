caption: 'Oncogenic mechanism of α9 nicotinic acetylcholine receptor (α9 nAChR) in
  triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC). α9 nAChR (encoded by CHRNA9) is overexpressed
  in TNBC cells and activated by acetylcholine (ACh) secreted by immune cells or T
  cells. Intracellularly, α9 nAChR activation drives two pro-tumor pathways: (1) The
  AKT/ERK/STAT3 pathway: α9 nAChR activates PI3K-AKT, Ras-Raf-ERK, and JAK-STAT3 signaling,
  promoting nuclear translocation of transcription factors to enhance TNBC cell proliferation;
  (2) The ferroptosis suppression pathway: α9 nAChR upregulates the SLC7A11/GSH/GPX4
  axis (facilitating cystine uptake and GSH synthesis to clear lipid peroxides) and
  the Keap1/Nrf2/HO-1 axis (enhancing antioxidant capacity), jointly inhibiting ferroptosis.
  α9 nAChR also upregulates Bcl-2 and downregulates BAX to suppress apoptosis. The
  α9 nAChR antagonist αO-conotoxin GeXIVA specifically blocks this receptor, inhibiting
  TNBC progression'
