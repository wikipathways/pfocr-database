caption: Mechanism of SLC16A1-AS1 in promoting GC proliferation via NSUN2-mediated
  m5C modification of GRP78 mRNAIn GC cells, SLC16A1-AS1 interacts with NSUN2 (an
  RNA methyltransferase) to form an RNA-protein complex, which enhances m5C methylation
  of GRP78 mRNA. Elevated SLC16A1-AS1 expression increases m5C modification levels
  on GRP78 mRNA, thereby stabilizing GRP78 transcripts and upregulating GRP78 expression.
  Consequently, this regulatory axis drives GC cell proliferation, highlighting a
  critical oncogenic role of the SLC16A1-AS1/NSUN2/GRP78 pathway in tumor progression
