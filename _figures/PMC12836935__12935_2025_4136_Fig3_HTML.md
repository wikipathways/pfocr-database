caption: Mechanisms of M6A Regulators and circRNA in Gastric Cancer. circORC5 functions
  as a molecular sponge for miR-30c-2-3p, counteracting the METTL14-induced upregulation
  of miR-30c-2-3p and the subsequent downregulation of AKT1S1 and EIF4B. METTL14-mediated
  m6A modification of circORC5 inhibits gastric cancer (GC) cell growth and invasion
  by affecting the miR-30c-2-3p/AKT1S1 axis. The expression of circFOXP1 is inversely
  correlated with miR-338-3p levels, regulating this miRNA to influence the miR-338-3p/SOX4
  signaling axis in GC. circUGGT2 acts as a sponge for miR-186-3p, leading to the
  upregulation of MAP3K9, which impacts GC cell processes. By binding to cytoplasmic
  IGF2BP3, circNFATC3 prevents its ubiquitination by the ubiquitin E3 ligase TRIM25,
  thereby affecting cellular signaling pathways. circ-TNPO3 serves as a decoy for
  the IGF2BP3 protein, impacting MYC expression and regulating GC cell migration through
  an IGF2BP3- and SNAIL-dependent mechanism. circARID1A interacts with IGF2BP3 to
  modulate the expression of SLC7A5 in GC, influencing nutrient transport and metabolic
  functions in cancer cells
