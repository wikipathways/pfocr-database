caption: Biogenesis of long non-coding RNAs (lncRNAs). 1. lncRNAs arise through canonical
  or non-canonical pathways. In the canonical pathway, pre-lncRNAs undergo splicing,
  5′ capping, and 3′ polyadenylation, which influence their stability and localization
  (A). Mature lncRNAs may be further stabilized by protein binding or chemical modifications
  (Ai). In a non-canonical route, splicing can generate small nucleolar lncRNAs (sno-lncRNAs)
  when snoRNA sequences are retained (Aii). Alternatively, as in MALAT1, ribonuclease
  P (RNase P) processing removes a tRNA-like structure at the 3′ end to yield a stable
  transcript (B). 2. lncRNAs function in both the nucleus and cytoplasm, where they
  regulate gene expression by interacting with DNA, RNA, or proteins. Designed with
