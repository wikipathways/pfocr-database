caption: Working model for the oncogenic SOX9-TRAIP-CPEB3-mTORC1 axis in gastric cancer
  progression. In gastric cancer (GC) cells, the aberrantly expressed oncogenic transcription
  factor SOX9 directly binds to the promoter of the E3 ubiquitin ligase TRAIP, driving
  its transcriptional upregulation. The resulting overexpression of TRAIP leads to
  the specific recognition and poly-ubiquitylation of the tumor suppressor CPEB3.
  This ubiquitination marks CPEB3 for proteasomal degradation, resulting in a significant
  reduction of its protein levels. The loss of CPEB3 relieves its inherent translational
  repression of key oncogenic targets, leading to the disinhibition and subsequent
  hyperactivation of the mTORC1 signaling pathway. Ultimately, the sustained activation
  of mTORC1 promotes critical malignant phenotypes, including enhanced cell proliferation
  and invasion, thereby driving the progression of gastric cancer. This model delineates
  a complete and hierarchical signaling cascade linking a master transcriptional regulator
  (SOX9) to the post-translational machinery (TRAIP) and a core metabolic signaling
  hub (mTORC1)
