caption: A) Genome-scale models that incorporate the secretion pathway can be used
  to identify genetic targets for cell engineering experiments. These can be performed
  in high throughput on cells within optofluidic devices to decode context effects
  dependent on the secretion host. Adapted from Gutierrez et al. 202018 and Yu et
  al. 2023112. B) Generative deep learning models, such as transformer-based protein
  language models, can be used to generate signal peptides to enhance secretion. The
  de novo peptides can be tested in high throughput single-cell assays performed on
  hydrogel-encapsulated cells. Adapted from Teufel et al. 202344 and Fischer et al.
  2024108. C) de novo protein design can be used to perform in silico mutagenesis
  guided by the Degreaser pipeline to remove transmembrane domains. The in silico
  mutagenesis is followed by heterologous production and functional assays in microwells
  to simultaneously optimise secretion output and function of a de novo designed protein
  nanoparticle vaccines. Adapted from Wang et al. 2023154 and Hendricks et al. 202498.
  Dashed arrows indicate the potential for iterative improvement of these methods
  by feeding data back into the GEMs, deep learning signal peptide generation or the
  de novo protein design models
