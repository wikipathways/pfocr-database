caption: 'Therapeutic strategies to reduce TAM accumulation in the TME. Approaches
  to target TAM recruitment and survival to limit their accumulation in the TME include:
  inhibition of the colony-stimulating factor 1 receptor (CSF1R) pathway (through
  genetic knockout (CSF1R KO) or pharmacological inhibition using CSF1R inhibitor
  BLZ945) (top); disruption of the CCL2-CCR2 axis using anti-CCL2 antibodies or CCR2
  antagonists (centre); chimeric antigen receptor (CAR)-T cells engineered to recognise
  the TAM marker folate receptor β (FRβ) (bottom). Collectively, these strategies
  contribute to reduced TAM accumulation in the TME, potentially enhancing anti-tumour
  immunity'
