caption: Abnormal visual stimulation propagating via the retina-choroid-sclera signalling
  cascade reduces Wnt5ahi fibroblast numbers and/or activity, and thus Wnt5a secretion.
  Subsequently, the expression of genes for ECM molecules such as Col1a1, Sparc, Pcolce,
  Pcelce2, Adamts2, Bmp1, and Igta2 is downregulated via the noncanonical Wnt signalling
  pathway (Wnt5a-Ca2+-CaMKII). This response may disrupt ECM structural organisation,
  decrease collagen I level, and reduce the collagen fibril diameter, finally resulting
  in myopia progression due to increased distension of the posterior sclera under
  constant intraocular pressure
