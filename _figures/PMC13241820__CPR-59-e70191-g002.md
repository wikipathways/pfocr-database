caption: 'The core mechanisms of EMC function. The EMC facilitates the insertion and
  folding of TMDs with low hydrophobicity or multipass transmembrane proteins, a process
  that may involve substrate‐specific chaperones such as Sop4 and Gsf2. The UPR system
  comprises three sensors: IRE1α, PERK and ATF6. UPR activation inhibits global protein
  translation while enhancing the degradation of misfolded proteins. The ERAD system
  primarily functions through HRD1, which translocates misfolded proteins to the cytoplasm
  for degradation via the ubiquitin‐proteasome pathway'
