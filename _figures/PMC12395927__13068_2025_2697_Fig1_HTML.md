caption: 'Structure and biosynthetic pathway of GD3 ganglioside. A Molecular structure
  of GD3 (d18:1/18:0), a disialo-ganglioside. The molecule consists of three main
  components: a ceramide backbone (brown, right), composed of a sphingosine base linked
  to a fatty acid; an oligosaccharide chain (green), including glucose, galactose,
  and N-acetylglucosamine residues; and two terminal sialic acid residues (pink, left),
  typically N-acetylneuraminic acid. B Simplified schematic of the biosynthetic pathway
  from glucosylceramide (GlcCer) to lactosylceramide (LacCer) and on to gangliosides
  GM3 and GD3. Enzymatic steps are labeled, with precursor pathways color-coded: galactose
  metabolism (grey), LacCer synthesis (green), sialic acid biosynthesis (blue), and
  ganglioside synthesis (orange). CMP-sialic acid is incorporated into GM3 and GD3
  via sialyltransferases (ST3Gal5 and ST8Sia1). Metabolites are symbolized by shapes:
  circles for sugars (blue: glucose, yellow: galactose) and diamonds for sialic acid.
  A. gossypii lacks all enzymatic activities depicted in this scheme'
