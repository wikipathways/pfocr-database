  iCCA and HCC, as well as the construction strategies of animal models. (a): Key
  oncogenic signaling pathways in current iCCA animal models. The diagram highlights
  driver pathways such as IL-6/JAK/STAT activation, TAA toxicity produces ROS and
  furan activates Nrf2 signaling that promote iCCA carcinogenesis. (b): The incidence
  of iCCA and, HCC and the similarities and differences in molecular characterization
  and oncogenic signaling pathways between them. (c): Experimental modeling strategies
  for iCCA and HCC: (Left) Differential tumor induction by TAA administration routes:
  Oral administration (predominantly iCCA) vs. intraperitoneal injection (predominantly
  HCC), demonstrating how delivery methods dictate tumor lineage. (Right) sleeping
  Beauty transposon-driven hybrid carcinoma model, where co-activation of specific
  oncogenes YAP + Akt generates tumors with mixed iCCA/HCC features'
