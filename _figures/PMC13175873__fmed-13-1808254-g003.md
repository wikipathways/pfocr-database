caption: UPR-mediated regulation of autophagy during ER stress. During ER stress,
  the three UPR branches regulate autophagy in distinct ways. The IRE1 pathway is
  required for uncontrolled autophagy under ER stress; an IRE1α–ROS positive-feedback
  loop can sustain high autophagy levels, leading to mitochondrial damage, whereas
  inhibiting ROS generation suppresses aberrant autophagy and apoptosis. Both PERK
  and ATF6 can activate autophagy. Under prolonged stress, p62 cooperates with ER-stress
  signaling and the transcription factors ATF4 and CHOP to activate a set of autophagy
  genes (Atg3, Atg5, Atg7, Atg10, LC3B), thereby promoting cell survival. The ER stress–eIF2α/ATF4
  pathway can also induce autophagy and injury via upregulation of COX2. In addition,
  the PERK–ATF4 axis mediates ferritinophagy-dependent ferroptosis; inhibition of
  this pathway or blockade of autophagy significantly attenuates tubular cell injury
