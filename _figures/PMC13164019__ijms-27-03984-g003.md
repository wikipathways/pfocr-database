caption: 'Mitochondrial Function in Microglial Metabolic Reprogramming. Mitochondria,
  as central metabolic hubs, undergo profound functional remodeling in microglia during
  phenotypic polarization. Pro-inflammatory activation reshapes mitochondrial dynamics
  through coordinated regulation of fission and fusion proteins including DRP1, MFN1,
  and OPA1, to alter organelle architecture and function. Key TCA cycle intermediates
  concurrently participate in inflammatory signaling: succinate stabilizes HIF-1α
  and amplifies ROS generation, while succinyl-CoA drives protein succinylation modifications.
  Fumarate activates the HCAR/Nrf2 anti-inflammatory pathway, and citrate accumulates
  under inflammatory stimulation. Furthermore, reverse electron transport at complex
  I exacerbates oxidative stress. Together, these mitochondrial processes orchestrate
  microglial metabolic reprogramming through tightly interconnected mechanisms'
