caption: Human DEAD-box helicase DDX3X, either N-D1D2-C (the full length) or D1D2-C
  (the N-truncated form), unwinds RNA duplexes in a cooperative manner with the involvement
  of three protomers (two unwinding protomers that hydrolyze ATP molecules and one
  loading protomer that does not hydrolyze ATP). The hypothetical pathway consists
  of four states and the interaction between the protomers features two types of dimers.
  As indicated in the pre-unwound state, the two unwinding protomers forms a D1:D1
  dimer, whereas the loading protomer and nearby unwinding protomer form a C:C dimer.
  The protein is illustrated as a semicircular model shown in sky blue, RNA is illustrated
  as a tube-and-stick model, and MgATP, ADP, and Pi are shown as spheres in green,
  black, and orange, respectively
