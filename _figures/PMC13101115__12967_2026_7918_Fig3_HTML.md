caption: Metabolic reprogramming of myeloid cells by SLC transporters. (A) Macrophage
  polarization. GLUT1 expression is induced under stress (e.g., via ATF4). GLUT3 promotes
  M2 polarization through a RAS-dependent pathway. (B) Neutrophil immunosuppression.
  Lactate uptake via MCT1 and CD16 signaling converge on NF-κB and STAT1 activation
  to drive PD-L1 expression. CD16 signaling also upregulates CD36. (C) Stress responses
  and cell death. SLC1A5-mediated glutamine uptake influences ER stress responses
  (IRE1α/XBP1). The xCT system maintains redox homeostasis, affecting ferroptosis.
  CD36-mediated fatty acid uptake promotes both NET formation and ferroptosis
