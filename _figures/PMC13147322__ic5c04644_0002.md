caption: Calculated Gibbs free energy and enthalpy changes (ΔG soln/ΔH soln in kcal/mol)
  in the N2 reduction pathway (energy not to scale) show a spontaneous reaction sequence.
  Initial reduction of the radical [(CAAC)­(Dur)­BBr] 1 generates borylene species
  2, two of which bind to dinitrogen to yield the dinitrogen bis­(borylene) compound
  4. Subsequent reduction and protonation steps yield 11. Addition of concentrated
  HCl releases NH4Cl and an incompletely characterized boron-containing mixture from
  11. Species 8_dimer and 10_dimer (structures in Section 5, Supporting Information)
  are calculated as K-bridged dimers, in agreement with the experiment. Species highlighted
  in gray boxes have been isolated experimentally
