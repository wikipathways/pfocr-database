caption: 'Lactate and lactylation regulate the vascular smooth muscle cell (VSMC)
  phenotype and vascular pathology. Left panel (light blue): Lactate promotes the
  phenotypic switch of VSMCs from contractile to synthetic, contributing to atherosclerosis
  (AS) and calcification. The κ-opioid receptor (κ-OR) agonist U50488H inhibits 6-phosphofructo-2-kinase
  (PFKFB3) expression and reduces lactate production, and alleviates calcification.
  Oxidized low-density lipoprotein (oxLDL) enhances pyruvate kinase M2 (PKM2)-mediated
  glycolysis, promoting VSMC proliferation and migration. Lactate also upregulates
  G protein-coupled receptor 81 (GPR81) and suppresses the miR-23b/SMAD family member
  3 (Smad3) axis, further facilitating phenotypic switching. Right panel (pink): Tumor
  necrosis factor (TNF) receptor-associated protein 1 (TRAP1) promotes glycolysis
  and lactate generation, leading to H3K12 lactylation, senescence-associated secretion
  phenotype (SASP) activation, and VSMC senescence. The lactylation of SRY-related
  HMG-box 10 (Sox10) induces macrophage-like transdifferentiation and vascular inflammation,
  which can be attenuated by regulator of G protein signaling 5 (RGS5) via the inhibition
  of the phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase (PI3K)/protein kinase B (Akt)/Sox10 pathway.
  Nuclear receptor subfamily 4 group A member 3 (NR4A3) promotes aldolase A (ALDOA)
  and phosphofructokinase (PFK) liver type (PFKL) transcription, increasing histone
  H3 lysine 18 (H3K18) lactylation, Phospho1 expression, and vascular calcification.
  β-GP: β-glycerophosphate; hiPSCs: human induced pluripotent stem cells; PGC-1α:
  peroxisome proliferator-activated receptor-γ coactivator-1α; MCTs: monocarboxylic
  acid transporters; H3K12la: histone H3 lysine 12 lactylation; la: lactylation; Cd74:
  CD74 antigen (invariant polypeptide of major histocompatibility complex, class II
  antigen-associated); C3: complement C3; Lyz2: lysozyme 2'
