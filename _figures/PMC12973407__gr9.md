caption: Schematic model of SeG-mediated salt tolerance in soybean. SeG alleviates
  oxidative damage by enhancing antioxidant enzyme activities and the ASA-GSH cycle,
  reducing ROS and lipid peroxidation. It also triggers metabolic reprogramming, activating
  α-linolenic acid pathway, upregulating key genes (e.g., LOX, OPC1), and promoting
  JA accumulation. These shifts modify root exudates, particularly enriching Arb,
  which acts as a signaling molecule to recruit a Syncom (Bacillus RSB1, Streptomyces
  RSS, Penicillium RSP, Aspergillus RSA). The Syncom likely promotes plant growth
  and salt tolerance via cooperative colonization, quorum sensing, nutrient uptake,
  osmotic regulation, and other beneficial physiological effects
