caption: Summary of NLRX1 working scheme. A, In physiological conditions, NLRX1 is
  needed for mPTP regulation and opening, maintaining a proper level of mitochondrial
  respiratory activity by regulating complex I activity and mitochondrial response
  to fatty acid (FA) carnitine. B, Under pathological conditions (IR [41, 72], Traumatic
  brain injury (TBI) [73], Rheumatoid arthritis (RA) [32], Chronic obstructive pulmonary
  disease (COPD) [30], Cancer [12, 77], and HIV [51]), NLRX1 is downregulated. The
  loss of NLRX1 results in the inhibition of the mPTP, while inducing higher complex
  I respiratory capacity and slower mitochondrial responses to FA substrates possibly
  due to impaired mitochondrial permeability of FA. This mitochondrial dysregulation
  then, possibly due to the impaired inner membrane permeability, activates AMPKα
  which impairs stress-induced activation of mTOR and the cardioprotective RISK pathway.
  Further studies are still needed to explore in detail how mitochondrial dysfunction
  induced by NLRX1 deletion activates AMPKα
