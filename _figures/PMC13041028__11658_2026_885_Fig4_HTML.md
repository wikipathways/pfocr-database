caption: TOP2B in Pol II promoter-proximal pausing regulation. In the left panel,
  Pol II pausing is established by Pol II pausing inducing factors including NELF
  and DSIF and stabilized by TRIM28. G4 and R-loop in the promoter, and TSS and the
  nucleosome ahead of stalled Pol II impose torsional stresses and contribute to Pol
  II pausing. At EGR1, a representative Pol II pausing-harboring gene, TOP2B is associated
  with the promoter and TSS. The BRCA1–BARD1 complex ubiquitinates TOP2B at the CTD
  (marked as a black triangle with a letter ub), which enhances the TOP2B–DNA interaction.
  A gene-specific, DNA binding transcription factor, ELK1 is bound to the promoter.
  In the right panel, upon the reception of signals that activate the MAPK pathway,
  ERK2 is phosphorylated (marked with a star with a letter p) and translocated into
  the nucleus. ERK2 phosphorylates and activates ELK1 and TOP2B. TOP2B catalysis,
  presumably relaxing both the negative and positive supercoiling, generates DSB to
  activate ATM and DNA-PK. ATM phosphorylates BRCA1 at S1524, which interferes the
  BRCA1–BARD1 complex with ubiquitinating TOP2B. Lack of ubiquitination facilitates
  TOP2B to better mobilize since the TOP2B–DNA affinity is compromised. ATM phosphorylates
  and activates DNA-PK, and both PI3K-like kinases phosphorylate TRIM28 at S824 and
  γH2AX. Phosphorylated TRIM28 and DNA-PK function as potent transcriptional activators
  in Pol II elongation
