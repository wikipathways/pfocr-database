caption: 'Schematic diagram illustrating the proposed antidepressant mechanism of
  escitalopram in improving dexamethasone-induced impairment of neuroplasticity. Exposure
  to dexamethasone in cortical spheroids downregulated mTORC1 signaling, thereby impairing
  pathways essential for neuroplasticity. Activation of mTORC1 signaling promotes
  the synthesis of synaptic proteins PSD-95 and GluA1, as well as BDNF. Secreted BDNF
  interacts with its receptor TrkB, further activating mTORC1 signaling via PI3K/Akt
  and MEK/ERK1/2 pathways. Consequently, neuroplasticity is enhanced. In cortical
  spheroids modeling dexamethasone-induced neuroplasticity deficits, escitalopram
  enhanced neuroplasticity through activation of the mTORC1 signaling pathway. Abbreviation:
  Akt, protein kinase B; AMPA, α-amino-3-hydroxy-5-methylisoxazole-4-propionic acid;
  BDNF, brain-derived neurotrophic factor; ERK1/2, extracellular signal-regulated
  kinase 1/2; GluA1, AMPA receptor subunit GluA1; MEK, MAP/ERK1/2 kinase; mTORC1,
  mechanistic target of rapamycin complex 1; p70S6K, p70 ribosomal protein S6 kinase;
  PI3K, phosphatidyl inositol-3 kinase; PSD-95, postsynaptic density-95; TrkB, tropomyosin-related
  kinase B; 4E-BP1, eukaryotic translation initiation factor 4E (eIF4E)-binding protein
  1. Original illustration created with BioRender (biorender.com)'
