caption: 'Pathophysiology of migraine. The components of the calcitonin gene-related
  peptide (CGRP) receptor (receptor amplifying membrane protein 1 [RAMP1] and calcitonin
  receptor-like receptor [CLR]) and the signal pathway from receptor component protein
  (RCP) to the adenylyl cyclase system [9, 13]. (a) Step 1: Upon release of CGRP,
  it docks into the space between CLR and RAMP1, causing its activation. Step 2: After
  activation it is removed from the cell surface and internalizes into the cell and
  couples with a receptor component protein (RCP) and adenylyl cyclase. This raises
  CAMP levels and in turn activates protein kinase A (PKA) and downstream phosphorylation
  leading to vasodilation and neural function. (b) CGRP antagonist blocks step 1 and
  hence step 2 and therefore activation of adenylyl cyclase is blocked'
