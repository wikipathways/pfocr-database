caption: CircRNAs regulate TGF-β signaling via multifaceted mechanisms in cardiovascular
  diseases (By Biorender). CircRNAs regulate the TGF-β pathway through diverse molecular
  mechanisms, leading to downstream effects in cardiovascular diseases. TGF-β binds
  with TGF-β receptor type Ⅱ (TGF-βR2), which recruits and phosphorylates TGF-β receptor
  type I (TGF-βR1). TGF-βR1 then regulates Smad2/3 by phosphorylation, while phosphorylated
  Smad2/3 binds with Smad1 and enters the nucleus. The complex regulates target gene
  expression and affects fiber formation and apoptosis in multiple cardiovascular
  diseases, such as myocardial infarction and cardiac fibrosis
