caption: Multi-pathway reprogramming of endothelial plasticity in diabetic heart and
  aorta. (A) Progressive EC state trajectory. Early diabetes shows expansion of angiogenic
  and EC-1 progenitor-like clusters with impaired proliferative capacity, alongside
  transiently adaptive lipid-handling populations. ECs progressively transition from
  angiogenic-homeostatic states with depletion of shear-responsive clusters toward
  inflammatory-injury clusters, with late-stage emergence of EndoMT-like, Impdh1 + pyroptotic,
  and metabolically exhausted populations, reflecting progressive loss of regenerative
  potential. (B) Cell-intrinsic signaling pathways. In health, active YAP drives KDR
  (VEGFR2) expression enabling VEGF-KDR signaling, while HIF-1α/HIF-2α heterodimerize
  with ARNT under hypoxia to induce VEGF transcription. Diabetic stress impairs angiogenesis
  through decreased YAP activity reducing KDR expression, ARNT downregulation reducing
  HIF-1α/ARNT dimerization and weakening hypoxic VEGF induction, and progressive Ets1
  and Stat5 loss. EndoMT and fibrotic programming are driven by SOX4 upregulation
  inducing mesenchymal markers while suppressing endothelial identity genes, indirectly
  reversible by metformin; and ATF3 directly activating TGF-β2 transcription and promoting
  apoptosis specifically in late-stage venous ECs. KLF11 upregulation epigenetically
  represses TXNIP, SNAI1, and NOTCH1 promoters via to counteract EndoMT, but proves
  insufficiently protective. Igfbp5 upregulation in late-stage EC clusters promotes
  fibrosis; Impdh1 marks the terminal differentiation trajectory driving pyroptosis;
  Irf7 enrichment amplifies interferon-stimulated gene expression promoting inflammation;
  and heat shock protein suppression impairs proteostatic buffering capacity under
  metabolic stress. (C) Multicellular crosstalk. Pericyte TNFα suppresses angiogenic
  endothelial subclusters while activating inflammatory transcription and SASP factors;
  targeted by adalimumab, infliximab, etanercept, and MAR001. Macrophage IL-1β engages
  endothelial IL1R1/IL1RAP progressively amplifying Cxcl1 and Vcam1 expression promoting
  leukocyte adhesion and endothelial injury; targeted by anakinra, canakinumab, nadunolimab,
  and AMG-108. Cardiomyocyte ANGPTL4 signals via endothelial CDH5/SDC3 promoting pathological
  angiogenesis; targeted by MAR001. Fisetin targets endothelial senescence. EndoMT-like
  ECs release fibronectin promoting VSMC activation and ECM deposition; VSMCs reciprocally
  release IGFBP2, predicted to reinforce EC dysfunction, though experimental validation
  remains pending. EC PDGFB/PDGFD activates PDGFR + fibroblasts driving fibroblast
  expansion and cardiac fibrosis, with fibroblasts reciprocally secreting PDGFB perpetuating
  vascular stress; targeted by imatinib and anti-PDGF-BB DARPins. Tgfbr2 enrichment
  in EndMT + ECs provides rationale for TGFβRI/II targeting by galunisertib to suppress
  EndoMT-driven fibrotic remodeling. Red therapeutics; repurposing/blue; preclinical.
