caption: 'Salient features of host pathways and mechanisms in the Mtb infection models.
  a Unique for the ‘Vit C-induced Dormant Mtb’ model: (1) Suppression of DNA repair
  and replication: In response to DNA damage, ATR activates DNA damage repair mechanism
  via the FA pathway. The inhibition of genes in the FA and HR pathway indicated an
  impaired host DNA repair machinery hence hindering the genomic integrity of the
  cell. Additionally, HR participates in DNA replication and its downregulation suggested
  lack of this crucial event while cell proliferation. (2) Enhancement in antigen
  processing and presentation: Induced expression of CIITA led to enhance MHC class
  II transcription that could present antigenic peptide to CD4+ T cells, resulting
  in T cell activation and initiation of immune response to restrain the bacterial
  burden. b Common between the ‘Vit C-induced Dormant Mtb’ and ‘Active Mtb’ models:
  (1) Inhibition of p53 signaling: In response to stress stimuli, p53 is activated
  via phosphorylation by ATR or CHK1, promoting the expression of target genes including
  pro-apoptotic factors. Downregulation of ATR, CHK1 and p53 transcriptional targets
  indicated suppression of p53-mediated apoptosis. Additionally, upregulation of MDMX
  suggested MDMX-mediated control of p53. (2) Downregulation of apoptosis: The repression
  of crucial apoptosis-promoting factors along with induction of anti-apoptotic genes
  negative regulators of pro-apoptotic factors, pointed suppression of apoptosis and
  cell survival. (3) Cell cycle arrest at G0/G1 phase, and (4) Suppression of DNA
  replication: Downregulation of E2F, DP-1/2, along with genes involved in subsequent
  phases and DNA replication machinery indicated blockage of cell cycle progression
  at G1 phase and DNA replication. Moreover, induction of p57 expression that tightly
  binds to several cyclin/CDK complexes suggested negative regulation of cell proliferation,
  leading to G0/G1 cell cycle arrest. (5) Tryptophan depletion and immune suppression:
  IDO and TDO enzymes catalyzes catabolism of tryptophan into kynurenine which indicated
  host tryptophan depletion, T cell proliferation inhibition and immune suppression
  by Treg cells, promoting Mtb persistence'
