caption: This diagram illustrates the molecular pathway through which ZDHHC2, a palmitoyltransferase
  enzyme, influences esophageal cancer progression. ZDHHC2 catalyzes the palmitoylation
  of proteins, affecting their membrane localization and function. The enzyme modulates
  the synthesis of N-Acetylaspartylglutamate (NAAG), which plays a pivotal role in
  tumor growth, immune evasion, and metabolic reprogramming in esophageal cancer cells.
  Specifically, NAAG acts as a glutamate reservoir, releasing glutamate to support
  tumor cell growth and energy production. Additionally, NAAG contributes to immune
  evasion by enhancing immunosuppressive pathways. The diagram highlights the interconnected
  oncogenic pathways facilitated by the ZDHHC2–NAAG axis in driving the progression
  of esophageal cancer
