caption: Biochemical pathways modulated by Artemisia phytochemicals depicting their
  therapeutic mechanisms. The antioxidant defense mechanism is initiated through activation
  of nuclear factor erythroid 2–related factor 2 (Nrf2) and its downstream antioxidant
  response element (ARE) signaling, which enhances the transcription of key antioxidant
  enzymes and strengthens cellular redox balance. Anti-inflammatory activity is achieved
  by suppressing nuclear factor kappa-light-chain-enhancer of activated B cells (NF-κB)–mediated
  cytokine signaling, thereby reducing the production of pro-inflammatory mediators.
  Regulation of apoptosis involves stabilization of mitochondrial membrane potential
  and inhibition of caspase activation, preventing programmed cell death and protecting
  cellular integrity. Neuroprotection is facilitated through modulation of neurotrophic
  pathways, particularly the phosphatidylinositol-3-kinase/protein kinase B (PI3K/Akt)
  signaling cascade, which promotes neuronal survival, enhances synaptic resilience,
  and mitigates neurodegeneration. Together, these interconnected mechanisms highlight
  the broad therapeutic relevance of Artemisia phytochemicals in combating oxidative
  stress, inflammation, cellular dysfunction, and neurological decline
