caption: '(a) The structures of congocidine, a previously known representative pyrrolamide
  (left) and manlipyrrolamides (right). The pyrrole-2-carboxamide units are colored
  red. The terminal cationic moieties in congocidine are colored blue. The terminal
  hydrophobic moieties in manlipyrrolamides are colored beige. The characteristic
  features of known pyrrolamides can be seen in the structure of congocidine: iterative
  pyrrole-2-carboxamide units that interact with DNA nucleotide bases, and terminal
  cationic groups that engage in ionic interactions with the phosphate backbone of
  DNA. (b) Previously reported pyrrole formation pathways from amino acids for tetrapyrrole
  and coumermycin A1 (upper two) and a non-amino acid precursor for prodigiosin (bottom).
  The first pyrrole moieties formed along each pathway are highlighted in red. Final
  products are shown in bold. CloN5 and CouN5 are acyl carrier proteins. (c) The outline
  of a previously proposed putative biosynthetic pathway for the synthesis of 4-acetamidopyrrole-2-carboxylate
  (10) in Streptomyces sp'
