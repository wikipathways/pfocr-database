caption: Working model illustrating FYVE4’s role in SOS pathway-mediated salt tolerance.Under
  normal conditions, FYVE4 interacts with SNF7 and functions in the ESCRT pathway
  to regulate MVE biogenesis. During salt stress, FYVE4 interacts with SOS2 and promotes
  the SOS2–SOS1 interaction. This interaction facilitates SOS1 phosphorylation, activating
  its Na+/H+ antiporter activity to expel cytotoxic Na+ and enhance plant salt tolerance.
  ESCRT, endosomal sorting complex required for transport; ub, ubiquitin; TGN, trans-Golgi
