caption: Factors modulating ILC2 activation and phenotypic plasticityActivating receptors
  [7], inhibitory receptors (bottom), and their regulation of ILC2s are indicated.
  Under conditions of lung inflammation, IL-12 and IL-18 promote the conversion of
  ILC2s into ILC1-like cells, which are characterized by the production of IFN-γ and
  TGF-β. ILC2-to-ILC1 conversion can be reversed by IL-4. Furthermore, the ILC2 subpopulation
  can be converted into ILC3-like cells, which produce IL-17 in response to IL-1β,
  IL-23 and TGF-β. The transdifferentiation of ILC2s into IL-17-producing ILCs induced
  by IL-1β and TGF-β is inhibited by IL-4, and the IL-23 signaling pathway is inhibited
  by vitamin D3
