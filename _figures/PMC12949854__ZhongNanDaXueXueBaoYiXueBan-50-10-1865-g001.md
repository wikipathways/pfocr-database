caption: 'IKZF3 基因在多种肿瘤中作用的信号转导通路Figure 1 Ignal transduction pathways of IKZF3 gene
  in multiple tumorsAbnormally high expression of IKZF3 gene can excessively activate
  BCR signal transduction by reducing the expression of SHIP1, including the NF-κB
  signaling pathway, the PI3K-Akt signaling pathway, and the MAPK signaling pathway,
  thereby promoting the metastasis of CLL. The BRD4 inhibitor can treat MCL by weakening
  the expression of IKZF3 and regulating the negative feedback BCR-related signaling
  pathway through the SHIP1 signaling regulator molecule. Additionally, inhibitory
  drugs such as DD-03-171 can affect the NF-κB signaling pathway by jointly degrading
  BTK and IKZF3 to inhibit the proliferation and metastasis of MCL. IKZF3 can reorganize
  the chromatin structure within the SHC1 gene, promoting the metastasis of lung cancer
  by inhibiting the growth factor signaling pathway. It can also promote the growth
  of MM cells and inhibit their apoptosis by positively regulating c-Myc and IRF4
  and negatively regulating CD38 and ISG. When the JAK-STAT signaling pathway is abnormally
  activated, the phosphorylated STAT5 dimer may enter the nucleus and interact with
  the mutated IKZF3 gene in the nucleus, promoting the development of B-ALL. IKZF3:
  Ikaros family zinc finger 3; BCR: B cell receptor; SHIP1: SH2 domain-containing
  inositol 5’-phosphatase 1; NF-κB: Nuclear factor-kappa B; PI3K: Phosphatidylinositol
  3-kinase; Akt: Protein kinase B; MAPK: Mitogenactivated protein kinase; CLL: Chronic
  lymphocytic leukemia; BRD4: Bromodomain containing 9; MCL: Mantle cell lymphoma;
  BTK: Bruton’s tyrosine kinase; SHC1: SHC-transforming protein 1; MM: Multiple myeloma;
  IRF4: Interferon regulatory factor 4; ISG: Interferon-stimulated genes; JAK: Janus
  kinase; STAT: Signal transducer and activator of transcription'
