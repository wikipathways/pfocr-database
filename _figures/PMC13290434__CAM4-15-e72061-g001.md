caption: 'Key signaling pathways of gut microbiota affecting NSCLC immunotherapy.
  This diagram shows key pathways: (1) Activation of PI3K‐AKT–mTOR signaling pathway
  by sodium butyrate affecting ICIs. (2) NLRP3 signaling pathway activates caspase‐1,
  producing IL‐1β and IL‐18, and A. muciniphila promotes M1 macrophage polarization,
  impacting immune responses through the NLRP3 signaling pathway. (3) TCR signaling
  activates T cells through LAT and PLCγ, with butyrate enhancing PD‐1 and CD28 expression.
  (4) NF‐κB signaling pathway responds to LPS, activating NF‐κB to regulate IL‐6 and
  IL‐10 expression. (5) The MAPK pathway has three branches: ERK, JNK, and p38, regulating
  cell functions via cascade phosphorylation and is activated by LPS, increasing PD‐L1
  levels that impact immune therapy'
