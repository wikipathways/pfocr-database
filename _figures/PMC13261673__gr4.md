caption: 'Metabolic reprogramming in plants in response to waterlogging-induced hypoxia.Under
  oxygen deficiency, mitochondrial aerobic respiration is inhibited, leading to a
  marked reduction in ATP yield. To survive, plants shift from aerobic respiration
  to anaerobic pathways and reconfigure their central carbon metabolism. This shift
  is directly regulated by stabilized ERF-VIIs, leading to the activation of fermentative
  enzymes such as PDC, ADH, and LDH. Soluble sugars (sucrose, glucose, and fructose),
  whose accumulation is promoted by SnRK1, then enter glycolysis in the cytosol, producing
  pyruvate. The fate of pyruvate diverges into three main branches: lactic acid fermentation
  (lactate), alcoholic fermentation (alcohol), and alternative pathways (Ala and GABA).
  ADH, alcohol dehydrogenase; Ala, alanine; ERF-VII, group VII ethylene response factor;
  GABA, gamma-aminobutyric acid; LDH, lactate dehydrogenase; PDC, pyruvate decarboxylase;
  and SnRK1, sucrose non-fermenting-1-related kinase'
