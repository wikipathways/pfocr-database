caption: 'Immune regulatory mechanisms of MNPs in oral inflammation (taking periodontitis
  as an example). In chronic periodontitis, pathogen-derived LPS activates macrophage
  M1 polarisation (left panel) via TLR4, triggering excessive ROS release and pro-inflammatory
  cytokines (e.g., TNF-α, IL-6, IL-1β), which collectively promote osteoclastogenesis
  and alveolar bone loss. This process is perpetuated by ROS-mediated NF-κB pathway
  activation, creating a vicious inflammation-oxidative stress cycle. M2 polarisation
  (right panel) promotes osteogenic repair via secretion of IL-10 and TGF-β. MNPs
  reverse pathological progression through multi-target interventions: CeO₂ NPs scavenge
  ROS and promote M2 polarisation. Au NPs upregulate IL-10 to enhance M2 polarisation
  while suppressing pro-inflammatory cytokine production. Ag/ZnO NPs inhibit COX2
  expression. Arrows indicate activation/stimulation, while T-shaped arrows indicate
  inhibition. Created in https://BioRender.com'
