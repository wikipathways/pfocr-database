caption: Metabolic profiles of undifferentiated and differentiated SH-SY5Y cells.
  In undifferentiated cells, glycolysis is the dominant energy production pathway.
  Expression of the less active pyruvate kinase M2 isoform, PKM2, may promote the
  accumulation of glycolytic intermediates such as FBP. The accumulation of FBP implies
  that pyruvate kinase is the rate-limiting step in undifferentiated cells. In differentiated
  cells, oxidative phosphorylation is the dominant pathway. Differentiated cells produce
  dopamine and accumulate glutamate and glutathione. Dopamine and glutamate are likely
  stored in vesicles. Expression of highly active PKM1 restores glycolysis to steady-state
  levels with phosphofructokinase as the rate-limiting step
