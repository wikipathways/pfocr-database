caption: We found that the EMT process is significantly activated in MVI⁺ tumors and
  exhibits spatial gradient dependence. Mechanistically, increased ZBTB7B in MVI⁺
  samples promotes TRIM29 transcription. TRIM29 directly binds IκBα via its BB2 domain,
  catalyzing its K48-linked ubiquitination and subsequent proteasomal degradation.
  This process releases inhibition of the NF-κB signaling pathway, leading to pathway
  activation and EMT induction, ultimately enhancing the invasive and metastatic capabilities
  of pancreatic cancer cells
