caption: The microbiota indirectly shapes ISC behavior by modulating immune cell function
  (e.g., ILCs, macrophages, γδ T cells) and cytokine production (e.g., IL-22, IL-13),
  forming a complex microbiota-immune-ISC axis. Early gut microbiota critically program
  ISC function and epithelial homeostasis through epigenetic and stromal cell pathways
  within a narrow developmental window, with lasting effects that cannot be fully
  restored by later microbial colonization. Maternal probiotics supplementation during
  pregnancy enhances offspring ISC function and development through microbiota-derived
  metabolites that activate the mTOR signaling pathway, with effects being specific
  and independent of postnatal factors. Created with BioRender.com
