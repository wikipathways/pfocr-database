caption: 'Glucoraphanin conversion to sulforaphane and its metabolites: The β-glucosyl
  moiety is cleaved from glucoraphanin by the β-thioglucosidase myrosinase, to form
  an unstable thiohydroximate-O-sulfonate. In a following non-enzymatic rearrangement
  the sulfate moiety is released producing sulforaphane (SF) and other breakdown products
  including SF-nitrile. SF then can be converted at extremely low levels to SF-glucuronide
  or SF-sulfate (not pictured), but primarily it enters the mercapturic acid pathway
  and sequentially forms one of 4 metabolites -- sulforaphane glutathione (SF-GSH),
  sulforaphane-cysteinylglycine (SF-Cys-Gly), sulforaphane-L-cysteine (SF-Cys), sulforaphane
  N-acetyl-L-cysteine (SF-NAC)'
