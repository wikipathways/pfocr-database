caption: 'Integrated schematic of the p53 and NF-κB signaling pathways and their regulation
  by microRNAs in apoptosis, stress response, and differentiation. The figure illustrates
  two major stress-responsive pathways that determine cell-fate outcomes in NSCLC:
  p53 signaling and NF-κB signaling. Although shown as distinct modules for clarity,
  both pathways integrate upstream stress cues and converge on transcriptional programs
  that regulate apoptosis, DNA repair, inflammation, and cell differentiation. Arrowheads
  indicate activation; blunt ends indicate inhibition. ATM/ATR, Ataxia-telangiectasia
  mutated/ATM- and Rad3-related; p53, Tumor protein p53; p21, Cyclin-dependent kinase
  inhibitor 1 A; BAX, Bcl-2–associated X protein; PUMA, p53-upregulated modulator
  of apoptosis; GADD45A, Growth arrest and DNA damage–inducible protein 45 alpha;
  TAK1, Transforming growth factor-β–activated kinase 1; IKKα, IκB kinase alpha; IKKβ,
  IκB kinase beta; IKKγ, IκB kinase gamma (NEMO); IκBα, Inhibitor of κB alpha; RELA,
  v-rel avian reticuloendotheliosis viral oncogene homolog A (p65); p50, NF-κB subunit
  p50 (processed from NFKB1)'
