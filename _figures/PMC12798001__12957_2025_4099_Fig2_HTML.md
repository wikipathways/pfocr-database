caption: Mechanistic basis of Klac in regulating gene expression and downstream pathways.
  This is exemplified by histone H3K18lac and non-histone protein ACAA2-K214lac, respectively.
  H3K18lac enriches at the promoter region of the c-Myc gene and activates its expression.
  Moreover, H3K18lac promotes USP39 transcription, leading to stabilization of the
  glycolytic enzyme PGK1, which in turn activates the PI3K/AKT pathway. For non-histone
  Klac, ACAA2-K214lac activates fatty acid (FA) metabolism. The resulting accumulation
  of free fatty acids (FFAs) stimulates autophagy and accelerates the cell cycle,
  thereby fueling tumor growth [61]. Together, these mechanisms collectively promote
  the progression of female-specific malignancies. Klac Lysine lactylation, ACAA2-K214lac Acetyl-CoA
  acyltransferase 2 lysine 214 lactylation, c-Myc MYC proto-oncogene, bHLH transcription
  factor, USP39 Ubiquitin specific peptidase 39, PGK1 Phosphoglycerate kinase 1, PI3K Phosphoinositide
  3-kinase, AKT AKT serine/threonine protein kinase, mTOR mechanistic target of rapamycin
  kinase, LC3 Microtubule associated protein 1 light chain 3 alph, p62 sequestosome-1,
  CDK Cyclin-dependent kinase
