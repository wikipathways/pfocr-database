caption: 'PM2.5 exposure modulates the TGF-β signaling pathway in ARPE-19 cells. A,
  KEGG pathway map of the TGF-β signaling cascade, annotated with differentially expressed
  genes following PM2.5 exposure in ARPE-19 cells. Genes are color-coded according
  to expression changes: red indicates upregulation and green indicates downregulation,
  with the color intensity proportional to the magnitude of change (deeper shades
  represent greater fold changes; scale: log₂FC from –2 to +2). Gray boxes represent
  genes with no significant change, and white indicates genes not detected. B, Bar
  graph depicting the expression levels of significantly upregulated genes (pink bars)
  involved in the TGF-β signaling pathway. C, Bar graph showing significantly downregulated
  genes (green bars) in the same pathway. D, ARPE-19 cells were treated with PM2.5
  (12.5-200 μg/mL) for 24 h. TGF-β production was measured using the ELISA. Signaling
  network. Data are presented as mean ± SEM from three biological replicates. KEGG
  = Kyoto Encyclopedia of Genes and Genomes; TGF-β = transforming growth factor-β'
