caption: Workflow for Identifying and Validating Key Genes in Cervical Cancer. This
  figure summarizes the study's workflow, starting with the selection of eQTLs as
  the exposure dataset and inflammatory proteins as the mediator dataset. Mendelian
  randomization (MR) was applied to assess causal relationships with cervical cancer
  outcomes, identify mediators, and evaluate mediated effects. Key genes were subjected
  to reverse validation and bioinformatics analyses, including pathway enrichment
  (GSEA/GSVA), regulatory network construction (TF and miRNA), immune infiltration,
  and clinical prediction using nomograms and decision curve analysis. Single‐cell
  RNA sequencing further validated key gene expression profiles and their cellular
  contexts. Finally, functional experiments, including siRNA knockdown and phenotypic
  assays, confirmed the roles of these genes in tumor progression
