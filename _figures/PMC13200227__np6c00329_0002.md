caption: Reduction of the α,β-unsaturated lactone in diverse coumarins using the ex
  vivo human fecal cultures. (A–H) Extracted ion chromatograms (XICs) of coumarins
  (black traces) and their metabolites (red traces) following 24 h incubation with
  ex vivo fecal cultures compared to coumarins in uninoculated media. Traces are normalized
  to facilitate direct comparison of peak height in the uninoculated media to the
  fermentation extract for each m/z. Metabolites annotated only by MS and MS/MS are
  labeled with *asterisks*. Chromatographic standards were used for panels (A–C, H).
  Proposed gut microbial biotransformation pathways of each coumarin are shown to
  the right. Metabolites confirmed by reference standards are shown with solid arrows,
  and metabolites annotated from MS and MS/MS spectra are shown with dashed arrows.
  (I) The microbiome can reduce the unsubstituted α,β-unsaturated lactone of coumarins
  to yield a dihydrocoumarin, which undergoes spontaneous hydrolysis to yield a free
  acid
