caption: 'This diagram illustrates the core biological mechanisms by which ZDHHC2
  and N-Acetylaspartylglutamate (NAAG) contribute to esophageal cancer progression.
  It is divided into two key pathways: Metabolic Reprogramming (Left): NAAG influences
  glutamate metabolism, which supports energy production in esophageal cancer cells.
  This energy is essential for tumor cell growth and helps sustain the metabolic demands
  of the growing tumor. Immune Evasion (Right): NAAG modulates the immune environment
  by interacting with immune cells. It activates the NR2B–NMDAR–p38–NOTCH1 signaling
  pathway, promoting the expansion of PMN-MDSCs (polymorphonuclear myeloid-derived
  suppressor cells), which suppress T-cell function and contribute to immune suppression,
  enabling the tumor to evade immune detection. Together, these processes drive the
  progression of esophageal cancer by enhancing metabolic support for tumor growth
  and facilitating immune evasion'
