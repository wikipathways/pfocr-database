caption: MagO exerted its inhibitory effects against the epithelial barrier damage
  caused by 5-FU. Firstly, Mag restored the 5-FU-induced alterations in gut microbiota
  by upregulating relative abundances of beneficial bacteria such as Lactobacillus,
  Alistipes and Parasuttella, while downregulating that of pathogenic Escherichia-Shigella.
  Secondly, MagO influenced various metabolic pathways, particularly arachidonic acid
  metabolism, and was found to upregulate PGE2 level andexpressions of EP2 and EP4.
  Additionally, MagO targeted JAK2, PIK3CA, PRKCA, and RXRA to inhibit the PI3K/AKT
  pathway, thereby upregulating Bcl-2 expression and downregulating Bax expression.
  These actions ultimately enhanced expressions of tight junction proteins ZO-1 and
  Occludin
