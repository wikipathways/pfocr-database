caption: Linking paternal diet to placental development workflow. (A) Mouse studies
  that implemented high‐fat diets where sourced and significant changes to sperm chromatin
  sncRNAs were extracted. The microRNA target prediction database (miRDB) was used
  to identify all downstream target genes. (B) In parallel, the International Mouse
  Phenotyping Consortium (IMPC) was mined for placenta‐related phenotypes, and all
  significant gene drivers were extracted. (C) Candidate regulators of placental development
  via the sperm epigenome were identified and subjected to in silico analyses with
  ingenuity pathway analysis (IPA). The final results are made accessible through
  an interactive ShinyApp, ShinySpermPlacenta (https://reproproteomics.shinyapps.io/ShinySpermPlacenta/)
