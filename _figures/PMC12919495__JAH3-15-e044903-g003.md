caption: Due to its strong hydrophobicity, Cer16:0 cannot readily diffuse into cells
  directly. Instead, EVs and lipoprotein (eg, HDL) facilitate its cellular uptake.
  Once internalized, Cer16:0 can induce EC apoptosis by triggering eNOS uncoupling
  and PP2A dysfunction, while also promoting ERS. Additionally, Cer16:0 can directly
  bind to specific cell surface receptors, including CYSLTR2 and P2RY6 on ECs and
  macrophages, as well as FPR2 on adipocytes. This interaction activates downstream
  Gq protein‐coupled and Gi‐cAMP signaling pathways, leading to the release of proinflammatory
  cytokines and suppression of energy metabolism‐related genes (eg, UCP1). Collectively,
  these mechanisms drive the development of atherosclerosis. × represents inhibition
  of expression. ASMase indicates acid sphingomyelinase; Cer16:0, ceramide C16; CYSLTR2,
