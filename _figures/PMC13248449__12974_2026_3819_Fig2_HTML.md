caption: Phosphatase-dependent scaffolding mechanisms of SHIP1 in AD. SHIP1 serves
  as a central regulator of microglial activation by modulating several key inflammatory
  signaling pathways. It hydrolyzes PI(3,4,5)P₃ to PI(3,4)P₂, thereby inhibiting the
  PI3K-Akt-NF-κB axis and downstream proinflammatory gene expression (A). SHIP1 also
  directly suppresses NLRP3 inflammasome assembly and activation (B). Furthermore,
  it regulates signaling from immune receptors, including TREM2 (C), TLR4 (D), NOD2
  (E), FcγR and CR3 (F), thereby influencing phagocytic and inflammatory responses
  in microglia. (Created at https://BioRender.com)
