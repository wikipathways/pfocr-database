caption: 'Proposed molecular mechanisms of crebanine (Cre): Cre attenuated receptor
  activator of nuclear factor-κB (NF-κB) (RANK) ligand (RANKL)-induced signaling,
  including reactive oxygen species (ROS), NF-κB, and mitogen-activated protein kinase
  (MAPK) pathways, by increasing p65 and nuclear factor of activated T cells 1 (NFATc1)
  deacetylation and reducing IκBα degradation through activating sirtuin 1 (Sirt1).
  These effects collectively lead to decreased expression of NFATc1 and osteoclast-specific
  proteins, thus inhibiting osteoclast differentiation and activity. TRAF6: tumor
  necrosis factor (TNF) receptor-associated factor 6; NOX1: nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide
  (NAD) phosphate (NADPH) oxidases 1; ERK: extracellular signal-regulated kinase;
