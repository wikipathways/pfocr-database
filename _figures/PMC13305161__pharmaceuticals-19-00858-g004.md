caption: Crosstalk between environmental signals, ferroptosis, and healing. This integrated
  flowchart compares the protective and injurious axes shaping the colitic mucosa.
  The Protective Axis (Left) shows how microbiota eubiosis and beneficial metabolites
  like butyrate activate the Nrf2/GPX4 pathway to maintain mucosal barrier integrity
  and promote M2 macrophage-mediated healing. The Injurious Axis (Right) illustrates
  how diet-associated dysbiosis, harmful metabolites, and iron loading fuel epithelial
  and macrophage ferroptosis, leading to barrier failure, persistent colitis, and
  tissue injury. AIEC, Adherent-invasive Escherichia coli; DCA, Deoxycholic acid;
  IEC, Intestinal epithelial cell; PUFAs, Polyunsaturated fatty acids; ROS, Reactive
  oxygen species; SCFAs, Short-chain fatty acids
