caption: 'Schematic representation of vascular complications associated with aortic
  thrombosis in COVID-19. The figure illustrates thrombus formation, vascular obstruction,
  and potential sequelae, including embolization and aortic dissection. Differences
  between CAC and VITT are highlighted, including the complete mechanistic pathway
  for VITT: vaccine-induced PF4-antibody complexes activate platelets via FcγRIIa,
  leading to aggregation; inflammatory cells (neutrophils) release NETs, and cytokines
  (IL-1β, TNF-α, IL-6) amplify endothelial activation and coagulopathy via NLRP3 inflammasome.
  In CAC, endothelial injury from SARS-CoV-2 triggers cytokine storm (IL-6, TNF-α)
  and complement activation, promoting platelet-rich thrombi on eroded plaques'
