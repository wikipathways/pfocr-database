caption: 'DFT-calculated mechanistic landscape for copper-photocatalyzed ATRA reactions
  of CF3SO2Cl with alkenes. The mechanism begins with outer-sphere single-electron
  transfer (SET) from the photoexcited copper complex [Cu]* to CF3SO2Cl, generating
  SO2Cl− and a ˙CF3 radical. The ˙CF3 radical then adds to the alkene to form the
  carbon-centred radical R˙, which furnishes the final product through one of three
  outer-sphere pathways: (a) S(vi)/S(iv) redox cycling (mechanism (i)), (b) 2c–3e
  Cl-coordination-induced SET (mechanism (ii)), or (c) direct radical oxidation to
  a carbocation (mechanism (iii))'
