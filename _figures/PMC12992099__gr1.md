caption: 'Autophagy signaling pathway and regulatory mechanisms. Autophagy begins
  with the formation of the phagophore (1), which elongates and matures into a double-membraned
  autophagosome (2). The autophagosome subsequently fuses with lysosomes to generate
  an autolysosome (3), where the sequestered cellular components are degraded and
  recycled (4). The process is orchestrated by key regulatory proteins, including
  Beclin-1, ATG proteins, VPS complexes, and LC3, and modulated by upstream signals
  such as PI3K activation, ER stress, calcium flux, and mTOR inhibition. AMPK promotes
  autophagy through mTOR suppression, whereas Rab family proteins facilitate vesicle
  trafficking. AMPK: AMP-activated protein kinase; ATG: autophagy-related protein;
  ATP: adenosine triphosphate; LC3: Microtubule-associated protein 1A/1B-light chain
  3; mTOR: Mechanistic target of rapamycin; PINK: PTEN-induced kinase 1; RABs: Ras-associated
  binding proteins; RAB7: Ras-associated binding protein 7; ULK1: Unc-51-like autophagy-activating
  kinase 1; VPS: Vacuolar protein sorting'
