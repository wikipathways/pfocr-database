caption: 'The cGAS-STING signaling pathway (Exogenous dsDNA derived from cell debris,
  invading viruses, bacteria or EVs can translocate across the plasma membrane, whereas
  endogenous DNA is released into the cytosol from damaged mtDNA or ruptured micronuclei
  generated during genomic instability. All cytosolic DNA species are sensed by cGAS,
  which converts ATP and GTP into the second messenger cGAMP. cGAMP binds to the ER
  resident adaptor STING, triggering its packaging into COPII vesicles and trafficking
  through the、ERGIC to the Golgi apparatus. At the Golgi, STING assembles a signalling
  platform that recruits TBK1 and the IKK complex. TBK1 phosphorylates IRF3, promoting
  its dimerization and nuclear translocation to drive IFN-I gene expression, while
  IKK phosphorylates IκBα, liberating NF-κB to enter the nucleus and induce inflammatory
  cytokines. Collectively, these events couple diverse intracellular and extracellular
  DNA insults to robust innate-immune and inflammatory responses.). Created in Biorender.
