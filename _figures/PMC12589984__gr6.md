caption: Schematic showing that isobutyric acid regulates gelatinase activity in hPDLSCs
  via NF-κB signalling. Isobutyric acid (IBA) induces canonical NF-κB activation in
  human periodontal ligament stem cells (hPDLSCs), resulting in the nuclear translocation
  of the p65 subunit. NF-κB (p-p65) signalling subsequently upregulates matrix metalloproteinase-2
  (MMP-2) and MMP-9 expression, thereby promoting periodontal tissue degradation and
  alveolar bone resorption. In the present study, the investigated pathway is highlighted
  by red arrows; black arrows denote pathways implicated but not explicitly examined
