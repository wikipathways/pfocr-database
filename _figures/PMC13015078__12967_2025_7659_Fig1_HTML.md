caption: Diagram of mitochondrial fusion, fission, autophagy and transport mechanisms.
  Mitochondria complete fusion through Mfn1/2 on the OMM and Opa1 on the IMM. Mitochondria
  promote mitochondrial fission through Drp1 on the OMM with its receptors FIS1, mff,
  and MiD49 and MiD51; mitochondrial movement along microtubules is driven by kinesin-1
  and dynein/dynactin complexes, which interact with TRAK family adapter proteins
  and are mediated by Miro proteins; mitophagy is divided into Ub-dependent pathways
  and Ub-independent pathways. The Ub-dependent pathways are mainly mediated through
  the PINK1-parkin pathway. The parkin pathway and Ub-independent pathways are mainly
  mediated by various proteins containing an LIR region
