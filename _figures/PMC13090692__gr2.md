caption: 'Molecular signaling pathways and epigenetic mechanisms of BPA-induced breast
  cancerBPA promotes breast cancer through integrated signaling and epigenetic axes.
  In the extracellular space and cytoplasm, BPA activates GPER, EGFR, and the voltage-gated
  potassium channel Kv3.4, triggering an ERK1/2/ERK2 cascade that drives cytoskeletal
  reorganization and migration. BPA also inhibits miR-381-3p to upregulate PTTG1,
  promoting the G1/S cell cycle transition. Within the nucleus, the ERK1/2/ERRγ axis
  induces metabolic reprogramming. Long-term effects are maintained via epigenetic
  reprogramming: BPA-bound ERα recruits EZH2 to increase repressive H3K27me3 marks
  and induces DNMTs to suppress the TET2 promoter, leading to reduced global DNA hydroxymethylation
  (5-hmC)'
