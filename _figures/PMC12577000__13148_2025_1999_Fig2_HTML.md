caption: Molecular pathway governing the cutaneous methylation pattern in the skin
  layer. Upon interferon (IFN) binding to the receptor complex IFNAR1/IFNAR2, the
  JAK1–STAT1 signaling axis is activated, leading to STAT1 phosphorylation (P) and
  nuclear translocation. Inside the nucleus, DNA methyltransferases DNMT3A and DNMT3B,
  together with the histone methyltransferase SETD2, are recruited to the ISRE region.
  SETD2 catalyzes H3K36me3 (trimethylation of histone H3 at lysine 36), which cooperates
  with DNA methylation (Me) and histone lactylation (Ac) to induce the expression
  of interferon-stimulated genes (ISGs) such as MX2. The diagram also highlights metabolic
  cofactors potentially supplied by mitochondria, the N-terminal histone sequence
  (N-terminal SG), and ubiquitination (Ub) sites
