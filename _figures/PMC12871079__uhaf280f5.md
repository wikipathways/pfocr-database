caption: Phenolic compound metabolic pathways and the involved AP2/ERF TFs. This figure
  outlines the major biosynthetic routes for phenolic compounds in plants, originating
  from primary metabolism (Glycolysis and Pentose Phosphate pathway) and proceeding
  through the core Shikimate and Phenylpropane pathways. The key intermediate, L-Phenylalanine,
  serves as the precursor for a wide array of phenolic substances. The diagram shows
  major subclasses derived from these pathways, including flavonoids (e.g., anthocyanins,
  flavonols), lignin, stilbenoids (e.g., resveratrol), and tannins. The green boxes
  list the names of AP2/ERF transcription factors that have been functionally characterized
  to regulate the biosynthesis of the corresponding phenolic subclass
