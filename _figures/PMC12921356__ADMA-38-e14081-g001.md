caption: Schematic illustration of the MSCi@MN platform mimicking the native exosome
  secretion pathway. Inspired by the stimulus‐responsive exosome release behavior
  of MSCs, the MSCi@MN platform incorporates NV conjugated with salmon sperm‐derived
  DNA into a hyaluronic acid‐based dissolving MN. Upon NIR irradiation, Ti3C2 MX in
  the base layer generates localized heat. Therefore, the controlled release of NV‐DNA
  into wound tissue. NV and DNA activate distinct but complementary regenerative mechanisms.
  Their synergistic co‐delivery of NV and DNA leads to immunomodulation, angiogenesis,
  extracellular matrix (ECM) regeneration, and collagen synthesis
