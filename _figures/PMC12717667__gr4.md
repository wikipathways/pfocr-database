  by multiple signaling pathways. TGF-β, BMP, FGF, IGF, HIF-1α, and Wnt pathways promote
  SOX9 expression through positive regulation, which in turn induces the expression
  of chondrocyte-specific ECM genes such as COL2A1, COL9A1, COMP, and Aggrecan (ACAN).
