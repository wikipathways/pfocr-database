caption: 'Metabolic pathways for EG assimilation. Six routes that begin with the oxidation
  of EG to glycolaldehyde (GA): glycerate, β-hydroxyaspartate cycle (BHAC), tartronyl-CoA
  (TaCo), synthetic acetyl-CoA (SACA), Ara5P-dependent synthetic glycolaldehyde assimilation
  (SAGA), and All6P-dependent SAGA. Metabolites: TSA, tartronate semialdehyde; PEP,
  phosphoenolpyruvate; BHA, β-hydroxyaspartate; acP, acetyl phosphate; acCoA, acetyl-CoA;
  GA3P, glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate; Ara5P, D-arabinose 5-phosphate; Ribu5P, D-ribulose
  5-phosphate; Xylu5P, D-xylulose 5-phosphate; All6P, allose-6-phosphate; Allul6P,
  allulose-6-phosphate; F6P, fructose-6-phosphate; E4P, erythrose-4-phosphate. Enzymes/genes:
  fucO, L-1,2-propanediol oxidoreductase; aldA, glycoaldehyde dehydrogenase; glc,
  glycolate oxidase; gcl, glyoxylate carboligase; garR, TSA reductase; glxR, tartronate
  semialdehyde reductase 2; garK, glycerate 2-kinase 1; glxK, glycerate 2-kinase 1;
  eno, enolase; pyk, pyruvate kinase; bhcC, β-hydroxyaspartate aldolase; bhcB, β-hydroxyaspartate
  dehydratase; bhcD, iminosuccinate reductase; PduP, aldehyde dehydrogenase; GCC,
  glycolyl-CoA carboxylase; TCR, tartronyl-CoA reductase; ACPS, acetyl phosphate synthase;
  pta, phosphotransacetylase; fsA, arabinose 5-phosphate aldolase; kdsD, D-arabinose
  5-phosphate isomerase; rpe, D-ribulose 5-phosphate 3-epimerase; pkt, phosphoketolase;
