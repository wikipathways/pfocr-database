caption: Schematic diagram of anthocyanin biosynthesis regulation in tomatoes. Exogenous
  signaling molecules, such as abscisic acid (ABA), jasmonic acid (JA), hydrogen sulfide
  (H₂S), ethylene, and melatonin, alongside environmental factors including light,
  temperature extremes (high and low), drought, and heavy metal stress, collectively
  trigger the anthocyanin regulatory pathway. These diverse signals modulate the pathway
  by either activating or suppressing a cascade of key TFs, which in turn regulate
  the expression of anthocyanin biosynthetic pathway (ABP) genes. This intricate regulatory
  network ultimately governs the synthesis and accumulation of anthocyanins, ensuring
  their production in response to both internal and external stimuli
