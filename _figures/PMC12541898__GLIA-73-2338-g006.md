caption: The buildup of harmful lipids like di‐ and triacylglycerides (DAGs and TAGs)
  or fatty acids, like palmitate, causes a disturbance in energy metabolism, ultimately
  leading to the deterioration of axons. DAGs and TAGs can activate protein kinase
  C (PKC), which phosphorylates and inhibits components of the insulin receptor signaling
  pathway, causing reduced glucose metabolism and, ultimately, axon degeneration.
  Certain lipids, such as palmitate, can suppress the production of NAD+, resulting
  in SARM1 activation, which results in axon degeneration
