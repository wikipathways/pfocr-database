caption: The role of GLP-1 RAs in retinal remodeling by stabilizing vascular integrity
  and extracellular matrix homeostasis. In vascular remodeling, GLP-1RAs enhance endothelial
  function by inhibiting the RhoA/ROCK signaling pathway, which reduces endothelial
  cell cytoskeletal breakdown and albumin leakage in DR. Additionally, they activate
  the PI3K-Akt-eNOS pathway, promoting nitric oxide production that helps regulate
  capillary tone and restores microvascular perfusion. In terms of ECM remodeling,
  GLP-1RAs prevent the activation of the Notch1-Hes1 pathway, reducing gliosis and
  inflammation in Müller cells, thus promoting ECM homeostasis and preventing fibrosis
  in retinal diseases like DR
