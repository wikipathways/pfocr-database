  axis. This diagram illustrates the pathogenic pathway of a Western diet: the diet
  acts on gut microbiota through the digestive system, producing substances like trimethylamine.

  activates the MAPK/NF-κB pathways, triggering pro-inflammatory factor release. This
  ultimately induces pathological changes (e.g., arterial plaques, platelet adhesion)
  and leads to cardiac injury'
