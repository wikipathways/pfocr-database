caption: Main pathways and metabolic pathways of ferroptosis. Fe3+ enters cells through
  a series of transporters and catalyses lipid peroxidation via the Fenton reaction,
  leading to ferroptosis. System Xc- transports cysteine into cells while exporting
  glutamic acid, with the critical subunit SCL7A11 being regulated by the P53 gene.
  Cys is transformed into GSH within cells, and GPX4 facilitates the conversion of
  GSH into GSSG, while PLOOH, which is harmful, is converted into harmless PLOH. GSR
  helps GSSG undergo retransformation into GSH, and VDACs provide NADPH that is crucial
  to this process. Such a process effectively inhibits cell ferroptosis
