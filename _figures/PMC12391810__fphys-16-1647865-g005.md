caption: The pathway where efferocytosis-induced lactate (EIL) promotes EIMP, supports
  tissue repair and reduces inflammation. Efferocytosis-induced glycolysis stimulates
  the secretion of lactate, which activates the cell surface receptor GPR132. GPR132
  then transduces signals through the PKA-AMPK-NAD + pathway. This pathway deacetylates
  Myc proteins, enhancing their stability. Consequently, this cascade promotes EIMP,
  facilitating tissue repair and reducing inflammation
