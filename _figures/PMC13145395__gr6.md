caption: Graphic summary showing the proposed mechanisms by which exercise-induced
  circulating exosomes protect against endothelial dysfunction induced by obesity.
  Long-term aerobic exercise enhances the production of miR-214-3p in the myocardium.
  miR-214-3p, carried by exosomes, is released into circulation and enhances EPC proliferation
  and migration directly, by regulating COL1A2 expression, and indirectly, through
  the PTEN-PI3K-Akt pathway, thereby promoting the repair of endothelial dysfunction
  induced by obesity in both humans and animals. Akt = serine/threonine kinase; COL1A2 = collagen
  type I alpha 2 chain; EC = endothelial cells; EPC = endothelial progenitor cells;
  miR = microRNA; PI3K = phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase; pre-miR = precursor microRNA;
  PTEN = phosphatase and tensin homolog
