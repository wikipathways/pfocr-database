  epithelial cells (IECs). The network consists of four primary nodes: (1) Antioxidant

  Remodeling Network, driven by the polyunsaturated fatty acid (PUFA) pathway and
  ACSL4 upregulation; and (4) Mitochondrial Amplification, where dysregulation of
  the VDR-SIRT3-SOD2 axis leads to mtROS accumulation and characteristic cristae loss'
