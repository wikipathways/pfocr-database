  PCOS is known for its chronic inflammatory status, and inflammasome activation is
  an important mechanism, among which the NLRP3 inflammasome has been the most studied.
  NLRP3 inflammasome activation is regulated by mitochondria in PCOS via mitochondrial
  ROS regulated by Ca2+ signaling, the TCA cycle and ATP production, glucose and lipid
  metabolism pathways, mtDNA synthesis regulated by CMPK2, and mitochondrial quality
  control regulated by MARCH5 and NEK7. This figure was created by Biorender (https://biorender.com/)
