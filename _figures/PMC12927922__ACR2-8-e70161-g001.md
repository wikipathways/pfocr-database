caption: 'Multienrichment of transcript and protein pathways across SLE and DM. The
  central network displays enriched pathway clusters, connected to pathway gene loci
  in each pathway cluster. Pathway clusters are labeled by cohort: “SLE” indicates
  enrichment specific to SLE, “DM” is specific to DM, and “SLEDM1” indicates enrichment
  across SLE and DM. Two signal analysis clusters are labeled “SLEDM2” and “SLEDM3,”
  which also indicate enrichment across SLE and DM. Nodes are purple or dark purple
  for SLE, and gold or orange for DM. Lighter color nodes (purple or gold) indicate
  gene loci identified by pathway enrichment, darker color nodes (dark purple and
  orange) for signaling analysis, and multiple colors for genes identified by multiple
  approaches. Node outlines indicate direction of change, red for up‐regulation and
  blue for down‐regulation. Heatmaps beside each cluster show expression changes versus
  healthy controls in the respective disease cohort. Heatmap rows are split by node
  colors. DM, dermatomyositis; IL, interleukin; SLE, systemic lupus erythematosus;
  TLR, Toll‐like receptor'
