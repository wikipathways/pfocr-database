caption: Proposed model illustrating how the Nrf2/Slc7a11 axis protects motor neurons
  from poly-GR-associated ferroptotic vulnerability. Poly-GR expression increases
  ferrous iron accumulation and lipid peroxidation, thereby sensitizing motor neurons
  to ferroptotic stimuli and contributing to cell death. Activation of the Nrf2/Slc7a11
  antioxidant pathway enhances cystine import and glutathione synthesis, limiting
  iron-dependent oxidative damage and reducing susceptibility to ferroptotic stress.
  Together, these findings support a model in which the Nrf2/Slc7a11 axis functions
  as a protective barrier against poly-GR-induced ferroptotic vulnerability in motor
  neurons
