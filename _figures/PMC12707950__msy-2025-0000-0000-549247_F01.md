caption: 'The purine salvage pathway. Inosine is metabolized to hypoxanthine by PNP
  then to xanthine by XDH, while guanine is metabolized to xanthine directly by GDA.
  Xanthine is then further metabolized to uric acid by XDH. Instead of being degraded,
  guanine and hypoxanthine can associate with HPRT to make IMP or GMP with phosphoribosylphosphate
  (PRPP). Metabolites: adenosine monophosphate (AMP); guanosine monophosphate (GMP);
  and inosine monophosphate (IMP). Enzymes: 5′-nucleotidase (5’n; EC 3.1.3.5); adenosine
  deaminase (ADA; EC 3.5.4.4); adenosine kinase (ADK; EC 2.7.1.20); adenylosuccinate
  lyase (ADSL; EC 4.3.2.2); adenylosuccinate synthase (ADSS; EC 6.3.4.4); AMP deaminase
  (AMPD; EC 3.5.4.6); adenine phosphoribosyl transferase (APRT; EC 2.4.2.7); guanine
  deaminase (GDA; EC 3.5.4.3); GMP reductase (GMPR; EC 1.7.1.7); GMP synthase (GMPS;
  EC 6.3.5.2); hypoxanthine-guanine phosphoribosyltransferase (HPRT); inosine kinase
  (IK; EC 2.7.1.73); IMP dehydrogenase (IMPDH; EC 1.1.1.205); purine nucleoside phosphorylase
  (PNP; EC 2.4.2.1); and xanthine dehydrogenase (XDH; EC 1.17.1.4)'
