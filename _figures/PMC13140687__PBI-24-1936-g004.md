caption: Flavonoid biosynthesis pathway analysis in transgenic tomato and tobacco.
  Naringenin and eriodictyol are present in tomato and tobacco. These were transformed
  using Agrobacterium tumefaciens to express four genes (FNSII, OMT, GlcT and RhaT),
  ultimately producing the target products (isohoifolin, linarin and diosmin). Only
  diosmin was detected in the transgenic tomato, whereas both isohoifolin and linarin
  were detected in the transgenic tobacco. Green arrows represent the biosynthetic
  pathways in transgenic tobacco, and red arrows represent the biosynthetic pathways
  in transgenic tomato. The chrysanthemum background shows the genes used in this
  study, the tobacco background shows the relevant genes originally present in tobacco,
  and the tomato background shows the relevant genes originally present in tomato
