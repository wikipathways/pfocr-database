caption: Mechanisms of ZDHHC5 in non-cancer diseases. A. ZDHHC5 adds palmitoyl groups
  to Cys837 and Cys838 within the LRR domain of NLRP3. This modification enhances
  the binding of NLRP3 to NEK7, ultimately leading to inflammasome activation. B.
  ZDHHC5 palmitoylation of NOD2 does prevent autophagic degradation via the SQSTM1/p62
  pathway, it also increases membrane localization and enables NOD function. However,
  pathogenic mutants like NOD2S-R444C exhibit enhanced binding to ZDHHC5, resulting
  in hyper-palmitoylation, aberrant stabilization, and excessive inflammation. C.
  In diabetic retinopathy, ZDHHC5 mediates the palmitoylation of the protective protein
  SMPDL3B. This modification enhances SMPDL3B’s protein stability, thereby helping
  to suppress vascular inflammation and oxidative stress under high glucose conditions,
  conferring a protective effect. D. In osteoarthritis, ZDHHC5 acts as the primary
  palmitoyltransferase to modify NOD2. This palmitoylation suppresses macrophage activation,
  thereby alleviating synovial inflammation and ameliorating disease progression.
  E. High-fat diet prompt ZDHHC5 to palmitoylate PKCδ. This modification activates
  PKCδ, which then triggers the NLRP3 inflammatory pathway in hypothalamic microglia.
  This process disrupts hepatic lipid metabolism remotely by suppressing the TRH-TSH-thyroid
  axis, ultimately leading to fatty liver disease. F. ZDHHC5 palmitoylates the host
  proteases Furin and PC7, targeting them to lipid raft microdomains at the plasma
  membrane. This spatial reorganization enables the proteases to co-localize with
  and proteolytically cleave the anthrax toxin precursor, which is essential for toxin
  activation. G. ZDHHC5 forms a complex with GOLGA7 to facilitate SARS-CoV-2 entry
  by directly binding to the viral S protein. This interaction is independent of ZDHHC5‘s
  palmitoyltransferase activity and does not alter S protein palmitoylation or localization.
  However, the ZDHHC5/GOLGA7 complex is essential for viral entry. H. ZDHHC5 palmitoylates
  the PEDV S protein. This stabilizes the S protein, thereby facilitating viral assembly,
  release, and replication
