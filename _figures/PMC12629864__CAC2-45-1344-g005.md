caption: 'Evolution of CAR architectures for CAR‐T cells and CAR‐Ms. (A) Progression
  of CAR‐T cells. First‐generation CARs contain only a CD3ζ signaling domain without
  costimulatory elements; second‐generation CARs include one costimulatory domain;
  third‐generation CARs integrate two costimulatory domains; fourth‐generation CARs
  are engineered to secrete immunomodulatory cytokines for TME modulation; and fifth‐generation
  CARs incorporate multiple strategies to optimize safety and efficacy, including
  JAK‐STAT pathway integration. (B) Progression of CAR‐Ms. First‐generation CARs consist
  of a single intracellular domain that triggers phagocytosis, such as CD3ζ, FcRγ,
  or Megf10; second‐generation CARs incorporate two intracellular domains: a phagocytosis‐related
  domain coupled with either a costimulatory domain, an additional phagocytic domain,
  or a cytokine inducer. Abbreviations: CAR, chimeric antigen receptor; CAR‐T, chimeric
  antigen receptor T cell; CAR‐M, chimeric antigen receptor macrophage; CD3ζ, CD3
  zeta chain; FcRγ, Fc gamma receptor; IL‐2, interleukin 2; IL‐2Rβ, interleukin 2
  receptor β; IL‐15, interleukin 15; JAK, Janus kinase; Megf10, multiple EGF‐like
  domains 10; scFv, single chain variable fragment; STAT3/5, signal transducer and
  activator of transcription 3/5'
