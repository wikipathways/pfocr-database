caption: 'Mechanisms of interaction between glucose metabolism and ferroptosis. The
  figure outlines how key glucose utilization pathways—glycolysis, the PPP, and the
  TCA cycle—modulate the core components of ferroptosis. Glycolysis influences ferroptosis
  bidirectionally by producing lactate (which can activate antioxidant defenses) and
  supplying substrates for the NADPH-generating PPP. The PPP is critical for producing
  NADPH to sustain the glutathione (GSH)-GPX4 and FSP1-CoQH2 antioxidant axes. The
  TCA cycle affects mitochondrial ROS production and provides metabolites that can
  regulate ferroptosis-related signaling (e.g., α-KG, succinate). α-KG: α-Ketoglutarate;
  Acetyl-CoA: Acetyl–coenzyme A; CoQ10: Coenzyme Q10; CoQH2: Ubiquinol; CPT1: Carnitine
  palmitoyltransferase 1; F-6-P: Fructose 6-phosphate; FAO: Fatty acid oxidation;
  G-6-P: Glucose 6-phosphate; GLS: Glutaminase; GPX4: GSH peroxidase 4; HIF-1α: Hypoxia
  inducible factor-1α; FSP1: Ferroptosis suppressor protein 1; IDH: Isocitrate dehydrogenase;
  NADPH: Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide phosphate; OXPHOS: Oxidative phosphorylation;
  PDH: Pyruvate dehydrogenase; PKM2: Pyruvate kinase M2; PPP: Pentose phosphate pathway;
  ROS: Reactive oxygen species; SDH: Succinate dehydrogenase; TCA: Tricarboxylic acid.
  Drawn by Figdraw'
