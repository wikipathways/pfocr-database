caption: 'Metabolic engineering and non-metabolic engineering of violacein production.
  A. Engineering E. coli for violacein production focuses on optimizing the vioABCDE
  biosynthetic pathway and enhancing L-tryptophan synthesis. B. Performance of the
  L-Trp biosensor. In the absence of L-Trp, the TnaC leader peptide is released from
  the tRNA and ribosome, enabling the Rho factor to bind to the Rho-dependent terminator
  on tnaCAB, thereby prematurely terminating transcription. In the presence of L-Trp,
  Rho binding is blocked, allowing transcription of tnaCAB to proceed. To leverage
  this mechanism, tnaAB was replaced with eGFP, creating a TnaC-based native-like
  regulatory device that can be induced by intracellular L-Trp, enabling real-time
  monitoring of L-Trp concentration via a fluorescent signal. C. Addition of antibiotics,
  antioxidants, or QS system-activating factors to enhance violacein production. D.
  Application of abiotic stressors to enhance violacein production. Abbreviations:
  G6P, glucose 6-phosphate; FDP, fructose 1,6-biphosphate; DHAP, dihydroxyacetone
  phosphate; G3P, glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate; PEP, phosphoenolpyruvate; PYR, pyruvate;
  AcCoA, acetyl-CoA; CIT, citrate; MAL, malate; OAA, oxaloacetate; E4P, erythrose
  4-phosphate; DS, 3-deoxy-7-phosphoheptulonate synthase; DAHP, 3-deoxy-d-arabino-heptulosonic
  acid 7-phosphate; CM, chorismate mutase; CA, chorismic acid; Phe, phenylalanine;
  Tyr, tyrosine; Trp, tryptophan; Vio, violacein; aroF, 3-deoxy-D-arabino-heptulosonate-7-phosphate
  synthase; aroG, 3-deoxy-D-arabino-heptulosonate-7-phosphate synthase; aroH, 3-deoxy-D-arabino-heptulosonate-7-phosphate
  synthase; trpE, anthranilate synthase component I; trpD, anthranilate phosphoribosyltransferase;
  serA, D-3-phosphoglycerate dehydrogenase'
