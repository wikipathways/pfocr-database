caption: 'SMYD2-mediated pathways in PKD1-mutant renal epithelial cells and kidneys.
  In PKD1-mutant renal epithelial cells and kidneys, upregulated SMYD2 contributes
  to cyst growth and fibrosis through multiple signaling mechanisms. SMYD2 methylates
  the p65 subunit of NF-κB and STAT3, enhancing their activation. It modulates cytokine
  networks through two independent positive feedback loops: the SMYD2-STAT3-IL-6-SMYD2
  axis and the SMYD2-NF-κB p65-TNFα-SMYD2 axis, thereby amplifying pro-inflammatory
  and proliferative signaling to promote cystogenesis. Additionally, TGF-β/Smad3 signaling
  upregulates SMYD2 transcription. In turn, SMYD2 enhances fibrotic gene expression
  via histone methylation, establishing a SMYD2-TGF-β-Smad3-SMYD2-positive feedback
  loop that drives renal fibrosis. Key downstream targets include fibronectin (Fn),
  collagen I (Col1a1), and alpha-smooth muscle actin (α-SMA)'
