caption: Effects of mechanical stimuli on tumor cells. Mechanical stimuli in tumor
  microenvironment regulate tumor cellular behaviors including proliferation, apoptosis,
  cell stemness, therapeutic resistance, cell migration and invasion and metabolic
  reprogramming. a ECM stiffness activates the integrin-FAK/Src axis, triggering downstream
  PI3K-Akt and MAPK-ERK pathways to promote cell proliferation [75]. b FAK/Src complex
  upregulates BCL2 and downregulates BAX, thereby supporting cell survival and suppressing
  apoptosis [76]. c Compressive forces generated by tumor growth mechanically compress
  blood vessels, reducing oxygen delivery and increasing HIF-1α, which promotes glycolysis
  [77]. Concurrently, increased ECM stiffness activates the MAPK-YAP pathway, upregulating
  glycolytic genes including GLUT1 and LDHA, thereby enhancing glycolysis and lactate
  production [78]. The combined effects shown in this figure represent a hypothesized
  integration of separately validated mechanisms. d ECM stiffness and HA activates
  YAP to enhance expression of stemness markers such as CD44 and SOX2, promoting tumor
  cell self-renewal and repressing differentiation [79]. SOX2 increase in GBM also
  promotes radioresistance and chemoresistance (not shown in the figure) [80, 81].
  e Compressive force enhance GBM cell migration through the Piezo1 activation [10].
  In brain metastatic cells, extracellular fluid viscosity activate TRPV4-mediated
  Ca2 + influx and RhoA-dependent contractility, driving enhanced motility and invasion
  [31]. The combined effects shown in this figure represent a hypothesized integration
  of separately validated mechanisms. f Compression arising from tumor growth and
  ECM deposition can impair vascular perfusion and reduce drug delivery [82]. Concurrently,
  increased ECM stiffness upregulates the ABCB1 drug efflux transporter, enhancing
  drug efflux and contributing to chemoresistance [83]. Radiotherapy activates YAP,
  which increases RAD51 expression and promotes DNA repair to confer radioresistance
  [84], although the upstream mechanism by which radiotherapy activates YAP was not
  elucidated in this study. The combined effects shown in this figure represent a
  hypothesized integration of separately validated mechanisms
