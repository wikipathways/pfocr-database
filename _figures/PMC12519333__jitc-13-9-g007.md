caption: Illustration summary of KMT5A regulation of CAR-T cell antitumor responses.
  (a) KMT5A mediates H4K20me1 modification and chromatin remodeling in CD8+T cells,
  functioning as a negative regulator. Both KMT5A deletion and its inhibition with
  small molecules improve the expression of effector-associated genes and enhance
  tumor control both in vitro and in vivo. (1) KMT5A deletion in CAR-T cells via genome
  editing reduces H4K20me1 at effector-associated genes, enhancing T-cell activation,
  effector differentiation, and T-cell cytotoxicity. Motif analysis reveals that SP1
  and IRF1 binding sites are enriched in more accessible regions of CART cells’ genome.
  (2) Pharmacological inhibition of KMT5A using UNC0379 augments CD8+T cell proliferation,
  anti-apoptosis and cytotoxicity, partially through the AKT/mTOR pathway. CAR, chimeric
  antigen receptor; GZMB, Granzyme B; IFN, interferon; IRF1, interferon regulatory
  factor; SP1, specificity protein 1; TNF, tumor necrosis factor
