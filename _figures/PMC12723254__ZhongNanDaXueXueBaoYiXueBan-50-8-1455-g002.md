caption: '脊髓小胶质细胞/星形胶质细胞参与EIA过程的可能机制Figure 2 Possible mechanisms of spinal cord microglia/astrocyte
  involvement in the process of EIARegular aerobic exercise can reduce the activity
  of microglia and astrocytes. Exercise downregulates the expression of P2X4 and P2X7
  receptors in microglia and inhibits the BDNF/Akt/mTOR pathway. Additionally, it
  suppresses the p38-MAPK, TLR4/NF-κB, and IL-6/JAK2/STAT3 pathways in both microglia
  and astrocytes. This leads to a reduction in the release of pro-inflammatory factors
  such as IL-1β, IL-6, BDNF, and TNF-α, thereby normalizing the function of glial
  cells. These processes ultimately result in the alleviation of pain. EIA: Exercise-induced
  analgesia; Akt: Protein kinase B; mTOR: Mechanistic target of rapamycin'
