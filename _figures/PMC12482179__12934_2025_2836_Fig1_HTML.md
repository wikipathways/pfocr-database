caption: Schematic overview of the metabolic pathway leading to the biosynthesis of
  thymol and carvacrol. The pathway begins with glucose, which is metabolized through
  several intermediate steps to produce acetyl-CoA. Acetyl-CoA is then converted to
  acetoacetyl-CoA, HMG-CoA, and MVA, followed by phosphorylation and isoprenoid intermediates
  such as IPP and DMAPP. Next, this branch of the pathway uses GPP as a substrate
  to form γ-terpinene, which undergoes further transformations through dienol and
  allylic ketone intermediates to yield thymol and carvacrol. HMG-CoA, ​​3-Hydroxy-3-methylglutaryl
  coenzyme A​; MVA, Mevalonate; MVAP, 5-phosphomevalonate; MVAPP, 5-diphosphomevalonate;
  IPP, Isopentenyl pyrophosphate; DMAPP, Dimethylallyl pyrophosphate; GPP, Geranyl
  diphosphate; FPP, Farnesyl diphosphate; ERG10, Acetyl-CoA thiolase; ERG13, HMG-CoA
  synthase; ERG12, Mevalonate kinase; ERG8, Phosphomevalonate kinase; ERG19, Mevalonate
  diphosphate decarboxylase; ERG20, Farnesyl diphosphate synthase; IDI, Isopentenyl
  diphosphate isomerase; TPS2, γ-terpinene synthase; CYP71D507, cytochrome P450 monooxygenases
  (P450s) from the CYP71D subfamily; SDR1, short-chain dehydrogenases/reductases
