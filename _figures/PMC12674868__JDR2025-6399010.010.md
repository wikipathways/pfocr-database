caption: QGSJS decoction inhibits the expression of HIF-1α through non–oxygen-dependent
  pathways, thereby improving kidney damage caused by excessive hypoxic responses.
  In db/db mice, the hyperglycemic conditions lead to high renal perfusion, a shift
  in energy metabolism, increased glucose reabsorption, inflammation, and fibrosis,
  which results in a relatively hypoxic state. This prevents PHD from degrading HIF-1α,
  and the overexpression of HIF-1α activates mechanisms related to abnormal angiogenesis,
  fibrosis, and inflammation, thus promoting the progression of DKD. In addition to
  oxygen-dependent pathways, non–oxygen-dependent pathways such as IL6/STAT3, NF-κB,
  and PI3K/AKT also regulate the transcription of HIF-1α. QGSJS decoction inhibits
  these pathways, thereby reducing HIF-1α expression and protecting the kidneys
