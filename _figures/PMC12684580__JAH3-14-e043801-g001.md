caption: 'Targeting lactylation in CVDs involves direct and indirect strategies (the
  preclinical research‐based hypothetical framework). Direct approaches focus on lactylation
  enzymes: (1) transferases such as p300 (inhibited by C646/A485; activated by CTB)
  and AARS1 (blocked by β‐alanine); (2) de‐transferases, such as HDACs (inhibited
  by TSA/SAHA) and SIRTs (modulated by SRT1720/EX527). Indirect approaches target
  lactate metabolism: (1) lactate transport via MCT1/MCT4 inhibitors (AZD‐3965/VB124)
  or CHC; (2) lactate production by inhibiting LDH (GNE‐1436/Oxa/FX‐11), HK (2‐DG/PROTAC
  HK2 Degrader‐1), PKM (DCA/shikonin), or mitochondrial complex I (rotenone). Other
  agents (dexmedetomidine/Dex, Aloe, A2–4–2–4, andrographolide/AGP) regulate lactylation‐linked
  pathways to ameliorate CVDs. 2‐DG indicates 2‐deoxy‐D‐glucose; α‐KG, alpha‐ketoglutarate;
  AARS1, aminoacyl‐tRNA synthetase 1; AGP, alpha‐glycerophosphate; Aloe, Aloe‐emodin;
  A485, a selective inhibitor of p300/CBP histone acetyltransferase; AZD‐3965, a monocarboxylate
  transporter 1 inhibitor; C646, a p300/CBP histone acetyltransferase inhibitor; CHC,
  α‐cyano‐4‐hydroxycinnamate; CTB, cholera toxin B subunit; CVD, cardiovascular disease;
  CoA, coenzyme A; DCA, dichloroacetate; Dex, dexamethasone; EX527, a selective SIRT1
  inhibitor; FX‐11, a lactate dehydrogenase A inhibitor; GNE‐14, a hexokinase II inhibitor;
  HDAC, histone deacetylase; HK, hexokinase; LA, lactate; LDH, lactate dehydrogenase;
  LDHA, lactate dehydrogenase A; MCT1, monocarboxylate transporter1; MCT4, monocarboxylate
  transporter 4; MDH2, malate dehydrogenase 2; Oxa, oxamate; PDH, pyruvate dehydrogenase;
  PDK, pyruvate dehydrogenase kinase; PROTAC, proteolysis‐targeting chimera; PKM,
  pyruvate kinase; p300, E1A‐binding protein p300 (a histone acetyltransferase); SAHA,
  suberoylanilide hydroxamic acid; SIRT, sirtuins (NAD+‐dependent deacetylases); SRIT1720,
  an SIRT1 activator; TSA, trichostatin A; and VB124, a monocarboxylate transporter
  4 inhibitor'
