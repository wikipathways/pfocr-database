caption: The biosynthesis and dynamic regulation of protein palmitoylation. a PA,
  the substrate for protein palmitoylation, is biosynthesized in cells through a sequential
  metabolic pathway beginning with glucose uptake, which proceeds via glycolysis to
  yield pyruvate that is subsequently converted to acetyl-CoA by pyruvate dehydrogenase;
  acetyl-CoA is then carboxylated to malonyl-CoA by ACC, followed by the multistep
  catalysis of FASN utilizing both acetyl-CoA and malonyl-CoA to ultimately generate
  PA. b PAT itself undergoes auto-palmitoylation to form a palmitoyl-enzyme intermediate.
  Protein palmitoylation involves the covalent attachment of a palmitoyl group to
  cysteine residues primarily catalyzed by PATs, while its removal is facilitated
  by depalmitoylating enzymes such as APTs, thereby constituting a dynamic regulatory
  cycle
