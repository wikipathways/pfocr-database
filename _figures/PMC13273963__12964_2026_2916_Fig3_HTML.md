caption: Interactors of IQGAP3. IQGAP3 interacts with multiple signaling molecules
  including RAS, ERK, CDC42, RAC1, PAK6, GLI1, and Rad17 to regulate cell proliferation,
  migration, cytokinesis, DNA repair, and radioresistance. It promotes RAS–ERK and
  PI3K–AKT signaling to enhance cell proliferation, activates ATM/Chk2–ATR/Chk1 pathways
  for DNA repair, and regulates CDC42-mediated motility for cell migration. IQGAP3
  also contributes to neuronal and auditory system development, cytokinesis, and stem
  cell maintenance, thereby enhancing cancer stemness and tumor progression. (Created
