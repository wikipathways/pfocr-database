caption: 'Exosome-driven cascades in omePOI: pathways linking ovarian microenvironment
  injury and repair. A Pathogenic Exosomes exacerbate ovarian niche injury—promoting
  granulosa-cell apoptosis/mitochondrial dysfunction, sterile inflammation (NF-κB/NLRP3),
  microvascular impairment, fibrosis (TGF-β/Smad, YAP/TAZ), and redox–metabolic imbalance.
  B Therapeutic Exosomes (e.g., MSC-derived) counter these processes by activating
  PI3K/Akt, Smad3, Hippo/YAP and SIRT3–PGC-1α, dampening NF-κB/NLRP3 and pyroptosis,
  and enhancing angiogenesis (miR-126-3p/S1P) and metabolic support (circDENND2A-LDHA;
  miR-21-5p–IRS1). (Created by biorender)'
