caption: Post-translational regulation of NLRP3 inflammasome licensing. NLRP3 licensing
  is regulated by phosphorylation (A), ubiquitination (B), acetylation (C), and SUMOylation
  and palmitoylation (D), which control the oligomerization of NLRP3 and further interaction
  with ASC and NEK7. These major modifications fine-tune inflammasome assembly, caspase-1
  activation, and maturation of IL-1β/IL-18. A detailed mechanisms are discussed in
  the text, highlighting how diverse enzymes and signaling pathways dynamically coordinate
  PTMs of NLRP3. NLRP3,NOD-like receptor family pyrin domain-containing 3; PYD, pyrin
  domain; NACHT, central NAIP CIITA, HET-E, and TP1 domain; LRR, leucine-rich repeat;
  BTK, Bruton’s tyrosine kinase; Akt, protein kinase B (PKB); JNK, c-Jun N-terminal
  kinase; PKA, cAMP-dependent protein kinase A; PAK1, p21-activated kinase 1; PTPN22,
  protein tyrosine phosphatase non-receptor type 22; HUWE1, HECT, UBA and WWE domain–containing
  protein 1 (E3 ubiquitin ligase); Cbl-b, Casitas B-lineage lymphoma proto-oncogene-b
  (E3 ubiquitin ligase); TRIM31, tripartite motif–containing protein 31 (E3 ubiquitin
  ligase); Pellino-2, E3 ubiquitin–protein ligase Pellino 2 (PELI2); Cullin-1, CUL1
  scaffold protein; GITR, glucocorticoid-induced TNF receptor (TNFRSF18); MARCH7,
  membrane-associated RING-CH 7 (axotrophin); PHGDH, phosphoglycerate dehydrogenase;
  KAT5, lysine acetyltransferase 5 (TIP60); SIRT2, sirtuin 2 (NAD-dependent deacetylase);
  HDAC, histone deacetylase; UBC9, SUMO-conjugating enzyme UBC9 (UBE2I); SENP3, sentrin/SUMO-specific
  protease 3; SENP6, sentrin/SUMO-specific protease 6; SENP7, sentrin/SUMO-specific
  protease 7; ZDHHC7, zinc finger DHHC-type palmitoyltransferase 7; ZDHHC17, zinc
  finger DHHC-type palmitoyltransferase 17 (HIP14); MAPL, mitochondrial-anchored protein
  ligase (also MUL1); ZDHHC5, zinc finger DHHC-type palmitoyltransferase 5; ZDHHC12,
  zinc finger DHHC-type palmitoyltransferase 12
