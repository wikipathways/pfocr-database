caption: 'Innovative applications of multifunctional nanomaterials and combined therapies
  in bone tumor treatment. A. Schematic illustration: In Vitro and in Vivo Mechanism
  of Bone Tumor Inhibition by Selenium-Doped Bone Mineral Nanoparticles. Reproduced
  with permission [208]. Copyright from American Chemical Society, 2016. B. A schematic
  representation of a combined treatment strategy using nano-C60 and CaMKIIα inhibition
  for OS therapy. Reproduced with permission [211]. Copyright from Wiley-VCH GmbH,
  2019. C. Summary of cell death mechanisms induced by Chemo-PDT. The nanoparticle
  is internalized by osteosarcoma cells through FA receptor-mediated endocytosis.
  Upon exposure to NIR light, the drug-loaded nanoparticle enhances intracellular
  ROS production and displays Chemo-PDT effects, leading to cell death. Concurrently,
  the release of MTH1 inhibitor TH287 from the nanoparticle boosts Chemo-PDT efficiency.
  At the molecular level, ROS triggers ER stress-induced apoptosis through the JNK/p53/p21
  signaling pathway. Reproduced with permission [213]. Copyright from Elsevier Ltd,
  2020. D. Synthesis of pDA/MTX@ZIF-8 and its Combined Tumor Therapy Approach. Reproduced
  with permission [214]. Copyright from Elsevier Ltd, 2022. E. Diagrammatic representation
  of the fabrication of AI-MPHA nanocomposites for effective synergistic ROS-activated
  NO-enhanced photothermal therapy. Reproduced with permission [217]. Copyright from
  Royal Society of Chemistry 2021. F. Depiction of the synthesis process of FeGA-DOX
  + AG and the intracellular mechanisms of FeGA-DOX + AG under CDT/PTT. Reproduced
  with permission [218]. Copyright from Frontiers Media SA, 2022. G. Diagrammatic
  representation of the fabrication mechanism of flexible nanomotors. Reproduced with
  permission [219]. Copyright from Elsevier Ltd, 2025. H. A schematic diagram depicting
  the creation of FeS2-based nanoparticles (pyrite-based NPs) and their function in
