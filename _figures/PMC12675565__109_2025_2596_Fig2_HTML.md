caption: 'Regulation of LRG1 expression. Schematic overview of the regulatory processes
  influencing LRG1 expression at transcriptional and post-transcriptional stages.
  Pro-inflammatory signals, including cytokines and bacteria-derived LPS, drive LRG1
  expression in a cell- and context-specific manner. TGF-β, PPARβ/δ, and ELK4 also
  enhance LRG1 expression. Mechanical forces activate LRG1 through the FAK/ERK/ELK1
  pathway, while hypoxia may induce LRG1 expression via HIF-1α binding elements in
  the LRG1 promoter. Non-coding RNAs also play a role in LRG1 regulation. The lncRNA
  TUG1 facilitates LRG1 transcription. The miRNA can regulate LRG1 gene expression
  at both the level of mRNA stability by accelerating mRNA degradation and the level
  of protein translation or degrading the polypeptides through binding complementarily
  to the 3’UTR of the LRG1 mRNA. Finally, LRG1 protein undergoes cell- and function-specific
  glycosylation before being secreted into the extracellular space. LPS: Lipopolysaccharide.
  NFkB: Nuclear factor kappa-light-chain-enhancer of activated B cells. OSM: Oncostatin
  M. Created with BioRender.com'
