caption: Myocardial metabolic inflexibility in HFpEFThe heart in HFpEF exhibits significant
  metabolic inflexibility, characterized by impaired glucose oxidation and the uncoupling
  of glycolysis from glucose oxidation, leading to the accumulation of lactate and
  protons. The mismatch between fatty acid uptake and oxidation exacerbates lipotoxicity
  in cardiomyocytes. Additionally, insufficient ketone body oxidation and disrupted
  BCAA oxidation pathways further limit energy production, worsen insulin resistance,
