caption: 'Phytochemical-mediated modulation of apoptotic pathways and synergy with
  venetoclax in AML: The figure highlights the mechanisms by which triptolide (TPL),
  parthenolide (PTL), and quercetin sensitize AML cells to apoptosis. TPL downregulates
  MCL-1 and disrupts BCL2 function; PTL inhibits the NF-κB pathway through TNFR-mediated
  oxidative stress and IκB degradation; quercetin targets multiple survival pathways
  including PIK3CA/AKT1/mTOR, MAPK/MAPK1, and JAK-STAT, while promoting autophagy
  and caspase-3 activation. Combination therapies show enhanced apoptosis: PTL with
  ABT-263/199 demonstrates synergistic cytotoxicity in vitro and in vivo; TPL with
  venetoclax facilitates cytochrome c (Cyt C) release and caspase activation; and
  quercetin with venetoclax enhances mitochondrial apoptotic signaling, PARP1 activation,
  and cell death, highlighting their potential in overcoming venetoclax resistance'
