caption: 'The ITM regulates human lipid metabolism through multiple mechanisms. This
  figure systematically summarizes the pathological network through which the ITM
  regulates tumor lipid metabolism via multi-dimensional mechanisms. The direct pathways
  are as follows: (1) Secretion of metabolites (such as OMVs/LPS) or 15-HETE, which
  directly inhibits AMPK and activates the TLR4/NF-κB pathway, inducing mitochondrial
  damage and lipid droplet accumulation; (2) At the epigenetic level, it regulates
  the SIRT1/PPARγ axis through Snhg9 lncRNA, reprogramming lipid absorption [184].
  The indirect pathways involve microbiota-immune interactions: For example, Fn activates
  the immunosuppressive phenotype of TAMs (with increased PD-L1/IL-10) [185–187],
  while inhibiting the CPT1A-driven glycolysis-dependent processes. Created with BioGDP.com
