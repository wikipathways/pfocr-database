caption: The complex regulatory mechanisms and factors involved in aerobic glycolysis
  in HCC. AMPK typically inhibits glycolysis but it can promote resistance under therapeutic
  stress. c‐Myc enhances glycolysis, contributing to energy supply and resistance
  to treatments, like sorafenib. HIF‐1 drives glycolysis under hypoxic conditions,
  leading to worsening prognosis and treatment resistance. The PI3K/Akt pathway boosts
  tumor growth and survival through glycolysis and it is associated with multidrug
  resistance. As a crucial tumor suppressor, p53 inhibits glycolysis, but is often
  dysfunctional in tumors, further promoting resistance
