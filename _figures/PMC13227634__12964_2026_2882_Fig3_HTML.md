caption: Functions of IKKα in cancer cell signalling pathways. Upon DNA damage, IKKα
  phosphorylates critical components of the DDR pathway, promoting DNA repair. IKKα
  also regulates the expression of Notch1-target genes. In ERα-signalling, IKKα promotes
  the estrogen-dependent expression of genes involved in cell cycle progression. IKKα
  has dual roles in Myc/TGF-β signalling where it promotes the expression of Myc antagonists
  in SCC, while in prostate cancer it promotes c-Myc transcriptional activity. In
  Wnt/β-catenin signalling, IKKα prevents the degradation of β-catenin, promoting
  transcription of its target genes. In mTOR, IKKα enhances mTORC1 activity through
  phosphorylation, and promotes Akt signalling through association with mTORC2
