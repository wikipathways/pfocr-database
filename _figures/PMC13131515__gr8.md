caption: 'Metabolic pathway reprogramming in antibiotic-resistant Escherichia coli.
  Key altered pathways and their functional significance: (A) Glycolysis/Gluconeogenesis:
  Provides continuous ATP production and metabolic intermediates to fuel energy-demanding
  resistance processes. (B) Pentose phosphate pathway: Provides NADPH for redox balance
  and ribose-5-phosphate for nucleotide biosynthesis during stress. (C) O-Antigen
  nucleotide sugar biosynthesis: Stabilizes outer membrane via lipopolysaccharide
  synthesis regulation. (D) Glutathione metabolism: Maintains redox homeostasis to
  mitigate antibiotic-induced oxidative damage. (E) Purine metabolism: Ensures sufficient
  nucleotide production for stress-responsive genetic material synthesis. (F) TCA
  cycle: Generates energy and precursors for membrane remodeling and protein synthesis
  under antibiotic pressure. (G) Cysteine and methionine metabolism: Regulates redox
  balance, methylation reactions, and gene expression'
