caption: 'cGAS-STING Signaling Network and Associated Pathways in Tumor Immunity and
  Cell Biology. Tumor-derived dsDNA activates cGAS-STING, triggering IRF3 and NF-κB
  pathways to produce type I interferon (IFN-I). IFN-I binds IFNAR, activating JAK-STAT
  to induce ISGs, which promote tumor apoptosis (via TRAIL/Fas), inhibit angiogenesis
  (via IP-10/CXCL10), and regulate immune cell interactions (upregulate CD80, MHC-I,
  CD25, PD-L1 on T cells/other cells).Tumor cells generate TNF-α via NF-κB: TNF-α
  increases vascular permeability, induces apoptosis (Caspase-8 → Caspase3/tBid → cytochrome
  c release), and modulates MHC-I (with p300/CBP).Dendritic cells (DCs) present antigens
  to CD8 + T cells (MHC-I-peptide/CD28), recruit immune cells (NK/T cells via CXCL9/CXCL10),
  and interact via CD80/MHC-I. NK cells secrete IFN-γ/TNF-α (via NKG2D/NKp30/KIR).
  T cells differentiate into effectors or central memory T cells (TCM; IFN-I/IL-6-STAT3
  upregulate Bcl-2, maintain stemness). Regulatory T cells (Treg) express CTLA-4/Foxp3,
  secrete IL-10/TGFβ, with plasticity regulated by IL-6. Macrophages polarize into
  M1/M2, interacting with NK cells/Treg.Cell cycle is regulated by cyclin-CDK (inhibited
  by p21/p27); VEGF drives angiogenesis. CXCR3 on NK/T cells binds CXCL9/CXCL10 (also
  regulated by IRF3/NF-κB). STING signaling enhances tumor survival via PD-L1 upregulation
  and treatment resistance. IL-6 (via STAT3/NF-κB) impacts Treg differentiation (Foxp3)
  and T cell responses'
