caption: A comprehensive overview of the three primary downstream signaling pathways
  activated by EGFR. The diagram visually summarizes how ligand-bound EGFR initiates
  parallel signaling cascades. The central RAS-RAF-MEK-ERK (MAPK) pathway drives proliferation,
  while the adjacent PI3K-AKT-mTOR pathway primarily regulates cell survival and metabolism.
  A key feature illustrated is the crosstalk between EGFR and the JAK-STAT pathway,
  notably showing how IL-6 can activate phosphorylated EGFR, leading to STAT3 phosphorylation
  and nuclear translocation to regulate target genes like FOS. The figure effectively
  uses spatial organization, placing the nucleus at the center to represent the convergent
  point for all three pathways, which ultimately coordinate to control critical cellular
  processes such as gene transcription, cell cycle progression (G1/S phase), and apoptosis.
  This integrated view highlights the complex network through which EGFR signaling
  influences cell fate. EGFR, epidermal growth factor receptor; IL, interleukin
