caption: The Wnt signaling pathway and the Dishevelled (DVL) PDZ domain dual binding
  mode switching mechanism.(A) A simplified schematic of the canonical and non-canonical
  branches of the Wnt signaling pathway. The Frizzled co-receptors LRP5/6 and ROR2
  are depicted. Adapter proteins such as GSK, CK and DAAM1 can interact with DVL alone
  or when in complex with Frizzled.(B) The dual-binding mode switching mechanism found
  in the DVL PDZ domain.(C) A possible mechanism for the regulation of DVL activity
  by PDZ domain dual mode switching. Dual mode switching allows for C-terminus availability
  to post-translational modifications such as polyglutamylation and phosphorylation,
  which may allosterically regulate transition into the DVL active state
