caption: Notch signaling in CAFs. A complex bidirectional regulatory interplay exists
  between CAFs and the Notch signaling pathway, which jointly modulates tumor progression.
  This interaction exerts significant influence on the initiation and development
  of various cancers, including lung and breast cancer. During the early stages of
  tumorigenesis, the transcription factor CSL—serving as a core repressor of Notch
  signaling—can suppress fibroblast activation. Loss or downregulation of CSL leads
  to fibroblast activation and promotes tumor initiation, with concomitant p53 deficiency
  further amplifying this oncogenic process. Additionally, CSL attenuates autophagy
  by downregulating the ULK3 enzyme, thereby inhibiting the tumor-promoting properties
  of CAFs
