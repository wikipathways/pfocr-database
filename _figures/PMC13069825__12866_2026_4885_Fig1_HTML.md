caption: Overall Bioinformatic Analytical PipelineAnalytical design comparing microbial
  composition changes including subjective increasing 1 xerostomia and 2 dysgeusia
  scores over an 18-month period prior to and post-radiation in a cohort of head and
  neck cancer (HNC) patients. Comparisons included the longitudinal comparisons (Baseline
  sampling (BL) vs. 18-month (18 m) sampling for subjective 1 xerostomia and 2 dysgeusia
  ‘high’ and ‘low’ scoring and cross-sectional comparisons at each timepoint (BL and
  18 m) comparing ‘high’ vs. low’ subjective scores. 1a and 2a Alpha (α)-diversity
  significance was determined via Mann–Whitney U-test and beta (β)-diversities were
  determined via PERMANOVAs using Bray–Curtis dissimilarity matrices, squared-root
  transformed and 9,999 permutations. 1b and 2b Pylogenetic Investigation of Communities
  by Reconstruction of Unobserved States (PiCrust2) software was used to infer predicted
  pathways from V1-V3 sequencing data. Microbiome Multivariable Associations with
  Linear Models (MaAsLin2) was used to determine associations between timepoint and
  the subjective scoring of xerostomia and dysgeusia from 3a. microbial and 1c and
  2c. pathway profiles. Receiver Operating Characteristic (ROC) curve analysis was
  used to determine potential dysgeusia and xerostomia biomarkers from significant
  3b MaAsLin2 taxa
