caption: During cellular stress, ribosomal proteins (RPL5, RPL11), along with 5S ribosomal
  RNA (rRNA), are released into nucleoplasm. These components inhibit the activity
  of MDM2 ubiquitin ligase, resulting in the stabilization and accumulation of P53.
  Concurrently, in the cytoplasm, ribosome-free RPL26 binds to 5’ untranslated region
  (UTR) of p53 mRNA, inducing its translation. CNPY2 has a prominent role in the cellular
  stress response and tumor-suppressive function. It suppresses MDM2 activity through
  disrupting ribosome biogenesis and promotes the assembly of the RPL5-RPL11-5S rRNA
  complex. P53 in stabilize position upregulates its downstream genes that orchestrate
  vital cellular pathways including apoptosis, cycle arrest, and senescence. The figure
