caption: 'This schematic illustrates five major disorders driven by sphingolipid metabolic
  dysregulation, with Cer as a central mediator. A) ACVD: High-fat diet induces gut
  microbiota dysbiosis, elevating pro-inflammatory Cer(d18:1/16:0) and activating
  NLRP3 inflammasome, leading to plaque instability. B) AIS​​: Progresses through
  acute (excitotoxicity, ROS, BBB disruption), subacute (neuroinflammation), and reparative
  phases, where Cer-mediated mitochondrial apoptosis impairs recovery. C) STEMI: Features
  atherosclerotic plaque rupture, Ox-LDL accumulation, Cer-driven platelet activation,
  and thrombosis. D) HF: Chronic hypoperfusion induces neuroinflammation, oxidative
  stress, and neuronal death, accompanied by decreased Cer(d16:1/24:0) levels. E)
  VaD: Myocardial stress (e.g., ischemia/diabetes) activates nSMase-mediated Cer generation,
  inhibiting Akt/PKB and PPARα/PGC-1α pathways, disrupting glucose/fatty acid metabolism,
  and promoting mitochondrial apoptosis via caspase-3 activation'
