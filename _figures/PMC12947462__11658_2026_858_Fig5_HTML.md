caption: 'The role of LONP1 in cardiovascular and pulmonary diseases. A Cardiovascular
  diseases: Under hypoxic conditions, LONP1 promotes apoptosis by increasing ROS production.
  Conversely, during I/R, LONP1 maintains mitochondrial homeostasis by selectively
  degrading p-COX or inhibiting lactate production by degrading PDK4, thereby suppressing
  myocardial cell death. The downregulation of LONP1 in myocardial cells impairs the
  integrity of MAM, resulting in OPA1 processing and Drp1 expression, while reducing
  MFN1 expression, thereby enhancing mitochondrial fission. In addition, LONP1 ablation
  resulted in decreased expression levels of mitochondrial complexes I, II, III, and
  IV subunits. Concurrently, it led to the upregulation of various key metabolic enzymes,
  promoting metabolic reprogramming. These events culminate in cell death and contribute
  to heart failure. Additionally, LONP1 downregulation triggers compensatory metabolic
  remodeling via activation of the eIF2α/ATF4 signaling axis: ATF4 upregulates GLUT1
  expression to enhance glycolytic energy supply, while directly inhibiting TFAM transcription,
  leading to reduced mtDNA replication. This cascade reaction ultimately leads to
  ROS production and DNA damage, causing cell death and cardiac developmental disorder.
  Moreover, the interaction between HMOX1 and LDHB enhances LONP1-mediated degradation
  of mitochondrial TFAM, causing mitochondrial dysfunction and macrophage death, which
  collectively contribute to atherosclerosis. B Pulmonary diseases: Downregulation
  of LONP1 promotes myofibroblast transformation by activating the PI3K/Akt/FGF2 pathway.
  Concurrently, LONP1 downregulation induces upregulation of TGF-β and PAI-1, thereby
  activating the p53/p21/Rb signaling axis, ultimately leading to AT2 cell aging and
  IPF occurrence. Furthermore, the downregulation of LONP1 triggers the PERK/eIF2
  α/ATF4 cascade reaction, which in turn activates the ISR dominated by ATF4, ultimately
  inducing ciliary cell death and COPD development. Additionally, LONP1 promotes the
  proliferation and migration of pulmonary artery smooth muscle cells by degrading
  MPC1, thereby facilitating the progression of PH. LONP1, Lon protease 1; ROS, reactive
  oxygen species; I/R, ischemia/reperfusion; COX, cytochrome c oxidase; PDK4, pyruvate
  dehydrogenase kinase 4; MAM, mitochondria-associated endoplasmic reticulum membrane;
  OPA1, optic atrophy 1; Drp1, dynamin-related protein 1; MFN1, mitofusin 1; eIF2α,
  eukaryotic initiation factor 2α; ATF, transcription factor; GLUT1, glucose transporter
  1; TFAM, mitochondrial transcription factor A; mtDNA, mitochondrial DNA; HMOX1,
  heme oxygenase 1; LDHB, lactate dehydrogenase B; PI3K, phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase;
  FGF2, fibroblast growth factor 2; TGF-β, transforming growth factor-β; PAI-1, plasminogen
  activator inhibitor-1; Rb, retinoblastoma; AT2, alveolar type 2; IPF, idiopathic
  pulmonary fibrosis; PERK, protein kinase R-like endoplasmic reticulum kinase; ISR,
  integrated stress response; COPD, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease; MPC1, mitochondrial
  pyruvate carrier 1; PH, pulmonary hypertension'
