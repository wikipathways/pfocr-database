caption: 'Mechanical stimulation triggers Ca2+ influx through Piezo1/Piezo2, and this
  process promotes cell proliferation via two distinct pathways: 1. The Piezo2 pathway:
  Ca2+ activates AKT, which inactivates GSK-3β. The inactivated GSK-3β stabilizes
  the transcription factor Snail, which then regulates epithelial-mesenchymal transition
  (EMT)-associated molecules, ultimately driving cell proliferation; 2. The Piezo1
  pathway (exemplified in cancer cells): Ca2+ activates signaling cascades including
  PI3K/AKT/mTOR and MAPKs. These pathways promote cell cycle progression and suppress
  apoptosis, thereby facilitating cell proliferation. Created with BioGDP.com.35'
