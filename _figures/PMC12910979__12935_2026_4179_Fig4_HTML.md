caption: 'The detailed mechanistic signaling pathways through which caspase-3 promotes
  tumor repopulation (with black arrows) and angiogenesis (with red arrows) are illustrated.
  Specifically, caspase-3 in dying tumor cells mediates tumor repopulation through
  pathways including caspase-3/iPLA2/PGE2, caspase-3/ATM/p53/COX-2/PGE2, and caspase-3/PKCδ/Akt/VEGF-A.
  Caspase-3 in dying tumor cells mediates surrounding tumor angiogenesis through pathways
  comprising caspase-3/p-Akt/VEGF-A, caspase-3/NF-κB/COX-2/PGE2, and caspase-3/p-eIF4E/VEGF-A.
  In addition, cleaved caspase-3 could act as a transcriptional factor to upregulate
  the expression of proangiogenic genes. iPLA2: calcium-independent phospholipase
  A2; PGE2: prostaglandin E2; ATM: Ataxia Telangiectasia Mutated; COX-2: cyclooxygenase-2;
  PKCδ: protein kinase Cδ; VEGF-A: vascular endothelial growth factor-A; NF-κB: nuclear
  factor kappa-B; eIF4E: eukaryotic initiation factor 4E'
