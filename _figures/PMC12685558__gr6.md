caption: Ferroptosis is a unique form of cell death dependent on iron and specific
  regulatory mechanisms [138]. Within the TNFR1 inflammatory signaling pathway, TNF
  signaling initiates and triggers the formation of necrosomes, such as complex IIB,
  through TNFR1 complex I [139,140]. In the context of JNK activation and ROS-regulated
  autophagy, these pathways not only regulate apoptosis in OCs but are also activated
  under elevated ROS levels, leading to the transcription of pro-apoptotic genes including
  caspase 3, FASL, and caspase 9 [141]. Central to this mechanism is the NLRP3 inflammasome,
  which plays a critical role in various inflammation-associated disorders and can
  be activated by ROS, thereby promoting pyroptotic cell death in OBs and further
  inhibiting the osteogenic process [142]
