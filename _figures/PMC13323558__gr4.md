caption: 'Neural orchestration of the lung cancer metastatic cascade and pre-metastatic
  niche formation. (A) Neural-driven local invasion and lymphovascular dissemination:
  In the primary tumor microenvironment, sympathetic fibers release NE, which binds
  to receptors on lung cancer cells to trigger EMT. Concurrently, nerve-derived factors
  such as VEGF-C and GDNF stimulate lymphangiogenesis, vasodilation, and PNI, thereby
  facilitating the systemic escape of tumor cells through newly formed neural and
  vascular conduits. (B) Sympathetic remodeling of the pre-metastatic niche: Primary
  lung cancer cells secrete exosomes into the systemic circulation to prime distant
  organs. In the lung parenchyma, sympathetic signaling recruits immunosuppressive
  populations, including MDSCs and macrophages. These exosomes act on local fibroblasts
  and macrophages to upregulate CCL1 and PD-L1 expression, creating a localized immunosuppressive
  microenvironment that supports the arrival and survival of disseminated tumor cells.
  (C) Organ-specific neural regulation of SCLC metastasis: In the brain, SCLC cells
  modulate the microglial state and activate serpins through the secretion of factors
  like α2-AP, IL-6, MIF, and downregulation of IFITM1, consequently maintaining an
  evasive, inhibitory state against host immune surveillance. In the bone microenvironment,
  SCLC-derived signals interact with β2-adrenergic receptors (β2-AR) on osteoblasts,
  triggering the RANKL signaling pathway to promote osteoclastogenesis and the subsequent
  establishment of bone metastatic lesions. α2-AP, Alpha-2-antiplasmin; β2-AR, Beta-2
  adrenergic receptor; CCL1, C-C motif chemokine ligand 1; EMT, Epithelial-mesenchymal
  transition; GDNF, Glial cell line-derived neurotrophic factor; IFITM1, Interferon-induced
  transmembrane protein 1; IL-6, Interleukin 6; MDSCs, Myeloid-derived suppressor
  cells; MIF, Macrophage migration inhibitory factor; NE, Norepinephrine; PD-L1, Programmed
  death-ligand 1; PNI, Perineural invasion; RANKL, Receptor activator of nuclear factor
  kappa-b ligand; SCLC, Small cell lung cancer; VEGF-C, Vascular endothelial growth
  factor C'
