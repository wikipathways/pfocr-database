caption: Mechanism link between depression and OA/RA. Depression significantly influences
  the onset and progression of joint diseases via neuro-immune-endocrine and behavioral
  pathways. In OA, activation of the HPA axis and sympathetic nervous system drives
  low-grade inflammation (IL-1β, IL-6, TNF-α), upregulates MMPs, and accelerates cartilage
  degradation, forming a vicious depression–OA cycle. Psychological stress lowers
  pain thresholds, increasing sensitivity to chronic joint pain. In RA, depression
  disrupts central monoaminergic signaling through IL-6 and other cytokines, heightening
  pain and fatigue, aggravating immune imbalance, reducing remission rates, and establishing
  a bidirectional RA–depression cycle
