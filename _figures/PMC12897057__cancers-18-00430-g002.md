caption: Modulation of lncRNAs by polyphenols and flavonoids. Polyphenols and flavonoids,
  including curcumin, resveratrol, epigallocatechin gallate (EGCG), quercetin, and
  berberine, selectively suppress oncogenic lncRNAs (e.g., HOTAIR, H19, MALAT1, NEAT1,
  UCA1, ROR) while inducing tumor-suppressive lncRNAs (e.g., GAS5, MEG3, NBR2, CASC2,
  INXS), converging on key regulatory pathways involved in proliferation, apoptosis,
  epithelial–mesenchymal transition (EMT), stemness, inflammation, and metabolic adaptation
  such as Wnt/β-catenin, TGF-β, PI3K–AKT–mTOR, and NF-κB signaling pathways. Red text
  denotes oncogenic lncRNAs, while green text denotes tumor-suppressive lncRNAs. Polyphenol-induced
  regulation of lncRNAs is concentration-, timing-, and context-dependent; therefore,
  individual lncRNAs are depicted according to their predominant reported functional
  role for clarity
