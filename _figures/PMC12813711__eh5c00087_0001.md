caption: Environmental sources and human exposure of MNPs. Types, sources, exposure
  methods, and processes associated with MNPs. The classification of plastics relies
  on the SPI (Society of the Plastics Industry) code, developed by the American Plastics
  Industry Association to categorize plastic products. Human exposure to MNPs can
  occur via the digestive tract, respiratory tract, and skin contact. MNPs can infiltrate
  intestinal and alveolar epithelial cells, enabling their entry into systemic circulation
  and eventual migration to the brain. Due to their diminutive size, MNPs may be capable
  of directly traversing the BBB through the olfactory nerve pathway
