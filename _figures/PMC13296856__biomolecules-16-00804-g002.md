caption: Canonical, non-canonical, and proposed mitomiR biogenesis and transport mechanisms
  (Created with BioRender.com). In the canonical pathway, RNA polymerase II transcription
  produces a primary-miRNA transcript which is cleaved in the nucleus by Drosha, resulting
  in a pre-miRNA. Transport machinery exports the pre-miRNA to the cytoplasm where
  it is further cleaved by Dicer, forming the double-stranded RNA duplex. One strand
  of this duplex becomes the mature miRNA which, when associated with RISC, directs
  Agonaute protein binding to target specific mRNAs for translation repression or
  degradation. Non-canonical miRNA pathways bypass one or more canonical steps. Multiple
  origins and transport pathways are likely involved in mitomiR biogenesis
