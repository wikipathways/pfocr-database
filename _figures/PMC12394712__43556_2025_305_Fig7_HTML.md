caption: 'Role of DAMPs in tumor immunity. Immunomodulation and inflammatory response
  in the tumor microenvironment: immunogenic cell death DAMPs such as HSP, HMGB1,
  mtDNA, etc., activate receptors such as TLRs, RAGE, NLRs, etc., which in turn triggers
  pathways such as MAPK, NLRP3 inflammasome NF -κB, etc., to promote the release of
  pro-inflammatory cytokines and recruitment of immune cells. At the same time, immunosuppressive
  cells are activated and expanded to secrete cytokines such as IL-10 and TGF-β, creating
  a complex balance between pro- and anti-tumor. Inflammatory response leads to the
  destruction of the epithelial barrier and the degradation of the extracellular matrix
  through MMPs, which ultimately promotes the growth, proliferation and metastasis
  of tumor cells'
