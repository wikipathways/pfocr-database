caption: Graphical representation of exosomal cargo‐loading mechanisms. a) The endosomal
  sorting complexes required for transport (ESCRT) pathway involves the recognition
  of ubiquitinated proteins by the ESCRT‐0 complex, which recruits ESCRT‐I and ESCRT‐II
  to facilitate the clustering of cargo on endosomal membranes, leading to the assembly
  of ESCRT‐III that drives membrane budding and the formation of intraluminal vesicles
  (ILVs).[ 114, 115, 116, 117 ] b) ESCRT‐III, in conjunction with Alix, participates
  in RNA loading into ILVs by recruiting specific RNA‐binding proteins, enabling the
  incorporation of RNAs into nascent exosomes.[ 118, 119 ] c) The exosomal‐LAMP2A
  Loading of Cargo (e‐LLoC) mechanism facilitates the selective incorporation of proteins
  with ExoSignal motifs into nascent exosomes via heat shock cognate (HSC)70 binding
  to lysosomal‐associated membrane protein (LAMP2A) in endosomes enriched with tetraspanins
  such as cluster of differentiation (CD)63, operating independently of the ESCRT
  pathway.[ 28, 120, 121, 122 ] d) Caveolin influences RNA loading by recruiting heterogeneous
  nuclear ribonucleoproteins (hnRNPs) to endosomal membranes,[ 123, 124, 125 ] whereas
  neutral sphingomyelinase (nSMase)2 promotes the sorting of miRNAs into exosomes
  by converting sphingomyelin into ceramide.[ 126, 127 ]
