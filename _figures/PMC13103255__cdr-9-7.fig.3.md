caption: 'Palmitoylation coordinates EGFR membrane dynamics and organelle-specific
  signaling. Catalyzed by DHHC-family palmitoyltransferases (such as DHHC13 and DHHC20),
  palmitoylation at specific cysteine residues critically regulates EGFR subcellular
  localization. Palmitoylation anchors EGFR to the plasma membrane through the ARF6-dependent
  sorting pathway. However, palmitoylation at different cysteines elicits distinct
  functional outcomes: palmitoylation at C1025 inhibits Grb2 binding and downstream
  signaling, whereas palmitoylation at C1122 promotes endocytosis and subsequent lysosomal
  degradation. Meanwhile, FASN-mediated palmitoylation at C797 facilitates both mitochondrial
  translocation - enhancing mitochondrial fusion and cell survival via the PHB2/OPA1
  axis - and nuclear import, thereby coordinating metabolic adaptation and proliferation.
  Created in BioRender. LIAN, J. (2026) https://BioRender.com/ivso7d7. EGFR: Epidermal

  P-AKT: phosphorylated protein kinase B; SREBP1: sterol regulatory element-binding
  protein 1'
