caption: This model illustrates how the transcription factor SlWRI3 orchestrates the
  production of acylsugars within type I/IV trichome tip cells. SlWRI3 bridges primary
  and specialized metabolism by directly binding to AW-box cis-regulatory elements
  in the promoters of its target genes. This dual regulation activates both the upstream
  fatty acid synthesis (FAS) pathway for precursor supply and the downstream acylsugar
  assembly pathway. SlαCT, SlBCCP2, and SlBC are subunits of the ACCase complex. ACCase
  catalyze the rate-limiting step in the conversion of acetyl-CoA to malonyl-CoA,
  which also constitutes the initiation step of fatty acid biosynthesis. SlASAT1 has
  been identified to utilize sucrose and various acyl-CoAs for the production of monoacylsucroses
  via pyranose R4 acylation. They were all confirmed to be direct targets of SlWRI3
