caption: Regulation of ferroptosis in MM. BMSCs increases the iron level in MM cells,
  thereby enhancing steroid biosynthesis pathway to produce lanosterol, which is a
  major source of ROS in MM cells. The direct coupling of CD40 ligand and CD40 receptor
  governs lanosterol biosynthesis, and CD40 neutralizing antibody or conditional depletion
  of Cd40l disrupts CD40/CD40L interaction eliminates the increased iron level and
  lanosterol production of MM cells localized in the Vk*MYC Vk12653 or NSG mouse models.
  MM cells induce BM adipocytes lipolysis, then the released free fatty acids (FFAs)
  secreted from adipocytes are taken up by MM cells through FA transporter proteins
  (FATPs). The higher FA uptake leads to lipotoxicity through a ferroptosis mechanism.
  Inhibition of either BM adipocytes lipolysis or FFA transporter into MM cells could
  be a potential novel strategy to prevent MM progression
