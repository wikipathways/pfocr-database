caption: Schematic model of β2-adrenergic signaling during prolonged T cell stimulation
  and exhaustion. Prolonged TCR/CD3 and CD28 stimulation induces sustained NFATc1
  signaling and TOX expression, leading to increased inhibitory receptor expression
  (PD-1, TIM-3, CD38) and impaired cytokine and cytotoxic mediator release (IFN-γ,
  TNF, perforin, granzyme B) in cytotoxic T cells. Canonical β2-AR activation by terbutaline
  signals primarily through the Gs–cAMP–PKA pathway and does not prevent exhaustion-associated
  transcriptional programs under chronic stimulation. In contrast, the β2-AR-biased
  agonist nebivolol preferentially engages β-arrestin-dependent signaling, stabilizes
  β2-AR expression, reduces NFATc1 and TOX activity, and partially restores effector
  function. Created in BioRender. Hajiaghayi, M. (2026) https://BioRender.com/kcvtg88
