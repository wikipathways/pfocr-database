caption: ARGs transmission network within poultry production systems. The model illustrates
  multiscale ARGs transmission routes. Vertical transmission occurs via maternal transfer,
  hatchery contamination, and early-life colonization. Horizontal transmission occurs
  via feed, drinking water, litter, air, and biofilms. Environmental dissemination
  via poultry manure contributes to soil, water, and crop contamination. These pathways
  form feedback loops that reintroduce ARGs into animals and humans. Network analysis
  highlights MGEs-mediated gene exchange between commensal and pathogenic bacteria
