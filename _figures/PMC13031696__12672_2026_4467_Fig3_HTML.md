caption: IL-24-induced IGFBP-3 stimulates apoptosis by inhibiting the mTOR pathway
  in PCa. Mcl-1 is transcribed in the nucleus, and its splicing to an oncogenic isoform
  is carried out by the contribution of RBP SRSF1 and is exported to the cytoplasm.
  IL-24 upregulates RBP IGFBP-3, which inhibits mTOR activity. mTOR triggers translation
  of Mcl-1 via phosphorylation of 4E-BP and S6K, allowing them to contribute to the
  translational machinery producing Mcl-1 protein. Mcl-1 promotes Poly (ADP-ribose)
  polymerase (PARP), leading to DNA damage repair and cell survival
