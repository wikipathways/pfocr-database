caption: Diagram of the selenium regulatory mechanism. Under heat stress conditions,
  cells generate free radicals. Selenium, as an essential component of the active
  center of glutathione peroxidase (GSH-Px), enhances enzyme activity, promoting its
  catalysis of reduced glutathione (GSH) to eliminate peroxides and reduce oxidative
  damage. Concurrently, heat stress impairs melanin synthesis pathways (the α-MSH-MC1R-cAMP-TRP
  pathway), and appropriate selenium supplementation can reverse this detrimental
  effect. Data from Xu [97]. Created with BioGDP.com [111]. α-MSH, α-melanocyte-stimulating
