caption: Schematic illustration of the preparation and therapeutic mechanism of personalized
  biomimetic bio-cornea (DBC@L-Cel). Bacterial nanocellulose (BC) with customized
  curvature and architecture was biosynthesized using precision-designed containers,
  followed by aldehyde modification to enhance transparency. Microparticles of lenticules
  (L) from small incision lenticule extraction surgery (SMILE) and celastrol (Cel)
  were incorporated onto dialdehyde BC (DBC) to generate the personalized biomimetic
  bio-cornea DBC@L-Cel, with optical and mechanical properties consistent with cornea.
  DBC@L-Cel was applied to treat a rabbit corneal large-area defect model using GelMA
  photopolymerization. DBC@L-Cel inhibits fibrosis and maintains corneal stromal homeostasis
  by blocking the AGE/RAGE/NF-κB and PI3K/AKT pathway signaling pathway, while upregulating
  Wnt/β-catenin activation to repair corneal epithelium through enhanced proliferation
  and adhesion functions, achieving scar-free healing
