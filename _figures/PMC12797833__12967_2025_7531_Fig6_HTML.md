caption: UBE2M-mediated neddylation of MKK7 upregulates EGR1 to inhibit melanoma proliferation.
  UBE2M mediates MKK7 neddylation in melanoma cells, reducing its ubiquitination and
  proteasomal degradation, thereby increasing MKK7 protein levels. Elevated MKK7 phosphorylation
  promotes JNK activation in the MAPK pathway, leading to enhanced transcription and
  expression of EGR1. EGR1 subsequently represses CCND2 expression, resulting in inhibited
  melanoma cell proliferation
