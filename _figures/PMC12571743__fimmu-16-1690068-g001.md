  Normal cells lead to mitochondrial oxidative phosphorylation. Tumor cells show the
  Warburg effect and lactate production with lactyl-CoA involvement. Immune cells
  exhibit similar glycolytic pathways with increased lactate and lactyl-CoA. The nucleus
  section highlights histone modifications affecting gene expression through lysine
  lactylation. Pathway proteins affected include P53, G6PD, and Eef1a2
