caption: Mechanistic overview of neurodegeneration by NMDAR-mediated excitotoxicity
  and neuronal death. Overactivation of NMDARs increases Ca2+ inside the cell, causing
  calpain activation and lysosomal dysfunction, thus the release of cathepsins, which
  triggers the apoptotic pathway and releases cell-destructive enzymes. Mitochondrial
  dysfunction causes reactive oxidative stress, leading to an increase in AIF. It
  also causes cytochrome c release and cleaves the cellular proteins, which further
  shrinks the neurons. On the other hand, increasing intercellular Ca2+ ions instigate
  nNOS, which forms ONOO− and leads to cell excitotoxicity that causes the excess
  release of glutamate and induction of Aβ, thus causing neuronal cell death. Thus,
  the above-mentioned mechanisms are responsible for neuronal cell death and further
  lead to neurological disease
