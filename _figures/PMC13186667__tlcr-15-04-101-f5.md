caption: A complex network of cell signaling pathways and genetic alterations associated
  with cancer cell growth and proliferation. It highlights key receptors such as EGFR,
  FGFR1, and IGF1R, activated by their corresponding ligands, leading to downstream
  signaling via pathways like RAS-RAF-MEK-ERK and PI3K-AKT-mTOR. Various gene mutations
  and amplifications, such as EGFR mutations and HER2 amplifications, result in abnormal
  signaling activity. The figure also covers processes related to cancer, including
  gene fusions, transcriptions, and the histological transformation of NSCLC to other
  types, involving EMT. Additionally, it outlines interactions between molecules,
  highlighting activation and inhibition dynamics, which are influenced by factors
  like AXL activation and PTEN loss. EGFR, epidermal growth factor receptor; EMT,
  epithelial-mesenchymal transition; NSCLC, non-small cell lung cancer
