caption: Schematic diagram illustrating how the GAS5-Notch-NF-κB/NLRP3 axis mediates
  M1 macrophage polarization, reprograms macrophage phenotypes, and aggravates CaOx
  induced kidney injury in CaOx nephrolithiasis. Upon COM exposure, Notch1 activates
  the NF-κB/NLRP3 inflammatory signaling pathway via TLR4, thereby amplifying the
  inflammatory response, promoting M1 macrophage polarization, and exacerbating renal
  injury. This occurs through a synergistic process involving macrophage polarization
  imbalance, hyperactivation of inflammatory signaling, and pyroptosis during CaOx
  crystal formation. Meanwhile, GAS5 regulates Notch1 expression by targeting distinct
  miRNAs in a species dependent manner (miR-449c-5p in human macrophages and miR-325-3p
  in murine macrophages), functioning as a ceRNA network, plays a critical role in
  macrophage polarization and serves as a key molecular mechanism bridging these inflammatory
  processes. The persistent pro-inflammatory immune microenvironment further intensifies
  damage to renal tubular epithelial cells, thereby promoting CaOx nephrolithiasis
  formation
