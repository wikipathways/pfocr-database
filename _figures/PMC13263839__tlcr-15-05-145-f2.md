caption: 'Mechanisms of immune resistance in lung cancer and mechanism-driven breakthrough
  strategies. The left panel illustrates the primary mechanisms of immune resistance
  in lung cancer, while the right panel presents corresponding intervention strategies.
  Regarding resistance mechanisms, tumor cells achieve immune evasion through multi-tiered
  pathways, including T-cell exhaustion, impaired antigen presentation, physical barriers
  formed by CAFs, enrichment of immune-suppressive cells, metabolic suppression, and
  immature or dysfunctional TLS. Mechanism-driven overcoming strategies encompass:
  (I) local vaccines to restore antigen presentation and immune priming; (II) chemoimmunotherapy;
  (III) bispecific antibodies for dual vascular-immune pathway reprogramming; (IV)
  anti-angiogenic therapies; and (V) TME remodeling. The overall emphasis is on transitioning
  from single PD-L1 axis therapy towards a precision immunotherapy framework of ‘multi-pathway
  synergistic regulation’. Created in BioRender. Xu Y. 2026. Available online: https://BioRender.com/fjleu3l.

  microenvironment; Treg, regulatory T cell'
