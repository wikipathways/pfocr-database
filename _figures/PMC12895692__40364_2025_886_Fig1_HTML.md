caption: The regulatory roles of circRNAs in Alzheimer’s disease. This shematic highlights
  some key circRNAs and how they function in the context of AD. For instance, ciRS-7
  plays a role in regulating Aβ production and microglial activation through the miR-7/UBE2A
  pathway. Then there’s circ-AXL, which modulates neuroinflammation and apoptosis
  via miR-328/BACE1. Additionally, circHOMER1 and circCORO1C are known to influence
  γ-secretase activity and app expression. Other circRNAs, like circRIMS2, are also
  implicated in synaptic dysfunction, signaling pathways, and neuronal death. Together,
  these circRNAs form a crucial network that contributes to the pathogenesis of Alzheimer’s
  disease
