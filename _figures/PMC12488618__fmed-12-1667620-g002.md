caption: RAGE pathway and its effects. Upon binding its ligands, RAGE activates Ras/MEK/ERK,
  p38/MAPK, JAK/STAT, PI3K/AKT, and Cdc42/Rac1 signaling pathways, along with NADPH
  oxidase through adaptor proteins such as DIAPH1, DAP10, and TIRAP. These downstream
  pathways subsequently activate transcription factors such as NF-κB, AP1, Egr1, and
  STAT3, driving gene expression and triggering a cascade of effects, including inflammation,
  cell migration, proliferation, senescence, death, and carcinogenesis. Moreover,
  activation of the RAGE pathway enhances the expression of RAGE itself and several
  of its ligands, like AGEs and HMGB1, creating a positive feedback loop that further
  amplifies RAGE signaling. This figure was created in https://BioRender.com
