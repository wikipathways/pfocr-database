caption: A Apoptotic cells release “find-me” signals to attract phagocytes. B “Eat-me”
  signals, such as PtdSer, are externalized on the plasma membrane and recognized
  directly or indirectly by efferocytic receptors expressed on phagocytes. The interaction
  of ACs through those receptors leads to a signaling pathway that culminates in actin
  filament polymerization, mediating AC internalization. C Phagosome maturation and
  subsequent fusion with the lysosome promote the degradation of the apoptotic body.
  The efferocytosis of non-infected ACs leads to the production of anti-inflammatory
  mediators and the differentiation of regulatory T cells. Created with BioRender.com
