caption: The proposed schematic diagram for NETosis‐like response induced by EVs‐derived
  viral nucleic acid delivery. During WSSV infection, viral mRNA wsv271 was packaged
  in EVs secreted by haemocytes and transmitted to the surrounding EVs‐recipient cells,
  followed by translation into viral protein, and then interacted with the TIR domain
  of Toll4 to recruit MyD88, so as to activate P38‐MAPK pathway and further facilitate
  PAD4 phosphorylation and nuclear translocation to citrullinate histone‐H3, which
  eventually suppressed the spread of viral infection by inducing NETosis‐like response
  in haemocytes
