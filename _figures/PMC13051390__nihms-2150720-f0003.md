caption: 'The signaling pathways involving PDE8 across various biological systems.
  PDE8 interacts with Raf-1, leading to the upregulation of the downstream MEK and
  ERK signaling cascade, which ultimately enhances the function of cyclic AMP-responsive
  element-binding protein (CREB) in the nucleus. Concurrently, PDE8 associates with
  the R subunit of PKA (PKA-R), resulting in a decrease in cytosolic cAMP levels.
  This reduction allows the C subunit of PKA (PKA-C) to interact with CREB, thereby
  further amplifying the CREB signaling pathway. Together, these interactions contribute
  to the downregulation of steroidogenesis, promote tumor progression, and influence
  T-cell motility. Additionally, the scaffolding protein AKAP5/79 facilitates the
  recruitment of PDE8 to the endoplasmic reticulum-plasma membrane junction, where
  the pathway is initiated by SOCE through the action of STIM1 and ORAI1 channels.
  Raf-1: Raf-1 proto-oncogene, serine/threonine kinase; MEK: Mitogen-activated protein
  kinase kinase; ERK: Extracellular signal-regulated kinase; CREB: cyclic AMP-responsive
  element-binding protein; PKA-R subunit: PKA Regulatory subunit; PKA-C subunit: PKA
  Catalytic subunit; ER-PM Junction: junction of endoplasmic reticulum and plasma
  membrane.w STIM1: Stromal interaction molecule 1; ORAI1: Calcium release-activated
  calcium channel protein 1; SOCE: store-operated calcium entry. Created in BioRender.
