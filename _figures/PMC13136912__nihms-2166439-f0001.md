caption: Maladaptive plasticity in the brainstem is associated with increased expression
  of NMDA receptors on the neuronal membrane. NMDA receptors in the trigeminal ganglia
  are linked to TRPV1 activation and pain responses. 1) BDNF binds to TrkB receptors
  in the brainstem, leading to phosphorylation of NMDA receptors. 2) NMDA receptor
  activation results in a calcium influx into the postsynaptic neuron. 3) The amplification
  of NMDA receptor activity, driven by glutamate binding and phosphorylation, contributes
  to maladaptive plasticity. 4) Calcium signaling induces phosphorylation of TRPV1
  channels, increasing sodium and calcium influx through these channels in trigeminal
  ganglion cells, thereby promoting nociception. 5) BK activates the PKC signaling
  pathway, leading to TRPV1 channels opening via phosphorylation and promoting gene
  transcription in the cell nucleus. BK, Bradykinin; BDNF, Brain-derived neurotrophic
  factor; TrkB, Tropomyosin receptor kinase B; NMDA, N-methyl-D-aspartate; TRPV1,
  Transient receptor potential vanilloid 1; GLU, Glutamate; PKC, Protein kinase C.
