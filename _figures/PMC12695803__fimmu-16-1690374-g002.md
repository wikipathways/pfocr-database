caption: Mechanisms of microbiota modulating PD1/PD-L1 axis. Butyrate increases PD-1
  expression and enhances the anti-PD-1 therapeutic response by enhancing histone
  H3K27 acetylation on the promoters of PDCD1 and CD28 in CD8+ T cells. The 3HB from
  KD inhibits the upregulation of PD-L1 in myeloid cells induced by immunotherapy
  and simultaneously promotes the expansion of CTLs. Plant-derived nanoparticles GELNs
  can enrich DHA through the regulation of intestinal bacteria (such as the Conidae
  and Lactobacillus families). DHA binds to the PD-L1 promoter and inhibits its transcription
  by antagonizing c-Myc, thereby making the tumor sensitive to PD-L1 inhibitors. FMT
  combined with salinomycin increases the levels of propionate and butyrate, promotes
  the infiltration of CD8+ T cells, and reduces the expression of PD-L1. The extracellular
  vesicles derived from Bifidobacterium upregulate PD-L1 through the TLR4-NF-κB pathway
  and simultaneously enhance the infiltration of CD8+ T cells
