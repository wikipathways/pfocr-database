caption: Postprandial hyperglycemia decreases cellular antioxidant capacity. Postprandial
  hyperglycemia inhibits the pentose phosphate pathway and consequently decreases
  NADPH production. In addition, it increases the polyol pathway and thus increases
  NADPH consumption. Therefore, postprandial hyperglycemia leads to NADPH deficiency,
  consequently decreasing cellular levels of GSH and cellular antioxidant capacity.
  Blue text represents enzymes for each reaction. ↑, increase; ↓, decrease; GSH, reduced
  glutathione; GSSG, glutathione disulfide; NADP+, oxidized nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide
