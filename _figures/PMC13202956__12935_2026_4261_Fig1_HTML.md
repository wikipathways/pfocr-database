caption: 'Cellular Regulatory Pathways of Apoptosis, Necroptosis, and Pyroptosis.
  A. Apoptosis Pathways: a. Extrinsic Apoptosis Pathway: Triggered by the binding
  of death receptor ligands to their receptors, forming the DISC, which subsequently
  activates Caspase-3/7. b. Intrinsic Apoptosis Pathway: Triggered by MOMP, leading
  to the release of cytochrome c, formation of the apoptosome with APAF-1, and activation
  of downstream apoptotic processes. B. Necroptosis Pathways: a. RIPK1/3-Mediated
  Necroptosis: Initiated by the binding of TNF to TNFR: (i. Ubiquitinated RIPK1: Does
  not induce cell death. ii. Non-ubiquitinated RIPK1: Induces apoptosis. iii. Non-ubiquitinated
  RIPK1 and absence of Caspase-8: RIPK1 binds and phosphorylates RIPK3, forming the
  necrosome, which induces necroptosis.) b. RIPK3-Mediated Necroptosis: Triggered
  by the binding of LPS to TLR4, inducing phosphorylation of RIPK3 and subsequent
  necroptosis. C. Pyroptosis Pathways: a. GSDMD-Mediated Pyroptosis: Triggered by
  LPS, leading to the cleavage of GSDMD and formation of GSDMD-N oligomeric pores
  in the cell membrane, inducing pyroptosis. b. GSDME-Mediated Pyroptosis: Activated
  by upstream Caspase-3 cleavage of GSDME, forming pore-like structures in the cell
  membrane and inducing pyroptosis'
