caption: Gene expression patterns of chitin degradation and metabolism in P. lilacinum
  FDZ8Y1 regulated by 40 MPa. Pathways involving all identified genes are indicated
  by solid arrows; dotted lines with square dots represent annotated metabolic processes
  (with glycolysis highlighted in blue dashed box, chitin degradation in golden, fatty
  acid degradation in grey, and the TCA cycle in orange). Short dashed lines indicate
  the interconnections between different metabolic pathways, while square-dotted arrows
  represent partially omitted metabolic conversions, long lines indicate the flow
  direction of intracellular synthetic substances. The purple boxes represent the
  fungal cell wall and cell membrane. The different background colors in the center
  indicate various intracellular metabolic processes involving GlcNAc (orange represents
  biosynthetic processes, while blue represents energy metabolism processes). Genes
  marked in red, green, and black indicate upregulated, downregulated, and non-significantly
  expressed genes under HHP conditions, respectively. Gene names and their corresponding
  encoded proteins are listed in Table S5
