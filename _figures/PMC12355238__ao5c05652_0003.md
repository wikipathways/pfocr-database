caption: Extrinsic or death receptor (DR) pathway. The intracellular portion of the
  DR is known as the death domain (DD). When receptor–ligand complexes get together,
  they form a death-inducing signaling complex (DISC). This complex brings in and
  sets up initiator caspase-8 and lets active caspase enzyme molecules into the cytosol.
  These molecules activate the effector caspases-3 and -7, resulting in nuclear protein
  cleavage and the initiation of apoptosis. FasL = Fas ligand; TNFR = tumor necrosis
  factor receptor; FADD = Fas-associated death domain; TRADD = TNF-associated death
  domain; C-FLIP = FLICE-like inhibitory protein. Reprint with permission from the
