caption: De novo and salvage pathways for tetrahydrobiopterin (BH4) biosynthesis.
  Synthesis of BH4 de novo is initiated by the metabolism of GTP to dihydroneopterin
  triphosphate via GTP cyclohydrolase I (GTPCH) followed by its metabolism to 6-pyruvoyltetrahydrobiopterin
  (PTP) by PTP synthase (PTPS). Sepiapterin reductase (SPR) catalyzes the metabolism
  of PTP to BH4. Sepiapterin is generated non-enzymatically from PTP. In the salvage
  pathway, SPR catalyzes the reduction of sepiapterin to BH2; dihydrofolic acid reductase
  (DHFR) mediates metabolism of BH2 to BH4
