caption: 'Mechanism of palmitoylation in bone metabolic diseases. In OP, ZDHHC13 improves
  mitochondrial function by promoting protein palmitoylation, or enhances bone homeostasis
  by promoting palmitoylation of MT1-MMP, and ultimately relieves OP. 2-BP inhibits
  protein palmitoylation, downregulates the expression of c-Fox and NFATc1, inhibits
  the osteoclastogenesis and relieves OP. APT1 inhibits palmitoylation of BMPR1a,
  upregulates BMP and Smad, and finally relieves OP. In OS, 2-bromopalmitate inhibits
  protein palmitoylation, upregulates CHOP expression, promotes apoptosis, and finally
  relieves OS. MiR-940 upregulates the expression of ZDHHC19, promotes protein palmitoylation,
  upregulates wnt/β-catenin signaling pathway, and finally induces OS. ZDHHC13: Zinc
  finger Asp-His-His-Cys palmitoyl-acyltransferase 13. MT1-MMP: membrane type 1-matrix
  metalloproteinase. 2-BP: 2-bromopalmitic acid. c-Fox: cellular fos. APT1: Acyl-protein
  thioesterase 1. BMPR1a: bone morphogenetic protein receptor type 1a. BMP: bone morphogenetic
  protein. Smad: small mother against decapentaplegic. ZDHHC19: Zinc finger Asp-His-His-Cys
  palmitoyl-acyltransferase 19'
