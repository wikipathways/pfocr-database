caption: Network-based analysis of side effect predictions for diphenhydramine and
  pemetrexed. (a) Baseline analysis for diphenhydramine, showing the recovery of a
  TP pathway phenotype, “hypertensive disease”, based on known drug-binding proteins.
  (b) Subnetwork for diphenhydramine after incorporating targets from ChEMBL, resulting
  in the recovery of both the baseline pathway phenotype, “hypertensive disease”,
  and a new TP pathway phenotype, “old myocardial infarction”. (c) Subnetwork for
  diphenhydramine with targets from PocketFEATURE, recovering both previous TP pathway
  phenotypes (“hypertensive disease” and “old myocardial infarction”) and identifying
  a newly defined TP pathway phenotype. (d) Analysis of pemetrexed, which had no TP
  pathway phenotypes in the baseline analysis but identified multiple new TP pathway
  phenotypes, including “genetic hypertension”, “thrombocytopenia 5”, and “neuropathy”,
  after integrating targets from New_DrugBank. A protein complex, which represents
  37 proteins (12 of which are shown in this subnetwork), connects pemetrexed’s binding
  protein ALB to downstream pathways, influencing these newly identified pathway phenotypes
