caption: Schematic representation of the effects of bindarit on post-status epilepticus
  brain injury. During the acute stage of SE, C3/C3aR-mediated BBB disruption contributes
  to cerebral edema. Besides, microglia activation induced by SE promote astrocytes
  to adopt a neurotoxic subtype, which facilitates C3 secretion to further promote
  pro-inflammatory microglia remodeling. This feedforward loop magnifies neurovascular
  inflammation and aggravates brain edema. On the chronic stage, the vicious cycle
  formed by C3/C3aR pathway triggers microglia-mediated synaptic pruning and causes
  cognitive dysfunction. However, bindarit markedly alleviates BBB breakdown, modifies
  the functional status of microglia to speed neuroinflammation resolution, and further
  mitigates synaptic elimination, thus ultimately improving brain edema and cognitive
  comorbidities
