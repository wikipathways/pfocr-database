caption: 'Strategies targeting CTCs. (A) Targeting platelets: CTCs interact with platelets
  to aid immune evasion and metastasis. Anti-platelet agents (e.g., COX-1 inhibitors,
  P2Y12/PAR1 antagonists, thrombin and P-selectin inhibitors, αIIbβ3 blockers) disrupt
  platelet activation, adhesion, and aggregation. (B) Immune checkpoint blockade:
  Inhibitors targeting PD-L1, CD47, and other checkpoints restore immune responses
  against CTCs. (C) Targeting oncogenic pathways: CTCs carry mutations (e.g., BRAF,
  EGFR, KRAS, PIK3CA); pathway-specific inhibitors have therapeutic efficacy. (D)
  Targeting CTC clusters: Na+/K+-ATPase inhibitors disrupt cell-cell adhesion; mobilizing
  immune cells with antibodies or inhibitors may weaken CTC support. BRAF: B-type
  rapidly accelerated fibrosarcoma; COX-1: Cyclooxygenase-1; CTC: Circulating tumor
  cell; EGFR: Epidermal growth factor receptor; KRAS: Kirsten rat sarcoma sarcoma
  viral oncogene homolog; Na+/K+-ATPase: Na+/K+-transporting adenosine triphosphatase;
  PAR1: Protease-activated receptor 1; PD-L1: Programmed death-ligand 1; PIK3CA: Phosphatidylinositol
  3-kinase, catalytic alpha subunit; P2Y12: Purinergic 2Y 12 receptor'
