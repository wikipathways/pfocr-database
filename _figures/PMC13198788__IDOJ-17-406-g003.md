caption: Flowchart depicting the PIK3CA/TSC/mTOR pathway and its effects on cell growth.
  PIK3CA and AKT are inactive in quiescent cells, which results in reduced levels
  of Rheb GTP due to active phosphorylation of TSC1 and TSC2. This causes a reduced
  activity of mTOR and inhibition of cell growth. Activating mutations in PIK3CA (in
  VEN) cause inhibition of phosphorylation of TSC, which increases Rheb GTP levels.
  This causes activation of mTOR which leads to abnormal cell growth. Similar effects
  are observed on inactivating mutations in the TSC complex (in tuberous sclerosis)
  or activating mutations of mTOR (in collagenoma)
