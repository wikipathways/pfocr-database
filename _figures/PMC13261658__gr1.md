caption: Schematic overview of the molecular regulatory mechanisms of RLKs and PUBs.Six
  S-domain receptor-like kinases (SD-RLKs), four lysin motif RLKs, seven leucine-rich
  repeat RLKs (LRR–RLKs), cysteine-rich RLK4 (CRK4), a chitinase-related RLK (CHRK1),
  four receptor-like cytoplasmic kinases (RLCKs), and plant U-box E3 ligases (PUBs)
  form reciprocal regulatory modules based on phosphorylation and ubiquitination.
  These modules collectively regulate plant immune responses, growth and development,
  reproductive processes, as well as drought stress responses and abscisic acid (ABA)
