caption: 'Irisin and Treg Cells in Neuroinflammation and Neuroprotection: Reducing
  Inflammation and Enhancing Antioxidant Defense. Irisin inhibits the release of inflammatory
  factors such as TNF-α, IL-1β and IL-6 in the central nervous system (CNS), thereby
  reducing neuroinflammation and protecting neurons from damage. The synergistic action
  of Irisin and Treg cells enhances immunosuppressive capabilities. Treg cells secrete
  anti-inflammatory cytokines IL-10 and TGF-β, further suppressing excessive immune
  responses and contributing to a neuroprotective environment. Additionally, Irisin
  increases the expression of antioxidants like SOD, GPx and GR, enhancing cellular
  antioxidant capacity and mitigating oxidative stress to neurons. This dual action
  of direct inhibition of cytokine release and modulation of intracellular signaling
  pathways positions Irisin as a promising candidate for therapeutic interventions
  in neurodegenerative diseases'
