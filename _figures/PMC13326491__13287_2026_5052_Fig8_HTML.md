caption: 'Pattern diagram: JAK/STAT pathway-mediated regulation of EMSCs osteogenesis
  by the LNGFR-regulated GH/IGF axis. In WT E16.5d fetal mice, normal LNGFR expression
  ensures proper binding of GH to GHR, initiating dual regulatory effects: directly
  promoting IGF-1 expression and activating the JAK2/STAT3 signaling pathway, as evidenced
  by phosphorylated forms of p-JAK2 and p-STAT3. Importantly, activated JAK2/STAT3
  signaling enhances IGF-1 expression through a feedback loop and increases the transcriptional
  activity of the osteogenic factor RUNX2. This cascade amplifies the osteogenic differentiation
  of EMSCs into osteoblasts, promoting osteogenesis. In lngfr-/- E16.5d fetal mice,
  the absence of LNGFR disrupts the interaction between GH and GHR, leading to impaired
  activation of the JAK2/STAT3 pathway, reduced IGF-1 expression, and decreased RUNX2
  transcriptional activity. These effects inhibit EMSCs osteogenic differentiation.
  Notably, exogenous GH supplementation reactivates both the GH/IGF1 pathway and the
  JAK2/STAT3 pathway via binding to GHR, rescuing the osteogenic inhibition caused
  by LNGFR deficiency. Furthermore, the JAK2/STAT3 inhibitor AG490 and the pathway
  activator RO8191 confirm that the JAK2/STAT3 pathway is a key mediator of LNGFR-regulated
  osteogenesis(This figure was created with BioRender.com.)'
