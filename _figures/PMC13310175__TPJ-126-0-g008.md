caption: 'Proposed galanthamine biosynthetic pathway from 4′‐O‐methylnorbelladine.
  From the cytochrome P450 (CYP) superfamily, the CYP96 family specifically members
  of the CYP96T subfamily have been reported to catalyze the para–ortho′ (p‐o′) oxidative
  phenol coupling of 4′‐O‐methylnorbelladine to yield nornarwedine. From there, two
  competing routes have been proposed: Route (i) with the reduction of nornarwedine
  to norgalanthamine followed by N‐methylation, and Route (ii) with the N‐methylation
  of nornarwedine to narwedine prior to reduction. Methyltransferase (MT) in pink
  that performs methylation in green circle and aldo–keto reductase (AKR) in pink
  that performs reduction as mentioned in brown circle, are the enzymes of this study.
  Structure in red and black is the backbone from tyramine and 3,4‐dihydroxybenzaldehyde,
  respectively, and blue is newly formed bonds on action of enzymes. Other abbreviations:
  Noroxomaritidine/norcraugsodine reductase (NR)'
