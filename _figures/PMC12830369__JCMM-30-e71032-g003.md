caption: The three isoforms of the BAF60 subunits (BAF60a, BAF60b and BAF60c) and
  BRG1 regulate lipid metabolism. (A) Post‐feeding/insulin, phosphorylated BAF60c
  translocates to the nucleus, recruits SWI/SNF via phosphorylated/acetylated USF‐1
  to activate lipogenic genes. (B) SREBP2/1c recruits BRG1 to promoters of cholesterol
  biosynthesis/fatty acid esterification genes. (C) BAF60a activates metabolic pathways
  through transcription factors (TFs)/cofactors interactions and SWI/SNF remodelling,
  regulated by fasting/circadian rhythm/diet. (D) BAF60b binds C/EBPβ to suppress
  PPARγ. (E) High‐fat diet downregulates BAF60b, upregulating PPARγ to promote lipogenesis.
  Ac, acetylation; P, phosphorylation; TFs, transcription factors; USF‐1, upstream
  stimulatory factor 1
