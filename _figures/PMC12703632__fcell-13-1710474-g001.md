caption: The cardiac metabolism of glucose, fatty acids, and ketones involves several
  major metabolic pathways. Glucose metabolism (blue) can be categorized into glycolysis
  and downstream pyruvate metabolism/glucose oxidation. Fatty acid oxidation (yellow)
  is regulated by malonyl-CoA, an endogenous inhibitor. Ketone oxidation (green) is
  primarily described using β-hydroxybutyrate (βOHB), the main circulating ketone
  body. The TCA cycle incorporates acetyl-CoA derived from these pathways and generates
  reducing equivalents —NADH and FADH2 — which enter the electron transport chain
  (ETC.) to drive ATP production
