caption: 'The mechanistic pathways of extrinsic and intrinsic apoptotic processes
  and the executioner role of caspase-3 in apoptosis. The extrinsic apoptotic pathway
  starts when external ligands bind to death receptors on cell surface, such as Fas
  or TNF receptors. This binding leads to the formation of DISC that activates caspase-8.
  Activated caspase-8 then activates caspase-3. The intrinsic apoptotic pathway is
  triggered by internal stresses, which activates BAX and BAK. BAX and BAK then results
  in MOMP and cytochrome c release. Cytochrome c and Apaf-1form apoptosome, which
  in turn cleaves caspase-9. Caspase-9 then proteolytically activates caspase-3. Initiator
  caspase-8 bridges the extrinsic and intrinsic pathways by proteolytically cleaving
  Bid into tBid, which then translocates to mitochondria and interacts with Bax and
  Bak to evoke MOMP and cytochrome c release. Executioner caspase-3 results in DNA
  fragmentation by cleaving substrates like DFF45, ICAD, PARP, and EndoG, et al.,
  and membrane blebbing by cleaving substrates like ROCK1. TNF: tumor necrosis factor;
  DISC: death-inducing signaling complex; MOMP: mitochondrial outer membrane permeabilization;
  DFF45: DNA fragmentation factor (DFF)45; ICAD: inhibitor of caspase-activated DNase;
  PARP: Poly (ADP-ribose) polymerase; EndoG: endonuclease G'
