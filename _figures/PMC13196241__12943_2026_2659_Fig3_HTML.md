caption: Interaction between KRAS and MYC in transcriptome (Created with BioRender.com).
  A In KRAS mutant NSCLC cells, upregulation of MUC1-C activates the WNT/β-catenin
  pathway and facilitates the formation of MUC1-C/β-catenin/TCF4 complexes on the
  MYC promoter. MUC1-C also recruits the p300 histone acetyltransferase, leading to
  histone H3 acetylation and subsequent transcriptional activation of the MYC gene.
  B In PDAC, activated KRAS promotes c-MYC-mediated IscU2 transcription, promoting
  mitochondrial functions and tumor growth. In addition, in KRAS- and MYC-driven PDAC
  xenografts, BRD4 and TOP1 cooperate to accelerate tumor progression. C In CRC, mutant
  KRAS promotes MYC translation, stimulating MYC-dependent transcriptomic adaptation
  to stress with four elevated genes (GADD45B, DDIT3, HSPA1L and CRYBA4). D In thyroid
  cancer, MAZ can significantly bind to the promoters of BRAF and KRAS, increasing
  their transcription and expression, thereby activating MAPK signaling. In KRAS-driven
  PLC, the TF MYC was identified as a switch factor in the lineage commitment between
  hepatocellular carcinoma and intrahepatic cholangiocarcinoma
