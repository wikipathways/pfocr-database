  chemokines and mediators of adaptive immunity in the acute phase of infection compared

  of cytokine storm signaling pathway genes in peripheral blood RNA, quantified by
  gene expression profiling of acute versus follow-up samples. Significant activation
  of innate immunity (46 upregulated and 7 downregulated genes, cutoff FC 1.5 and
  adjusted P < 0.05)'
