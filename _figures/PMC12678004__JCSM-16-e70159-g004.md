caption: Schematic model of apoptotic activity modulates myogenic differentiation.
  During the differentiation of skeletal muscle cells, significant apoptosis occurs.
  Apoptotic cells can release apoptotic vesicles (apoVs), which carry abundant Igf1r.
  After being taken up by surrounding surviving cells, apoVs promote the activation
  of the PI3K/AKT/mTOR signalling pathway within the recipient cells, thereby enhancing
  myogenic differentiation, promoting skeletal muscle hypertrophy, and slowing age‐related
  muscle loss
