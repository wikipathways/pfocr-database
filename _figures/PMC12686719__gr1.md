caption: Schematic illustration of the multifunctional anti-inflammatory hydrogel
  PPT for enhancing OA treatment by modulating the stem cell pathological microenvironment.
  (A) Schematic illustration of the engineered multifunctional hydrogel platform PPT
  integrating temperature-responsive gelation and anti-inflammatory properties, thereby
  establishing a favorable niche to enhance stem cell survival and promote chondrogenic
  differentiation for OA treatment. (B) PPT enhances stem cell survival and retention
  within the joint cavity, promotes chondrogenic differentiation, and exerts paracrine
  effects that further improve chondrocyte autophagy and oxidative stress resistance.
  (C) A schematic diagram depicts molecular mechanisms through which PPT orchestrates
  inflammatory microenvironment modulation, thereby preserving BMSC stemness and stimulating
  proliferation. (D) A diagram illustrates signaling pathways by which PPT-modulated
  BMSCs exhibit paracrine effects to upregulate autophagy and oxidative stress resistance
  in chondrocytes
