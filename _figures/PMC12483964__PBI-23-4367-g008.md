caption: Proposed transcriptional regulation model for the SmMYB39‐SmbHLH51‐SmMTB111‐SmTTG1
  module synergistically regulates both phenolic and diterpenoid biosynthetic pathways
  in S. miltiorrhiza. JA signalling promotes the biosynthesis of both PAs and TAs
  in S. miltiorrhiza, inducing the transcription of SmbHLH51, SmMYB111 and SmTTG1,
  which facilitates the formation of the SmMBW complex. The SmMBW complex positively
  regulates the accumulation of both PAs and TAs by activating the expression of SmC4H1
  and SmKSL1. SmMYB39 serves as a negative regulator of PAs and TAs biosynthesis and
  its expression is suppressed by JA. SmMYB39 interacts with SmbHLH51, SmMYB111 and
  SmTTG1 to prevent SmMBW formation and inhibits the activation of SmMBW on SmC4H1
  and SmKSL1. Additionally, SmMYB39 interacts with SmJAZ1, which is induced by JA.
  It is speculated that the interaction between SmMYB39 and SmJAZ1 prevents the interaction
  between SmMYB39 and SmMBW. However, this mechanism requires further investigation.
  In summary, the SmMYB39‐SmMBW module mediating JA signalling and synergistically
  regulating the biosynthesis of two types of secondary metabolites in S. miltiorrhiza
  is constructed
