caption: De novo lipogenesis pathway. De novo lipogenesis (DNL) integrates glucose,
  acetate, and glutamine metabolism. Glucose-derived pyruvate generates mitochondrial
  acetyl-CoA and citrate, which is exported to the cytosol and cleaved by ACLY to
  produce acetyl-CoA. ACC converts acetyl-CoA to malonyl-CoA, the first committed
  intermediate, and FASN catalyzes the synthesis of palmitate (C16:0). Palmitate is
  desaturated by SCD1 to yield monounsaturated FAs for membrane remodeling and lipid
  storage. Acetate contributes via ACSS2-derived acetyl-CoA, while under hypoxia or
  impaired citrate export, glutamine-derived α-ketoglutarate fuels lipogenesis through
  IDH1/2-mediated reductive carboxylation
