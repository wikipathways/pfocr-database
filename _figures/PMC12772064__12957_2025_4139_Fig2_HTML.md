caption: AKT/AMPK signaling pathway mediates the nuclear-cytoplasmic shuttling of
  FOXO1 by regulating its phosphorylation. (a) PI3K phosphorylates and activates AKT.
  Subsequently, after AKT induces FOXO1 phosphorylation, cytoplasmic 14-3−3 proteins
  bind to the phosphorylation sites of FOXO1, forming a FOXO1-14-3−3 complex. This
  complex then translocates from the nucleus to the cytoplasm, where AKT-mediated
  phosphorylation inhibits the activity of FOXO1 in the nucleus. Abbreviations：PI3K,
  phosphoinositide 3-kinases; AKT, protein Kinase B (b) AMPK phosphorylates Ser22
  of FOXO1 by directly inhibiting the binding of 14-3−3 proteins or by phosphorylating
  the TAD domain of FOXO1 at Thr649, thereby enhancing its nuclear localisation and
  subsequent transcriptional activity. Activation of PP2A can dephosphorylate AMPK-phosphorylated
  FOXO1 to promote its nuclear translocation. Abbreviations：AMPK, AMP-activated protein
  kinase; PP2A, protein phosphatase 2A
