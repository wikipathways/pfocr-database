caption: 'The top panel shows the ren biosynthetic gene cluster (BGC) of the endosymbiont
  of Haliclona sp., as reported by Tianero et al.13 Genes are color-coded according
  to our revised functional annotation: dark blue, revised substrate preparation;
  brown, NRPS core biosynthetic genes; green, revised tailoring enzymes; light blue,
  revised C2-generating enzymes; pink, revised PKS-related genes. The middle panel
  depicts the domain organization of the encoded NRPS (in blue and grey) and PKS (in
  pink) enzymes according to our re-annotation. Domains are indicated by colored circles:
  A adenylation, C condensation, PCP peptidyl carrier protein, PS Pictet–Spengler,
  TE thioesterase, FAAL fatty acyl-AMP ligase, ACP acyl carrier protein, AT acyltransferase.
  The bottom panel illustrates the proposed mixed NRPS-PKS biosynthetic pathway, highlighting
  the sequential incorporation of modified tyrosine units, formation of the bis-THIQ
  scaffold through dual Pictet–Spengler condensations, and subsequent late-stage tailoring
  reactions. Acetylation mediated by the renK-encoded AT–ACP modules leads to the
  formation of renieramycin E (3), whereas hydrolytic cleavage of the angelate side
  chain gives rise to the novel hexacyclic compound jorumycidine (4). Key homologous
  genes in the biosynthesis of quinocarcin (qcn) and saframycins (sfmc) are reported
  in green'
