caption: The tumor cells of EBV-positive NPC secrete large amounts of chemokine CCL5,
  which recruits mononuclear macrophages. These cells then secrete interleukin 10
  (IL-10) and colony stimulating factor 1 (CSF1) to induce the polarization of tumor-infiltrating
  macrophages into tumor-promoting M2-TAMs, thereby inhibiting CD8 + T cells. Additionally,
  the EBV-encoded cleavage gene ZTA can attract more monocytes into the tumor microenvironment
  by upregulating the expression of chemokine ligand 1 (CXCL1), granulocyte-macrophage
  colony-stimulating factor (GM-CSF), and interleukin-8 (IL-8). EBV also remodels
  and polarizes tumor-associated macrophages (TAMs) through its encoded proteins and
  non-coding RNAs. Regarding the regulation of EBV on M2-type TAMs, the EBV cleavage
  protein BRRF2 is packaged into extracellular vesicles (EVs), and BRRF2 activates
  the cyclic GMP-AMP synthase-interferon gene stimulator signaling pathway (cGAS-STING),
  interfering with the interaction between cyclic GMP-AMP synthase (cGAS) and dsDNA,
  and regulating the expression of P53-dependent apoptotic regulator (TP73) in in
  vitro co-culture experiments with EBV-infected NPC cells. It was observed that the
  proportion of M1-type TAMs decreased, while the proportion of M2-type TAMs significantly
  increased. EBV upregulates the expression of the secreted phosphoprotein 1 (SPP1)
  gene, activating the cell adhesion molecule 44/Janus kinase 2/signal transducer
  and activator of transcription 3 (CD44/JAK2/STAT3) signaling axis, greatly promoting
  the development of M2 + TAMs, participating in angiogenesis and promoting tumor
  cells
