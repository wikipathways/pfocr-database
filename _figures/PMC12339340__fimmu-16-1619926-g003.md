caption: In effector T cells, glucose and lipid metabolic pathways converge to support
  cellular energy and lipid biosynthesis. TCR signaling activate multiple metabolic
  pathways. Glucose is metabolized to pyruvate, which is either exported via monocarboxylate
  transporter 1 (MCT1) or enters the mitochondrial tricarboxylic acid (TCA) cycle.
  Glutaminolysis contributes α-ketoglutarate (αKG) to the TCA cycle, ultimately supporting
  the generation of citrate. Cytosolic citrate is converted into acetyl-CoA (Ac-CoA),
  serving as a precursor for fatty acid synthesis. Along with glycerol, imported via
  aquaporin 9 (AQP9), Ac-CoA supports triacylglycerol (TAG) biosynthesis. Additionally,
  the ketone body β-hydroxybutyrate (βOHB) is imported and converted into Ac-CoA,
  which is further utilized in the TCA cycle. In addition, the ketone body βOHB is
  taken up from the extracellular space and metabolized into acetyl-CoA, which then
  enters the mitochondrial TCA cycle
