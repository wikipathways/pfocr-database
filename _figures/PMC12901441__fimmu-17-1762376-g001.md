caption: Formation and molecular features of extracellular vesicles. Exosomes (40–160
  nm) are generated through the endosomal pathway, involving early endosomes, MVBs,
  and secretion through membrane fusion. The microvesicles are formed through direct
  budding from plasma membrane. Both types carry functional molecules, such as DNA,
  mRNA, non-coding RNA, and proteins. Common exosome markers include CD9, TSG101,
  CD81, CD63, ALIX, Hsp60, and Hsp90, which help define their identity and biological
  functions
