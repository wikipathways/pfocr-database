caption: 'Schematic of the S2‐PepAnalyst framework for SSPS identification and family
  classification. (a) Parallel feature extraction: An input protein sequence is processed
  independently by two protein language models (ESM‐2 and TAPE) to generate residue
  embeddings (https://liambai.com/protein‐representation). Each embedding is projected
  into a 2D image‐like representation and analysed by the GeoTop module to extract
  topological features. For each pathway, the original 2D projection is concatenated
  with its corresponding GeoTop features. (b) Adaptive selection and classification:
  A RL Agent dynamically selects the most informative concatenated representation
  from the two pathways (Li and Pearl 2022). This optimal n‐dimensional representation
  is classified by a LeNet‐5 CNN to predict whether the protein is an SSP (visualised
  in the bottom‐left panel). The prediction accuracy generates a classification reward
  to train the RL agent. Simultaneously, for family classification of the identified
  SSP, the GeoTop features are quantified using the Wasserstein distance from a topological
  target. Example SSP families are visualised in the bottom‐right panel, including
  post‐translationally modified peptides (e.g., with proline hydroxylation/glycosylation
  or tyrosine sulphation) and cysteine‐rich peptides with characteristic disulphide
  bridges'
