caption: Reassignment of the cascade enzymes in the 4-HPA degradation pathway in A.
  baumannii TH, which highlights five putative enzymes, including CHMSD, CHMI, OPETD,
  HHDDI, and OHEDH, located in the middle of the pathway. CHMSD can compensate for
  the function of SSADH. The effective decarboxylation activity requires a binary
  complex of OPETD and HHDDI. Without CHMI, the decarboxylation can proceed, however,
  the presence of CHMI can accelerate the decarboxylation process by generating a
  tri-acid isomeric form readily for decarboxylation
