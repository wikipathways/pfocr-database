caption: Hyperglycemia-driven pathways sustaining inflammation in diabetic wounds.
  Excess glucose fuels the polyol pathway (GLUT1/3→AR/ALR2→SORD), consuming NADPH
  and lowering GSH, which amplifies ROS to activate JNK/AP-1 and NF-κB. The hexosamine
  biosynthetic pathway (GFAT→UDP-GlcNAc→OGT) increases protein O-GlcNAcylation, further
  biasing transcription toward inflammation. AGEs activate RAGE, adding to mitochondrial/NOX-linked
  ROS. GPR132 senses low pH/9-HODE and signals via Gα13–RhoA/ROCK to increase cytoskeletal
  tension, with a context-dependent dampening effect on NF-κB consistent with M1→M2
  macrophage reprogramming. Solid arrows denote activation; dashed T-bar denotes context-dependent
  inhibition
