caption: The known pathways through which JAK/STAT acts on AMD. The figure illustrates
  the pathways of the Janus kinase/signal transducers and activators of transcription
  (JAK/STAT) signaling pathway and its inhibitors in retinal pigment epithelium (RPE)
  cells during the pathological process of age-related macular degeneration (AMD).
  The JAK/STAT signaling pathway regulates various effects, including oxidative stress,
  inflammation, and neovascularization, primarily through STAT1 and STAT3, thus exerting
  different effects on the development of AMD. Furthermore, research has demonstrated
  that STAT5 is associated with neovascularization
