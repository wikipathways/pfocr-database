caption: The synthesized TPAQu-Pt@HA NPs enhance chemo-immunotherapy against ovarian
  cancer through multiple integrated mechanisms. First, they induce nuclear and mitochondrial
  DNA damage, leading to cytoplasmic dsDNA leakage and effective activation of the
  cGAS–STING pathway. Second, the aggregation-induced emission (AIE) effect enhances
  reactive oxygen species (ROS) generation, causing mitochondrial dysfunction and
  promoting gasdermin E (GSDME)-N terminal pore formation, which ultimately triggers
  pyroptosis. Third, photothermal therapy (PTT) further aggravates mitochondrial damage
  and intensifies pyroptosis, thereby amplifying cGAS–STING pathway activation and
  boosting the downstream anti-tumor immune response
