caption: Pleural Mesothelioma (PM) dysregulated pathways modulated by non-coding RNAs
  (ncRNAs). ncRNAs modulate the Wnt/β-catenin, Wnt/Fzd, and PI3K/AKT/mTOR signaling
  pathways. In the Wnt/β-catenin pathway, Wnt5A and Wnt16 are targeted by the miR-200
  family, miR-141, and miR-429; Wnt3A and Wnt5B by miR-205; and Wnt4 and WIF1 by miR-200b/c,
  miR-429, and miR-203. The lncRNA SGMS1 also interacts with miR-200c. In the Wnt/Fzd
  pathway, tumor-suppressive miRNAs (e.g., miR-126, miR-200 family, miR-34 family,
  and miR-217) are downregulated, while oncomiRs miR-944 and miR-135b are upregulated,
  targeting WIF1 and APC, respectively. In the PI3K/AKT/mTOR pathway, miR-206 suppresses
  signaling by targeting PTEN, while miR-1294 inhibits the pathway under regulation
  by circRNA PLK1. Designed with biorender.com
