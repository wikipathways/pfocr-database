caption: In the endogenous Notch pathway, the wild-type Notch receptor binds to its
  ligand, Delta, on an adjacent cell. This binding induces proteolytic cleavage of
  the intracellular domain (NICD) of the Notch receptor, which is then released. To
  design a synthetic signaling circuit in which input sensing and cellular response
  are modifiable, Morsut et al. (2016) replaced the recognition domain of the Notch
  receptor and the intracellular transcription domain to customize genes targeted
  by the pathway. The synNotch receptors, consisting of the engineered recognition
  and effector domains, only retain the transmembrane core domain of the natural Notch
  receptor
