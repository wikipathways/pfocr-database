caption: 'This figure illustrates the two activation pathways of MAIT cells and the
  functions of their two main subtypes at present. First, regarding activation, two
  activation pathways are presented here: the ligand pathway and the cytokine pathway.
  In the ligand pathway, APCs produce MR1, and intestinal microorganisms generate
  5-OP-RU. The combination of the two activates MAIT cells. In the cytokine pathway,
  APCs, stimulated by viral infection and inflammation, produce IL-12 and IL-18 to
  activate MAIT cells. In the middle, the surface proteins (TCR Vα7.2, Jα33, CD3,
  CD161), chemokine receptors (CCR5, CCR6, CCR9, CXCR6), activation-related surface
  proteins (IL-12R, IL-18R, CD28, CD69), and core transcription factors (T-bet, RORγt)
  of MAIT cells are presented. The MAIT1 phenotype is mainly mediated by the transcription
  factor T-bet, which exerts antiviral and cytotoxic effects by secreting GzB, TNF-α,
  and IFN-γ. The MAIT17 subtype is mainly mediated by the transcription factor RORγt
  and exerts pro-inflammatory and anti-infective effects by secreting IL-17A'
