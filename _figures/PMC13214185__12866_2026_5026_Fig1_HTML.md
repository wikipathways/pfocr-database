caption: Scheme of PG recycling pathway in E. coli, P. aeruginosa, and S. maltophilia.
  The PG recycling model of S. maltophilia is mimically established based on known
  PG recycling models of E. coli and P. aeruginosa. The periplasmic muropeptides generated
  by lytic transglycosylase, amidase, and LMW PBPs are transported into cytosol via
  inner membrane transporters (such as AmpG and Opp) and reutilized in the process
  of PG recycling. Most of the members involved in PG recycling are conserved within
  E. coli, P. aeruginosa, and S. maltophilia. The unique features are the MurQ and
  NagK-mediated routes in E. coli and MupP-AmgK-MurU-mediated route in P. aeruginosa.
  S. maltophilia harbors the orthologs of NagK, MupP, AmgK, and MurU; thus, PG recycling
  route of S. maltophilia seems to be a hybrid of those of E. coli and P. aeruginosa
