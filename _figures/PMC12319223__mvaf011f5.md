caption: Drosophila Toll is a stress sensor. Drosophila Toll is activated during infections.
  Fungal infections cleave pro-Spätzle into its active form. The resultant Spätzle-Toll
  interaction induces antimicrobial peptide production, underscoring the role of Toll
  as a stress sensor. Meanwhile, PGRPs function as dedicated pathogen sensors by binding
  bacterial peptidoglycans and activating either the Toll or IMD pathway. Thus, Drosophila
  employs both pathogen- and stress-sensing mechanisms to combat infections
