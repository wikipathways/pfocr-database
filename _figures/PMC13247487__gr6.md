caption: Schematic diagram of the mitochondrial PRKN-dependent pathway. (a) The key
  proteins in this pathway are PINK1, a serine/threonine kinase located on the depolarized
  outer mitochondrial membrane, and parkin, an E3 ubiquitin ligase. (b) When an abnormal
  change in the mitochondrial membrane potential occurs, PINK1 accumulates on the
  outer mitochondrial membrane, leading to the phosphorylation and activation of parkin,
  which results in the attachment of ubiquitin (Ub) to the mitochondrial substrate,
  (c) which is subsequently encapsulated by the lysosome and ultimately degraded by
  the mitochondria
