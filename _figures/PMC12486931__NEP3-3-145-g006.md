caption: 'Beta‐cell‐centric construct: Egregious eleven. Targeted therapies address
  individual pathways that mediate hyperglycemia. (A) Eleven pathways currently known
  to mediate hyperglycemia are known, called the egregious 11. Many of these contribute
  to beta cell dysfunction (liver, muscle, adipose tissue), brain, colon/biome, and
  immune dysregulation/inflammation, and others result from beta cell dysfunction
  through downstream effects (reduced insulin, decreased incretin effect, a‐cell defect,
  stomach/small intestine via reduced amylin, and kidney). (B) Targeted therapies
  for each of the current pathways mediating hyperglycemia, weight reduction, and
  cardiovascular benefits based on the beta‐cell‐centric model. Adapted from Schwartz
  et al.57 Reuse permission granted. GLP‐1, glucagon‐like peptide‐1; SGLT, sodium‐glucose
  cotransporter; TZDs, thiazolidinediones'
