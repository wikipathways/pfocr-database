caption: The schematic shows pathways of adenine production and recycling in four
  proximal tubule cell subtypes that were identified from the kidneys of patients
  with diabetic kidney disease using single-cell RNA sequencing18. The top 1,000 marker
  genes for each proximal tubule subtype (with a maximum adjusted P value of 0.05)
  were mapped to the indicated enzymes involved in the polyamine metabolism pathway.
  Enzymes are depicted in ovals and metabolites in rectangles. The colour coding indicates
  the proximal tubule cell subtypes that are enriched for mRNA encoding the particular
  enzyme. Increases in the levels of these enzymes can lead to increased levels of
  adenine in these proximal tubule cell subtypes. As excess adenine is secreted in
  urine, urinary adenine is a potential biomarker of kidney disease. PT, proximal
  tubule; S1–S3, segments 1–3; dPT, degenerative proximal tubule; aPT, adaptive proximal
  tubule
