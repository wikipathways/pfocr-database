caption: Mechanistic overview of SHIP1 in regulating lipid metabolism and lysosomal
  function in AD. Extracellular components, including APOE4, Aβ, and necrotic cell
  debris, activate the TREM2/DAP12 and ITGB8 signaling pathways. SHIP1 acts as a key
  negative regulator of the PI3K pathway by dephosphorylating PIP3 to PIP2. Loss of
  SHIP1 or the use of SHIP1 inhibitors leads to excessive PIP3 accumulation, impairing
  lipid processing capacity and resulting in the accumulation of lipid droplets. Furthermore,
  SHIP1 decline triggers lysosomal dysfunction and the release of Cathepsin B via
  CapZ interaction, which ultimately activates the NLRP3 inflammasome. These combined
  effects drive lipid metabolism disorders and chronic neuroinflammation in Alzheimer’s
  disease. Detailed descriptions of these pathways are provided in Sect.  4.1. (Created
  at https://BioRender.com)
