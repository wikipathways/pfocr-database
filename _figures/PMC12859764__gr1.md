caption: 'Mechanisms by which plasminogen activator inhibitor-1 (PAI-1) contributes
  to treatment resistance and the potential therapeutic effect of TM5614. (A) Radiotherapy:
  Radioresistant cancer cells upregulate PAI-1 expression, which induces epithelial-to-mesenchymal
  transition (EMT) via TGF-β, and activates the AKT and ERK1/2 signaling pathways,
  leading to the inhibition of apoptosis. (B) Chemotherapy: Myofibroblastic cancer-associated
  fibroblasts (CAF) that secrete PAI-1 and TGF-β, attenuating cisplatin efficacy against
  lung cancer cells through TGF-β-mediated EMT. (C) Targeted therapy: PAI-1 activates
  integrin signaling pathways in drug-tolerant persister cells, promoting EMT through
  the integrin but not the TGF-β signaling pathway'
