caption: Schematic of a metabolic-nanoadjuvant that fuels a self-reinforcing pyroptosis–STING
  loop for potent metalloimmunotherapy. The GOCoF₂ nanoadjuvant induces glucose depletion
  and ROS generation, causing metabolic stress and mitochondrial damage in tumor cells.
  This triggers caspase-1/GSDMD-mediated pyroptosis, releasing mtDNA that activates
  the cGAS-STING pathway. Co²⁺ ions amplify STING signaling by enhancing TBK1 phosphorylation,
  while glucose metabolism inhibition reduces TREX2-mediated suppression of cGAS-STING.
  The resulting interferon production and pro-inflammatory cytokine release further
  potentiate pyroptosis, creating a robust immunostimulatory loop that remodels the
  tumor microenvironment
