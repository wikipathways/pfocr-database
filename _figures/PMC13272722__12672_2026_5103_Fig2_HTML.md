caption: 'Role of FAT4 in cancer progression.Across 30 + cancer types, FAT4 acts as
  a pan-cancer gatekeeper: it sustains planar cell polarity and adherens junctions,
  thereby silencing the Hippo–YAP, Wnt/β-catenin and PI3K/AKT hubs that drive EMT,
  proliferation and immune escape. Loss-of-function mutations, promoter methylation
  or miRNA-mediated repression of FAT4 unleash these pathways, accelerate cytoskeletal
  remodeling and foster stem-like, metastatic and immunosuppressive phenotypes, while
  its mutation signature simultaneously marks high tumor mutational burden and predicts
  both prognosis and immunotherapy response'
