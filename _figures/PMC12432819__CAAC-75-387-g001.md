caption: Structural and functional organization of the EGFR protein. EGFR exists as
  an inactive monomer embedded in the cell membrane. Upon ligand binding (e.g., epidermal
  growth factor), the receptor dimerizes, transitioning to an activated state. This
  activation triggers intracellular signaling cascades, such as MAPK, PI3K‐AKT‐MTOR,
  which regulate cellular proliferation, survival, and differentiation. Mutations
  in the kinase domain of EGFR can lead to a constitutive and ligand‐independent activation
  of the protein and downstream pathways. EGFR indicates epidermal growth factor receptor.
