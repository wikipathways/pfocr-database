caption: Dietary fiber maintains bone homeostasis by modulating gut microbiota and
  intestinal barrier function. Dietary fiber undergoes microbial fermentation and
  produce short-chain fatty acids (SCFAs). These SCFAs serve as a source of energy
  and go through various biological processes to modulate pro- and anti-inflammatory
  phenotypes. Short-chain fatty acids act through GPR43/GPR109a signaling to induce
  inflammasome activation, thereby promoting IL-18 secretrion and improving gut integrity.
  Meanwhile SCFAs block NF-κB by inhibiting HDAC, reducing pro-inflammatory cytokine
  production suppressing the activity of macrophages and neutrophils. Short-chain
  fatty acids promote the development of a tolerogenic phenotype in dendritic cells
  by stimulating the production of IL-10 and retinoic acid. IL-10 increases TGF-β
  and suppresses the activity of effector T cells, whereas retinoic acid activates
  the retinoic acid receptor in naïve T cells, facilitating their differentiation
  into regulatory T cells (Tregs). Short-chain fatty acids also regulate the process
  of angiogenesis (new blood vessels formation) through mTOR pathway. Collectively,
  these mechanisms contribute to bone health by reducing systemic inflammation and
  promoting a balanced immune response. HDAC = histone deacetylase; HIF = hypoxia
  inducible factor; PPAR = peroxisome proliferator–actiavted receptor; GPR109a = G-coupled
  protein receptor 109a; GPR43 = G-coupled protein receptor 43; MAPK = mitogen-activated
  protein kinase; TJP = tight junction proteins; ARG1 = arginine 1; ERK = extracellular
  signal-regulated kinase; JNK = Jun N-terminal kinase; NLRP6 = nucleotide-binding
  oligomerization domain, leucine rich repeat and pyrin domain containg 6; DC = dendritic
  cell; mTOR = mammalian target of rapamycine; NF-κB = nuclear factor-kappa B; STAT3
  = signal transducer and activator of transcription 3; G.cells = goblet cells; Ig
  = immunoglobulins; 5HIAA = 5-hydroxyindoleacetic acid; AhR = aryl hydrocarbon receptor;
  AMP = antimicrobial peptides; TGF-β = transforming growth factor- β; FOXP3 = forkhead
  box P3; T-regs = T-regulatory; Ac-CoA = acetyl-CoA; TCA = tricarboxylic acid; HAT
  = histone acetyl transferase, IL = interleukin; AC = acetyl CoA
