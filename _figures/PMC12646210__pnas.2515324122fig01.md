caption: Engineering yeast longevity for enhanced biosynthesis of sclareol. Classic
  metabolic engineering enabled high-level production of sclareol in strain SCX42
  by optimizing the isoprenoid biosynthetic pathway and enhancing the supply of acetyl-CoA
  and the cofactor NADPH, which greatly damaged the healthy state of the cells. In
  this study, we engineered cell longevity to significantly improve sclareol production
  by manipulating four longevity modules, namely, nutrient sensing, mitophagy, protein
  homeostasis, and genome stability. This engineering endeavor provides a feasible
  approach for improving cellular performance, rather than classic metabolic engineering,
  and it provides a sustainable route for Ambrox supply, rather than extraction from
  sperm whales
