caption: Acetate is activated by acyl-CoA synthetase short-chain family isoform 2
  in the cytosol/nucleus or isoform 1 in the mitochondria. Cytosolic acetyl-CoA is
  carboxylated by acetyl-CoA carboxylase to form malonyl-CoA, which fuels fatty acid
  synthase while simultaneously inhibiting CPT1, thereby blocking β-oxidation. Lipogenesis
  is maintained through citrate export from mitochondria (via ATP-citrate lyase),
  reductive carboxylation, or acetate production from pyruvate. AACS Acetoacetyl-CoA
  synthetase, ACAT1 Acetyl-CoA acetyltransferase 1, ACAT2 Acetyl-CoA acetyltransferase
  2, ACSS1 acyl-CoA synthetase short-chain family isoform 1, ACSS2 acyl-CoA synthetase
  short-chain family isoform 2, CS citrate synthase, CPT1 carnitine palmitoyltransferase
  1, CPT2 carnitine palmitoyltransferase 2, FAS fatty acyl synthase, KADHAses keto-acid
  dehydrogenases, mal malate, ME1 malic enzyme, MCD Malonyl-CoA decarboxylase, PC
  pyruvate carboxylase, PPP pentose phosphate pathway, pyr pyruvate, Prx peroxisome.
