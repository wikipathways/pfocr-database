  pathway, and SUMOylation of transcription factor Etv1 acts as a switch between stem
  and nonstem cancer cell states in mammary tumors. Moreover, SUMOylation‐dependent
  mechanisms in cancer cells also contribute to the establishment of a tumor‐permissive
  microenvironment
