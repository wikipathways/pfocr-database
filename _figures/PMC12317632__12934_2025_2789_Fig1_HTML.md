caption: Expression of engineered neoxanthin biosynthetic pathway in S. cerevisiae.
  Carotenoid precursor FPP is generated by the endogenous mevalonate pathway, which
  is potentiated by the overexpression of tHMG1. The β-carotene pathway incorporates
  the heterologous genes CrtE, CrtYB and CrtI to synthesize β-carotene from FPP. The
  introduction of the β-xanthophyll pathway leads to the conversion of β-carotene
  into neoxanthin. The sequential hydroxylation and epoxidation in both rings of β-carotene
  by CrtZ and ZEP yield zeaxanthin and violaxanthin, respectively. The tautomerization
  of violaxanthin catalyzed by VDL1 yield neoxanthin. FPP farnesyl diphosphate, GGPP
  geranylgeranyl diphosphate, CrtE geranylgeranyl diphosphate synthase, CrtYB bifunctional
  lycopene cyclase/phytoene synthase, tHMG-CoA HMG-CoA reductase truncate version,
  VDL1 violaxanthin de-epoxidase like 1, ZEP zeaxanthin epoxidase, CrtZ β-carotene
  hydroxylase, CrtI phytoene desaturase
