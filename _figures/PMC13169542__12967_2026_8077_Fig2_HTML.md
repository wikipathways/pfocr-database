caption: 'Overview of MET downstream signalling pathways. Binding of HGF to MET induces
  receptor dimerization and autophosphorylation at Tyr1234/1235 (kinase domain) and
  Tyr1349/1356 (C-terminal multifunctional docking site), recruiting key adaptors
  and effectors such as GRB2, GAB1, PI3K, SHP2, and STAT3. This activates multiple
  canonical pathways: (1) MAPK cascades (GRB2–SOS→Ras→Raf→MEK→ERK; Ras–PI3K→Rac→MEKK→JNK/p38),
  regulating proliferation, differentiation, and stress responses; (2) PI3K–Akt axis,
  promoting survival, inhibiting apoptosis, and activating mTOR; (3) STAT3 pathway,
  driving transcription of genes linked to cell cycle progression and invasion; (4)
  IκBα–NF-κB pathway, in which PI3K/Akt and Src activate IKK, relieve NF-κB inhibition,
  and induce pro-survival/pro-inflammatory gene expression. MET signalling is further
  amplified via co-receptors such as α6β4 integrin and CD44v6, linking to cytoskeletal
  rearrangement, and sustained in early endosomes to support migration. Collectively,
  this network coordinates proliferation, survival, migration, EMT, and brain metastasis
  formation in NSCLC'
