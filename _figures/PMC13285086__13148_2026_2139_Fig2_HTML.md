caption: 'In hepatoma cells, heightened Warburg effect floods lactate and fuels targeted-drug
  resistance via three routes. Pathway 1: Enhanced Warburg effect generates lactate,
  which lactylates IGF2BP3. Lactylated IGF2BP3 stabilizes the m6A-modified mRNAs of
  PCK2 and NRF2, boosting serine metabolism and antioxidant defense. Increased serine
  metabolism promotes SAM synthesis, which in turn enhances m⁶A methylation on PCK2/NRF2
  mRNAs. This forms a self-reinforcing lactyl-IGF2BP3 → PCK2/NRF2 → SAM → m6A positive
  feedback loop that sustains the antioxidant capacity and drives lenvatinib resistance.
  Pathway 2: ZNF207 lactylates PRDX1 at K67, enhancing its activity and driving NRF2
  nuclear translocation. NRF2 activates antioxidant gene expression, neutralizing
  RGF-induced ferroptosis and conferring rivoceranib resistance. Pathway 3:Lactate-induced
  H3K18la upregulates HECTD2, which ubiquitinates and degrades KEAP1 to release NRF2.
  NRF2 then activates an antioxidant transcriptional program that clears lenvatinib-induced
  ROS, promoting oxidative stress evasion and lenvatinib resistance'
