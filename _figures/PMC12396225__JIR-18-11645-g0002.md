caption: In lung ischemia-reperfusion injury, MSC-EVs exert protective effects on
  lung tissue. BM-MSC-EVs suppress inflammatory responses by releasing miR-38, miR-335,
  TNF-α, IL-1β, and miR-21-5p. AD-MSCs-EVs inhibit inflammatory responses by releasing
  miR-206 to suppress the NF-κB pathway. hUC-MSCs-EVs reduce inflammation by inhibiting
  IL-17, TNF-α, CXCL1, HMGB1, and TNF-α. BM-MSCs-EVs inhibit pyroptosis by releasing
  miR-202-5p to regulate CMPK2. BM-MSC-EVs suppress apoptosis by releasing miR-335,
  miR-21-5p, and LncRNA-ZFAS1. AD-MSCs-EVs inhibit apoptosis by releasing miR-206
  to suppress CXCL1
