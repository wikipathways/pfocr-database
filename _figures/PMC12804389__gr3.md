caption: 'Activation of the ATF6 pathway in the unfolded protein response (UPR). When
  unfolded or misfolded proteins accumulates within the ER lumen, ATF6, a transmembrane
  protein, is activated and translocated to the Golgi apparatus. There, sequential
  cleavage by site-1 protease (S1P) and site-2 protease (S2P) releases the active
  cytosolic domain, ATF6 (P50). This active transcription factor translocates to the
  nucleus and regulates the expression of secondary transcription factors (TFs). These
  secondary TFs can be grouped based on their primary roles: (i) neurodevelopment
  (SP4, RFX4, LM03, FOXP2, POU3F1); (ii) cell differentiation (NF1A, MEF2D, ETV1);
  (iii) gonadogenesis (DMRT1); (iv) hematopoiesis (ZNF410, TAL1, TFDP2); and (v) immune
  system (MEF2D)'
