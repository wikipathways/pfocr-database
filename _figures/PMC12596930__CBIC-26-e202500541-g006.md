caption: 'The sequential positions (numbered 1–18) constructed for NH3 in the egress
  pathway. The pictures are simplified in order to maintain the clarity of the multiple
  positions for NH3 and its hydrogen bonds (black and white). H atoms on the water
  molecules and their hydrogen bonds are not shown, for clarity. The thin lines to
  β‐CH3 of Ala65A and γ‐CH2 of Gln191A signify steric boundaries for NH3 at positions
  3 and 4. The side chain CH2OH function of Ser115B is disordered in PDB 3U7Q, and
  both configurations are shown (beachball and filled): only one forms a good OH →
  NH3 hydrogen bond at position 14. The lines from side chain CH3 of Thr58A are contacts
  of ca 2.7 Å to N in positions 12, 13, 14, and 15 and are possible C–H → NH3 hydrogen
  bonds'
