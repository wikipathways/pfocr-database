caption: 'Biological mechanisms underlying successful ICI rechallenge in HCC. (1)
  Mechanism of targeted therapy combined with anti-PD-L1: induction of ICD – lenvatinib
  induces ICD in HCC cells, releasing DAMPs and a greater variety of tumor antigens.
  This enhances antigen presentation by APCs to T cells, boosting T-cell immunity
  against HCC cells. Remodeling of TME: lenvatinib, apatinib, or regorafenib remodels
  the abnormal tumor vasculature into a structure resembling non-tumor tissue. This
  vascular normalization increases immune cell infiltration, facilitating the ICI-mediated
  antitumor immunity. Upregulation of PD-L1 expression: sorafenib induces a hypoxic
  condition in HCC, activating the HIF-1α pathway in HCC cells, which upregulates
  PD-L1 expression, providing targets for anti-PD-L1 therapy. Lenvatinib also upregulates
  PD-L1 expression through the MCT1/NF-κB/COX-2 pathway in HCC cells. Cisplatin promotes
  PD-L1 expression by activating the HGF/c-Met pathway in HCC cells. (2) Switching
  treatment between anti-PD-1 and anti-PD-L1: anti-PD-L1 treatment upregulates the
  expression of PD-L2, leading to resistance to anti-PD-L1 therapy. Switching to anti-PD-1
  blocks the interaction between PD-1 on T cells and PD-L2 on tumor cells and APCs,
  reactivating T cells in certain tumors. Similarly, anti-PD-1 can upregulate the
  expression of B7-1 on T cells, leading to resistance to anti-PD-1 therapy. Switching
  to anti-PD-L1 blocks the interaction between B7-1 on T cells and PD-L1 on tumor
  cells or APCs, relieving T-cell inhibition. (3) Dual immunotherapy mechanism: the
  expression of CTLA-4 on T cells may be upregulated in tumors resistant to PD-1/PD-L1
  inhibitors. Anti-CTLA-4 treatment blocks the interaction between CTLA-4 on CD8+
  T cells and B7 molecules on APCs, enhancing tumor-reactive CD8+ T-cell immunity.
  Anti-CTLA-4 also blocks the interaction between CTLA-4 on Tregs and B7 molecules
  on APCs, reducing the inhibitory activity of Tregs. Additionally, anti-CTLA-4 treatment
  ameliorates TME immunosuppression by eliminating CTLA-4-positive Tregs via ADCC.
  ADCC, antibody-dependent cell-mediated cytotoxicity; APCs, antigen-presenting cells;
  c-Met, cellular-mesenchymal epithelial transition factor; COX-2, cyclooxygenase-2;
  CTLA-4, cytotoxic T lymphocyte-associated protein 4; DAMPs, damage-associated molecular
  patterns; HCC, hepatocellular carcinoma; HIF-1α, hypoxia inducible factor 1 subunit
  alpha; HGF, hepatocyte growth factor; ICD, immunogenic cell death; ICI, immune checkpoint
  inhibitor; MCT1, monocarboxylate transporter 1; NF-κB, nuclear factor kappa B; PD-1,
  programmed cell death 1; PD-L1, programmed cell death-ligand 1; PD-L2, programmed
  cell death-ligand 2; TME, tumor microenvironment; Tregs, regulatory T cells'
