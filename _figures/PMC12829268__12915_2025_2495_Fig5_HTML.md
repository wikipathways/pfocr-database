  a Summary diagram of HIF-1 signaling pathway. HIF, a transcription factor coordinating
  the cellular response to hypoxia, can be activated by the MAPK and PI3K-Akt signaling
  pathways. HIF regulates target genes expression to increase oxygen delivery and
  reduce oxygen consumption. b Diagram categorizing rapidly evolving, accelerated
  conserved noncoding element related, and highly expressed genes in the deep-diving
  group according to their functions, with rapidly evolving genes highlighted in green
  background, accelerated conserved noncoding element-related genes in orange background,
  and upregulated genes in red background. G1P, glucose 1-phosphate; G6P, glucose
  6-phosphate; LDL, low-density lipoprotein. c Different tissue responses to hypoxia.
