caption: 'Potential mechanisms of GLP-1RAs regarding the asthma and clinical outcomes.
  GLP-1RAs exert their effects primarily by improving airway inflammation and mucus
  secretion. GLP-1R activation inhibits the release of epithelial-derived cytokines
  (TSLP, IL-33) induced by allergens such as Alternaria extract, leading to reduced
  production of type 2 cytokines (IL-4, IL-5, IL-13) and decreased eosinophil infiltration.
  This signaling pathway also downregulates cytokine expression (IL-4, IL-8, IL-13)
  in LPS-stimulated inflammatory environments. PBS, phosphate buffered saline. Orange
