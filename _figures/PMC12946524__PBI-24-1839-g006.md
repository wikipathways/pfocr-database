caption: 'Mapping of (putative) Camelina sativa ref1 marker metabolites onto the phenylpropanoid
  pathway reveals contrasting REF1 activity in seeds and leaves. The scheme is based
  on phenylpropanoid biosynthesis as described for most Brassicaceae and features
  Camelina‐specific adaptations: Chlorogenic acid, the major product accumulating
  in Camelina leaves, is highlighted in green; sinapine, the major product accumulating
  in seeds, is highlighted in light brown. In contrast to Arabidopsis thaliana and
  Brassica napus, Camelina does not accumulate sinapoyl malate in seeds, leaves or
  germinating seedlings (as indicated by x). REF1 is a central enzyme in sinapine
  biosynthesis and oxidises coniferyl aldehyde to ferulate as well as sinapaldehyde
  to sinapate (in bold). CRISPR/Cas9‐mediated inactivation of all three REF1 homoeologues
  resulted in accumulation and depletion of marker metabolites. Marker metabolites
  were tentatively mapped within the pathway based on chemical identity and mutant‐based
  intensity pattern. Red and blue lettering and arrows indicate metabolite markers
  showing enrichment (red) or depletion (blue) in ref1 triple kos as compared to WTs.
  UNCs (unknown compounds) are boxed and placed in upper and lower left corners as
  inferred from their accumulation/depletion phenotype. Abbreviations of marker IDs
  indicate origin (tissue) and accumulation phenotype: MLD, marker leaf downregulated;
  MLU, marker leaf upregulated; MSD, marker seed downregulated; MSU, marker seed upregulated;
  UNCs, unknown compounds. (†) Proposed COMT mediated o‐methylation of caffeate to
  ferulate and 5‐OH ferulate to sinapate may account for residual sinapine levels
  in ref1 ko mutants. (‡) Postulated CCoAOMT mediated o‐methylation of 5‐OH feruloyl
  CoA to sinapoyl CoA may account for residual sinapine levels in Arabidopsis comt1
  mutant (Do et al. 2007). Inset depicts the structure of chlorogenic acid (3‐O‐caffeoyl
  quinate), for which several isomers and biosynthetic routes have been described
  (Alcázar Magaña et al. 2021). 4CL, 4‐coumarate:CoA ligase, CSE, caffeoyl shikimate
  esterase; CAD, cinnamoyl alcohol dehydrogenase; CCoaOMT; CCR, cinnamoyl‐CoA reductase;
  COMT, caffeic acid/5‐hydroxy ferulic acid:o‐methyltranserase; F5H, ferulic acid
  5‐hydroxylase; HCT, hydroxycinnamoyl‐CoA:shikimic acid/quinic acid hydroxycinnamoyl
  transferase; HQT, hydroxycinnamoyl‐CoA:quinic acid hydroxycinnamoyl transferase;
  PAL, L‐phenylalanine ammonia lyase; PTAL, bifunctional L‐phenylalanine/L‐tyrosine
  ammonia lyase; REF1, reduced epidermal fluorescence1; S, syringyl moiety; SAD, sinapyl
  alcohol dehydrogenase; SC, sinapoyl choline; SCE, sinapine esterase; SCT, sinapoyl
  glucose:choline sinapoyl transferase; SGT, UDP‐glucose:sinapate glucosyltransferase;
  SMT, sinapoyl glucose:malate o‐sinapoyl transferase'
