caption: (A) AIE-Mito-TPP, upon light stimulation, induces mitophagy and promotes
  mtDNA release, which activates the cGAS-STING pathway to produce IFN-β and CXCL10,
  thereby activating dendritic cells (DCs) and M2 macrophages and promoting T cell
  activation. Created in https://BioRender.com. (B) Lien, released from ART/Lien-PLGA/CPT/DSSP
  after targeting mitochondria that is aided by glutathione (GSH), blocks the autophagy
  flux via Parkin/PINK1/LC3, leading to damaged mitochondria, increased ROS, decreased
  MMP, and ultimately cell death. Created in https://BioRender.com. (C) Fluorescence
  images of MitoTracker and DCF (for ROS detection) in different treatment groups
