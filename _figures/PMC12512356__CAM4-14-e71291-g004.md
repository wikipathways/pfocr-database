caption: Proposed model for the role of ALDH1L1 in cellular proliferation. ALDH1L1
  restores THF pool, which is central to one‐carbon metabolism. This reaction also
  competes with the de novo purine biosynthesis for the same substrate. In rapidly
  proliferating cells, ALDH1L1 is downregulated allowing high flux of 10‐formyl‐THF
  (fTHF) toward de novo purine biosynthesis, which also restores the THF pool. If
  this purine pathway is moderately active, low expression of ALDH1L1 could be advantageous
  to restore the THF pool more rapidly
