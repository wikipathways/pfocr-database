caption: 'Schematic illustration of age-associated circadian rhythms in liver tissue.
  The figure illustrates the gradual deterioration of the liver’s circadian rhythm
  function with aging, as follows: In a young liver, a robust Egr-1/Per1/BMAL1/Egr-1
  feedback loop maintains normal lipid metabolism and clock gene expression; aged
  livers exhibit reduced Egr-1 expression, disrupted circadian gene oscillations,
  and impaired Egr-1/Cidea interactions, which lead to lipid droplet accumulation.
  During aging, the peak expression time (ZT) of the transcription factor Egr-1 shifts
  earlier with increasing age. This demonstrates how aging disrupts the coordination
  between metabolic and circadian pathways. Blue arrows indicate age-related decreases
  in gene expression amplitude, while red arrows indicate increased lipid droplet
  accumulation'
