caption: Mechanical signaling network in tumors, including mechanosensors, signaling
  pathways and cytoskeletal and nuclear effectors. Mechanical cues are sensed by integrins,
  Piezo1 and TRPV4 channels, and membrane-associated structures such as the glycocalyx,
  junctional complexes, and Plexin-B2. These inputs activate canonical signaling pathways,
  primarily integrin-FAK-Src and Ca2+-dependent cascades, which engage MAPK-ERK and
  PI3K-AKT pathways alongside RhoA-ROCK mediated actomyosin contractility and focal
  adhesion dynamics. Additional pathways, including Wnt/β-catenin and TGF-β/Smad,
  further integrate mechanical signals with transcriptional regulation. Actomyosin
  contractility functions as a central hub that integrates upstream signals and transmits
  forces to the nucleus via the LINC complex, while promoting YAP/TAZ activation.
  Together, these pathways coordinate nuclear responses, including chromatin remodeling
  and transcriptional programs governing proliferation, migration, stemness, and ECM
  remodeling
