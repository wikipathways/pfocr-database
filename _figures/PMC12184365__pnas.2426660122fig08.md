caption: PARP12 provides a checkpoint for apoptosis and necroptosis and IFN responses.
  PARP12 mediated MARylation of RIPK1 restricts its interaction with FADD/caspase-8,
  thereby reducing TNFα-induced apoptosis. Under caspase-deficient condition, PARP12-mediated
  MARylation of RIPK1 and RIPK3 facilitates their interaction and the formation of
  complex IIb, thereby promoting necroptosis induced by TNFα or IFNγ. In the IFN pathway,
  PARP12 negatively regulates the expression of a subset of ISGs involved in the antiviral
  response, such as ZBP1, in a RIPK1-dependent manner
