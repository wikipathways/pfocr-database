  salvage pathway, and blue arrows indicate the de novo pathway. (B) Under conditions
  that stimulate adipogenesis, acetyl-CoA is derived from glucose and BCAAs, generating
  ATP via oxidative phosphorylation (OXPHOS), and performing lipogenesis using citrate
  from the TCA cycle. Under purine and pyrimidine inhibition, flux of pyruvate to
  acetyl-CoA is disrupted and FAO is elevated. Likewise, lipogenesis and OXPHOS are
  suppressed. Nucleotide inhibition results in arrested adipogenesis
