caption: 'Proposed model of cellular crosstalk between macrophages and adipocytes
  in obesity and SDBs. In patients with SDBs, macrophages exhibit significant activation
  of NF-κB, a master transcriptional regulator of inflammation. This activation drives
  the robust production of proinflammatory cytokines, including IL-6, IL-1β, and TNF-α.
  Moreover, the NLRP3 inflammasome pathway is markedly upregulated in SDBs-associated
  macrophages, leading to caspase-1-dependent processing and secretion of these inflammatory
  mediators. The elevated TNF-α levels promote adipose tissue lipolysis and worsen
  insulin resistance through multiple mechanisms. Furthermore, NF-κB activation in
  adipocytes creates a proinflammatory microenvironment by both increasing secretion
  of inflammatory mediators and suppressing production of anti-inflammatory factors.
  This dual effect establishes a vicious cycle of metabolic dysfunction and inflammation.
  Abbreviations: IRS: insulin receptor; NF-κB: nuclear factor x-light-chain-enhancer
  of activated B cells; NLRP3: nucleotide-binding domain (NOD)-like receptor protein
  3; TLR4: Toll-like-receptor 4; TNFα: tumor necrosis factor-alpha'
