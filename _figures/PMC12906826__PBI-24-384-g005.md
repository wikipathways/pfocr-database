caption: Proposed model of SmWRKY33‐mediated tanshinone biosynthesis in Danshen. SmWRKY33
  acts as a key transcription factor in JA‐induced tanshinone biosynthesis. JA triggers
  SmWRKY33 expression and promotes SmJAZ8 degradation via the SCFCOI1‐mediated 26S
  proteasome pathway, thereby releasing SmWRKY33. Once SmJAZ8 is removed, SmWRKY33
  activates the expression of CPS1, KSL1, CYP76AH3, CYP71D373 and 2‐ODD14, leading
  to enhanced tanshinone biosynthesis. JA also induces SmMPK3 expression. SmMPK3 phosphorylates
  SmWRKY33, thereby enhancing its transcriptional activity and DNA binding ability,
  and ultimately positively regulates the biosynthesis of tanshinone
