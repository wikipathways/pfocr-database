caption: KEGG pathway map of the intestinal immune network for IgA production in bath-immunized
  channel catfish. Schematic representation of the Kyoto Encyclopedia of Genes and
  Genomes (KEGG) pathway for the intestinal immune network involved in immunoglobulin
  A (IgA) production, representing a KEGG-defined pathway used here as a proxy for
  mucosal antibody signaling in catfish, likely representing IgM. The pathway highlights
  differentially expressed genes (DEGs) identified in the bath intestine versus control
  intestine comparison (Bath_I vs. Bath_CI) at 7 days post-vaccination (dpv). Red-colored
