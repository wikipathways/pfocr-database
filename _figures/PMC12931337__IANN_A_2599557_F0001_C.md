caption: 'Mechanisms of pyroptosis in the regulation of bone metabolism. The diagram
  illustrates two major pyroptosis pathways: the canonical pathway initiated by NLRP3
  inflammasome activation, and the non-canonical pathway triggered by intracellular
  LPS via caspase-4/5/11. NLRP3 inflammasome activation requires two signals: priming
  upregulates NLRP3 expression via TLR/NF-κB signaling, while triggering stimuli such
  as ATP or crystals induce inflammasome assembly via K+ efflux and mitochondrial
  ROS. Both pathways converge on GSDMD cleavage, leading to membrane pore formation,
  cytolysis, and release of IL-1β and IL-18. In the bone microenvironment, NLRP3-driven
  pyroptosis disrupts homeostasis by impairing osteoblast function, promoting RANKL-dependent
  osteoclastogenesis, and biasing mesenchymal stem cell differentiation toward adipogenesis.
  These effects collectively accelerate bone loss and osteoporotic progression'
