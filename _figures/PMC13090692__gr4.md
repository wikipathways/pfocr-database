caption: Mechanistic basis for BPA-induced endocrine and chemotherapy resistanceBPA
  interferes with the efficacy of standard therapeutic regimens in experimental models.
  (Left) Endocrine resistance to tamoxifen is promoted via the MAPK/ERK/ERRγ axis;
  BPA-induced activation of ERRγ and its coactivators PGC-1αβ bypasses ERα blockade
  to sustain cell proliferation and upregulate hTERT. (Right) Chemoresistance to agents
  like doxorubicin and cisplatin is mediated through the GPR30/PI3K/AKT pathway, which
  upregulates anti-apoptotic proteins Bcl-2 and Bcl-xL. Additionally, BPA has been
  shown to stabilize microtubules, potentially antagonizing the effects of antimitotic
  drugs such as vincristine
