caption: In murine cells, ectopic expression of huNTCP allows HBV attachment and early
  entry, a step shared with HDV, whereas murine NTCP (mNTCP) does not support HBV
  entry. Our study indicates that murine hepatic and non-hepatic cells can support
  HBV cccDNA formation via the intracellular amplification pathway, suggesting that
  nucleocapsid uncoating and nuclear import of rcDNA are not restricted. Instead,
  our results point out a block at a late entry step prior to nucleocapsid uncoating
  that restricts productive HBV infection. Green checkmarks indicate steps that are
  permissive in murine cells, and the red cross marks the restricted step
