caption: 'Schematic of convergent GCN2-ZAK signaling pathway targeted by phenyl-sulfonamide
  kinase inhibitors.A, schematic diagram illustrating that environmental stresses,
  such as UV irradiation, amino acid starvation, or ribosome elongation inhibitors,
  can activate two stress-sensing pathways: the GCN2 eIF2α kinase and ISR to promote
  cellular stress adaptation, and the ZAK kinase-mediated MAPK pathway in the RSR,
  which signals phosphorylation of p38 and JNK to drive cell-cycle arrest and apoptosis,
  respectively. The small-molecule inhibitor GCN2iB, originally developed to inhibit
  GCN2, also directly inhibits ZAK, thereby suppressing both ISR signaling and ZAK-dependent
  p38/JNK activation. This dual inhibition dampens GCN2iB-induced cytotoxic responses.
  B, chemical structures of phenyl-sulfonamide kinase inhibitors. Compounds targeting
  BRAF including Dabrafenib, Encorafenib, and Vemurafenib (60), inhibitors of ZAK,
  such as compound 3g (21) and compound 5d (22), and inhibitors of GCN2, that include
  GCN2iB (11, 12), HC-7366 (13), compound 20 (14), AST-0513 (15), and compound 13
  (16) are depicted. ISR, integrated stress response'
