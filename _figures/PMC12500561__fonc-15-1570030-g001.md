caption: 'Molecular mechanisms of SIRT1 and SIRT2 in CRC pathogenesis. (A) NCAPD2
  inhibits SIRT1, promoting apoptosis and cell cycle arrest. Downregulation of miR-34a
  upregulates SIRT1, which suppresses the NF-κB/B7-H3/TNF-α axis to facilitate immune
  evasion. The lncRNAs SNHG15 and SUN2 interact with SIRT1 to promote CRC proliferation,
  migration, and invasion while inhibiting apoptosis. (B) SIRT2 promotes angiogenesis
  and glucose metabolism via STAT3 and AKT signalling. Downregulation of SIRT2 inhibits
  the NF-κB and MEK/ERK pathways, suppressing proliferation, migration, and chemoresistance.
  Legend: Red upwards arrows: Target upregulation or activation; Red downwards arrows:
  Target downregulation or inhibition; Black arrows: Promotion or activation; Red
  blunt ends: Inhibition'
