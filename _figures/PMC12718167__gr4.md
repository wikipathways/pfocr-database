caption: Molecular targets and intervention points of autophagy inhibitorsSmall-molecule
  inhibitors interfere with autophagy at distinct stages through specific molecular
  targets. Early-stage inhibitors targeting ULK1 complex, PI3K complex, and ATG4B
  block autophagosome formation during the initiation, nucleation, and elongation
  phases. Mid-stage inhibitors targeting V-ATPase prevent autophagosome-lysosome fusion.
  Late-stage inhibitors targeting lysosomal acidification and proteolytic activity
  impair cargo degradation within autolysosomes. These interventions collectively
  represent key regulatory points for pharmacological modulation of the autophagy
  pathway
