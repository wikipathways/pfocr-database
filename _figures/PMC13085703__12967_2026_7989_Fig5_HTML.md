caption: 'This schematic illustrates the central role of Cer accumulation in linking
  lipotoxicity to diabetic pathogenesis. In ​pancreatic β-cells​ (left), elevated
  palmitic acid upregulates CerS6, leading to Cer-induced endoplasmic reticulum stress,
  peroxisome dysfunction, and ultimately β-cell apoptosis. In ​target organs​ (right),
  Cer impairs insulin signaling through multiple pathways: (1) In ​skeletal/cardiac
  muscle​ and ​adipose tissue, Cer activates PP2A, which dephosphorylates/inhibits
  AKT, blocking GLUT4 translocation and glucose uptake. (2) In ​adipose tissue, Cer
  promotes chronic inflammation via NF-κB and inhibits PPARγ, exacerbating lipotoxicity.
  (3) In the ​liver, Cer enhances gluconeogenesis by activating FoxO1 and impairs
  insulin receptor substrate (IRS) signaling. Cer synergizes with other lipid metabolites
  (e.g., DAG, FFA) to amplify oxidative stress, inflammatory responses, and mitochondrial
  dysfunction, establishing a vicious cycle of insulin resistance and metabolic dysregulation
  across tissues'
