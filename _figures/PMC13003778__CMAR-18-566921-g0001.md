caption: Wnt/β-collagen signalling pathway. (A) Free β-catenin attaches to its destruction
  complex (which includes APC, CK1, GSK-3β, and AXIN) in the absence of a WNT ligand.
  Phosphorylation of β-catenin causes ubiquitination, which is followed by proteasomal
  disintegration. (B) When WNT ligand is present, WNT + FZD receptor + receptor LRP
  binding results in LRP phosphorylation, which in turn attracts DVL, a destructive
  complex that stops β-catenin from degrading. β-catenin then accumulates and moves
  to the nucleus, where it interacts with TCF transcription factors to activate the
  transcription of WNT target genes
