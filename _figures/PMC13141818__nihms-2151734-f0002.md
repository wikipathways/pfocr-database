caption: (A) Diagram of the PI3K/AKT pathway. Binding of neurotrophins signal phosphorylation
  and dimerization of Trk receptors. This causes the conversion of membrane-bound
  phosphatidylinositol 3,4-biphosphate (PIP2) into phosphatidylinositol 3,4,5-triphosphate
  (PIP3). Akt binds to PIP3, resulting in Akt activation and release to promote cell
  proliferation and protein synthesis. Mutations in PI3K, PIK3CA, or PIK3R1 cause
  hyperactivation of the pathway by inhibiting PTEN’s ability to block the conversion
  of PIP3 back to PIP2. This results in a feedback loop that can cause tumorigenesis.7(B)
  Diagram of the Ras/MAPK pathway. Binding of neurotrophins causes the phosphorylation
  of Trk receptors. This allows the Shc complex to bind and recruit SOS to convert
  GTP to GDP. GDP then binds to membrane-bound Ras, causing it to dimerize and bind
  Raf. The activated Raf will dimerize, recruiting MEK, resulting in the phosphorylation
  of ERK. Phosphorylated ERK interacts with nuclear membrane-bound Importin 7 to facilitate
  its entry into the nucleus, promoting cell proliferation. Activated ERK causes a
  feedback loop, which results in the dimerization of RAF. These dimers result in
  hyperactivation of the ERK pathways which deregulates cell proliferation and causes
  tumorigenesis.8
