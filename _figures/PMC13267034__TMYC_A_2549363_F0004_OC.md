caption: 'The G protein signalling pathway of Aspergillus nidulans. In A. nidulans,
  vegetative growth, sterigmatocystin (ST) biosynthesis, and sexual/asexual development
  are regulated by two distinct yet interconnected G protein signalling pathways.
  Upon binding developmental signals, the receptor GprD activates its downstream G
  protein pathway, which in turn activates the transcription factor BrlA to repress
  reproductive development. Conversely, when reproductive signals are transduced via
  a separate non-G protein signalling pathway, the RGS protein FlbA terminates G protein
  signalling. This suppression of G protein activity inhibits BrlA expression, thereby
  activating reproductive development. Gα: FadA and GanB; Regulators of G protein
  signalling: FlbA and RgsA; CyaA, adenylate cyclase. The dotted line represents indirect
  regulation, and the solid line represents direct regulation. This figure was created
  with BioGDP.com'
