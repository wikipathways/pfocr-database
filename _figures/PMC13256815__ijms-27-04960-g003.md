caption: Stress-dependent integrative NF-κB signaling network in acute kidney injury.
  Upon stimulation of AKI-associated mediators, such as ischemia-reperfusion injury,
  sepsis, nephrotoxins, and rhabdomyolysis, cellular receptors are activated, leading
  to activation of canonical and non-canonical NF-κB signaling pathways. NF-κB activation
  modulates various chemokines, inflammasome, oxidative stress, autophagy, and apoptotic
  signaling via extensive bilateral crosstalk. Early transient activation of NF-κB
  results in adaptive inflammatory responses, such as autophagy and tissue repair,
  whereas sustained activation of NF-κB leads to persistent inflammation, oxidative
  stress, apoptosis, and fibrosis, leading to progression from AKI to chronic kidney
  disease (CKD)
