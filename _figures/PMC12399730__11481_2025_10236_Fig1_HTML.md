caption: Involvment of LINGO-1 in Aβ accumulation and oligodendrocyte function. LINGO-1
  forms a receptor complex with NgR and p75NTR, which contributes to the progression
  of Alzheimer's disease by promoting the pathological cleavage of amyloid precursor
  protein via the β-secretase amyloidogenic pathway, leading to increased Aβ deposition.
  This complex also activates downstream signaling through the RhoA/ROCK pathway,
  resulting in impaired oligodendrocyte differentiation and myelination. However,
  the use of anti-LINGO-1 antibodies or inhibition of LINGO-1 receptor expression
  has been shown to suppress Aβ plaque formation, promote oligodendrocyte maturation,
  and enhance myelin integrity
