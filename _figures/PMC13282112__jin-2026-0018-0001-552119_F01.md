caption: Neutrophil metabolic pathways. Glucose uptake via GLUT transporters fuels
  glycolysis for ATP generation and provides G6P for NADPH production through the
  PPP, supporting NOX-dependent ROS production. Glycogen cycling maintains intracellular
  glucose availability under nutrient-limiting conditions, with the final step of
  glycogenolysis mediated by ER-embedded G6Pase. Glycolytic intermediates drive the
  synthesis of cytoplasmic UDP-sugars and glycans, supplying substrates for protein
  glycosylation in the ER. Functional mitochondria contribute via the TCA cycle and
  OXPHOS, utilizing pyruvate, glutamine, fatty acids, and propionate-derived intermediates.
  In addition to ATP production, metabolic intermediates support various intracellular
  biosynthetic processes. Figure created with BioRender (BioRender.com)
