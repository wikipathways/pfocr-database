caption: 'a Under physiological conditions, peptides self-assemble into nanoparticles,
  which subsequently interact with receptors on the cell membrane through receptor-ligand
  recognition, inducing the transformation of the nanoparticles into nanofibers. Upon
  enzymatic cleavage by bacteria-secreted enzymes, these nanofibers revert to nanoparticles,
  facilitating cellular uptake. b After cellular uptake of the peptide nanoparticles
  into the cells, they specifically target intracellular MRSA and induce bacterial
  cell death via a physical membrane disruption mechanism. Additionally, they further
  regulate the ferroptosis pathway in macrophages to help the cells resist damage
  and oxidative stress. SplB: serine protease-like B protease; ROS: reactive oxygen
  species; LTA: lipoteichoic acid; PGN: peptidoglycan; CM: cytoplasmic membrane'
