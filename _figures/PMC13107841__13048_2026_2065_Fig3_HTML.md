caption: Hh mediated multidrug resistance signaling pathways. A GLI regulates the
  expression of Hh target genes, which is crucial for the maintenance of CSCs and
  chemoresistance. B GLI1 regulates HGF to down-regulate miR-199a-3p and enhance tumor
  resistance through the c-Met/PI3K/AKT signaling axis. In addition, HGF induced HSP27
  phosphorylation through the p38-MAPK pathway to enhance tumor drug resistance. C
  GLI1 suppresses TUBB3 expression by directly binding to the GLI-binding site in
  the TUBB3 promoter region, leading to altered microtubule stability and reduced
  cellular sensitivity to microtubule-stabilizing agents. D GLI1 upregulates FANCD2
  expression by directly binding to the GLI-binding site "GACCACCC" in the FANCD2
  promoter region. The upregulated FANCD2 promotes tumor drug resistance. On the other
  hand, FANCD2 promotes tumor resistance to chemotherapeutic agents targeting DNA
  repair mechanisms by promoting HRR and inhibiting DSBs. E Rab23 can promote the
  expression of Shh and facilitate the nuclear localization of GLI1. As a transcription
  factor, GLI1 can increase the expression of ABCG2, enabling the tumor to pump out
  therapeutic drugs and reducing the accumulation of drugs within the cells. F GLI1
  enhances the stemness of tumor cells by activating the expression of NANOG, while
  NANOG regulates the efflux of drugs by influencing the ABCB1/MDR-1 gene. G The elevated
  level of PGE2 in the tumor microenvironment will activate the integrin β1 on the
  cell surface, and through the activation of GLI1, it will regulate BMI-1, ultimately
  enhancing the stemness and drug resistance of OC cells
