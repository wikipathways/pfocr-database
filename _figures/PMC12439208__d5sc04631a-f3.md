caption: The workflow of DeepMetab. The input module begins by converting molecules
  into molecular graphs or graph structures seamlessly integrating multi-scale descriptors
  into the atomic feature representations. From there, the substrate module analyzes
  graph representations to predict which CYP450 isoforms will participate in metabolism.
  The SOM module then builds upon this analysis to precisely identify specific sites
  of metabolism within the molecule. Once these sites are determined, the rules module
  applies established reaction mechanisms to generate potential metabolites. The process
  culminates in the output module, which presents a comprehensive integration of the
  results, clearly displaying the predicted substrates, metabolic pathways, and resulting
  metabolites in a cohesive manner
