caption: The schematic diagram demonstrates the reciprocal feedback between Schwann
  cells and HNSCC cells. In HNSCC, the expression of LIS1 enhances the synthesis and
  metabolism of serine. Within the tumor microenvironment, the release of serine interacts
  with NMDAR on Schwann cells, which subsequently regulates the AKT signaling pathway.
  This activation of Schwann cells significantly facilitates tumor neural invasion.
  Our findings underscore the importance of the LIS1/serine/NMDAR/AKT feedback loop
  as a crucial mechanism in regulating PNI. This insight presents the potential for
  identifying new biomarkers and therapeutic targets for HNSCC
