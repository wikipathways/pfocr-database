caption: The designed medicarpin biosynthetic pathway in engineered yeast. (a) The
  designed medicarpin biosynthetic pathway. Glucose is the substrate, and L-Phe, L-Tyr,
  and malonyl-CoA are synthesized via the native glycolytic pathway and shikimate
  pathway of Saccharomyces cerevisiae. The designed medicarpin synthetic pathway enables
  the production of a series of flavonoid and isoflavonoid compounds, including medicarpin.
  G6P, glucose-6-phosphate; Ru5P, ribulose 5-phosphate; PEP, phosphoenolpyruvate;
  E4P, erythrose-4-phosphate; DAHP, 3-deoxy-D-arabino-2-heptulosonic acid 7-phosphate;
  CHA, chorismic acid; PPA, prephenate; PPY, phenylpyruvate; HPP, para-hydroxy-phenylpyruvate;
  L-Phe, L-phenylalanine; L-Tyr, L-tyrosine; CA, cinnamic acid; p-HCA, p-coumaric
  acid; Pyr, pyruvate; AcP, acetyl-phosphate; ARO1, pentafunctional aromatic protein;
  ARO2, chorismate synthase; ARO3, DAHP synthase; ARO4, DAHP synthase; ARO7, chorismate
  mutase; PHA2, prephenate dehydratase; PDH1, L-tyrosine prephenate dehydrogenase;
  PAL, phenylalanine ammonia lyase; TAL, tyrosine ammonia lyase; C4H, cinnamate 4-hydroxylase;
  ATR2, cytochrome P450 reductase; CYB5, cytochrome B5; Xfpk, phosphoketolase; Pta,
  phosphotransacetylase; 4CL, 4-coumaroyl coenzyme A ligase; ACC1, acetyl coenzyme
  A carboxylase 1; CHS, chalcone synthase; CHR, chalcone reductase; CHI, chalcone
