caption: PKM2 promotes tumor cells to secrete cytokines such as MIF and CXCL1, thereby
  mediating the recruitment and aggregation of MDSCs in the tumor microenvironment.
  In addition, PKM2 promoted the production of S100 calcium-binding protein A8/A9
  (S100A8/A9) in tumor cells. These dimeric proteins then bind to Toll-like receptor
  4 (TLR4) expressed on adjacent tumor cells, thereby activating the NF-κB signaling
  pathway in tumor cells. Activation of the NF-κB signaling pathway leads to the upregulation
  of MIF and CXCL1 expression, ultimately forming a positive feedback loop that enhances
  the infiltration of MDSCs into the peritumoral area
