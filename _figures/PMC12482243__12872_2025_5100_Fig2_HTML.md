caption: 'a-b The three-dimensional structures of the wild-type and the p.Glu756Ter
  mutant proteins of NOTCH1 predicted using Swiss Model (https://swissmodel.expasy.org/repository/uniprot/P46531?template=2he0)
  and shown in Pymol. The p.Glu756Ter nonsense variant leads to the loss of 1800 amino
  acids, including part of the purple region (extracellular domain), all of the green
  region (intracellular domain), and the yellow region (transmembrane domain). c Structure
  diagram of the NOTCH1 receptor, with an arrow indicating the location of the novel
  nonsense variant p.Glu756Ter discovered in this study. EGF repeats (36), 36 tandem
  epidermal growth factor-like repeat sequences; LNR, LIN12-NOTCH repeats; HD, heterodimerization
  domain; NRR, negative regulatory region; TMD, transmembrane domain; RAM, RBPjκ association
  module; NLSs, nuclear localization sequences; ANK, seven ankyrin repeats; PEST,
  harbors conserved proline/glutamic acid/serine/threonine-rich motifs; TAD, transactivation
  domain. RAM, ANK, NLSs, and TAD all belong to the intracellular domain (NICD). d Classic
  NOTCH signaling pathway: Newly synthesized Notch receptor is initially cleaved by
  furin-like convertases in the trans-Golgi network (S1 cleavage), and the EGF repeats
  of the extracellular domain (NECD) undergo glycosylation modifications, producing
  heterodimers (NOTCH1 extracellular domain-NOTCH1 transmembrane and intracellular
  domain) that bind to the cell surface through non-covalent interactions. NOTCH receptor
  is activated by binding to ligands expressed by adjacent cells, and ligand endocytosis
  generates mechanical forces to pull NOTCH, causing a conformational change that
  exposes the S2 cleavage site for metalloproteinase cleavage. NECD is internalized
  by ligand-mediated endocytosis and degraded by lysosomes. Subsequently, the remaining
  portion of NOTCH is cleaved by γ-secretase/presenilin at the S3 site. After cleavage,
  NICD is released and translocated to the nucleus of the signal-receiving cell and
  binds to the transcription factor CSL. NICD-CSL binding promotes the translocation
  of corepressor (Co-R) proteins and competes with other proteins for transcription
  coactivator (Co-A) mastermind (MAML). The CSL-NICD-MAML complex recruits other CO-As
  to induce the transcription of target genes'
