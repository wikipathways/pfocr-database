caption: 'Cellular Regulatory Pathways of Ferroptosis, Cuproptosis, and Disulfidptosis
  (A) Ferroptosis Regulatory Pathways: The accumulation of intracellular lipid peroxides
  and the imbalance of redox systems, including GPX4 and SLC7A11, lead to cell membrane
  damage and cell death, triggering ferroptosis. (B) Cuproptosis Regulatory Pathways:
  Copper ions entering the cell bind to sulfurylated tricarboxylic acid cycle proteins,
  leading to instability of Fe-S cluster proteins and promoting toxic stress within
  mitochondria, ultimately resulting in cuproptosis. (C) Disulfidptosis Regulatory
  Pathways: Under conditions of high SLC7A11 expression and glucose starvation, there
  is excessive consumption of NADPH, leading to the abnormal accumulation of disulfides
  such as cystine, inducing disulfide stress and rapid cell death'
