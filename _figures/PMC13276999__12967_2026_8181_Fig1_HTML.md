caption: Canonical tumor-suppressive function of the wild-type PP2A holoenzyme. The
  PP2A holoenzyme, composed of the scaffold Aα subunit (encoded by PPP2R1A), catalytic
  C subunit, and regulatory B subunit, dephosphorylates multiple oncogenic kinases.
  PP2A removes the activating phosphate group from AKT at Ser-473, thereby inhibiting
  the PI3K/AKT/mTOR pathway and suppressing cell growth. Similarly, dephosphorylation
  of MEK and ERK attenuates MAPK signaling and reduces proliferation. PP2A also regulates
  β-catenin within the Wnt pathway, preventing tumorigenesis. Collectively, these
  actions underscore PP2A’s role as a master tumor suppressor
