caption: Mechanistic illustration of IL12@NAM for promoting CD8+ T cell activation
  while reducing exhuastion in the TME. (A) Schematic representation of IL12@NAM preparation
  and its acid-responsive release of Mg2+ and IL12. (B) Following peritumoral injection,
  IL12@NAM promotes CD8+ T cell infiltration into deep tumor tissues. (C) Released
  Mg2+ enhances CD8+ T cell activation via phosphorylation of FAK and ERK1/2 while
  synergizing with the Ca2+–NFAT pathway, leading to augmented T cell activation including
  CD69 and CD25 and cytotoxic cytokines release. Additionally, H+ neutralization and
  IL12/Mg2+ release alleviate immunosuppression by inhibiting Tregs and promoting
  Th1 CD4+ T cell differentiation. This further reduces CD8+ T cell exhaustion by
  downregulating PD1 expression
