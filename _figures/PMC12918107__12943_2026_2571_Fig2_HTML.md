caption: 'Basic processes for maintaining mitochondrial homeostasis. The figure illustrates
  the key mechanisms involved in mitochondrial homeostasis, divided into four main
  processes. 1) Mitochondrial Energy Metabolism and Signal Transduction: The figure
  shows essential metabolic pathways, including glycolysis, TCA cycle, and oxidative
  phosphorylation, as well as important lipid and protein metabolism. It also highlights
  signal transduction pathways involved in regulating cell death and gene expression,
  among others. 2) Mitochondrial Quality Control: Mitochondrial quality control mechanisms,
  such as fission, fusion, mitophagy, mitochondrial transfer, and the unfolded protein
  response (UPRmt), are depicted to maintain mitochondrial integrity and function.
  3) Mitochondrial DNA: The diagram highlights the role of mitochondrial DNA (mtDNA)
  in encoding key proteins and the enzymes required for mitochondrial activities.
  4) Ion Homeostasis: Ion balance, including calcium and metal ions (iron, copper),
  is critical for maintaining mitochondrial function and supporting energy production.
