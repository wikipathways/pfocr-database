caption: 'The important role of mitochondria in neutrophil differentiation, chemotaxis,
  apoptosis, and physiological functions. Mitochondria play a crucial role in the
  metabolic reprogramming process from glycolysis to OXPHOS during neutrophil differentiation.
  Spermine enhances neutrophil polarization and chemotaxis by activating the MCU on
  the IMM, thereby modulating mitochondrial calcium homeostasis. Ru360 exerts an inhibitory
  effect on MCU activity. Mitochondrial-derived Mfn2 and ATP also influence the chemotactic
  behavior of neutrophils. Mitochondria regulate neutrophil apoptosis through modulation
  of mitochondrial membrane potential and the release of pro-apoptotic factors. Mitochondria
  influence neutrophil degranulation by regulating fMLP release through the production
  of mtROS and by regulating mitochondrial dynamics. Mitochondria are primarily involved
  in two distinct pathways of NETs formation: NOX-independent NETosis, which requires
  mtROS production and increased intracellular Ca2+ concentration, and mitochondrial
  NETosis, which is accompanied by the release of mitochondrial DNA (mtDNA)'
