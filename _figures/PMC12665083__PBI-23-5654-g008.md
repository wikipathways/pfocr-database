caption: 'Identification of DEGs in the carbon metabolic pathway and NR biosynthesis
  pathway, and differentially accumulated metabolites. (a) The schematic of the carbon
  metabolic pathway and NR biosynthesis pathway and expressions of DEGs encoding structural
  enzymes of these pathways, sugar signalling and transport in 1‐sst1‐fft and WT.
  Expression levels for each gene in 1‐sst1‐fft and WT were shown by heatmap using
  FPKM. Each value represents the mean of three biological replicates. FPKM, fragments
  per kilobase of transcript per million fragments mapped. (b) Heatmaps of carbohydrate
  accumulation in the roots of 1‐sst1‐fft and WT. Three biological replicates of 1‐sst1‐fft
  mutants (sf‐1, sf‐2 and sf‐3) and WT (WT‐1, WT‐2 and WT‐3) plants at ~200 DAC were
  used for analysis. The normalised signal intensities of sugar contents (unit variance
  scaling) were visualised as a colour spectrum and presented as heatmaps using hierarchical
  cluster analysis. Upregulated sugars are indicated by red asterisks, and downregulated
  sugars are indicated by blue asterisks. Ribose: D‐ribose; GlcNAc: 2‐Ace‐2‐Deo‐D‐glucosamine;
  Raf: Raffinose; Fru: Fructose; Cel: Cellobiose; Suc: Sucrose; Lac: Lactose. (c)
  Heatmaps of differentially accumulated terpenoids in the roots of 1‐sst1‐fft mutants
  and WT. Differentially accumulated metabolites were determined by VIP > 1, fold
  change ≥ 3, and p < 0.01. Monoterpenoids, sesquiterpenoids, diterpenoids, triterpenes
  and terpenes are highlighted in blue, green, yellow, orange and black font, respectively'
