caption: Mechanism of STING-mediated apoptosis regulation. During apoptotic signaling,
  pro-apoptotic proteins such as BAK/BAX induce mitochondrial membrane permeabilization,
  leading to the release of cytochrome c and mitochondrial DNA (mtDNA). The released
  mtDNA activates the cGAS-STING-TBK1 pathway, driving IFN-mediated immune responses.
  Concurrently, cytosolic cytochrome c facilitates the assembly of the Apaf1-caspase-9
  apoptosome, which subsequently activates effector caspases (eg, caspase-3 and caspase-7)
  to execute apoptosis. Caspase-3 negatively regulates the cGAS-STING pathway by cleaving
  IRF3 and STING. On the other hand, STING activation triggers ER stress, ultimately
  leading to apoptosis through BAK/BAX activation and cytochrome c release. This intricate
  interplay highlights the dual roles of STING in modulating apoptosis and immune
  responses
