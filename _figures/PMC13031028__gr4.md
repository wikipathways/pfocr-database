  effects of key gut-derived microbial metabolites on atrial tissue. These include
  profibrotic and proinflammatory actions of TMAO, oxidative and immune responses
  driven by indoxyl sulfate, anti-inflammatory effects of short-chain fatty acids,
  and the dual influence of bile acids through FXR and muscarinic pathways. These
  pathways converge to drive structural and electrophysiological remodeling associated
  with atrial fibrillation. FXR = farnesoid X receptor; NADPH = nicotinamide adenine
