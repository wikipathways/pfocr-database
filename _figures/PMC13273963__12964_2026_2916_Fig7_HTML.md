caption: Mechanistic overview of IQGAP3-mediated cancer therapy resistance. IQGAP3
  promotes DNA repair and radioresistance by interacting with Rad17 and activating
  the ATM/Chk2, ATR/Chk1, and AKT signaling pathways, facilitating the efficient repair
  of double-strand DNA breaks and promoting cell survival after genotoxic stress.
  IQGAP3 upregulates drug resistance–associated efflux transporters such as P-glycoprotein
  (PGP), multidrug resistance–associated protein 1 (MRP1), and glutathione S-transferase
  π (GSTP1), leading to enhanced efflux of chemotherapeutic agents and reduced intracellular
  drug accumulation. IQGAP3 enhances cancer stemness by transcriptionally upregulating
  stem cell–associated factors (KLF4, NANOG, OCT4), thereby increasing the population
  of cancer stem cells. This enrichment of cancer stem cells contributes to therapy
  resistance and tumor relapse following treatment
