caption: Working model of the impact of AMOTL2 on the type I IFN signaling pathway.
  (A) With endogenous expression levels of AMOTL2, basal unphosphorylated STAT1 levels
  are low, and STAT1 activation occurs normally following IFN-β treatment. ISG transcription
  is high, and ZIKV replication is better controlled. (B) AMOTL2 knockout results
  in high basal levels of unphosphorylated STAT1. Upon IFN treatment, the cell exhibits
  reduced STAT1 phosphorylation and nuclear translocation and low ISG transcription,
  consistent with increased ZIKV replication
