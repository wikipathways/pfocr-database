caption: Host signaling responses to microbiome interactions. (a) Interaction of commensal
  bacteria with pattern recognition receptors (PRRs), such as Toll-like receptors,
  activates NF-κB and MAPK pathways, producing pro-inflammatory cytokines, IL-10,
  and T-regulatory cells to maintain immune balance. (b) Bacterial metabolites such
  as short-chain fatty acids (SCFAs) enter the circulation system, cross the Blood-Brain
  Barrier (BBB) and the Central Nervous System (CNS), and modulate neurotransmitter
  production. Moreover, LPS binds with TLR to activate the NF-κB/MAPK pathway in immune
  cells, resulting in neurodegenerative diseases. (c) Microbial metabolites like butyrate
  are natural inhibitors of histone deacetylases (HDAC), which contribute to altered
  gene expression, resulting in the proliferation of epithelial cells or apoptosis
  of cancer cells. Additionally, butyrate can induce the proliferation of epithelial
  cells. (d) Incorporation of microbial metabolites on aryl hydrocarbon receptor (AHR)
  induces AHR signalling, resulting in Keratinocyte differentiation for barrier restoration
