caption: 'Logistic regression model displaying the relationship between predictor
  variables and adhesive capsulitis diagnosis probability. Peripheral cytokines stimulate
  the brain depending on their concentration through the neural and humoral pathways.
  Neural pathway: cytokine-producing cells can stimulate primary afferent nerve fibers
  in the vagus nerve. Sensory afferents from the vagus nerve relay information to
  areas of the brain through the activation of the nucleus tractus solitarius and
  the area postrema. Humoral pathway: cytokines can stimulate the brain through the
  choroid plexus and the CVOs. Within the brain parenchyma, the activation of endothelial
  cells is responsible for the subsequent release of second messengers such as PGE2
  and NO that act on areas susceptible to stimulation by cytokines in the brain. Mφ,
  macrophage; NK, “natural killers”; T, T lymphocyte; F, fibroblast; EC, epithelial
  cell; CVO, cirvunventricular organs; PG, prostaglandin; NO, nitric oxide; NTS, nucleus
  of the solitary tract; MCP, monocyte chemotactic protein; PGE2, prostaglandin E2'
