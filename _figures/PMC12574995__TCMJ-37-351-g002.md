  IκB degradation. Simultaneously, LH stimulates progesterone (P4) synthesis through
  the LHR–cAMP–PKA–CYP11A1/3β-HSD axis. P4, via PGR activation, restores IκB and suppresses
  NF-κB signaling, thereby resolving inflammation and preserving luteal cell function.
  This E2–ROS–NF-κB and P4–PGR–IκB balance ensures a tightly regulated inflammatory
  transition during ovulation
