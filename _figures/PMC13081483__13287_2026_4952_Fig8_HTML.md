caption: A proposed epigenetic model and hypothesized pathway in SHED by BA-inducible
  inflammatory exposure experienced-based etiology. A TNFA and IFNG caused by BA-inducible
  inflammation may impact its influence on tissue-specific stem cells of tooth germs,
  BA-SHED. The TNFA and IFNG stimulation primes the chromatin architecture remodeling
  to enhance transcription activity of the HNF6 promoter via the BRM–P65-P50 complex
  in BA-SHED. The enhancement of HNF6 expression may be epigenetically imprinted in
  BA-SHED in the absence of TNFA/IFNG exposure. As one of the epigenetic memory mechanisms,
  the HNF6 promoter by an auto-sustained positive feedback loop of the BRM–P65 complex.
  B Enhanced HNF6 significantly suppresses TGFBR2 levels in BA-SHED, inhibiting bile
  duct formation via the underlying TGFB signaling by TGFB1 stimulation. Meanwhile,
  HNF6-enhanced BA-SHED exhibit hepatogenic potency. A, B All images were created
