  distinct microbial pathways channel PCA into central carbon metabolism. B PCA degradation
  pathways across organisms (inspired by Wilkes et al. 2023): the 2,3-meta cleavage

  putida, Pseudomonas umsongensis, and Rhodococcus jostii, and the 4,5-meta cleavage

  Faint enzymes are absent in V. natriegens; bold, coloured enzymes are genome-annotated.
  C Annotated 4,5-meta cleavage pathway for PCA catabolism in V. natriegens. Enzyme
  presence and reaction directionality are based on KEGG annotations'
