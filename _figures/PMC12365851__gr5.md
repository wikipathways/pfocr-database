caption: Activation of RVE/LNK and LWD/TCP genes is crucial for circadian rhythm maintenance
  and clock output regulation.(A) RVE and LNK family members control various developmental
  processes in plants, including hypocotyl elongation, starch synthesis and degradation,
  and double fertilization, by regulating the expression of key genes.(B) RVE and
  LNK proteins improve resilience to abiotic and biotic stresses by directly regulating
  the expression of stress-response genes associated with cold and heat responses
  and with jasmonic acid, salicylic acid, and abscisic acid signaling pathways across
  multiple species.(C) RVE8, LNK1, and LNK2 regulate the biosynthesis of secondary
  metabolites such as anthocyanins and phenylpropanoids.(D) TTG1 and TCP proteins
  are multifunctional regulators with crucial roles in diverse aspects of plant growth
  and development.(E) LWD and TCP proteins serve as essential regulators of flowering
  time by controlling the expression of key floral integrator genes such as FT, SOC1,
  and AP1a in multiple plant species.(F) CHE (TCP21) serves as a versatile regulator
  of biotic stress responses by directly regulating the expression of genes related
  to salicylic acid (SA), jasmonic acid (JA), auxin (AUX), and strigolactone (SL)
  signaling
