caption: 'Pathways involved in the generation and accumulation of Ery-S1P under normal
  physiological conditions. Sphingolipid metabolism occurs in intracellular matrix
  in which ceramide plays a central role from which sphingosine and then S1P is generated
  and released to the extracellular matrix and circulatory system. Sphingosine and
  S1P is also generated in extracellular matrix from ceramide and secreted to the
  circulatory system. Sphingosine is imported to erythrocytes from the surrounding
  environment and get phosphorylated to S1P by the enzyme sphingosine kinase 1 (SPHK1).
  Intact S1P is also imported directly to the erythrocyte from the surroundings. (SMase:
  sphingomyelinase; SMS: sphingomyelin synthase; SPT: serine palmitoyl transferase;
  CK: ceramide kinase; C1PP: ceramide 1 phosphate phosphatase; GCS: glucosyl/galactosyl
  ceramide synthase; GCase: glucocerebrosidase; CDase: ceramidase; CerS: ceramide
  synthase; SPHK1: sphingosine kinase 1; SPHK2: sphingosine kinase 2; SPPase: S1P
