caption: 'This schematic illustrates the bidirectional, self-reinforcing oncogenic
  cycle between metabolic reprogramming and DNA methylation in thyroid cancer. Top:
  Reprogramming of glucose, lipid, and amino acid metabolism changes the levels of
  key metabolites (e.g., SAM, α-KG, D-2-HG) that regulate the activity of DNMT and
  TET, thereby inducing hypermethylation in the promoter regions of tumor suppressor
  genes (e.g., RASSF1A, PTEN). Bottom: The resulting abnormal DNA methylation silences
  or activates specific genes (such as FAM111B, METTL16, FASN), thereby regulating
  signaling pathways (such as Wnt/β-catenin, mTORC1), and systemically enhancing tumor-promoting
  metabolic phenotypes, including glycolysis, lipid synthesis, and glutamine metabolism.
  This interactive cycle jointly promotes TC development and treatment resistance.
  Red and green arrows represent up-regulation/promotion and down-regulation/inhibition,
  respectively. Data Source:[20, 21, 30, 35, 55, 66–69, 71–90, 92, 93]. Created with
