caption: Integrated regulation of ferroptosis by oncogenic signaling, immune microenvironment,
  and gut microbiota. This figure summarizes the diverse molecular and environmental
  regulators of ferroptosis in cancer. Ferroptosis inducers such as erastin and RSL3
  target RAS-mutant cells, while RIT1 enhances ferroptosis sensitivity through key
  oncogenic pathways. TP53 activation by oxidative stress promotes ferroptotic death
  in DLBCL, whereas Nrf2 and ATF4 mediate context-dependent resistance. Gut microbiota,
  including Efm and F. nucleatum, modulate GPX4 and coenzyme Q10 synthesis, influencing
  ferroptosis and chemotherapy resistance. Cytokines such as IFN-γ and IL-12 synergize
  with targeted therapies to enhance ferroptosis. EMT-related transcription factors
  (e.g., ZEB1) and hypoxia-induced HIF-1α promote resistance by regulating lipid metabolism
  and cystine uptake. These insights reveal the complexity of ferroptosis regulation
  and its therapeutic potential
