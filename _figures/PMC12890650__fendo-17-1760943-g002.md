caption: 'Key pathways conferring resistance to ferroptosis. This diagram summarizes
  the principal endogenous defense systems that protect cells against ferroptosis.
  (A) The canonical System Xc–/GSH/GPX4 axis: The cystine/glutamate antiporter (System
  Xc–, composed of SLC7A11 and SLC3A2) imports cystine for glutathione (GSH) synthesis.
  GPX4 utilizes GSH to reduce lipid hydroperoxides (L-OOH) to non-toxic lipid alcohols
  (L-OH), thereby preventing lethal lipid peroxidation. Compounds like Orientin and
  Selenium can enhance this pathway. (B) Non-canonical parallel pathways: The FSP1/CoQ10
  system on the plasma membrane regenerates the antioxidant ubiquinol (CoQ10H2) using
  NAD(P)H. The mitochondrial DHODH enzyme independently produces ubiquinol within
  the inner mitochondrial membrane. The GCH1/BH4 pathway suppresses ferroptosis in
  a GPX4-independent manner by promoting lipid remodeling. Together, these systems
  constitute a multi-layered antioxidant network that maintains cellular redox homeostasis
  and inhibits ferroptosis'
