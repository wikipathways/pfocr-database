caption: (A) Network of interactions between the SNPs significantly associated with
  CD. The association of SNPs with celiac disease was analyzed using PLINK v1.07,
  and 18 significantly associated SNPs were tested for interactions using the SNP
  interaction pattern identifier of the R package SIPI. 48 pairs of SNP-SNP interactions
  were identified as significant from this analysis. Hub SNPs from this analysis include
  rs1018326, rs10800746, rs6441961, rs2395182, rs10892258 and rs5891164. (B) Network
  of interactions between the genes significantly associated with CD. The genes corresponding
  to the 18 significantly associated SNPs shown in Fig. 1 were tested for interactions
  using the R package SIPI. In the case of HLA-DQ2.5, HLA-DQ2.2 and HLA-DQ8 which
  are haplotypes, the actual gene names are shown instead of the haplotype names.
  (C) Cartoon depicting some of the molecules involved in the T cell activation through
  the T cell receptor signaling pathway downstream to binding of gliadin peptides
  to HLA-DQ2 (or HLA-DQ8). The proteins depicted here correspond to the products of
  genes that were significantly associated with CD in this study. TCR – T cell receptor.
  NF-κB – nuclear factor kappa B
