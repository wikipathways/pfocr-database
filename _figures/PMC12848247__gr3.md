caption: 'Exploiting the tumor landscape to counteract resistance mechanisms and maximize
  therapeutic efficacy of oHSV(A) Glioma oncogenic pathways limit oHSV efficacy and
  are circumvented with combination treatments. Commonly upregulated in glioma, downstream
  signaling of different growth factor receptors converge in the PI3K/AKT signaling
  pathway. The PI3K/AKT pathway leads to mTOR activation and protein translation.
  Endothelial growth factor (EGF) signaling also contributes to glioma progression
  via the MAPK/ERK signaling pathway. Both an EGF receptor (EGFR) inhibitor (panitumumab)
  and MEK inhibitors (trametinib, dabrafenib, regorafenib) have been combined with
  oHSV. Axitinib also inhibits c-Kit, platelet-derived growth factor receptor, and
  EGFR downstream signaling. Despite oHSV utilizing cellular microtubules for nuclear
  transport of viral capsids, the use of the microtubule stabilizer paclitaxel synergizes
  with oHSV for tumor cell killing. (B) Strategies to counteract ECM barriers to oHSV
  treatment in tumors include oHSVs armed with angiogenesis inhibitors including IL-12,
  ECM proteases such as MMPs, and combination with agents such as BVZ. (C) oHSV has
  been used in combination with CAR-T cells in pre-clinical studies following three
  strategies: (1) infection of the tumor with oHSV followed by CAR-T cell delivery,
  (2) arming of an oHSV with a CAR antigen for membrane expression, and (3) loading
  of the CAR-T cells with oHSV for delivery into the tumor'
