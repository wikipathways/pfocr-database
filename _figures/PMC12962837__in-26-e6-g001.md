caption: Signaling pathway and biological functions of IL-2 family cytokines in TME.
  (A) The IL-2 family cytokine receptors share the common γ chain. Unlike IL-2 and
  IL-15 receptors, IL-4, IL-7, IL-9 and IL-21 are composed of 2 subunits, including
  the γc. IL-2R and IL-15R share the IL-2/15Rβ subunit. Upon cytokine binding, receptor
  dimerization or oligomerization is induced, leading to activation via phosphorylation
  of JAK1 and JAK3. The activated JAKs phosphorylate specific tyrosine residues on
  STAT proteins. While the specific STAT proteins activated vary depending on the
  cytokine, STAT1, STAT3, and STAT5 commonly serve as the primary signal transducers.
  (B) These receptors are expressed on various immune cells, including T cells, B
  cells, and NK cells, and each cytokine signal precisely regulates distinct biological
  functions. They regulate proliferation, differentiation, activation, and survival
  of immune cells. Notably, through complex regulatory mechanisms within the TME,
  these signals can exert either anti-tumor or pro-tumor effects
