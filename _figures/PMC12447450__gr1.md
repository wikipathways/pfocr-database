caption: 'Tryptophan metabolic pathways.Tryptophan metabolism consists of five distinct
  pathways: the 5-hydroxytryptophan (5-HTP) pathway, the tryptamine (TAM) pathway,
  the indole-3-pyruvic acid (IPA) pathway, the indole-3-acetamide (IAM) pathway, and
  the indole-3-acetaldoxime (IAOx) pathway. The 5-HTP and TAM pathways produce serotonin
  and melatonin as their primary metabolites. Both the IPA and IAM pathways generate
  auxin as the principal metabolite. The IAOx pathway yields the plant antitoxin camalexin
  and indole glucosinolates as its key metabolites. Solid arrows represent single
  reactions, whereas dotted arrows indicate multi-step reactions. Core metabolites
  are denoted with a star symbol. White boxes with question marks denote transporters
  or steps that have yet to be identified'
