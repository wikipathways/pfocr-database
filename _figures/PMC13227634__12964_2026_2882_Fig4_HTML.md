caption: The roles of IKKα in the tumour microenvironment. IKKα influences several
  different pathways and cell types within the tumour microenvironment. In IKKα-deficient
  lung tumours, HIF-1α levels increase, enhancing tumour growth under hypoxic conditions.
  Additionally, low levels of IKKα in lung tumour cells promote the infiltration of
  monocyte-derived macrophages and Treg generation, resulting in a highly immunosuppressive
  TME. In prostate cancer, infiltrating B cells promote the activation of IKKα, which
  enhances the E2F1-dependent expression of cell proliferation genes. Activation of
  IKKα in endothelial cells promotes the expression of cell adhesion proteins, enabling
  attachment of cancer cells and promoting metastasis
