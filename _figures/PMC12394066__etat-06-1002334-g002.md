caption: Mechanisms of radiation-induced cytokine storm and inflammation. Radiation
  treatment can trigger cytokine storms via 3 different pathways. Radiation treatment
  induces damage in cancer cells, and damage-associated molecular patterns (DAMPs)
  released from the cancer cells are sensitized by pattern recognition receptors (PRRs),
  leading to cytokine storms. These DAMPs can also induce the release of chemokines
  such as CXCL16 and CCL5, which lead to cytokine storms. When nuclei of cancer cells
  are damaged, DNA particles can accumulate in the cytosol, and these DNA particles
  can activate the cGAS pathway, which will induce a DNA-driven immune response via
  type I interferon (IFN) and ultimately induce cytokine storms. Created in BioRender.
