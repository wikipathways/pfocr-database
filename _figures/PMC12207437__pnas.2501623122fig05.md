caption: KLYLS as the sole source of tyrosine required for laccaic acid biosynthesis.
  (A) Free tyrosine concentration (relative proportion) was significantly higher in
  adult K. lacca females as compared with the phloem diet (Welch’s t test, BH-adjusted
  P-value = 0.040). (B) Genes encoding the complete pathway for tyrosine synthesis
  were present only in the KLYLS genome and not in the host or the Wolbachia genomes.
  Fungal and insect genes along with homologous prokaryotic gene names are provided
  inside each box. (C) Transcriptomic evidence from KLYLS showing expression of all
  enzymes required for tyrosine biogenesis. (D) Proposed pathway involving the role
  of KLYLS in polyketide backbone synthesis and tyrosine provisioning for laccaic
  acid biosynthesis. Possible routes of conjugation of tyrosine with the kermesic
  acid backbone and routes of formation of laccaic acids A, B, and E are represented
  by dashed arrows
