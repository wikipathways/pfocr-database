caption: Modulation of MAPK and STAT Pathways by Agarwood. Modulation of MAPK and
  STAT Pathways by Agarwood. External stimuli activate the Mitogen-Activated Protein
  Kinase (MAPK) family, including JNK, ERK, and p38, as well as the Signal Transducer
  and Activator of Transcription (STAT) pathway (STAT1/3), leading to their phosphorylation.
  These phosphorylation events are crucial for transmitting inflammatory signals.
  Agarwood active components, particularly sesquiterpenoids such as Agarperoxinol
  B, intervene at this upstream level by inhibiting the phosphorylation of JNK, ERK,
  p38, and STAT1/3. This upstream intervention blocks signal transmission, leading
  to a comprehensive reduction in inflammatory responses
