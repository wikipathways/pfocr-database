caption: The mechanism of PCSK9 inhibitors in intervening neuroinflammation after
  AIS. PCSK9 promotes neuroinflammation after AIS by activating the NF-κB inflammatory
  pathway in nerve cells, causing damage to nerve cells and disruption of the BBB,
  which allows peripheral inflammatory factors to pass through the damaged BBB and
  exacerbate the immune-inflammatory response in the CNS. PCSK9 inhibitors may alleviate
  neuroinflammatory responses after AIS through multiple pathways, including indirect
  inhibition of the NF-κB inflammatory pathway via PCSK9, direct inhibition of the
  NF-κB inflammatory pathway via AMPK, and reduction of BBB damage. In addition, PCSK9
  inhibitors can also reduce the accumulation of toxic Aβ proteins in neurons. PCSK9
  inhibitors exert neuroprotective effects on AIS through multiple mechanisms and
  pathways. Solid arrows indicate promotion; dashed arrows indicate inhibition. AIS
  acute ischemic stroke, AMPK adenosine 5′-monophosphate (AMP)-activated protein kinase,
  ApoER2 apolipoprotein E receptor 2, BBB blood-brain barrier, ERK-p phosphorylated
  extracellular signal-regulated kinase, ICAM-1 intercellular adhesion molecule 1,
  I-κB inhibitor of nuclear factor κB, IL-6 interleukin 6, IL-10 interleukin 10, IL-1β
  interleukin 1 beta, Lp(a) lipoprotein (a), NF-κB nuclear factor κB, PCSK9 proprotein
  convertase subtilisin/kexin type 9, PCSK9i PCSK9 inhibitors, ROS reactive oxygen
  species, TLR4 toll-like receptor 4, TNF-α tumor necrosis factor alpha, VCAM-1 vascular
  cell adhesion molecule 1, TGF-β transforming growth factor beta
