  IRS-1 and PI-3 kinase, interrupting the insulin-signaling pathway. Psychological
  distress also stimulates the sympathetic nervous system, stimulating the renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system.
  These mechanisms collectively inhibit the movement of GLUT-4 to the surface membranes
  of muscle and fat cells, thereby impeding insulin-mediated glucose uptake, creating
  a milieu of insulin resistance and metabolic dysregulationHPA: hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal;
